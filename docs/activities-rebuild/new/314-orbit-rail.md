## The skill
Order the planets from the Sun and tell the small rocky planets from the giant planets.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE ORBIT RAIL: the Sun at the left edge and eight orbit rails of increasing radius. The child drags each planet onto a rail; a planet set on a rail already taken bumps the other off, and a planet on its own rail starts orbiting (inner ones faster, so the motion itself encodes order). Sort mode: the planets are shown at true relative size; the child drops each into the rocky or giant tray, and a distractor (Moon, Sun, Pluto as dwarf) goes to 'not a planet'.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Thinking the planets are evenly spaced or random; calling the Moon or the Sun a planet; Pluto still a planet.
- **Pictures:** space (earth, saturn, moon, sun, planet) exist; MISSING/WRONG: accurate Mercury (grey, cratered), Uranus (pale cyan), Neptune (deep blue), plus lcs-pictures labels for mercury, venus, mars, jupiter, uranus, neptune, sun ×11.
- **Settings:** Mode: order from the Sun / rocky or giant · Planets: first four / all eight
- **Rounds:** ≥ 8 rounds: order rounds give 4-8 planets in shuffled trays and sometimes pre-place one; sort rounds draw 5-6 bodies incl. one non-planet.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G3-394, G3-395
- **Distinct from:** No space activity exists.
- **Build risk:** Blocked on corrected planet art + labels. Pluto appears only as "not a planet" (dwarf planet) and needs a picture too.

## BLOCKED — needs an operator decision before building
The planet pictures are in the image cache but not in lcs-pictures (no vocabulary labels), and three are scientifically wrong: Mercury is drawn banded like a gas giant, Uranus orange-banded, Neptune dark-banded. A planets activity cannot ship wrong planets.
