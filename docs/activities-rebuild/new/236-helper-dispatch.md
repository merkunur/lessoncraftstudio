## The skill
Know community helpers, the jobs they do and the tools they use.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE CALL THAT NEEDS THE RIGHT HELPER. A town street (apparatus) with five doorways; at one door a request lights up as a library picture (a car to mend, a cat to treat, a cake order, a book to return, a vegetable patch to dig). Helpers (library occupations) wait at the station, each with their tool beside them. The child drags a helper to the lit door; the helper walks there. Right: the helper's tool acts on the request (the mechanic's car drives off, the vet's stethoscope pulses over the cat and the cat sits up, the baker's cake appears in the window). Miss 1: the helper arrives, the tool does nothing, the helper walks back. Miss 2: the right helper's tool glows at the station. TOOLS mode (K-213): the helper stands at the door; the child gives one of three tools.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Thinking any helper can fix any problem; gendered assumptions about jobs (the library pictures show varied helpers).
- **Pictures:** occupations (firefighter, doctor, veterinarian, chef, baker, farmer, teacher, mechanic, librarian, gardener, barber, pilot, nurse); requests and tools: vehicles (car, airplane), animals/pets (cat, dog), bakery (cake), classroom (book), vegetables (carrot), around-the-house (hose, rake, shovel), hospital (stethoscope, bandage, thermometer), kitchen-tools (whisk, pan), tools (hammer, ladder). MISSING: postal worker, police officer.
- **Settings:** Mode: send the helper / give the tool · Helpers: 4 / 6
- **Rounds:** 9 requests per pass, each helper the answer ≤ 2 times, station order shuffled; one right helper per request (the firefighter appears only in tools mode, with the hose).
- **Worksheet types it covers:** K-213
- **Distinct from:** No community activity exists.
- **Build risk:** Occupation pictures show one gender each — the reviewer checks the set is varied overall. Postal worker/police absent from both modes until art exists.
