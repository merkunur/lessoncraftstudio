## 6. Interaction & UX
- **Runtime: identify-start → place-zero → measure → read.** `rulerlab-core.js` holds the round `{ id, cog, object:{spriteKey, startLatt, trueLenUnits (NEVER rendered)}, ruler:{initialOffsetLatt (≠ startLatt), latticePerUnit, scaleUnits, brokenZeroAt?}, tools[], unitSystem ('cm'|'in'), startIdentified (child's assertion) }` + `identifyStart`/`placeZero`/`measureCommit`/`tapNumeral` + `validate(state)` → toolChosen===ruler AND startIdentified-correct AND offsetLatt===startLatt AND tappedValue===readingAt(far edge). **The answer = `f(identify the start + align zero + read the scale)`, never `f(nudge until green)` / `f(read a pre-aligned number)` / `f(read the answer)`.**
- **State separation (anti-cheat):** `snapshot()` exposes `{ cog, object (no number, no length), ruler (numerals slide WITH it), readLine (fixed to the far edge — NO number of its own), startMarker (child's assertion), NO live lamp, NO proximity narration }`. `facts()` exposes per-round `{ noAutoAlign (initialOffsetLatt≠startLatt), noProximityGradient (===true; feedback non-monotonic in distance-to-correct), alignmentGraded (===true), startIdentifiedByChild (===true), readingRelativeToPlacement (===true), offByOneReachableAndShown (===true), lengthNotPreshown (across ALL channels incl aria-valuetext), spanReadingIsCountNotSubtract (===true), standardUnits, integerScale }`.
- **Place + nudge + measure + read:** drag the ruler (whole body = grab handle, ≥44px) to bring zero to the asserted start; **one-step nudge buttons** carry the precision (the whole task is doable with nudge alone; ≥44px); press **"Measure it"** to commit (resolves the read-line; NO right/wrong feedback at this press — just resolves); tap the scale numeral the read-line crosses. NO Check button (the "Measure it" commit is the child's deliberate resolve, not a correctness checker).
- **Mobile @280px (the critic's F9):** the ruler + strip on the SVG stage; **responsive 8-unit scale on narrow screens** (objects ≤8 units on mobile rounds); nudge buttons + "Measure it" ≥44px; numerals get invisible ≥44px hit-rects. - **Accessibility (the critic's F1b — position not proximity):** the ruler is an **ARIA slider**; `aria-valuetext` narrates the **absolute relationship of two named landmarks** ("zero is at the 3-cm line; the ribbon's start is at the 5-cm line") — NEVER proximity/warmer-colder, NEVER the length.
