## 2. Concept & creative hook
**You are the Ferry-Keeper — a kind otter in a lantern-hat — at the Mirrorwater dock at dusk. The river-creatures must cross to Lantern Island, and the rule of the water is absolute: the two-sided pontoon ferry only sails when every traveler has a partner, and the boat balances LEVEL with the SAME number on each bench. EVEN = everyone pairs, the boat rides level, it sails. ODD = one traveler is always left standing alone on the dock, lantern in hand, waiting for a ferry that can't balance.** The lone waiting traveler is the emotional anchor of "odd."
- **THE ANSWER IS THE LEFTOVER, NOT THE LABEL (the pedagogy headline + the critic's deepest fix):** the child PAIRS travelers (tap two → they hold hands as a partner-pair) and the answer they commit is the LEFTOVER-STATE — did the cozy dock-bench end EMPTY (all paired → even) or OCCUPIED (one waits → odd)? There is no "even/odd" button to tap from a digit; the parity is a fact about whether anyone is left.
- **THE EQUATION IS READ OFF YOUR OWN PAIRING, NOT TYPED (the halving-fact kill):** after pairing, the partner-pairs board — one of each pair to the LEFT bench, the partner to the RIGHT bench — so the two benches ARE the two equal addends (`8 = 4 + 4`), and the `4` is READ OFF the benches the child BUILT, never typed from 8÷2. On an ODD crowd, a pair is impossible (one traveler can't board both benches) → the leftover is FORCED, the boat can't balance, the equation can't complete → "one waits for the next ferry" (the parity meaning, made falsifiable — odd counts ARE offered).
- **PREDICT BEFORE YOU PAIR (the eye-equalize kill):** the child first commits "will everyone get a partner, or will one wait?" (a leftover claim, NOT even/odd) — then pairs to confirm. Eye-balancing the boat can't pre-commit; the halving-fact predicts the label, not the leftover.
- **YOU CAN'T RE-PAIR YOUR WAY OUT OF ODD (the inevitability):** for an odd crowd, the child can re-pair the travelers any way they like — one is ALWAYS left standing. The inevitability is felt through the child's own failed attempts to "fix" it.
- **THE SAME 8, TWO WAYS (the equivalence-bridge — the spine, a child ACTION):** the child takes their 4 partner-pairs and RE-DEALS them into the 2 benches (one from each pair → bench-L, the partner → bench-R), discovering BY HAND that 4 pairs ≡ 2 benches of 4 ≡ the same 8.
- **THE PAYOFF:** all paired + the benches equal → the boat RIDES LEVEL (a CSS consequence-animation of the correctly-built split, NOT a graded balance) + sails to Lantern Island + a lit lantern joins the island (`GameCollection` — a brightening island; NEVER a count). `ctx.correct()`. An odd crowd → no fail; the lone traveler waits on a cozy bench ("we'll wait for the next ferry") + joins a FUTURE crowd (the parity broken so it's not a sequence-trick — F-7).
- **Anti-generic statement:** NOT tap-even-or-odd — (a) the answer is the LEFTOVER-STATE not a label (kills the digit-rule's bit-channel); (b) the equation is pair-bound not typed + the `HALVING_FACT_SOLVER` (kills 8÷2=4); (c) the balance is demoted to animation + the `EYE_EQUALIZE_SOLVER` (kills eye-balancing); (d) odd counts offered + predict-before + the inevitability (the parity MEANING falsifiable); the gate proves digit + count-recall + mechanical-pair + halving-fact + eye-equalize + brute solvers ALL fail while the parity-meaning oracle passes.
