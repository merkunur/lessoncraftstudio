## The skill
Search a busy field systematically for every copy of a target — a picture, a shape, a letter among look-alikes, or a sight word — without missing any or marking one twice.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE LENS THAT LEAVES A TRAIL. A dense scene (or a grid of letters / words) fills the stage; the target sits in a small frame. The child drags a magnifying lens over the field; under the lens things enlarge, and everything the lens has passed over is faintly tinted, so the unsearched parts stay visibly bright — a scanning strategy the child can see. Tapping a found item under the lens pins a tag on it and adds a bead to the counter tray. Check: missed copies glow inside the still-bright region first ("you have not looked there yet"); a wrong tag (a d for a b, a cat for a fox) flips the two side by side. Shapes-around-us setting: library objects (clock, plate, book, window) and the child finds every one shaped like the target shape.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Random searching (missing items in unvisited corners), double-counting, and accepting mirror-twin letters (b/d/p/q) or look-alike words.
- **Pictures:** any colour theme for scenes (identical instances of the target), plus school/classroom, kitchen tools, household nouns for the shapes setting; letters and words are type
- **Settings:** Target: pictures / shapes in objects / letters / sight words · Field: sparse / busy
- **Rounds:** 9 rounds; 3–8 copies of the target, copies spread across all four quadrants every round (no quadrant empty in more than 1 round in 3); count answered by the tray beads, so no option position tell; letter rounds include its mirror-twins ≥ 2 each.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** K-059, K-070, K-229, K-327, K-371
- **Distinct from:** Classify-and-count (Sort and Count) works on a handful of pictures; this is SEARCH through clutter. Not the Peek Window (glance) nor the Counting Table (tagging a small set).
- **Build risk:** Dense scenes must keep each item ≥ 44 px at 360 px — the lens solves legibility but the tap target under the lens must be measured; the per-locale sight-word lists already exist.
