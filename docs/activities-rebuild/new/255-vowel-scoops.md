## The skill
Every syllable has exactly one vowel sound: split a word so that each part keeps its vowel.

## The apparatus and its invention
SCOOPS UNDER THE WORD. A picture word lies on a line (letters as tiles) with its library picture above. The child drags little dividers into the gaps between letters; under each part a scoop forms and holds those letters. A scoop holding no vowel sags and drops its letters back onto the line (the divider springs out); a scoop holding a vowel lights the vowel like a heartbeat. Check (when every letter is scooped): right — the gated syllable split matches and each scoop beats once in order, then the picture nods. Miss 1: a scoop with two vowel sounds (ba|nana as two scoops) pulses twice, showing it needs another divider. Miss 2: the vowels on the line glow and the child tries again. COUNT setting: before splitting, the child taps how many scoops the word needs (1–3 scoop tiles), then splits to check.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Splits a word into chunks of equal length or into letters, ignoring the vowel; counts letters instead of beats.
- **Pictures:** animals, fruits, toys, vehicles — nouns in approved-words-<locale>.json (gated splits).
- **Settings:** Words: 2 syllables / 2-3 syllables · Task: find the vowels / split and find the vowels.
- **Rounds:** 9 words per pass (2- and 3-syllable), split positions never in the middle of the word in more than a third of rounds; core setting excludes silent-e and vowel-team words; team setting (en ea/ou/ai, de ei/au/eu, nl ij/ui/oe, fi long vowels aa/uu) lights the team as one vowel; one right split.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G1-329
- **Distinct from:** Domino's Two-Part Words and the syllable builder assemble given syllables; neither states or tests the vowel-per-syllable rule.
- **Build risk:** Per-locale: fi/es/it are clean; en/fr/da need careful pools (no silent final e in the core pool; fr mute-e ruling open); da uses the K-1 orthographic pool.
