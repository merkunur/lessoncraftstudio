# Reviewers' notes: registry `en`

Written by the reviewers of the crosswalk (`scripts/curricula/apply-review.js`). 111 teaching points carry a reviewer's row; the reason for each is in `frontend/content/curricula/en/crosswalk-overrides.json`.

## review-math

- Ordinal numbers, repeating patterns, the calendar and 24-hour time are not Common Core content; ordinals and 24-hour time now map to nothing, patterns map to 4.OA.C.5 as readiness (the only standard on shape patterns that follow a rule).
- 3:thermometer is mapped to 3-ESS2-1 as teaches; that id is not in PART 2 so I left the point alone, but reading a scale is at most 'prepares' for that science standard. In mathematics the nearest fit is 3.MD.B.3 as prepares (reading a scale that counts by 2, 5 or 10).
- 1:sort-three-groups carries L.K.5.a, an ELA id not in PART 2; left untouched. K.MD.B.3 is right.
- 2:fraction-of-a-set (prepares 3.NF.A.1) and 3:fraction-of-set (teaches 3.NF.A.1) are the same skill with two relations. I left both; strictly, a fraction of a set as an amount (1/3 of 12 is 4) is nearer 3.OA.A.2 equal shares, and the Common Core treats a fraction of a quantity in 4.NF.B.4 and 5.NF.B.4.a.
- 2:share-and-group (grade 2, teaches 3.OA.A.2) is the same activity as 3:division-sharing; teaches is right only if the grade 2 sheets write the division sentence. If they stop at sharing pictures, make it prepares.
- 1:coins is left as teaches 2.MD.C.8, the usual US alignment for counting coins, although the standard is worded as word problems with dollar and cent symbols; check that the sheets use US coins and the cent symbol.
- K:subitize, K:tally-marks and K:estimate have no standard of their own in the Common Core; K.CC.B.5 is the accepted home and I left them.
- Several changes turn on the number range of the sheets, which the skill text does not give (1:bar-model, 1:open-number-line, 2:hundreds-chart-moves): if a sheet does reach within 100 two-step problems, or within 1000, restore teaches.
- Relations were judged on content only: where a lower-grade point does what a later standard asks (1:odd-even-pairs, 2:symmetry-line) it is teaches; where it works in a smaller range than the standard (two-digit expanded form, number words to 50) it is prepares.
- 3:balance-equations: 3.OA.A.4 is kept as extends on the assumption that the sheets use multiplication or division; if they use only addition and subtraction, replace it with 1.OA.D.8 as extends.

## review-ela-science

- Strand-level ids (L.1, K-LS1, K-PS, K-2-ETS, 2-ESS2, 5-ESS1, K.G) are not in the registry; every point that used one has been given an exact standard or none.
- Common Core has no cursive standard: the two cursive-writing points are marked as extending L.1.1.a (print all letters); reading cursive is left with nothing.
- NGSS standards are performances (investigate, model, argue); a worksheet that teaches the underlying idea is marked teaches only where the idea is the standard's content, otherwise prepares.
- Life cycles belong to grade 3 (3-LS1-1) and the water cycle and planets to middle school; grade K-2 worksheets on them can only prepare.
- Identifying word classes (noun, verb, adjective) is first asked for in L.3.1.a; grades 1-2 only ask children to use them, so the sorts are mapped to the 'use' components plus L.3.1.a as prepares.
- L.K.5.a / L.1.5.a were added only where the whole task is a category sort (fruit-vegetable, natural-made, transport, food groups); other health and social studies sorts (wants-needs, healthy food, community helpers) were left unmapped on purpose.
- 1:body-parts and K:human-body are the same skill and now carry the same row (1-LS1-1 prepares); drop both together if body-part naming should stay health-only.
- Maps, continents, family, road safety, hygiene, drawing, mazes, logic grids, sudoku and feelings have no counterpart in Common Core ELA or NGSS and stay unmapped.
- K:alphabet-order (RF.K.1.d) and 2:moon-phases (1-ESS1-1) are loose but acceptable fits and were left as they are.
