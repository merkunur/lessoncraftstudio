## 5 Quality verdict

I would print the base, F2 and F4 for a second-grade class tomorrow: one highlighted sentence per row, four identical chips, a portrait that tells the child who the sentence is about and nothing that tells them the answer; the question is decided by READING the highlighted words, which is the whole point of a question-word page, and I checked that the picture cannot do the reading for them (the pedagogy's original rows could be solved by a child who cannot read a word, and that is the defect that would have embarrassed me most, because it looks like a beautiful page). F1 with the short answers and their pictures is the page a G1 teacher wants for the "frågeord och svar" lesson; F3 without pictures is honest but plain, and a teacher who wants pictures will notice they are gone; F5 is the one open page and lives or dies on the panel's scene. What would embarrass me: a fi chip in the wrong case for its verb, a sv "kon" printed for a cow, a place literal that is really a time ("at the party"), or a 9 px clock numeral that survives the lint and is unreadable on paper; the first three are panel signatures the gate cannot replace, the last is why every shipped clock sits at 88 or 120, never at the 74 floor.
