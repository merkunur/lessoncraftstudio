## Facts on existing pages that need fixing (top priority)
1. **K-208 "Day and Night":** the Moon (and the cloud) are only in the Night group. The Moon is often seen in the daytime sky, and "the Moon only comes out at night" is a known misconception. Drop the moon and the cloud, or let both groups accept them. My K lesson teaches that the Moon can be seen in the day.
2. **K-202 "Fruits and Vegetables":** tomato, cucumber and corn are filed under Vegetables. That is a kitchen grouping. In science they are fruits (seed) parts, and G2-363 "Parts of a Plant We Eat" marks tomato and cucumber as the fruit part, so Kindergarten contradicts Grade 2. Remove those three items or label the groups as kitchen groups.
3. **K-211 "Hot and Cold":** mittens are filed under Cold. Mittens are not cold; they keep hands warm. A child who says "warm" is right.
4. Lower priority (two right answers or a gap):
   - **K-204:** rabbit and parrot are filed under Pets, but wild cottontail rabbits are common in the US.
   - **K-209:** duck is filed under Farm, but wild mallards are common in the US.
   - **K-201:** every non-living picture is a made object and there is no seed. Children can come away thinking "non-living = made by people". Add a rock, water and a seed.
   - **G1-407:** "Every animal needs food and a home" leaves out water and air.
