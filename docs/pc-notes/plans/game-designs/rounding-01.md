## §1 — Concept / world
**The Halfway Harbors of Tidecove.** Marina, a calm lighthouse-cat, keeps a dusk archipelago where every number is a tired little boat that wants to dock for the night. Boats can only rest at **round-number harbors**: every multiple of 10 is a small **lantern-harbor**, every multiple of 100 a big **lighthouse**. Between any two neighbor harbors floats a **halfway-buoy** (the midpoint) — the tipping point of the channel — and each harbor casts a **welcome tide-line** marking the first and last boat it claims (the band). The warm forward act — *send the boat home to the nearer harbor* — is the felt MODEL/teaching of "rounding = nearest round number." But the GRADED acts are the ones a rote digit-rounder can't do: **set a harbor's welcome tide-line (the band boundaries), read which harbors are guiding tonight (place-governance), and catch the false-flag boats (the meaning-traps).** Non-numeric abundance vessel: across a session Tidecove's **harbor-lights multiply into a growing constellation of safe lights along the coast** (never a count/score). `ctx.correct()` auto-advances; no Check button.

## §2 — The standard + the load-bearing cognition
**3.NBT.A.1 (Grade 3, SOLE) — use place value understanding to round whole numbers to the nearest 10 or 100.**
**The honest finding (credit the critic).** FORWARD rounding, given competent place value, is a LEGITIMATE digit/place-value PROCEDURE — the standard literally says "use place value understanding to round." So a **SMART rote rounder** (reads the digit immediately right of the TOLD place; ones for nearest-10, tens for nearest-100) produces every forward answer — the bracket, the distances, the nearer home — with ZERO nearness/meaning. The cognition that distinguishes understanding from rote is therefore NOT the forward task (both produce the same numeral); it is:
- **(a) MIDPOINT / BOUNDARY / INVERSE reasoning** — the band a harbor claims (the edges 45 & 54 → 50), the halfway-buoy (the midpoint between 200 and 300 is 250), "is it past halfway." **A forward rote procedure cannot run backward to produce the band edges.** ← the digit-PROOF spine (defeats even the SMART solver).
- **(b) PLACE-GOVERNANCE** — the same number rounds differently to 10 vs 100 (247 → 250 / 200); which place governs. **The NAIVE ones-digit reader fails nearest-100** (reads the 7 → 300).
- **(c) The MEANING** — rounding = nearest, NOT truncate-the-digits and NOT always-bigger. **The TRUNCATION procedure fails 168→200; the ALWAYS-UP procedure fails 42→40 and 40→40.**

LOAD-BEARING grade = (a)+(b)+(c); the forward "dock the boat" is SCAFFOLD/MODEL. **Four distinct adversaries, each defeated by different proof-content; the SMART rote rounder — the hardest — is defeated ONLY by (a).** Numbers: nearest-10 within ~1000 (2-3 digit), nearest-100 within ~1000; band/boundary items at 50/100/200/400/500; place-governance contrasts (247, 350, 1450); midpoint-LOCATION graded ("right in the middle" celebrated); the round-half-up **convention is TOLD, never graded** (grading it penalizes the better reasoner — pedagogy ruling). Traps: already-a-multiple (40→40), round-bigger (42), truncation (168→100-is-wrong), cross-boundary (98→100). All numbers = real DOM/SVG `<text>`. **FENCES:** the OWNED nothing (gate-check: zero 3.NBT live; number-line tool is a plain manipulative; place-value family = decomposition only); NOT comparison (</=/> is order, this is nearness-to-a-multiple); NOT E12 place-value (value-of-a-digit, not nearness); the half-up convention out-of-grade-to-grade.

## §3 — THE ARCHITECTURE REWORK (the heart; the deepest flaw + fix — RE-CENTER onto the inverse/boundary the rote rounder can't produce)
**The critic's catch — a Game-75-class SUFFICIENT-STATISTIC IRREDUCIBILITY.** For a fixed, TOLD place the governing digit `d` is a SUFFICIENT STATISTIC for the entire forward nearness structure (nearest-10: distLo=d, distHi=10−d, nearer=sign(d−5); nearest-100: the tens-and-ones remainder vs 50). Therefore EVERY graded artifact of the bracket-first design is digit-derivable:
- **the BRACKET is a TRUNCATION MACHINE, not a nearness machine** — lower = truncate-to-place, upper = +place; neither references the number's position between them (a child who thinks rounding IS truncation produces the bracket correctly). Grading bracket-production as a "nearness artifact" is incoherent — it rewards the truncation misconception as a first-class skill.
- **the produced DISTANCE (countable knots) = the digit instantiated** — the knot counts ARE `d` and `10−d`; "produce the distance" hard-codes the digit into the manipulative, adding NO proof layer.
- **the told-place binary NEARER-tap = the literal digit rule in a rope costume** (50% guess for a non-knower; 100%-for-the-wrong-reason for the digit child).
You cannot decorrelate nearness from the digit for a fixed place — the digit IS the nearness. The bracket/rope apparatus is a beautiful MODEL but is digit-equivalent ASSESSMENT.

**THE GATE-DESIGN catch (new doctrine) — arm the SMART solver, not the naive one; forbid the false pass.** Two candidate adversaries differ wildly: **NAIVE** ("always read the ONES digit" — the classroom miseducation; fails nearest-100) vs **SMART** ("read the digit right of the TOLD place" — the competent-but-empty rote rounder; passes every told-place forward round). A gate armed only with NAIVE happily certifies the game (it has nearest-100 rounds NAIVE flunks) — **a FALSE PASS that proves nothing about nearness.** The gate MUST be armed with SMART, and **the pass-condition is "SMART fails the GAME"; a NAIVE-only-fail is explicitly forbidden.**

**THE FIX — RE-CENTER the GRADE onto the content the SMART rote rounder CANNOT produce (the forward dock becomes the MODEL):**
- **GRADED (load-bearing):**
  1. **MIDPOINT / BAND BOUNDARIES — the spine (defeats SMART).** Produce a harbor's **welcome tide-line edges**: the lowest AND highest boat it claims (the 50-harbor → 45 and 54; the 200-lighthouse → 150 and 249). Producing the band edges is a midpoint-from-outward computation (45 = lower midpoint that ties-up; 54 = last before the upper midpoint 55) — a forward rounder is a one-way function and CANNOT produce it. Plus **halfway-buoy** placement (produce the midpoint between two neighbor harbors) + "past halfway?" judgments where graded as the boundary, not the forward answer.
  2. **PLACE-GOVERNANCE (defeats NAIVE).** Same-number contrasts (247: lantern-night → 240/250, lighthouse-night → 200/300) where the child must **read which harbors are lit / decide the place** and the **wrong-place distractors are present** (250 = the nearest-10 answer, 300 = the ones-digit misapplication for 247-nearest-100). On undeclared/which-harbor rounds the SMART solver's "digit right of the told place" has no referent.
  3. **MEANING-TRAPS (defeat TRUNCATION + ALWAYS-UP).** The false-flag boats: 168 (a truncator sails it to the 100-lighthouse → wrong, it belongs to 200), 42 (an always-up child sails it to 50 → wrong, 40), 40→40 (already home — an "it must move" child mis-sends it).
- **SCAFFOLD / MODEL (ungraded — keep the warm UX):** the forward dock-the-boat-at-the-nearer-harbor, the knotted-rope distances (a confirmation animation, NOT an assessment), the told-place binary nearer-tap, the post-commit halfway-buoy/line reveal.
- **child-output STRICTLY before game-output** (`ANSWER_NOT_PRE_REVEALED`): the halfway-buoy + tide-line draw in only AFTER the child commits; nothing pre-placed; and the band/place scaffolds must **NOT flash correct-on-approach** (no proximity glow = no hot/cold ECHO that hands the boundary).
