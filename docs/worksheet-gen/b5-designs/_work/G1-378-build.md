The counts are a permutation of the configured counts, with no run of 3, no period of 2 or 3, and row 1 is never the Sun. ## Deviations (each measured)
1. **Chart width = the body (675), not the 639 lane.** The fact column grows to 1fr (~339 px), which gives de/fi facts more room; heights are unchanged. At 677 the ~62-char fixture facts run to exactly 2 lines.
2. **d1 head box is 88, not 96.** A 96 px Sun in the 92 px column put its rays on the column separator in the first d1 render. The spec now guards `headBox <= col - 4`.
3. **Row cap `tick + 36`.** This is not in the design; it is the sparse ruling.
4. **Sun rays stop at r 48.15**, so the round cap ends on r 50 instead of being clipped by the box.
5. **Rims are non-scaling 3 px on paper.** The design gives this for Earth only; I applied it to the Moon as well, with craters at 1.5 px. Otherwise the 26 px head Moon would print its rim at 0.8 px.
6. **Mercury is a pale creamDeep disc** (the design says `grid`) with the design's filled inkSoft r 4 craters. On `grid`, the design's own greyscale gate FAILED the Mercury–Earth pair: L 185–191 vs 178–184, under 8 levels, and I tried two crater variants that could not separate them. On creamDeep Mercury is L 223. Every planet it now sits near carries a ring or bands.
10. **The Earth is redrawn (coordinator review 2026-09-23).** The design's two land blobs read as footprints or a face. It is now a globe showing the Americas plus Greenland (simplified orthographic view near 75° W 15° N, loosely traced from real outlines) in teal on a tealSoft ocean, with a thin white cloud swirl. `planets.js` reuses the same land (`EARTH_LAND`). The gate checks land/ocean ≥ 8 grey levels, land 15–50 %, ≥ 2 land masses and no land on the Moon, and a poison is killed for each.
7. **Strings are keyed by MODE**, following the K-368 / G1-376 precedent, because the face ids are not allocated yet. The fact set is exactly 13 ids and forbiddenStems includes the season/weather words; the §5 loc shape has no separate list for them.
8. **Rule 10 (per-face apparatus words) and the answer-key ban run in en only**, because there is no per-locale lexicon (the same open item as K-368). The rule 5 bank-width check is a character-count estimate; the F2 render gate is the final word. `genderNeutral: true` is a block-level signature, since facts are plain strings.
9. **d3 counts are 3/4/3**, the only split the non-size pool allows.
