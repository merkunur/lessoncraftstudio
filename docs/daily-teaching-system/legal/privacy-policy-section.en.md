## Classes and results

Teachers with a Teacher plan can make a class on LessonCraftStudio, give its children worksheets to do on a screen, and see how each child did. This section explains what that feature stores. If you use the site without a class link, none of this applies: we store nothing about a child who simply opens a worksheet.

### Who is responsible

The school, or the teacher where no school is involved, decides whether to use this feature and for which children. They are the "controller" of the class data. We run the feature for them and follow their instructions; in data-protection terms we are their "processor". Schools can accept our data processing agreement at [link].

### What we store about a child

- **A display name**, if the teacher types one: a first name or a nickname. A teacher can also run a class with numbers only, and then we store no name at all.
- **A seat number and a picture of an animal**, so that a child can find themselves on the class page.
- **Results**: for each worksheet a child does through the class link, how many answers were right when the child first pressed Check, how many answers there were, which ones were right, how many times Check was pressed, whether the page was finished, and the date and time. For a set with levels we also store which level the page was.

That is all. We do not ask for or store a surname, a date of birth, an email address, a photo, an address or a location. We do not store what a child typed or drew, only whether an answer was right. We do not measure how long a child takes.

### How it works for a child

A child opens the class link, taps their own name or picture and does the worksheet. No account, no password, nothing to install. The child sees no score list, no ranking and no timer, and cannot see how another child did.

The class page shows the seats of the whole class, including the display names if the teacher uses names, so that each child can find their own. Anyone who has the class link can see that list. The link contains a long random key, and the teacher can replace it at any time, which switches the old link off.

### Who can see results

Only the teacher who made the class, when signed in. We do not show results to other teachers, to other customers or to the public, and there is no page for parents. A parent who wants to see what is stored about their child asks the teacher or the school.

### What we use the data for

Only to show teachers the results of their own classes. We do not use class data for advertising, we do not build profiles of children, we do not sell or share it, and we do not use it to train any system.

### Cookies and tracking

When a child's worksheet sends a result, the request carries no cookie and we do not identify the device. The class page and the result request do not load any third-party tracking. Like every web server, ours keeps a technical log of requests (IP address, time and page address) for security; that log is not connected to seats or results and is deleted after [N] days.

### How long we keep it

- The teacher can delete one child's results, one child, or the whole class at any time. Deletion is immediate in our database.
- A class that has not been used for 14 months is deleted automatically.
- If the teacher's plan ends, their classes are deleted 60 days later. The teacher can still read and delete them during those 60 days.
- Deleted data disappears from our backups within 35 days.

### Where it is

On a server we rent from Hetzner Online GmbH in the European Union. We use Cloudflare, Inc. to deliver the site; class data passes through it and is not kept by it. [Sub-processor list at link.]

### Your rights

Parents and children have the rights their law gives them, for example to see, correct or delete data. Because the school or teacher is responsible for the class, please ask them first; we help them answer. You can also write to us at [privacy contact] and we will pass the request to the teacher and make sure it is answered.

### United States

We provide this feature to schools and teachers for educational use only. A school may consent on behalf of parents to the collection described here. We collect nothing from a child beyond what is listed above and use it for no commercial purpose.
