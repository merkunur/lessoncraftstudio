**Two rejections, 2026-08-09, same day.** Batch 1 (*"they don't solve any teaching problem"*): number
pyramids, sliced arrays, Galton boards — **maths objects**, produced by optimising for §23 ruling 5
("must be verifiable"), which selects for tidy theorems, and tidy theorems are mathematics, not
teaching. Batch 2 (*"you keep getting stupid tool ideas"*): "The Fade", "The Cold Start", "Somebody
Else's Work" — **pedagogy abstractions**; correct instructional moves, but a teacher scanning a shelf
cannot tell what "The Fade" teaches. Both misses were META — about mathematics, then about teaching.

**The anatomy that finally landed came from the operator's own two good examples, not a theory.**
`learning-clock` and `money-mat` are each:
1. **A CURRICULUM TOPIC the teacher must teach and dreads** (telling time · money). The two tools the
   operator called "complete shit" (`home-language-bridge`, `feelings-check-in`) are both
   **non-curriculum** — that alone separates good from bad on this shelf.
2. **A concrete apparatus whose NAME says the topic** — legible before clicking.
3. **A superpower the physical version cannot have** — geared hands + "halb drei"; your country's
   coins + no-wrong-tap. Localisation-as-apparatus is the house moat, so hunt for per-country
   *notation/convention* differences (the German Übertrag is written BELOW the answer line, NL/US
   above; the barred 7; tally-mark styles differ by country).
4. **Fun in the MECHANISM, never decoration** (gears, not stickers — cute materials measurably hurt).

**How to apply (any future idea round):** go TOPIC-FIRST. Map the shelf by curriculum topic → list
the uncovered dreaded topics (forum dread ranking 2026: regrouping #1, word problems #2, time #3 ✓,
money #4 ✓, place value #5, tables #6) → per topic find the best incumbent, its gap, and **the
beloved analog teacher trick to steal as the hook** (rounding roller-coaster, partner-up line,
curtain-and-counters) → punch up until there is a gasp. Kill anything a whiteboard can do.

⚠ **Agents cannot see the shelf — adjudicate their proposals against the live tools yourself.** In
one round, five panel/web "finds" were already shipped tools (thermometer=`cold-line`, vote
towers=`class-graph`, equal-sign balance=`number-balance`, Bauplan=`build-plan`, sharing
table=`lids`). The genuine finds were the ones invisible to a US-first lens (Kombinatorik: DE Klasse
2 mandated, zero tools anywhere).

⚠ Still true underneath: check evidence at the target age (most instructional research is teenagers;
several moves reverse at 5-9), and ruling 5 relaxes to "a tool must be honest about what it knows" —
the operator relaxed it by keeping THE MISSING QUESTION. See [[project_premium_tools_v5_ideas]].
