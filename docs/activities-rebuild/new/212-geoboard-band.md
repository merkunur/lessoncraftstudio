## The skill
Build named 2D shapes (triangle, square, rectangle, hexagon) by joining corners with straight sides.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE BAND THAT SNAPS FROM PEG TO PEG. A pegboard (4×4 or 5×5 pegs) and one rubber band. The child taps a first peg, then each next peg: the band stretches to it as a straight side; tapping the first peg again closes the shape. Corners light only where the band really turns (three pegs in a line make one straight side, not a corner), and each straight side glows. The board then shows what was made: a corner dot and a side tick for every part, and the band stays put — if the task asked for a triangle and the child made four corners, the four lit corners are plain. A band that would cross itself snags (a small knot mark) and will not close. Right: the band twangs, the inside fills with colour and the shape slides onto a little shelf above the board, so the round's earlier shapes stay visible. Miss 1: the bubble counts what was made ('4 corners — a triangle has 3'); miss 2: the asked shape's corner count appears as dots beside the task line. 'A different way' rounds refuse a shape congruent to one already on the shelf (any turn or flip), so a triangle that does not point up, or a turned square, is needed. A square is accepted for 'rectangle' (it is one); for 'square', unequal sides show their peg-counts.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Thinking a shape is defined by its look (a "triangle" must point up) rather than by its sides and corners; leaving shapes open.
- **Pictures:** none (pegboard and band drawn as apparatus)
- **Settings:** Shapes: triangle–square–rectangle / plus pentagon–hexagon · Board: 4×4 / 5×5
- **Rounds:** 9 rounds; the asked shape never repeats twice in a row; any correct construction is accepted (many right constructions, one right property), ≥ 2 'a different way' rounds per pass; free play beside the task names the corners and sides of anything made.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** K-372
- **Distinct from:** Count the Sides / Pip's Museum name existing shapes; Mim's Glow Workshop composes from pieces; nothing builds a shape from sides.
- **Build risk:** Pentagon and hexagon only on 5×5. 'A different way' needs a lattice-polygon congruence test (rotations/reflections of the vertex list). Peg tap targets ≥ 44 px at 360 → the 5×5 board is ≥ 260 px.
