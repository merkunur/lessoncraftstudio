## 2 The base page

**Concept.** "Build the family walls." Two walls stand side by side, each on a heavy teal-outlined foundation STONE carrying a picture and the root word (sun · help / Sonne · fahren / laulaa), five empty dashed courses stacked above each stone; across the top, a strip of ten loose word bricks. The child reads each brick, finds the stone it grows from, and writes it into a course of that wall. German teaching calls word parts *Wortbausteine* and frames the *Wortstamm* in a box: the brick IS that box. One focal apparatus, ten answers, generous 60 px courses with school lines, and ONE silhouette grammar that all six faces reuse, so a child who has done one page reads the other five without being taught the chrome. Role is carried by SHAPE, never by colour, so the page reads the same from a mono copier.

**Chrome budget.** Body **722** (3-line title + 3-line instruction); every stack below is designed to **677** (4-line fi title) and slack goes to the body's flex gaps, never into the apparatus.
