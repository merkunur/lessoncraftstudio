# Reviewers' notes: registry `de-sn`

Written by the reviewers of the crosswalk (`scripts/curricula/apply-review.js`). 306 teaching points carry a reviewer's row; the reason for each is in `frontend/content/curricula/de-sn/crosswalk-authored.json`.

## author-math

- Number range: Saxony keeps Klasse 1/2 in the 'Zahlenraum bis 100'. Every grade-2 sheet with three-digit numbers (2:read-build-3-digit, 2:expanded-form-digit-value, 2:chart-within-1000, 2:compare-order-3-digit, 2:hundred-more-less, the by-100s part of 2:skip-count-5-10-100, 2:add-with-blocks, 2:subtract-with-blocks) meets Klassenstufe 3 lines (MA.3.2.x) and should be offered to Saxon teachers as Klasse 3.
- Written column method and rounding are Klassenstufe 3 in Saxony (MA.3.2.5, MA.3.2.6, 'Runden' in MA.3.2.2.1): 2:column-method and 2:round-to-ten are a year early. Four-digit numbers (3:compare-4-digit) are Klassenstufe 4.
- Multiplication and division start earlier than on the site: Klasse 2 already asks all tasks to 100 by strategies and mastery of the 2, 5 and 10 tables (MA.1-2.2.6, MA.1-2.2.7). The grade-3 points on equal groups, number line, commutativity, sharing and grouping therefore map to MA.1-2 lines; Klassenstufe 3 itself asks automation (MA.3.2.8), 'Division mit Rest' and the link between the operations.
- Fractions: the Saxon Grundschule plan has only 'Bilden von Bruchteilen' (MA.3.2.7.1) and the Bruchteile 1/4, 1/2, 3/4 with Größen. Fractions on a number line, comparing and equivalent fractions and whole-from-part are marked 'extends'; Saxon teachers of Klasse 3 will not look for them.
- Größen sit in other years: Masse (g, kg) is Klassenstufe 3, so 2:mass-grams is early; Volumen (l, ml) is Klassenstufe 4, so 2:read-jugs is two years early; K:heavy-light points to a Klassenstufe 3 line. Temperature is not in the mathematics plan at all (3:thermometer has no row). Inches and feet in 2:choose-units must be dropped for Germany.
- Time: Klasse 1/2 goes to '5-Minuten-Genauigkeit' and to calculations with full hours or inside one hour; minute accuracy is Klassenstufe 3. The plan never names 24-hour time, so 3:time-12-24 is placed by judgement. Seconds ('Sekunde', MA.3.3.3.1) and converting time units have no teaching point.
- Geometry placed earlier in Saxony: 'rechter Winkel', 'Symmetrieachsen' and symmetric completion are Klasse 1/2 goals, so 3:right-angles and 3:symmetry map to MA.1-2 lines. Area and perimeter are only 'Einblick' in Klassenstufe 3 (counting, comparing); unit squares, calculating perimeter and volume by unit cubes are Klassenstufe 4. The hierarchy of quadrilaterals (3:classify-quadrilaterals) is Klassenstufe 4, and the Raute is not named.
- Saxon content of Klassenstufen 1 to 3 with no teaching point (geometry): drawing Linie, Gerade, Strahl, Strecke with a ruler; 'zueinander parallel / senkrecht'; Kreis with Mittelpunkt, Radius and Durchmesser; Würfelgebäude and Baupläne (MA.1-2.1.1.2, MA.3.1.1.3 beyond counting cubes); describing ways (MA.1-2.1.2); Parallelogramm; Würfel- and Quadernetze (MA.3.1.6); Bandornamente with Schiebe- and Drehsymmetrie (MA.3.1.7).
- Saxon content with no teaching point (number and Größen): Teilbarkeitsregeln 2, 3, 5, 9, 10, 100 and Quersumme; multiplying with multiples of 10; written multiplication (three-digit by one-digit, MA.3.2.10); the Vorrangregel Punkt vor Strich (MA.3.2.9.2); Quadratzahlen; converting units (mm, cm, dm, m, km; Euro and Cent) and Kommaschreibweise for money and lengths; Kilometer; estimating lengths and time spans; inventing word problems for a given calculation (MA.1-2.2.8.3).
- Daten und Zufall: no teaching point covers Kombinatorik (MA.1-2.4.3 'Finden und Darstellen von Kombinationen', MA.3.4.3 all combinations, Tachometerprinzip) or Zufallsexperimente with 'sicher, möglich, unmöglich, wahrscheinlich' (MA.1-2.4.4, MA.3.4.4), and none reads a Fahrplan (MA.3.4.2.2). Saxon teachers will look for all three. Line plots and pictographs are not named in the plan; they are placed under the general Schaubild and data goals.
- Points whose skill line was too vague or loose to place firmly: 3:magic-squares, 3:input-output and 3:balance-equations (no number range or operations given), 2:grid-coordinates (the plan names no Planquadrate), K:scene-search (only its shape sheets fit), K:estimate (the plan names estimating only for lengths and time spans), K:colour-by-number.
- Year-K points: two of them are Klasse 1 content in Saxony rather than pre-school readiness (K:fact-fluency-colour-code is marked 'teaches' for the Grundaufgaben; K:number-bonds-20 and K:teen-numbers are Klasse 1 work in the range to 20). All other year-K points are marked 'prepares'.
- Thinking skills: 10 of the 19 points have no row (visual discrimination, spot-the-difference, mazes, shadows, analogies, codes, picture matrices). Sudoku and logic grids are tied to the games lines (MA.1-2.2.8.4, MA.3.2.12.5 'Denk-, Scherz- und Legespiele'), which ask describing strategies; a 'tell how you found it' task on those sheets would make the fit strong. A maze sheet with a 'describe the way' task would reach MA.1-2.1.2.2.

## author-rest

- Saxony places several language skills one stage later than the worksheets' year: pronouns (1:pronouns, 2:pronouns) and possessives are Klassenstufe 3 ('Pronomen', 'weitere Artikelwörter'); past tense (2:verb-forms) is Klassenstufe 3 ('Präsens und Präteritum'); ABC order by the second letter is Klassenstufe 3; categories ('Bilden von Oberbegriffen') are Klassenstufe 3.
- Saxony places several science and geography skills in Klassenstufe 3 or 4: the senses (K and 1 sheets), sink and float (K, 1, 2 sheets), plan and map reading, the four compass directions, traffic signs, reading a weather forecast are all Klassenstufe 3; animal adaptation and animal reproduction are Klassenstufe 4. These rows are 'prepares'; German teachers will look for these sheets under Klasse 3.
- No Saxon home at all (no rows): Sun / Earth / Moon, moon phases and planets; continents and oceans (the plan stops at the Freistaat Sachsen); living / non-living; natural / made; materials and recycling (three points); family members; wants and needs; drawing (Kunst). 2:day-and-night has only a loose 'extends' to the light-and-shadow line.
- Body parts (K:human-body, 1:body-parts) are tied only to the Lernbereich 'Mein Körper und meine Gesundheit' (id SU.1-2.2, an area, not a goal), because no goal asks for naming body parts. If the registry must not hold area-level rows, treat these two as having none.
- Family: the Sachunterricht names family only in Klassenstufe 4 ('Veränderungen im Familienleben', then and now). The two family sheets with clues and riddles are placed on the reading-comprehension lines of Deutsch; feelings (K:feelings) and family life are subjects of Ethik / Religion in Saxony, which this registry does not hold.
- Opposites have no dash line: Klasse 1/2 names only 'Wortfamilien' and 'Wortfelder' under vocabulary extension, so both opposites points sit on the goal DE.1-2.5.6. Word fields are strong in Saxony (Klasse 1/2 and 3), so synonym, shades-of-meaning and word-field sheets are well placed.
- English-shaped skill lines that need German content to be true for Saxony: digraphs (German sch, ch, and the Zwielaute ei/au/eu; no th); 2:spelling-rules (German ck/k, tz/z, doubled consonants, later silbentrennendes h; not tch/dge/ai-ay/-ies); sight words (German Funktionswörter); CVC words (short sound-true words); 'said' (Wortfeld sagen); a/an (der/die/das, ein/eine).
- Cursive: Saxony demands 'Beherrschen der Schulausgangsschrift' by the end of Klasse 2 and moves to an individual handwriting in Klasse 3, so the Klasse 3 cursive-sentence sheet sits on the Klasse 1/2 line. The sheets must use the Saxon Schulausgangsschrift letter forms to count.
- Deutsch content of Klassenstufen 1 to 3 that no teaching point here covers and teachers will look for: capital letters for nouns ('Großschreibung als wortbezogene Markierung'), the spelling strategies named in the term list (Verlängern, Ableiten, Erweitern, Zerlegen), Umlaut and Zwielaut, using a Rechtschreibwörterbuch, the four sentence kinds incl. Aufforderungs- and Ausrufesatz, and in Klasse 3 the comparison of adjectives (Grundstufe, Mehrstufe, Meiststufe), Subjekt and Prädikat, wörtliche Rede with Begleitsatz, word division at the line end, Sprichwörter and Redensarten, and text kinds (Gedichte, Märchen, Fabeln).
- Sachunterricht content of Klassenstufen 1 to 3 that no teaching point here covers: five deciduous and three coniferous trees with the tree terms (Stamm, Ast, Zweig, Krone); four Frühblüher; four birds and birds through the year; care of pets, farm animals and their products; light and shadow; properties of air; water as a solvent; thermometer with Gefrier- und Schmelzpunkt; a healthy breakfast; first aid and avoiding accidents; body knowledge for protection (Geschlechtsmerkmale); behaviour at bus stops; and in Klasse 3 grain and potato, the meadow habitat, states of water (gefrieren, schmelzen, verdampfen), the Landkreis, the safe bicycle, protecting the sense organs.
- 2:synonyms~doing-stage has the same skill line as 2:synonyms and received the same row; the skill line does not say what the moved part contains.
- Animal life cycles (three points) have no direct Saxon line before Klassenstufe 4; only the plant cycle in 1:life-cycles is a true 'teaches' ('Entwicklungsstadien von Blütenpflanzen'). Animal classes by body covering are likewise beyond the primary plan.
