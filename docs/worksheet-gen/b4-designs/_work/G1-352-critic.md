## 5 Quality verdict

I would print the base page for a first grade tomorrow: eight faces, eight name tags, eight rows of three white pills, and a child who can read "Mia" can do the whole page without a teacher; the de version with three captioned things among the people is exactly the Klasse 2 lesson. The three rulings I fought hardest for are the ones a teacher would have caught at the photocopier: a mixed-sex pair captioned with two boys' names (the mock shows it; the name-to-portrait tag equality is now a poisoned node check, not a composer hope), a Finnish "who is he?" page that runs out of names on card five (16 names), and the pretty "possessive pronouns" grid that could never hold a 43-character Italian frame in a 302 px card (lanes). What would embarrass me: a portrait chosen because it was pretty rather than legible (the firefighter's visor, the paramedic's helmet, the veterinarian behind her cat: all out, by opening them, not by their filenames), a French page where "elles" appears once under a floor that promised twice (fixed by the pair mix, in two faces), a Swedish possessive page where a child's correct "sin" is marked wrong (never a chip), and a fi title that wraps to four lines and pushes card eight into the footer (a validator cap with a render poison). The residual risk no gate sees is the one both inputs name: an anaphora sentence whose content gives the sex away ("{P} trägt ein Kleid."). `contentNeutral` is a signature; the critic reads every anaphora frame in every locale before the wave; that is the whole defence, and it is a human one.
