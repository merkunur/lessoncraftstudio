## The skill
Picture analogies: find the relation between a pair and apply it to a new picture.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE SAME-WAY MACHINE. A machine with two input chutes and one output tray; a gear window in the middle. The first pair goes in by itself (chick → hen): the gear turns and shows the relation as a moving glyph (grows-up: a small dot growing; part-of: a piece fitting into a whole; lives-in: a dot entering a shape; used-with: two shapes clicking together). The third picture drops into the first chute (calf); three library pictures wait for the output (cow, barn-like haystack, milk). The child drags one into the output tray. Right: the gear turns the same way and both pairs light together. Miss 1: the gear grinds, the output pops out, and the first pair re-runs slowly beside it. Miss 2: the first pair's glyph is placed between the third picture and each candidate in turn — the one that fits clicks.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Picks by theme association (cow goes with farm, so barn) instead of by the same relation.
- **Pictures:** farm-animals (chick/hen, calf/cow, lamb/sheep, foal/horse, duckling/duck), body-parts (hand/finger, face/nose), around-the-house (key/lock, door, toothbrush/toothpaste), spring (bird/nest), ocean-life (fish) + beach (water), kitchen-tools (cup/teapot), clothing (glove/hand).
- **Settings:** Relations: young-adult and whole-part / plus where-it-lives and what-it-is-used-for.
- **Rounds:** 9 rounds with distinct triples, ≥ 2 relations per pass; the three outputs always include one same-theme distractor (calf → haystack); answer position balanced among 3; one right answer checked by the reviewer.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G1-157
- **Distinct from:** No activity in the catalogue teaches analogical relations; Ziggy's Odd One Out is category exclusion.
- **Build risk:** Glyph legibility without words must be tested with the reviewer; triples where two outputs fit are refused.
