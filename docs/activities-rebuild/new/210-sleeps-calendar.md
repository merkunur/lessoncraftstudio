## The skill
Know the days of the week in order, what comes before and after today, and that the week repeats.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE NIGHT BETWEEN EVERY DAY. Seven day cards (twelve month cards in the year setting) lie in a closed loop with a small moon — one sleep — on the rail between each pair of neighbours, so the last day of the week is joined to the first by one moon exactly like every other pair. Some cards are blank; the matching cards wait in a tray in shuffled order. The child drags a card onto a blank. Right: it clicks in and the moons on both sides of it light. Wrong: it bounces back and the moons from the nearest filled card to the blank light and pulse, so the child counts the sleeps from a known day; miss 2: the right card in the tray lifts a little. Yesterday–today–tomorrow mode: the full loop is shown with a library sun (weather/sunny) resting on today; the task line asks for tomorrow or yesterday (with a forward/back arrow icon) and the child drags the sun along the rail — it steps moon by moon, and stopping two moons away leaves both moons lit. Year setting: the same loop holds the twelve months; 'the month before/after' moves the sun one step. Right: the loop gives one slow turn so the wrap-around is felt.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Treating the week as a line that ends; mixing the order of the middle days.
- **Pictures:** weather/sunny as the today marker; cards, rail and moons drawn as apparatus; day and month names are the content
- **Settings:** Ring: the week / the year (months) · Task: fill the blanks (2 or 4) / yesterday–today–tomorrow (one step back or forward)
- **Rounds:** 9 per ring × task. Week start per locale (Monday; en follows en-US Sunday — the worksheet locale rule). Blanks spread so the wrap-around (last→first) is crossed in ≥ 3 rounds; today stands on every weekday across a pass and on the first or last day in ≥ 2 rounds so tomorrow/yesterday crosses the wrap; one right card per blank; tray order shuffled.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G1-319, G1-320, G1-321, G1-322, K-321, K-337
- **Distinct from:** No calendar activity exists.
- **Build risk:** Day/month spelling and capitalisation per locale (lowercase in fr/es/it/pt/nl/sv/da/no/fi); full names on cards, never abbreviations — card width set from the longest locale name (fi 'keskiviikko', de 'Donnerstag'). Seasons are NOT in this instrument (→ #238 season-wheel).

## Merged into this instrument (cover each as a setting or round set)
- grade 1 "The Year Ring" (year-ring): Days of the week and months of the year go round in order; yesterday is one step back, tomorrow one step forward; months belong to seasons. — A RING THAT TURNS: a ring of 7 day-beads (or 12 month-beads) with a "today" window. The child turns the ring with a drag; it clicks one bead at a time. Yesterday/tomorrow: the child turns to today and moves one click back or forward. Months in order: loose month beads must be threaded onto the ring after the one before; a bead in the wrong place will not close the ring. Seasons: arcs of the ring are painted with the season colours and the child drops months on the arc (hemisphere set per locale).
