## The skill
Choose the right article for a noun (en: a or an by the first sound; gendered locales: the article that matches the noun's gender).

## The apparatus and its invention
THE CAP THAT FITS ITS NOUN. Library pictures stand bare-headed; the locale's article caps hang on pegs (en a · an; de der · die · das; fr le · la · l'; es el · la; it il · lo · la · l'; pt o · a; nl de · het; sv en · ett; da en · et; no en · ei · et; indefinite setting: de ein · eine, fr un · une, es un · una, it un · uno · una · un', pt um · uma). Drag a cap onto a picture: it settles and the picture's word is written under it with the cap as its first word ("ett äpple"). Right: the phrase glows and the cap clicks. en: the "an" cap carries a little bridge that only seats on a word whose first SOUND is a vowel (an umbrella seats; a uniform does not), so the rule is the cap's shape. Miss 1: the cap tips off and the WRITTEN phrase with the right article appears faded under the picture for two seconds (sound off still teaches). Miss 2: the right cap's peg glows. Free play: try every cap and read each phrase. GARAGE mode (absorbed G1 article garages, G1-292): word-only cards (no picture) are sorted under the article caps as columns.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** en: choosing by the first LETTER instead of the first sound; gendered: matching by meaning (a "female" thing) instead of the word's gender.
- **Pictures:** Any colour theme; nouns + gender from image-vocabulary.js (per-locale gender authority §A.13.58); en lure pairs available: umbrella / uniform (accessories, clothing), apple, orange, owl, airplane. easter/egg refused (a decorated Easter egg).
- **Settings:** Pictures: 4 / 8 per round · Articles: indefinite / definite
- **Rounds:** 9 rounds of 4 (or 8) pictures; each article ≤ 40 % of answers per pass; it/fr ≥ 2 elision nouns (l') per pass; it ≥ 1 lo/uno noun (s+consonant, z); es nouns with stressed initial a- (águila) refused; one right article per noun.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G1-292, K-288, K-306, K-307, K-348
- **Distinct from:** No article activity exists; Daisy's Plate Stack is number (plural), not article choice.
- **Build risk:** fi has no articles → refused in fi (recorded). sv/da/no definite = suffix → indefinite only there. no: bokmål allows en for feminine nouns — feminine nouns refused (or both accepted, reviewer call). nl "een" is gender-blind → nl definite de/het only.

## Merged into this instrument (cover each as a setting or round set)
- grade 1 "The Article Garages" (article-garages): Each noun takes its article: a/an (en), der/die/das (de), en/ett (sv), un/une (fr) ... — GARAGES WITH DOORS: two or three garages carry the article words on their doors (the content). Noun cars (a picture plus its word) drive in when dragged to a door; on parking, the plate prints the whole phrase ("ett äpple") and the car is used in a little sentence above the garage. A car in the wrong garage stalls in the doorway and the door shows the sound / ending clue (en: the word begins with a vowel sound; de/sv: no visible clue - the garage plays the phrase with the right article and the child tries again).
