## The skill
Find the constant step of a growing or shrinking number pattern and continue it.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE STEP SPRING. A row of stepping stones with some numbers shown and some blank; a library grasshopper sits on the first. The child sets the grasshopper's spring with a step dial (+ or −, and the size, including 25, 50, 100) and presses Hop: it hops stone to stone by that step, and at the first stone whose shown number disagrees it misses the stone, tumbles into the grass beside it and hops back to the start — so the wrong step is located, not just rejected. When the spring is right it lands on every shown number; the child then types the blank stones it passed. Miss 2: the difference between two neighbouring shown numbers is drawn as a bracket. Right: the grasshopper makes a last long leap off the end of the row.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Using the difference of the first two numbers when they are not neighbours, adding when the pattern shrinks, and assuming every pattern grows by 1, 2, 5 or 10.
- **Pictures:** forest-creatures/grasshopper as the hopper; stones and the step dial drawn as apparatus
- **Settings:** Direction: growing / shrinking / both · Steps: 2-10 / 25, 50, 100
- **Rounds:** Generated start and step, ≥ 9 rounds, blanks at varied positions (never only the last), at least 2 rounds where shown numbers are not adjacent; typed answers.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G3-326, G3-327, G3-329
- **Distinct from:** Lily Hops skip-counts with the step given; the spring makes the child discover the step and the splash locates the wrong one. Distinct from skip-rod (multiplication from 0).
- **Build risk:** Low.
