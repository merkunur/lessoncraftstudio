## The art rule (above every other rule)
Every picture is produced by `lib/fd-scene.js` (from `data/fd/<id>.json`) or `lib/htd-steps.js` (from `data/htd/<slug>.json`). The spec never composes, draws or edits a drawing; it reads the engine data, picks (seeded) and lays out. Apparatus on the token palette only (rings, boxes, grids, dashed guides, arrows, fold marks, writing rows, word tags, number boxes). If a face needs something the engine cannot give, the face is REFUSED with the measured reason — never approximated with a hand-drawn shape.

## find-the-differences (K-395) specifics
- **Composer** (`lib/fd-compose.js`, new): given a scene record and the face config `{n, kinds, mode, minPx, scale}` pick `n` candidate ops with the rng such that: one op per drawing; every ring bbox ≥ `minSepPx` (24 printed px) from every other ring; rings spread (a difference in ≥ 3 of the 4 quadrants when n ≥ 4; never two in the same quadrant when n ≤ 3); printed ring size ≥ the face's floor (`bbox` × the panel's px/unit); `kinds` honoured (the colour face needs ≥ 1 `colour` op; the missing face uses `remove` only; the mirror face mirrors the WHOLE picture 2 — a panel-level transform, not an op — and still carries `n` ops); a scene whose candidates cannot satisfy the config THROWS (refused for that face). The page order of ops is irrelevant; the ring positions are what a child sees — the pooled position-tell gate (≥ 400 seeds) must show no quadrant above 35 % of all rings.
- **Stamps**: panel 1 `data-lcs-fd-panel="1"`, panel 2 `="2"`; each ring/hotspot `<rect data-lcs-fd-hotspot=i data-lcs-fd-diff="1" data-lcs-label=...>` for a difference and `data-lcs-fd-hotspot` WITHOUT `data-lcs-fd-diff` for every decoy (the unchanged drawings' bboxes); `data-lcs-fd-count=n` on the root. The printable page draws no ring and no visible hotspot; the answer key (`ctx.answerKey`) draws the rings on picture 2; the screen (`ctx.interactive`) draws picture 2 at ≥ 560 px wide over picture 1 (stacked) so every hotspot is ≥ 100 page-px (≥ 44 px at 360).
- **verify(page)** re-derives everything from the stamps AND from the pixels: draw both panel SVGs onto canvases at the printed scale, subtract, dilate 3 px, count components (≥ 10 px² real change) — their number equals `data-lcs-fd-count`, each lies inside exactly one `data-lcs-fd-diff` hotspot, no component outside any hotspot, every hotspot with `data-lcs-fd-diff` contains a component; decoys contain none; ring/hotspot separation ≥ 24 px; the title's digit (when the face has one) equals the count; no two hotspots on the same `data-lcs-fd-layer`.
- **Interactive**: `interactive: { kind:'tap-select', item:'[data-lcs-fd-hotspot]', answerAttr:'data-lcs-fd-diff', labelAttr:'data-lcs-label', metaAttrs:['data-lcs-fd-hotspot','data-lcs-fd-layer'], instructionKey:'fd-tap', oracle }` where the oracle recomputes the answers from the scene data in the manifest meta (never from the page); the answer key = picture 2 with rings; `i18n/interactive-instructions.json` gets the `fd-tap` sentence ×11 from the panels. The F5 (how many) face is `tap-choice` on the number; F6 (what changed) is `tap-select` on the word tags; F10 keeps the find part as `tap-select`.
- **Poisons (≥ 12)**: an undeclared change; a declared change the pixels do not show; two rings touching; a ring on the frame; a difference on a drawing changed twice; the colour face with no colour op; count in title ≠ count on page; a decoy hotspot over a changed drawing; all rings in one quadrant; a scene reused twice in one locale's family; a picture-pair with two differences; a mirror face whose panel 2 is not mirrored.

## how-to-draw (K-396) specifics
- The spec reads `data/htd/<slug>.json` (REFUSED entries skipped) and lays out `stepSvg` / `fullSvg` panels + practice boxes; the practice box's inner aspect = the drawing's `bbox` aspect, ≥ 300 px wide at K; the step strip never below 110 px per panel (a 7-unit stroke ≥ 1.2 px); new lines coral, earlier lines ink, the final step in ink.
- **Stamps**: `data-lcs-htd-step=k` on every step panel, `data-lcs-htd-steps=n` on the strip, `data-lcs-htd-box` (w × h) on every drawing area, `data-lcs-htd-order="3,1,4,2,5"` on the shuffled strip of the order face (the true order re-derived from the step stamps; the shown order is never the identity or the reverse; the number boxes are `blankNumeralBox`).
- **verify(page)**: step count 4–6 and ascending; every step panel ≥ the floor; the union of the step paths' bboxes ⊆ the full drawing's bbox; the practice box ≥ the declared floor and EMPTY (no path inside); the grid face's grid = 4×4 on both sides with equal cells; the trace face's trace is dashed (`stroke-dasharray`) and the freehand box empty; the shapes face's guides are the engine's `shapes` (count ≥ 1, coverage ≥ 0.75); the memory face carries a fold mark at the stated y; the scene face's frame holds exactly the stated props and a ground line and NO hero; the noun on the word face equals the drawing's vocab word for the locale (traceable).
- **Gate `qa/verify-b7-how-to-draw.js`**: for every drawing the face uses: `stats.missing === 0 && extra === 0`, 4 ≤ steps ≤ 6, `steps[0].kind === 'outline'`, first share ≥ 0.25, every share ≥ 0.02; poisons (≥ 10): a step removed (union ≠ full), a step repeated, the order face in true order, a reversed order, a practice box with a path inside, a trace that is solid, a grid 3×4, a hero drawn inside the scene frame, a refused slug rendered, a noun that is not the vocab word, shapes with coverage < 0.75 drawn.
