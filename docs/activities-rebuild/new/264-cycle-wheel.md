## The skill
Living things grow in a cycle: egg/seed, young, adult, which makes new eggs/seeds again.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE WHEEL THAT GROWS EACH STAGE FROM THE LAST. A round wheel of 4 slots joined by arrows; the first stage sits in place. The child drags stage cards into the slot the arrow points to next. Right: the stage grows out of the one before in a short morph and the arrow lights. Miss 1: a stage dropped out of turn slides back along the arrow it skipped; Miss 2: the next slot shows a faint outline. Full wheel: the child turns it and the adult leads back to the first stage — the cycle does not end. WHAT-NEXT mode (G1-390): one stage, three candidates from the same cycle. WHOSE-YOUNG mode (G2-366): a young stage drops on the hub and the wheel turns to its adult. TWIN-WHEELS mode (G3-393, merged): butterfly and frog wheels side by side; fact cards dropped on the left wheel, the right wheel or the shared hub; the wheel turns to the window that proves it.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Thinks the cycle is a line that ends with the adult, or that a caterpillar comes before the egg.
- **Pictures:** Exist: farm-animals (chick, hen), forest-creatures/spring (caterpillar), insects (butterfly), frog, spring (bud, flower), insects (ladybug). MISSING: plain egg (bird/butterfly/frog), chrysalis, frogspawn, tadpole, froglet, seed, seedling, ladybug larva/pupa.
- **Settings:** Cycle: chicken / butterfly / frog / plant / mixed · Task: fill the wheel / what comes next.
- **Rounds:** 8+ rounds across cycles; the slot asked to fill varies; next-stage distractors drawn from the same cycle.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G1-203, G1-377, G1-388, G1-389, G1-390, G2-365, G2-366, G3-393
- **Distinct from:** Story sequencing orders events of a story; this is a closed biological cycle.
- **Build risk:** Blocked on stage art. Stage names ×11 by the native panel when built.

## BLOCKED — needs an operator decision before building
The library has no plain egg (easter/egg is a painted Easter egg), chrysalis, tadpole, froglet, frogspawn, seed or seedling; no cycle can be completed honestly. It now also holds twin-wheels (G3-393), the two-cycle comparison, as a mode.

## Merged into this instrument (cover each as a setting or round set)
- grade 2 "The Life-Cycle Wheel — From Egg to Butterfly" (life-cycle-wheel): Order the stages of a life cycle (egg, caterpillar, chrysalis, butterfly; egg, tadpole, froglet, frog) and match a young stage to the adult it becomes. — A WHEEL with four slots and a hand that turns. The child places the stage cards in the slots; when the wheel is turned, the hand passes each stage and the next one grows out of it in a short morph; a wrong order breaks the growth (the hand stops at the stage that cannot come next). Whose-baby mode: young stages drop onto the hub; the wheel turns to the adult it becomes.
- grade 3 "The Twin Life Wheels" (twin-wheels): Compare the butterfly and frog life cycles: both change form (metamorphosis), with different stages. — THE TWIN WHEELS: two turning wheels side by side, each with four windows. The child turns a wheel to put its stages in order (egg -> caterpillar -> chrysalis -> butterfly; egg -> tadpole -> froglet -> frog); a wrong order jams the wheel. Then fact cards ('lays eggs in water', 'has a stage in a case', 'starts life breathing with gills', 'its young look different from the adult') are dropped onto the left wheel, the right wheel, or the hub between them (both); the wheel the fact belongs to turns to the window that proves it.
