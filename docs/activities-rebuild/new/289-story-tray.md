## The skill
Decide from a word problem which number is the number of groups and which the size of each group, then solve by multiplying or dividing.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE STORY TRAY: the story is the task line (read aloud where TTS works); its two numbers sit as tags in the sentence. Below is a tray with two slots: 'how many groups' (drawn as a row of empty plates) and 'how many in each' (drawn as a scoop showing its count). The child drags each number tag into the slot it describes; the tray then ACTS the story — plates appear and are filled scoop by scoop (multiplying), or a pile is dealt out (sharing) or scooped into groups (grouping). A tag in the wrong slot makes a scene that does not match the story (4 plates when the story said boxes of 4), and the child sees the mismatch before typing the answer.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Always multiplying the two numbers seen; confusing 'how many groups' with 'how many in each'; treating sharing and grouping as different operations.
- **Pictures:** food (cookies, apples, muffins), school (pencils, crayons), toys; plates/boxes drawn tool-grade
- **Settings:** Stories: multiply only / divide only (share and group) / mixed · Numbers: tables to 5 / to 10
- **Rounds:** Generated story templates × numbers per locale, ≥ 9 rounds, unknown type (product, group count, group size) balanced; typed answer.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G3-370, G3-371, G3-372, G3-373, G3-374, G3-375, G3-376
- **Distinct from:** Maple's Bakery models division with bare instructions; Vet's Diagnosis Window models K-1 add/subtract stories. The tray adds the decision step from story to model.
- **Build risk:** Medium: native story templates in 11 locales (reviewer load); reading at grade 3 is fine.
