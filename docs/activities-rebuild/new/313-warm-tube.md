## The skill
Read a thermometer whose scale counts by 2, 5 or 10, including below zero.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE WARM TUBE: a big thermometer stands beside a scene. The child drags a sun (warm) or an ice cube (cold) toward the bulb; the liquid rises or falls. In read mode the liquid stops at a temperature and the child types it; in set mode the child warms or cools until the liquid top reaches a stated temperature. Only some marks are numbered; the unnumbered marks between count by the scale, and a magnifier the child can drag over the top of the liquid enlarges the nearest marks.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Counting each unnumbered mark as 1 when the scale counts by 2, and misreading below zero (-5 read as 5).
- **Pictures:** weather theme (sun, snowflake, cloud), clothing (coat, swimsuit) for a 'what to wear' delight
- **Settings:** Scale: by 2 / by 5 / by 10 · Range: 0 and above / below zero too
- **Rounds:** Generated temperatures off the numbered marks in ≥ 60% of rounds, ≥ 9 rounds; read and set alternate on mixed.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G3-345
- **Distinct from:** Pippa's Pond-Juice Lab reads volume in mL on a scale by 50/100; the warm tube reads small-step scales and negatives. Not the Jar Label (K).
- **Build risk:** Low.
