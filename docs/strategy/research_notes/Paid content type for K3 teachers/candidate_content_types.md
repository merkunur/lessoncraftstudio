# Demand evidence for candidate paid content/product types — K-3 (ages 5-9) teachers, 2024-2026

**Method + limits (read first).** 32 WebSearch queries in EN / DE / FR / NL / SV were completed before the session-wide search budget (200 calls, shared with parallel agents) was exhausted; a planned 4th batch of 14 queries (Worksheet Crafter / Twinkl / Heggerty list prices, r/ScienceOfReading voice on decodables, ES / IT / PT-BR / DK-NO-FI marketplaces, French cover-teacher resources, German multilingual parent letters, assessment-to-practice loop products, Google Trends) could NOT be run. WebFetch was blocked for every domain, so **every fact below is a search-summary fact** (the search tool's own summary of the linked page) unless a price/quote is visible in the link title. Facts marked **[summary-only]** were not verified on the page. Prices are as reported in 2025-2026 search summaries unless dated otherwise. Where a claim has no source it sits in *Gaps*, never in *Cited Findings*.

Two cross-cutting findings that condition every score:

1. **The US teacher-paid market is large but softening.** US teachers spent an average of **$895 out of pocket in 2024-25** (AdoptAClassroom Spring-2025 survey) — [AdoptAClassroom 2025](https://www.adoptaclassroom.org/2025/06/09/2025-teacher-survey-spending-stats-classroom-needs/); DonorsChoose's 2024 survey put it at **$610 for 2023-24** — [Chalkbeat 2024](https://www.chalkbeat.org/2024/07/17/teachers-spending-money-on-back-to-school-supplies-survey/). >90 % of teachers spend own money; spending up 49 % since 2015 [summary-only] — [NEA](https://www.nea.org/nea-today/all-news-articles/why-are-educators-still-buying-their-own-school-supplies). But TPT seller income is described as no longer doubling yearly: "Competition is fiercer, and there are fewer buyers … some districts are cracking down on teachers using their own curriculum … forcing teachers to … follow canned curriculum" — [It's Lit Teaching](https://itslitteaching.com/selling-on-teachers-pay-teachers-has-changed/); in 2026 most TPT sellers earn $0-100/month and the top ~1 % earn more than the bottom 99 % combined [summary-only] — [SEOLumina 2026](https://seolumina.com/blog/how-much-do-tpt-sellers-make-in-2026-real-data-income-breakdown); a 2025 seller podcast is literally titled "Is AI the End of Teachers Pay Teachers?" — [Buzzsprout](https://www.buzzsprout.com/2544118/episodes/18289984-is-ai-the-end-of-teachers-pay-teachers).
2. **Europe is a different buyer.** Sweden: "It is far from uncommon that teachers pay for parts of their teaching materials with their own money"; one teacher estimates ~**100 SEK/month**; a Facebook ask by Tidningen Grundskolan drew ~50 confirmations in a day, while three of four principals said they had never heard of the problem — [Vi Lärare / Grundskolläraren](https://www.vilarare.se/grundskollararen/hogskolan/larare-betalar-ur-egen-ficka). Money "increasingly … goes to purchasing various digital tools" rather than materials — [Gleerups](https://www.gleerups.se/pa-gang/aktuellt/alla-pengar-har-gatt-at-till-elevdatorer-skolorna-saknar-digitala-laromedel-b105754). Germany: eduki ("Lehrermarktplatz") records up to **~1 million searches/month** for its brand name [summary-only] — [Thomas Felzmann on eduki](https://www.thomasfelzmann.at/eduki/); sellers set prices, keep <50 % at level 1 and 60 % after 20 uploads, minus €0.30/transaction — [eduki help](https://help.eduki.com/hc/de/articles/4402353391890-Wie-setzt-sich-mein-Honorar-zusammen-%C3%9Cber-Honorarstufen-Umsatzsteuer-und-Transaktionsgeb%C3%BChr). Zaubereinmaleins (the German K-4 material blog) sells access to "well over 3,000 materials" for **€60 one-off unlimited / €15 for one year / €12 for 6 months** — [Zaubereinmaleins intern](https://intern.zaubereinmaleins.de/127/unterrichtsmaterial-klasse-1-4/info.html) — i.e. German teacher price anchors are ~5-10× below TPT bundle prices. France: Lutin Bazar's entire CP-CE2 resource base is free under Creative Commons — [Thot Cursus on Lutin Bazar](https://cursus.edu/fr/12089/lutin-bazar-des-ressources-gratuites-imprimables-pour-les-enseignants-du-primaire); Bout de Gomme's CP handwriting/graphisme progression is likewise a free blog download — [Bout de Gomme](http://boutdegomme.fr/cahier-et-progression-ecriture-graphisme-cp). Netherlands: Gynzy and Squla are **school-wide or parent-paid** licences, not teacher-paid (see §2, §12). **Teacher-paid content in Europe is therefore a low-ticket (€3-15) or membership (€15-60/yr) market with strong free competitors; US-style $50-200 bundles have no observed European equivalent.**

## 1. Differentiated small-group / intervention kits (same skill at 3-4 levels + placement check + tracking sheet)

### Takeaway
Strong, proven US teacher-paid demand — TPT phonics-intervention bundles sell at $19-$63, with "growing bundles" priced up to $165 — but the category is crowded and its *text* half (levelled passages) is exactly what Diffit/MagicSchool give away free; the defensible part is the picture-based, placement-gated, tracked kit for pre-readers and for number sense, and per-locale versions (nothing comparable found in DE/FR/NL search results).

### Cited Findings
- TPT "Kindergarten Phonics Activities Bundle | Centers, Small Groups, Interventions" is priced **$62.75** for 190 activities from 7 products (2025) — [TPT](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Kindergarten-Phonics-Activities-Bundle-Centers-Small-Groups-Interventions-5525055)
- A "growing phonics intervention mega bundle" is priced **$50 now, to rise to $165 on completion**; the "Ultimate SOR Intervention MEGA BUNDLE" is listed at **$19** limited-time [summary-only] — [TPT search: phonics intervention programs](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/browse?search=phonics+intervention+programs)
- Product positioning language in this category: "standards-aligned resource for struggling students … individually, in small-group, or digitally"; "Science of Reading-aligned … perfect for reading interventionists working with small groups" — [TPT Phonics Intervention Kit](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Phonics-Intervention-Kit-Games-Activities-Digital-Resources--9825868); [CVCe Small Group Support](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/CVCe-Small-Group-Support-Phonics-Intervention-Science-of-Reading-11512479)
- On TES (UK) a "phonics intervention group activities phases 1 and 2" pack advertises "over 20 separate activities and over 45 sessions" — [TES](https://tes.com/teaching-resource/phonics-intervention-group-activities-phases-1-and-2-12216187)
- The German equivalent instrument is *software*, not kits: Worksheet Crafter's "strength lies particularly in Grundschule, Förderschule and Inklusion, where teachers can design layout, task blocks, ruled lines, illustrations and **variants**" — [Lernmarktplatz review](https://lernmarktplatz.de/products/worksheet-crafter); school licences sold in blocks of 5 personalised seats — [Worksheet Crafter FAQ](https://worksheetcrafter.com/de/faq/schullizenz-wie-kann-ich-die-schullizenz-erweitern-lizenzen-hin)
- Free-AI threat, text side: Diffit "instantly generates multiple versions of reading materials, tailored to varying grade levels … automatically creates comprehension questions, vocabulary lists" and "is free for teachers with generous limits"; Brisk "adapts text by reading level directly inside Google Docs" and is "free forever for individual educators"; MagicSchool's free tier includes "text levelers and rewriters" — [TeachTools 2026 comparison](https://teachtools.co/blog/best-free-ai-worksheet-generators-2026); [Kuraplan Diffit alternatives](https://www.kuraplan.com/blog/diffit-alternatives)

### Inferences
- The kit's *differentiation-of-prose* layer is commoditised by free AI; its *pre-reader picture layer*, *placement check → group assignment*, and *tracking sheet* are not (none of the AI tools listed produce a placement-gated sequence with a record sheet).
- Teacher-paid in the US (out-of-pocket bundles); in DE the money goes to Worksheet Crafter licences (school-paid seats) and €3-6 eduki singles; a €60 "everything" membership is the German ceiling.
- Demand score **4/5** (US), **3/5** (EU). Biggest risk: category saturation on TPT + "canned curriculum" district mandates reducing teacher discretion.

### Gaps
- No direct teacher quote of the "I wish someone made…" kind surfaced for this category (the Reddit-targeted query returned academic/government pages only).
- No verified per-country data for FR/ES/IT/PT/Nordics on paid intervention kits (4th-batch queries not run).

## 2. Progress monitoring / mastery record for K-2 (DIBELS-style checks, ESGI-like 1:1 assessments, "who has mastered what" grids)

### Takeaway
The pain is real and documented ("in kindergarten they must pull each student individually and test them on every single letter and number"), and ESGI proves a **$259/year teacher-paid** price point for exactly this — but the strong products are software/data systems, largely school-purchased (DIBELS/mCLASS, Gynzy, Boom), and the printable "tracker grid" tier on TPT is dominated by free/cheap items.

### Cited Findings
- **ESGI annual subscription $259**; $60 off first year ($199) via trial; 60-day free trial; 2,500 pre-loaded tests; volume discounts at 2+ licences; sold as 12-month SaaS "based on a 9-month school year with 3 months free" — [ESGI pricing](https://esgisoftware.com/pricing/); [ESGI purchasing](https://info.esgisoftware.com/support/pricing/quotes/purchasing); [Simply Kinder on ESGI](https://www.simplykinder.com/esgi-kindergarten-assessments/)
- Teachers crowdfund ESGI: a DonorsChoose project "ESGI: Kindergarten Friendly Assessment Platform 2025-2026" — [DonorsChoose](https://www.donorschoose.org/project/esgi-kindergarten-friendly-assessment-p/9112056/?challengeid=20469070) → strong signal the teacher, not the school, is the payer for this tool.
- The need in teachers' words: "In older grades, teachers can give a single test to the class … but in kindergarten they must pull each student individually and test them on every single letter and number, along with numerous other things"; teachers "spend hours poring over data points" — [Mrs. B's Beehive](https://mrsbsbeehive.com/individual-data-tracking-in-kindergarten/); [Little Minds at Work](https://littlemindsatwork.org/teacher-friendly-data-collection-freebie)
- DIBELS 8: printed material kits **$42-$76** per grade; scoring training **$49/person**; platform pricing by district agreement — [DIBELS UOregon materials](https://dibels.uoregon.edu/materials/dibels); Ohio approved-assessment listing for mCLASS/DIBELS 8 (2024-25) — [Ohio DOE](https://education.ohio.gov/getattachment/Topics/List-of-Approved-Assessments/mClassDIBELS8thAIF24-25.pdf.aspx?lang=en-US); TPT sells "DIBELS 8th Edition Progress Monitoring Sheets" as a teacher add-on — [TPT](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Dibels-8th-Edition-Progress-Monitoring-Sheets-7048867)
- Boom Cards: **$6.99/month** flexible Premium; older tiers $25/yr (150 students, data reports) and $40/yr (unlimited self-made decks, live reporting); decks are self-grading with per-student accuracy/error/speed reports — [Boom monthly plan](https://www.boomlearning.com/boom-learning-news-blog/monthly-membership); a World-Language teacher FB thread asks "Are Boom Cards worth the $50/year subscription?" — [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/groups/WorldLanguageTeachers/posts/1874754039672178/)
- NL: Gynzy is priced per school by pupil count, "school-wide, meaning all teachers and students have access"; new prices effective 1 Jan 2027 — [Gynzy pakketten](https://www.gynzy.com/nl-nl/pakketten); Gynzy Plus adds "adaptief leren" — [Gynzy Plus](https://www.gynzy.com/nl-nl/pakketten/plus)
- Free tier of the printable form exists: "Standard Mastery Tracker" free listings — [TPT free](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/browse/free?search=standard+mastery+tracker); a Kindergarten TEKS digital data tracker is sold as a paid item — [TPT](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Kindergarten-Digital-Math-TEKS-Data-Tracker-Progress-Monitor-Student-Checklist-9919468)

### Inferences
- Willingness to pay is proven ($259/yr, self-funded) but only for a *system* that administers, scores and reports; a static grid is a $0-5 item. The winning shape is "assessment items + auto-filled mastery grid + report-card evidence sentences", which none of the free AI tools do (they generate text, not per-child records).
- EU: assessment vocabulary differs (Lernstandserhebung / bilan / kartläggning) and is mostly school-mandated; teacher-paid demand unverified.
- Demand score **4/5** (US, as software-plus-content), **2/5** (EU, unverified). Biggest risk: it is a software build, competing with entrenched ESGI/Boom; and districts already pay for DIBELS/mCLASS.

### Gaps
- No Reddit thread located (query returned resource blogs). No EU data on teacher-paid assessment tools; Gynzy prices not visible in summaries.

## 3. Substitute / cover-teacher packs ("sub tubs", Vertretungsstunden, remplaçant)

### Takeaway
A perennial, emotionally-charged need in the US ("easier to go to school sick than … deal with writing sub plans") with mature TPT best-sellers (1,600+ reviews) and, in Germany, a whole *publishing* category (Beltz, Auer, Kohl, Verlag an der Ruhr, Friedrich, eduki) — demand is undisputed, but the buyer is already well served and free AI can now draft a generic sub plan in seconds; what still sells is the no-prep, print-and-go, age-specific *content* plus the organisational wrapper.

### Cited Findings
- Teacher voice: "Writing sub plans is one of the most dreaded parts of being a teacher … tedious and often useless"; "it's easier to go to school sick than it is to deal with writing sub plans"; "takes roughly the same amount of effort as a grad school dissertation" — [Bored Teachers](https://www.boredteachers.com/post/writing-sub-plans); [Catlin Tucker, July 2025](https://catlintucker.com/2025/07/insta-lesson/)
- TPT: a sub-plans best seller has "over 1600 reviews" and "five literacy and math printables"; a kindergarten pack offers "10 days of absences" with editable forms, a sub-plan template, editable slideshow and sub report; a K-5 emergency bundle exists; 2025 reviews say teachers "got their sub plans done in a short time" [summary-only] — [TPT search](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/browse?search=%22emergency+sub+plans%22); [Angela Watson kindergarten sub plans](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Kindergarten-Sub-Plans-EVERYTHING-you-need-for-10-days-of-absences-4006923); [K-5 bundle](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Emergency-Sub-Plans-for-Sub-Binder-or-Sub-Tub-K-5-BUNDLE-3380785)
- Germany — an established book category: "101 Vertretungsstunden für die Grundschule 1./2. Klasse" (Beltz) — [Beltz](https://www.beltz.de/fachmedien/paedagogik/produkte/details/30454-101-vertretungsstunden-fuer-die-grundschule-1-2-klasse.html); "30 x sinnvolle Vertretungsstunden für 45 Minuten – Klasse 1/2" (Verlag an der Ruhr) — [Verlag an der Ruhr](https://www.verlagruhr.de/30-x-sinnvolle-Vertretungsstunden-fuer-45-Minuten-Klasse-1-2/9783834636836); "33 fix und fertige Vertretungsstunden" (Kohl) — [Kohl](https://www.kohlverlag.de/33-fix-und-fertige-vertretungsstunden-grundschule/10644); Auer and Friedrich category pages — [Auer](https://www.auer-verlag.de/grundschule/faecheruebergreifend/vertretungsstunden.html), [Friedrich](https://www.friedrich-verlag.de/shop/vertretungsstunden-1820006); eduki category "Vertretungsstunden" with materials "from free to several euros" — [eduki](https://eduki.com/de/unterricht/2745/fachuebergreifendes/vertretungsstunden); Lehrer-Online free unit — [Lehrer-Online](https://www.lehrer-online.de/unterricht/grundschule/faecheruebergreifend/unterrichtseinheit/ue/unterrichtsmaterial-fuer-vertretungsstunden-grundschule/)
- Free AI threat: MagicSchool markets "80+ tools" incl. lesson/worksheet generation; ChatGPT "requires heavy editing … no built-in standards alignment" — [aitooldiscovery r/Teachers picks](https://www.aitooldiscovery.com/guides/ai-for-teachers-reddit); Catlin Tucker's 2025 post explicitly pitches AI-assisted "insta-lessons" for sick days — [Catlin Tucker](https://catlintucker.com/2025/07/insta-lesson/)

### Inferences
- Teacher-paid in the US; in Germany both teacher-paid (books €15-25, eduki singles) and school-library-paid. France/Spain: not verified (4th batch not run) — but French primary substitution is handled by state ZIL/brigade teachers, which shifts the need to the *replacement* teacher rather than the class teacher.
- The differentiator for a multilingual platform: a sub pack the *cover teacher can run without knowing the class or the language* (picture-led, self-checking interactive decks + printables) — this is the one variant free AI cannot produce and existing books do not offer.
- Demand score **4/5** (US and DE); **3/5** elsewhere (unverified). Biggest risk: heavily commoditised; AI drafts the plan wrapper for free.

### Gaps
- No French / Spanish / Nordic cover-teacher resource evidence collected. No TPT price visible for the top sub-plan sellers in summaries.

## 4. Home-school connection packs (weekly "how to help at home" sheets, in the family's home language)

### Takeaway
The category exists on TPT only as English/Spanish bilingual handouts and editable newsletter templates; the *translation* problem is being solved for free by school-paid platforms (TalkingPoints: 110+ languages, free to teachers) and by ChatGPT, so the paid opportunity is narrow — but a K-2, picture-based, activity-per-week pack in 11 languages tied to specific skills has no observed competitor and is exactly the multilingual-school niche.

### Cited Findings
- TPT products: "Building Skills at Home Parent Handouts (English & Spanish)" for K-2 "tips … for building literacy, letter recognition, sight word recognition, fine motor, handwriting, writing, spelling, math skills … perfect to attach to a weekly newsletter" — [TPT](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Building-Skills-at-Home-Parent-Handouts-English-Spanish-351013); "Parent Handouts for Preschool Home to School Connection (English and Spanish)" — [TPT](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Parent-Handouts-for-Preschool-Home-to-School-Connection-English-and-Spanish-9329192); bilingual newsletter templates in Google Slides/PowerPoint — [TPT bilingual newsletters](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/browse?search=bilingual+newsletters). No price visible in summaries. Only EN/ES pairs surfaced — no third language.
- TalkingPoints: "two-way communication with all families across over 100 home languages … automatically translated into 110-plus home languages"; "free for teachers in the U.S./Canada"; texting requires "a paid TalkingPoints subscription" bought by the school — [TalkingPoints families](https://talkingpts.org/families/); [G2 pricing](https://www.g2.com/products/talkingpoints/pricing); district-wide adoptions in Fairfax, Cincinnati, Seattle — [FCPS](https://www.fcps.edu/communications/communication-resources/talkingpoints), [Seattle](https://www.seattleschools.org/news/texting-with-talking-points/)
- Europe: no German/French/Dutch multilingual parent-pack product surfaced in the completed searches (the DE "Elternbrief mehrsprachig" query was in the un-run batch).

### Inferences
- School-paid where it is software (TalkingPoints); teacher-paid where it is a printable handout ($ low). Free AI kills the *translation* and *generic tips* layer entirely (ChatGPT translates a newsletter in seconds).
- Survives only as *content*: a weekly 1-page parent activity with pictures, matching the class's worksheet of the week, native-authored per language (not machine-translated) — a differentiation the platform already has (11-locale vocabulary). Likely a *bundled feature* that increases retention rather than a standalone SKU.
- Demand score **2/5** standalone, **3/5** as a bundled add-on. Biggest risk: free translation + low willingness to pay for parent-facing paper.

### Gaps
- No teacher quote requesting home-language help sheets; no evidence of search volume; no EU evidence.

## 5. Morning work / spiral review / "Tagesplan" — year-long sequenced daily pages (K-2)

### Takeaway
One of the most reliable US teacher-paid categories: multiple year-long kindergarten bundles at **$15-$72** (A Teeny Tiny Teacher $57.60 for the mega bundle; Ashley Krause math spiral $50), a $210 first-grade number-talks yearlong, and a Second Story Window K-5 "all-access" mega bundle; sequencing and consistency are the value, which free AI does not deliver (it produces one page, not a coherent 180-day scope). In Germany "Tagesplan" means the daily *schedule card*, not a review page — the German analogue is Zaubereinmaleins' Wochenplan generator (€15/yr membership).

- Europe: "Tagesplan"/"Wochenplan" is a *structure* (self-directed weekly plan) that teachers fill with material — a "Wochenplan-ready" set of graded pages matches German pedagogy better than "morning work".
- Demand score **5/5** (US), **3/5** (DE — as Wochenplan material), others unverified. Biggest risk: curriculum-mandate districts; price compression on TPT.

### Gaps

## 6. Centre / station rotation systems (Daily 5-style — rotations, sorting, recording sheets, "I can" cards)

### Takeaway
A mature US category (Daily 5 rotation kits, editable "I can" station posters) with no price surfaced; the *management* layer (rotation charts, I-can cards) is cheap/free and easily AI-generated, so demand accrues to the *content* filling the stations — which overlaps candidates 1 and 5. US-only framing ("Daily 5", "centers"); European primary practice uses Stationenlernen / ateliers / Werkstatt but no paid-product evidence was gathered.

### Cited Findings
- TPT "Daily 5 Literacy Rotations Kit" (Blue Sky Designs) and "Daily 5 Literacy Center Rotation" (Hub City Classroom) exist; "many Daily 5 rotation resources include editable station posters where you can write out expectations or 'I can' statements" [summary-only] — [TPT Rotations Kit](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Daily-5-Literacy-Rotations-Kit-3323303); [TPT rotation](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Daily-5-Literacy-Center-Rotation-2789537)
- Differentiated Kindergarten (blog) gives rotation set-up + a freebie for organising Daily 5 stations — [Differentiated Kindergarten](https://differentiatedkindergarten.com/setting-up-for-a-successful-year-of-daily-5-in-kindergarten/); [freebie post](https://differentiatedkindergarten.com/organizing-daily-5-literacy-stations-in/)
- Free Daily 5 resources are abundant on TPT — [TPT free Daily 5](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/browse/free?search=daily+5)
- The $62.75 kindergarten phonics bundle is explicitly marketed as "Centers, Small Groups, Interventions" in one — [TPT](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Kindergarten-Phonics-Activities-Bundle-Centers-Small-Groups-Interventions-5525055) — i.e. centre content and intervention content are the same SKU.

### Inferences
- The rotation *system* is not a paid driver on its own (free/editable, trivially AI-drafted); the *station content* is candidate 1 re-packaged. Teacher-paid, US-centric vocabulary.
- Demand score **2/5** standalone; folds into #1/#5. Biggest risk: zero defensibility of the wrapper; "Daily 5" is a trademarked method (The 2 Sisters) — naming risk.

### Gaps
- No prices, no teacher quotes ("I don't have time to prep centers" query was in the un-run batch), no European evidence.

## 7. Decodable readers & phonics-sequence-aligned practice (Science of Reading; UFLI is free)

### Takeaway
Despite free UFLI passages and "hundreds of free decodable readers", a substantial *paid* ecosystem sells UFLI-*aligned* books (Benchmark 128-title K library, Teacher Created Materials 90 titles, Developing Decoders 195 books at ~$4/book) — what still sells is **physical/illustrated, scope-and-sequence-locked** book sets bought by schools, plus teacher-paid TPT passages; this is English-orthography-specific (UFLI, SoR) and does not transfer to transparent-orthography European languages, where decodables are not a product category.

### Cited Findings

## 12. Assessment-to-worksheet loop (quick check → automatically the right next worksheet per child)

### Takeaway
This is the shape of *adaptive software* (IXL Diagnostic "crafts individual assignments … in real time", ~$80/yr parent-paid; Gynzy Plus "adaptief leren", school-paid; Boom reports) rather than of content; no *printable* K-2 product doing this was found, which makes it either a genuine white space or a sign that teachers do not expect paper to be adaptive. ### Cited Findings

- ESGI: 1:1 assessment → parent letters/flash cards from results (the closest "assessment-to-printable" loop in K), $259/yr teacher-paid — [ESGI for teachers](https://esgisoftware.com/how-we-help/for-teachers/)
- Squla (NL, parent-paid): normally **€10/month**; a €349.99 lifetime-basisschool offer expired Sept 2025 — [Pepper.com](https://nl.pepper.com/aanbiedingen/squla-basisschoolperiode-370095), [Squla](https://www.squla.nl/)

### Inferences
- Paid by parents (IXL/Squla) or schools (Gynzy) far more than by teachers; ESGI is the one teacher-paid proof point at $259/yr.

- Demand score **3/5** (latent; strongest as the *engine* behind #1 and #2 rather than a SKU). Biggest risk: build cost + no precedent + validity claims.
