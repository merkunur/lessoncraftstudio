## 1 Page concept (base)

One rule picture, twelve lines. At the top a dashed coral rule banner holds the rounding hill: a teal arc on a ground line, the ones digits 0 1 2 3 4 climbing the left slope and 5 6 7 8 9 climbing the right, a dashed divider at the peak, the two tens of the worked number on pills at the feet, a coral ball carrying the 7 of 47 sitting on the arc with a coral arrow rolling down to 50, and beside the hill the line `47 ≈ 50` in big numerals with the 7 in coral. Below, in one cream lane, twelve numbered lines in two columns: `47 ≈ [ ]`, the number in Baloo 2 700 28, the wave glyph drawn in teal, an 84 x 44 dashed coral box. The child reads the ones digit, finds it on the hill in their head, writes the ten. Pencil first, one apparatus, no chips, no line, no words on the lines (the only words on the page are the two captions under the hill's feet and the chrome). Quality = the hill drawn once and well (3 px teal, palette fills, a real arrowhead), generous line spacing that grows into the page's slack, nothing that prints the answer.

## 4 Answer-hiding + uniqueness

Rounding half-up is a total function on the integers: exactly one answer per line (`Math.floor(n / step + 0.5) * step`, re-derived by verify from the stamps; the F3 row from `a`, `b`; the F4 field from `val` + `target`). Nothing printed on a line says where it goes: no tint at d2, no digit box, no candidate, no line; the only place a number meets its ten is the worked line inside the rule strip (`data-lcs-worked="47" data-lcs-worked-to="50"`), and verify asserts the worked number is not an item (poison: 47 also on line 3 -> FAIL). The child WRITES the ten into the box; a wrong answer is visibly wrong to the teacher (a written 40 under a 47, a 3-digit answer in a 2-digit line's box, an empty box). On F1 nothing is written but the numeral itself, in one of two bins; the bins hold 9 cells each while a bin never receives more than 7 (design section 7, a deliberate divergence from the pedagogy's 6 / 6 in 6 cells: with cells === count the last decisions are forced; here `cells > max(split)` and `cells !== either count` are asserted, the G1-351 leak rule). On F4 no membership is stamped at all (`data-lcs-val` + the field's `data-lcs-target` only; verify re-derives membership; the pedagogy's `data-lcs-correct` stamp is dropped so the DOM carries nothing that separates members from distractors). Verify (base): 12 `[data-lcs-row]`, `n` distinct in range, `n % 10 !== 0`, the `mix` exact, `>= fiveMin` with `n % 10 === 5`, up / down quotas, `<= carryMax`, every box `data-lcs-answer === round(n)` and `<= 999`, no text node inside any `.ws-blankbox`, exactly one `[data-lcs-worked]` whose numeral is not an item and whose `-to` equals `round(worked)`, the hill's `data-lcs-ball` === the deciding digit of the worked number and its `data-lcs-lo` / `-hi` === the two feet, digit labels 0-4 at x < 150 and 5-9 at x > 150 inside the hill (read from the SVG), no `[data-lcs-prim="number-line"]`, no `.ws-chip` (base), no text node `=` immediately after a `[data-lcs-num]` (poison: `47 = [ ]` -> FAIL; control: F3's `[ ] + [ ] = [ ]` passes), no text node `≈` / `→` anywhere (the font trap), no number >= 1000 in any text node.
