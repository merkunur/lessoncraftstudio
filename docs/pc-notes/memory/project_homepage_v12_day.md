**The reading model that finally worked (after three critic rounds).** Each act's card is `position: sticky` inside a track that runs ONE SCREEN PAST its act (`height: calc(100% + 100svh)`) and cross-fades at the act boundary (`hv12Flip` on `--tN` 0–2%, `hv12FlipOff` on `--t(N+1)` 0–2%, opacity + visibility). Travelling cards (the first model) lingered into the next act, covered the objects they named, and drove under the bar. The hero's words travel out; the closing frame (15:00) is released as ONE picture 30svh into a 130svh act.

**Traps bought (each cost a round):**
- ⭐⭐ **A sticky element with `margin-bottom: -100svh` is NEVER released** — the negative margin empties its margin box, so the constraint never pushes it; the catalogue painted OVER the stage. Pull the first act up with `margin-top: -100svh` instead.
- ⭐⭐ **A sticky box holds only while its CONTAINER has room below it** — a track exactly as tall as the act released the card after ~50px. And `top: <percentage>` on sticky resolves against the SCROLLPORT, so `min(100%, …)` meant the viewport height; use `100vw` for the scene width.
- ⭐⭐ **`max(0.72, <length>)` mixes a number and a length → the custom property is INVALID at computed-value time → every `calc(N * var(--pt))` collapsed to 0/16px** (paper padding 0, "$69" at 16px, pins over the heading). Use `0.72px`.
- ⭐ **A `.hv12-track { min-height: 100% }` inside a min-height act is ZERO**: a ONCE band positioned in % of it had no height and the IntersectionObserver could never fire — the 13:00 blind never dropped in any headless run.
- ⭐ **`{children}` rendered inside the trolley** put the 13:00 veil inside a small box; the veil must sit in the ROOM's stacking context so the lit front wall (`z 7`) and the desks with lit screens can sit above it (`z 6`).
- ⭐ **Sticky bar stamps flip ON per track and never OFF** — every hour stayed lit ("18:00" = 11:00 over 13:00). Each needs the off-animation on the NEXT timeline.
- ⭐ **08:20 shares the MORNING plate whose scene is fold-sized (2×(100svh−chrome−band)), not width-sized** — a track box in width-cqw cannot overlay it; give `#t-0820 .hv12-scenebox` the hero's formula.
- ⭐ **Phone padding on EVERY track (`padding-top: 48svh`)** made 300–400px dead windows; only the first act starts under the pinned picture.
- ⭐ **A phone override appended BEFORE later desktop rules loses** — the "last word" phone block must be the LAST thing in the stylesheet.
- ⭐ **The dev-server LCP/frame numbers are meaningless** (LCP 21s, 80ms frames at 4× on the dev bundle); image bytes are real: 298KB→48KB before scroll and 3.0MB→193KB total once thumbnails went through `next/image` with per-slot `sizes`.
