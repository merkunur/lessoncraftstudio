# Reviewers' notes: registry `pt`

Written by the reviewers of the crosswalk (`scripts/curricula/apply-review.js`). 306 teaching points carry a reviewer's row; the reason for each is in `frontend/content/curricula/pt/crosswalk-authored.json`.

## author-math

- The BNCC places clock reading in the 3º ano (EF03MA23). The time objectives of the 1º and 2º ano are about the events of a day, the calendar and intervals on a digital clock, so the grade 1 and grade 2 clock sheets map to a 3º ano code.
- The BNCC has no fraction notation before the 4º ano: the 2º and 3º ano name only 'metade, terça, quarta, quinta e décima parte' (EF02MA08, EF03MA09); unit fractions are in the 4º ano (EF04MA09) and general fractions, equivalence and comparison in the 5º ano (EF05MA03, EF05MA04, EF05MA05). The grade 2 and grade 3 fraction sheets therefore map to 4º and 5º ano codes.
- Other grade 2-3 content that the BNCC places later: reflection symmetry (4º ano, EF04MA19), right angles (4º ano, EF04MA18), area by counting squares (4º ano, EF04MA21), perimeter by name (4º ano, EF04MA20), same perimeter with different area (5º ano, EF05MA20), volume with cubes (5º ano, EF05MA21), temperature (4º ano, EF04MA23), the unknown in an equality (4º ano, EF04MA15), change in shopping (4º ano, EF04MA25), letter-number grid cells (5º ano, EF05MA14).
- No habilidade in PART 2 names odd and even numbers, and none names rounding. The four odd/even points and the three rounding points have only 'prepares' rows to the nearest wording (pairing, doubles and halves, remainders; approximate calculation). Brazilian teachers do teach both and will look for them.
- Multiplication problems of the 3º ano are limited to 'por 2, 3, 4, 5 e 10' (EF03MA07) and division to divisors up to 10 (EF03MA08). The tables of 6 to 9 are covered only by the unlimited wording 'fatos básicos ... da multiplicação' (EF03MA03); the 2º ano has 2, 3, 4 and 5 (EF02MA07).
- The pre-school stage has only a few mathematics objectives (EI03ET01, 04, 05, 07, 08). Patterns, position words, shape names, ordinals and the days of the week have no EI03 objective, so those K points map to 1º ano (or 2º ano) habilidades, as 'teaches' where the wording is met and 'prepares' otherwise.
- Content of these years that no teaching point covers and that teachers will look for: chance (EF01MA20, EF02MA21, EF03MA25); writing a date (the first half of EF01MA18); the sequence of events of a day (EF01MA16); sketching routes and plans of rooms (EF02MA13, EF03MA12); nets of solids (EF03MA14); congruent figures as such (EF03MA16); 'the result of a measure depends on the unit' (EF03MA17); triple and third part with numbers (EF02MA08); reading and writing numbers to 9999 in numerals and words (EF03MA01, EF03MA02); banknotes besides coins (EF01MA19, EF02MA20); trapezium and parallelogram (EF03MA15); the class surveys (EF01MA22, EF02MA23, EF03MA28), which a worksheet can only prepare.
- The Brazilian versions must be localised for the rows to hold: real and centavo coins and banknotes with prices such as R$ 2,50; metric units only (no inches or feet in 2:choose-units and in the ruler sheets); degrees Celsius on the thermometer; the 24-hour clock; and months matched to southern-hemisphere seasons (December is summer) in 1:days-months-seasons.
- Line plots (2:line-plots, 3:line-plot-halves) are not a representation the BNCC names for these years; it names tables, simple column or bar graphs and, from the 4º ano, picture graphs. The scaled pictograph of grade 2 also has no 2º ano objective of its own.
- Skill lines too vague to place exactly: 1:sort-three-groups (the attribute is not given), 2:share-and-group (number of bins), 2:equal-groups-times and 2:arrays (factors), 3:input-output (which operations the rules use), 1:coins (currency). Each was judged by the year the worksheets sit in, as the reason says.
- Ten thinking-skills points have no rows (mazes, find-the-differences from grade 1 up, analogies, sudoku, logic grids). The BNCC has no objective for them; they can be offered as 'raciocínio lógico' enrichment without a code.

## author-rest

- Grammar sits later in the BNCC than on the site: word classes are 3º ano (EF03LP08, EF03LP09), pronouns 3º ao 5º ano (EF35LP06, EF35LP14), article and number agreement 4º ano (EF04LP07), verb tense and compound words 5º ano (EF05LP05, EF05LP08). The grade 1 and 2 worksheets on these can only be shown as preparation.
- Digraphs are a 3º ano objective (EF03LP03, 'lh, nh, ch'), but the worksheets sit in K, 1 and 2. The Portuguese edition must use ch / lh / nh; sh and th do not exist.
- Science placed in other years than the worksheets: animal groups by body covering is 3º ano (worksheets grade 1); life cycles 3º ano; map key 3º ano (worksheets grade 1); compass directions 4º ano (worksheets grade 2); Moon phases, the Earth's rotation, the water cycle and food groups are all 5º ano.
- Three points have no row: bilingual word cards (no second language in these years), wants and needs (no objective), and the planets (nothing in the 1º to 5º ano asks for their order or kinds). Continents and oceans are attached only loosely, as going beyond EF03CI07; the BNCC of these years names neither.
- The four seasons are never named in the objectives of these years; the BNCC speaks of 'variação de temperatura e umidade' and 'chuva, vento, calor'. Season worksheets built on snow and falling leaves will not match what most Brazilian teachers plan from these lines.
- Sink or float has no objective of its own before 'densidade' in the 5º ano, and no Ciências objective names the fair test; the three sink-float points and the investigation point are shown mostly as preparation.
- Road safety and feelings from the 1º ano on have no objective in the components given. Road signs were attached as texts of the street (EF15LP01, EF15LP04), which is how Brazilian teachers usually work with them.
- Language content of these years that no teaching point covers and teachers will look for: nasal marks til / m / n (EF02LP05), accent marks and the stressed syllable (EF03LP04, EF03LP06), -ão and -inho / -zinho (EF02LP11), the exclamation mark, colon and dash in dialogue (EF03LP07), and the everyday text kinds the BNCC lists by name: listas, bilhetes, convites, receitas, parlendas, quadrinhas, trava-línguas, cantigas, cartas, notícias and anúncios.
- Other content with no teaching point: História (clock and calendar as time markers EF02HI07, 'antes, durante, ao mesmo tempo e depois' EF02HI06, personal objects and documents as sources, games of other times, school and family celebrations); Geografia (the child's own places, rules of living together, work in town and country, waste and water use); Ciências (the Sun's position and shadows EF02CI07, sound and light EF03CI01 to EF03CI03, soil EF03CI09 and EF03CI10, accidents at home EF02CI03); Arte beyond drawing (music, dance, theatre, cultural heritage).
- Skill lines too English to place with certainty: 2:spelling-rules (mapped to the Portuguese contextual rules c/qu, g/gu, r/rr, s/ss, on the assumption that the Portuguese edition drills those), K:cvc-build (first Portuguese words are CV-CV), 2:verb-forms (be / have / do / go would be ser / ter / fazer / ir), the two articles points (o / a, um / uma) and 'said' in 2:synonyms (disse).
- 2:synonyms~doing-stage has the same skill line as 2:synonyms and received the same rows.
- Many year-K literacy points (syllables, first sounds, sound boxes, letter cases, alphabet order, sight words) map to 1º ano objectives marked 'teaches', because the Educação Infantil objectives stop at rhyme, spontaneous writing and recognising known words. A Brazilian pre-school teacher may see these sheets as 1º ano work.
