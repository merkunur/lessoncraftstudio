## §1 — Concept / world
**The Mending Fences of Meadowmoss.** Bramble, a gentle badger fence-keeper, tends the paddocks of a sleepy hill-farm where round little animals graze. Bramble keeps the fences in good repair with a **roll of fence-rope that is exactly as long as it is** — the perimeter made into a PHYSICAL, conserved thing (spend more here → less there; the rope never stretches). A two-character world makes the around-vs-fill split concrete: **Bramble fences the EDGE** (the boundary path) while **Clover the sheep plants GRASS inside** (the fill) — two jobs, two units, the same field. Bramble's GRADED jobs are the ones a side-summer can't shortcut: **mend a missing fence-board (the doubling-inverse), judge which field needs more fence vs grows more grass (the decoupling), and tell fencing-the-edge from planting-the-inside (around-vs-fill units).** The forward "walk the whole fence and total it" is the felt MODEL, never the test — and the rope is given as a budget, so the child never sums sides to win. Non-numeric abundance vessel: across a session the meadow **comes alive — more grazing animals settle into the mended paddocks, more wildflowers open** (never a count/score). `ctx.correct()` auto-advances; no Check button.

## §2 — The standard + the load-bearing cognition
**3.MD.D.8 (Grade 3, SOLE) — solve problems involving PERIMETERS of polygons: find the perimeter given side lengths, find an UNKNOWN SIDE LENGTH, and EXHIBIT same-perimeter-different-area / same-area-different-perimeter.** Perimeter = the distance AROUND the boundary (a length/path), distinct from area (the fill). **Pedagogy ruling (MID-SIZE, NOT thin), ranked by owned-arith-proofness × non-area-duplication:** **Rank-1 the DECOUPLING** (same-perimeter-different-area — the anti-covariation core; "more perimeter ⇒ more area" is the most durable measurement misconception; the cognition is the RELATIONSHIP between two computations, unfakeable by summing); **Rank-2 the INVERSE-WITH-DOUBLING** (rectangle: given P + one side → other = ½P − side; the undoing-2×(l+w) is the non-owned step — bare P−Σshown is OWNED SUBTRACTION); **Rank-3 AROUND-vs-FILL** (unit/structure discrimination — boundary segments vs interior squares — NOT a keyword/character task). Forward-sum = substrate only, NEVER graded. Numbers: whole-number sides 1-10, perimeters ≤~36 (rich factorings 12/16/18/20/24 for the decoupling families), small magnitudes so arithmetic load never masks the geometry. All numbers = real DOM/SVG `<text>`. **FENCES:** the FORWARD SUM (owned addition — substrate, never graded); bare general-polygon P−Σ inverse (owned subtraction — cut); produce-the-AREA (the LIVE/sibling Game-80 area act — `deriveArea` NEVER reaches the grade); the half-up… (n/a). **Distinctness from Game-80 (area, 3.MD.C.5/6):** Game-80 grades produce-the-area; this grades the boundary-inverse + the ordinal-independence judgment; Game-80 explicitly fenced perimeter out — complementary.

## §3 — THE ARCHITECTURE REWORK (the heart; the five cheats + the re-center)
**The critic's catch — five owned/perceptual/motor cheats shipped as load-bearing:**
- **(A1) general-polygon missing side = P − Σ(shown sides) is OWNED SUBTRACTION** (the operands sit on screen; an `OWNED_SUBTRACTION_SOLVER` passes 100%; the standard's "find an unknown side" satisfied typographically, the cognition is owned). → CUT/substrate.
- **(A4) the drag-the-peg "close-the-loop" is a MOTOR FLAIL (Game-77/80).** A visible slack/gap is a continuous error signal → the child flail-drags until the gap hits zero, computing NOTHING (a `DRAG_FLAIL_SOLVER` gradient-descends it); worse, it TEACHES "adjust until it fits," not "compute the missing length." → SCAFFOLD; **the gap signal MUST be HIDDEN at grade time;** the graded inverse is a DISCRETE commitment (pick/type among misconception-product foils).
- **(B1) "which holds more area, same perimeter" is PERCEPTUAL + area-duplicate.** For fixed-perimeter rectangles the squarer one really holds more (isoperimetric fact) → a `SQUARER_LOOKS_MORE_SOLVER` passes by eye; and the child is comparing AREAS = the Game-80 act. → SCAFFOLD.
- **(B2) "group by same fence" reintroduces the FORWARD-SUM** (sum each shape's sides to compute its perimeter, then match — owned addition). → CUT/scaffold.
- **(around-vs-fill) "route the shape to Pip-vs-Tilly" is a CHARACTER-KEYWORD task** (learn "fence question → Pip" without discriminating the attribute; a `CHARACTER_KEYWORD_SOLVER` passes). → RE-CENTER onto the UNIT.

**THE FIX — RE-CENTER onto FOUR genuinely owned-arith-proof, non-perceptual, non-area-duplicate graded acts:**
- **GRADED (load-bearing):**
  1. **MEND THE MISSING BOARD — the rectangle DOUBLING-inverse.** A rectangular paddock, one board missing, the total fence-rope given as the premise; the child PICKS the missing board from candidates that **MANDATORILY include the `P − side` misconception foil** (the answer a child gets if they forget the opposite side exists) — only ½P−side is right. Discrete commit; gap HIDDEN. The foil-presence is load-bearing (a gate that only checks "the right answer exists" misses it). Defeats `OWNED_SUBTRACTION` + `DRAG_FLAIL`.
  2. **MORE FENCE OR MORE GRASS? — the JOINT-RANKING decoupling.** Present fields where perimeter-order ≠ area-order (long-thin 2×9: P=22, A=18 vs square 5×5: P=20, A=25 → the thin one needs MORE fence but grows LESS grass); the child answers BOTH "which needs more fence?" and "which grows more grass?" → must rank OPPOSITELY. Defeats `AREA_COVARIATION_SOLVER` (same ranking for both) — which no other decoupling form defeats. **Guard the residual two-perceptual-rules cheat** (extent→fence, blob→grass): decorrelate bbox from both, and include ≥1 item where the thin shape is NOT the longest bbox (an L/staircase: high perimeter, compact extent) so "longest = most fence" mis-fires.
  3. **FENCE IT OR PLANT IT? — around-vs-fill UNIT-discrimination.** A field pre-marked with BOTH its boundary-length ticks AND its interior unit-squares; "to buy FENCE, which marks do you count?" → the boundary ticks; "to plant GRASS, which?" → the interior squares. The child selects the UNIT STRUCTURE. **The BORDER-SQUARES foil is load-bearing** (counting the ring of border cells = 2n+2m−4 ≠ perimeter 2n+2m, ≠ area). Defeats `CHARACTER_KEYWORD` + `BORDER_SQUARES`.
  4. **DOES THE ROLL REACH? — one non-sum forward check (the critic's §7 patch).** A fixed roll of given length; does it reach all the way AROUND field X? (reach / falls-short — a closure judgment relating a given total to a boundary, NO tally, NO flail signal). Honestly touches the standard's clause-1 ("find the perimeter") without grading the owned sum and without the game tallying for the child.
- **GRADED — ADVANCED TIER (operator-ruled GATED behind the core 4 acts):** **L-shape CLOSURE inverse** (missing run = difference of opposite runs — not a perimeter subtraction at all; rectilinear-closure reasoning) + **SAME-AREA-DIFFERENT-PERIMETER** (the converse decoupling). Gated so younger/struggling kids never hit the hard floor, but full 3.MD.D.8 clause coverage for kids who reach it.
- **SCAFFOLD (ungraded model/exploration):** the forward fence-walk; the drag-to-close (gap HIDDEN at any commit); which-holds-more (intuition); stretch-and-squash predict (a memorized-direction `MONOTONE_RULE` — intuition only); same-fence sort (only if kept, perimeters PRE-LABELLED so no summing).
- **child-output STRICTLY before game-output** (`ANSWER_NOT_PRE_REVEALED`); the missing-board edge is BLANK (no numeral, no grid, no tick); any countable model reveals only AFTER commit; the drag-gap is hidden at grade time.

## §4 — Anti-cheat architecture (the measured gate)

Solvers — ALL must FAIL the GAME:

- **`DRAG_FLAIL_SOLVER`** (gradient-descend the visible gap to 0) → must FAIL (gap HIDDEN at grade time; the graded inverse is a discrete commit).
- **`SQUARER_LOOKS_MORE_SOLVER`** (pick most-square by eye) → must FAIL (which-holds-more is scaffold; the joint-ranking requires OPPOSITE orderings).
- **`AREA_COVARIATION_SOLVER`** (same rank for fence & grass) → must FAIL on the joint-ranking (orders disagree by construction).
- **`TWO_PERCEPTUAL_RULES_SOLVER`** (extent→fence, blob→grass) → must FAIL (bbox decorrelated; ≥1 thin-but-compact L item).
- **`FORWARD_SUM_SOLVER`** (sum each shape's sides) → must FAIL (no graded sort/sum; the reach-around is a closure judgment, not a tally).
- **`CHARACTER_KEYWORD_SOLVER`** (map verb→character) + **`BORDER_SQUARES_SOLVER`** (count the ring of border cells) → must FAIL on around-vs-fill (graded on the UNIT; border-squares foil present).

- **Wrong-answer re-show (no-reveal):** the missing-board re-show pulses the rectangle's opposite-side equality (two long + two short) WITHOUT revealing the value; the joint-ranking re-show lays the two fields side by side — "same... no, this fence is LONGER but look, less grass fits" (the decoupling IS the re-teach); around-vs-fill re-show highlights boundary-ticks vs interior-squares as the two distinct unit kinds; never the answer numeral first.

## Cohort doctrine this game adds
**The OWNED-OP-RECURS-INSIDE-THE-INVERSE + the ISOPERIMETRIC-PERCEPTUAL-SHORTCUT + the MOTOR-FLAIL-at-grade lesson — PROGRAM-WIDE (a sharpening of Games 78/80/82 applied to a measurement standard whose forward op AND naive inverse are both owned).** Three linked findings. **(1) "Grade the inverse" is necessary but NOT sufficient — the OWNED OPERATION can recur INSIDE the inverse.** A bare "missing side = P − Σknown" is OWNED SUBTRACTION (the Game-78 thin-cheat reappearing one level down, in the inverse you re-centered onto). The inverse is non-owned ONLY when it forces a STRUCTURAL step the owned op skips — the rectangle DOUBLING (½P−side, undoing 2×(l+w), with the `P−side` misconception-product as a MANDATORY present-and-wrong foil) or the L-shape CLOSURE (missing run = difference of opposite runs, where P need not even enter). **The gate must assert the misconception-product foil is PRESENT and selectable** — "a correct answer exists" is insufficient; the cheat is caught only by making the owned-op's output a wrong option. **(2) The ISOPERIMETRIC perceptual shortcut + the disguised forward-sum sink the obvious decoupling forms.** "Which holds more area (same perimeter)" has a TRUE perceptual answer (squarer = more, by the isoperimetric fact) → a believer of the very misconception you're testing passes by eye; AND it's the area-game's produce/compare-area ACT (Game-82 duplicate). "Group by same fence" reintroduces the FORWARD-SUM. The clean, non-owned, non-perceptual, non-duplicate decoupling is the **JOINT-RANKING**: shapes where perimeter-order ≠ area-order, graded on the judgment that the two attributes rank-order OPPOSITELY (defeats `AREA_COVARIATION`, the only test that does) — guard the residual two-separate-perceptual-rules cheat (decorrelate bbox from both attributes). **(3) A "drag-until-it-closes/fits" mechanic is a MOTOR FLAIL when a continuous error signal (gap/slack) is visible at grade time** (gradient-descend it, compute nothing — the Game-77/80 leak) → the boundary-balancing drag is SCAFFOLD; HIDE the gap at commit; grade a DISCRETE choice among misconception-product foils. **(4) Around-vs-fill / attribute-identity must grade the UNIT/STRUCTURE (boundary segments vs interior squares, with the BORDER-SQUARES hybrid as a foil), NOT a keyword or a character-association** ("fence→Pip"). Generalizes to EVERY measurement/notation standard whose forward op AND naive inverse are both owned (perimeter, elapsed time, unit conversion, missing-addend-within-a-known-total): the load-bearing grade is the inverse-that-forces-a-structural-step + the ordinal/relational independence judgment + the unit-discrimination — and the gate must make the OWNED-OP'S OUTPUT a present-and-wrong foil, hide any continuous error signal, and decorrelate perceptual shortcuts that happen to be TRUE.
