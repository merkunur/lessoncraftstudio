# Reviewers' notes: registry `sv`

Written by the reviewers of the crosswalk (`scripts/curricula/apply-review.js`). 306 teaching points carry a reviewer's row; the reason for each is in `frontend/content/curricula/sv/crosswalk-authored.json`.

## author-all

- Lgr22 states the central content once for årskurs 1–3 and places nothing in a single year. Every 1–3 row is therefore a band-level match; where a year matters (number range, clock detail) the reason says it rests on Swedish practice, not on the text.
- The förskoleklass content (part 3) is very short: 30 points for the whole year, with three for mathematics and no calculating, decoding or spelling. Most K points teach F.3.2, F.3.3 or F.1.4. Two K points (fact fluency to 20, number bonds to 20) are really årskurs 1 content and are marked as teaching the 1–3 point and extending F.3.2; sight-word reading and CVC building sit on the same edge.
- Content Lgr22 names only for årskurs 4–6 that our årskurs 2–3 sheets already do: area and perimeter (3:area-model, 3:same-area, 3:perimeter, 3:same-perimeter → MA.4-6.3.3), angles (3:right-angles → MA.4-6.3.2), coordinates (2:grid-coordinates → MA.4-6.5.2), equivalent fractions and fractions on the number line (→ MA.4-6.1.1), pyramids, prisms and the rhombus (→ MA.4-6.3.1), solving a balance equation (→ MA.4-6.2.3). A Swedish teacher looking in årskurs 3 will not expect these.
- Grammar: Lgr22 names «ordklasser», «ords böjningsformer» and rule-based «Stavning» only for årskurs 4–6 (SV.4-6.1.7). Word classes, plurals, verb forms and pronouns in our årskurs 1–2 are mapped to the general words «Språkliga strukturer och normer» (SV.1-3.1.6) and marked as preparing the 4–6 point. Swedish teachers do teach substantiv, verb and adjektiv in årskurs 2–3, so the sheets are wanted, but the 1–3 text does not ask for them by name.
- Rounding is not named anywhere in Lgr22 1–3; the two rounding points are tied to number relations and to «överslagsräkning». Temperature is not among the quantities Lgr22 lists for measuring in any band, so 3:thermometer is marked extends.
- Points whose skill line is English and needs a Swedish rebuild, not a translation: digraphs sh/ch/th (Swedish: sj-, tj-, ng, ck), spelling rules ck/tch/dge/ai-ay (Swedish: dubbelteckning, ng/nk, j-/sj-/tj-sounds), a/an articles (en/ett), sight words (frequent small words), prefixes un-/dis-/re- (o-, miss-, åter-), family words (mormor/farmor, moster/faster), four food groups (kostcirkeln or tallriksmodellen), inches and feet, a.m./p.m., coins (kronor), the alphabet (29 letters, nine vowels).
- Cursive: Lgr22 says only «Handstil». Sweden has no required joined script, so the three cursive points are optional work under SV.1-3.1.5; the site already ships no cursive type in Swedish.
- Topics teachers treat as standard but Lgr22 1–3 does not name: weather, parts of a plant, parts of a flower, outer body parts, family. They are mapped loosely (prepares / extends) or to a Swedish reading point, and the reasons say so. Four thinking-skills points have no row (K:spot-the-difference, 1:find-the-differences, 1:mazes, 1:picture-analogies) and five have readiness rows only (K:same-different-visual, K:mazes, K:shadow-closure, 1:grid-copy, 1:secret-code); sudoku and logic grids are placed under Problemlösning (MA.1-3.6.1) as strategy work, which is how Swedish books use them.
- Mathematics 1–3 content that no teaching point covers and teachers will look for: «Slumpmässiga händelser i konkreta situationer» (MA.1-3.4.1, chance), «Skala vid enkel förminskning och förstoring» (MA.1-3.3.4, only prepared by grid copying), the history of number symbols (MA.1-3.1.3, for example Roman numerals), stepwise instructions as a basis for programming (MA.1-3.2.4, only prepared), «Formulering av matematiska frågeställningar» (MA.1-3.6.2, writing one's own problems), and older units of measure (MA.1-3.3.3).
- Science 1–3 content with no teaching point: simple food chains (NO.1-3.1.3, only prepared), gravity, balance and friction (NO.1-3.3.1), mixtures and how to separate them (NO.1-3.4.2), magnetism as a sorting property (NO.1-3.4.1), fungi and local species names (NO.1-3.1.2), organs and their function (NO.1-3.2.1), sleep and exercise (NO.1-3.2.3), stories of how science grew (NO.1-3.5.2). Solid, liquid and gas (NO.1-3.4.3) is reached only through the årskurs 3 water cycle.
- Social subjects 1–3 are the thinnest area: nothing covers local history and the ice age, the Stone / Bronze / Iron Age in the Nordic countries, religions and their festivals and stories, myths, human rights and the child convention, democracy, migration, timelines, mental maps and the globe, countries and places near the child, or environment questions. In Teknik nothing covers how everyday objects work, simple mechanisms, or computers; in Bild nothing covers picture analysis.
- Svenska 1–3 content with no teaching point: information search and source criticism (SV.1-3.5), all of speaking and listening (SV.1-3.2), children's literature and authors (SV.1-3.3.1, 3.3.3), basic text revision (SV.1-3.1.4), spoken versus written language (SV.1-3.4.4). In the förskoleklass nothing covers rules, democracy, children's rights and life questions (F.4.2–F.4.5 apart from road safety), digital tools (F.1.6, F.1.7, F.2.4), building and construction (F.4.10) or play and outdoor activity (F.5.1–F.5.3); these are mostly not worksheet content.
- The two points with a ~ in the id carry the same skill line as their parent. 2:synonyms~doing-stage and 3:two-attribute-sort~logic-cabinet got the parent's rows; 1:days-months-seasons~season-wheel was read as the months-in-seasons part and given NO.1-3.1.1 as teaches — check that reading.
