## Templates from today's worksheet types

**Odd and even: share a group fairly between two / circle in pairs; is anything left over?**

- `G1-351` Odd and Even Numbers: Odd House, Even House — Write each number in the house where it belongs, the odd house or the even house. Three numbers have moved in already and show their pairs. · screen version (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/g1/G1-351-odd-and-even.js)
- `G1-369` Can Two Friends Share Fairly? — Share the pictures, one each in turn. Write how many each friend gets and how many are left. Is the number of pictures odd or even? Circle it. · screen version · picture themes (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/g1/G1-369-odd-and-even-can-two-friends-share-fairly.js)
- `G1-370` Odd or Even? Count the Pictures in Pairs — Count the pictures and circle them two by two. Write how many pairs you made. Is the number of pictures odd or even? Circle it. · screen version · picture themes (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/g1/G1-370-odd-and-even-count-the-pictures-in-pairs.js)

**Decide odd/even by pairing objects, as two equal addends (n + n or n + n + 1), and by the ones digit for numbers under 100**

- `G2-351` Odd or Even? Write Two Equal Addends — Circle the dots two by two. Then write the number as two equal parts and write what is left over in the small box. · screen version (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/g2/G2-351-odd-and-even-write-two-equal-addends.js)
- `G2-352` Odd and Even Numbers Under 100: Look at the Ones Box — Read the rule at the top. Look only at the ones box of each number and tick the circle under even or odd. · screen version (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/g2/G2-352-odd-and-even-under-100-look-at-the-ones-box.js)

**Predict whether a sum is odd or even from the addends' parity, without adding**

- `G3-386` Odd or Even Sum? Decide Without Adding — Do not add. Look at the underlined ones digit of each number, use the table, and tick whether the sum is even or odd. · screen version (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/g3/G3-386-odd-and-even-sum-decide-without-adding.js)
