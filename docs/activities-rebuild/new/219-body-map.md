## The skill
Name and find the parts of the body and face, count them, and know which come in twos.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE LABEL THAT PINS ITSELF. The library doll (toys/doll, front-facing full body) stands centre stage at ≥ 60 % stage height, with small pin dots at hand-set points (head, eyes, nose, mouth, arms, hands, fingers, legs, feet — plus elbow, knee, shoulder as word-only labels in setting 2). Labels wait in a rail: library part pictures (body-parts: eye, ear, nose, mouth, hand, foot, finger, leg, arm, head) for pre-readers, or words. The child drags a label; near its own point it pins itself with a little thread and the dot fills. Miss 1: it swings back and the dot it was dropped on flashes its own label for a moment. Miss 2: its own dot pulses. Success: every thread drawn, the doll gives a small hop. TWOS mode (K-363, K-360): tap every part we have two of — tapping one hand lights its partner on the other side; a one-of part (nose, mouth) answers with a single ring; then the count chips (1 / 2 / 10 for fingers) are dropped on the part. HIDDEN mode (K-362): a paper sticky note covers one part; the child picks the part picture it hides and the note peels off.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Mixing up elbow/knee, wrist/ankle, and not realising that most parts come in left–right pairs.
- **Pictures:** toys/doll (the figure); body-parts (arm, ear, eye, face, finger, foot, hand, head, leg, mouth, nail, nose, tooth) as labels; elbow, knee, shoulder, wrist exist in the cache but not in lcs-pictures — word labels only (from the K-354 bank) until labelled.
- **Settings:** Labels: pictures / words · Mode: label / which come in twos / what is missing
- **Rounds:** 9 rounds per mode; parts balanced over head / trunk / limbs; tray order shuffled; one right pin per label.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G1-356, K-344, K-354, K-360, K-361, K-362, K-363
- **Distinct from:** Sense Face is about what organs do; this is naming and pairing body parts.
- **Build risk:** The doll's ears are under her hair — ear is pinned on the side of the head and checked by the reviewer, or refused. Pin coordinates are hand-set once for the one picture.

## Merged into this instrument (cover each as a setting or round set)
- grade 1 "The Body Tag Figure" (body-tag-figure): Name the parts of the body. — A FIGURE WITH TAG STRINGS: library body-part pictures assemble into a figure; numbered strings run from parts to empty tags. The child drags word tags (the content words, from image-vocabulary) to the strings; a correct tag tightens its string and the part glows; a wrong one hangs loose and its string points to where that word belongs. Builder setting: the child assembles the figure from the parts first.
