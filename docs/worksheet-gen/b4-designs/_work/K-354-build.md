## Deviations from the design file (each with the measurement)
1. **Head anchor (122,86)/(178,86), not (118,72).** At y 72 a lane-centred leader runs straight through the ear bump (ear D spans y 53..79 at x 95..104; the ear anchor sits 5.4 px from the line). At (122,86) the r-7 ring is inside the disc by >= 1.4 units on every point of its circumference (disc edge at y 91.5 is x 113.1; ring's lower-left point 116.5) and 7 units below the ear; the leader leaves the cheek 7 units under the ear (4.8 px net of the 3 px ear stroke).
2. **Hair anchor (130,34)/(170,34), not (128,30).** At (128,30) the ring's top (y 22.2) touches the cap's outer arc (disc edge 23.6 at x 128, cap = disc + 4); at (130,34) the whole ring is inside the cap (disc edge 22.6, cap 18.6) and above the fringe (47 at x 130).
3. **Neck anchor is a MIDLINE point (150,118)**, not bilateral (140/160, 118). The neck is 20 units wide; a r-7 px ring is 15.6 units across, so off-centre it straddles the neck's outline; centred it ENCIRCLES the neck (ring x 142..158 inside 140..160). Either side may claim it (like nose / mouth).
4. **Toe anchor at the two OUTER toes (102,537)/(198,537)**, not the big toe (140/160, 538): a leader from the big toe (inside, nearest the midline) runs across the four other toes; from the outer toe it leaves the foot at once (the ring encircles the two small toes).
5. **Upper arm starts at (196,141), not (196,138)** (width 30 unchanged): at 138 the cap top (123) left a neck leader at y 118 only ~1.7 px of paper above the sleeve; at 141 the sleeve seam meets the torso top (126) and the gap measures 4.5 px. The shoulder anchor (106,132) stays inside the cap (9.2 units from its centre). F2 widths are unchanged.
6. **Lane HINTS for eye (+90 px) and nose (+24 px)** (the lane's wanted centre sits below the anchor): a lane-centred horizontal leader from the eye passes THROUGH the ear bump (ear anchor 0 px from the line) and from the nose 1 unit under it. With the hints the eye's leader clears the ear circle by 6.4 units beyond the 3 px margin (measured on the segment), the nose's by 9.2. Lanes on a side are ordered by (anchor y + hint); crossings are still caught by the sweep.
7. **The sweep also checks DRAWN SHAPES, not only target rings**: (c) no leader's drawn part crosses a shape of the figure it does not belong to (the primitive's `obstacles`, 3 px margin, own regions per anchor), and (d) a lane on side L takes the L coordinate of a bilateral anchor (never the far half). The design's rule (16 px from a foreign target ring + no intersections) stays as written and is what `verify()` re-runs from the stamps, plus the side rule and the ring-edge geometry; the obstacle sweep is re-run in node by the gate with an INDEPENDENT sampled-point method (PO kills a leader rewired across the torso that the 16 px rule cannot see).
8. **Limbs are single tapered silhouettes** (thigh 40 → shin 34 as one path; upper arm 30 → forearm 26 as one path with a rounded elbow; the hand = palm arc + a four-finger comb in ONE outline with three 1.5 px seams; thumb under the palm; toes as circles over the foot's lower edge). Two overlapping capsules of different widths draw a step or a crease line across the limb; the region bboxes and the F2 widths (forearm 25.1 / shin 32.8 / palm 32.8 / foot 34.7 / hair 46.3 at h 540, matching the design's measured numbers) are as specified. The knee arc lives inside its leg group (nothing later covers it).
9. **`regions[]` carries TWO measures**: `bboxMin` (the smaller bbox dimension — the F4 "an omitted class >= 14 px" floor reads it: arm bbox 45 px at h 300) and `min` (the NARROWEST drawn width a crayon meets — the F2 >= 24 px floor reads it: forearm 25.1 at h 540). The design's two floors are two different measurements; the table prints both.
10. **The stage is CENTRED in its grid cell** (`align-items:center`) rather than "re-placing the lanes against a taller stage": a static page cannot re-run `placeLanes` per chrome; centring splits the slack above and below the figure (en chrome: 66 px each side; fi 677 chrome: 14 px).
11. **The throwing accessors `partWord / bankWord / pluralWord / factLabel` live in the spec** (`literal()`), not in `lib/b4-common.js` — the shared lib is off-limits to a family build; `bank('human-body', loc)` is the only door.
12. **Rule 10 ("titles differ by a NOUN or verb, never only an adjective") is implemented as a MOVE check**: every face title = base title + `:` + a suffix that ends with `?` or carries a verb / question word of the locale (a built-in list x11, extensible via `bank.moveWords`), and suffix token sets are pairwise distinct beyond a stoplist. P13 (`Parts of the Body: Big Words`) fails it; the §6 candidates pass in all 11 lists.
13. **Fixtures**: the fi 677 chrome needs a 3-line instruction, and Nunito 18 holds ~78 chars a line — a 150-char fi instruction stays at two lines (body 700, five variants measured), so the fixture runs a 176-char instruction to reach the README's third line. The 150 cap is the validator's for shipped copy.
14. **`fixed:true`** is a config seam the gate uses to build the design's fixed set deterministically (never a shipped config; the base configs do not carry it).
