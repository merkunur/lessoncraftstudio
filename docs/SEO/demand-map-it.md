**Purpose:** map real Italian K-3 search demand → the single-axis topic page (`/it/topic/<slug>`) or type×grade intersection that should own it, with honest verdicts and final-form titles/metas. Method: real google.it autocomplete harvest (`docs/SEO/harvests/it.json`, hl=it&gl=it, 2026-07-06; 147 seeds / 849 suggestions / 812 unique) + live it deck inventory (3,774 published decks) + 18 SERP samples. **Surface ownership (locked, do NOT cannibalize):** subject×grade hubs own "Schede didattiche di matematica per la classe prima – da stampare gratis (PDF)"; deck landings own type+theme long-tail ("Schede di … – Tema | da stampare PDF"). **This map's surfaces = single-axis topic pages (type / theme / level heads) + type×grade intersections** — all titles below deliberately use different formulations from both owned surfaces.

## Core findings

### 1. The head-noun system: "schede didattiche" + "da stampare"
Italian K-3 search runs on **schede (didattiche)** as the teacher/parent head noun (179 hits across the harvest; "schede didattiche" 47), with **esercizi** as secondary (28) and **giochi** for the enigmistica genres. The modifier stack, by real frequency: **da stampare (144) >> pdf (17) > per bambini (15) > gratis (10) > online (8)**. "Da stampare" is near-mandatory on print intent; "gratis" and "PDF" are honest for us and should ride along. **"online" is small but real** ("cruciverba online gratis", "sudoku online", "crittogramma online") and — combined with the heavy **wordwall presence (35 hits)** — shows teachers actively hunting interactive exercises: our playable-deck + PDF combo is the differentiator no incumbent has.

### 2. Grade register: "classe prima" beats "prima elementare" — decisively at grade 2
Harvest counts: **classe prima 100 vs prima elementare 78** and **classe seconda 94 vs seconda elementare 5**. "Prima elementare" is a live parent register at grade 1 only; the teacher register ("classe prima/seconda") wins overall and is the only viable form at grade 2+. **Rule: titles use "classe prima / classe seconda / classe terza"; metas and body weave "prima elementare" once for the parent register.** "Scuola primaria" (17) is the umbrella; "scuola dell'infanzia / scuola infanzia" (24) is the preschool band's own head.

### 3. The #1 structural mismatch: symbolic arithmetic tagged 5-7 vs "classe prima" demand
Italian formal arithmetic starts in **classe prima** — scuola dell'infanzia does no written operations. But our it catalog tags **addition (197), subtraction (201), code-addition (199), math-worksheet (150)** at platform level 5-7 ("Scuola dell'infanzia 5-7 anni"), while ALL the demand keys to classe prima ("addizione classe prima", "sottrazione classe prima", "schede addizione prima elementare" — the richest typeGrade clusters in the harvest). This is the exact analog of the sv "within-10 symbolic = åk-1 CARRIES" ruling (§22.5): **within-10/20 symbolic arithmetic should present as classe prima on the it surface.** Until a presentation-layer re-band is ruled, the pragmatic play used below: the **type-head pages** (no level in URL) carry the classe-prima keyword honestly ("Addizioni per la classe prima…" — all 197 decks are within-10/20 addition, which IS classe prima content), and the DB-backed intersections are titled by age ("5-6 anni") rather than a school band they don't carry.

### 5. Intent traps confirmed by the harvest (qualify or skip)
- **bingo** → gambling halls; only "cartelle bingo da stampare (per bambini)" is ours.
- **crittogramma** → adult enigmistica/crypto; low kid demand. Keep page, low priority.
- **pezzi mancanti** → LEGO/puzzle spare parts; reframe "completa l'immagine".
- **misurazione** → blood pressure/glycemia; use "misure" + school qualifier.
- **logica visiva** → police/aptitude tests; qualify "giochi di logica visiva per bambini".
- **caccia al tesoro** → ready-made party kits with riddles; our maze decks are honest only as "caccia al tesoro nel labirinto".
- **indovina la parola** → TV/party game; qualify "dalle immagini".
- **preposizioni** → Italian grammar (semplici/articolate) or English prepositions — NOT our spatial decks; target "indicatori spaziali / concetti topologici" instead.
- **treno dell'alfabeto** → the song ("bob il treno"); keep as body phrase, lead with "ordina le lettere / alfabeto".

### 6. Content gaps (record; do NOT fake-title)
- **pregrafismo** — VERY high demand, the biggest Italian infanzia genre (pianetabambini/portalebambini/maestraemamma all own dedicated sections). We have **no honest content**: pattern/patterns decks are sequences, not tracing; the tracing-capable apps (writing, drawing-lines) are PDF-only and unpublished. This is the it analog of de "Schwungübungen" — the top content-production fork. Only honest adjacency allowed: labirinti/percorsi metas may say "utili come avviamento al pregrafismo".
- **cornicette** — sibling giant genre (greche/cornicette a quadretti), same gap.
- **unisci i puntini** and **trova le differenze** — real printable genres we don't have (dot-to-dot; spot-the-difference ≠ our visual-discrimination find-the-same).
- **decine e unità** (12 decks) and **linea dei numeri** (9 decks) — rich harvested demand ("decine e unità classe prima", "linea dei numeri fino a 20 da stampare"), thin inventory. Production candidates.
- **frazioni classe terza** demand is bigger than classe seconda; we have 34+34 — adequate but the demand center ("frazioni schede didattiche classe terza") is grade 3.
