## What the base page is

## Deviations from the design file (each measured)

1. **`pictureStrip` is exported as `lineUpStrip`.** The design says "Reused (exact): `pictureStrip` from G1-308 §2 (ABSENT today, owned by G1-308)". It is absent from `templates/components-b3/` (measured: the namespace owners are letter-of-the-week, sound-boxes, syllable-reading, syllable-split), and `templates/components-b3.js` THROWS for every b3 family on a duplicate export name. If G1-308 lands `pictureStrip` in parallel and K-320 also exported it, every b3 spec would fail to load. `lineUpStrip` carries the exact G1-308 option contract (`{theme, items, tile=76, pic=64, gap=5, arrow=true, band=true}`) plus K-320's three additive options with byte-identical defaults (`start`, `flagScale`, `under:'band'|'box'|'none'`; `given[]` for F1's solid cells). **Reviewer: when G1-308 lands, fold the two into one owner and point the other at it.** The arrow is an internal `startArrow` (not exported) for the same reason.
2. **Arrow row 24 px, not 20.** A 1.5-scale flag = a 20 px pole with a 15×12 pennant, a 3 px rule and a 12 px head; in 20 px the head (12) sits on the rule at y 13 and touches the pole's foot. Measured arrow row = 24 → panel min d2 = 24 + 4 + 84 + 22 + 44 + 24 = **202** (design 198), d1 **222** (design 214), d3 **194** (design 190). Stacks 690 / 630 / 606 all fit the measured worst body (next item).
3. **The body under the worst LEGAL chrome measures 733 (or 710), not 722.** Measured in the real pipeline (letter, fonts loaded): the instruction column is 631 px ≈ 76 chars/line, so a 150-char instruction wraps to TWO lines → 3-line title (99 px) + 2-line instruction (46 px) = body **733**. A legal-length instruction reaches three lines only through long-word wrapping (a 156-char German string does; five 150-char English/Finnish/Portuguese candidates did not) → body **710**. The README's 722 sits between. The gate renders BOTH chromes at d1/d2/d3 and asserts the 3-line probe really wraps to 3 lines (a vacuous probe fails). d1's 690 stack leaves 20 px at 710; slack per panel 0/15/23 there, 10/30/38 at 733, 25/45/53 with the shipped 2-line title (body 778).
4. **`blankNumeralBox` is defined here** (no sibling exports it; measured). ⚠ Every b3 family got the same instruction, so a parallel family may define the same name → the namespace throws. Reviewer: keep ONE owner (this file's is `.ws-blankbox`, dashed coral 2.5, r 10, `data-lcs-answer` = the hidden answer, default 68×44).
5. **The instruction avoids the relation word "on".** The design's own base instruction ("Count to the number **on** the chip") violates its validator rule 6 (no on/under/left/right/between/inside). Shipped: "Start at the flag. Count to the number of each chip and make the mark it shows." (77 chars). The same trap sits in F4's copy ("start on the right") — the EN F4 string in the bank says "it can stand at either end" instead; the panels must not reintroduce left/right.
6. **Widest chip:** "10th" at 30 px measures **58.6 px** (design est. 58.6, real font) → chip ≈ 131 px; d2 "7th" chips render 117 wide.
7. **Mono print:** `out/dev/K-320-animals-d2-en-greyscale.png` (+ `-strip.png`) — the pennant is a filled mid-grey triangle on the ink pole, the head solid; direction survives by shape.
