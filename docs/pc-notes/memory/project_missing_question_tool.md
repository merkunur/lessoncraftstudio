Gates: verify **7,005** · poison 16/16 + control · mutate **43/43** · probe **76 consequence

0 lines to `lcs-shell.*` or any protected core.

**What it is now — THE STAND · THE NICHE · THE SLIP.** Three fixed niches in one of three
arrangements (change / bracket / compare). At rest they are **empty because nothing has been
said**, not because anything is hidden. Each press adds one fact: the relation is drawn, then
one number, then another, and the remaining niche becomes `?`. The `?` never prints a numeral —
it resolves by becoming **countable**, and the class counts the strip. The teacher speaks the
story, so the same picture runs a different problem every day. `stageOf(state) → 0..4 | null`
is DERIVED, never stored: the ladder is a **path**, not a state machine, so nothing is locked
and stepping off it is allowed.

## ⭐⭐ WHY THE FIRST BUILD HAD TO GO: A SIBLING HAD ALREADY RULED ITS MECHANIC DEAD
`lids.js:47` — *"cover part of a known total and ask what is under it"* — **DEAD**, because
`part-whole-frame` #27 ships **three cloths over whole/a/b in its FREE tier** (`:151`). The
shipped #55 was that ruling violated: two shutters instead of three cloths, a random deal
instead of a teacher-set whole, a printed list of pairs instead of a record of what the class
enacted — **a strictly weaker instance of a free tool.** It shipped because its header fenced
against `lids` and `vet-diagnosis` and **never fenced against `part-whole-frame`**.
⭐ The fix is a different VERB: **it accretes, it never covers.** If a reviewer can point at
something being *uncovered*, the rebuild has drifted back into dead territory.
⚠ The eleven old locale titles all named a window covering (*Fensterladen, persiana, tapparella,
luifel, gardin, luukku*) — the fossil of the dead mechanic, and the reason an art panel argued
hard to keep it. **Evidence beats vocabulary**; the pedagogy ruling won the mechanic and the art
panel's craft was re-aimed.

## ⭐⭐ A LIVENESS GATE CANNOT SEE A CONSEQUENCE-FREE CONTROL — twice now (#39, then here)
The size steppers changed the model and moved **nothing on screen**: at stage 0 every slip is
empty by law, the lattice is sized from the **band** not the value, and `_sayForStage()` returned
the same fixed sentence either way. A teacher pressed `+` in front of a class and the board did
not move. **Nine gates were green.** Found only by writing a probe that asks what each control
changes ELSEWHERE. Fixed with `saidTotal` (`{n}`, native panel ×10) spoken into the status line —
deliberately **not** a numeral parked on the stand, which would sit there all lesson and, when
the asked-for niche IS the total, print the answer.

## ⭐⭐ THE VACUITY TRAP CERTIFIED THE EXACT DEFECT IT EXISTED TO CATCH (the #40 lesson, again)
The ± steppers were `position:absolute` **on top of a niche at every width the tool has** —
1,411px² at 704, 1,638px² at 412; on a phone the pair closed around the niche's own numeral so
the slip read `− 1 +`. **I read it off a render.** The check I then wrote to confirm it selected
`.mqu-size .mqu-btn` — but these are built by `_mkIconBtn`, whose class is **`mqu-icon`** — so it
compared two EMPTY NodeLists and reported *"no overlap at any of the six widths."*
Fixed **structurally** (normal flow under the stand; two boxes in flow cannot overlap), not by
nudging a coordinate. The collision check now lives in the 396-cell locale gate **with a
non-vacuity guard in front of it**, and fires on 134 cells against the pre-fix layout.

## ⭐ EQUIVALENT MUTANTS: RECORD THEM, DO NOT CHASE THEM
20 of 50 mutations survived the first run; **14 were real gate holes** (law L9, +817 assertions:
no-op refusals, what a move CLEARS, cap propagation, and an aliased `told` array that would make
every move mutate its caller). **Six were equivalent mutants** and are recorded with reasons —
`legal`'s floor clause is unreachable given FLOOR=3, `told[ask]` is false in every reachable
state, `setTotal`'s post-loop legality check cannot fire. ⚠ `legal`'s floor and `setTotal`'s are
**mutually redundant**: no single edit can express the defect that would matter (removing both).
Hunting an equivalent mutant is how a suite acquires assertions that cannot fail.

## ⚠ VERIFY THE MEASUREMENT BEFORE THE DEFECT — I nearly filed two against a correct tool
(1) A five-of-six counter frame that was a **mid-stagger capture** — counters arrive on a 260ms
stagger, so a fixed screenshot wait photographs a half-counted board and reads as a wrong answer.
The pace **is** the counting; not a defect. (2) A stepper that *looked* like it touched a niche
at 704 and measured clear. Only the **third** reading, at 360, was real — and that one needed a
non-vacuous selector to prove.

## The art pass
The apparatus lived in six percentage points of lightness (four creams), so nothing in it was
dark, nothing was lit, nothing had a top and a bottom. Now: warm oak stand with two crossing
grain gradients, niches as **recesses** with a chamfered lip and top-inset occlusion, drilled
sockets in the counting tray, the house 3-stop coral counter with a specular and an overshoot
seat, four event animations (linkage landing / question OPENING / counter seating / slip placed).
⭐ **Transitions reverse and animations do not** — so every reversible material fact stays on a
transition governed by `data-dir="back"`, and animations are reserved for events that happen
once. A fifth (a slow pulse on the unanswered `?`) was REFUSED: a persistent animation nags and
turns a question the class is thinking about into a notification badge. Module unit 23cqi → 27cqi
was the single highest-leverage line in the file.
