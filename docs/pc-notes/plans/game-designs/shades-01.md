## 2. Concept & creative hook
**Pesto the soup-stall parrot is picky about STRENGTH — he won't take "any hot word"; the baby's soup needs it just a LITTLE warm, not scalding. Same-meaning spices heat his broth to different lines on a heat-gauge — the GRADIENT made physical — and the child hands Pesto the spice of exactly the RIGHT strength for the customer's need.**
- **ALL SPICES ARE THE SAME FAMILY — ONLY THE STRENGTH DIFFERS (the same-set invariant, the plain-synonym kill):** every round's spice-jars are ALL from one near-synonym family (warm/hot/scalding are all heat-spices) — so "is it a heat word?" is true for all and gives no signal. The child must pick the right SHADE (the strength), never just a same-meaning word. A same-family WRONG-strength jar is always present and is wrong.
- **THE CUSTOMER'S NEED FIXES THE SHADE — AND THE WHOLE CUE DECIDES (the context-keyword kill — the critic's deepest fix):** the need is a FULL degree-cue the child must READ — "a baby's cry that you can BARELY hear" → quiet, vs "a baby's cry that fills the whole house" → loud (the SAME keyword "baby's cry" maps to DIFFERENT answers depending on the rest of the sentence). So "baby → tiny" collocation recall FAILS; the child must read the whole cue. A `CONTEXT_KEYWORD_SOLVER` provably fails.
- **ORDER ONLY THE NON-OBVIOUS LADDERS (the rank-recall kill):** the order mode uses LESS-ROTE gradients (warm < toasty < hot — "toasty" forces reasoning) where the child must REASON the relative degree, not retrieve a memorized order; rote ladders (big<huge<gigantic) are demoted to which-is-stronger / pick-for-context (where the context, not the rote rank, decides). A `FAMILIAR_RANK_SOLVER` (memorized rank table) provably fails the order mode.
- **MANNER IS CHOSE-FOR-CONTEXT, NEVER ORDERED (the manner mechanic):** look/peek/glance/stare/glare have no "more" — Pesto's customer describes the MANNER ("he was SHY and didn't want to be seen — did he STARE or PEEK?"); the context (shy/unseen) decides; the mime-pose is decorative + ambiguous (both "looking"), so picture-recall underdetermines.
- **THE PAYOFF:** the right shade → the broth rises to exactly the customer's line, the customer sighs happily, the jar drops in with a curl of steam; a happy customer + a ladle of broth joins Pesto's growing pot (`GameCollection` — non-numeric AND non-content, NO count). `ctx.correct()`. A wrong shade → no fail; Pesto "tastes" and gives a DIRECTIONAL nudge ("too fierce for a baby — a touch gentler!"); the child re-places (the nudge teaches the gradient direction).
- **Anti-generic statement:** NOT trade-a-same-meaning-word — (a) the same-set invariant kills plain-synonym-match; (b) the FULL-degree-cue + `CONTEXT_KEYWORD_AMBIGUOUS` + the `CONTEXT_KEYWORD_SOLVER` kill the context-collocation cheat (the critic's deepest fix); (c) the less-rote order ladders + the `FAMILIAR_RANK_SOLVER` kill the rank-recall cheat; (d) connotation OUT, manner-not-ordered, no-ties; the gate proves plain-synonym + familiar-rank + context-keyword + antonym + first/random solvers ALL fail while the rank/degree-cue oracle passes.
