Word-class-by-FUNCTION (adjective modifies a noun / adverb modifies a verb — CHOOSE between them by what is modified) is genuinely UNOWNED (live ELA is phonics-only; the whole L-grammar family unowned) + a NEW grammar cognition distinct from Game-2 "Sentence Surgeon" (L.2.1 broad REPAIR), Game-70 "Lighthouse Keeper" (L.1.1.e TENSE), and the vocab games (27/51/56/64). ## §1 — Concept / world
**Pip, a thread-of-light ghost** in the **House of Soft Light** (cozy picture-book rooms). A sentence renders as tappable real `<text>` tokens; one describing-word floats near Pip, **UN-classified** (no "adjective/adverb" label, no bins anywhere). The child **taps the word IN THE SENTENCE that the describing-word points to / modifies**; Pip's thread of light reaches across and lands on it. The CLASS is the **OUTPUT** — the thread landed on a **thing (noun) → it was an adjective**; on an **action (verb) → it was an adverb** — named only AFTER, as a consequence, never selected. The thread literally DRAWS "modifies" as a directional relationship between two words (the most literal possible picture of the standard). Non-numeric abundance vessel: the room slowly fills with warm motes of light (never a count). `ctx.correct()` auto-advances (no Check button).

## §2 — The standard + the load-bearing cognition
**L.2.1.e (SOLE primary)** — use adjectives and adverbs, and CHOOSE BETWEEN them depending on what is to be MODIFIED. **also_teaches L.1.1.f** (use frequently occurring adjectives — rides along on every adjective round, NEVER steers/tests). **FENCES:** Game-2 L.2.1 broad sentence-REPAIR (this game never fixes a broken sentence / never supplies a word from open vocab / varies exactly ONE thing: is this word an adjective or adverb HERE), Game-70 L.1.1.e tense (verbs are modification TARGETS only — never inflect/judge tense; keep verbs simple-past/present so tense is never a confound), vocabulary/meaning (the describer's MEANING never decides a round — only what it modifies; a correct answer is unchanged swapping "fast"→"hard" in the same slot), spelling (the `-ly` ending is the thing we teach the child to DISTRUST). **Cognition (pedagogy ruling):** the word's class = its FUNCTION (what it modifies) in BOTH directions — CLASSIFY (recognition) + CHOOSE-to-add (production) — two faces of one function model. **Adverbs = VERB-MODIFYING ONLY (how/when/where) at Grade 2; EXCLUDE intensifiers (very/too/so → adverb-modifies-adjective)** — keeps a clean noun↔adjective / verb↔adverb binary. Sentences are well-formed, decodable Grade-2, real DOM/SVG `<text>` (the child READS — it's L, text-based).

## §3 — THE ARCHITECTURE REWORK (the heart; the deepest flaw + fix)
**The critic's catch — the no-jars / point-at-what-it-modifies / dual-class fix kills suffix+jar+keyword, but TWO cheats survive ONE LEVEL BELOW it:**
1. **ADJACENCY (the BINDING cheat — ~95%+).** In English the describer is GLUED next to its target by word order: attributive adjective immediately BEFORE its noun ("a fast CAR"), manner/time adverb immediately AFTER its verb ("ran FAST"). So **"tap the content word adjacent to the floating describer" passes ~95%+ WITHOUT ever judging thing-vs-action** — and it SURVIVES the dual-class rounds (fast→car adjacent / fast→ran adjacent — adjacency is RIGHT in BOTH; the dual-class witness is INVISIBLE to a position-relative-to-describer reader). No seeded solver computes the describer's index + taps its neighbour (POSITION tests ABSOLUTE index). **Adjacency is orthogonal to every defense built.**
2. **2-CANDIDATE BINARY DEGENERACY + SEMANTIC-COLLOCATION (the DEEPER floor).** A minimal Grade-2 sentence has ~one noun + one verb as the only plausible tap targets → the class is a ONE-BIT output → a blind 50/50 guess is undetectable per-round, and ONE weak correlated cue pushes 50%→~90%. The strongest such cue (co-binding with adjacency) is **semantic fit**: "sleepy" only sensibly attaches to an animate noun, "quickly" only to a verb → the child asks **"which word does this describer make SENSE next to?"** (a collocation judgment) and the MEANING answers WITHOUT any grammatical-function judgment. Invisible to suffix/jar/keyword/position.

**THE FIX — attack the CANDIDATE SPACE, not just the labeling (4 baked structural constraints + 2 new gate solvers):**
- **FIX A — non-adjacency, ANDed (kills adjacency).** Every LOAD-BEARING round's true target is **NON-adjacent** to the describer AND a content word of the **OPPOSITE POS sits adjacent** (the trap seat). The two Grade-2-legal templates that yield this — **the entire cheat-proof corpus** (this is why MID-SIZE): (i) **fronted/comma adverb + subject-noun decoy** — "Slowly, the turtle walked." (slowly→walked [verb/adverb]; `turtle` [noun] adjacent → adjacency-solver taps `turtle` → WRONG); (ii) **copular/linking-verb predicate adjective + verb decoy** — "The dog looked sleepy." / "The soup smells good." (sleepy→dog [noun/adjective]; `looked` [verb] adjacent → adjacency-solver WRONG). Attributive-adjective ("a fast car") + post-verbal-adverb ("ran fast") rounds are **WARM-UP ONLY — UNGATED, never counted toward the load-bearing N.**
- **FIX B — ≥3 content candidates, ≥1 same-POS decoy (kills the binary + "tap the only noun/verb").** Each load-bearing round presents ≥2 tappable content words of the TRUE POS (or 2+2) so "tap the noun" can't win (two nouns; the child must judge WHICH). `MULTI_CANDIDATE_PRESENT ≥ N`.
- **FIX C — semantic-ambiguity required (kills meaning-fit).** Load-bearing rounds use the **dual-class words** (fast/hard/early/late/high/low) whose describer is collocationally plausible with BOTH a noun and a verb candidate ("hard rock"/"hit hard", "fast train"/"ran fast") — verify both candidates plausible per sentence so meaning-fit can't disambiguate.
- **FIX D — pre-commit feedback identical for correct/incorrect** (closes the picture-change-oracle residue): tapping a candidate produces NO differential animation/sound/warming until commit; no candidate telegraphs correctness.

## §4 — Anti-cheat architecture (the measured gate)
NEW `verify-wordclass-core.js` (window-shim; drives the REAL core; adversarial solvers MUST FAIL, the oracle MUST PASS). Solvers — ALL fail:
- **`ADJACENCY_SOLVER`** (NEW — the missing gate) — taps the content word at min |index − describerIndex|. Assert **FAILS on ≥N** load-bearing rounds + **`ADJACENCY_DECORRELATED`** (across the set, nearest-content-word == true-target ≤ chance+ε, NOT ~95%). The single most important new gate.
- **`SEMANTIC_FIT_SOLVER`** (NEW) — a frozen hand-authored collocation table; taps the highest-collocation candidate. Assert **FAILS on ≥N** (FIX C both-plausible rounds).
- **`LY_SUFFIX_SOLVER`** (-ly→adverb) — FAILS via the seeded `-ly`-adjectives (friendly/silly/lovely/ugly/curly) + non-`-ly`-adverbs (fast/hard/well/late/soon/now/here); `FUNCTION_NOT_SUFFIX_DECIDABLE ≥ N`.
- **`PRECLASSIFIED_JAR_SOLVER`** (read a given label) — FAILS: `WORD_NOT_PRECLASSIFIED` (render payload carries NO class/pos for the describer or candidates).
- **`REQUEST_KEYWORD_SOLVER`** — FAILS, **explicitly run against MODE-3 rounds too** (`REQUEST_KEYWORD_DECORRELATED`); mode-3 prompt strips all POS keywords (no "thing/action/noun/verb"), uses a grammatical-SLOT frame ("Finish: 'The dog is ___.'" → `lovely` fits the predicate-adjective slot, `slowly` is ungrammatical-in-slot — the cue IS the target cognition).
- **`POSITION` / `FIRST` / `RANDOM`** baselines — under ceiling.
- Structural asserts: `DUAL_CLASS_WITNESS_PRESENT` (same lemma → opposite class in its two rounds), `ANSWER_DERIVED_NOT_STORED` (mutate the modified-token's POS → class flips; the dual-witness flip; describer-pos-corruption teeth — the grader NEVER reads the describer's own pos), `MULTI_CANDIDATE_PRESENT ≥ N`, ≥7 variety.
- **Wrong-tap re-show (no-reveal):** the thread SLIPS off (reduced-motion: fade), the picture does NOT change (the felt tell), Pip re-floats the word, and the re-show **points at the two candidate targets + names the FUNCTION** ("does `fast` tell about the **car** [a thing] or how she **ran** [an action]?") — never the class label. Legitimate because only function-decidable rounds ship.

(b) **Romance (es/it/pt/fr — `-mente`)** — `-mente` is FAR more reliable than `-ly` → the suffix-lie weakens; PIVOT the trap to **AGREEMENT** (adjective inflects gender/number with its noun, adverb is invariant) + Romance flat adverbs ("habla claro" / "corre rápido") as the apex. ## Cohort doctrine this game adds
**The ADJACENCY / CANDIDATE-SPACE lesson — PROGRAM-WIDE.** For any "tap the target / point at the right word" comprehension or grammar game, the load-bearing cheats live in the **CANDIDATE SPACE**, not the labeling — and they are INVISIBLE to solvers that test the stimulus in isolation or by ABSOLUTE position: **(1) ADJACENCY / position-relative-to-the-cue** — natural language glues a modifier/referent NEXT TO its target (English attributive-adjective-before-noun, manner-adverb-after-verb), so "tap the adjacent content word" passes ~95%+ WITHOUT the target cognition, and it SURVIVES dual-class/witness structure (adjacency is computed fresh per-round, blind to the witness). A `POSITION` solver that tests absolute index does NOT catch it — you need an explicit **`ADJACENCY_SOLVER`** (tap the cue's nearest content neighbour) forced to FAIL + an **`ADJACENCY_DECORRELATED`** assert (nearest == target ≤ chance, not ~95%). (2) **TWO-CANDIDATE BINARY DEGENERACY** — when the answer reduces to a one-bit choice between ~one-of-each-type, a blind guess is undetectable per-round and ONE weak correlated cue (adjacency OR **semantic collocation** — "which word does this make SENSE next to") pushes 50%→90% with no grammatical judgment. Fix the candidate SPACE: **≥3 content candidates with a same-type DECOY** (`MULTI_CANDIDATE_PRESENT≥N`) + a **`SEMANTIC_FIT_SOLVER`** (frozen collocation table) forced to FAIL via **both-plausible** items (the dual-class words). And the structural COROLLARY: forcing non-adjacency + multi-candidate + semantic-ambiguity + grade-legality SIMULTANEOUSLY collapses the qualifying corpus to a FEW sentence templates — so an objective-gold standard can still be CONTENT-YIELD-thin → MID-SIZE, and the post-fix gate ENFORCES it (it rejects the trivial padding). Generalizes to EVERY point-at-the-word game (referent/pronoun, modifies-which-word, find-the-evidence, antecedent): audit position-relative-to-the-cue + candidate plurality + semantic-collocation, not just the label surface. (Distinct from Game-72's SELF-CONSISTENCY-vs-VALIDITY: there the GOLD was contestable [needs a human panel]; here the gold is OBJECTIVE [the gate decides], but the SUPPLY of cheat-proof items is small — two different roads to MID-SIZE.)
