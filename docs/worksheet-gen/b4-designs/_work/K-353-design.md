## 4 Answer-hiding + uniqueness

- Base, F3: OPEN (lay pieces). No `verify()`; layout lints + the data gate only. The base prints the model WITH seams by design (K copies a model); F3 prints solid shadows, the tan polygons are NOT in the DOM (`mode:'silhouette'` emits one `<path>` from `silhouette()`), so nothing on the G1 page or in its HTML shows a solution.
- F2 compose: the outline is one `<path>` (white fill), the tans are absent from the DOM; the child draws the seam in pencil. Uniqueness is a DATA property: every compose figure carries `unique:true` and the node gate proves it by brute force (§5 gate, m: the 6 two-tan and 3 three-tan outlines have exactly ONE tiling by their tans; the large triangle from S+S+Q has TWO and is REFUSED; controls square-from-2S = 2, rectangle M+S+S = 2 behaved). A wrong seam is visibly wrong: it does not split the outline into the two (three) legend shapes.
- F4 missing tan: the hole is drawn as a `tealSoft` region with a 2 px dashed teal edge and NO `data-lcs-tan` (plain `data-lcs-hole`); the root stamps `data-lcs-missing="<class>"` (invisible, the house `data-lcs-*` convention). Exactly one chip carries that class; the other two chips are DIFFERENT classes (never the twin S or L), so congruence decides: the hole is congruent to exactly one class (areas L .25 / M .125 / S .0625 / Q .125 / P .125, and M, Q, P are non-congruent). The child circles a chip; a wrong circle is a chip whose shape does not fit the hole.
- F5 match: the target is a solid shadow; the three candidates are solutions (seams) of the SAME figure under transforms; exactly one candidate's normalised silhouette equals the target's. Rule for a valid distractor: its transform must lie OUTSIDE the figure's silhouette symmetry group (m, §5 table), otherwise its shadow equals the target's and it would be a second correct answer. This is the chirality rule the lock asks for: a mirror of a mirror-symmetric figure (tree, triangle, trapezoid, arrow, rectangle) has the same shadow and a flipped parallelogram inside, so it is NEVER a distractor; a mirror of an asymmetric figure (cat, rabbit, boat) is. `square` (all 8 symmetries) is refused for F5.
- F6 count: the seams are printed (the task is to count pieces), answers live in `answerBox` `data-lcs-answer` (triangles, squares) and are re-derived from the polygons' `data-lcs-class`.

**NEW `primitives/tangram.js`** (pure SVG, tokens only):

Tan set, unit square side 1, SVG axes (y down). Local polygons, reference vertex at the origin:

| id class | local polygon (unit) | area | kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| `L` (x2: L1 L2) | (0,0) (1,0) (0.5,0.5) : hyp along +x, apex +y | 0.25 | triangle |
| `M` | (0,0) (0.5,0) (0,0.5) : right angle at the origin | 0.125 | triangle |
| `S` (x2: S1 S2) | (0,0) (0.5,0) (0.25,0.25) | 0.0625 | triangle |
| `Q` | (0,0) (0.25,0.25) (0,0.5) (-0.25,0.25) : diamond, vertex at the origin | 0.125 | square |
| `P` | (0,0) (0.25,0.25) (0.25,0.75) (0,0.5) : long sides vertical; CHIRAL | 0.125 | parallelogram |

Placement `{id, x, y, rot, flip}`: `flip` mirrors local x before rotation (legal on P only; the gate rejects it elsewhere); `rot` in 45 degree steps applies the SVG matrix (x cos - y sin, x sin + y cos) (clockwise on screen); then translate by (x, y); then multiply by S. `h = sqrt2/4 = 0.353553`.

Classic square (the template): `L1 (0,0,0) · L2 (1,0,90) · M (0,1,270) · Q (0.5,0.5,0) · S1 (1,1,180) · S2 (0.25,0.75,270) · P (0,0,0)` (m: area 1.000, 0 overlap). Two reusable half-assemblies: **H(ox,oy)** = the five small tans as the right triangle (0,0)(1,1)(0,1): `M (ox,oy+1,270) · Q (ox+.5,oy+.5,0) · S1 (ox+1,oy+1,180) · S2 (ox+.25,oy+.75,270) · P (ox,oy,0)`; **SMALLSQ(ox,oy)** = the five small tans as an axis-aligned square of side 2h: `M (ox+h,oy+h,45) · Q (ox+2h,oy,45) · S1 (ox+2h,oy+2h,225) · S2 (ox+h,oy,135) · P (ox+h,oy,45)`.

API:
- `placeTans(tiling, S)` -> `[{id, cls, pts:[[px,py]..], area}]` (px, origin = bbox min minus the stroke pad `sp = stroke/2 + 1`).
- `silhouette(tiling)` -> `[loop]` (unit coords). Algorithm (m, this is the one that traced all 30 tilings): orient every polygon to positive signed area; split every edge at every vertex of every polygon that lies on it (|cross| < 1e-9, 0 < t < 1); drop segment pairs that are equal and opposite (key = endpoints at 6 decimals, |v| < 5e-7 snapped to 0); chain the survivors, and at a vertex with several outgoing segments take the one with the LARGEST signed turn from the incoming direction (the tightest turn toward the interior; this is what fixes pinch vertices, e.g. the boat's hull/sail corner); finally drop collinear vertices. Assert sum of loop areas = tan-area sum (1e-9).
- `tangramFigure({figure|tans, S, mode, missing, stroke})`, modes: `template` (cream tans, 3 px teal seams + edge), `solution` (cream tans, 2 px teal seams, 3 px teal outline; `data-lcs-tan` + `data-lcs-class` per polygon), `outline` (one `<path>` of the silhouette, white fill, 3 px teal, NO tan polygons), `silhouette` (one `<path>`, `T.teal` fill, 3 px teal stroke round joins, no seams), `hole` (solution minus `missing`: the six polygons as in solution, the hole as `<path data-lcs-hole>` fill `T.tealSoft` stroke 2 px dashed `8 6` teal, root `data-lcs-missing=<class>`). Root: `svgRoot` sized to bbox x S + 2 sp, `data-ws-content data-lcs-prim="tangram" data-lcs-figure data-lcs-mode data-lcs-scale data-lcs-tans=<count>`.
- `transformTiling(tiling, t)` for `t in {id, rot90, rot180, rot270, flipLR, flipTB}` (rotate about the origin, add the step to `rot`, toggle `flip` on P for the mirrors AND mirror the placement; re-bbox).
- `symmetryGroup(tiling)` -> the subset of the 8 transforms whose silhouette equals the original's as a point set after bbox normalisation (6 decimals).
- `tanGlyph(cls, Sg)` -> inline svg icon in the canonical pose: L hyp horizontal apex up (Sg x Sg/2), M right angle bottom-left (Sg/2 square), S hyp horizontal apex up (Sg/2 x Sg/4), Q axis-aligned square (0.354 Sg), P long sides horizontal leaning right (0.75 Sg x 0.25 Sg); cream fill, 2 px teal; `data-lcs-glyph=<cls>`. Sizes are RELATIVE by construction (an S glyph is half an L glyph), which is the only way a child tells big from small without a word.
- `tanLegend({rows:[{cls,color}], Sg:64})` -> the 5-row swatch + glyph block.
- Minimum sizes asserted in the type gate (not the primitive): smallest tan edge >= band floor; F4 chips 100 x 56 >= 44; F5 boxes 160.
