## 2. Concept & creative hook
**Bo the bunny** is stocking his pantry for winter. He gathers wild berries and **packs them into crates** — and a **crate holds exactly ten** (it's a sealed, latched wooden crate stamped with a ten-leaf cluster; *you can't see or count the berries inside — a crate just IS a ten*). Up in the lavender dusk float **pantry shelves**, each already stocked with a hoard of crates + loose berries. Bo's slingshot sends his berries up to the shelf that matches. The agency hook: **a crate is one big ten — pack Bo's berries the smart way and sling them home before the snow.**
- **THE UN-COUNTABLE CRATE (the critic's §9 one-move fix — the count-match-killer):** a ten-crate is a **single sealed composite icon worth ten** — NOT a 2×5 grid of countable berries. A child literally cannot count berries inside it; they must treat the crate as **one unit that means ten**. This (a) kills the count-match cheat (counting "4 crates" only gets you to 47 if you know each crate is worth ten — a count-non-unitizer thinks 4 crates = 4 things), (b) solves 280px legibility (a crate is 1 element, not 10 dots), (c) locks the Game-16 separation (a sealed un-counted unit is categorically not a counted ten-frame). Loose berries = ones (arranged in a 5-frame so 7 reads as "five-and-two," subitizable). **NO numerals on any shelf.**
- **THE UNITIZING SPINE — VALUE-rounds where count-match DIVERGES (the critic's load-bearing fix):** the prompt is usually a **VALUE, not a tidy numeral whose digits you can map to pile-sizes** — so a count-match child (who maps digit↔pile-count without knowing crate=ten) FAILS:
  - **PACK-THE-PILE (the core):** Bo dumps a loose pile of berries (a quantity — capped small + in 5-frames so it's subitizable, e.g. 34 = six 5-frames + 4); the child picks the shelf that **packs it into the right crates-and-loose** (3 crates + 4). A count-match child has NO digits to map → must KNOW crate=ten to compute a shelf's value and match the pile → fails without unitizing.
  - **MORE / FEWER value-comparison (the ten-dominates insight, a touch of 1.NBT.B.3):** "which shelf is MORE — 2 crates + 9 loose [29], or 4 crates + 1 loose [41]?" A count-non-unitizer picks the one with more LOOSE berries (9); a unitizer knows 4 crates = forty > twenty. Catches count-match.
  - **BUNDLE / how-many-tens:** "Bo wants to carry sixty in as FEW crates as he can — which shelf?" → 6 crates (value not digit).
- **THE NUMERAL-DECODE ENTRY TIER (honestly the count-match-able tier):** some rounds DO show a numeral pebble ("47") → match to the 4-crate-7-loose shelf. This is the accessible entry (a legit partial skill: the positions map to tens-and-ones) — but it is NOT the unitizing proof; the VALUE-rounds carry that. Every heterogeneous numeral round still carries the REVERSAL distractor (74).
- **THE LAUNCH IS COMMIT-THEN-DELIVER (the aim-skill-killer):** the child **selects** the target shelf — via a coarse directed lock (swing the sling across discrete detents that snap to each floating shelf — you *point*, no precision) OR a tap / keyboard-arrow equivalent — and the **THWANG launches a CSS-keyframe arc to the LOCKED shelf** (right or wrong). The launch reads ONLY the locked shelf (no pointer geometry in correctness) → an aim-skill solver locks a shelf without decoding → launches a perfect arc into the WRONG shelf → FAILS. **No motor-failure is possible** (any pull fires; the arc always lands on the locked shelf — a wrong answer is always a wrong READING, never a wrong aim).
- **THE PAYOFF (the celebration PERFORMS the decode — per-locale):** correct → the crates **crack open and count by TENS as they pop** ("ten… twenty… thirty…" — universal, no ordering conflict), the loose berries tumble, and Bo names the whole number (`_NUMBER_WORD_HELPERS`). **Per-locale (the de/nl/da/no fix):** for ones-first number-word locales (German "siebenundvierzig"), the count-up does NOT build "forty-one, forty-two…" (which fights the word order) — the crates count by tens, then Bo speaks the whole word once. Native-confirmed (§8).
- **Anti-generic statement:** NOT a slingshot aiming-toy + place-value cards — (a) the un-countable crate makes unitizing structurally load-bearing (a crate IS one ten, uncountable); (b) the VALUE-rounds make count-match diverge + FAIL (gate-proven); (c) the launch never grades motor skill (commit-then-deliver, no-shame); (d) the celebration performs the place-value count (per-locale); (e) a board ships only if a **digit-match solver** (no numerals to read) + an **aim-skill solver** (launches to its locked wrong shelf) + a **reversal solver** (0%) + the **COUNT-MATCH solver** (chance on the value-rounds — the unitizing proof) all FAIL while a **unitize solver** passes (§8).

## 3. Core loop & the 4 genuine cognition-lanes (+ honest ≥7)
**Loop (`stage`: read → lock → launch):** Bo presents the prompt (a pile / a value-question / a numeral) → the child DECODES/UNITIZES + LOCKS the matching shelf (coarse detent or tap/keyboard) → the THWANG launches the arc to the locked shelf → correct → the crates count-by-tens + pop + Bo names the number → the pantry stocks one cubby → repeat → `ctx.correct()`. **No fail, no timer, no score, no Check button.** A wrong lock → the pebble floats back VERTICALLY (non-directional) + a NON-leaking re-decode prompt ("that shelf holds seven crates — count again for Bo's berries"; **never speaks the pebble's value**); targets stay, POSITIONS reshuffle on retry (spatial memory ≠ reading). **First-attempt-correct stocks the pantry** (blind-cycling through shelves does NOT fill a cubby — re-tries are "Bo waits," no fail-state, no score, but the reward tracks first-read success). First-attempt is the internal signal (never shown).

**The genuine PLACE-VALUE cognition-lanes (the critic's honest 4; the VALUE-lanes are the unitizing proof; "match 47 vs 53" = number-variant BLOCKLISTED; blocks-vs-expanded-vs-words = surface forms of ONE decode). Each carries a declared `lane`:**
| # | Lane (`lane`) | Prompt → act | Why distinct + unitizing-status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ★ | **Unitize-decode** (`decode` — VALUE-led core + a numeral entry tier) | a loose pile (value, capped/5-frames) OR a numeral → lock the crates-and-loose shelf | the core decode; the PILE-prompt is **count-match-PROOF** (no digits → must know crate=ten); the numeral sub-tier is the count-match-able entry |
| 2 ★ | **Decade / zero-ones** (`decade` — the trailing-zero understanding, 1.NBT.B.2.c) | 40/70 → lock the 4-crates-ZERO-loose shelf; reject the "4 crates + a stray loose" near-miss | n tens and ZERO ones — a distinct concept; the empty loose-row is load-bearing |
| 3 ★ | **Bundle / how-many-tens** (`unitize-count` — value not digit; the purest unitizing, 1.NBT.B.2.a) | "carry sixty in as FEW crates as possible" → 6 crates; reject the 60-loose pile (right total, wrong grouping) | the grouping, not the total — **count-match-PROOF** (the 60-loose pile has the right count of berries, only "sixty = six tens" wins) |
| 4 ★ | **Reverse-decode + value-compare** (`encode` — hoard → numeral; MORE/FEWER) | a fixed crate-hoard → grab the right NUMERAL (reversal + a 503 three-digit-drift-guard distractor); OR "which shelf is MORE" | the reversed direction + the ten-dominates comparison (a touch of 1.NBT.B.3) — **count-match-PROOF** (value, not digit-count) |

- **Honest framing (the critic's de-padding + §A.13.60):** **4 genuine cognition-lanes** — three of them (decode-via-pile / unitize-count / encode-compare) are the **count-match-PROOF, unitizing-load-bearing** core; the numeral-decode sub-tier is the count-match-able entry. **CUT** (the critic): teen-must-be-bundled (a number-variant of `decode` — teens demoted into the decode pool, never a named lane) + regroup-readiness "4 tens 12 ones = 52" (**1.NBT.C DRIFT** — the same out-of-band reach as the excluded one-more-ten). The **≥7 distinct ROUNDS** are met as a DATA requirement (§A.13.60: ≥7 distinct rounds the child sees) by populating the 4 lanes × varied numbers + varied prompt-forms (pile / value-question / numeral) + the mandatory distractor sets — NOT by dressing number-variants as cognition-lanes. The variety gate keys on `lane` + prompt-form with a **BLOCKLIST**: "differs only in the number / a different berry-skin / a teen vs a non-teen / blocks-vs-expanded-vs-words surface" do NOT count. Order-only reshuffle.
- **Free slice = pack-the-pile decode (1) + unitize-count (3) + decade (2)** — the unitizing-load-bearing core visible immediately.

## 5. Pedagogy
- **1.NBT.B.2 instantiation (unitizing load-bearing):** the child decodes a two-digit quantity into tens-and-ones treating a ten as ONE composite unit worth ten — proven by the VALUE-rounds (pack-the-pile, unitize-count, value-compare) where a count-non-unitizer (crate = "one thing") cannot win. The un-countable crate makes "a crate IS one ten" structurally required.
- **THE KEYSTONE RULES (non-cheatability — the critic's fixes):** (i) **UN-COUNTABLE COMPOSITE CRATE** — a sealed "one ten" icon, not a countable grid; counting crates only reaches the value if you know crate=ten (kills count-the-berries-inside + the Dewey-collapse). (ii) **VALUE-ROUNDS WHERE COUNT-MATCH DIVERGES** — the pile-prompt (no digits to map), the unitize-count (right-total-wrong-grouping), the value-compare (ten-dominates) → a count-match solver scores at CHANCE (the unitizing proof). (iii) **BLOCKS-ONLY TARGETS + MANDATORY REVERSAL** — no numerals on shelves; the 74-for-47 distractor every heterogeneous round. (iv) **COMMIT-THEN-DELIVER** — the place-value lock is gated; the launch always lands cleanly + never grades motor skill (an aim-skill solver hits its locked wrong shelf). (v) **NO ANSWER-LEAK + FIRST-ATTEMPT-PANTRY** — the miss-prompt never speaks the pebble's value; blind-cycling 4 shelves doesn't stock the pantry. (vi) **≥4 SHELVES** with a designed distractor set (correct + reversal + single-place near-miss + value/grouping trap).
- **Count-match is the digit-match cheat one level down (the critic's deep catch):** on STANDARD-form numeral→blocks matching, count-match (crate-count ↔ tens-digit, no crate=ten value) and unitizing are observationally identical — so the numeral-decode tier cannot prove unitizing. The proof lives in the VALUE-rounds where value ≠ digit-count. This is why the spine is VALUE-led, not numeral-led.
- **also_teaches (re-based per the pedagogy ruling):** primary **1.NBT.B.2** (a/b/c); a noted touch of **1.NBT.B.3** (compare two-digit by tens-and-ones — the value-compare lane, in service of B.2's ten-value); **STRIP RF.K.3.a + RF.K.2.d** (phonics — pure drift, the game emits ZERO phonics signal), **DROP 2.NBT.A.3** (three-digit to 1000; this is within-100; the 503 distractor in the encode lane GUARDS against three-digit drift, it does not teach it).
- **Number ranges (pedagogy):** **within 100, LED BY DECADES + heterogeneous two-digit** (47, 63, 85 — tens≠ones so reversal distractors bite); **teens (11-19) DEMOTED** into the decode pool (framed as decode "1 ten 3 ones," never as Game-16 ten-frame building). PILE-prompt values capped for 280px legibility (≤~40, 5-framed). No three-digit.
- **Distinctness from Game 16 + match-pairs:** Game 16 BUILDS teens on a ten-frame (compose); this READS pre-formed hoards (decode/match) with an un-countable crate (categorically not a counted ten-frame). vs match-pairs: cross-representation decode (value/numeral ↔ block-hoard) + the place-value celebration (NOT the trajectory — honestly, the launch is a deterministic function of the locked choice). If the game could be won by count-matching piles to digits, it's hollow — REJECTED (the VALUE-rounds + the count-match gate solver are the guard).
- **No-shame + §20.8 demonstrate-don't-touch:** the catalog aim-and-match only TOUCHED two-digit numbers (a dexterity + digit-match surface); the un-countable-crate VALUE-rounds DIRECTLY instantiate unitizing — the gate (digit-match + aim-skill + reversal + count-match solvers all fail; unitize passes) is the structural proof. The launch never punishes motor skill; the miss never leaks; the crate-count celebration teaches the ten-value.

## 8. Runtime mapping + MEASURED build-gate + PER-LOCALE

  2. **COUNT-MATCH solver scores at CHANCE (the unitizing proof — the critic's deepest fix):** a solver that maps prompt-digit↔crate-count + ones-digit↔loose-count WITHOUT knowing crate=ten must FAIL on every VALUE-round (pile: no digits to map; unitize-count: right-total-wrong-grouping; value-compare: ten-dominates).
