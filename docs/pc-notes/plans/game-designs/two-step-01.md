## §1 — Concept / world
A warm gatekeeper (Pip the lock-keeper) tends a sanctuary **GATE with TWO STACKED LOCKS**. A two-step word problem is the gate's puzzle: the **TOP lock holds the INTERIM** (step-1's result — the carved "HOLD token"), the **BOTTOM lock holds the FINAL**. The gate opens when both are set true; the animals stroll into a **non-numeric meadow** (ambient abundance — never a count/score). Numbers are entered on a tap **number-builder dial** (tens/ones steppers, ≥44px) as real DOM `<text>` — NEVER counted off sprites. Warm, no-shame: a wrong lock springs back and the story RE-TELLS (paraphrased, one rung lower), never a beep/fail.

## §2 — The standard + the load-bearing cognition
**2.OA.A.1 (primary)** — add AND subtract within 100 to solve TWO-STEP word problems. **2.OA.B.2 (also_teaches)** — fluency within 20 (the honest vehicle: two within-20 sub-steps per round). NOT 2.NBT.B.5 (two-digit regrouping is the vehicle, not the goal). **Number band (STRUCTURE is the load, not computation):** each sub-step within-20-friendly, **interim ≤50, final ≤100 target 30-80**.
**The load-bearing cognition = the CHAINING**, and — per the critic — its two irreducible sub-acts are **(a) SELF-SEGMENTING** the single situation into two ordered steps + deciding which quantity is the interim, and **(b) FORMING + HOLDING** the interim across the problem. These two are what 2.OA.A.1 actually assesses; single-fact arithmetic is not.

## §3 — THE THREE-TIER ARCHITECTURE (the rework; the heart of the game)
The critic's deepest catch: the sealed two-lock apparatus **PERFORMS the load-bearing cognition for the child** — it announces "two steps," enforces the order, presents each step as its own labeled one-step, and holds the interim on-screen — so a child who can do two HANDED one-steps in a forced order passes WITHOUT ever self-segmenting or holding. The tier ladder was cosmetic (same apparatus, only prose changed); the gate was blind (all solvers presume a pre-segmented descriptor). **A permanently-collapsed step-1 = the LIVE one-step product = a fence breach.** The fix splits the ANSWER SURFACE by tier so the envelope TEACHES but a distinct surface ASSESSES:

- **TEACH tier (S3 join→join, S1 anchor).** The sealed two-lock EXACTLY as designed (two labeled prompts, step-2 occluded `?` until the interim commits, HOLD token visible). This DEMONSTRATES the structure + keeps the sealed-envelope's anti-back-fill value. Explicitly **non-assessing** of self-segmentation.
- **SELF-SEGMENT tier (load-bearing — S1/S2/S4/S5 + the S7 keystone).** The WHOLE un-segmented situation is shown as one prose block. Before any lock/dial, the child commits a **graded PLAN on a NON-LEAKING surface**: build `[quantity] [+ / −] [quantity] → my FIRST step` from **N tappable numeral tokens that INCLUDE 1-2 distractor quantities** (named in the prose but not operands; compare-rounds supply the trap `3 = 8−5`) **+ illegal operand-pairings**. NOT a 2-option fork → a syntactic-first-pair guess can't reliably hit. Only after a CORRECT plan does the dial appear for the interim, then (sealed) the final. The PLAN is the graded `SEGMENTATION_IS_GRADED` answer; a wrong plan re-tells one rung lower.
- **WORKING-MEMORY-HOLD tier (top of S7 + a fraction of S1/S2).** Self-segment PLUS the carved HOLD token **FADES** once step-2's dial is active (reduced-motion-safe: it goes into Pip's pocket — a static occluded state, NOT animate-vanish; `aria-hidden` + removed from tab order). Tap-to-peek (visual) / an "ask Pip to remind me" button (SR parity) is allowed, no fail/cost, but is **soft-logged** → the gate offers a same-rung re-tell instead of advancing the no-peek mastery path. The child carries the interim.
- **Per-round RESETTING ladder.** Each round STARTS at the child's current mastery rung (default: self-segment), drops ONE rung on a miss FOR THAT ROUND ONLY, and the NEXT round resets to the assessment rung — so a struggling child is never silently demoted to a permanent one-step-scaffold experience; the assessment tier keeps being re-presented (`LADDER_RESETS_PER_ROUND` + `ASSESSMENT_RUNG_REPRESENTED`). No rung/counter shown to the child.

## §4 — Anti-cheat architecture (the gate)
### §4.1 The deepest cohort cheat — PRE-SEGMENTATION / SCAFFOLD-DOES-THE-STRUCTURING
An anti-cheat SCAFFOLD (the sealed envelope) that PRE-STRUCTURES the problem can PERFORM the standard's cognition for the child (the Game-44/46 cosmetic-fade lineage; §20.8 demonstrate-don't-touch). The obvious anti-back-fill fix (seal step-2) is what CREATES the deeper breach (the seal pre-segments). The build-gate is blind because every solver presumes a pre-segmented descriptor. **Fix = the §3 three-tier split** + the new solver below.
### §4.2 Closed shallow cheats (sealed-envelope + graded-interim + keyword-neutralization)
- **SINGLE_PASS / BACK_FILL** — killed on the teach/sealed tier (step-2's operand withheld until interim commits → no single input touches all three numbers; nothing to back-derive from). On the assess tier the PLAN-then-gated-dial flow keeps them dead.
- **KEYWORD** — SAME verb for both operations + ~40% inconsistent-language rounds; the operation lives ONLY in the descriptor `op`, never the verb. **Compare-first (the "5 FEWER → step-1 ADDS" anchor) capped ~15-20%, single-elevated-load, arithmetic pinned within-10**, never co-occurring with within-100-hard or S7 (the critic's anti-three-loads ruling; native-reviewed per locale).
- **BRUTE_TAPPER** — no fail-state (operator-locked) so brute can't be made impossible; made **slower-than-reading**: a wrong commit FORCES an interactive re-told-story interstitial (tap-through) before the dial re-arms (state change `STEP1_OPEN → RETELL → STEP1_OPEN` the brute loop can't skip). Two-action deliberate commit (set THEN press the lock).
- **COUNT-VISIBLE-OBJECTS (Game-54 residual)** — `ABUNDANCE_DECOUPLED` (the meadow vessel's cardinality is driven by a +1-per-completed-round counter NEVER read from any quantity, clustered/overlapping so it shows "many" not exactly-N) + `STORY_ART_NON_COUNTABLE` (story art shows the situation — a basket, Pip — never a countable set equal to a prose quantity; quantities live ONLY in DOM/SVG `<text>`).
### §4.3 Order-load + S7 (made genuinely load-bearing, not decorative)
- **ORDER-LOAD** — the critic killed the decorative "reverse-goes-negative" (narrative order makes subtract-first obvious). Genuine order-load rounds **decouple narrative order from operation order** ("Maya will give away 15; she has 12 and bakes 20 more first") so subtract-first is a LIVE tempting error the negative-tripwire actually catches. `ORDER_LOAD_BEARING` asserts narrative-order ≠ operation-order. Small fraction; drop the claim per-round if it can't be made genuine.
- **S7 missing-interim** — a DISTINCT state-machine branch: the final is GIVEN in prose + bottom lock pre-carved, step-2's quantity GIVEN, the child produces the TOP lock (the interim) that makes it consistent. The strongest anti-back-fill round (nothing to back-fill from — the interim IS the ask). `S7_INTERIM_IS_GRADED_ANSWER`.
### §4.4 The MEASURED build-gate `verify-two-step-core.js` (window-shim — drives the REAL core)
- **ORACLE** PASSES (computes interim+final from the event chain).
- **MUST-FAIL solvers:** `SINGLE_PASS_SOLVER` · `BACK_FILL_INTERIM_SOLVER` · `KEYWORD_SOLVER` · `BRUTE_TAPPER` · **`SCAFFOLD_DEPENDENT_SOLVER` / `PRE_SEGMENTED_SOLVER`** (does single facts + follows the enforced order but CANNOT self-segment + needs the interim displayed → MUST score 100% on the TEACH tier [proves the scaffold works] and **≤ chance on the SELF-SEGMENT + HOLD tiers** [proves those require the child's structuring]) · **`COUNT_SPRITE_SOLVER`** (reads every on-screen collection's rendered count) · FIRST · RANDOM.
- **Structural asserts:** `ANSWER_DERIVED_NOT_STORED` (+ mutations: flip op → interim+final move; bump step-2 qty → only final moves; **`REORDER_EVENTS` → interim changes**; **`ADD_DISTRACTOR_QUANTITY` → canonical plan unchanged but PRE_SEGMENTED_SOLVER drops**) · `INTERIM_REQUIRED_AND_NOT_BACK_DERIVABLE` · `OPERATIONS_NOT_KEYWORD_RECOVERABLE` · `TWO_STEP_NOT_COLLAPSIBLE` · `SEGMENTATION_IS_GRADED` · `PLAN_NOT_SYNTACTIC_RECOVERABLE` (canonical first pair ≠ the textually-first two numerals in ≥40% of self-segment rounds) · `ORDER_LOAD_BEARING` · `S7_INTERIM_IS_GRADED_ANSWER` · `ABUNDANCE_DECOUPLED` · `STORY_ART_NON_COUNTABLE` · `HOLD_TIER_TOKEN_NOT_IN_A11Y_TREE_WHEN_FADED` · `LADDER_RESETS_PER_ROUND` · within-100 band (interim ≤50, final ≤100) · ≥7 variety floor.

## Cohort doctrine this game adds
**The TEACH-TIER-vs-ASSESS-TIER split (the scaffold-performs-the-standard lesson) — PROGRAM-WIDE.** An anti-cheat SCAFFOLD that PRE-STRUCTURES a problem (segments it, enforces the order, holds the interim on-screen) can PERFORM the standard's load-bearing cognition FOR the child — and the build-gate is BLIND to it because every adversarial solver presumes the pre-structured descriptor the scaffold produces (the solvers test arithmetic-bypass, not whether the child did the STRUCTURING the scaffold already did). This is the Game-44/46 cosmetic-fade lineage at the problem-STRUCTURE layer: the very fix for the obvious cheat (seal step-2 to stop back-fill) over-scaffolds and performs the cognition. **The fix: split the ANSWER SURFACE by tier — the scaffold TEACHES (demonstrates the structure, keeps its anti-cheat value) but a DISTINCT non-leaking surface ASSESSES the child's OWN structuring (a graded segmentation/plan the child produces from a surface with distractor inputs + illegal pairings, NOT a handed labeled step), with the scaffold's externalizations (the displayed interim) FADED on the assessment tier; and add a `SCAFFOLD_DEPENDENT_SOLVER` that MUST pass the teach tier and FAIL the assess tier — the structural proof the tier split is real, not cosmetic.** Generalizes to ANY game whose mechanic pre-structures the problem the standard asks the CHILD to structure (multi-step, sequencing, sorting-into-given-bins, fill-the-given-frame): if removing the apparatus would leave the child unable to do the task, the apparatus is performing the standard — assess on a surface that doesn't.
