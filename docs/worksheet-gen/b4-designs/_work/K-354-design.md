## 1 Page concept (base)

One drawn child, standing, arms a little out, the height of the page. Six empty label lanes, three per side, each tied by a straight teal leader to a coral ring on a part of the body. A word bank of six body-part words on top. The child copies each word onto the lane whose leader ends on that part. Pencil first, one apparatus, whitespace around the figure, no picture library art on the base (the figure IS the art). Quality comes from the figure: clean 3 px teal outline, no skin colour, no gender markers, hair as a cap with three strand lines so it reads in mono, five countable fingers and five toe bumps per side.

## 4 Answer-hiding + uniqueness

The bank prints the six words; the lanes are EMPTY; the mapping word -> lane is the only answer and lives in `data-lcs-label="<part>"` on each lane (never text). Each part has exactly one lane and one ring, so every word has exactly one correct lane. A wrong answer is visibly wrong: the teacher follows the leader from the written word to the ring and sees it sits on the wrong part. Verify: bank set <=> lane set (bijection); exactly 3 lanes per side; every pointer end within 2 px of the primitive's anchor for its part and inside the figure bbox; the sweep asserts no leader intersects another target's 16 px disc and no two leaders intersect (poison: swap the sides of head and arm -> FAIL); lanes contain no text; no `<img>` on the page; `[data-lcs-region]` count 12 (nothing hidden on the base); at least 4 of 6 targets in the lead set.
