## The four operator rulings that define v4 (asked and answered this session)

1. **Clean-sheet replacement.** Unbuilt v3 entries dead unless independently re-earned.
   ⚠ This kills **L2 Asking Bench (was in progress)** and every grammar-based v3 entry.
2. **NO language-teaching tools of any kind** — not grammar, not phonics, not syllables, not
   vocabulary. Maths · measurement · geometry · data · time · money · spatial · science ONLY.
3. **Language-light universal** — identical in all 11 locales, almost no authored language.
4. **Free apparatus, PAID depth + record** (curated repertoire, saved setups, print).
5. (standing) **If the thing the tool teaches cannot be verified, do not design it.**

## ⭐ THE CONSEQUENCE NOBODY HAD STATED — the moat had to move

Rulings 2+3 **remove** the v3 thesis ("build what cannot be translated into existence"). A
language-light maths tool is cloneable in a week. **v4 thesis: the instrument is free, the
REPERTOIRE is the product, and the PROOF is the moat.** Three surviving moat locations:
1. **THE ROUTINE** — the world's best K-3 routines are rotting in presentation software (Splat's
   reveal does not work in Google Slides; Esti-Mysteries ship an animation-failure FAQ; Math
   Visuals still links Jamboards, dead Oct 2024; **wodb.ca is a parked domain**).
2. **THE REPERTOIRE** — which is also the paid layer, so depth IS the deliverable (answers the
   prior research's "depth not breadth" objection by construction rather than deferral).
   ⚠ **Repertoire must be 200+ per instrument, not wodb's 21** — a language-light catalog with
   wodb-sized repertoires has no moat at all.
3. **CORRECT-BY-CONSTRUCTION** — Amplify shipped WODB on Polypad with a **random generator, which
   cannot guarantee a valid WODB**. A provably-valid repertoire is work nobody can shortcut.

## ⭐ THE NEW DESIGN LAW — NO WORDS ON THE APPARATUS

Stage carries **numerals, icons and the material only**; authored language is chrome. Forced by
three independent things: (a) ruling 3; (b) **`LCSAudio` never calls `getVoices()` and silently
substitutes a wrong-language voice — TTS is reliable only in en/de/es/fr/it, so SIX OF ELEVEN
LOCALES GET SILENCE. No v4 tool may depend on hearing.** Sound may confirm, never carry;
(c) better pedagogy for pre-readers/EAL — an unclaimed default across every competitor.
**Acceptance test: legible with the sound off.** Mechanised as a gate: zero `<text>` nodes in
stage SVG other than numerals and unit marks.

### ⭐ THE LESSONS, each bought by a measurement rather than a reading

1. **⭐ THE GATE WAS MARKING ITS OWN HOMEWORK — 19 of 51 mutations survived.** It built its board
   corpus with the tool's own builder and then checked it with the tool's own predicates, so every
   predicate mutation stayed perfectly self-consistent. **Fix: the gate implements the six families
   ITSELF** (folding-sheet's M3 closed-form shadowing) and the tool must agree, 38,260 evaluations.
   ⚠ **And the gate's copies of `COARSE`/`MAX_DOTS` are HARDCODED** — reading them off the tool
   would make a mutation to them invisible.
2. **⭐ PROVABLY VALID IS NOT THE SAME AS WORTH TURNING OVER.** Greedy-by-strongest-cut produced
   240 decks averaging TWO cards — every one valid, none a routine. Now each card aims at a
   geometric step toward one.
3. **⭐ THE BUILDER FOUND "AT MOST 37" + "AT LEAST 37"** — a pincer that does not narrow, it says
   the number out loud. **Fixed in the APPARATUS, not the search:** magnitude bounds snap to a
   coarse grid (10s / 5s) and the dot card caps at 20 (you cannot show 87 dots). The degenerate
   move is now unrepresentable.
4. **⭐ THE PRECISE RULE IS "NO CARD MAY IMPLY ANOTHER", not one-per-family.** One-per-family fixed
   the nesting but left 78 targets unbuildable. Comparability (one survivor set ⊆ another) is the
   real cause: the contained card is redundant and the prune always takes it.
5. **⭐ THE MEAN HID A THIRD OF THE CORPUS COLLAPSING.** Dropping the no-implication guard leaves
   the mean at 2.84 — over any mean threshold — while 81/240 decks fall to two cards. **Assert the
   MEDIAN (≥4), not the mean.** Fix what is measured, never the threshold.
6. **A greedy that follows a schedule is myopic** — 22 awkward targets (11, 13, 47, 49, 97, 107)
   ran out of non-comparable cards. A deterministic **retry ladder [4,3,5,2,6]** leaves none behind.
7. **`Number(null)` is 0**, so `null` satisfied "at most five" by being treated as zero. A thing
   that is not a number survives nothing — `typeof n !== 'number'`, never coercion.
8. **THREE INERT MUTATIONS IN ONE SMALL FUNCTION.** Inverting both comparability masks is
   provably symmetric (`¬A⊆¬B ⟺ B⊆A`); loosening `_isolates`' final return is shadowed by its early
   exit, and loosening the early exit is shadowed by the final return. **An inert mutation is a bad
   mutation, not a gate hole** — the loop body was the part that carried the answer.
9. **The ban on the word `score` is right even for a search heuristic** — renamed the variable to
   `off` rather than loosening the regex.
10. **⭐ AN OR-SHAPED ASSERTION HID A MISSING FLOOR (2nd platform instance).** The tap-floor check
    read "the chip carries 44px OR anything carries 44px" — the card's own 44px satisfied it
    forever, so shrinking the chip was unfalsifiable. **Name every control class and check each on
    its own.**
11. **⭐ THE ID SPELLED THE ANSWER AND THE JSON STORED IT.** Boards were `s20-003` with a `target`
    field — the answer in a public file and in the id. **The target is now DERIVED from the cards**
    (`loadBoard` intersects), so a board whose answer disagrees with its own clues is
    unrepresentable, and ids are a plain sequence. ⚠ And the first id check banned "three digits in
    a row", which fired on `s100-05` — the FIELD SIZE. Structure, not pattern-matching.
12. **THE HARNESS IDENTIFIED THE BOARD BY A NON-UNIQUE KEY** (card count + field size) and reported
    a defect that was its own. **Drive the BUILDER on known targets** — deterministic, and it
    exercises the path most teachers use.
13. **FOUR DEFECTS ONLY LOOKING FOUND, every gate green throughout:** (a) `justify-content:center`
    on an overflow scroller **puts the start out of reach** — at 360 the field was clipped at BOTH
    ends and column one could not be scrolled to; use `margin:0 auto` on the CHILD. (b) Changing
    the field wiped the deck and left a teacher looking at 120 numbers with nothing to do.
    (c) TWO hint lines saying different things at once. (d) Mid-deck the hint fell through to the
    opening instruction while the marker was down and three cards were up — **mid-deck the tool
    must say nothing and wait.**
14. **A 12-ROW FIELD PLUS A DECK BELOW IT RAN 942px AT 1024×900.** Fixed structurally — field and
    deck side by side once there is room (and only when the field is tall, or the 1-20 field gets a
    wide empty band beside it: the sparse-layout defect). ⚠ **vh is the natural fix and is
    FORBIDDEN inside a manipulative** — its iframe grows to content, so a vh rule creates the
    feedback loop the shell has deliberately no path for.
15. **A 3-digit numeral at the 34px cell floor rendered 11.56px** — under the 14px legibility floor,
    and only on the 1-120 field. The floor is the platform's; the RATIO was what was wrong.
16. **⭐ THE DEAD-STRING RULE PAID AGAIN (4th platform instance) AND ONE WAS DEAD BY STATE.**

17. **⭐ CONCATENATING TWO AUTHORED STRINGS IS THE RECORDED LOCALISATION SMELL** — the gate line and
