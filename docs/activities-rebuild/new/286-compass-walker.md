## The skill
Know the four compass directions on a compass rose and use them to find things on a map.

## The apparatus and its invention
A town map made of library pictures placed on a grid, with a compass rose whose orange tip points north. A walker stands on a start picture. The child taps a direction on the rose (N/E/S/W letters are the content) and the walker's gaze beam shines that way across the map; the child taps the picture that lies in that direction. Rose mode: the rose is missing letters; the child drags E, S, W onto the arms, and the walker turns to face each one in turn.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** East and west swapped; north always meaning "up the page" even when the map's rose is turned (some rounds rotate the rose 90°).
- **Pictures:** Map landmarks: miscellaneous (house), tree (apple tree), vehicles (car, bus), camping (tent, lake, mountain), the compass rose is apparatus (camping/compass refused — its letters are English).
- **Settings:** Mode: label the rose / directions on the map · Rose: always up / turned
- **Rounds:** ≥ 8 rounds: start points and landmarks shuffled; directions balanced; three candidate pictures, one in the direction asked.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G2-369, G2-371
- **Distinct from:** Parking Tower uses above/below/next to; no activity uses compass directions.
- **Build risk:** Rose letters per locale: en N E S W; de N O S W; fr/es/it N E S O; pt N L S O; nl N O Z W; sv N Ö S V; da/no N Ø S V; fi P I E L. Turned-rose rounds (north not up) only in setting 2.
