## 2. Concept & creative hook
**Dot is a small honey-colored story-dragon whose BACK is the story: three soft plates along her spine, each a different SHAPE for a different JOB — a flat GROUND-plate (the SETTING, "where we start"), a raised TANGLED-KNOT plate over her heart (the PROBLEM, "the trouble"), and a smooth ARCH plate near her tail (the SOLUTION, "the fix"). The plates sit in a TRIANGLE, never a row — there is no "this comes first" line to read.** The child heals Dot by understanding what each moment is FOR: a moment only settles onto the plate whose JOB it matches.
- **THE PLATES ARE FUNCTION-SHAPED, NOT A TIME-LINE (the order-kill):** the triangle arrangement carries the role-MEANING (function), never the time-MEANING (order). The beat-cards arrive SHUFFLED (out of story order). A child reaching for "first card → setting" fails — because the deck is authored so the PROBLEM is sometimes introduced first + the SETTING revealed mid-story (role ≠ order).
- **THE SAME STORM IS THE WORLD IN ONE STORY, THE TROUBLE IN THE NEXT (the keyword-kill + the affect-matched fix):** across the deck the same picture-card plays DIFFERENT roles (a storm is the SETTING in story A, the PROBLEM in story B) — so no content-word/picture maps to a role. And the swing-pair storm is rendered with the SAME menace in BOTH (affect-matched) — so "this storm feels scarier → problem" is dead too (the critic's deepest fix: the affect-proxy can't ride the swing-pairs).
- **THE TROUBLE ISN'T ALWAYS THE SCARIEST PART (the affect-kill):** a tense SETTING (a spooky-but-fine forest the friends just live in) + a quiet SOLUTION (the lost thing quietly found) break the "most-dramatic→problem, calmest→setting, most-relief→solution" affect heuristic; the role is decided by what the beat DOES (establishes / disrupts / resolves), not how it FEELS. (PROBLEM stays affect-defended via the matched swing-pairs.)
- **NOT EVERY PART HAS A JOB (the decoy + the elimination-kill):** a 4th "just-for-fun" card belongs in NO plate (Mo scratches his nose) — it goes in Dot's cozy "extra" pocket (a rewarded positive home, not a stuck no-where). This makes "place 3 of 4 by elimination" impossible (the child must KNOW which is the decoy = comprehension), and Dot's spine blooms when the THREE plates are satisfied — over-declining never wins.
- **THE PAYOFF:** all three plates matched → Dot's spine snaps straight, she stretches + breathes a tiny three-star story-constellation into the dusk-sky (`GameCollection` — a sky filling with story-shapes; NEVER a count). `ctx.correct()`. A wrong plate → no fail; the card slides back into the SHUFFLED tray (not the next slot) + Dot asks a CAUSAL FUNCTION question ("does this part START our story, make the trouble, or make it BETTER?" — never the answer) + re-decide.
- **Anti-generic statement:** NOT sort-the-pictures — (a) the triangle (non-row) + the shuffle + role≠order + the `ROLE_BY_ORDER_SOLVER` kill the order-proxy; (b) same-content-different-role + the `ROLE_BY_KEYWORD/PICTURE_SOLVER` kill the keyword/picture-proxy; (c) the affect-matched swing-pairs + the affect-incongruent setting/solution + the GRADED PER-ROLE `AFFECT_SOLVER` kill the affect-proxy (the critic's deepest); (d) the load-bearing decoy + reshuffle-on-reject + the `ELIMINATION_SOLVER` kill the brute-force/elimination cheat; the gate proves order + keyword + picture + affect + elimination + the inherited solvers ALL fail while the role oracle passes.
