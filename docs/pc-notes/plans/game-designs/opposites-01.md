## 2. Concept & creative hook
**Quill the hedgehog trader runs the Mirror Market, where every word-card has a TWIN at the OTHER END — and Quill only seals a trade when you bring the twin that's the OPPOSITE, not just a friend.** A correct opposite clasps into Quill's Mirror-Book (the non-numeric abundance vessel).
- **THE TWIN-AT-THE-OTHER-END (the pedagogical wedge — F7 decoupled from "mirror-image"):** the market shelves words in twin-pairs (big↔small, open↔closed) so the child SEES that words come in opposite poles. But "opposite" means **the OTHER END of the same kind**, NOT a look-alike flip — the copy + feedback never say "mirror it / flip it backwards" (that mis-fires on full/empty, hot/cold); they say **"the other end, not just a friend."** The mirror is a memory hook, not the definition.
- **THE DECOY IS A SAME-KIND SIBLING (the critic's load-bearing fix — every categorical round):** Quill posts BIG; the crates offer **small** (opposite, CORRECT), **round** (same kind — a descriptor — but NOT the other end), and an unrelated word. The child must reject the same-kind sibling and bring the OTHER END. (For black → **white** vs **red**; up → **down** vs **sideways**; push → **pull** vs **lift**.) This makes "pick the same-dimension word" FAIL — only "find the reverse" wins. (Synonyms like huge are OUT — Grade-1 drift; gradable middles like warm are OUT — ambiguous.)
- **COMPLEMENTARY PAIRS ARE THE RECALL BACKBONE (capped):** open/closed, on/off, full/empty, day/night have no third pole, so the same-dimension word IS the opposite — these are honest RECALL rounds (rote IS the K standard), tagged + capped, never carrying the discrimination weight.
- **VERBS via single-frame + arrow (F2):** a verb round shows the action once (a hand PUSHING a box, arrow →) and asks for the OTHER way (pull, arrow ←) among a same-axis-non-opposite motion decoy (lift) — never a bare printed verb, never a fragile 2-frame guess.
- **THE PAYOFF:** a correct opposite clasps a facing-page spread into the Mirror-Book (`GameCollection` — abundance, never a count). `ctx.correct()`. A wrong trade → no fail; Quill peers and gives a **relation-branched** warm nudge (a same-kind sibling → "that's the same KIND — but the OTHER end?"; an associate → "that's a friend of the word — find its opposite"), the crate eases back, choices reshuffle.
- **Anti-generic statement:** NOT match-the-opposite-pair — (a) the same-dimension-sibling decoy on every categorical round makes a dimension-match heuristic FAIL (the critic's deepest fix); (b) rote complementary rounds are honestly capped recall (the standard IS partly recall); (c) **7 genuinely-distinct child-ACTIONS** (pick / route / judge-pair / scene-flip / generate / balance / verb-direction — operator chose invest over a §A.13.60 waiver); (d) verbs ride as a subject, not a fake distinctness axis; (e) the gate proves the `DIMENSION_MATCH_SOLVER` + association + familiar-pair + first/random solvers all FAIL on categorical rounds while the opposite-relation oracle passes.

## 3. Core loop & the 7 experiences (operator-chosen INVEST — 7 distinct child-ACTIONS)
**Loop (grasp the other-end → act):** Quill posts a word (pictured + printed, optional TTS) → the child performs the round's ACTION on the choices (each a same-dimension-sibling-defended set) → a correct opposite → Mirror-Book clasp + `ctx.correct()`; a wrong choice → a relation-branched nudge (F8) + reshuffle. **No timer/score/streak/countdown/fail/attempts/"X of Y".** `nextTask` reshuffle (order + distractor set).

**The 7 genuinely-distinct experiences (distinct = a different child-ACTION/reasoning — §A.13.60; verbs are a SUBJECT within these, NOT a separate experience):**
| # | Experience (`cog`) | The child's ACTION | Why genuinely distinct |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ★ | **pick-the-opposite** (`pick`) | tap the OTHER-END word among a same-dimension-sibling + an unrelated decoy | the core discrimination pick (reverse vs same-kind sibling) |
| 2 ★ | **two-door relation-router** (`route`) | a stream of words arrives; route each to the **"OPPOSITE of X"** door or the **"SAME KIND"** door | classify the RELATION itself as a sorting action (not pick one) — distinct from Game 27 (relation-judgment, not category) |
| 3 ★ | **odd-pair-out** (`oddpair`) | 3 word-pairs shown; tap the one that is NOT an opposite-pair (an associate pair: cat/dog) | pair-level relation JUDGMENT (one meta-level up) |
| 4 ★ | **fix-the-backwards-world** (`scene`) | a scene where multiple elements are the wrong state ("it's bedtime but the sun is UP, the lamp is ON, eyes OPEN"); tap each to flip it to its opposite until the scene matches | sustained multi-reversal scan-and-flip IN CONTEXT (not a single pick) |
| 5 ★ | **generate-the-opposite** (`generate`) | no canonical partner shown; from full-context scene-choices, PRODUCE the opposite of the target | production from context (no pre-shown pair to recognize) |
| 6 ★ | **balance-the-seesaw** (`balance`) | the target sits on the HIGH end; drag the card that LEVELS it — only the OPPOSITE balances; a same-axis sibling stays tilted | opposition as two-ends-in-tension; a drag-to-balance action (the same-dimension-sibling decoy is load-bearing — a sibling can't level it) |
| 7 ★ | **verb-direction-set** (`verb`) | a verb action shown once with an arrow (push →); set/aim the REVERSED action (pull ←) among a same-axis-non-opposite motion decoy (lift) | the dedicated VERB vehicle — set a direction (a distinct interaction), satisfying "verbs AND adjectives" without an adj/verb re-skin |
- **Honest framing (operator INVEST + critic F3):** 7 genuinely-distinct child-ACTIONS (pick / sort-stream-by-relation / judge-the-fake-pair / scene-multi-flip / produce / drag-to-balance / set-a-direction) — NOT the adjective/verb split (that would be the §A.13.60 padding the operator rejected). Verbs are concentrated in #7 and appear as SUBJECTS in #1/#4/#6. **The same-dimension-sibling decoy invariant (F-A) applies to ALL categorical rounds across ALL 7 actions** (the load-bearing anti-cheat is the decoy structure, not the interaction skin); complementary rounds are the capped recall exemption. `audit-game-variety.js` live 2-pass confirms ≥7 distinct ACTIONS + reshuffle.
- **Free slice = pick + two-door-router + balance-the-seesaw** — proves the discrimination across 3 distinct actions; the `DIMENSION_MATCH_SOLVER` + association + familiar-pair + first/random solvers all fail on the categorical rounds.

## 6. Interaction & UX
- **Runtime: post → act → clasp.** `opposites-core.js` holds the round `{ id, cog, target:{word,picture,oppositionId,pos:'adj'|'verb'}, choices:[{word,picture,relation:'opposite'|'same-dim-sibling'|'related'|'synonym'|'unrelated'}], complementary:bool }` + `oracle(round)` (DERIVES the answer as `choices.find(c=>c.relation==='opposite')`; **no `isCorrect`/`correctIndex` stored** — a hand-authored flag is IGNORED; `oppositionId` is gate-only, NEVER rendered). **The answer = `f(the opposite relation)`, never `f(same dimension)` / `f(most-associated)` / `f(familiar pair)` / `f(index)`.**
- **State separation (anti-cheat):** `snapshot()` exposes `{ cog, target (word+picture, no oppositionId), choices (word+picture+nothing-else), doors/scene/seesaw per cog }`. `facts()` exposes per-round `{ answerFromRelationNotStored, sameDimensionNonOppositeNonMiddleDecoyPresent (every categorical round), dimensionMatchSolverFails (categorical), complementaryTaggedAndCapped, noGradableMiddleAsChoice, oppositionIdNotRendered, verbRoundHasSameAxisNonOppositeMotion, oppositeIsNotMostAssociated, notAllFamiliarPairs, multiplePlausibleChoices, correctNotIndex0, choicesImageAnchored }`.
- **Act + commit (no Check button):** the child performs the round's ACTION (tap a word / route to a door / tap the fake pair / flip scene elements / produce / drag to the seesaw / set the verb arrow). A correct opposite → `ctx.correct()` auto-advance (the act IS the deliberate selection; the only feedback is the clasp or the relation-branched nudge — NOT a retry-checker). A wrong choice → the relation-branched nudge (F8) + reshuffle.
- **Mobile @280px (F2/§A.13.55):** vertical flex; choice grid `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(120px,1fr))` (NEVER fixed columns — the keypad lesson), ≥44px tiles, real DOM `<text>` words with `overflow-wrap:anywhere` (DE compounds); the verb single-frame+arrow + destination tiles stack VERTICALLY <360px (no side-by-side 2-frame under the legibility floor); the two-door router = two stacked drop-zones + tap-to-assign (drag is enhancement); the seesaw = a `<line>` rotating via transform; the scene = one scaling `<svg>` + tappable state-elements. `audit-game-mobile.js` empty/mid/answered at 280-768.
- **Accessibility (SR first-class — no rhyme-game tension):** picture buttons `role=button` + `aria-label=word` (the SR child hears/reads the words + picks the opposite — fully on-standard); optional whole-word TTS (support, not load-bearing); full keyboard (tab choices/doors/scene-elements/seesaw; Enter=act; arrows within a grid; the seesaw + doors + scene-flips are Enter/Space-operable); verbs presented as `aria-label`=word + a described action; reduced-motion (instant swaps); mute-safe (print+picture carry everything).
