# Curriculum research — six systems, ages 5–9 (US · England · Germany · France · Netherlands · Finland)

Source file for GAME-DESIGN-BRIEF.md §4.2. Researched 2026-09-05 from official curriculum documents (fetched and read in full where the host allowed it) plus reliable secondary summaries where the official host blocked fetches. Every claim carries an inline URL. Ages are the age at the START of the school year (a child in England's Year 1 is 5 turning 6).

Conventions: "—" = not expected in that system by age 9 (or not specified at national level). Where a system defines content per 2-year block (Bavaria Klasse 1/2 and 3/4; Finland grades 1–2 and 3–6) the block is stated and the conventional year split is noted as such.

## 1. United States — Common Core (CCSS-M, CCSS-ELA) + NGSS

**Status.** CCSS-M and CCSS-ELA (2010) remain the reference standards in most states (some states have re-branded copies). Science is not in Common Core; NGSS (2013) is the de-facto national reference. Cursive is *not* in CCSS; by July 2024, 25 states had legislated cursive instruction, typically by grade 3–5 ([EdWeek 2024](https://www.edweek.org/policy-politics/the-number-of-states-that-require-schools-to-teach-cursive-is-growing/2024/11)).

### 1.1 Start ages
- Kindergarten at 5 (Sept-1 cutoff in 35 states) ([ECS](http://www.ecs.org/clearinghouse/79/58/7958.pdf)). Formal reading (phonics) and written arithmetic (equations) both begin in K ([RF.K.3](https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/RF/K/), [K.OA](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/K/OA/)).

### 1.5 Notable US divergences
Imperial + metric units side by side from grade 2; money is a distinct strand (coins with names, not decimal); fractions as a full number-line concept at 8 (earlier "halves/fourths" only as shape partition); no national handwriting/cursive beyond printing; light & sound taught at 6 (earliest of the six); tables "from memory" only at 8; no national reading test.

## 2. England — National Curriculum (2014) + EYFS + phonics screening check

**Status.** Programmes of study 2014 remain statutory. The Curriculum and Assessment Review reported in Nov 2025; assessment arrangements for 2026–27 stay unchanged but "future assessment arrangements are under review" ([gov.uk phonics screening check collection, updated 1 Sep 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/phonics-screening-check-administration)). A revised EYFS framework applies from September 2026 ([EYFS 2026 PDF](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a5f8e4fb00f3323bf1a23a1/EYFS_group_and_school_based_from_September_2026.pdf)).

### 2.1 Start ages
Reception at 4 (EYFS), Year 1 at 5, Year 2 at 6 (KS1), Year 3 at 7 (KS2). Formal reading = systematic synthetic phonics from Reception (ELG expects sound-blending by end of Reception); formal written arithmetic (number sentences within 20) from Year 1.

### 2.2 Reception (age 4) Early Learning Goals — the pre-Year-1 baseline
- **Number**: "deep understanding of numbers to 10, including the composition of each number"; "Subitise … up to 5"; "Automatically recall … number bonds up to 5 (including subtraction facts) and some number bonds to 10, including double facts" ([EYFS 2026, ELG: Number](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a5f8e4fb00f3323bf1a23a1/EYFS_group_and_school_based_from_September_2026.pdf)).
- **Numerical patterns**: "Verbally count beyond 20"; compare quantities to 10; odds/evens, doubles, equal sharing (same source).
- **Word reading**: "Say a sound for each letter in the alphabet and at least 10 digraphs"; "Read words consistent with their phonic knowledge by sound-blending" (same source).
- **Writing**: "Write recognisable letters, most of which are correctly formed" (same source).

### 2.5 Science per year ([science programmes of study](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-science-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-science-programmes-of-study))
- **Y1 (5)**: plants (common wild/garden plants, deciduous/evergreen); animals incl. humans (fish/amphibians/reptiles/birds/mammals; carnivores/herbivores/omnivores; **body parts and the senses**); everyday materials (wood, plastic, glass, metal, water, rock — properties); **seasonal changes** (weather, day length).
- **Y2 (6)**: living things and their habitats (living/dead/never alive; food chains); plants (seeds and bulbs, needs water/light/temperature); animals incl. humans (**offspring, basic needs**, exercise/food/hygiene); uses of everyday materials (squashing, bending, twisting, stretching).
- **Y3 (7)**: plants (parts and functions, water transport, life cycle of flowering plants); animals incl. humans (nutrition, **skeletons and muscles**); rocks and fossils; **light** (dark = absence of light, shadows, sun safety); **forces and magnets** (surfaces, attract/repel, poles).
- Note: sound and electricity arrive in Year 4 (age 8) — outside the tables above but inside our age band.

### 2.6 Notable England divergences
Earliest formal start (4) of the six; a statutory national phonics test at 6; halves/quarters at 5 and fractions with thirds/three-quarters at 6; multiplication tables staged 2/5/10 → 3/4/8 → all 12 by age 8; time to 5 minutes at 6, Roman numerals on clocks at 7; £/p money; joined handwriting starts at 6; science is a named subject with physics topics (light, magnets) at 7.

## 3. Germany — KMK Bildungsstandards Primarbereich (2022) + Bayern LehrplanPLUS Grundschule

**Status.** The KMK standards (adopted 23 June 2022) define what must be reached by the end of Klasse 4 in Mathematik and Deutsch ([KMK Mathe PDF](https://www.kmk.org/fileadmin/Dateien/veroeffentlichungen_beschluesse/2022/2022_06_23-Bista-Primarbereich-Mathe.pdf), [KMK Deutsch PDF](https://www.kmk.org/fileadmin/Dateien/veroeffentlichungen_beschluesse/2022/2022_06_23-Bista-Primarbereich-Deutsch.pdf)). Each Land writes its own Lehrplan; Bavaria's LehrplanPLUS is used here because it is fully online and organised per 2-year block (Jahrgangsstufe 1/2 and 3/4). Science/social studies = **Heimat- und Sachunterricht (HSU)**.

### 3.1 Start ages
Compulsory schooling from 6 ("having reached their sixth birthday before a statutory qualifying date", [Eurydice DE](https://eurydice.eacea.ec.europa.eu/eurypedia/germany/organisation-primary-education)); Bavaria's date is 30 September ([schule-in-deutschland](https://schule-in-deutschland.de/die-grundschule-in-bayern/)). Kindergarten (3–6) is not part of the school system and has no subject curriculum; Schulkindergarten/Vorklasse exist only for deferred children (Eurydice DE). Formal reading and written arithmetic both begin in Klasse 1 (age 6).

### 5.4 Science / world knowledge ("Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld", kerndoelen 40–46; TULE per block)
- Kerndoelen: KD 40 common plants/animals and how they function; KD 41 structure of plants, animals, humans; KD 42 "onderzoek doen aan materialen en natuurkundige verschijnselen, zoals licht, geluid, elektriciteit, kracht, magnetisme en temperatuur"; KD 43 weather and climate (temperature, precipitation, wind) ([kerndoelen 2006](https://www.slo.nl/publish/pages/17358/kerndoelen-primaironderwijs2006-overzicht_1.pdf)).
- **Groep 1/2 (4–5)** (TULE): plants change with seasons; humans/animals need food; senses "gezicht, gehoor, reuk, tast, smaak"; plant parts and germination; light vs dark; loud/soft, high/low sounds; floating/sinking; magnetic or not; warm/cold ([TULE KD40](https://www.slo.nl/thema/meer/tule/orientatie-jezelf-wereld/kerndoel-40/), [KD41](https://www.slo.nl/thema/meer/tule/orientatie-jezelf-wereld/kerndoel-41/), [KD42](https://www.slo.nl/thema/meer/tule/orientatie-jezelf-wereld/kerndoel-42/)).
- **Groep 3/4 (6–7)**: animals sorted into "zoogdieren, vogels, vissen, amfibieën, reptielen, insecten en spinnen"; animals need "water, voedsel en beschutting", plants need "voedsel, water, licht en de juiste temperatuur"; "planten en dieren doorlopen verschillende ontwikkelingsstadia"; heart, lungs, muscles, bones, senses; seed dispersal, bulbs; **dark vs shadow**; order sounds; **magnetism experienced**, magnetic vs non-magnetic objects; temperature changes.
- **Groep 5/6 (8–9)**: species groups; camouflage; metamorphosis; light from sources, reflection; sound from sources; **electricity in a closed circuit**; magnets have poles; Celsius scale, melting/freezing/evaporation.

### 5.5 Notable Dutch divergences
School at 4 but formal literacy/numeracy at 6 (kleuterklas is deliberately play-based); the legal curriculum only fixes end-of-primary, so year expectations are advisory; multiplication tables split 1–5,10 (age 7) vs the rest (age 8); analogue and digital clocks taught together from age 6; fractions stay informal until 9; no prescribed handwriting script; AVI/DMT reading-level system rather than a phonics test; magnets and shadows at 6–7 but electricity only at 8–9; a national kerndoelen reform lands in this exact window (Aug 2026 → 2031).

## 6. Finland — National core curriculum (OPS 2014) + pre-primary (esiopetus 2014)

**Status.** Perusopetuksen opetussuunnitelman perusteet 2014, in use since Aug 2016 for grades 1–6 ([OPH](https://www.oph.fi/en/education-and-qualifications/national-core-curriculum-primary-and-lower-secondary-basic-education)); subject content is defined for grade blocks 1–2 and 3–6, and municipalities allocate content per year ([OPS 2014 PDF](https://www.oph.fi/sites/default/files/documents/perusopetuksen_opetussuunnitelman_perusteet_2014.pdf)). Pre-primary follows Esiopetuksen opetussuunnitelman perusteet 2014 ([PDF](https://www.oph.fi/sites/default/files/documents/esiopetuksen_opetussuunnitelman_perusteet_2014.pdf)).

### 6.1 Start ages
Pre-primary at 6 (the year the child turns 6), compulsory since 1 Aug 2015; basic education "starts in the year that a child turns 7" ([Eurydice FI](https://eurydice.eacea.ec.europa.eu/eurypedia/finland/organisation-single-structure-education), [fi.wikipedia](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esiopetus_Suomessa)). Formal reading and written arithmetic start in grade 1 (age 7). Pre-primary is integrated (no subjects): it builds number concept, counting sequence, comparing quantities, 2D/3D exploration and position words, and lets children notice that "puheen voi jakaa pienempiin osiin, kuten sanoihin, tavuihin ja äänteisiin" — but does not teach reading ([esiopetus 2014](https://www.oph.fi/sites/default/files/documents/esiopetuksen_opetussuunnitelman_perusteet_2014.pdf)).

### 6.3 Literacy — Suomen kieli ja kirjallisuus grades 1–2 (7–8) ([OPS äidinkieli 1–2](https://www.tavoitteet.fi/fi/opetussuunnitelman-perusteet/vuosiluokat-1-2/suomen-kieli-ja-kirjallisuus/))
- Reading: "Opetellaan lukemaan ja harjoitellaan siinä tarvittavia käsitteitä äänne, kirjain, tavu, sana, virke, lopetusmerkki" — sound–letter–syllable–word progression on a fully transparent orthography; the widespread classroom method is KÄTS (kirjain–äänne–tavu–sana) ([fi.wikipedia](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esiopetus_Suomessa) notes KÄTS/LPP/Trageton as the common methods). Comprehension strategies practised from the start.
- Sight words: **not a curriculum concept** (transparent orthography).
- Handwriting: "Opetellaan isojen ja pienten tekstauskirjainten piirtämistä sekä niillä kirjoittamista ja näppäintaitoja" — **print letters plus keyboard skills; cursive (sidosteinen kirjoitus) dropped from autumn 2016** ([Yle](https://yle.fi/a/3-7635713)).
- Spelling/punctuation: "sanaväli, tavutus, virke ja sen lopetusmerkit, iso alkukirjain virkkeen alussa ja tutuissa erisnimissä".
- Grammar: language awareness only ("kielitietoisuus"); no named parts of speech in 1–2.
- Writing: narrative and descriptive texts, also in multimedia environments.

### 6.4 Science / world knowledge — Ympäristöoppi grades 1–2 ([OPS ympäristöoppi 1–2](https://www.tavoitteet.fi/fi/opetussuunnitelman-perusteet/vuosiluokat-1-2/ymparistooppi/))
- S1 growth: "ihmisen kehon osista ja elintoiminnoista sekä elämänkulusta" (body parts, vital functions, life course); emotional skills.
- S2 home/school: safety, traffic, "säänmukaista pukeutumista" (dressing for the weather).
- S3 local environment: common species and habitats, "luonnon ominaispiirteitä … kaikkina vuodenaikoina" (seasons), weather described with concepts, a yard map and the idea of a map.
- S4 investigating: "Havainnoidaan liikettä ja pohditaan syitä liikkeen muutoksiin" (motion and its causes — the only physics), growing plants, everyday technology.
- S5 basic needs of life: "ravinnon, veden, ilman, lämmön ja huolenpidon"; food production, drinking water.
- S6 sustainability: recycling, sorting waste.
- Light, sound, magnetism, electricity, states of matter: grades 3–6 (age 9+), not 1–2.

### 6.5 Notable Finnish divergences
Latest start (7) and only two school years inside our 5–9 band; no subject teaching at 5–6; multiplication tables 1–5 and 10 by 8 but 6–9 only after 9; fractions and decimals after 9; print-only handwriting plus keyboarding from 7; no national reading test; content per 2-year block with local allocation, so "grade 3" specifics are municipal; environmental studies is a single integrated subject.

## 8. Common across all six (by age 8–9)

These are the concepts every one of the six systems requires somewhere in ages 5–9, and therefore the safest targets for a game that must be useful in every market.

1. **Counting and cardinality to 100**, one-to-one counting, comparing quantities (all six by 8).
2. **Number composition to 10 / number bonds** and fluent **addition–subtraction facts to 20** (all six by 8; four of six by 7).
3. **Place value with tens and ones**, then hundreds (all six by 8).
4. **Addition and subtraction within 100** by mental strategies (all six by 8).
5. **Multiplication as equal groups** and the **first multiplication tables** (all six by 8; the table set differs — see §9).
6. **Division as sharing/grouping** at least conceptually (all six by 8–9).
7. **Halves and quarters** as equal parts of a whole (all six by 8, informal in DE/NL/FI).
8. **2D shapes** circle/square/rectangle/triangle and **3D shapes** cube/sphere (ball) plus at least cylinder/cone or cuboid (all six by 7).
9. **Position and direction words**, simple maps/plans (all six by 7).
10. **Length with a ruler in cm and m** (all six by 7).
11. **Reading an analogue clock** at least to whole and half hours (all six by 8); most also digital.
12. **Tables and bar charts / pictograms** from collected data (all six by 8).
13. **Repeating and growing patterns** (all six by 7).
14. **Letter–sound correspondences taught explicitly and systematically** before or at the start of reading; blending sounds/syllables into words (all six — synthetic phonics in EN/US, grapheme-phoneme + syllabic in FR, syllable-method in DE, klankzuiver phonics in NL, KÄTS in FI).
15. **Sentence conventions: capital letter to start, full stop / question mark to end**, spaces between words (all six by 8).
16. **Spelling by segmenting sounds**, then rule-based patterns (all six by 8).
17. **Reading comprehension** of short narrative and informational texts; retell/sequence (all six by 8).
18. **Living things**: needs of plants and animals (water, food, light), growth and life cycles, common local species, seasons (all six by 8).
19. **Human body parts and senses; hygiene and health** (all six by 8).
20. **Properties of everyday materials / states of water** (five of six by 8; FI at 9+).

## 11. Per-age digest — what is expected in ALL six systems at each age (derived from §7)

Read this as "a child of this age in any of the six markets is being taught this at school right now or has already been". A concept listed at age N is fine to target at N and above in every market; at N−1 it will be new material in at least one market.

### Age 5 (US K · EN Y1 · DE Kindergarten · FR GS · NL groep 2 · FI daycare)
- Only two systems have statutory content (US, EN); FR has a maternelle programme; DE/NL are play-based; FI has none.
- Safe in all six only as pre-academic play: counting objects to 10, comparing "more/fewer", sorting by attribute, naming circle/square/triangle/rectangle, position words, repeating patterns, rhyme and syllable clapping, letter recognition in the child's own name.
- NOT common: letter-sound decoding (only US/EN/FR-GS), written numerals beyond 10, any operation symbols.

### Age 6 (US G1 · EN Y2 · DE Klasse 1 · FR CP · NL groep 3 · FI esiopetus)
- Five of six now teach formal reading (all except FI); all five use explicit letter/grapheme–sound teaching with blending.
- Common maths: count to 20 with numerals; number bonds within 10; +/− within 10–20 with objects; tens/ones informally; 2D shapes; direct comparison of length/weight; whole-hour clock; coins of the national currency (not FI).
- Common science: plants and animals need food/water/light; seasons; body parts and senses.
- Divergent at 6: EN already has 2/5/10 tables, quarter fractions, 5-minute clock; FR has column addition and cursive; DE/NL are in the first months of print.

### Age 7 (US G2 · EN Y3 · DE Klasse 2 · FR CE1 · NL groep 4 · FI grade 1)
- Common maths: count to 100 and beyond; +/− facts to 20 from memory (FI still consolidating); place value tens/ones; +/− within 100 mentally; multiplication as equal groups; first tables (set varies: EN 2/5/10 done, NL 1–5/10, DE core facts, FR all, US arrays only, FI concept + 1–5/10 in progress); halves/quarters; cm and m; hour/half-hour clock everywhere, quarter/5-minute in EN/DE/FR/NL/US; bar charts/pictograms; €/£/$ amounts.
- Common literacy: decoding is established in all six (FI just started); capital letter + full stop; simple spelling by sounds; reading short texts for meaning; nouns/verbs named in EN/FR/DE (not NL/FI).
- Common science: life cycles/growth stages, habitats, materials and their properties, weather; human body internal parts (heart, lungs, bones) in NL/EN/FR.

### Age 8 (US G3 · EN Y4 · DE Klasse 3 · FR CE2 · NL groep 5 · FI grade 2)
- Common maths: numbers to 1000 (FI: to 100 nationally, 1000 local); all tables to 10 known or nearly (FI 1–5/10 only); division facts/sign (FI concept); +/− within 1000; column addition/subtraction (FI/NL beginning); fractions with notation in US/EN/FR (informal in DE/NL/FI); g/kg, l/ml, km; clock to the minute (FI unspecified); money with cents/decimals in FR/NL, £/p change in EN, $/¢ in US.
- Common literacy: fluent reading of paragraphs; irregular spellings and suffixes; comprehension with inference; paragraphs/sentence types; nouns/verbs/adjectives named in US/EN/FR/DE/NL.
- Common science: plant and animal life cycles, skeletons/nutrition, states of water; physics diverges (magnets US/EN/NL, electricity DE/FR/NL, light EN/US/NL, nothing yet in FI).

### Age 9 (US G4 · EN Y5 · DE Klasse 4 · FR CM1 · NL groep 6 · FI grade 3)
- Outside most of the researched content; every system has written multiplication, all tables, formal fractions (DE only as measures), decimals (FR/NL/DE), angles and symmetry.

### Implication for a game catalogue
Concepts in §8 (the "common" list) are safe at the ages in §7's most conservative column for that row — usually Finland's or Germany's. A game that must work "everywhere" should be levelled by the Finnish/German timing for maths content and by the English/US timing only for phonics-style literacy if it is language-specific anyway. Currency, units (imperial), handwriting style, and physics topics are the four places a single global design cannot be shared without per-market variants.
