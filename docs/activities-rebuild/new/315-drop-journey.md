## The skill
The water cycle: evaporation, condensation, precipitation and collection, in order and why each happens.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE DROP'S JOURNEY: a scene with sea, sun, sky and a hill running down to a river (scene geometry drawn tool-grade, sun/cloud/rain from the library weather theme). A water drop sits in the sea. The child must give the drop what it needs to move: drag the sun's warmth onto the sea and the drop rises as vapour (evaporation); drag it into cold high air and it joins a cloud (condensation); add drops until the cloud is heavy and it rains (precipitation); the drop runs down the hill back to the sea (collection). Each stage's word lands on the scene where it happened. Dragging the wrong cause (cold onto the sea) does nothing but frost the sea's edge. Label mode: words from a bank go onto numbered places.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Thinking clouds are made of smoke or cotton, that rain comes from nowhere, and getting the stages out of order.
- **Pictures:** weather (raindrop as the drop, cloud, rainy, sunny, snowflake); scene (sea band, hill wedge, river line, cold high-air band) is apparatus geometry; ocean-life optional decoration refused (clutter).
- **Settings:** Mode: make the drop travel / label the diagram · Words: 4 stages / 4 stages + runoff/ground water
- **Rounds:** ≥ 7 rounds: label rounds shuffle which places are blank; journey rounds start the drop at a different stage (sea, cloud, hill) so the child must know what comes next.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G3-385
- **Distinct from:** No science activity exists; distinct from twin-wheels (life cycles).
- **Build risk:** Medium: the scene is geometry, but must not read as hand-drawn art (rule 5); the stage words are content and need native terms ×11.
