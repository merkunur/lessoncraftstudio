**The rule that locks the type.** A panel's rank is legal only because every consecutive pair of panels strictly INCREASES at least one IRREVERSIBLE state variable and decreases none; each variable is DRAWN as a count of outlined `[data-irr="<var>"]` parts, and the primitive's gate counts them in the render. Direction never comes from a change a child can undo with their hands (stacking, inserting, placing, moving, filling, pouring, opening, inflating, lighting, blowing out, folding). A story that needs a reversible step is refused at validation, never rescued by wording. **Greyscale law (m, Rec. 601): white 255 · cream 244 · creamDeep 234 · coralSoft 233 · tealSoft 230 · grid 192 · codeYellow 166 · coral 151 · inkSoft 131 · teal 80 · ink 54.** The pale trio (234/233/230) is one grey, so no state may rest on a pale-vs-pale fill: every carrier is an outlined shape, a count, a size change ≥20 %, or a fill from the strong set {white, grid, codeYellow, coral, teal, ink}. ## 2 The base page

**Concept.** "Story Sequencing: Number the Pictures" (K). Two teal cords sag across the page; four 4:3 picture cards are pegged to each, one story per cord, in a scrambled order; under every card a dashed tag dangles on a short string. The child writes 1, 2, 3, 4 on the tags. The line says "these belong together" and "read me left to right" without a word (the classroom story washing line, de "Bildergeschichte an der Wäscheleine"; each landing names its local routine, the sheet never does). Inside every card the STAGE (table, snow, sand, road, pavement, desk, garden) is drawn pale and byte-identical across the story; only the teal-outlined PROP changes, so the child compares one thing.

**The scramble law (m, `K-379-perms.js` exhaustive; supersedes all three inputs).** A derangement is NOT the law: requiring every card off its true slot makes the leftmost card NEVER the first and the rightmost NEVER the last (m: n=4 derangements with no rotation leave 4 permutations, rank-1 slot share 0 % in slot 1: an inverted leak). The law, per story row, reading left to right: not the identity, not the reverse; **forward chain ≤ 1** (at most one k with rank k+1 immediately RIGHT of rank k: kills the rotations 2341 / 4123) ; **backward chain ≤ 1** (at most one k with rank k+1 immediately LEFT of rank k: kills reversed rotations); **fixed points ≤ 1**. n=4 leaves 12 permutations (m): 1342 1423 2314 2413 2431 3124 3142 3241 3421 4132 4213 4312. **SCRAMBLE4 weights** make rank 1 and rank 4 each land in every slot with probability exactly 0.25 (m, IPF): 1342 .146 · 1423 .104 · 2314 .104 · 2431 .073 · 3124 .146 · 3241 .104 · 3421 .073 · 4132 .104 · 4213 .073 · 4312 .073 (2413 and 3142 weight 0). n=3 (F1 strips): allowed 132 213 231 312 (m); **the middle slot then holds rank 1 with probability 0.50 and no weighting can lower it** (m: first-slot shares are a, b+d, c and last-slot shares d, a+c, b, so one middle share is always ≥ 0.5 unless the identity or the reverse is admitted); recorded, uniform sampling, per page the two strips never share a permutation. n=5 (d3 only): the same law, 74 permutations (m).
