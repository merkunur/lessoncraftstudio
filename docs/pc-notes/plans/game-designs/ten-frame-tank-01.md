## 2. Concept & creative hook
Dewey the duckling fills his pond-tank with glowing bubbles — but the magic a 5-year-old waits for is **the SEAL**: when the tenth bubble drops, the ten loose bubbles **fuse into ONE shimmering ten-raft** (a single object with a "10" and its ten dots still visible inside), with a satisfying *clunk-glug*. Ten things become ONE thing in front of you — that transformation IS K.NBT.A.1, dramatized. Then the leftover bubbles are the "and 3 more." Thirteen reads, left-to-right, exactly like the standard: **[one sealed ten-raft] + [3 loose bubbles] → "a ten and 3 more — 13."**
- **The ten is WIELDED, not just witnessed (the premium-vs-free fix):** once sealed, the ten-raft is a **grabbable, persistent, reusable single object** — the child drags it as ONE piece, it's editable only as a whole (remove the whole ten, never dot #7 of it), and it can carry between rounds. The left frame **physically refuses a loose single** with a **charming visible rejection** (Dewey shakes his head, the bubble bounces back: *"that's a ten-spot — singles go in the pond!"*) — the constraint is FELT, never a silent gate. This wielding + the felt refusal is what makes the premium game cognitively distinct from the free tap-to-fill (which just fills cells).
- **The ten-as-a-unit is the load-bearing cognition (the make-or-break):** the **double ten-frame "tank"** — left = the sealed TEN (one unit), right = the ONES (caps at 9). The left frame accepts ONLY the ten-unit (`singleDotPlacedInLeft` must stay 0); the win REQUIRES a sealed ten (`sealedTenPlaced`). So a teen is **unreachable** by 13 loose singles (structurally impossible) and unreachable by a timed pour-stop (no timed surface).
- **The ready-made ten is honest, never a token (the rework):** in the "operate on a unit" experience, the placeable ten is **a visible 2×5 frame of ten dots** (`tenUnitSubDots===10`) that announces "a ten — ten ones together" on placement — NEVER a bare "10" token — and it appears **only after the child has personally sealed a ten this session** (gate-ordered #1→#2), so the child has SEEN ten get made before operating on a ready-made one.
- **The live 10 + N record:** a real DOM equation `10 + N = 1N` updates as the child composes (the standard's recording requirement). Deterministic, no-fail, no motor-timing (the fill is a synchronous cell-toggle; the liquid-rise is decorative CSS on an already-decided cell).
- **Anti-generic statement:** NOT the free ten-frame activity + a duckling — (a) the ten is a wielded unit + the left frame's single-refusal is felt; (b) unitizing is gate-provable (left accepts only the ten-unit; win requires a sealed ten; the ready-made ten shows visible 2×5, never a token); (c) BOTH compose + decompose (the ten stays sealed in decompose — no cracking); (d) the live 10+N record; (e) a board ships only if a **count-13-singles solver**, a **stop-by-timing solver**, a **fill-without-a-full-ten solver**, a **token-placement (bare-numeral-ten) solver**, and a **brute-force (random-tap/incremental-drop) solver** ALL fail the gate while a genuine seal-a-ten + place-N-ones composer passes (§8).

## 3. Core loop & the ≥7 distinct experiences
**Loop (sub-steps via `stage`, no inter-stage auto-advance):** Dewey shows the order ("make 13!") → the child **gets a ten** (completes-and-seals it, OR places a ready-made sealed ten) → the left frame locks it as ONE unit → the child **places N ones** in the right frame (cap 9; a 10th refused with a charming rejection) → the live `10 + N = 1N` equation lights → on the exact composition (`sealedTenPlaced && ones===target-10`) → `ctx.correct()` → the round joins the non-numeric vessel. **Decompose rounds** present a teen and the child SEPARATES it. **No fail, no timing, no Check button.** Overfill freely correctable (tap a placed one to remove; tap the ten to send it back) — no penalty.

**The ≥7 distinct experiences — HONEST re-count (the critic's catch: it was ~5; refilled): distinct = the terminal CHILD-ACT + input-modality + target-source, NOT a different teen number.** The genuinely distinct terminal-acts:

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ★ | **Compose-by-completing-and-sealing** (CAUSE the unit; on-ramp) | fill the first frame to ten → it SEALS (10 dots collapse to 1 unit) → place N ones | the unitizing TRANSFORMATION — the child makes ten become one; gate-ordered FIRST |
| 2 ★ | **Compose-by-placing-a-ready-made-ten** (OPERATE on the unit) | a visible 2×5 sealed ten (never a token) → place it + N ones; **only after #1 sealed one** | operate on a ten AS a single unit — the place-value efficiency once the unit is understood |

## 5. Pedagogy
- **K.NBT.A.1 instantiation:** the child composes/decomposes 11–19 as **one SEALED ten (a single composite unit) + N ones**, recorded as **10 + N**. UNITIZING is enacted: a complete ten SEALS into one editable-only-as-a-whole object the child wields; the teen is built/read as ten + ones, never as N undifferentiated singles.
- **THE KEYSTONE RULES (non-guessability):** (i) **THE TEN IS ONE SEALED UNIT** — the left frame accepts ONLY the ten-unit (`singleDotPlacedInLeft===0`); the win REQUIRES `sealedTenPlaced` (count-13-singles structurally impossible). (ii) **THE READY-MADE TEN IS VISIBLY TEN, NEVER A TOKEN** — it renders as a 2×5 frame of ten dots (`tenUnitSubDots===10`) + announces "ten ones together," and appears ONLY after the child has personally sealed a ten this session (gate-ordered #1→#2; closes the unitizing-skip). (iii) **THE SEAL COLLAPSES 10→1** — completing the ten drops the count of independently-editable child-objects from 10 to 1 (the unitizing transformation is visible, not "ten dots sitting there"). (iv) **DECOMPOSE = SEPARATE, NOT CRACK** — the ten stays SEALED through decompose (a teen → ten-tray + ones-tray); the ten NEVER bursts into singles on any win-path (cracking would un-teach the unit; an optional reversible "see-inside" peek may exist but is never the act). (v) **NO MOTOR-TIMING** — the fill is a synchronous cell-toggle; nothing is clocked; pour-and-stop is dead. (vi) **THE LIVE 10 + N RECORD** is actively produced (the standard's recording requirement; an appearance-match without it fails).
- **Number ranges (pedagogy):** **11–19 ONLY** (the teen numbers — the standard). ≤10 appears ONLY as the flagged on-ramp (#1) where completing/sealing a ten is the goal (the unit dramatized). The 5+5 two-row ten-frame structure is a cardinality/subitizing aid (full = ten without recounting). **6–9 rounds**, mixing compose + decompose, spanning the range (11, 12 easy / 15 mid / 18, 19 hard).
- **The cheats (all four closed):** (a) count-13-singles [most dangerous — the silent unitizing miss] → the left frame accepts only the ten-unit. (b) stop-by-timing → no timed surface. (c) fill-without-a-full-ten (7+6=13) → win requires exactly one sealed ten. (d) token-placement (bare "10") → the ready-made ten shows visible 2×5 + comes after the child sealed one.
- **No-shame:** overfill freely correctable; the refusal is a charming character beat; the seal is the intrinsic reward. **Scaffold-not-do-for-them:** the engine SEALS the ten when complete (the ten-frame's job — the perceptual support) but the child PLACES the ten + the ones + reads/confirms 10 + N; don't auto-narrate the structure before the child acts. **Gate-honesty caveat:** the gate proves STRUCTURE + visible-ten + saw-it-made (the closest to gate-provable unitizing the medium allows — not mind-reading); conceded explicitly.

## 6. Interaction & UX
- **Runtime: the discrete compositional act (the one primitive).** Tap-primary (drag enhancement, same code path). **Get-a-ten:** complete-and-seal (fill the first frame to 10 → `seal()` collapses 10 editable dots into one `.tft-ten` unit) OR place a ready-made `.tft-ten` (a visible 2×5 unit). **Place-ones:** each tap on the ones-dispenser drips ONE one into the next empty right-frame cell (`placeOne()` — the sole ones-advancing path; cap 9). The left frame accepts ONLY a `.tft-ten` drop (a loose single → charming bounce-back). `_busy` serializes the seal animation. No auto-fill, no timed surface, no "fill all" button.
- **State separation (anti-cheat):** `__TFT_TRACE__()` exposes `{ target, ones, sealedTenPlaced, looseOnesPlaced, singleDotPlacedInLeft (must stay 0), tenUnitSubDots (must be 10), sealedThisSession, won }` for the measured gate.
- **The double ten-frame + the wielded unit:** the `.tft-ten` is one element with `pointer-events:none` children (only the whole ten is grabbable; the 2×5 ten-dots are visible inside). - **Decompose:** a teen shown (sealed ten + N ones); the child drags the **sealed ten** (as one unit) to the tens-tray + the ones to the ones-tray; win when the unit is in tens + all N ones in ones. The ten stays sealed throughout (no cracking).
- **Mobile @280px (truth-tested, budget reworked — the critic's overflow fix):** **the sealed ten collapses to a compact one-unit CHIP** (a small 2×5 ten-block, ≥44px as a WHOLE — you only interact with it as one unit, so its sub-cells needn't be 44px) once placed/sealed; **only the ACTIVE ones-frame needs full 44px cells** → only ONE full 2×5 grid of 44px cells is on screen at a time (resolves the "4 cell-rows overflow"). The ten-SOURCE collapses to a single ten-token affordance; the ones-source to a compact dispenser; **Dewey shrinks to a corner sprite** (charm, not a control); the equation is one line. `audit-game-mobile.js` 280–768 empty + filled, **HALTS on any sub-44px interactive cell** at 280px for all 19 states (assert ≥44px actually achieved, not assumed).
- **Accessibility:** TTS speaks "a ten… and three… thirteen" (`_NUMBER_WORD_HELPERS` + the per-locale connective); `ctx.announce()` mirrors every state ("1 ten placed" / "10 + 3" / "a ten and three — thirteen"); keyboard path = the ten-source + the ones-dispenser + placed-ones are `<button>`s (Enter places/removes; the whole game is tap/keyboard-completable, drag is enhancement); ≥44px; reduced-motion (the seal collapse is instant, no liquid-rise); mute-safe; Direction-A contrast (teal ten-band / coral ones on cream).
