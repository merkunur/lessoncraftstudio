## 5 Quality verdict

I would print the base and F1 for my kindergarten tomorrow: two stories on two washing lines is exactly how I teach this with real cards, the pictures are big, and every step has one thing a child can put a finger on. What would have embarrassed me is now out: a block tower that a clever child can "unbuild" into a second right order, a birthday cake whose only change is candles going in, a scramble rule that quietly never puts the first picture on the left (my sharpest pupils would have learned that in a week), and a "what happens next" where the skipped bite is a better answer than the one we mark. What still worries me is the art itself: fourteen stories drawn from geometry nobody has looked at yet. A snowman with no face and a banana peel lying open are exactly the pictures a child names differently, so the build is not done until a person has held the greyscale print of every story at arm's length and said out loud what changed.
