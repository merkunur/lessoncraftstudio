### G1-408 · Heavy or Light? (G1, CODE `layout:'scale'`)
**Spec:** `types/g1/G1-408-heavy-or-light-sink-or-float-scale.js`. **PNG:** `scripts/worksheet-gen/out/dev/G1-408-null-d2-en.png` (plus `-grey100.png`).

- **What the child does:** reads four balances, where the heavier pan is DOWN, and circles the object that floats.
- **Page mix:** each page has 2 cards where the heavier object floats and 2 where the lighter one floats, so a weight, size or "pick the lighter" strategy each scores exactly 50 %.
- **Composer:**
  - Draws 2 P pairs and 2 Q pairs with 8 distinct nouns.
  - The floater is on the left in exactly 2 of the 4 cards, and the scale tilts left in exactly 2 of the 4.
  - Neither sequence may be constant or alternating; this is checked per page and re-measured by `verify()`.
- **`verify()` asserts:**
  - The tilt matches the heavier side from the PAIR table.
  - The heavier pan sits ≥ 20 px lower (measured on the emitted pan paths).
  - The heavier picture's rendered height is ≥ 1.6 × the lighter one's.
  - Each picture sits centred in its pan.
  - Exactly one floater and one sinker per card, per the claim table.
  - No text on the page body.
- **Poisons it owns:** PR2 (tilt lies), PR3 (the heavier thing floats on all 4 cards), PR4 (the floater drawn smaller), PA1 (the instruction names a tank), FL-scale.
- **Deviation (measured), lead review round 2:**
  - **Layout.** The design asked for a 2×2 grid of 312 px cards with a 300×220 balance. I built one column of four 639 px cards with a **560×146** balance; the pictures stay at the design's **76/46 px**. The 2×2 cards left about 40 % of each card empty.
  - **The balance is now drawn by the family** (`sfBalance` in `templates/components-b6/sink-or-float.js`). `primitives/balance.js` is NOT edited: G2-252 is its live consumer, and the baseline still passes.
    - The defect: `balance.js` hangs its pans only 24 px under the beam, so any object as tall as 46–76 px had to sit ACROSS the beam end. The lead's review of the first build found every object "skewered" by the beam, with the pans hanging empty below.
    - `sfBalance` keeps the same visual language (5 px teal beam, coral pivot, tealSoft pedestal, 1.5 px strings, white 3 px dish, ±7° tilt). It adds a pan drop of **82 px** (≥ the heavier picture + 2) and puts the beam at **0.2 h**.
  - **Placement.** Each object RESTS IN its pan:
    - its bottom sits on the dish floor (the chord + 8), centred on the pan;
    - it lies wholly under the beam, drawn in FRONT of the strings.
  - **Guards in the builder.** It refuses a pan drop shorter than the heavier picture + 2, and a heavier dish floor that falls outside the drawing.
  - **Tilt difference:** 43.6 px.
  - **Worst-chrome stack:** 4 × 154 + 3 × 12 = 652 ≤ 667.
- **New render assertions in `verify()`,** measured on the rendered geometry:
  - each object's box bottom is within ±3 px of its pan's dish floor (the lowest point of the pan path);
  - it is centred on the pan within 2 px;
  - its box never intersects the beam segment (5 px stroke, sampled);
  - it is drawn after the balance, i.e. in front of it.
- **New poison PR14** (the old placement, objects centred on the beam end, via `forceOldPlacement`): KILLED.
- **Renders I read after the fix:**
  - `out/dev/G1-408-null-d2-en.png` (d1 and d3 render the same config, since the face spec is one object for all three levels)
  - `out/dev/G1-408-null-d2-en-grey100.png`
  - `out/dev/G1-399-gate/G1-399-gate-face-scale-d2-long-fi.png` (the 667 body)
