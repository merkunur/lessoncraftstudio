## The skill
Animals are grouped by their body covering: mammals have fur or hair, birds feathers, fish and reptiles scales, amphibians smooth wet skin.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE FIELD LENS. Library animals wait on the left; four drawers on the right show a swatch on the front (fur: soft strokes; feathers: chevrons; scales: overlapping arcs; smooth wet skin: plain glossy) — pattern geometry, not drawings of animals. The child drags the lens over an animal: under it a patch of the picture enlarges so the covering can be seen. Then the animal goes into a drawer. Right: the drawer closes and the swatch flashes. Miss 1: the drawer will not close and the lens re-opens on the same patch next to the drawer's swatch. Miss 2: the right drawer's swatch glows. GROUP-NAME setting (G1-201): the drawers carry the class names (mammal, bird, fish, reptile, amphibian) and surprise animals appear (penguin → bird by its feathers; bat → mammal by its fur; turtle → reptile by its scales); whale and dolphin appear ONLY here, with a second lens clue (the blowhole: "breathes air") and go to mammals.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Calls a whale a fish, a penguin not a bird (it cannot fly), a turtle an amphibian.
- **Pictures:** Mammals: animals, zoo-animals, farm-animals; birds: birds, birds-2; fish: ocean-life (fish, clownfish, tuna, shark, angelfish); reptiles: reptiles-and-amphibians (turtle, iguana, gecko, alligator, cobra, chameleon, tortoise); amphibians: frog, salamander; surprises: penguin, bat, whale, dolphin.
- **Settings:** Coverings: 3 (fur, feathers, scales) / 4 (+ wet skin) · Show group names: off / on.
- **Rounds:** 10 animals per pass, each drawer 20–35 %; covering rounds exclude whale, dolphin, manatee, narwhal, orca; group rounds include ≥ 2 surprises once the basic pass is done; one right drawer from a reviewed table.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G1-201, G1-205
- **Distinct from:** No science activity exists; Mim's Memory Baskets sort by everyday category, not by biological trait.
- **Build risk:** The cartoon patch must actually show the covering (some library animals are flat colour) — the reviewer opens every lens patch; refused if unreadable.
