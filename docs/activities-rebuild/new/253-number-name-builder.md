## The skill
Number words are built from parts (six, -teen, -ty / twen-, ones before or after tens in some languages) and say how many tens and ones.

## The apparatus and its invention
WORD TILES THAT CARRY QUANTITY. A numeral sits above an empty name rail; beside the rail its value is shown as tens rods and ones cubes. Word-part tiles lie below — the parts a child of that language actually learns (en six, -teen, -ty, twen-, thir-; de sechs, und, zwanzig; fr seize, soixante, dix; fi kaksi, kymmentä, kuusi, toista; da tens words such as 'halvtreds' as ONE tile). As each tile snaps into the rail, its quantity appears under it as rods and cubes (a multiplicative tile such as fr 'quatre-vingts' shows four bundles of twenty). The rail lights only when the rods and cubes under the whole name equal the ones beside the numeral; 'sixty' for 16 shows six rods against one rod and six cubes, and the rail rejects it — the tiles bounce back. Where the language says the ones first (de/nl/da 'sechsundzwanzig'), the quantities still add up, so the spoken order is learnt without being told the digits are swapped. Reading mode: a name is given as tiles and the child builds its rods and cubes (tap to add a rod or a cube), then types the numeral. Miss 1: the quantity under the wrong tile glows; miss 2: the numeral's rods and cubes slide next to the rail's for a direct comparison. Right: the rail and the numeral glow together and the name is read in the bubble.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Confuses -teen and -ty (sixteen / sixty), or (de/nl/da) writes the tens first because the digits read left to right.
- **Pictures:** none (word tiles are the content; rods drawn tool-grade)
- **Settings:** Numbers: to 20 / tens to 100 / to 50 · Direction: numeral to word / word to numeral.
- **Rounds:** 8+ rounds per pass with at least two teen/ty confusables; tile tray always contains the confusable partner tile; numerals distinct within a pass.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G1-210, G1-223, G1-224, G1-225, G1-226, G1-227
- **Distinct from:** No activity reads or writes number names; place-value activities never attach words.
- **Build risk:** High native load: one morphology table per locale (fr 70–99, da 50–99 vigesimal names as unanalysed tiles, de/nl/da ones-before-tens, fi agglutinated 'kaksikymmentäkuusi' split kaksi|kymmentä|kuusi, sv/no regular). Each locale's tray must contain its own confusable partner (en sixteen/sixty, de sechzehn/sechzig, fr seize/soixante, fi kuusitoista/kuusikymmentä). Strong native review per locale.
