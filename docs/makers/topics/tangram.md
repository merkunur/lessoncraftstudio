## Templates from today's worksheet types

**Compose shapes from pieces (tangram figures; where two or three pieces meet)**

- `K-353` Tangram Puzzles: Cut Out the Pieces and Build Two Figures — Color each piece like the legend, cut out the seven pieces along the lines, then lay them on the two figures below, line for line. (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-353-tangram.js)
- `K-358` Easy Tangram Puzzles: Draw the Lines for 2 and 3 Pieces — Look at the pieces above each shape. Draw the lines inside the shape to show where those pieces meet. (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-358-tangram-draw-the-lines-2-and-3-pieces.js)

**Name 2D shapes in any orientation or proportion; follow a shape-coded key; count triangles and squares in a figure**

- `K-359` Tangram Puzzles: Count the Triangles and Squares — Count the triangles in each picture and write the number in the first box. Then count the squares for the second box. (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-359-tangram-count-the-triangles-and-squares.js)

**Compose figures from tangram pieces; find the missing piece**

- `G1-354` Tangram Puzzles: Four Shadows to Build — Build each dark figure with the seven pieces of your tangram. Use all seven pieces every time. (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/g1/G1-354-tangram-four-shadows-to-build.js)
- `G1-355` Tangram Puzzles: Which Piece Is Missing? — One piece is missing from each figure. Circle the piece that fits the empty space exactly. (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/g1/G1-355-tangram-which-piece-is-missing.js)

**Match a tangram solution to its shadow exactly, rejecting turned or flipped ones**

- `G2-347` Tangram Puzzles: Which Solution Matches the Shadow? — Look at each shadow. Circle the solution that stands exactly like the shadow. Turned or flipped ones do not match. (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/g2/G2-347-tangram-which-solution-matches-the-shadow.js)
