## Deviations from the design file (each with the measured reason)
1. **Cut frame inset by half the stroke** (`x = y = 1.25`, `w = 674 − 2.5`, `h = 692 − 2.5`). The design's "roundedRect r 0 frame" at x 0 would put half of the 2.5 px dash outside the SVG viewport (the outer `<svg>` clips) — 1.25 px visible instead of 2.5. The overlay ROOT is still exactly the sheet (674 × 692; P14 proves a 1 px shortfall fails).
2. **Twin cells 168.5 × 173, not 168 × 173.** `cellW = 674 / 4`; the design's 168 leaves 2 px unaccounted. The gate's ± 1 tolerance holds (168.5 vs 168); the overlay and the grid derive from the same division so the cut sits on the card edge (endpoint check ± 1).
3. **The instruction is count-neutral** ("Cut along the dotted lines to make the cards. Say the name of each picture, then read its word.") — one `i18n.en.instruction` serves d1 (4 cards) and d3 (16 cards, pictures apart from words), so "eight cards … the word under it" would be false on two of three levels. The face strings in the bank (`twin` …) carry their own counts.
4. **d1 two-line labels stay at 26 (plate 66)** with the picture at 220 (220 + 6 + 66 = 292 ≤ 314) — the design specifies d1 only for one line; nothing to shrink. `pic2` (the 84 px picture under a two-line plate) applies at d2 only. No en theme carries a two-line label; es/fr/fi carry 1/2/1 (see Measured) — they will exercise this path at the panels' renders.
5. **Face strings keyed by MODE** (`twin` · `article` · `plural` · `bilingual` · `syllable`), not by face id: the ids (`K-325+`, `G1-311+`) are "TBD by the emitter". The emitter maps mode → id when the faces land; the validator's rule 5 counts 6 (fi 5) keys.
6. **Bank validator rules 1-7 live in the gate** (`validateBank`, exported) because `tools/validate-b3-draft.js` is a shared/TBD tool (README item 10: owned by the first design built). The gate validates every block present (en today) + the in-memory fi/de-shaped blocks the poisons need.
7. **Poisons P6 / P7 / P8 / P9 / P19 / P20 are DEFERRED, not run**: each poisons a FACE (article / plural / bilingual / syllable) that does not exist on the base; there is no rendering to poison. They are printed in the gate output as `DEFERRED — Phase 2` and not counted (14 of 20 run, all killed). P3 gained a second half (a BW picture forced into a rendered card → `verify()` "BW directory"), P10 runs both halves (the label rule refuses the token; the forced 26 px plate overflows in the real pipeline), P13 asserts both the missing overlay and the per-card border.
8. **The 722 block vs a FOUR-line title (open item, measured).** Under the README's ruling the body is 722 only with a three-line title AND a three-line instruction; measured, a realistic ≤ 150-char instruction never reaches three lines (144-151 chars → 46 px = two lines in de/fi/en), so the real floor under a three-line title is **733** and the block fits with 5 px. But a 69-char de title with long tokens wraps to FOUR lines and drops the body to **700**, where the fixed 722 block prints 22 px into the attribution band. Kept the design's fixed 722 block (an adaptive shrink to 700 would put the 146 px card stack into a 143.5 px inner and clip the plate; a 700 re-budget = pic 100 / pic2 78 / cells 167.5 changes the 89 × 46 mm card the whole family is designed around). The gate proves `qa/lints.js` catches the class at generation (the four-line backstop render). **Panels must keep titles to three lines** (≈ 55-60 chars for German with compound tokens); a title that wraps to four fails the deck at generation, never silently.
9. **`verify()` exists** although the design says "NO face has a `verify()`" — that sentence means no ANSWER verification (nothing is graded); the brief's spec contract requires `verify(page) → string[]`, so it carries the structural §5 checks (the design's "structural node gate" logic) and `render/one.js` reports them.
