## 2. Concept & creative hook
**Bramble the badger runs a woodland berry-juice stand** and can never remember which of his mismatched cups holds more — because **his eyes lie to him** about tall ones. The child is his Pouring Pal: *call it before he pours.*
- **BRAMBLE MODELS THE MISCONCEPTION — AND IS SOMETIMES RIGHT (the critic's F9 — the no-shame engine + the real cognition):** on clear-cut rounds Bramble trusts appearance and is RIGHT ("Yep, the big jug! Easy one"); on illusion rounds he's FOOLED ("I was SURE the tall one… nope!"). A wrong prediction lands on BRAMBLE, not the child. Crucially Bramble is **sometimes-right-sometimes-fooled** — modeling the real cognition ("appearance is SOMETIMES trustworthy — decide per round"), NOT a dumb "always distrust height" invert-heuristic.
- **PREDICT-FIRST, THEN THE POUR VERIFIES (the binding runtime):** two EMPTY containers (no liquid, no level — zero capacity info in the DOM). The child commits **HOLDS MORE / HOLDS LESS / HOLDS THE SAME** (3 buttons, relative to a named cup). **The pour is structurally unreachable until the prediction is locked.** Then the first cup's juice transfers into the second → **OVERFLOWS** (first holds more) / **EXACTLY FILLS** (same) / **LEAVES ROOM** (second holds more). The pour VERIFIES the child's claim — it never compares for them before they've spoken.
- **CAPACITY IS DECOUPLED FROM APPEARANCE (the conservation mechanism):** each container's true capacity is an authored integer, NOT computed from its drawn shape — so a tall-skinny glass can hold LESS than a short-wide bowl. The eye is honestly fooled.
- **THE RE-OFFER TEACHES THE RULE, NOT THE PAIR (the critic's deepest fix):** when a child mis-predicts an illusion, a later round re-tests the SAME conservation structure (narrow-holds-less) on a NOVEL-looking pair (different art/proportions/colors) — so the child can only succeed by generalizing "narrow doesn't mean less," never by remembering "those two cups."
- **THE PAYOFF:** a correct prediction → Bramble waters... no — serves the guest, a berry drops into his woven basket (a non-numeric abundance vessel). `ctx.correct()`. A wrong prediction → Bramble takes the hit warmly ("My eyes lied to BOTH of us — the wide one held more! Now we KNOW"), a berry STILL drops (a no-shame DISCOVERY), and the structure is re-offered later on a fresh pair.
- **Anti-generic statement:** NOT a pour-and-read-the-result demo (the thin weight-version's flaw) — (a) predict-FIRST is the graded act; the pour verifies (the standard, per pedagogy); (b) capacity decoupled from appearance + ≥60% anti-correlated assessed rounds make the conservation cognition load-bearing; (c) the **novel-instance re-offer + the stateful gate solver** kill the pair-memorization cheat (the critic's deepest fix); (d) the gate proves judge-by-height/width/area + observe-the-pour + constant + **memorize-exceptions** solvers ALL fail while the reason-about-capacity oracle passes.

## 3. Core loop & the cognitions (6 K cognitions → ≥7 experiences)
**Loop (`stage`: meet-the-cups → predict (commit) → pour (verify) → describe):** two empty cups appear (Bramble guesses, sometimes right) → the child commits more/less/same (locked BEFORE the pour) → the pour transfers + resolves (overflow/exact/room) → Bramble reacts in-character → on illusion+same rounds the child RE-SELECTS the word from understanding (describe-by-recall) → berry drops → `ctx.correct()` (or the warm-discovery path on a miss, then a novel-instance re-offer later). **No timer/score/streak/countdown/fail/attempts/"X of Y".** Per-pass order-only reshuffle within the assessed body.

**The 6 K cognitions → ≥7 distinct EXPERIENCES (the critic's F4 — enumerated, NOT fixture-row counting):**
| # | Experience | The child's act | Why genuinely distinct |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **clear-cut match** (on-ramp, NON-assessed) | predict when appearance MATCHES reality (tiny cup vs huge jug) | learns the predict→pour→confirm loop; Bramble trusts appearance + is right |
| 2 ★ | **illusion: wide-short WINS** (`wide-wins`) | predict more for the short-wide over the tall-narrow | suppress "taller = more" (width-cue fools) |
| 3 ★ | **illusion: tall-narrow holds LESS** (`tall-less`) | predict the tall-narrow holds LESS | the sharpest "tall ≠ more" confront |
| 4 ★ | **will-it-fit** (`fit` — binary directional) | "pour the BOWL into the BOTTLE — will it fit, or overflow?" (yes/no) | a one-directional containment judgment (different answer-set than more/less/same) |
| 5 ★ | **same-capacity reveal** (`same` — CELEBRATION, NON-assessed) | predict; the pour reveals EXACTLY-fills → "they're the SAME!" (Bramble surprised whichever you said) | the conservation capstone + the "same" word (an exposure, not a graded test — the critic's F2) |
| 6 ★ | **describe-by-recall** (`describe` — on illusion+same) | after the pour, the result hidden again, RE-select the more/less/same word from the now-understood model | productive RECALL (vs recognition-during-prediction) — a distinct cognition (the critic's F3) |
| 7 ★ | **novel-instance transfer** (`transfer` — the re-offer) | a fooled structure re-tested on a fresh-looking pair → apply "narrow≠less" to a NEW pair | the generalized-rule application (the critic's 1a — kills pair-recall) |
- **Honest framing (pedagogy + critic):** **6 K cognitions** (clear-cut / wide-wins / tall-less / will-it-fit / same-reveal / describe-recall) → **≥7 distinct EXPERIENCES** via the illusion-type split (wide-wins vs tall-less) + describe-by-recall + the novel-instance transfer. Container-swaps are flavor ONLY. **DROPPED:** order-three (Grade-1 `1.MD.A.1` seriation — not a K cognition); any numeric/unit/line content (`3.MD.A.2`); counting (Game 19). The gate asserts ≥7 by enumerating these experiences.
- **The assessed-set split (the critic's 1c):** **Phase 1 on-ramp** (~3 clear-cut rounds) = NON-assessed scaffolding (excluded from the gate's pass/fail denominator). **Phase 2 assessed body** = ≥60% anti-correlated (appearance fails), never two hard illusions back-to-back; the judge-by-height solver is scored ONLY against Phase 2 (must drop ≤40% = clear fail).
- **Free slice = clear-cut on-ramp + wide-wins + tall-less + the novel-instance transfer** — proves the predict-first + the conservation cognition + the transfer; the judge-by-height + memorize-exceptions solvers fail.

## 5. Pedagogy
- **K.MD.A.2 instantiation:** the child COMMITS a more/less/same comparison claim about a non-visual attribute (capacity), confronting the conservation illusion, and the pour DIRECTLY COMPARES (transfers) to verify — "directly compare... to see which has more... and describe."
- **THE KEYSTONE RULES (pedagogy + critic):** (i) **grade the PREDICTION** (the pour is the standard's verification, not a cheat). (ii) **predict-FIRST** (lock before the pour/level exists — the only guard; structurally enforced). (iii) **capacity decoupled from appearance** (the conservation mechanism; ≥60% anti-correlated assessed). (iv) **the re-offer re-tests the STRUCTURE on a NOVEL pair** (kills pair-recall — the deepest fix). (v) **mandatory clear-cut on-ramp** (non-assessed) then interleaved illusions (never 2 hard back-to-back); warm surprise, never a fail. (vi) **same-capacity = celebration-reveal**, not a graded K trap. (vii) **strictly non-numeric** (more/less/same).
- **§20.8 demonstrate-don't-touch (the critic's headline):** as an identical-pair-re-offer design it TOUCHED K.MD.A.2 (a child rote-memorizes which pairs are tricky — zero conservation; the gate blind to the stateful cheat). As a novel-instance-re-offer + decoupled-capacity + assessed-anti-correlated design it DIRECTLY INSTANTIATES capacity-conservation (generalizing "narrow≠less" is the only path to predict right). The line is exactly: the re-offer is a fresh-looking pair of the same structure. The gate (memorize-exceptions + judge-by-height/width/area + observe-the-pour + constant all FAIL; the reason-about-capacity oracle PASSES) is the machine-checked proof.
- **also_teaches:** NONE (order-three Grade-1 `1.MD.A.1` dropped; no order-three round ships). Distinct from E2 (visual length), Game 28 (measure-to-line units), Game 19 (count).
- **Grade fit:** Grade K; NON-NUMERIC more/less/same; the conservation illusion is in-grade as a "surprise to learn" (pre-conservers get the disequilibrium, immediately resolved by the concrete pour); same-capacity is the hardest → celebration-reveal not a test.
- **No-shame:** the heaping berry basket is the reward; Bramble takes every wrong-hit; the same-reveal is pure delight; the re-offer converts a surprise to mastery; the badger never red-X'es the child.

## 6. Interaction & UX
- **Runtime: meet → predict (commit) → pour (verify) → describe.** `capacity-core.js` holds the round `{ id, cog, phase ('on-ramp'|'assessed'), assessment (bool), containers:{A,B:{shape, drawnHeight, drawnWidth, trueCapacity (integer, NEVER in pre-commit DOM)}}, structuralSignature (for the gate's re-offer/transfer), committedPrediction (null until commit), options ('A_MORE'/'B_MORE'/'SAME' or 'FITS'/'OVERFLOWS') }` + `commitPrediction`/`playPour` (throws if not committed) + `trueComparison` (pure fn of trueCapacity) + `isCorrect` (committedPrediction vs trueComparison). **The answer = `f(reason about capacity + commit a claim)`, never `f(read the pour)` / `f(judge by height/width)` / `f(memorize the pair)`.**
- **State separation (anti-cheat):** `snapshot()` (pre-commit) exposes `{ cog, containers (shape + drawn geometry ONLY — NO trueCapacity, NO liquid level, NO fill), options }`. `facts()` exposes per-round `{ predictionBeforePour (===true), resultNotPreshown (no level/capacity/numeric in pre-commit DOM, BOTH render modes), capacityIsInteger, appearanceCanMislead, heightAnticorrelatedInAssessedBody (≥60%), reofferUsesNovelSurfaceSameStructure (===true), noPairRepeatsIdentically (===true), sameCapacityIsCelebrationNotAssessed (assessment:false), willItFitIsBinaryDirectional, gradeIsPredictionNotPour }`.
- **Predict + pour + describe:** 3 real `<button>`s (more/less/same — keyboard/SR-native, ≥44px; the graded act); commit locks (a 2nd commit is a no-op); the pour plays (`transform:scaleY` liquid + a static fading spill glyph on overflow); on illusion+same rounds the describe-by-recall re-selects the word (result hidden again). NO Check button (the buttons ARE the commit).
- **Mobile @280px (the critic's R6):** ONE shared SVG coordinate space holding BOTH containers (so the relative drawn-height contrast is honest — NOT per-box auto-fit, which would flatten the illusion); the 3 choice buttons flex-wrap ≥44px; the pour animates in-place (no layout shift). `audit-game-mobile.js` at 280/360/768.
- **Accessibility (the critic's F8 — the surprise as a CONTRAST):** the prediction is keyboard/SR-native (the graded act is identical for an SR child). aria-live announces the SHAPE ("a tall narrow glass / a short wide bowl" — never capacity), the committed choice, and the pour result **as an expectation-violation**: "[your choice] — let's see… the tall narrow glass actually held LESS! It overflowed the bowl." (the "actually" + shape descriptor carries the conservation surprise verbally — spoken for sighted too). Reduced-motion → instant VERIFIED render (result ONLY then; `RESULT_NOT_PRESHOWN` holds on both paths). Mute-safe. Not color-only.
