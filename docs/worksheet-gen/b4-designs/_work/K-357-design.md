## 4 Answer-hiding + uniqueness

- **Sort (base):** the answer is the item's material -> the locale bin whose `materials` list holds it. The bank stores ONE material per item, fixed by opening the picture (`picOpened:true`), composites excluded outright (egg carton, pasta bag, binder, crayon, tape, lantern, sauce bottle). Uniqueness = every material drawn on a page maps to EXACTLY ONE bin in that locale (gate; no glass+metal ONE bin except where the locale table says so, no `[NSR-FLAG][no]`), and every bin gets >= 1 item (5-bin quota above). A wrong line is visible: a bin with none, or an apple in the paper bin.
- **Which bin:** one product per row, the N mini bins in the FIXED locale order on every row (d3 shuffles per row); the child circles one. Uniqueness = the same single material -> bin map; wrong = a circled bin of another colour, visible at a glance because the order is constant and the legend strip at the top shows the order with the words.
- **Write the material:** the row stamps `data-lcs-answer-word` = the locale material literal (`materials.paper` etc.); the boxes count equals its grapheme length; the bank prints the 4 words; wrong = a word in the wrong row or a word twice.
- **Odd one out:** 3 products of one material + 1 of another; `data-rc-odd="1"` on the odd tile only; the odd index never the same on 3 consecutive rows; wrong = the pencil circle on a same-material tile.
- **Colour the bins:** the bin outline carries `data-lcs-fill="<colourKey>"` on its fill group; the legend pairs chip + word; legend order != bin order (shuffle until they differ in >= 3 positions, N = 2: swapped); nothing prints the colour on the bin (the word on the bin's pill is the same string as in the legend, a visual match a K child does without reading; the COLOUR is the answer). Wrong = a lid crayoned in a colour the legend gives another word.
- **Three things I recycle:** open; no verify() beyond the layout lints + `verify-ruling-starters.js`.
- Every page stamps `data-rc-mode`; verify() branches on it, never on the level index.

## 8 Two alternatives + recommendation

- **Alt A, the 4 + 1 bin split (four bins on the bin row, the fifth on a second row above it).** Rejected with arithmetic: bins 4 x 146 + 3 x 14 = 580 on row 2 and one 146 bin on row 1; row 1 sits 176 + 30 above row 2, so every line from the strip to a row-2 bin crosses row 1's zone and the fifth bin's pill; the K line zone shrinks from 425 to 219; and the fifth bin (always the residual one) reads as "the odd bin", a false cue. The overhang budget (§2) keeps all five on one row in every measured locale.
- **Alt B, cut-and-glue sort (a scissors strip of products, glue into bins).** Rejected: `picture-word-cards` (K-324) owns the cut-and-play materials family; cut lines eat ~40 px; the line sort delivers the same classification in one pencil.
- **Chosen: one strip over one bin row (base) + five distinct-act faces on the same drawn bin at three sizes.** The `.sci-*` strip-over-bins look is the house science pattern (K-211, K-235 renders), the K child draws eight short lines, and the SAME bins (colour + lid mark + word) recur on every face as the type's constant, which is the whole teaching: this colour, this word, this bin.

## 9 Risks, mitigations, print check

- **Long labels.** The only real width risk is the residual/composite bin (it `Indifferenziato` 140.3, no `Glass og metall` 140.0, fr a colour-named bin). Mitigation: the §2 overhang budget (pill <= 151, adjacent half-sum <= 125, row margins 17) is measured, not assumed, by the type gate with the real fonts on every locale table; the fix on failure is a shorter panel literal. A bin table with two adjacent 140 px labels does not exist in the panel's sets.
- **Greyscale.** Lids: blue and brown print as one dark grey, green and `inkSoft` as two mid greys 3 % apart (mock rendered with `filter:grayscale(1)`); the mandatory lid MARK (solid / dots / bars / bar / zigzag, keyed on position) plus the pill word keep five bins distinct in mono; teal strokes and coral dots print as the house's two greys. The engineer renders the sort, which-bin and colour pages greyscale for the critic before the wave. On the colour face nothing is coloured by the page, so mono is moot.
- **Mini bins at 44 px (which-bin chips).** The mark features are 2-3 px; `dots` is the weakest (mock at 44: marginal). Mitigation: the clamp `max(2px)` in `primitives/bin.js`, the fixed row order, the 56 px key strip with words at the top, and the K min element counted on the 64 px tile. Never draw a bin under 40 wide.
- **Picture ambiguity.** The library draws products: a milk bottle that is glass, a soda bottle whose material nobody can read. Mitigation: one material per item fixed by OPENING (the bank's `picOpened:true`), the OUT list in §5, twins never on one page, and the panel's "when it is empty" instruction. A locale panel may still reclassify (fi: `bucket` is not packaging) and does so in its bank, never in code.
- **Thin plastic pool (4).** d2 quota asks plastic for 2 at most; the odd face uses plastic as the odd item; the write face draws 2 plastic rows from 4. The gate refuses a quota a pool cannot fill.
- **Overflow in long locales.** No wrapping text exists: pills nowrap + measured, bank one measured row, starters gate-measured, letter boxes <= 9. Three-line title budgeted (722); fi four-line (677) costs the sort zone 45 px and the which-bin face its 20 px slack (702 -> **the fi title of face b must stay <= 3 lines**, a validator check).
- **Pencil space.** Sort zone 425; which-bin chips 64 x 80 (a circle fits); letter boxes 30; colour bins 84 x 224 bodies; lanes 400 x 56 x 2 + a 170 x 150 draw box.
- **Cut lines:** none on any face. **9 px floor:** the smallest text is the 15 px legend pill; the lint cannot fire.
- **What the QA lint catches:** overflow (the first mock proved it would: 681.6 > 675), footer intrusion, palette (the `.rc-*` CSS and `bin.js` use only token hex; the mock's white marks are `#FFFFFF` = `color.white`), the 9 px floor, a blank body (`[data-ws-content]` on every root). **What only the type gate catches:** icon floors (60 K / 52 G1), tile-row no-wrap, pill widths + adjacency (real fonts), material -> exactly one bin per locale, quota reachable, twins, marks == index, `fill:'none'` purity, legend != bin order, boxes == graphemes, starter width. **What only a human eye catches:** a lid colour that is not the locale's street colour (the panel's job), a residual-bin label that is adult register, a product a national scheme sorts differently (de toothbrush = Restmüll; kept OUT of the bank for that reason), the mono marks in a real print.
- **Factory traps (recorded):** `science-category-sort.js` cannot hold 8 items above the K floor (52 px) and has no pill overhang budget; `sort-to-bins.js` bins are icon-circles; `colorLegend` has no grey. None of their `build()`s is called; their looks are.
