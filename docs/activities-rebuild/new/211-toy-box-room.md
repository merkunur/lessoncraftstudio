## The skill
Use position words — on, under, beside, in front of, behind, inside, outside, left and right — to place and find objects.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE TOY THAT SETTLES WHERE THE WORD SAYS. One big open crate on four short legs stands in the middle of a room floor drawn in gentle perspective with three lanes: a back lane (behind), the crate's lane, and a front lane (in front of); a faint seam down the floor marks left and right. A library toy waits in a corner; the task line names one position word with a small icon (crate + dot). The child drags the toy anywhere and lets go: it settles into the nearest zone — on the lid → ON, into the open top → INSIDE (half hidden by the rim), between the legs → UNDER, the floor at the crate's side → BESIDE, the back lane → BEHIND (partly hidden by the crate), the front lane → IN FRONT OF; in the inside/outside rounds the crate is replaced by a drawn fence pen and the far floor is OUTSIDE. The toy only ever rests in one zone. Wrong: it stays where it settled and a ghost of it appears in the asked zone with a dotted arrow from the crate; miss 2: the asked zone glows. Find mode: three different toys already sit in three zones and the child taps the one the word names. Left/right rounds: the room is seen from the child's side, with two library hands (body-parts/hand, one CSS-mirrored) lying on the floor edges, so left and right are the child's own. Right: the toy gives a small hop and the crate creaks.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Confusing on/in, under/behind, and left/right (mirror reversal).
- **Pictures:** toys/ball, car, doll, blocks, robot, dinosaur, train; pets/cat, dog as movers (one per round); body-parts/hand (mirrored for the other hand); crate, fence pen and room lanes drawn as apparatus
- **Settings:** Words: on–under–beside–inside–outside / left–right–front–behind · Mode: place it / find it
- **Rounds:** 9 rounds per word set, every word used at least once and none three times running; in find mode the three toys' screen positions rotate so the answer is left/middle/right a third of the time each; one right answer because the crate (or pen) is the single reference.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** K-064, K-065, K-066, K-067
- **Distinct from:** The Parking Tower (above / below / between on floors) remains for vertical order; this covers the in-room words it lacks.
- **Build risk:** Words live only in the task line, never on the apparatus. Native check that each zone matches the locale's word: de auf/unter/neben/in/vor/hinter, fr sur/sous/à côté de/dans/devant/derrière, fi postpositions (päällä, alla, vieressä, sisällä, edessä, takana, ulkopuolella). 'Behind' must stay legible: the toy is drawn partly hidden, never fully.
