# The year map, language `en`

GENERATED by `scripts/units/year-map.js` from the live units, the checklist pages and the curriculum registry. Do not edit by hand; the rulings are in `scripts/units/year/answers.en.json`.

A school year is 36 weeks. By the ruling of a US teacher-reviewer (2026-11-12) 28 of them are teaching weeks (26 in Grade 3): the rest goes to the first weeks of school, the benchmark windows, report-card weeks, lost days and the last week. A lesson carries 2 days in Kindergarten and 2.5 on average in Grades 1 to 3; a unit ends with 3 days (review, check, respond). Literacy is counted with the same rule until it has had its own review.

> Two class days is the base for one lesson in every grade: a teach day and a practice-and-check day. A lesson earns a third day only when it is a story-problem, missing-number, fact-family, make-ten, teen-number, regrouping, fraction-on-the-number-line or cut-the-shape lesson AND it has a classroom tool, pages at three levels and a worksheet-maker page with new numbers for the re-teach group; about one lesson in three in Kindergarten and about half in Grades 1 to 3 meet that. A practice-format lesson with no new idea (Color by Number, a maze, an addition pyramid) is one day or a center, and a fluency lesson (Fact fluency within 10, 2.OA.B.2, 3.OA.C.7) is one launch day plus five minutes a day for weeks, never a three-day block.
