# Landing audit pass 1 — nl

1. 1. K-381: both cut-out strips are a cyclic ROTATION of the answer order (apple strip prints core, whole, bitten = eerst/dan/tot slot shifted by one; letter strip prints drawing, envelope, blank sheet = the same shift). A child who learns take-the-last-card-first solves both strips without reading the story; the nt10-E rotation tell again. Landing p3 warns teachers; the generator should derange differently per strip.
2. 2. K-382: step 1 (hands wet under the running tap) and step 4 (rinse) are drawn almost identically, hands under the tap with soap on the edge; the only cue is a few bubbles in the basin on the rinse card. For a kleuter 1 and 4 are effectively interchangeable, a practical second right answer. Landing p2 names the cue.
3. 3. G1-403: sheet title/h1 says voor, TIJDENS en na het poetsen, but every card prints the chips voor / BIJ / na het poetsen and the instruction says voor, bij of na. The design file (§4 nl) asked for ervoor / tijdens / erna. Title and page use different words for the middle phase; landing title follows the page (bij) and p2 glosses bij = tijdens.
4. 4. G1-404: title names one habit (hoest en nies in je elleboog) but the page has FOUR rows: elbow sneeze vs spray, soap-and-foam vs water only, own cup vs one shared cup, tissue in the bin vs on the floor. Title undersells three of four rows. Row 2 is subtle: both figures wash under the tap, only the bubbles differ.
5. 5. G1-405: title lists poetsen, wassen, bewegen en slapen but the chart prints FIVE rows; water drinken is on the page and missing from the title (7 days ma-zo, 35 boxes; week starts Monday, correct for nl).
6. 6. G2-379: title Hygiëne en verzorging: waarom doen we dat? but only 2 of the 5 habits printed are hygiene (handen wassen, tanden poetsen); the others are running/bewegen, sleeping and a hat in the sun (Zo verbranden we niet). The head word hygiëne over-claims; landing p2 says so.
7. 7. K-380: child 3 (hand at nose with a puff) reads as sneezing OR blowing the nose; tissue box is the intended match but a teacher may accept soap (wash after sneezing). Minor ambiguity; landing p3 turns it into a talking point.
8. 8. G1-398: bever is printed with habitats Bos / Regenwoud / Vijver / Savanne; beavers live in water at the forest edge, so A (bos) is a defensible second answer. On this draw Bos gets only ONE animal (hert) while Vijver gets three (zwaan, bever, eend), which nudges a child to balance the letters and put the bever under A.
9. 9. G1-407: the header line Elk dier heeft eten, water en een huis nodig names WATER, but the page has only two columns (Eten, Huis) and no water option: apparatus named but not drawn. Also the correct home sits in the first home slot in 3 of 4 rows (beverburcht, web, hol) - a mild position pattern.
10. 10. G2-381: instruction says Teken drie dieren but the page has NO drawing box; under Dieren die hier wonen there are only three ruled writing lines (the child must draw into the vijver scene). Instruction also says omcirkel IN ELK PAAR het juiste woord but only ONE pair (nat / droog) is printed.
11. 11. G2-382: omcirkel wat drijft has no visible target. The two clay shapes (ball, boat) get a coloured circle in their own bak; the unlabelled strip below with a nail and a ship is presumably the circle task, but nothing on the page ties the verb to it.
12. 12. G2-380: seven animals A-G for six sentences; D (gorilla) matches no sentence. Probably an intended distractor, but the instruction does not say one letter stays unused, so children hunt for a seventh sentence.
13. 13. G2-377: the sheet title printed ON the page says Aan elkaar schrijven GROEP 3, while the family band is G2 = groep-4 level key, so the landing eyebrow/level chip says groep 4 and the sheet says groep 3. A level word inside a sheet title contradicts the band (the Germanic SEO record says never hand-add the level word). Landing p3 explains it; the sheet string should drop groep 3.
14. 14. G2-385: title twee letters tegelijk and instruction de twee grijze letters, but row 2 is ij, which Dutch schools teach as ONE letter (de lange ij); the family design file §4 nl itself says IJ is one letter. Either drop ij from the nl joins pool or reword to verbindingen. Landing p2 flags it.
15. 15. K-379: story 2 (a drawing that grows while a crayon shrinks) draws the crayon as a flat orange bar lying in the card corner; a kleuter is unlikely to read it as a krijtje, so only the growing outline drawing carries the order. Not wrong, but weaker than the banana story.
16. 16. G1-400: story 3 (sneeuwbal) option 1 is a plate with dark dots and a striped object (a crumb/cake frame from another story) that a child cannot name; harmless distractor but unnameable. Correct answers sit at positions 1, 3, 2 (no position tell).
17. 17. K-383: title names four homes (nest, hol, mierenhoop, beverburcht) while SIX are drawn (plus spinnenweb and bijennest in a hollow tree). Acceptable, noted; landing title swaps mierenhoop for web to cover the page better.
