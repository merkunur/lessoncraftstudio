**The ruling (operator, 2026-07-10, verbatim intent):** "An interactive storybook means a storybook with activities in it to teach a teaching point." The first 14 shipped library items (shellys-seashells … poppys-market-day) were ruled activity sequences — one bare instruction line per page, no story — "the same as the activities we created before, so we can add them to the activities."

**Why:** the storybooks are the premium product; a mascot + an imperative ("Tap five fireflies!") is an activity with decoration. A storybook = picture-book NARRATIVE — multi-cue prose + character dialogue, a page-1 promise paid off at the end, story-only opener/closer pages — where each activity is DIEGETIC: the prose sets it up ("the tall reeds went rustle, rustle…"), the child's act IS the next story event, the success line is the story consequence ("Out waddled the first little duckling. One!"), and the next page's prose continues from the new state.

**How to apply (structural, gate-enforced since `5cfc7061`):**
- `gate-story.js` `story-presence` HARD gate: ≥half the pages carry ≥2 narration cues; ≥2 distinct speaking characterIds (dialogue); ≥1 no-characterId cue (narrator voice). GRADE_ENV re-specified for prose (PK 4 cues/page × 12 words/cue … G3 6×18; per-cue cap = the caption band shows one cue at a time).
- The player already supported it all: sequential cues w/ speaker prefix + talk animation, `gate:"end"` locks the activity until the story lines finish, interaction-less pages auto-advance. `qa-storybook.js` traverses narrative-only pages; `blueprint-to-skeleton.js` treats characterId-less lines as narrator.
- Authoring: playbook §intro/§2/§3 hold the storybook definition. First true exemplar: `five-little-ducklings` (30 cues, 12 narrator lines, Willa+Pip dialogue, 2 story-only pages, 7 diegetic activities, the found-duckling line painted growing in every scene).
- The old 14 stay on disk pending the operator-directed activities migration; registry rows get re-pointed as each is rebuilt as a true storybook (`displacedActivity` note field, row 1 = the precedent).

**v2 (2026-07-10, operator: "there is still no story" — after the first 3 "true" storybooks).** The first fix was insufficient in BOTH dimensions and the deeper lesson is now structural (`af787c11`):
1. **DISPLAY: story text must be a READABLE BOOK PAGE.** Multi-cue captions were still subtitle fragments that erased themselves. The player now has the **book panel** (`.sb-book`): the page's sentences ACCUMULATE into one flowing paragraph in a cream picture-book text panel (current sentence highlighted while read), collapsing to a 📖 pill when the activity starts; story-only pages keep the full text. If the page's text isn't sitting there readable like a printed book page, it is not a storybook.
2. **WRITING: prose, not stage directions.** No chat prefixes EVER — dialogue is embedded in narrative prose with quotes + said-verbs ('"Oh no," quacked Willa.'); narrative past tense, sensory detail ("the pond glowed like warm honey"), character interiority, a mid-arc wobble, and interactive pages END on a direct-address invitation ("Can you find her?"). ~40-70 words/page, ~340-450/story. If a sentence could be a subtitle, rewrite it.
Gate v2: sentence caps (PK 16w × 6/page) + story-presence v2 HARD (≥3 sentences on ≥half the pages; ≥2 speakers; narrator voice; **≥220 total EN words**). All three books rewritten to this standard in place (scenes/interactions untouched).

**v3 (2026-07-10, operator's third correction: "no connection between the pages and the exercises… the starting page disappears immediately… Story! Fucking story!").** Two EXPERIENCE laws, both structural now (`e2f8bf24`):
1. **THE READER TURNS THE PAGE — the engine NEVER auto-advances.** Story pages were flipping ~600ms after the narration promise resolved (instantly with sound off) — the named bug. Now every completed page pulses the next arrow gently and waits. QA requires the page-done pulse + turns via `SB_PLAYER.next()`.
2. **THE CHILD ACTS ON THE STORY'S OBJECTS IN THE STORY'S WORLD — quiz chrome = instant failure.** White option cards, borders, Check buttons make the story vanish at the moment of action. sb-listen options are now frameless scene objects; sb-listen-place drags the bare story object (no white disc; invisible full-size hit circle keeps the PK drag floor); `sb-choice-board` is BANNED in storybooks (its pages became in-scene find-object taps: the correct object completes, the decoy wobbles). Scene paint must not duplicate module-rendered objects (the four-doors lesson).
The full concept in the operator's words: **"Story! Activities related to the story which become more interesting because of the story."** Story first; the activity is a story moment, felt inside the story world, at the reader's own pace.

**v4 (2026-07-10, operator's FOURTH correction: "You write a beginning and end and fill the pages between them with exercises out of the blue… Every page should develop the story. Also, why long text? Is it age appropriate!").** Two PLOT/TEXT laws, both structural (`06d3a87c`):
1. **EVERY PAGE DEVELOPS THE STORY — the GOAL-DECOMPOSITION anti-pattern is unshippable.** The rejected shape (the-green-berry v1): a beginning + an end with N similar steps toward ONE static goal between them ("find the reds… place the blue… now the yellow…") — after each page NOTHING has changed. The law: **the page-turn test** (after every page the child can answer "what NEW thing just happened?" with a fact that wasn't true before) + **causal chaining** (each bravo CREATES the next page's situation — "Found! Then a magpie swooped down and grabbed the blueberry!"). Design a story as a chain of EVENTS (new problem / new place / new discovery / rising clock), never as one goal split into steps. Gate: every blueprint page requires `storyTurn` ("what changes on this page"); missing or pairwise near-duplicate turns (Jaccard >0.6 on content words) = HARD.
2. **BOARD-BOOK TEXT — long dense pages are NOT age-appropriate.** The v2 "40-70 words/page paragraph" spec is REJECTED for young grades. PK: ≤9 words/sentence × ≤4 sentences × ≤32 words/page, whole book 140-300 EN words (K 11×5×44 [170-360] … G3 17×6×88 [280-620]). GRADE_ENV enforces per-sentence + per-page + whole-book (floor AND ceiling) in both blueprint and story modes.
The corrected reference: **the-green-berry v2** — spill → reds found → magpie theft → stream rescue → boil-over save → the strange green berry → planted in the sun → the fair bell → just-in-time triumph → the rosy payoff (36 cues, 239 words, every bravo a turn). NOTE: shipped #1-#4 (incl. ratified five-little-ducklings, ~380 words) are GRANDFATHERED — they now exceed the PK band and vary in page-turn strength; retrofit is an operator call, flagged not auto-run.
