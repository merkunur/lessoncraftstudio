**THE REAL CAUSE (triangulated: live-site + codebase audit + `site:`/query searches) — three compounding structural failures, NOT domain authority, NOT a canonical/noindex/hreflang bug (those are all CLEAN):**
1. **(DOMINANT) The bulk of the ~44k sitemap URLs is thin/near-duplicate/noindex, so Google won't index it.** ~30k `/worksheets/[slug]` landings are slot-fill `p1/p2/p3` bodies with only the theme noun swapped (the generator even runs a *slot-normalized* similarity gate — proof of structural dup); ~22k `/topic/[a]/[b]` intersections are `noindex` until hand-authored (`[secondary]/page.tsx:472-482`); topic/intersection meta descriptions fall back to an identical ~95-char template (`topic/[slug]/page.tsx:252-259`). Empirically: `/en/worksheets/treasure-hunt-bakery` does NOT surface even for its own title → the 30k landings are effectively unindexed.
2. **Google's index is a stale graveyard of the DISCONTINUED seller identity.** `site:` + real queries surface the OLD KDP/Etsy pages — `/apps/*` ("Bulk Create KDP Activity Book Pages" now 301→/tools), `/guides/*` `/blog/*` (410), `/faq` (404), `/tools/profit-hub` (410) — still indexed with "Create & Sell / KDP / Etsy" titles. Muddies the entity, repels teachers, wastes crawl budget. (One `/en/tools/treasure-hunt-maker` result even shows a stale Norwegian title — past locale-bleed, since fixed.)
3. **Demand misalignment + no authority concentration.** Combinatorial theme×type×grade landings target near-zero-volume long-tail; the finite HIGH-demand queries are served by thin/stale pages. Authority spread across ~65k thin URLs. Keyword research only ever did es/en/de titles (§22.4).
