## 2. Concept & creative hook
**Nila** is a calm otter who floats on her back in a still **reading-pond**, listening to little stories the pond tells. A short informational paragraph is **read aloud** (Nila "hears" it; the child hears it). Four **idea-fish** drift up — each, when tapped, **speaks a short phrase about the story** (a same-grain statement; NO printed general word, NO distinguishing picture — audio only). The child's job: **find the one big-idea fish all the others belong to**, net it, and watch the little detail-fish **come home** beneath it.
- **THE SPOKEN-PHRASE FISH (Designer B's source-level anti-cheat, baked):** each fish carries a SHORT spoken situation-phrase, all four **same-grain, same syntactic register** (declaratives; the critic's rework B kills the "how…/about…" frame). There is NO printed generality to grab by vocabulary, and NO per-fish content-picture to match to the scene (rework C) — **the ONLY signal that sorts topic-from-detail is having heard the paragraph.** Natively 11-language-ready (phrases are real DOM/SVG text + TTS).
- **THE NET → COMMIT → REVEAL (the hide-reveal payoff + the Game-29 anti-brute-force, rework D):** the child casts the net over a fish (it's HELD in the net — no gold yet) and presses **"That's the big idea!"** (the commit; the shell's no-Check-button rule is honored — the commit IS the single deliberate act, not a free exploratory tap). On a CORRECT commit → the fish rises, glows gold, an **umbrella unfurls** over it (the hierarchy made visible), and the details school beneath. On a WRONG commit → no gold, no leak; Nila **replays the paragraph** ("let's listen again") AND **regenerates a DIFFERENT paragraph from the same swap-group** (the same four fish, a different correct topic) → the target MOVES, so swap-until-umbrella collapses (brute-force structurally defeated — a guesser can't tap the next fish on the same board because the board changed).
- **THE SCHOOL + REJECT-THE-NON-BELONGER (the "retell KEY DETAILS" clause, load-bearing):** after the topic is netted, the remaining fish must be **dragged home** beneath the umbrella — but one is a NON-BELONGING detail (a plausible-but-absent fact, e.g. "bears catch fish in summer rivers" — true of bears, NOT in THIS paragraph). The child nests the true details and **sends the non-belonger back to the pond** (a child-initiated "this one wasn't in our story" release — NOT try-every-slot; rework D). Rejecting requires having heard what the paragraph actually said.
- **NO-SHAME:** a wrong cast/nest never buzzes, never reddens, never reveals the answer; the fish wriggles free with a giggle (IDENTICAL for every wrong fish → leaks nothing) and Nila **replays the paragraph** (the redirect is to the TEXT, never the answer — re-hearing IS the intervention). Unlimited replays + tries, no penalty, no counter. Non-numeric vessel = lily pads blooming.
- **THE PAYOFF (earned by comprehension):** correct topic + true details schooled + non-belonger released → the umbrella bobs, the school settles beneath it, Nila does a happy barrel-roll + names the idea in a full sentence ("So it was all about how a bear gets ready for winter!" — modeling the RI.K.2 retell). `ctx.correct()`.
- **Anti-generic statement:** NOT a vocabulary game with fish — (a) spoken same-grain register-uniform phrases (no printed generality, no content-picture) make comprehension the only signal; (b) PER-LABEL swap-invariance + the armed surface-solver gate-prove a frame/length/position/odd-one-out/head-noun solver scores ≤ chance while a comprehension oracle scores 100%; (c) commit + regenerate-on-wrong kill the Game-29 brute-force hole; (d) the non-belonging-detail makes the "retell key details" clause load-bearing; (e) the umbrella reveal is the hierarchy made spatial (the hide-reveal family at its most literal).

## 3. Core loop & the honest cognition-classes
**Loop (`stage`: net → school):** the paragraph reads aloud (auto + a "Read again" replay, sentence-highlighted) → the child taps fish to hear their phrases → casts the net on the gist fish + COMMITS ("That's the big idea!") → correct → the umbrella unfurls → the child drags the true details home + releases the non-belonger → all true details schooled → Nila names the idea → `ctx.correct()`. **No fail, no timer, no score, no Check-then-Next, no attempts-counter.** Wrong commit → replay + regenerate-from-swap-group (non-stationary retry). Wrong nest → replay + reset, no leak. First-attempt-correct is the internal signal (never shown).

**The genuine comprehension COGNITION-CLASSES (the critic's rework E — the core is honestly ~4 distinct ACTS, NOT 7; a new PARAGRAPH is the in-activity variety reserve, NOT a new cognition). Each round carries a `cog`:**
| # | Cognition-class (`cog`) | The child's act | Why distinct + comprehension-load-bearing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ★ | **pick-the-topic** (`pick-topic` — the SPINE) | among 4 same-grain spoken phrases, net the GIST the heard paragraph is mostly about | topic-vs-detail synthesis of the heard text; per-label swap-invariance + armed surface-solver make it un-cheatable by surface |
| 2 ★ | **nest-key-details + reject-non-belonging** (`nest-reject` — the 2nd RI.K.2 clause) | drag the true details home; SEND BACK the plausible-but-absent fact | "retell KEY DETAILS" — requires knowing what the paragraph actually stated (the non-belonger's noun is absent from the text) |
| 3 ★ | **reject-too-narrow** (`reject-narrow`) | a candidate topic fish is actually ONE detail dressed as the gist ("a bear eats food" offered as the topic); the child must NOT net it | the classic RI.K.2 error (a detail ≠ the topic) made a discrimination; distinct from pick-topic (rejecting an over-narrow gist, not selecting among details) |
| 4 ★ | **supply-the-missing-detail** (`supply-detail`) | after netting the topic, an empty nest-slot remains; pick which of 2 NEW fish was a key detail OF the heard text | recall + breadth — reach BACK into the heard text for an absent slot (distinct from rejecting; an active retrieval) |
- **Honest framing (the critic's reword + §A.13.60):** **4 genuine comprehension cognition-classes** (pick-topic, nest-reject, reject-too-narrow, supply-missing-detail). **CUT:** **too-BROAD** rejection (resurrects the most-general-word vocabulary cheat — the pedagogy excluded it); **two-true-topics/BEST-FIT** (a contestable graded-breadth judgment even adults dispute → a no-shame-game shame-machine at K; a Gr2-3 skill); **retell/ORDER** (informational text often has no canonical order → ambiguity; deferred). **The ≥7 distinct EXPERIENCES = the 4 cognition-classes × the paragraph pool** — and here a fresh INFORMATIONAL PARAGRAPH (new subject, new vocabulary, new world) is GENUINELY distinct-to-the-child (qualitatively unlike a number/shape-variant — the fraction-shape "≈2-reshuffled" precedent does NOT apply: each paragraph is a fresh comprehension act, not a cosmetic reskin). The pool is the variety reserve; the 4 classes are the cognition floor. Order-only reshuffle (fish positions per mount; paragraph order per pass). **Transparency note:** the ≥7 is 4-classes × genuine-paragraph-variety — surfaced honestly (not silently treating paragraph-variety as the cognition source).
- **Free slice = pick-topic (1) + nest-reject (2)** — both RI.K.2 clauses; a phrase-frame/length/position/odd-one-out/picture/audio-rote solver all fail immediately.

## 4. Win-states & juice (no competition)
No timer/score/streak/countdown/**fail-state**/attempts-counter/"X of Y". **THE UMBRELLA REVEAL (signature — the hierarchy made visible):** correct commit → the topic fish rises + glows gold + an **umbrella unfurls** (SVG canopy opening) → the true details **school home** beneath it (each a gentle swim-path settle) → the non-belonger flicks its tail at the pond's edge ("not this story!") → Nila barrel-rolls + **names the idea in a full sentence** (`ctx.speak()` — modeling the RI.K.2 retell, reinforcing the IDEA not the win). **Non-numeric vessel = lily pads blooming across the pond** (`GameCollection` — each solved round blooms a pad where Nila rests; the pond grows greener; full pass → Nila naps on the fullest pad). **Negative signal = a NON-LEAKING replay ONLY:** wrong cast/nest → the fish wriggles free with a giggle (IDENTICAL animation for every wrong fish → leaks nothing about which) + Nila replays the paragraph ("let's listen again"); on a wrong COMMIT, regenerate a different paragraph from the swap-group (non-stationary retry). Never a buzzer/red-X/reveal. **`ctx.wrong()` is a soft "let's listen again."** `ctx.correct()` on a complete correct round → auto-advance. Full-pass keepsake = the bloomed pond → cross-game `GameCollection`. CSS/SVG/WebAudio + TTS; reduced-motion (instant reveal); mute-safe (the paragraph text + the fish phrases-as-text + the replay carry it).

## 5. Pedagogy
- **RI.K.2 instantiation (main topic + key details, heard text):** the child identifies the GIST the heard paragraph foregrounds among same-grain candidates (clause 1: main topic) AND selects/retells its key details, rejecting a plausible-but-absent one (clause 2: key details). Both acts require the heard text; neither is solvable by vocabulary, surface-cue, picture, or rote.
- **THE KEYSTONE RULES (the critic's reworks — comprehension not surface):** (i) **SPOKEN same-grain REGISTER-UNIFORM phrases** (no printed generality, no "how/about" frame, no content-picture) → the only topic-vs-detail signal is the heard paragraph. (ii) **PER-LABEL swap-invariance** — every phrase is a topic under ≥1 paragraph AND a non-topic under ≥1 → no phrase-frame/audio-rote cue can be stable. (iii) **NON-BELONGING detail** (noun absent from the text) → the nest carries the "retell key details" clause. (iv) **COMMIT + REGENERATE-ON-WRONG** → comprehension, not swap-until-umbrella. (v) **REDIRECT TO THE TEXT** (replay), never the answer → re-hearing is the intervention. (vi) **NO content-pictures on fish** → no picture→scene-match cheat.
- **The comprehension cheat is the cohort through-line one level deeper (the critic's catch):** the spoken-phrase fix kills the printed-vocabulary cheat, but a PHRASE-FRAME cue ("how/about"-framed topic vs flat-declarative details) is the same cheat reborn at the syntax level — defeated ONLY by per-LABEL swap-invariance + register-uniform phrasing + the armed surface-solver (modeling frame/register/head-noun-generality). The picture→scene-match cheat is defeated by cutting per-fish pictures. The brute-force cheat is defeated by commit + regenerate-on-wrong (the Game-29 lesson).
- **§20.8 demonstrate-don't-touch:** as NAIVELY specified (superordinate over subordinates, or framed topic + pictures) it TOUCHES (vocabulary/syntax/picture-matching); as reworked (same-grain register-uniform spoken phrases + per-label swap-invariance + no pictures + non-belonging detail) it DIRECTLY INSTANTIATES both RI.K.2 clauses. The line is exactly the cheat-closures; the gate (armed surface-solver ≤ chance, comprehension oracle 100%) is the structural proof.
- **also_teaches:** **RI.1.2 only** (same skill, lighter scaffold). **RI.2.2 DROPPED** (multi-paragraph demand not instantiated by a single paragraph — drift; a genuinely multi-paragraph variant would be a separate deferred game).
- **Grade-K fit + scaffold:** 3 sentences (4 max), simple present-tense declaratives, ONE concrete picturable subject, Tier-1 vocabulary, informational (NOT narrative — a story has a "message," RL not RI). Read aloud, unlimited replays (the K "with prompting and support" accommodation). Scene art on the paragraph card illustrates but does NOT distinguish the topic fish (no picture-match cue).
- **No-shame:** the served-comprehension (the idea named, the pond bloomed) is the intrinsic reward; wrong → replay-the-text, never reveal; the giggle-wriggle is identical for every wrong fish.
