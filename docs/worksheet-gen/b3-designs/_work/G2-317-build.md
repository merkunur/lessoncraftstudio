## Deviations from the design file (each with the measured reason)
1. **Body budget = 710, not 722 (README) nor 760/736 (design).** Measured on this gate's WORST chrome (3-line title + 3-line instruction: body 710 px, the same floor G1-308's gate measured). Every stack budgets 710: tense 360 + 16 + 3 × 96 + 24 = 688; persons d2 364 + 16 + 312 = 692; d1 400 + 16 + 204 = 620. The lanes are `repeat(N, minmax(laneMin 96, 1fr))` and take the chrome's slack FIRST (flex-grow 100, capped at `laneMax` 132 / d1 150 each), then the table rows (`flex:1 0 rowH`) share what is left — so a one-line chrome (body 814) is not sparse and the worst chrome is not cut off. The design's lane 110 is therefore a *range* 96-132 (measured 103 at the worst chrome, 122 at the two-line title, 127 at a one-line title).
2. **en d1 `pool:'regular'` cannot make a 6-row tense table** (regular pictured = jump dance wander = 3): the bank's `poolMap:{regular:'all'}` (the design's own d2 exception, L.2.1.d) now applies at every level in en. The pool NAME is data per locale (`poolMap`: en `all`, fi `noGradation`, sv/da/no `weak`), never a locale branch in code.
3. **en d1 "6 gaps, past only" is impossible with `read` on the table** (its past is the anchor; only 5 past cells are gappable). d1 uses `gapCols:['past','pres'], gapOrder:'first'`: every row gaps its past, and a row whose past is an anchor gaps its present instead (`read` → `reads`). Six gaps, one per row, the present column otherwise printed — the nearest faithful thing.
4. **Item window at d1 = [4,16], not the G23 [8,16].** The design's d1 ladder (one table, 3 givens, minHardGaps 2, 2 lanes) yields 4-5 items in persons mode by construction (de `laufen`: 2 hard gaps + 2 lanes = 4). d2/d3 keep [8,16] (measured en 11 / 13, de fixture 9 / 11). `items` is a config key stamped on the root; `verify()` reads it.
5. **Tense-table box width is a constant 206** (d1's `boxW:400` is the persons single-table box; a 400 box does not fit the 220 tense column — the first d1 render overflowed the page box until this was split).
6. **Tense-row picture = 56 at d1, 44 at d2/d3** (`tenseIcon`, ≥ 36): the design fixes 44; at d1 the table has 190 px of slack to absorb (2 lanes) and 44 px pictures in 73 px rows read sparse.
7. **Rule (3) "pairwise-distinct matchPersons forms" is enforced on the REGULAR pool only, with a per-verb `match:false` opt-out.** Irregular verbs collide by nature (de `lesen` du/er `liest`, fr `courir` je/tu `cours`), and so do regular -s/-z stems (de `tanzen` du/er `tanzt`, `sitzen`) — a rule the design missed; the fixture marks `tanzen` `match:false`. Face 2 (Phase 2) must draw only `match !== false` regular verbs and count them against its floor.
8. **Rule (3) tense "past !== present !== infinitive" is enforced as present !== infinitive AND past !== present; past === infinitive is an ANCHOR (reported, allowed)** — the design's own `read` rule; a tense verb keeps ≥ 1 gappable cell by construction. Rule (4)'s "≥ 3 non-anchor rows" applies to persons mode only.
9. **Rule (6) "every verb has ≥ 3 distinct forms" would fail en on `read`** (read / reads / read). Implemented as a report ("F5 excludes read") + a Face-5 floor (≥ 8 such verbs; en has 27). Face 5 (Phase 2) must skip the excluded verbs.
10. **Floors (rule 8) are FAIL for en and REFUSALS (reported) for every other block** — the design's own wording ("the face is REFUSED for that locale, reported, never filled"); the hub-gate expectation is lowered from the report, never padded.
11. **The lone table is always inside the `data-lcs-tables` row wrapper.** Dropped straight into the column stack, a 675 px persons table read its `flex:0 0 675px` as a HEIGHT basis and pushed the lanes off the page (caught by the fixture d1 render + the lints).
12. **`en` verbs with no allowlist picture**: `writing` / `baking` / `artist` match no word-classes citation form (write / bake / paint are not in the en bank) and stay unassigned; `wander` takes `activities/hiking` (the critic's en candidate — a hiker with a backpack stepping over rocks; a weak cue, recorded as a panel call). `athlete` dropped (a second runner); swimming / singing / painting / playground are OBJECTS (goggles, a microphone, a palette, a climbing frame) — refused, and the gate refuses them on any block.
