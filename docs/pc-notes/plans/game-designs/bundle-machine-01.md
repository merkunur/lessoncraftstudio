## 2. Concept & creative hook
**Bundle Bot** ("Bolt") is an eager little factory-robot who LOVES tidying loose ones into neat tens — but Bolt is a clumsy counter and is **FORBIDDEN to bundle without you.** You're the foreman; Bolt is the apprentice who *wants* to bundle the second loose ones pile up, gets antsy ("mm? mm?!"), and needs YOU to say "that's ten — bundle it!" The agency hook: **you're in charge of the eager robot — count the loose ones, and when it's exactly ten, give Bolt the go to clunk them into one neat ten.**
- **THE EMBODIED SCATTER-TRAY (the critic's §9 fix — the child AUTHORS the ten):** loose ones land in a **loose, slightly-scattered tray** (NOT a pre-slotted clean ten-frame — that would let the child read "full" as a gestalt without ever holding "ten"). To bundle, the child must **count/recognize ten in the scatter themselves.** A **subitizable clump-feeder** drops small die-face groups (1-3 cubes per tap) — so every feed is a *"how many now? will the next overflow ten?"* judgment, NOT a blind tap. **An OPTIONAL "tidy tray" ten-frame** is available as the child's STRATEGY-tool (drag cubes into it to organize + verify) — the scaffold the child INVOKES, never the layout's pre-given answer.
- **THE CHILD-GATED BUNDLE (the no-auto-bundle spine):** Bolt is eager but inert. The child judges "that's ten" + **pulls the BUNDLE LEVER** (always present, NEVER glows — recognition, not a told-you-so). The lever reads the tray count at the pull-instant: **exactly 10 → the 10→1 CSS collapse + slide to the tens column** (Bolt's joyful clunk-reward); **NOT ten (a 9-that-looks-~10, or 8) → REFUSE** (Bolt sheepish, non-shame, "not ten yet — keep counting"). **The bundle is the CHILD's judged trigger; Bolt EXECUTES.** There is NO `if(ones===10)bundle()` code path — bundling is only reachable through the child's lever-pull.
- **Build toward a target ≥20** (2-4 tens; range 20-49) by repeated count-to-ten + author-the-bundle + the final loose ones. `solved = value===target && tens===tensNeeded` (the **CANONICAL-FORM requirement** — 34 reached as "2 tens 14 loose" is NOT solved → bundling is load-bearing, not optional). `ctx.correct()` from the solve.
- **THE OVER-FILL RECOVERY (a genuine decision the embodied-feed creates):** if a clump pushes the tray past ten (11+), the child must recognize "too many — I have to bundle the ten first" (a real judgment, NOT a blocked tap). The reborn over-build cognition.
- **UN-BUNDLE / TRADE-DOWN:** tap a banded rod in the tens column → it CSS-explodes back into a scatter of 10 loose cubes (the decompose direction; the rote-killer — a child who can un-bundle on demand can't be faking the bundle).
- **THE IMPOSTOR (count-not-gestalt, now NATIVE to the scatter):** sometimes the scatter LOOKS like ~ten but is nine — a child pulling on "looks full" gets a REFUSE; only a counted ten bundles. The single mechanic that forces actual counting over a fullness-gestalt.
- **NO-SHAME:** refuse-at-non-ten = "not ten yet — keep counting" (Bolt sheepish, non-directional, rooting for you); over-fill = "whoa, that's more than ten — bundle ten first?"; no buzzer. Non-numeric vessel = **Bolt's TIDY SHELF** filling with finished bundles (`GameCollection`).
- **Anti-generic statement:** NOT a tap-to-fill-and-watch-the-machine-bundle toy — (a) the embodied scatter-count makes the child AUTHOR the ten (the composite-unit is theirs, gate-proven); (b) the child-gated bundle (no auto-path) + the impostor-refuse kill the machine-does-the-cognition AND the fullness-reflex; (c) un-bundle proves reversibility (the rote-killer); (d) the over-fill recovery is a real decision; (e) a board ships only if a **tap-only solver** (never authors a bundle → tray maxes, tens empty), a **bundle-at-any-fill solver** (refuse-at-non-ten), AND a **fullness-reflex solver** (pulls on a looks-~10-but-9 scatter → REFUSE) all FAIL while a **count-and-author solver** passes (§8).
