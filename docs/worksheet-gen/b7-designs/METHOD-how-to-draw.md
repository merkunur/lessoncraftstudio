# METHOD — How to Draw (the template for every future expansion)

Operator OBS (2026-10-09): *"document the methods you use for creating these worksheets in detail because they will also serve as templates for future expansions."* This file is that record for `how-to-draw` (K-396 + 10 faces). Sections 1–4 are final (the engine); 5–9 are completed as the batch ships.

## 1 What a page is
A drawing lesson for ONE library drawing: a strip of 4–6 steps (each step adds lines in coral over the earlier lines in ink; the last panel is the finished drawing), plus the face's practice apparatus (a big empty box, a trace, a grid, a scene frame, a shuffled strip, ruled rows, a fold mark). PDF only: no answer key, no screen (the pencil is the point). Every face names its animal; the animal is the UNIT (`unitAxis`), so a future expansion adds animals, never faces.

## 2 Where the drawing comes from — and only from
One B&W library drawing (`cache/themes/<dir>/<noun>@3x.webp`), composed alone at `fit [520, 480]` units. The reviewed pool = the 200 heroes of the Color by Number scenes (`tools/htd-build.js` builds all of them); any other drawing is added with `--src="<dir>/<noun>"` after it was OPENED. Nothing is redrawn: every step is a subset of the drawing's own lines, and the union of the steps IS the drawing (gated pixel-exact: `stats.missing = extra = 0`).

## 3 The engine (how a lesson is derived) — `lib/htd-steps.js buildSteps(src, {steps})`
1. **Ink → parts.** `lib/cbn-lineart.js segmentInk` finds every closed part of the drawing; `silhouette` finds its body.
2. **Step 1 = the outer line**: the ink within 11 px of the paper (the silhouette's outline) — what a child draws first. Measured shares 0.25–0.63 of the ink.
3. **Inner lines → OWNERSHIP by part.** Every inner ink pixel belongs to the SMALLEST part within 14 px of it (a bounded BFS from each part through the ink and the unlabelled ring round it, smallest parts first); a part owns a line only if it is round enough to hold a 2.5-unit disc or ≥ 0.1 % of the body (a sliver — the gap an arm leaves — never owns). Unreached specks join the outline. This replaced connected-component "strokes": a dinosaur's spikes, legs and face lines are ONE connected ink component, which no component grouping can split.
4. **Classes per part:** structure (a part ≥ 3 % of the body: head/body dividers, legs, wings, ears) · features (≥ 0.4 %, not texture: eye rings, markings, paws) · details-thick (a blob thicker than 1.6 × the line: pupils, nostrils) · details-thin (whiskers, fur, hatching: a thin mark under 0.5 % of the ink).
5. **Order:** structure by part size (big first), then everything else top to bottom in 40-unit bands (so a face — eyes, pupils, nose, mouth — is one step), left to right.
6. **Groups:** `steps − 1` groups of roughly equal ink; the structure group closes when the first non-structure line arrives (if it carries ≥ ¼ of a target); a group may close at a change of part or band once it holds 70 % of its target; lines at one height are never split; too many groups → the smallest adjacent pair merges, never the face into the structure step while another pair exists.
7. **Shapes guide:** for the 4 biggest parts, the ellipse of inertia (coverage ≥ 0.75 both ways → ellipse) or the rounded box (fill ≥ 0.72 → box), else none.
8. `stepSvg(S, k, {width, shapes, outline:false, upTo, lastInInk})`, `fullSvg(S, {width, fill, without:[steps]})` render the panels; `stats` carries `outerShare`, `missing`, `extra`, `nSteps`.

`tools/htd-build.js` → `data/htd/<slug>.json` for all 200 heroes (plus `--src`), reading `data/htd/review.js` (`REFUSED[slug]`, `OVERRIDES[slug].steps`). Measured 2026-10-09: 200 built, 0 failed, 0 missing pixels, 4–5 steps each.

## 4 The human read (never skipped)
`node tools/htd-sheets.js --out=<dir>` → one row per drawing (every step, the full drawing, the shapes guide), four drawings per sheet. Read EVERY sheet of the pool a face may pin; a drawing whose strip does not read as a lesson (a step of one speck, a face split across steps, a filled blob counted as outline) gets `OVERRIDES[slug].steps` tried at 4 / 5 / 6, then `REFUSED[slug]` with the reason. The read of 2026-10-09 (sheets 02–05, 07, 08, 10, 13, 17, 25, 29 after the ownership rewrite): cat, dog, rabbit, dinosaur, elephant, owl, frog, fish, bear, hummingbird, pony, unicorn, butterfly all read as lessons; the panda's black ears count as outline (accepted); the bee's wings come late (accepted).

## 9 Traps paid for (running list)
- Connected components cannot split connected inner lines — own the pixels per part.
- Tiny eye whites fall under an area-only sliver threshold and the head claims the eye lines; the sliver test must be about SHAPE (inscribed radius), not area.
- Merging "the smallest adjacent groups" folds the face into the structure step; protect the structure boundary.
- A short line that separates a big part (a leg) is structure however short it is — a texture rule keyed on stroke length alone demotes legs.
- `lib/b7-common.js bankModule()` takes the module's FIRST export as the locale map — export `HOW_TO_DRAW` (or `FIND_THE_DIFFERENCES`) first.
- A unit token that carries an article (`{L}` = "a Horse") cannot sit after "the"; a bare-noun token `{N}` is a third, explicit form — never strip an article in code.
- An inline `<svg>` row inside a flex column carries a 6 px line-box descender (54 rendered for h 48): `line-height:0` on the container, or the 660 column overflows.
- A full card renders ph + 10, not the design's ph + 6: the FINAL's recorded 296 / 283 fallback geometry is what shipped; measure the rendered band before trusting an arithmetic stack.
- The greyscale gate measured anti-aliased rims and condemned every correct card (luma 177 / 88 instead of 151 / 54): measure the CORE pixels of each line (mask luma < 40), never move the band.
- `gen-b7var-specs.js` SKIPS an emitted spec that differs from what it would write (a hand-edit guard): a changed row needs `--overwrite`, or the gate keeps testing the old config (the finish fallback "did not apply" for one run).
- A comment appended to a one-line statement swallows the rest of the line: put the comment on its own line.
- `tools/b3-baseline.js` hashed pages whose clip ids came from a process-global counter (`animal-home.js` / `habitat-tile.js _uid`): 16 phantom drifts in the habitats family whenever the run order changed — the baseline now resets the counters before every build.
- **A locale's short skill sentence is a BAND INSTRUMENT, not a slogan.** The deck description = lead (free + title + worksheet word + level) + ONE whole middle sentence + the PDF tail, banded 120-170; the builder tries the instruction, the full sentence and the short one and keeps the longest in band. A Nordic face with a four-word title ('Tegn hesten færdig') has an ~85-char lead + tail; its full sentence (161) overshoots, and a 30-char short reached only 116 — da shipped 116-119 on three faces. The short must be ~45-55 chars: long enough for the shortest titles, while the longest-titled faces fall back to their bare lead (~140, in band). Check every locale's shortest-title faces with `tools/check-b7-zips.js` before the upload.
- **The fold is a plain dashed line.** Its outward arrowheads read as 'cut here' / 'this wide' to a child (the sv panel); the instruction says 'the dotted line', so that is what is drawn.
- **An Italian instruction promised orange on 'the following steps'** while the last card is black: the fix to the EN source ("the new lines") must be re-read by every locale built from it — the de/fr/it strings kept the orange promise until each was re-read against the render.
- **The finish copy omits EVERY inner step but the details.** Omitting one features step still left 'a complete pony with a blank face' (it + nl panels); with the mane, the leg lines and the face gone and the eyes kept, the gap is a visible part of the body and the model beside it shows what to draw. `data-lcs-omit` is now a comma list; the gate's PR5 poison matches `[\d,]+`.
- **Card 1 of the shapes face carries no guides** (`shapesOnFirst:false`): at card size the ear ellipses were 6 px smudges (nl panel); the paper shows them large.
- **No string promises orange on every card** — the last card is all black by the greyscale law (de/fr panels flagged it, the it + nl panels found it still in the EN bank): base and copy-steps say 'the new lines'; copy-steps says the paper below is BIGGER (the point of the face).
- **The scene face never says 'back' / 'where it was'** — the child has not seen the frog in the picture (it panel); 'draw the frog on the empty pond in the picture'. The de + pt strings had copied 'wieder' / 'de volta' from my corrected English — a fix to the EN source must be re-read by the locales that were built from it.
- **The finish face must omit a VISIBLE chunk, never the details step.** The largest inner step of the pony was its eye irises + mane edge; without them the copy read as a complete pony with its eyes CLOSED (pt panel). The omit rule now takes the biggest step strictly between the outline and the last (details) step.
- **A shape guide is fitted on the part FILLED with the parts it encloses.** A cartoon face is a ring round its eyes and nose; the bare ring failed the ellipse test, so the dog got guides for its ears and body and none for its head (es + pt panels). `fitShape(..., extra, {major})` fills the enclosed regions + the ink between them, and the two biggest parts take a loose ellipse (cov ≥ 0.6, recorded `loose:true`) when the tight test fails — 104 loose guides across the 200 drawings, every drawing now ≥ 2 guides. Rebuilding the lessons (`tools/htd-build.js`) is needed after any `htd-steps.js` change: the shapes live in `data/htd/*.json`.
- **"Trace the GREY rabbit" printed beige**: the trace fill was the grid token (#C8BFAE). A dedicated `TRACE_GREY` (#CBCBCB) — the instruction names a colour, so the render must show it (every locale inherits the word).
- **The scene face's instruction must name WHERE the hero was**: the removed frog sat ON the pond, the sentence sent the child to 'the empty bank next to the pond'. Written from the model, not the render; the pt panel measured the hero's footprint against the pond ellipse.

- **A PAGE FILLS ITS BODY (operator, 2026-10-10).** The lanes were 660-677 tall inside a body the browser measures at 766 px (733 at the tallest shipped chrome): every page stopped at 83-87 %. `BODY_LADDER = 728` (the ladder faces: cards grow to `(H - 20 - (n - 1) * 12) / n`, capped 172 / 140 for the write and order ladders) and `BODY_STRIP = 740` (the strip faces) in K-396; the strip faces' papers are HEIGHT-bound (`paperSize(S, { h, maxW })` — a width-bound paper never grows): the trace twins are tall boxes that centre the drawing, the finish / grid / copy papers fill the lane, the scene is 639 wide, the memory paper is a free full-lane box. ## 13 Dotted-shape pages are DRAWN, not fitted (2026-10-13)
Operator (2026-10-12): the dotted shapes "don't match the target image and the kids need to erase some parts … draw the images yourself so that the dotted shapes match exactly the parts of the target image". Correct: an ellipse fitted to a library drawing (section 3 step 7) never coincides with its lines.
- **Scope.** Every page that prints dotted guides: the published K-401 dog + the Level Set K-396 L1 (12), K-401 L1-3 (17), G2-391 L1 (6) = 36 worksheets × 11. - **The drawings.** `data/htdv/src/<key>.js` (22): parts back to front (`shape` white fill + ink line, `line`, `solid`, `shine`), each with a `step`; the step-1 parts are exactly the `guide`s (circle / oval / triangle / rectangle / half-circle), `major` marks the two biggest (level 3). `tools/htdv-build.js` compiles them (`lib/htd-vector.js`: painter's-order raster → `maskToPath`) into the same S the pages read (`data/htdv/<key>.json`, `lesson:'vector'`) and writes a contact sheet per drawing (`out/htdv/<key>.png`) — read it. `data/b7/how-to-draw.js VECTOR` maps unit → drawing; `vectorStepsFor` throws on a missing/broken one (no fitted fallback); K-396 uses it when `mode==='shapes'` (boxGuides ≠ none), base `guideShapes` or memory `hintShapes`, and stamps `data-lcs-htd-lesson="vector"` (only there).
