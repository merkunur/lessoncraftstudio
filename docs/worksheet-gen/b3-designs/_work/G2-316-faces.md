## Deviations from §3 (each with the measurement)
1. **F1 is 8 full-width ROWS, not 2×4 cards, at glyphH 24 and a 12-letter cap.** The design's card line 2 lane is 302 px; the platform's own school-hand model (`letter-strokes.textGlyphs` × the lane scale × 1.5 — the base's load-bearing gate rule, which measured the 14-letter cap exactly right for the base's 410 lane) needs 352 px for `Glockenblume` (12) and 365 for `Taschenlampe` at glyphH 28 — no 2-column card can hold even an 11-letter whole.
