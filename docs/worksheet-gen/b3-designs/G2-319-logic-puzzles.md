Each case is a band: three numbered clue sentences at left (the PICTURE stands in for every noun, so no locale prints a noun), the **elimination grid** at right (name tiles down, pictures across, nine white pencil cells), and under both an **answer strip** where the child CIRCLES each child's picture.
