## 2. Concept & creative hook
**Lily the frog crosses a pond — a 0..20 number line — and can only rest on the golden Ten Stone.** Ten (and 20) is the wide golden stone, the ONLY solid mid-pond rest. To add 8+6, Lily hops 8 → **10** (rest!) → 14. The two hops the child plans ARE the make-ten decomposition.
- **THE MAKE-TEN BOND DRIVES THE HOP (the critic's deepest fix; operator B1):** before Lily hops, the child fills the **always-visible bond frame "8 + ⬚ = 10"** — declaring the to-ten part (the `8+2=10` number bond). The declared part BECOMES the hop; Lily jumps exactly what the child said. The computation is **explicit, pre-commit, gradeable** — there is no "spin the length until the frog lands on the stone" (the block-on-crossing search gradient is gone; the hop length is the *declared* number, not a probed one). Then a second bond ("now I have 6 − 2 = ⬚ left → 10 + 4 = 14") carries the remainder.
- **TEN IS THE ONLY MID-POND REST — WHEN THE JUMP CROSSES IT (honest multi-strategy):** on crossing rounds the bridge through ten is required (the bond targets ten). On NON-crossing rounds (6+3) **the golden stone simply isn't in Lily's path** — the child SEES there's nothing to rest on, and one hop is natural ("no stone in the way this time — hop straight across!"). The child learns to *look for the stone in the path* = the find-where-ten cognition.
- **THE EQUATION IS SHOWN, THE ANSWER IS THE UNKNOWN (the critic's RANK1):** the prompt reads "8 + 6 = ?" (the `?` is the unknown SUM the child computes via the bridge); the number-line POSITION of the answer is un-flagged (the honest "compute it" pressure) but the equation is visible + self-checkable (not a hidden-target guessing game).
- **SUBTRACTION HOPS BACK HOME (the critic's RANK7):** 13−4 = Lily hops *back* toward home — bond "13 − ⬚ = 10" (−3 to the stone), then "10 − ⬚ = 9" (−1 more). Leftward = "hopping back home" (a narrative the child has); sequenced after addition is solid.
- **THE PAYOFF:** the bond sequence completes → Lily hops the golden bridge, the answer fades in on the far stone, a reed-bank dragonfly joins the abundance vessel, `ctx.correct()`. The made-ten moment ("you made ten!") is the loudest beat — the strategy IS the reward.
- **Anti-generic statement:** NOT free-hop-to-the-answer — (a) the bond frame makes the make-ten DECOMPOSITION the explicit, graded, gate-visible act (kills the spin-to-ten search the block-rule would otherwise leak); (b) the stone-only-when-crossing rule keeps it honest to "strategies" (not a make-ten-only law); (c) the equation-shown + answer-unflagged forces computing without a hidden-target guess; (d) the gate proves one-big-hop + count-by-ones + glow-chaser + overshoot + read-answer + **spin-to-ten-searcher** solvers ALL fail while the declare-the-bond oracle passes.
