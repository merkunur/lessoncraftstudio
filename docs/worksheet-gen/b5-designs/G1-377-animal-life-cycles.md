## 2 The base page

**Concept.** "Butterfly Life Cycle: the Field-Guide Plate" (B's concept; A's magnified egg; the pedagogy's biology). One flowering host plant stands in the middle of the page. The whole life of the butterfly happens ON it at real scale: a speck of an egg on one leaf, a small caterpillar on another, a chrysalis hanging under a third, a butterfly on the flower, each circled by a small coral ring. From each ring a thin tether runs to a big lens in a page corner that shows that stage magnified. The egg's box carries a printed **1**; the other three lenses each have an empty dashed box. Below the plate, one "loop row": the butterfly lens, a curling coral arrow, one more empty box. The child writes 2, 3, 4 by the lenses, then in the loop box writes the number of the picture that comes after the butterfly (the egg, **1**). A child who thinks life is a line writes 5; a child who has the cycle writes 1: the misconception is visible to the teacher in one glance, which a wheel with a printed return arrow never shows.
