## The skill
Read a sentence and do exactly what it says: how many, which colour, which picture, in which place, two steps.

## The apparatus and its invention
A PAINTBOX AND A PICTURE ROW: a sentence ("Colour 3 fish red.") sits above a row of library pictures; colour pots (no words, swatches only) sit below. The child dips the brush and taps pictures; as each part of the sentence is satisfied, its words light in the sentence (the number, the colour, the noun). Painting a 4th fish, or a crab, makes the extra drip off and the matching word in the sentence blinks. Settings add place words (the 2nd bird, between the cat and the dog) and two-step sentences (colour ... and circle ...), and a true/false face where the child stamps the sentence true or false against the row.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Paints all the fish (ignores the number), ignores the colour word, or does only the first half of a two-step instruction.
- **Pictures:** ocean life, fruits, farm animals, toys, insects and bugs (identical instances where counting)
- **Settings:** Sentence: one step / two steps / place words · Check: do it / true or false.
- **Rounds:** 8+ sentences from templates over the vocab; the target pictures are scattered among distractors of the same kind count + 2; true/false 40-60%.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G1-242, G1-251, G1-252, G1-308, G1-338, G1-339, G1-340, G1-341, G1-342, G1-409, G1-410, G1-411
- **Distinct from:** Granny's Mending Basket checks meaning; Otto's Picture Book picks a picture. Neither makes the child execute a multi-part instruction.
- **Build risk:** Colour words must be readable to grade-1 readers: TTS bonus only; colour-blind safe palette (the swatch shows a texture/letter-free pattern too).
