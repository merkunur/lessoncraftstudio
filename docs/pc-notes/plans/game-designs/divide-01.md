## 2. Concept & creative hook
**You are the keeper of the Saltspray Lighthouse, whose magic runs on glowbulbs — luminous tide-pool creatures the sea washes up in tangled piles (an abundance vessel with NO readable total). The lamp room has two machines that are the two MEANINGS of division made physical:** the **Lantern Wheel** (shares the pile equally among a known number of cups → how bright EACH cup gets — PARTITIVE) and the **Crate Conveyor** (packs the pile into cups/crates of a known size → how MANY crates — QUOTITIVE).
- **THE GRADED ACT IS THE STRUCTURE, NOT THE MACHINE (the critic's deepest fix):** before any machine runs, the keeper reads the work-order and decides the STRUCTURE — "is the 3 the NUMBER of cups, or HOW MUCH each cup holds?" — by placing the 3 into the right slot of `glowbulbs ÷ ☐ = ?` (slot A "how many cups" vs slot B "how much each cup holds"; the unknown `?` is the other one). The machine (Wheel or Conveyor) then animates as the CONSEQUENCE of that structural choice. The child can't win by picking a machine from a keyword — they must know which quantity is GIVEN.
- **THE GRAMMAR GIVES NOTHING AWAY (the keyword kill):** the work orders use the SAME verb + noun for both — "route into 3 cups, how bright each?" vs "route into cups that hold 3, how many cups?" — so "share/pack/crate" trigger-words are gone; the ONLY discriminator is what the 3 counts + what's asked (pure structure). A `KEYWORD_SOLVER` sees identical tokens → forced to chance. (Same-keyword-opposite-structure pairs — both say "share", one partitive one quotitive — prove it.)
- **SAME PILE, SAME `12÷3`, TWO STRUCTURES (the number-cheat kill, inherited):** the quotient 4 is true on BOTH machines — so deal-and-count + mult-fact-recall produce the number but say NOTHING about which structure; the child must interpret which quantity is known. The pile has no readable total (count-the-total dead); the answer is the structural placement + the meaning, not a counted plate.
- **THE PAYOFF:** the structure read right → the matching machine glows to life (the Wheel's cups rise to equal brightness / the Conveyor ships full crates), the lighthouse beam sweeps once, a harbor village winks on (`GameCollection` — a standing lit coast at dusk; NEVER a count, NO quotient-as-score). `ctx.correct()`. A wrong structure → no fail; the machine gently re-explains the STRUCTURE ("the 3 told you how many cups — so we still need how bright EACH") + re-try (never the machine-name, never auto-walks to the answer).
- **Anti-generic statement:** NOT route-by-keyword — (a) the no-readable-total pile + the inert remainder kill count-the-total/deal-and-count; (b) the SAME_QUOTIENT_MULTIPLE_MEANINGS axis kills mult-fact-recall; (c) the grammar-neutralized orders + the structural-placement-graded + the SAME-KEYWORD-OPPOSITE-STRUCTURE pairs + the `KEYWORD_SOLVER`/`SCENE_PICTURE_SOLVER` kill the keyword/picture surface-match (the critic's deepest); the gate proves deal-and-count + mult-fact + count-result + numeral-extract + keyword + scene-picture + alternation + brute solvers ALL fail while the interpretation oracle passes.
