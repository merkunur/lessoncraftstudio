# Reviewers' notes: registry `fi`

Written by the reviewers of the crosswalk (`scripts/curricula/apply-review.js`). 306 teaching points carry a reviewer's row; the reason for each is in `frontend/content/curricula/fi/crosswalk-authored.json`.

## author-all

- The core curriculum plans in two blocks (1–2 and 3–6) and names no single year. Several things the site puts in grade 2 stand only in the 3–6 wording: the written column method, rounding, line symmetry, grid coordinates, word classes (sanaluokat), verb tenses and pronouns. A Finnish teacher will look for these under 3. luokka.
- Calculation in years 1–2 is limited by the wording to 0–20 and then 0–100, and multiplication to the tables 1–5 and 10. Adding and subtracting within 1000 in grade 2 is therefore marked 'extends'. The tables 1–5 and 10 are 2. luokka content in Finland, but the site's tables-from-memory point sits in grade 3; teachers of 2. luokka will miss them.
- The esiopetus (K) points 'fact fluency within 5 / 10 / 20', 'number bonds' and 'number bonds to 20' are 1. luokka work in Finland; esiopetus does not ask for written calculation.
- Clock time is named only in the 1–2 wording ('Harjoitellaan kellonaikoja ja ajanyksiköitä'); the 3–6 mathematics text in the registry has no sentence on time, so the grade-3 time points hang on the 1–2 content as 'extends'. Finnish sheets need the 24-hour clock (no a.m. / p.m.) and the spoken forms puoli kolme, varttia vaille.
- Units: years 1–2 name metri, senttimetri, kilogramma, gramma, litra and desilitra. Jugs should be read in dl (not mL), and inches, feet, cups or pounds must not appear. Thermometers need degrees below zero.
- Money is not named anywhere in PART 2. Coin and shopping points are placed as addition 'erilaisissa sovellustilanteissa'. Finnish sheets need euros and cents; 1 and 2 cent coins are not used in Finland, and prices with cents are decimal numbers of the 3–6 block.
- Joined handwriting is not in the curriculum: the 1–2 wording teaches only 'tekstauskirjainten' (print letters). The three cursive points have no rows.
- Finnish has no articles (two points have no rows) and no he / she difference (hän). There are no irregular plurals or irregular verb lists, almost no prefixes (only epä-), and sh / ch / th do not exist. The digraph, spelling-rule and prefix points are mapped only for a Finnish counterpart: ng / nk, and single against double letters for short and long sounds (tuli, tuuli, tulli).
- The most important Finnish spelling content of years 1–2, long and short sounds (pitkä vokaali, kaksoiskonsonantti) and the äng-äänne, has no teaching point of its own. The same is true of the capital letter 'tutuissa erisnimissä' (only the sentence capital is covered), of keyboard skills (näppäintaidot) and of reading whole books.
- Sight words, CVC words and word families are English notions. Finnish children learn to read by sound and syllable (KÄTS, ta-lo), so the syllable points are the centre of 1. luokka and should be the largest group.
- Ympäristöoppi content of years 1–2 that no teaching point covers: safety skills and bullying prevention (turvataidot), the yard map of a familiar place (pihakartta), movement and the reasons it changes, everyday technology, where drinking water and food come from, and the home region (kotiseutu). Emotional skills have one esiopetus point only.
- Space (Sun, Moon, planets, day and night), the continents and the world map stand in the 3–6 wording only. The grade 1 and 2 points on them are marked 'prepares' or, for day and night, 'teaches' a 3–6 content.
- Programming basics ('laatimalla vaiheittaisia toimintaohjeita, joita myös testataan', MA S1 and T12) are part of mathematics from 1. luokka; only the read-and-follow and how-to-draw points touch it.
- Mazes and the secret-code point have no rows. Line plots (X marks over numbers) and the four food groups are not used in Finnish schools; bar charts, tables, the lautasmalli and the ruokakolmio are.
- Points whose skill line was too English to place firmly: K:digraphs-k, 1:digraphs, 2:digraphs, 2:spelling-rules, 2:prefixes, 1:root-words, 2:root-words, K:sight-words-reading, K:cvc-build, 1:pronouns. Points placed on a loose fit because PART 2 has no sentence for them: the family points, K:community-helpers, the alphabet-order points, the grid-copy points, K:shadow-closure.
- The registry text itself: the kuvataide 3–6 content areas repeat their heading inside the wording ('Omat kuvakulttuurit: S1 Omat kuvakulttuurit:', 'Taiteen maailmat:: S3 Taiteen maailmat:'). The date 'in force since 2014-12-31' will surprise teachers: the 2014 perusteet were taken into use in grades 1–6 on 1 August 2016.
- What the registry does not hold and a luokanopettaja will expect: the esiopetus curriculum, the transversal competences L1–L7 that every objective is tied to, the assessment descriptions (what a pupil should manage at the end of 2. and 6. luokka), and the subjects musiikki, liikunta, käsityö, uskonto / elämänkatsomustieto and the A1 language that now starts in 1. luokka. Of äidinkieli only the suomen kieli ja kirjallisuus syllabus is present (no suomi toisena kielenä, no ruotsi).
- Strand names: all are acceptable to a Finnish teacher. Two remarks without a change: the curriculum's own name for the logic part is 'Ajattelun taidot', and it joins measurement to geometry ('Geometria ja mittaaminen') and data to statistics ('Tietojenkäsittely ja tilastot').
