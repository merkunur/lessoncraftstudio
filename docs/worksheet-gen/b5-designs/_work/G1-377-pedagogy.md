## B. The six faces

Common: NEW `primitives/life-stage.js` `lifeStage({animal, stage, size, substrate:true}) -> {svg, width, height, meta:{animal, stage, parts:{leg,wing,antenna,spot,tail,egg}}}` (viewBox 200×200 per stage; palette only; teal 3 px outline, fills cream / creamDeep / tealSoft / coral / coralSoft / ink; every counted anatomical element carries `data-part`). **Anatomy, biologist-signed (the primitive's render verify counts `data-part`):**
- butterfly `egg`: ONE small ribbed oval egg on a leaf, egg height ≤ 8 % of the leaf length (the leaf is the scale cue; stage cards are equal size, so scale is carried by the substrate) · `larva` (caterpillar): side view on a leaf edge, ≥ 9 body segments, 3 small pointed true legs behind the head, 4 stubby prolegs mid-body, 1 clasper at the rear, no smiling face (a dot eye only) · `pupa` (chrysalis): hanging head-down from a twig by a short stalk, smooth teardrop, 3-5 coral dots, NO silk wrapping · `adult`: dorsal view, 4 wings (fore + hind each side), 3-part body (head, thorax, abdomen), 2 **clubbed** antennae (the butterfly-not-moth cue).
- frog `spawn`: a clump of 12-18 touching jelly spheres (tealSoft fill), each with ONE ink dot, at a water line (tealSoft band) · `tadpole`: oval body + long finned tail ≥ 1.2 × body length, eye dot, 0 legs · `legged`: the same + exactly 2 bent hind legs at the tail base, each leg ≥ 0.14 × size long (legible at 72 px) · `froglet`: small frog shape, 4 legs, tail stub ≤ 1/3 body length, on a lily pad edge · `adult`: sitting frog, 4 legs, 0 tail.
- ladybird `egg`: cluster of 10-15 upright ovals on a leaf, each ≤ 6 % of leaf length · `larva`: elongated, tapering, ≥ 8 segments, 6 legs at the front third, rows of coral dots (the "tiny alligator") · `pupa`: rounded dome fixed to the leaf at its rear end, 0 legs, 4-6 dots · `adult`: dome, coral fill, **7 ink spots** (3 + 3 + 1 split at the midline, *Coccinella septempunctata*, the European school species), 2 short antennae, 6 legs (3 visible each side).
