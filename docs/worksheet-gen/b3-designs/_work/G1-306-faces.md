## Deviations from §3 (each measured in the real render)

1. **Circle: `carpetRows:1` → the d2 carpet (2 rows), cards 3×2 with STACKED pills (design: 1 row, 2×3 cards, picture 96, pills in a row, `maxCellChars:3`).** An EN rime row holds ≤ 5 pictured words, so a 1-row carpet cannot fill 6 cards in EN at all. Three whole-word pills in a ROW (`swan` 26 px = 119 px, `clock` 133 px + padding) measure 357 + gaps against a 294-px 2×3 stage; a 2×3 card at the worst chrome is 166 tall (stage 126) and cannot hold 96 + 44 either. Stacked: 72 + 8 + 3×44 + 2×6 = 224 ≤ 227 at the worst chrome (measured, the gate asserts containment on every card). `maxCellChars:3` is dropped — pill width comes from the measured advance table and the gate asserts text ≤ pill.
2. **Circle R pills print the whole cell (onset ink + rime coral), not bare onsets.** A bare `c / f / sw` beside a picture is K-221 (a first letter from a picture — the family's own boundary); reading three printed words and circling the one that names the picture is RF.1.3.b. S / B locales print the cell text as designed.
3. **Circle answer position balanced per PAGE** (each position exactly ⌈6/3⌉ = 2 times, shuffled) instead of the 20-seed "≥ 4 of 18" spread — deterministic and stronger; the 10-seed sweep still asserts every position occurs.
4. **Join: lane 302×64 → 290×60** — the `.ws-card-stage` inner width is 294 (padding 4), and the worst-chrome stage is 195 tall: 72 + 8 + 44 + 8 + 60 = 192.
5. **Carpet: `perRowMin:1`** (the d2 spread's 2 × 5 rows > 6 cards is the base's own "ladder defect" throw; 1 per row also delivers "targets from ≥ 3 rows" by construction); **card = picture 96 LEFT + ring 44 RIGHT** (the design's vertical 100 + 10 + 40 = 150 does not fit the 141-px stage at the worst chrome); **`reps:2` (d3) is refused by build** — a carpet never prints a cell twice (the base's own invariant), so "each target occurs exactly reps times" only exists at 1; **`cellFont:28` resolves to 26** on every EN carpet (a 4-5-letter cell) — the base's per-carpet font rule.
6. **Complex is HANDWRITTEN** (config unchanged from the PARAM row: `{...d2, structure:'complex'}`): the unit fan runs over `complexUnits`, and `unitAxis.units(loc)` receives only the locale, so a row cannot express it (the K-317 → G1-311 precedent). **Ladders of MIXED blends** (`l1`-`l4`, the design's own example row `bl|ack fl|ag cr|ab dr|um sw|an`), not one blend per row: EN has 1-5 words per blend. **`complexRime` is stricter than `simpleRime`** (no `w`) and `sloth` is banned: "short-vowel closed blend words" is the claim, and brown / crown / crow / sloth are not. **Pool 19, not the design's 29** (measured 25 blend-onset simpleRime words; −3 `ow`, −1 sloth, −1 plum, −1 black [pictures]; the design's count also admitted digraph onsets).
7. **Syllabified rows flex between 70 and 92 px** (fixed 92 overflowed the footer by 29 px at the worst chrome — the real worst-case body under a 4-line title + 3-line instruction is ~668 px, not the 733 the base record budgeted); **bank gap 8** (at the component's default 14 the eight 64-px pictures wrapped to a second line — seen on the first render); **`minThree:1`** (≥ 1 three-syllable row per page, or "syllable by syllable" is a two-syllable page most of the time); **"bank order ≠ row order" made precise** = the six answer numbers never read in ascending order.
