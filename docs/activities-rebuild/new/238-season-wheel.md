## The skill
Name the four seasons in order as a cycle, and tell which season a picture or a piece of clothing belongs to.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE WHEEL THAT ONLY TURNS FORWARD. A big wheel in four quarters around a hub; the hub shows the season sign of the quarter under the pointer, built from library pictures (spring: tulip + bud; summer: sun + swimsuit; autumn: autumn leaf + pumpkin; winter: snowflake + snowman). Things wait in a basket (clothing, weather, food, activities). The child turns the wheel to a quarter and drags each thing into it. Right: it settles on the rim of its quarter. Miss 1: it slides along the rim back to the basket and the right quarter's sign flashes once. Miss 2: the right quarter tilts toward the child. ORDER mode (K-339): one sign is on the wheel; the child drags the other three signs into the blank quarters; the pointer can only turn forward, and a wrong order makes the hub jump backwards (autumn after winter) and stop. ODD-ONE-OUT mode (K-340): one thing in a quarter does not belong; the child flicks it out.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Thinking the seasons are a list that ends, and placing summer next to winter; clothes for the wrong weather.
- **Pictures:** Signs: flowers (tulip), spring (bud), beach (sun), clothing (swimsuit), miscellaneous (leaf — autumn), vegetables (pumpkin), weather (snowflake), christmas/winter (snowman). Things: clothing (coat, scarf, mitten, snowsuit, raincoat, swimsuit, t-shirt), winter (sled, skiing), summer (pool, popsicle, sandcastle), thanksgivinng (acorn); refused as all-year: umbrella, kite, pinecone.
- **Settings:** Mode: sort into seasons / put the seasons in order / odd one out (one thing in a quarter does not belong) · Hemisphere: north (all 11 locales are north; pt-BR note below)
- **Rounds:** 9 rounds; each season the answer equally often; ambiguous things refused by the reviewer (umbrella, pinecone, rainbow); order rounds start at a different season each; one right season.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G1-323, K-207, K-322, K-338, K-339, K-340, K-341
- **Distinct from:** No seasons activity exists; the Weather Window is the day's weather, this is the year.
- **Build risk:** pt = Brazilian Portuguese: seasons are inverted and weak — follow the worksheet program's pt decision (south ordering with December = summer, or a refusal recorded). Christmas pictures are refused as winter signs (summer in Brazil, and religious).
