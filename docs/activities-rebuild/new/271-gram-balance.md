## The skill
Find the mass of an object in grams on a pan balance with weights (100 g, 50 g, 20 g, 10 g, 5 g) and read a dial scale with small steps.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE GRAM BALANCE. A pan balance with one library object on the left pan and a box of drawn weights stamped 100 g, 50 g, 20 g, 10 g, 5 g. The child drags weights onto the right pan; the beam tilts by the real difference and levels only when the weights equal the object's mass, while a small needle at the pivot shows how far off it is. When level, the child types the total of the weights. Dial mode: the same object sits on a drawn kitchen scale whose dial is marked every 10, 20 or 50 g with numerals only every 100; the child drags a reading tag to the needle's mark and types the reading. Miss 1 (balance): the weights on the pan show their running sum above it; (dial) the marks between the two numbered marks light one by one; miss 2: the bubble names the step ('each mark is 20 g'). Big-looking-but-light objects (a balloon, a pillow) are weighed against small heavy ones, so size never decides. Right: the beam settles level with a soft click (balance), or the tag locks onto the needle (dial).

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Bigger looks heavier (a big balloon vs a small apple — masses chosen to contradict size in some rounds), and on the dial counting each small mark as 1.
- **Pictures:** single library objects with a hand-checked mass table: fruits/apple, banana, pineapple, watermelon; vegetables/potato, carrot, pumpkin; toys/ball, balloon, car; kitchen-tools/mug, pot, teapot; around-the-house/pillow; tools/hammer — the balance, weights and dial are drawn apparatus (no scale picture exists in the library)
- **Settings:** Mode: balance with weights / dial scale · Range: to 500 g / to 1000 g
- **Rounds:** ≥ 8 objects per setting with masses on the 5 g grid, ≥ 2 rounds where the larger-looking object is lighter; dial step size varies (10/20/50 g) between rounds; order-only reshuffle.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G2-252, G2-261, G2-262
- **Distinct from:** Pond-Juice reads capacity; Measurement Bench (tool #17) weighs in CUBES (non-standard). Grams with standard weights and a scale with small steps are not covered.
- **Build risk:** Medium: per-locale unit words (g) are fine; object masses must be plausible — a native/pedagogy check of the mass table.
