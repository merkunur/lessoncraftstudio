## Deviations from §3 (each with the measurement)
1. **F4 frames are 2-line rulings (46 × 28) per starter, not one 80-px row.** The design's `rulingBlock({rows:4, h:80, glyphH:28})` places a 28-px rule band at the bottom of an 80-px row (`yBase = 0.82·h`), leaving ~40 px of dead air above every band, and a G2 sentence built from the printed file ("The hedgehog is a mammal that lives on land.") needs a second line at 643 px. Each frame is now `rulingBlock({rows:2, h:46, glyphH:28, gap:4})` (96 px) with the starter on line 1; the lane is `flex:1` and the frames are spaced `space-between`, so the chrome slack opens BETWEEN frames (6 px at the fi chrome, ~35 at the en chrome), never as a hole above the footer (the K-323 anti-pattern the base record flagged). Stack 200 + 12 + 450 = 662 ≤ 700.
2. **The frame rows carry `line-height:0`.** An inline `<svg>` inside a `<div>` adds descender space; four frames gained 2–12 px and the fourth row crossed the footer at the fi chrome (measured lowest 923 vs 921 on F4, 943 on F5 at the de chrome). Same fix on the F5 lane rows.
3. **F5 lane rows 42 × glyphH 24, not 48.** With the design's 48 the fixed stack was 712 > the family's measured 700 floor; 694 fits and the rows still hold the G2 24-px glyph (floor 24 + 12).
4. **F5 pair choice is pattern-first** (above): the design's "rng sample of two animals … 200 tries" is uniform over pairs and, on forest, produced the SAME chip pattern (class+covering) on all six quick seeds — the sweep assertion "the chip pattern is not constant" failed on a correct composer. Drawing the same-subset first makes the pattern vary by construction; the refusal ("no two verifiable animals share exactly 2 …") now means every subset was tried.
5. **F5's two files are stacked (hero above the banner above the rows), not hero-beside-banner.** Beside a 120-px hero the banner's text area is 108 px; `Hedgehog` at Baloo 26 measures 120, `Hippopotamus` 173 — the design's own 328-px vertical budget (120 + 44 + 4 × 36 + 20) is the stack, so it is implemented as designed; recorded because the base card's identity is hero-left/banner-right.
6. **F6's bank knob is `bankSize`, not `bank`** — `bank:true` is F3's knob on the same spec, and one key cannot be a boolean on one face and a count on another. `bankNameMax:9` (design "names ≤ 9 letters") is the composer's node-side proxy; the gate proves the bank on one row by `clientHeight ≤ 110` (P14 fails at 202).
7. **F6's 5-clue subsets are drawn at random, then filtered** (≤ 60 draws per target, the bank re-sampled ≤ 200×) rather than the design's "choose 5 of the 7 … else re-pick"; same contract, and the answer's bank position is proven not to leak (best fixed-position bot 0.22 over 60 riddles — the design's "≤ 1/6" is unattainable as a MAX over six positions, the assertion is ≤ 2/6 with the per-slot "not pinned" check beside it).
8. **The mini file's rule sits inside the row.** `miniFactFile` rows are `minmax(rowH,1fr)`; with the 1-px `border-top` the intrinsic row was 21, the file 130 and the F4 column ended 2 px under the hero (verify() caught it). Line-heights are now `min(px + 4, rowH − 2)`.
9. **F3's bank words take `line-height:1.2`** — the fox bank (3 rows of 10 words at 17 px) measured 157 in a 156 slot; now ≤ 156 on every render, the gate measures it.
10. **F2's fly row is 2 chips in shuffled order** (`no | yes` or `yes | no`), as the design allows; the correct-index spread rule is asserted over the 3-chip rows only.
11. **Titles carry the unit as the head** (`{U} Fact File: Tick the Facts`, …) — the allocation's "Animal Fact File: Tick the Facts" would give every unit one title; the base validator's `{U}` rule applies to the three unit faces. F5/F6 titles are the allocation's verbatim and never name an animal.
12. **`mystery.covering` takes `{inFrame}`** in EN (the design tabled `{cell}`): `shell` → "I have a shell." (article inside the literal); `fur / feathers / scales / spines / smooth skin` are unchanged. Every clue frame may take either slot — the validator asserts exactly one.
13. **d1 / d3 variants of the faces are not built** (the emitter ships ONE config); the code accepts the design's d1/d3 ranges (`choices` 2..5, `bankExtra` 0..8, `frames` 1..3 + `free` 0..3, `puzzles` 1..4 / `bankSize` ≤ 8 / `cluesPrinted` 3..7) where a wave ever asks.
14. **The sweep counts the tick's 3-chip rows** (the `wide` set) — with 5 such rows every page takes all three positions; over 20 seeds the union is 012.
