## The skill
Count the unit cubes in a solid, including the ones you cannot see.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE CUBE STACKER: an isometric tower of unit cubes. The child predicts the total, then lifts the top layer off with a drag (it floats up and lays out flat as a grid of squares, so every cube in that layer is visible), then the next layer, until the tower is all laid out; the layers stack back when released. In build mode the child drops cubes onto a footprint grid to make a tower matching a picture from a corner view, and must fill the hidden back cubes for it to match.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Counting only the visible faces/cubes and missing hidden ones.
- **Pictures:** none (cubes drawn tool-grade)
- **Settings:** Solids: boxes (layer × rows × columns) / stepped towers · Mode: count / build
- **Rounds:** Generated solids of 4-36 cubes with ≥ 1 hidden cube, ≥ 9 rounds; typed answers.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G3-346
- **Distinct from:** No volume activity exists; Block Towers (#20) stacks one column per number. The layer lift is new.
- **Build risk:** Low.
