## The skill
Objects are made of materials (paper, plastic, glass, metal, wood) that we can tell by their properties, and that are recycled separately.

## The apparatus and its invention
A BENCH OF TESTS. A library object sits on the bench; four test tools wait (apparatus): a MAGNET, a WATER DROP, a BEND clamp, a LAMP. Each test the child drags onto the object leaves a mark on its card: magnet — sticks (steel) or not; water — soaks in (paper) or beads up; bend — bends (paper, plastic) or stays stiff (glass, metal, wood); lamp — shines through (glass, clear plastic) or not. The marks make a signature: metal = sticks; paper = soaks; plastic = bends, no soak; glass = light through, does not bend; wood = none of these. Then the child drops the object into a material bin. Right: it lands and its marks light as the proof. Miss: the bin rejects it and shows the mark that contradicts it (a clear bottle in "glass" — its BEND mark lights). ODD-ONE-OUT mode (G2-348): three objects of one material and one different; the tests reveal the odd one.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Thinks all shiny things are metal, or that a plastic bottle is glass because it is clear.
- **Pictures:** kitchen-tools (jar, glass, bottle, spoon), at-the-supermarket (can — steel food can, water bottle — plastic), post-office (envelope, letter, package), classroom (notebook, ruler), furniture (chair, stool — wood), tools (nail, hammer, bolt).
- **Settings:** Materials: 3 / 5 · Tests: available / off (sort only).
- **Rounds:** 10 objects per pass, materials balanced; each object a single clear material whose picture shows it (steel can, wooden chair); one right bin; tests may be switched off (sort only).
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G1-364, G1-365, G2-348
- **Distinct from:** The K recycling-which-bin face sorts by bin; this tests properties to decide the material.
- **Build risk:** Every object's test results come from one reviewed table; aluminium drinks cans refused (the library can is a food tin = steel). G1-365 (draw three things you recycle) stays a worksheet.

(material-bench): Group objects by the material they are made of (metal, glass, plastic, paper, wood) using their properties, for recycling. — A bench with test tools: a MAGNET, a LAMP (does light shine through?), a WATER DROP (soaks in?), a BEND test.
