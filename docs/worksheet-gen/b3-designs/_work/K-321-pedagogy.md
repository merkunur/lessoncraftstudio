## B. The six faces

Reused: `cardGrid`, `answerBox` (`components.js:105`), `.ws-tile .ws-card .ws-bin .ws-pill .ws-answerbox`, `rulingBlock`/`schoolLines` (`components-b2.js`, `trace-path.js`), the K-042 line-draw sort idiom (`sort-to-bins.js`). NEW in `components-b3.js`: `nameTile({text, w, h=64, fontPx=26, given=false})` (cream tile, teal 3 px frame, r 10, `data-lcs-name-index`; `given` = tealSoft fill for the anchor) · `rankCircle({n?, d=56})` (dashed coral circle, pre-printed numeral in teal when `n` given) · `nameLane({w, h, glyphH})` (schoolLines writing lane, `data-lcs-answer`). Instructions are slot-free in every face (no `{noun}`, no `fillSlots`), so nothing is ever inflected. Tile text = the name EXACTLY as `calendar.js` prints it (casing included); hyphenated pt days may wrap at the hyphen only.

### Base: Days of the Week in Order (K-321, CODE, `mode:'base'`, `unit:'days'`)
**Move:** the week as an ordered cycle that starts on the locale's own first day. **Child:** 7 rows, one scrambled day per row: `[rankCircle 60][16][nameTile 440x72, Baloo 2 700 28]`, block 516 centred; the first day (`dayNames[weekStart]`) is pre-numbered **1** (teal, `given`); the child writes 2..7 in the other circles. 7 x 72 + 6 x 14 = 588 <= 722 *est.*; circle 60 >= K 56, numeral box >= 30, 6 answers in [4,8]. Instruction: "Which day comes first? It is already marked 1. Number the other days 2 to 7." **d2** `{unit:'days', given:[1], scramble:true}`; d1 `given:[1,4,7]`; d3 `given:[1]` + the 7 tiles in a 4+3 grid at 300 wide (the anchor is the contract: all-blank = ambiguous start). **verify():** every `[data-lcs-name-index]` distinct, 7 present, text === `CALENDAR[lang].dayNames[idx]`; given circle index === `weekStart`; each blank `data-lcs-answer === ((idx - weekStart + 7) % 7) + 1`, answers distinct and = {2..7}; page order != calendar order (>= 3 tiles displaced) so the order is never printed; no `<img>`. **Query face:** the bare head "days of the week" (K).

### F1: Missing Days: Write the Week in Order (K-3xx, CODE, `mode:'gaps'`)
**Move:** PRODUCE the sequence: a 7-rung ladder with some rungs printed and the rest written by the child, the names-analogue of K-019 (missing numbers). **Child:** bank row at the top (the missing days as 3 shuffled `.ws-tile` 48 high, Baloo 22) then 7 rungs: `[teal rung badge 44 with 1..7][12][nameTile 520x64 given | nameLane 520x64, glyphH 40 (K whole-word floor)]`. 48 + 16 + 7 x 64 + 6 x 10 = 572 <= 722 *est.* Rung 1 is ALWAYS given (the anchor; `weekStart` day). d1 2 blanks; **d2 3 blanks, never adjacent, rung 1 given, bank shown**; d3 4 blanks, adjacent allowed, NO bank (write from memory; G1 in landing prose, level key stays K). The bank prints WORDS, not POSITION (the K-240 precedent). **verify():** rung r text/answer === `dayNames[(weekStart + r - 1) % 7]`; rung 1 given; every blank reachable by stepping from a given rung (an all-blank ladder FAILS); bank set === blank-answer set, bank order != ladder order; d2 no two blanks adjacent. **Query face:** "missing days / fill in the days" (de "fehlende Wochentage", nl "ontbrekende dagen").

### F2: Weekdays and Weekend: Sort the Days (K-3xx, CODE, `mode:'weekend'`)
**Move:** the week's two KINDS of day (school days vs the weekend); the child classifies, not orders. **Child:** 7 shuffled `nameTile` 150x56 (Baloo 22; pt shrinks to 20 so `segunda-feira` 128 -> ~117 fits 150 - 16) in a 4 + 3 grid (4 x 150 + 3 x 12 = 636 <= 675); below, two `.ws-bin` 260x220 (`page.css:234`) 40 apart, each with a `.ws-pill` word label above it (panel literals: `weekday`, `weekend`), weekday bin LEFT; the child draws a line from every tile to its bin (7 lines; the K-042 idiom). 414 <= 722 *est.* d1 5 tiles (Sat, Sun + 3); **d2 all 7**; d3 all 7 + the child also writes the two weekend names on the bin's `.ws-bin-lines`. **Weekend = Saturday + Sunday in all 11 locales; `weekStart` does NOT define it** (the pt/en Sunday-first calendar still has a Sat+Sun weekend). **verify():** tile `data-lcs-bin === (idx === 0 || idx === 6 ? 'weekend' : 'weekday')`; exactly 2 weekend tiles at d2; bins 2, labels non-empty, weekday bin first; tiles shuffled. **Query face:** "weekdays and weekend" (de "Wochentage und Wochenende / Schultage", sv "vardagar och helg", fi "arkipäivät ja viikonloppu").

### F3: Yesterday, Today, Tomorrow (G1-3xx, CODE, `mode:'neighbors'`, `unit:'days'`)
**Move:** the day's two NEIGHBOURS as vocabulary + reasoning, incl. the wrap (Sunday -> Monday); the gestern/heute/morgen gap query the panels name in de/nl/es/pt/fr/it. **Child:** `cardGrid({cols:2, rows:2})`, card 320x200: three stacked rows `[label 96, Nunito 800 16][box 214x44]`: yesterday (`answerBox`), today (given `nameTile` tealSoft), tomorrow (`answerBox`). 2 x 200 + 16 = 416 <= 722 *est.*; 8 blanks in [6,12]; glyphH 26 in a 44 box; pt 13 glyphs x ~16 px = 208 <= 214 *est.* (the validator measures per locale; the label column shrinks to 80 where the labels are short). d1 3 cards, tomorrow only; **d2 4 cards, distinct todays, >= 1 wrap card (today = Sunday or Monday)**; d3 6 cards (3 x 2, card h 190; 12 blanks). **verify():** card `data-lcs-today=idx`; yesterday answer === `dayNames[(idx + 6) % 7]`, tomorrow === `dayNames[(idx + 1) % 7]`; todays distinct; d2 >= 1 wrap; labels === `LABELS[lang].yesterday/today/tomorrow` verbatim. **Query face:** "yesterday today tomorrow".

### F4: Months of the Year in Order (G1-3xx, PARAM `{...base.difficulty[2], unit:'months', cols:2}`)
**Move:** the SAME numbering act on a different, longer cycle: 12 month names, January anchored. G1 because 12 names are read (de Kl. 1-2, it classe prima, fr CP). **Child:** 12 scrambled rows in 2 columns of 6: `[rankCircle 52][12][nameTile 240x60, Baloo 24]`; January pre-numbered 1; 11 answers (G1 ceiling 12). 6 x 60 + 5 x 14 = 430 <= 722 *est.*; numeral 26 in a 52 circle. **verify():** base rules with `monthNames`, anchor idx 0, answers = {2..12}. Distinct in RESOLVED config (`unit`, `cols`) and in what the child LEARNS (12 names, a year cycle); the mechanic is shared with the base on purpose (one act, two sequences). **Query face:** "months of the year".

### F5: The Month Before and After (G1-3xx, PARAM `{...F3.difficulty[2], unit:'months', labels:'beforeAfter'}`)
**Move:** the F3 neighbour reasoning on the year cycle, incl. December -> January; the "which month comes after" point §16 credits at pedagogy 4. **Child:** 4 cards as F3 with labels `before` / (given month) / `after`; frame-free, so no fi genitive (`maaliskuun`) is ever needed. d2 >= 1 wrap card (January or December given). **verify():** `monthNames[(idx + 11) % 12]` / `[(idx + 1) % 12]`; labels === `LABELS[lang].before/after`. **Query face:** "month before / month after / which month comes next".

**Mechanic map:** number (base, F4) · ladder-fill (F1) · bin sort (F2) · neighbours (F3, F5). Four mechanics over six faces; the two doubled pairs differ in the LEARNED object and each owns a distinct head (§E). If the critic rules a doubled mechanic a non-face, the swap-in is **abbreviation match** (Mon <-> Monday, line-draw, G1; honest x11 since `dayAbbr` is a prefix of its name in every locale, m) with the panels' C-tier demand recorded.

**Rejected non-moves.** Trace the day names (K-284) · any month GRID or date question (G2-277 family: which day is the 14th, how many Fridays) · months + seasons (K-322) · count the days in a week / months in a year (one numeral, trivial, no page) · "mark your birthday month" (open-ended, no verify; may ride F4's landing prose as a classroom extension) · abbreviation match (C-tier demand; held as the swap-in) · a Sunday-first face for Monday-first locales (a wrong calendar, not a face) · d1/d3 relabelled · a cut-and-paste twin of the base (K-240's mechanic, same content)· "today is ___" (a date; open-ended).
