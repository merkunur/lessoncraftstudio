## Deviations from §3 (each with the measurement)
1. **The wheel and the bare tree are components in `templates/components-b3/seasons.js` (`seasonWheel`, `seasonBareTree`), not `primitives/season-wheel.js` / `primitives/bare-tree.js`.** The face brief allows new code only in the family's component file; the names are type-scoped and the namespace loader accepted them.

Glyph 40 is the base sign's own glyph size; the bank tiles keep 56. Both wide names now verify clean (the fr/es spring-start controls in the gate).
3. **F2 arc arrows +22° … +66° per quadrant with a 12-unit head, not +18° … +72° / 10.** A 110 slot on a 160 ring covers ±20° (asin 55/160), so the design's arc started and ended under the slot edges and the heads were hidden behind the dashed rings (first render read); at 22-66 the teal ring shows between arrow and slot and the head is visible entering each slot.
4. **F2 stack vertically centred in the body** (design: wheel then bank from the top). 440 + 24 + 96 = 560 in a 766 px en body left 206 px of blank under the bank; centred it splits (lowest ink 818 vs footer 921). Under the fi 700 body the stack still clears by 70 px.
5. **F4 tiles 217 × 88..128 with column gap 12 and circle 48, not 210 × 88 / gap 22 / circle 44.** (a) At the design's numbers the es tile has 8.5 px of slack (`septiembre` 113 + 12 + 44 + 32 = 201 vs 210) and a 1 px overflow at 200 (the P14 poison reproduces it exactly); moving 10 px of column gap into the tiles (3 × 217 + 2 × 12 = 675 = the body) buys the widest month ≥ 20 px of clearance in all 11 locales. (b) The circle grew to 48 (≥ the G1 floor 44) because (c) the rows now GROW from 88 up to 128 (`minmax(88px,128px)`, the tile `min-height:88; align-self:stretch`): at the design's fixed 88 the page ended ~300 px above the footer at the en chrome (first render read); at 128 the leftover is ~130 px (en) / 85 (de) / 52 (fi). 6. **F3 title is §6's "Seasons: What Does Not Belong?"**, not the §3 heading "What Does Not Belong?" that the allocation record copied — the collective head keeps the band title unique against the K odd-one-out family (`build-en` title lint clean) and matches §6's title pattern.
