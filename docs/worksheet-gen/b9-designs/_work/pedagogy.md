# b9 Picture Tracing — the PEDAGOGY CONTRACT (pedagogue, 2026-10-22)

Binding on both scene designers and the critic. Numbers are in page px on the A4 body (~640 × 730 px; 1 px ≈ 0.28 mm).

## 1. The progression

**What tracing a picture serves.** Guided tracing is the first rung of the visual-motor ladder *trace → copy a model → draw
freely* (Beery VMI's imitation → copy sequence). It trains pencil control along a visible path, stopping on purpose,
closing a shape back at its start, and the eye-hand loop of keeping the pencil on a line while looking ahead. A picture
gives the child a reason to finish the line: the frog only "appears" when the line is done. The research supports a
developmental ORDER, not effect sizes. VMI-type skills correlate with early writing and maths (correlational, not proven
causal), and grasp moves from digital-pronate (2-3 y) to static tripod (3½-4) to dynamic tripod (4½-6). Claim "builds
pencil control and readiness for writing". Never claim measured gains.

**Stroke order of difficulty (Beery developmental shapes, approx. age of mastery):** vertical line (≈3) · horizontal (≈3)
· circle (≈3) · cross (≈4) · right oblique / square (≈4½-5) · left oblique / X (≈5) · triangle (≈5½-6) · diamond (≈7).
Faces use this order. PreK pictures are mostly verticals, horizontals and big round curves. Obliques and points (zigzag,
triangles) come at K. Small closed shapes, symmetry and dense detail come at G1.

| parameter | PreK (3-4) | K (5-6) | G1 (6-7) |
|---|---|---|---|
| guide | P1 thick dashes (≈4 px stroke, 14 on / 8 off), mid-grey | P2 (≈3 px, 10/6) | P3 (≈2 px, 6/5) or P4 dots (≈2.5 px Ø every 7 px); darker grey so thin lines survive cheap printers |
| traced pictures per page | 1-3 | 3-8 | 4-12 |
| smallest traced picture (longest side) | ≥ 300 px (single picture ≥ 420 px) | ≥ 130 px | ≥ 80 px |
| separate dashed strokes per page (connected runs) | ≤ 12 | ≤ 30 | ≤ 50 |
| shortest dashed segment allowed | 50 px | 25 px | 12 px |
| minimum gap between two neighbouring guide lines | 28 px (≈ 8 mm) | 18 px (≈ 5 mm) | 10 px (≈ 3 mm) |
| grade from `_SUBSTRATE-POOL` | (1) only | (1)-(2) | (2)-(3) |
| start dot + arrow | on every picture (one per picture) | only on face #9, and optional on #2/#5 | none (except #13 letters) |

**Hard rules from the table.**
- Any inner detail shorter than the band's shortest-segment value, or closer than the band's gap to another line, prints
  **SOLID** (eyes, whiskers, freckles, small fur ticks), or the drawing is rejected. Look at the contact sheet: the Christmas
  gingerbread man's wiggly limb trim, the reindeer's antlers and the hedgehog's spines are full of 10-px fragments. That is
  not tracing at PreK, it is noise.
- Pupils and other filled black parts stay solid. A face must stay readable.
- Two guide lines may never **cross** or **touch** unless the drawing itself joins them (a T-junction in a house is fine;
  two pictures overlapping is not). Pictures never overlap one another; a ground line passes BEHIND an object, never
  through it.
- Size: from PreK to G1 pictures get smaller and the lines get thinner and closer together, but they never get crowded.
  Leave ≥ 30 px clear paper round every traced picture at PreK, ≥ 16 px at K, ≥ 10 px at G1.
- The page fills its body (house rule: the stack 620-740 px). Fill it with bigger pictures or scene ground. **Never** fill
  it with extra small items at PreK.

**Direction convention (all locales).** Lines start at the top and run down. They start at the left and run right. Round
shapes start near "2 o'clock" and go anticlockwise (the start of c / o in every school script of the 11 locales). An arrow
must never contradict this. Where a picture outline makes the convention impossible, put no arrow on that line.

## 2. The 20 faces

Legend: **S** = must be a true SCENE (one place, sensible sizes and positions, a ground line, nothing floating without a
reason). **P** = separate pictures on a clean layout (a row, a grid, a frame per picture).

**#1 BASE — Trace the garden** · K · S · grade (1)-(2) · P2
Move: trace every dashed line in one garden scene, picture by picture, then look at the finished garden.
Must contain: a house or shed (library), a tree, 2-3 flowers, a bird or butterfly, a cat or rabbit, sun and clouds
(vector), a ground line and a path. 5-7 traced objects. The sky items are traced too.
Must NOT contain: crossing lines; more than 30 strokes; a grade-3 drawing.
Instruction: "Trace all the dashed lines to finish the garden."
Traps: a fence drawn through an animal (keep the animal in front of a clear stretch of fence); a sun touching a cloud (keep
the gap ≥ 18 px).
Strand: fine motor / pre-writing.

**#2 My first tracing — one big picture** · PreK · P · grade (1) · P1 + one start dot
Move: trace ONE big picture with long, steady lines; start at the dot.
Must contain: one picture whose longest side is ≥ 420 px and which has ≤ 8 strokes (star, bell, stocking, gift, apple,
whale, umbrella, heart). A solid ground line or small vector grass is allowed (not traced).
Must NOT contain: a second traced item; inner details traced; a face with tiny eyelashes.
Instruction: "Start at the dot. Trace the big picture."
Traps: an "easy" drawing that is secretly fussy (gingerbread trim). Use only drawings whose inner details are all ≤ 3 runs.
Strand: fine motor (grasp, large controlled strokes).

**#3 Look, then trace** · PreK/K · P · grade (1) · P1-P2
Move: look at the solid model on the left, then trace the same picture on the right. Repeat for 2-3 rows.
Must contain: rows of model (solid, ~70 % of the tracing size) beside the dashed twin (same drawing, same orientation),
with a vector arrow or eye-and-pencil icon between them. 2 rows PreK, 3 rows K.
Must NOT contain: a mirrored twin (that is #12); a model that is a different drawing.
Instruction: "Look at the picture. Then trace it."
Traps: the child traces the model. Make it visibly solid and smaller, and give it no guide.
Strand: visual-motor integration (attend to the model).

**#4 Trace and colour** · K · S (small scene, e.g. a fruit picnic or a farm corner) · grade (1)-(2) · P2
Move: trace every picture, then colour them in any colours, inside the lines.
Must contain: 3-5 big traced objects with large, closed, colourable areas (≥ 40 px across); enough white space.
Must NOT contain: a colour code, numbers or colour words (that is colour-by-number); grade-3 drawings with tiny cells.
Instruction: "Trace the pictures. Then colour them."
Traps: open outlines that cannot be "coloured inside". Every traced shape must be closed.
Strand: fine motor (two grips: tracing, then colouring control). Owns "trace and colour / je repasse et je colorie / cubra e
pinte".

**#5 Straight-line pictures** · PreK/K · P · grade (1) · P1/P2
Move: trace pictures built almost only from straight lines, first down, then across, then a few slants.
Must contain: 4-6 pictures where ≥ 80 % of the guide length is straight (gift, house, ladder, envelope, cross-free items
such as a door, table, flag, a vector train of boxes). Order on the page: vertical-dominant → horizontal → slanted.
Must NOT contain: round-dominant pictures; religious symbols (the Easter cross is excluded from every face).
Instruction: "Trace the straight lines: down, across, and slanted."
Traps: a "house" whose roof is a curve. Check the drawing really is straight.
Strand: pre-writing strokes (l, t, i, E).

**#6 Round pictures** · PreK/K · P · grade (1) · P1/P2
Move: trace curves and closed circles. Start near the top and go round anticlockwise.
Must contain: 4-6 round-dominant pictures (balloon, sun, apple, orange, ball, snowman, bubbles of vector circles, moon).
A start dot on each circle at ~2 o'clock with an anticlockwise arrow.
Must NOT contain: a spiral (that is #8); ovals mislabelled as circles (no such claim is made here, but keep circles round).
Instruction: "Trace round and round, like the letter o."
Traps: the dot set at the bottom, so the child goes clockwise. Put it at 1-2 o'clock.
Strand: pre-writing (c, o, a).

**#7 Zigzag and point pictures** · K · S (mountains, fir trees, a crown on a table, lightning in the sky) or P · grade (1)-(2) · P2
Move: trace slanted lines that meet in sharp points: up-down-up, with a clear stop at each point.
Must contain: mountains (vector), 2-3 fir trees, a crown or star, lightning; points ≥ 40 px apart.
Must NOT contain: abstract zigzag rows with no picture (that belongs to pre-writing).
Instruction: "Trace the zigzags. Stop at every point."
Traps: points so tight that the child rounds them. Keep the angle at each point ≥ 40°.
Strand: obliques (v, w, k, z, the triangle).

**#8 Loop and spiral pictures** · K · P · grade (1)-(2) · P2
Move: trace curls that turn and turn again: snail shell, lollipop swirl, pig tail, sheep wool, rolled ribbon.
Must contain: 4-5 pictures whose main guide is a loop or spiral (snail, lollipop, Easter bw/lollipop, curly pig tail);
spiral turns ≥ 18 px apart.
Must NOT contain: sea waves (they belong to #18); spirals tighter than the K gap.
Instruction: "Trace the curls round and round."
Traps: the lollipop swirl on the contact sheet has turns about 10 px apart. Scale it so the turns are ≥ 18 px apart, or
choose another drawing.
Strand: pre-writing (e, l, ℓ loops, cursive readiness).

**#9 Where do I start?** · K · P · grade (1) · P2 + green start dot (grey-safe: a solid black dot ringed white) + arrow on EVERY line
Move: find the start dot, follow the arrow, stop at the end, and do every line in that order.
Must contain: 4-5 simple pictures with ≤ 4 strokes each; every stroke starts at the top or left (§1 convention); 1-2 dots
per picture, numbered nothing.
Must NOT contain: numbers next to the dots (dot-to-dot owns numbers); arrows that contradict the convention.
Instruction: "Start at the dot. Follow the arrow."
Traps: a start dot that only colour tells apart. It must be a shape (a filled dot) that reads in grey.
Strand: directionality for letter formation.

**#10 Trace the dots** · G1 (top of the ladder may reach G2 detail) · S (a town street or a farmyard) · grade (2)-(3) · P4 dots
Move: follow lines of dots, keeping the pencil exactly on the dots, including the small details.
Must contain: 6-10 detailed pictures in one coherent place, dots evenly spaced (one rhythm on the page).
Must NOT contain: numbers; dash guides mixed in.
Instruction: "Trace the dotted lines carefully."
Traps: the dot spacing changing between pictures because the pictures are scaled differently. Keep one spacing across the
whole page.
Strand: fine motor (precision).

**#11 Finish the picture** · K · P · grade (1)-(2) · P2 on the missing parts only
Move: look at what is missing (wheels, ears, tail, handle, legs), trace only those parts, and the picture is whole.
Must contain: 4-6 pictures drawn solid except 1-2 dashed parts each. The dashed parts are clearly PARTS, not random
fragments.
Must NOT contain: left/right halves (that is #12); dashed fragments < 25 px.
Instruction: "Trace the missing parts to finish each picture."
Traps: the "missing" part is so small that the picture already looks finished. The dashed part must be ≥ 20 % of the
outline.
Strand: visual closure / part-whole + control.

**#12 The other half** · G1 · P · grade (1)-(2), FRONT-FACING drawings that really are symmetric (butterfly, bell, gift,
ornament, owl, an animal face from `faces bw`, a house) · P3
Move: look at the solid left half, trace the dashed right half so both sides match.
Must contain: 3-4 pictures, a thin dotted axis line, left half solid, right half dashed (the drawing's own lines, no
invented mirror).
Must NOT contain: side-view animals (not symmetric); a picture whose two halves visibly differ (asymmetric hair, a
bow); the symmetry claim on an un-symmetric drawing.
Instruction: "Trace the other half so both sides look the same."
Traps: library drawings are hand-drawn and only nearly symmetric. Measure them: if the two halves differ by more than
the G1 gap anywhere, reject the drawing.
Strand: geometry (symmetry readiness; 4.G.A.3 is later, so claim fine motor + visual perception, not a CCSS code).

**#13 Trace the picture, trace its name** · G1 (K-ready) · P · grade (1)-(2) · P3 picture + dotted letters with a start dot
per letter
Move: trace the picture, say its name, then trace the word under it letter by letter.
Must contain: 3-4 pictures with 4-6 dotted letter words under them, on a writing line (seated and measured, house rule).
Must NOT contain: articles; words of more than 7 letters in that locale; a picture a child could name differently.
Instruction: "Trace the picture. Say its name. Trace the word."
Traps: the word set is chosen PER LOCALE from the vocabulary file (§4), never a fixed English list. Bad pictures are those
a child names otherwise (`cup` vs `mug`, a teddy bear vs a bear, a bird vs a crow, a ship vs a boat).
Strand: letter formation / print concepts (RF.K.1, L.K.1.a; native: écriture GS/CP, Schreiben Klasse 1, etc.).

**#14 Trace and count** · K · S (one place: a pond with ducks, a tree with birds, a sky with balloons) · grade (1) · P2
Move: trace all the pictures of one kind, count them, write the number in the box.
Must contain: ONE counted kind, 3-10 items (never 0), scattered, none overlapping, all the same drawing (mirroring allowed).
Other scene items are visibly a different kind and are not dashed. One answer box with a seated writing line.
Must NOT contain: two drawings of the same animal (a duck and a duckling are "two kinds" to some children); partly hidden
items; a second box; items in a straight row (that is a different count skill).
Instruction: "Trace the ducks. Count them. Write how many."
Traps: the child counts a solid decoy. Decoys must be of a clearly different category. In every locale the instruction
names the counted kind in its plural (authored natively).
Strand: counting K.CC.B.5 (count up to 10 scattered and answer "how many").

**#15 Shape pictures** · K · P (one big vector picture: a shape robot, a shape house or a shape train, made from true
shapes) · vector only · P2
Move: trace a picture made of circles, squares, triangles and rectangles, then find and colour ONE named kind (circles).
A small key at the bottom shows that kind as an icon. The teacher names the other shapes aloud.
Must contain: true geometric shapes (circle = circle, square = equal sides and right angles), varied size AND orientation
(a tilted square, an upside-down triangle: K.G.A.2 "regardless of orientation or size"), ≤ 14 shapes.
Must NOT contain: library drawings (never true shapes); ovals or rounded-corner boxes; printed shape names.
Instruction: "Trace the shapes. Then colour every circle."
Traps: a tilted square read as "diamond". Teach it as a square, and the teacher note says so.
Strand: geometry K.G.A.2.

**#16 Big, middle, small** · PreK/K · P · grade (1) · P1 → P2 → P3 on the same page
Move: trace the same picture three times, biggest first, with the guide getting finer as the picture shrinks.
Must contain: 2 rows (PreK) or 3 rows (K), each with one drawing at about 100 % / 65 % / 40 % (the small one is still
≥ the band's smallest size), ordered left-to-right big → small.
Must NOT contain: different drawings in one row; a small copy below the band's smallest size.
Instruction: "Trace the big one, then the middle one, then the small one."
Traps: the gaps on the small copy fall under the gap floor. Choose drawings with ≤ 6 strokes.
Strand: fine motor (grading movement size, a prerequisite for letter sizing).

**#17 Christmas: small closed shapes** · K · S (INDOORS: tree, gifts, stocking on a fireplace, star; no snow, so it works
in Brazil's summer Christmas) · grade (1)-(2) · P2
Move: trace the tree and the room, then trace many small closed shapes (baubles, stars, gift boxes). Each one ends where
it began.
Must contain: 8-12 small closed shapes 40-70 px across (vector circles/stars on the tree + library ornaments/gifts).
Must NOT contain: religious figures; snow outdoors; a shape left open.
Instruction: "Trace the Christmas tree and close every bauble."
Traps: baubles touching the tree's dashed outline. Keep ≥ 18 px clear.
Strand: fine motor (closure of small shapes, readiness for a, d, g, o).

**#18 Summer at the sea: lines across the page** · K · S (a beach: horizon, 2-3 wave lines, sand line, a boat, umbrella,
sandcastle, a crab) · grade (1)-(2) · P2
Move: trace long lines from the left edge to the right edge across the middle of the page (horizon, waves), then the
beach pictures.
Must contain: ≥ 3 full-width vector lines (one straight horizon, two waves), ≥ 28 px apart; 3-5 library pictures.
Must NOT contain: loops/curls (#8); text bubbles ("Hello summer").
Instruction: "Trace the waves from left to right. Then trace the beach."
Traps: waves meeting the boat. The boat sits ON a wave, never crossed by one.
Strand: fine motor (crossing the midline, sustained left→right line = line of writing).

**#19 Trace it, then add to it** · G1 · S (a park, a farm, a street) · grade (2) · P3
Move: trace the scene, then draw ONE more thing that belongs there in the framed empty space, and tell the class about it.
Must contain: a scene with a deliberate empty area (≈ 160 × 160 px), marked by a dashed vector frame or a natural space
(an empty field, an empty sky); 5-8 traced objects.
Must NOT contain: a model of what to draw (open-ended); a "wrong" answer.
Instruction: "Trace the picture. Then draw one more thing in the empty space."
Traps: an empty space too small to draw in. Keep it ≥ 160 px.
Strand: fine motor + oral language / drawing to tell (SL.K.5, W.K.3 readiness). The ONLY open-ended face.

**#20 Find and trace the hidden animals** · G1 · S (an autumn forest: trees, falling leaves, mushrooms, a path; the
pt-BR panel may call it "floresta", without autumn) · grade (2)-(3) · solid scene + P3 for the hidden animals
Move: look carefully through a busy solid drawing, find the dashed animals (exactly N, N = 4-6, the same N stated in the
instruction), trace each one.
Must contain: a solid busy scene and N dashed animals at natural places (a squirrel on a branch, a hedgehog under leaves, an
owl in a tree, a fox behind a stump), each fully visible.
Must NOT contain: dashed non-animals; animals partly covered; a dashed animal touching solid lines.
Instruction: "Find the 5 hidden animals. Trace them."
Traps: the instruction says 5 and the page carries 6. A gate must count the dashed drawings on the rendered page.
Strand: visual perception (figure-ground) + fine motor.

## 3. Cross-face rules

1. **The title's promise is on the page.** "Straight lines" means ≥ 80 % straight guide; "count" has one right number;
   "shapes" are true shapes; "hidden 5" means 5.
2. **No theme-only faces.** Each face owns one move (above). The three seasonal faces carry closure (#17), crossing the
   midline (#18) and figure-ground (#20). Themes may repeat across faces, moves may not.
3. **Each face uses its own drawings.** Each library drawing appears in at most two faces of the 20, and never as the
   main picture twice. The 20 pages must look different at thumbnail size.
4. **B&W only.** Colour never carries meaning: the start dot is a shape, the shape key uses patterns, and there are no
   colour words.
5. **No numbers on guides** (dot-to-dot). The only numbers are the child's own answer in #14 and N in #20's instruction.
6. **Warm, no pressure:** no timer, score, stars or "perfect"; no ✗; no "don't go outside the lines" scolding text.
7. **One right way**, except #4 (free colours) and #19 (free drawing), which are open by design.
8. **No religious or culture-locked imagery** (no cross, church, angel, bible, nativity); Christmas = tree, gifts, stars.
   Never a weapon (the toys "tank"); no tools with blades (axe, knife, saw, sickle) on a child's page.
9. **Nameable pictures only:** a 5-year-old in every locale names it at a glance. No erlenmeyer, durian, biberon,
   chainsaw, passport.

## 4. Language rules (11 locales)

**Printed words.** Title + one instruction everywhere (read by an adult, authored natively per locale, never translated
from English, imperative to the child, ≤ 12 words). Only #13 prints words the child traces, #14 names the counted kind
inside the instruction, and #20 carries N.

**#13 word set (per locale, from `image-vocabulary.js`):** choose 3-4 pictures whose singular is 3-7 letters **in that
locale**, has no apostrophe, hyphen or space, and stays unambiguous. Safe cross-locale candidates: sun, moon, cat, fish,
duck, house, apple, ball, frog. The panel still checks each one (fr *poisson* 7 ✓, fi *aurinko* 7 ✓, de *Schmetterling* ✗,
fi *perhonen* 8 ✗, pt *borboleta* ✗; never butterfly). No articles (they would print the gender: *le/la, der/die/das,
en/ett*). **Letter case:** lowercase school print in en es pt it nl sv da no fi. **de:** noun with its capital initial
(*Sonne*). **fr:** capitales d'imprimerie (the maternelle norm; cursive belongs to the cursive family). Letters are seated
on a measured writing line, a start dot per letter, no stroke numbers. Letters å ä ö æ ø keep their marks as solid
dots/rings (a dashed ring on å is unreadable).

**Risky printed items.** de/nl/sv/da/no/fi compounds in instructions (*Weihnachtsbaumkugeln*, *joulukuusenpallot*): the
panel shortens to the base noun (*Kugel*, *pallo*). The #14 noun is the plural object of "trace" (fi: *Jäljennä ankat. Laske ne.*;
the number the child writes stands alone in its box, so no numeral agreement is printed). The #20 number agrees (fi *5 piilossa olevaa eläintä*, the panel's call). fr never
uses *repasser* without *les pointillés / le dessin* (ironing trap); sv never uses *spåra* without *bilder / linjen*.

**What the native panel must author:** the 20 titles (from the harvested heads), 20 instructions, the #13 words and the
#14 plural noun per locale, and the #17/#20 season words. pt-BR: Christmas = summer, the scene is indoors; the #20 panel
decides whether "outono" fits. Nothing on the page may be English in a non-EN locale.
