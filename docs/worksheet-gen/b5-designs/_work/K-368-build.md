## Deviations (each with its measurement)
1. **d1 is lens-OVER-tags** (layout `column`: lens 188, then a row of two 140 × 48 tags at 20 px). The design's lens-left row needs 188 + 14 + 140 = 342 > the 302.5 card interior. Vertical stack 188 + 14 + 48 = 250 ≤ 303.5 (677). Tag width 140 because fi `suorakulmio` at 20 px = 112.6 px + 16 padding + 4 border.
2. **"Turned" is measured as "no drawn side within 15° of level"**, not read from the stamp. Consequence: the skinny triangle's third side runs 59° off its base, its second 160.8°, so rot 15..34° leaves a side within 15° of level (at 20° it is 0.8° off — the triangle SITS on it). Slot C therefore draws rot **36..74** unmirrored and **16..43** mirrored (the mirror's sides run 0 / 19.2 / 121°). The design's [15, 75] would ship prototypical skinny triangles. Every other turned draw is 16..74 (1° inside the line so 0.01 px coordinate rounding cannot drop a figure out; sweep found the 15° equality case).
3. **Slot D rectangle aspect 2.3..2.6** (design 2.2..2.6): an aspect of exactly 2.2 measures 2.199 after 0.01 px rounding and fell off the skinny line in the sweep (d3 seeds 11, 19). Still skinny by the design's own threshold, never at it.
4. **`curved` shrinks the whole figure about the centre** until the Bézier's farthest point is R: the 0.20 L bulge otherwise left the box by +4..+9 px at R 44..69 (measured, hexagon/rectangle), breaking the rotation-invariant box and the 7.5 px lens clearance.
5. **Variants refuse below R 44** (a variant is a near-miss; the design says near-misses are never drawn below 44), and **a `gap` on a side too short for gL − sw ≥ 11 THROWS** (the design states ≥ 11 as a consequence of the formula, but the 0.30 cap cannot reach it on a side < 46.7 px: hexagon side at R 44 = 10.2 px visible, 3:1 rectangle short side = 5.3). The F1 composer must gap a long side.
6. **Rule 6 apparatus words** (write-name names the line and the box, dot-draw the dots, no tick / letter boxes / line to) are checked for **en only**: the §5 bank shape has no per-locale lexicon to check them against. Open item for the panel brief (either add an `apparatus` word list per locale or accept panel review).
7. **Hexagon riddles carry `clue: 'six'`** (the design lists clue tags for the four core kinds only).
8. Composer details the design leaves open: E/F triangle = right at 0/90/180/270 ± 8° or obtuse at 0/180 ± 8° (mirrored at random); d3 = E hexagon (rot 0..59) + F one TURNED polygon (the 4th turned figure); d1 = one of each core kind with `turnedMin` of the three polygons turned.
9. The spec stamps `data-lcs-names` (kind → literal) on the root so `verify()` can compare every tag text without the bank (the gate's node cross-check compares against the bank itself).
