**The object:** a bare strip, a wall at each end, **three posts** (each end + the middle) and nothing

## ⭐⭐ THE PEDAGOGY PANEL REFUTED THE CATALOG'S OWN EVIDENCE — and it was right
Ruled **DO NOT BUILD 3-0** on the pitch as written:
- ⚠⚠ **The headline claim is BACKWARDS.** Max placement error is at **x ≈ 21.7** — a child puts **ten
  in the MIDDLE** of a 0-100 line — and by 90 the error has collapsed. The top of the strip is where
  children are MOST accurate. "Everyone crams 60-90 into the last inch" aims a class at the wrong end.
- ⚠ **"Single best predictor of later arithmetic" is FALSE** — Geary 2011, N=177: **0.14, t<1, n.s.**
- ⚠ **The zoom dies on its own evidence** — Nuraydin et al. 2022, RCT N=188: training FAILED TO
  TRANSFER across a single axis rescaling.
- ⚠⚠ **The ghost cloud is banned BY NAME**: `verify-estimation-jar.js` P14, gate-enforced + poison-
  tested in 11 locales — *"the single most likely thing to be added to this feature next."*
⭐ **And it handed over the tool while refusing it** — its own remainder: *commit to a BENCHMARK
before placing.* The catalog states its dread in benchmark form ("is 71 nearer 20 or 90?") and then
builds a placement tool; **that mismatch is the design.** And the recursion stops being a rescaling
stunt — the QUESTION recurs, which is the one reading Nuraydin's null result does not touch.

## ⚠⚠ THE NATIVE PANEL FOUND FIVE LIVE MODEL BUGS MY 16,626-ASSERTION GATE PASSED
**Because my oracle carried the same misconceptions as my code.** All five confirmed by driving the
model, not by reading the copy:
- **The nudge labels LIED**: `nudge()` multiplies by `step()`, a TENTH inside a ten, so "−10" moved
  ONE (measured 1.000 / 0.100). The glyphs were false in the VISUAL channel where no string reaches.
- **`back()` guarded only on DEPTH** → commit → rerule → back resurrected phase `shown` with post and
  guess BOTH null: the truth wedge drawn with nothing committed, in **two presses**. My docblock
  claimed that state was unreachable.
- **`saidRerule` was DEAD** — `rerule()` returns the post phase and `_say()` tested phase first, so
  the tool's whole invention was never spoken.
- **The trace cap always ate the LEFT column**: 26 EVEN choices read **[3, 9, 8]**.
- **`nearestPost`'s stated tie rule was false** — 25 → low post, 75 → middle, the two quarter points
  resolving asymmetrically, and those are exactly the cases the deal favours. ⚠ **My gate's oracle
  encoded the same bug**, which is why it passed.
All fixed; three now gated (**L7b** shape-preserving cap, **L7c** no resurrected commit, the named
quarter points) and **poison-proven 3/3**.

## Design rulings worth keeping
**NO TICKS** — a ticked strip would carry different furniture after the re-rule, the opposite of
self-similarity; a bare strip is identical at every depth and that identity IS the proof.
**NO GLIDER** — the number lands where the class SAID and the truth appears elsewhere, so the
aircraft ends up in the wrong place: a right/wrong verdict delivered as a funny pictogram.
**T_BEAT never through `_dur()`** (a wait is not movement) and **T_REVEAL never scaled by the error**
(the verdict would leak into the time channel where no contrast audit can see it).

## Two defects only the RENDER showed

and the posts sat in the strip's CONTENT box while the plaque/wedge used the ARENA — 2.3% drift, #43
again — now an inset shadow so the boxes are IDENTICAL, not within tolerance.
