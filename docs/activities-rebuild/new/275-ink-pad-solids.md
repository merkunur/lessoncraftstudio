## The skill
Count the faces, edges and vertices of 3D solids, and match a solid to an everyday object of the same shape.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE INK PAD. A drawn 3D solid (cube, rectangular prism, triangular prism, square pyramid, cylinder, cone, sphere) sits beside an ink pad and a sheet of paper. Faces: the child taps a face; the solid rolls onto it, presses on the pad and prints that face's outline on the paper, and the face keeps an ink dot so it cannot be counted twice — rolling brings the hidden back and bottom faces into view; the prints on the paper ARE the count, and the child types it. A curved surface rolls but prints nothing flat (a cylinder prints two circles, a sphere nothing). Edges: a turn handle rotates the solid; the child runs a finger along each edge (a trace path; hidden edges show dashed until turned into view) and a traced edge turns ink-blue. Corners: each tap on a vertex leaves a dot. Real-objects setting: a library object is pressed on the pad and its prints are compared with the prints of three solids; the child drags it to the solid that prints the same. Miss 1: the un-inked faces/edges/corners wobble; miss 2: the solid turns once round slowly showing every part. Right: the paper slides out with the full set of prints.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Counting only the faces/edges you can see (a cube "has 3 faces"): the ink and the turning reveal the hidden ones; a curved surface prints nothing flat (cylinder = 2 faces).
- **Pictures:** real-object setting only: toys/ball, dice, blocks; christmas/present, candle; post-office/package; classroom/book; at-the-supermarket/can; music/drum; space/earth; fruits/orange. No real cone or pyramid object exists, so cone and pyramid appear only as drawn solids, never in the real-object match. Solids, pad and paper drawn as apparatus.
- **Settings:** Count: faces / edges / corners · Objects: solids only / real objects match
- **Rounds:** ≥ 7 solids per count setting (curved solids only in faces/corners), order reshuffled; real-object mode ≥ 8 objects among cube / prism / cylinder / sphere candidates, the right solid's slot balanced across the three.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G2-242, G2-243, G2-245, G2-246
- **Distinct from:** choice-board.flat-solid only sorts 2D vs 3D; Blueprint (tool #44) builds cube structures, not the parts of a solid. No activity counts solid parts.
- **Build risk:** Medium: a believable rotating solid in SVG geometry (isometric projection, 2–3 px outlines) is apparatus, not illustration — fine under rule 5, but needs care so hidden edges read as hidden.
