## §1 — Concept / world
Mossbeard the Story-Keeper; two moons (gold/silver) each hold one short tale over one meadow. **BEAT-ALIGNED layout** (the pedagogy's decisive UX ruling): both tales laid out with beats aligned in rows — problem-row, action-row, outcome-row — both stories' problem beats on the same row, so the layout offloads cross-text TRACKING (not judgment). The child compares **ONE beat-row at a time** (the moonseam highlights the active row), tapping "twin-light (SAME)" / "two-light (DIFFERENT)" for the NAMED experience-dimension of that row. The same/different cards stay disabled until BOTH tales' lines are text-REVEALED (relational gate). Warm, no-shame; a non-numeric firefly-tree fills as ambient abundance.

## §2 — The standard + the load-bearing cognition
**RL.1.9 (SOLE primary)** — compare and contrast the adventures/experiences of characters in stories. **RL.2.9 REJECTED** — not "Grade-2 drift" but a CONTAMINANT (two-versions-of-the-same-story makes prop-matching CORRECT → trains the exact cheat). **"Experience" = {problem}+{action}+{outcome}+{event-bound feeling}**; build on PROBLEM→OUTCOME (spine) + ACTIONS + feelings; EXCLUDE standing TRAITS + bare event-sequences. **The load-bearing test: compare what HAPPENED TO the characters, NOT what is IN the stories** — every round UNANSWERABLE from one text AND not prop/picture-matchable. Two stories only; 3-4 PARALLEL beats each (~25-45 words); TTS-supported.

## §3 — THE ARCHITECTURE REWORK (the heart; the deepest flaw + fix)
**The critic's deepest catch — the gate validates the STORED ENUM while the child plays the RENDERED TEXT (the Game-66 render-a-proxy lesson, reborn for the experience channel).** The verdict is `A.experience[dim] === B.experience[dim]` — a SET-EQUALITY over closed enums. The pedagogy forces the experience into TEXT (for SR parity). If the enum renders VERBATIM ("found it" / "found it" → same), the oracle and a pure LEXICAL-STRING-MATCHER **collapse into the same function** — a child matches the two outcome-WORDS without comprehending the EXPERIENCE, the prop-match solver is BLIND (it's the experience-DESCRIPTION words, not the props). **WORSE — it defeats the pedagogy's own construction-(ii):** lost-hat-FOUND vs lost-boat-FOUND is meant to FORCE abstraction (different props, same experience → SAME), but if both render "found it," a lexical-matcher wins WITHOUT abstracting — the cleanest teaching vehicle is itself string-solvable. And paraphrase alone doesn't close it (the **1c polarity-token** residue: "found it" vs "did NOT find it" → a negation-scanner wins).

**THE FIX — render a varied-wording proxy; the gate consumes the render:**
1. **The gate CONSUMES the RENDERED text, not the enum.** `EXPERIENCE_WORD_MATCH_SOLVER` (synonym-set-aware lexical-overlap, e.g. Jaccard over content-lemmas) MUST score at CHANCE in BOTH directions; `EXPERIENCE_NOT_LEXICALLY_MATCHABLE` (a SAME-verdict pair's two rendered descriptions share NO decisive content-lemma; a DIFFERENT-verdict pair is NOT separable by a trivial lexical diff).
2. **FIXED decodable sentence SKELETON + VARIED experience-bearing SLOT, authored from a curated decodable-SYNONYM BANK per enum** (each enum — `FOUND_IT`/`GAVE_UP`/`GOT_HELP`/… — ships a small set of Grade-1-decodable, semantically-equivalent renderings pre-vetted for decodability AND pairwise lexical-distance; the author picks two sharing no content-lemma). The fixed skeleton keeps the SR parallel-beat scaffold + decodability; only the load-bearing slot varies → resolves the anti-cheat-vs-Grade-1-decodability-vs-SR tension.
3. **`NO_POLARITY_ONLY_DIVERGENCE`** — DIFFERENT rounds are CONTRASTING POSITIVES ("Mia found it" / "Sam traded for a new one"), NOT positive-vs-negated; removing negation tokens from both descriptions must NOT make them lexically equal.

**The 2nd cognition (operator-chosen — adds depth to the thinnest standard):** a **WHICH-PART-DIFFERS** round-type — the child IDENTIFIES which experience-dimension (problem / action / outcome / feeling) differs between the two tales (a search-and-find across all dimensions, genuinely distinct from judging a single NAMED dimension). `EXACTLY_ONE_DIMENSION_DIFFERS` on those rounds; the differing dimension NOT recoverable by lexical/prop surface.

## §4 — Anti-cheat architecture (the gate; consumes RENDERED text)
- **EXPERIENCE-WORD-MATCH (the deepest cheat) — killed** by §3 (varied-wording bank + render-consuming gate + `EXPERIENCE_WORD_MATCH_SOLVER`@chance + `EXPERIENCE_NOT_LEXICALLY_MATCHABLE` + `NO_POLARITY_ONLY_DIVERGENCE`).
- **SURFACE-ELEMENT / PICTURE / WITHIN-TEXT — killed:** the two divergence constructions MANDATORY (`DIVERGENCE_ROUNDS_PRESENT ≥~0.4·deck` BOTH directions: (ii) diff-surface/same-experience + (i) same-surface/diff-experience) → `EXPERIENCE_NOT_SURFACE_DECIDABLE`; `BOTH_TEXTS_REQUIRED` (flip story B's asked-dim → verdict flips); the relational gate SEQUENCES (cards unlock on text-REVEAL of both), the MUTE gate FORCES comprehension. Art is **DECOUPLED, NOT anti-correlated** (anti-correlation is still exploitable — a meta-child trusts the opposite of the picture): `PICTURE_NOT_LOAD_BEARING` + `PICTURE_VERDICT_MUTUAL_INFORMATION ≈ 0`; the **MUTE/art-hidden QA gate is HARD** (every round playable as gray boxes + text or REJECT).
- **FEELING-WORD-MATCH (designers' flag) — killed:** the action-beat carries independent signal + the varied-wording bank (§3) closes the feeling/outcome-word string-match too.
- **BASE-RATE cheat — killed:** `VERDICT_BALANCED_PER_DIMENSION` (per named dimension, same/different ~50/50) + `VERDICT_NOT_PREDICTABLE_FROM_QUESTION` (a classifier from (dimension, pair-id) → verdict scores at chance) — the asked dimension must NOT predict the verdict.
- **AMBIGUITY — killed:** `VERDICT_UNAMBIGUOUS` (an enum-distance matrix; forbid same-dimension pairs in the ambiguous band — SCARED vs WORRIED a Grade-1 child reasonably calls "same"); per-row self-contained (or a pinned one-line problem-recap) so each row is judgeable from its own two lines + the named dimension (verified in the MUTE-QA per-row pass).
- **Structural asserts:** `ANSWER_DERIVED_NOT_STORED` (no same/answer/verdict key in data; mutate story B's outcome → verdict flips; mutate a prop → unchanged), `EXACTLY_ONE_DIMENSION_DIFFERS` (which-part-differs rounds), ≥7 variety.

## Cohort doctrine this game adds
**The CLOSED-ENUM-RENDERED-VERBATIM lexical-collapse lesson (the SET-EQUALITY twin of the render-a-proxy doctrine) — PROGRAM-WIDE.** When an answer is a SET-EQUALITY or SET-MEMBERSHIP over closed enums (Game-66 `canSee`, this game's experience-elements) AND the enum is RENDERED AS TEXT (forced by SR parity / a text-based standard), the oracle and a pure LEXICAL-STRING-MATCHER **collapse into the same function** — the gate certifies a comprehension game that PLAYS as string-match, and the construction that best TEACHES the standard (the abstraction-forcing same-category-different-surface case) is the MOST prone to collapse (same category → same enum → same word). **The fix: a VARIED-WORDING rendering layer from a curated per-enum SYNONYM BANK (fixed decodable skeleton + varied load-bearing slot) so the rendered text's LEXICAL same/different does NOT track the enum's SEMANTIC same/different; the GATE CONSUMES THE RENDER (a `*_WORD_MATCH_SOLVER` over rendered strings must score at chance in BOTH directions); and a polarity-symmetry assert (`NO_POLARITY_ONLY_DIVERGENCE`) closes the negation-scan residue.** The real COST this surfaces: a comprehension game whose answer is enum-equality must budget the paraphrase bank as a FIRST-CLASS, per-locale, NON-PORTABLE artifact (~2× the per-locale authoring) — paraphrase is not free, and it is the most language-bound layer there is. (Companions, each a reusable rule: a "re-represent on wrong" that CO-PRESENTS the two compared facts is a REVEAL in disguise for a one-bit judgment → re-teach by TEMPORALLY-SEPARATED re-experience, never side-by-side; art for a comprehension judgment must be DECOUPLED [zero mutual information with the verdict], NOT anti-correlated [negative correlation is still exploitable]; and BASE-RATE balance — assert the asked dimension does not predict the verdict, or a child learns the dimension→verdict prior and never compares.)
