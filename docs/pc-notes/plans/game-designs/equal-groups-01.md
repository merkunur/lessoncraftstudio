## §1 — Concept / world
**The Echo Grove.** Pim, a small round owl, tends a row of magical **Echo Trees** that grow fruit ONLY in identical bunches — every branch on one tree holds the same number of fruits (the law of the Grove: trees grow in EQUAL GROUPS). A tree is a two-tier object that makes the multiplicative roles PHYSICAL: **branches** are the GROUPS (visibly separate arms off the trunk), **fruits-on-a-branch** are the SIZE (the bunch inside one group) — you can never mistake a branch for a fruit. Lantern-Moths arrive carrying a glowing **× rune** — a *sentence about a tree's shape*: how many branches, how many fruits each. Pim's GRADED job is to **READ the shape of already-grown trees and tell them apart**: name a tree's rune, match a moth's rune to the right tree among same-harvest trees, spot the tree that doesn't fill the order, and notice what grew between two trees. (Pim can also *grow* a practice tree on the dial-bench — but that's just play; it isn't the test.) The total ("the harvest") is NEVER the point — the SHAPE is. Non-numeric abundance vessel: the **Moonwell** beside the Grove brims with rising water + a canopy that fills with drifting fireflies across a session (never a count/score). `ctx.correct()` auto-advances; no Check button.

## §2 — The standard + the load-bearing cognition
**3.OA.A.1 (Grade 3, SOLE) — interpret products: 5×7 = the total number of objects in 5 groups of 7 each.** The genuine cognition = interpret the × SYMBOL as equal-groups STRUCTURE: the FIRST factor = number of GROUPS, the SECOND = SIZE of each group; the structure IS the meaning; the total is a CONSEQUENCE, not the meaning. Pedagogy ruling (MID-SIZE, NOT thin): 3 load-bearing sub-cognitions — **(A) ROLE-ASSIGNMENT** (symbol↔structure), **(B) FACTOR-ORDER IN CONTEXT** (the premium differentiator), **(C) STRUCTURE-AT-FIXED-TOTAL / WHAT-CHANGED** (the clean inversion of the live 2.OA.C.4). **CARDINAL commutativity ruling:** factor-order is graded ONLY in CONTEXT (the world fixes roles — "5 bags of 7" ≠ "7 bags of 5" as situations); on a BARE symbol both readings are AFFIRMED (commutativity, 3.OA.B.5 — grading bare-symbol order punitively PENALIZES the better reasoner; forbidden). **The critic's re-center:** of A/B/C, the transcription-proof AND non-2.OA.C.4-duplicate content is the **INVERSE (read→name) + DISCRIMINATION (tell-apart)**; CONSTRUCTION ("set groups=N, size=M") is the live 2.OA.C.4 act minus the total → scaffold only. Numbers: factors ≤10, products ≤100; same-total decoy sets (12 = 2×6/3×4/4×3/6×2; 24 = 6×4/4×6/3×8/8×3/12×2). All numbers/symbols = real DOM/SVG `<text>`. **FENCES:** the TOTAL (never graded/typed — the firewall vs the LIVE `array-core.js` 2.OA.C.4 "make N groups of M, type the total"); CONSTRUCTION-as-the-graded-act (the 2.OA.C.4 act); bare-symbol factor-order (affirm, don't grade); the array model (its row/col symmetry belongs to 3.OA.B.5 commutativity — keep to a single teaching rung, not load-bearing).

## §3 — THE ARCHITECTURE REWORK (the heart; the two deepest flaws + fix — RE-CENTER onto read/name/discriminate)
**Flaw 1 — POSITIONAL DIGIT-TRANSCRIPTION (a Game-75-class clever-hans on digit ORDER; the cheapest cheat in the design).** The symbol→structure "set the dials" round has a PERFECT positional map: token-1 → groups-dial, token-2 → size-dial. A child who thinks × means "smoosh the two numbers" passes 100% with ZERO grasp that the factors MEAN groups-and-size — it needs no lexicon, only left-to-right reading order (cheaper than keyword-parse). **The anti-leak "reveal the model after commit" makes it WORSE** — by hiding the countable model pre-commit, the only available signal IS the two digits + their order. Likewise "what-changed" on a SYMBOL→SYMBOL pair (5×7→5×8) is digit-DIFF ("the right token changed → the right dial"), not structure.
**Flaw 2 — ACT-LEVEL 2.OA.C.4 DUPLICATION (deeper than answer-level).** Grading the structure-not-the-total is NECESSARY BUT NOT SUFFICIENT: "set groups=N, size=M on two dials" is the SAME CONSTRUCTION ACT as the LIVE 2.OA.C.4 "make N groups of M" — minus the total. Fixing transcription (via context) just turns it into "2.OA.C.4 construction driven by a context prompt," still construction. The gate's `DUPLICATE_OF_2OAC4` (no `answer===groups×size` path) is too weak — it only catches total-grading; it must catch the ACT.

**THE FIX — RE-CENTER the GRADE onto READ / NAME / TELL-APART pre-built groupings (the child never CONSTRUCTS the answer):**
- **GRADED (load-bearing) — inverse + discrimination, all transcription-proof AND non-duplicate:**
  1. **NAME-THE-TREE (reverse-recipe, grouping→symbol).** A tree is already grown (4 branches of 3); the child ASSEMBLES its rune from tiles (places "4" in the branches-slot, "3" in the each-slot). No symbol to copy; the graded artifact is the SYMBOL's two ROLES, not the total.
  2. **MATCH-THE-RUNE (pick-the-grouping among SAME-TOTAL decoys).** A moth's rune (or context) → pick the matching tree among decoys that ALL share the total (incl. the commutative twin 5×2 vs 2×5). The total carries zero signal; positional-copy has nothing to act on; the child must map first-factor→branch-count.
  3. **WHAT-GREW (what-changed STRUCTURE→STRUCTURE).** Two TREES side by side differ in ONE dimension (more branches vs taller bunches); the child says which. Presented as structures, NOT symbols (defeats digit-diff).
  4. **WHOSE-ORDER (factor-order IN CONTEXT, lexically DECORRELATED).** A story-vignette fixes the roles by the WORLD ("clusters of 3 on 7 hooks" → branches=7, size=3, NOT copy-3-then-7) — and the lexicon is DECORRELATED (the same noun appears as a CONTAINER in one item and as CONTENTS in another, so no noun is a stable role-cue → defeats the NOUN_ROLE_LOOKUP vocab cheat). The child parses the relational frame ("X per Y"), the genuine interpretation.
  5. **SPOT-THE-WRONG-TREE (impostor).** Among trees filling a moth's order, one doesn't match — the commutative twin (same total) is ALWAYS paired with a HONEST non-match that ALSO shares the total (so a TOTAL-counter can't resolve by elimination), and "pick the swap" isn't learnable.
- **SCAFFOLD (zero grade weight — teaches the affordance/vocabulary, the play-bench):** set-the-dials FROM A BARE SYMBOL, set-the-dials FROM CONTEXT, what-changed SYMBOL→SYMBOL. These are construction/transcription surfaces → never counted.
- **child-output STRICTLY before game-output** (`ANSWER_NOT_PRE_REVEALED`); and the **two new leaks closed:** (i) any pre-commit dial PREVIEW shows a NUMBER on the dial face, NOT 5 instantiated countable crates (instantiated empties leak group-count); (ii) **perceptual-salience decorrelated** — in MATCH/IMPOSTOR rounds, randomize so neither "more branches" nor "taller bunches" nor "leftmost option" correlates with correctness (else the child picks by EYE, not by reading group-count).

- **Structural asserts:** `GRADED_IS_READ_OR_DISCRIMINATE_NOT_CONSTRUCT` (the headline — every graded round's artifact is a NAMED symbol / a PICKED grouping / a STRUCTURAL judgment, never a constructed grouping), `NO_GRADED_TOTAL` (no `graded===groups×size` path — inverts the array gate's "total correct / total−1 wrong"), `TOTAL_NON_DISCRIMINATING` (≥N same-total decoys on every option round, incl. the impostor's honest non-match), `LEXICON_DECORRELATED` (no noun is a stable role-cue across items), `PERCEPTUAL_SALIENCE_DECORRELATED` (count/height/position uncorrelated with correctness), `ANSWER_DERIVED_FROM_STRUCTURE_NOT_TOTAL` (mutation probe: swap groups↔size with product HELD → graded answer MUST change; mutate to a different same-total pair → still distinguishable; frozen-descriptor probe), `ANSWER_NOT_PRE_REVEALED`, `CONTEXT_FIXES_ORDER` (factor-order graded only where context pins roles), VARIETY ≥7 + `nextTask` reshuffle.
- **Wrong-answer re-show (no-reveal):** the moth lands on the tree to COMPARE shapes (the two numbers drift to hover over the branches vs over one bunch) WITHOUT surfacing the total as the answer; the swap-confusion (4-branches-of-6 vs 6-branches-of-4) gets the tender "same fruit, different shape — the order wants 4 arms holding 6" beat (the constant total is the TEACHING point, never the graded answer).

## Cohort doctrine this game adds
**The ACT-LEVEL-DUPLICATE + POSITIONAL-TRANSCRIPTION lesson (for an INTERPRET standard adjacent to a PRODUCE standard) — PROGRAM-WIDE.** Two linked findings. **(1) ACT-LEVEL duplication is deeper than answer-level.** When a new game is adjacent to a LIVE one, grading a different ANSWER is necessary but NOT sufficient — check whether the graded ACT is the same. "Set groups=N, size=M" is the SAME CONSTRUCTION ACT as a live "make N groups of M," even after you stop grading the total. For an INTERPRET-the-notation standard sitting next to a PRODUCE/construct standard, the non-duplicate cognition is the **INVERSE** (read a structure → NAME its symbol) **+ DISCRIMINATION** (tell structures apart among same-value decoys), NOT construction → re-center onto read/name/discriminate; demote construction to scaffold; arm a `DUPLICATE_OF_<live>_ACT` solver (graded artifact = the live act's product, total-graded or not). **(2) POSITIONAL TRANSCRIPTION is the cheapest clever-hans** — a symbol→structure "copy the notation onto the structure" round is solved by positional token-copying (token-1→slot-1) with ZERO meaning; it needs no lexicon (cheaper than keyword-parse), and a "reveal-the-model-after-commit" anti-leak makes it WORSE (the child commits on the tokens alone). → the graded surface must DECORRELATE the structure from the notation's literal token-ORDER: context with prose-order ≠ role-order (and LEXICALLY decorrelated, or it degrades to a NOUN_ROLE vocabulary lookup), structure→symbol where the symbol is the ANSWER (nothing to copy), structure→structure what-changed (not symbol-diff); arm `POSITIONAL_TRANSCRIPTION` + `NOUN_ROLE_LOOKUP` solvers. **Plus a perceptual-salience guard:** any pick-the-picture discrimination round leaks through the EYES if count/size/position correlates with the answer → randomize salience + option position; arm a `PERCEPTUAL_SALIENCE` solver. Generalizes to EVERY interpret-the-notation standard adjacent to a produce standard (interpret-the-fraction vs build-the-fraction, interpret-the-equation vs solve-it, read-the-graph vs make-the-graph): grade the inverse + discrimination, decorrelate from the notation's token-order AND its lexicon AND its perceptual salience, and test the ACT (not just the answer) against the live neighbor. (Lineage: the POSITIONAL-TRANSCRIPTION cheat is a token-order specialization of Game-75's lexical-tell/clever-hans + Game-81's keyword-parse; the ACT-LEVEL-DUPLICATE check extends the gate-check's stale-INDEX/owned-surface discipline from "is the ANSWER owned" to "is the ACT owned"; the re-center onto read/name/discriminate is the symbol-first/grade-the-genuine-cognition family applied to an interpret-vs-produce boundary.)
