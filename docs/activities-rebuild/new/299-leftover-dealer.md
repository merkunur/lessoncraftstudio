## The skill
Divide with a remainder and know the remainder must be smaller than the divisor.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE LEFTOVER DEALER: a pile of N pictures and d boxes. The child deals with a swipe: each swipe sends one picture to every box (a full round). When the pile has fewer pictures than boxes, the next swipe cannot complete a round — the pictures slide into a leftover cup instead. The child types the quotient (per box) and the remainder (cup). In 'find the error' mode a friend's answer is shown with a cup holding d or more pictures: the cup tips and its pictures deal one more round, showing why the remainder was too big. Exact-or-not mode: the child predicts whether the cup will stay empty before dealing.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** A remainder equal to or bigger than the divisor; writing the remainder as part of the quotient; thinking every division comes out exact.
- **Pictures:** fruits, toys, school supplies (identical per round)
- **Settings:** Mode: share it out / exact or not / find the error · Divisors: 2-5 / 2-9
- **Rounds:** Generated N, d with N ≤ 9d, remainder 0 in ~25% of rounds and 1..d-1 otherwise, ≥ 9 rounds; typed answers.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G3-377, G3-380, G3-381, G3-382, G3-383, G3-384
- **Distinct from:** Maple's Bakery uses exact divisions only; skip-rod measure-back shows a leftover gap but does not teach the remainder rule.
- **Build risk:** Low; 4.NBT.B.6 is above grade 3 — keep the grade-3 label as the worksheets do.
