# Torn Picture Mender — kid-facing "Granny's Mending Basket" — APPROVED DESIGN SPEC
**id:** repair-restore-12 · **standard:** RF.1.4.a (Grade 1 — read on-level text with purpose and UNDERSTANDING; the fluency-strand comprehension-MONITORING act) · **family:** repair-restore (head of the reusable "restore-the-text" engine family) · **engine:** new `engine-mend-page.js` (locale-neutral; ONE abstract op = tear-slots + piece-tray + ONE validator `attempt(piece)`; round-type is a `type` strategy field on a data descriptor; NEVER contains a sentence) · **runtime:** SIMPLE · **EN-first DEEP**
**Status:** approved 2026-06-21 (6-expert ensemble; all 7 critic-required fixes). Build deferred.
**also_teaches:** L.1.5 (word nuance) · L.K.5.A (categories/semantic field) · L.1.1.E (prepositions by meaning).
**Distinct from Sentence Surgeon (repair-restore-04):** that EDITS grammar tokens (L.2.1); here the child never edits grammar — they restore MEANING. No shared engine/character/mechanic.

## Concept & creative hook
**Granny's magic basket** holds torn, faded album/storybook pages — the tear pulled a piece of the MEANING out + the picture sits grey & still. The child = Granny's helper: read the page, work out the piece that makes it make sense, **drag the mending-tape to where the meaning says it goes** → the photo **develops into color (Polaroid)** + the torn half swings home + the figures come alive + **Granny reads the healed sentence aloud** (karaoke sweep).
- **Frame recast (fix #1a):** the 6-year-old feeling = **"I made the broken thing whole and it CAME ALIVE — the grey turned to color because of me."** Granny = warm delighted guide; the adult nostalgia/loss/grief subtext is DROPPED (ToM-heavy, not felt at 6). Reward = transformation.
- **The single emotional + pedagogical invariant:** **the picture is the REWARD for understanding, never the source of the answer.** Visible art ∩ candidate-pieces = ∅; the answer-detail lives in the TORN-AWAY region + is **never shown until the correct mend** (fix #5).
- **Anti-generic:** NOT eight comprehension-quiz formats in a paper skin — (a) the picture structurally can't be the answer-key (closes the #1 cheat of every reading game); (b) ONE interaction verb across all rounds → one coherent game; (c) a page ships only if **four dumb non-reading solvers all FAIL it** (build-gate).

## Core loop & the 7 distinct experiences
**Loop:** torn leaf (grey/faded picture + caption with a `.tear`) → read → **drag a piece to where meaning says it goes** → ONE validator `attempt(piece)`:
- **Correct →** FLIP-seat + tape-wipe + seam-heal (zigzag→flat) + **the picture develops in ONE motion** (grey→color + figures `.is-alive` + Granny speaks the healed sentence w/ karaoke) + lantern brightens + auto-advance.
- **Wrong →** **torn edges gently refuse to meet** ("let's read it again"); **NO torn-region pixels ever shown** (no wrong picture, no partial develop — fix #5); tiered no-shame re-teach. Nothing lost; grey page is "still waiting," emotionally neutral.

**ONE unifying interaction grammar (fix #4b — required):** every round = "drag the piece/tape to where the meaning says it goes." Drag (`window.LCS.drag.linear`) primary; tap-piece-then-tap-target accessible fallback through the SAME `attempt()`. cloze→drag strip across the tear; pronoun-connect→drag a thread from pronoun to person; preposition→drag figure into zone; category→drag word into the meaning-licensed basket; twin-scene→drag page DOWN onto the matching picture; sequence→drag strips into order; contradiction→**drag mending-tape OVER the wrong word to patch it** (on-metaphor).

**7 distinct experiences (fix #4a — distinct TO THE CHILD = a different ACTION/board/render, not an adult's reasoning-taxonomy over the same tap):**
| # | Experience (`type`) | The child's DISTINCT action / render | Why non-guessable | Standard |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ★ | context-clue cloze (`cloze`) — SPINE | drag the strip across the tear | KEYSTONE: answer fixed by an UPSTREAM content word NOT in gap ±1; POS-locked + grammar-valid + Tier-1 distractors (1 picture-trap + 1 local-fit decoy); all 4 dumb-solvers must fail | RF.1.4.a (core) |
| 2 | meaning-CONTRADICTION (`contradiction`) — purest monitoring | read whole sentence, drag tape OVER the one word that fights the meaning | no option menu — MONITOR meaning + locate the break; contradiction licensed by an upstream clue (remove clue → stops contradicting) | RF.1.4.a (core) |
| 3 | pronoun-connect / anaphora (`connect`, 2-sentence) | TWO-STEP: tap WHO "she/it/they" is (up in sentence 1), then drag the fitting piece | referent set upstream; candidates all real prior nouns → only tracking the referent across the boundary works; connect render = visibly NOT a cloze | RF.1.4.a |
| 4 | preposition-by-meaning (`placement`) | read intact sentence, drag the figure into the named zone | config not 1-of-N; minimal-pair sibling ("UNDER" vs "ON the quilt") shares all pieces → keyword reader caught; guess-rate ENUMERATED ≤0.20 | L.1.1.E + RF.1.4.a |

- **Honest count:** 7 experiences across 6 distinct renders. The 3 former "cloze" rounds are now genuinely distinct (spine cloze / two-step connect / drag-to-basket) per §A.13.60.
- **Round 3 category re-authored (fix #1b):** old "apples, pears, and ___ → fruit" = pattern-completion (fails strip-the-skin) → folded into #7 sort-by-meaning where the field is licensed by an upstream action ("Granny packed a snack for the trip: …") + a POS-valid non-field distractor ("spoon") is the trap.
- **LEAD with #1 (context-cloze) + #2 (contradiction)** — the two truest RF.1.4.a acts. **Dropped as too-advanced:** figurative/idiom, author's purpose, 3+-sentence inference, ambiguous-pronoun, attribute-the-speaker.
- **Free slice = #1 + #2 + one placement (#4)** — proves the real read-for-meaning skill. Per-pass order-only reshuffle (validated items only).

## Win-states & juice (no competition)
No timer/score/streak/countdown/fail. Reward = restoration + coming-alive (tape-wipe → seam-heal → Polaroid develop → figures alive → Granny reads the healed sentence). Vessel = a **memory-lantern that ONLY ever brightens** — monotonic, never dims on a miss, never shows a cap/target/number (fix #3c). Wrong = "let's read it again," torn edges refuse to meet, grey page isn't mended YET — no buzzer, no candy on the wrong path, no wrong-picture flash. Full-pass keepsake = the mended-page card → cross-game `GameCollection`. CSS/WebAudio; reduced-motion → cross-fade + spoken sentence; mute honored.

## Pedagogy
- **Instantiation test:** strip the picture AND the gap's two neighbor words — a competent reader still answers, an inattentive one still fails. Surviving text FULLY DECODABLE + sight words (decoding free, comprehension the only bottleneck); 1 sentence/page core (6-10 words); 2 sentences only for pronoun-connect (#3) + causality (#6).
- **THE KEYSTONE RULE (anti-guess backbone):** the correct choice is determined by a content word BEFORE the gap and NOT in its ±1 neighbors → forces global, not local, reading.
- **Worked example REPLACED (fix #2a — old soup example broken: "too hot to carry" is sense-valid → would punish a comprehending child).** Canonical clean: *"The puppy was so tiny that Granny had to look ___ to find him in the tall grass."* → {**carefully**=correct; **loudly**=POS-valid-but-senseless (you don't *look* loudly); **quickly**=local-fit decoy}. "tiny + tall grass + find him" licenses only careful searching; keystone words far from the gap. **The broken soup sentence is the gate's canonical REJECT test** (frequency-sense solver picks "hot" → page rejected).
- **MINIMAL-PAIR CONTRAST (unified w/ keystone):** core sentences authored in pairs sharing all referents, differing by one meaning-bearing word (different answers) → keyword/POS/picture reader answers identically across the pair → provably must read the differentiating word. Machine-checked.
- **Cheat-closing (each has a structural + MEASURED closer):** POS-matching → POS-uniform + grammar-valid + POS-only-solver-fails; picture-only → answer-detail in torn region never shown until correct + picture-only-solver-fails; local-reading → keystone upstream + keyword-only-solver-fails; frequency/idiom → Tier-1 familiarity-matched + **frequency-sense-solver-fails (the soup-catcher)**; 1-of-N → enumerated per-round guess-rate ceiling (NOT a cross-round success requirement — fix #3a).
- **No-shame re-teach = "read it both ways," AVAILABLE HELP not earned-by-failing (fix #3b):** miss-count internal + NEVER surfaced; Granny just gets more eager — (a) re-read whole sentence w/ the upstream clue glowing + read it w/ the wrong word then w/ the sense so the child HEARS which makes sense; (b) narrow to two + "which makes sense?"; (c) Granny models fully — and **the child ALWAYS performs the final mend** (agency; the page comes alive by the child's hand). Never "wrong 3 times."
- **Picture rule (hard):** visible art's objects ∩ candidate set = ∅; the answer-detail is in the torn region + renders ONLY on the validated-correct mend (fix #5).

## Interaction & UX
- `engine-mend-page.js`: `renderRound(ctx)` reads the descriptor; one abstract op (≥1 `.tear` + piece tray + ONE `attempt(piece)`); round-type = the `type` strategy field, never a code fork; never holds a sentence (strings via `ctx.round` data or `ctx.t` chrome).
- **DOM:** `.mend-leaf[ .mend-picture(.pic-whole/.pic-torn/.pic-seam) + .mend-caption(.tok×N | .tear) + button.readaloud ]` + `.mend-tray[.piece×2-4]` + `[aria-live=polite,sr-only]`. Pieces = real `<button>` (≥44px + keyboard/AT). Tear = CSS `clip-path` zigzag (complementary polygons); paper = `repeating-linear-gradient` (0 image bytes). Seat = FLIP piece→tear + tape-wipe (`.tear::after` `scaleX 0→1`). Reunion = seam-heal (zigzag→flat) + torn half swings home + `.is-alive` idle.
- **ONE validator, tap + drag:** `attempt(piece)` serves tap (primary) + `window.LCS.drag.linear` (ghost → hit-test target → same `attempt`); drag has no own success logic.
- **Wrong-state render contract (fix #5):** on a wrong `attempt` the render path MUST NOT paint any torn-region/answer sprite; edges refuse to meet; MEASURED gate check.
- **Mobile @280-768 (fixes #6a/#6b/#6c):** picture ABOVE caption at ALL widths — no structural reflow (where reading games break). Caption = `flex-wrap` real DOM tokens, font `clamp(17px,4.4vw,22px)` floor 17px; `.tear min-width` = measured MAX piece width (capped 60vw) → no reflow jump on seat; pieces ≥44px. Content lint ≤14 words/≤90 chars per round (DATA discipline, never a CSS line-clamp that hides words). **Round 5 twin-scene: two scenes STACK VERTICALLY (A above B), caption above BOTH, route = drag the page-token DOWN** (keeps caption-above-pictures + full 280px width so the single-detail diff is perceivable). **Round 7 sort: cap 3 items + exactly 2 bins ≤360px, bins stacked below, drag-down.** Drag-zones ≥44px AND ≥8px apart @280 (gate assertion 14). Audit {280,320,360,414,540,768}×{empty,filled}.

## Runtime mapping + MEASURED build-gate + PER-LOCALE
- **Round descriptor (DATA; engine never holds a string):** `{ id, type:'cloze|contradiction|connect|placement|match|sequence|sort', tokens:[{t}|{gap:true}], pieces:[{value,text}], answer, keystoneIndex, minimalPairId, picture:{family, spec, visibleObjects:[…], answerDetailInTornRegion:true, aliveFx:[…]}, tts:{prompt,options,healed}, extras:{contradictionIndex|referentIndex|zones|matchPair|sequenceOrder|sortBins}, collection, lint }`. `visibleObjects` derived from the actual render, not a free tag.
- **`verify-mend-page-core.js` (MEASURED — loads the real descriptors + drives the real engine; HALTS build):**
  - **THE ADVERSARIAL SOLVER BANK (fix #7 — the headline; analog of Game 5's counting-solver). For every `cloze`/`contradiction`/`connect` page, run four dumb non-reading solvers + ASSERT EACH FAILS; a page ANY solver passes is REJECTED:**
    1. **keyword-only** — picks the option with strongest local association to the gap's ±1 neighbors. Success → answer locally-cued → FAIL (the keystone, MEASURED).
    2. **POS-only** — picks the most "default" POS-fit. Must be at chance (all options POS-uniform); beating chance → not POS-uniform → FAIL.
    3. **picture-only** — picks any option matching a `visibleObjects` entry. Success → picture-disjoint violated → FAIL.
    4. **frequency-sense** — picks the most frequent/idiomatic completion. Success → FAIL (**the soup-catcher: picks "hot" for the broken soup sentence → correct REJECT**).
  - **Structural:** (5) POS-uniformity (real POS tagger over all options); (6) keystone upstream-clue index `< gap` AND not `±1` (connect: clue = prior sentence); (7) `visibleObjects ∩ pieceValues = ∅` AND `answerDetailInTornRegion=true`; (8) **wrong-state render emits NO torn-region/answer sprite** (drive engine on a wrong pick, assert the answer-region node never painted — fix #5); (9) ≥1 picture-trap + ≥1 local-fit decoy; (10) familiarity-match Tier-1; (11) decodability of surviving text; (12) MINIMAL-PAIR coverage (≥2 share referents, differ by one meaning-token, different answers); (13) **MEASURED guess-rate ceiling — ENUMERATE the option/arrangement space** (placement/sort ≤0.20; sequence-3=0.167; cloze=1/N mitigated by the frequency-sense solver, NOT by any cross-round success requirement — fix #3a); (14) per-type: contradiction-licensed-upstream (remove clue → non-contradictory) · placement/sort no-degenerate-zone AND zones ≥44px & ≥8px-apart @280 · twin-scene picture-minimal-pair + far-detail-word · sequence exactly-one-order + connective load-bearing; (15) ≥7 distinct (type+render signature) + reshuffle order-only; (16) position uniformity across the pass; (17) **3-native-speaker independent-agreement per cloze/contradiction page** (all 3 pick the same answer, zero "X could also work") logged per page — the human backstop for residual semantic judgment.
  - Plus standing gates: `audit-game-mobile.js` (every round type, base + filled, incl. twin-scene + sort), `audit-game-variety.js`, `audit-no-competition.js` (lcs-shell 0-line; lantern monotonic), SEO/JSON-LD §21.
- **Authoring discipline (fix #2c):** target ~5-6 deeply-authored pages per round at EN launch, NOT volume — better 5 flawless pages reshuffled (the §20.2 Fisher–Yates gives replay variety) than 20 that smuggle an ambiguous one past the gate. Run the solver bank against EN pages FIRST (it WILL reject early drafts — that's the gate working).
- **PER-LOCALE — EXPENSIVE + IRREDUCIBLE (the honest moat):** translation FORBIDDEN (breaks POS-uniformity + the upstream-clue word-order dependency + prepositions — Finnish encodes under/on as case-endings, not words). **EN-first deep.** Each locale = a §A.13.48 3-agent native ensemble that RE-DESIGNS (not translates): every sentence + semantically-controlled distractor set + minimal-pair sibling + picture-trap, natively authored; the gate (incl. the 4 solvers + 3-native agreement) re-runs per locale against that locale's own POS tagger + decodable/sight-word list. **Honor native round-exclusions** (the Finnish-no-prepositions / onset-rime EN-only precedent); engine + gate accept a reduced-but-valid round set; ≥7-distinct enforced only where culture-neutral.
- **Feasibility:** SIMPLE engine + DOM/CSS/WebAudio; the cost is the WORDS (irreducible, per-locale). Stub-first; local preview; tune the develop-sweep + tape-wipe + karaoke BEFORE wiring the pool.
