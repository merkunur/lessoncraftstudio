_**⭐ CONSEQUENCE-CLAUSE / ARITHMETIC-FACT lesson (Game 63 — PROGRAM-WIDE):** many standards weld a MEANING clause to a CONSEQUENCE clause — 2.OA.C.3 = "determine odd/even by PAIRING (the meaning)" + "express an even number as a SUM OF TWO EQUAL ADDENDS, 8=4+4 (the consequence)". The trap: the consequence clause is gradeable via a memorized ARITHMETIC FACT (8÷2=4) **divorced from the meaning** — a child types 4+4 by halving-arithmetic with zero grasp of "pairs-with-none-left-over," and the `ANSWER_DERIVED_NOT_STORED` / grade-the-artifact gate is satisfied while the meaning is absent (the cohort cheat one level below the obvious "grade the construction not the label"). Two structural moves fix it: **(1) BIND the consequence-artifact to the child's CONSTRUCTED meaning-structure** — the equation slots AUTO-BIND to the pairs the child physically made (the "4" is READ OFF their own benches, never free-typed from the count), so a fact-solver has no free `n/2` slot to fill; **(2) OFFER THE FALSIFYING CASE** — present the inputs where the consequence FAILS (odd numbers, where no two-equal-addends exist), so the child must DISCOVER "one is always left over" — that discovery IS the meaning, and removing it (even-only) deletes the only place the meaning is testable. Generalizes: whenever clause-B is a CONSEQUENCE of clause-A, **grade B THROUGH A's construction + always include the case where B fails**, or B degrades into an arithmetic-fact / lookup drill; add a `*_FACT_SOLVER` (the bare arithmetic) that MUST fail. Companion (re-confirmed): a "make-it-balance / make-it-equal" MOTOR affordance (a see-saw, a level boat) is ALWAYS eye-equalize-able (produce the equal split by visual balancing, no number) → DEMOTE graded balance/motor modes to a consequence-ANIMATION; the meaning rests on the predicted/constructed structure, not the visual balance (the rounding-order-mode / division-fewest-coins demotion precedent)._

_**⭐ ENACTED-PROCEDURE-IS-STILL-THE-PROCEDURE + WHO-PRODUCES-THE-INVARIANT (engine vs child) (Game 86 — PROGRAM-WIDE; for "enact the manipulative / use the model" games):** when a standard's cheap path is a MECHANICAL PROCEDURE (the ×k "multiply top and bottom" rule — owned + often above-grade), **MOTORIZING the procedure into a manipulative gesture (raking a ×k comb, dragging a ×k tool, sliding a model) does NOT remove the procedure-cheat — it RELOCATES it** from a symbolic box to a hand motion (a child who knows "target÷base = k, use that tool" passes with zero meaning — the Game-78 owned-op + the Game-77/80 motor-flail combined). AND the deeper structural test: **ask WHO PRODUCES THE INVARIANT the standard is about.** If the ENGINE renders it (auto-shades the proportional pieces, auto-snaps the equivalent, auto-balances the scale), the SOFTWARE demonstrates the target concept and the child only operates the tool → the invariant rides FREE. **THE FIX — grade the act WHERE THE CHILD MUST PRODUCE THE INVARIANT THEMSELVES** (the child owns the proportional shading / the conserved quantity / the balanced state, with error states the engine can't paper over) **+ add an independently-graded judgment the procedure CANNOT satisfy** (here the "did the AMOUNT move along the number line?" motion-judgment — a procedure computes numbers, not a sameness-of-quantity judgment; it scores chance on it). Two companions: **(1)** a foil only guards the cheat it actually stops — the additive foil (1/2→2/3) catches a NON-procedure child, NOT a procedure-without-meaning child (a foil the target cheat passes is NOT load-bearing against it — verify each foil stops its intended cheat); **(2)** a FIXED-PALETTE manipulative with a readable target label invites BRUTE-FORCE-BY-ELIMINATION (try tools till the label matches) → defeat by making the grade a child-PRODUCED assertion elimination can't fake, not a tool-selection. Generalizes to EVERY enact-the-manipulative game (fraction bars, base-ten blocks, number-line tools, balance scales, area models): motorizing a procedure ≠ teaching the meaning; CHECK who produces the invariant (engine-renders → re-center onto the child producing it); grade a procedure-ORTHOGONAL judgment; verify each foil. _**⭐ CLEVER-HANS / LEXICAL-TELL lesson (Game 75 — PROGRAM-WIDE; the classify-from-language game's blind spot):** when a standard's target distinction is FAITHFULLY SIGNALED BY NATURAL LANGUAGE (division partitive "shared among" vs quotitive "groups of"; author's-purpose cue-words; main-idea topic-words; tone words; part-of-speech-from-a-sentence; word-problem-type), the answer is recoverable by **KEYWORD-MATCHING it to the PROMPT** — a CLEVER-HANS cheat: the child copies the noun/cue from the question and passes WITHOUT the cognition. The crucial trap — **an ADAPTIVE keyword-matcher (reads the prompt, flips its answer exactly when the meaning flips) tracks the meaning PERFECTLY while modeling NONE of it, and a FIXED-policy gate solver (the usual "always-answer-X" conflation solver) STRUCTURALLY CANNOT catch it** → you need an ADAPTIVE (prompt-reading) gate solver that maximizes prompt↔answer lexical overlap. The TELL is an **inverted quality gradient**: the MORE natural/readable the prompt, the MORE leaky (good prose = good keywords) → the classify-from-language task is inherently a partial VOCABULARY task that must NOT sit on the grade boundary. The FLOOR — if the VALUE/answer-core is independently recallable (a times-table fact, a memorized answer) AND the only residual signal is the keyword-copyable label, the WHOLE assessment reduces to "copy the noun from the question," and `ANSWER_DERIVED_NOT_STORED` does NOT save you (the cheat RECALLS the value, doesn't store it). **THE FIX — (1) a `PROMPT_KEYWORD_MATCH_SOLVER` (adaptive, lexical-overlap) must FAIL + NEUTRAL noun-free prompts + a generation-time `token-overlap(prompt, correct) ≤ token-overlap(prompt, distractor)` assert; (2) RE-CENTER onto the cognition that survives KEYWORD-STRIPPING — INFER FROM WHICH QUANTITY/FEATURE IS GIVEN (hold the NOUN CONSTANT, vary only what-was-named: a COUNT vs a SIZE) so the disambiguator is STRUCTURAL not LEXICAL, AND make the structure CHILD-PRODUCED not surface-handed; (3) raise the answer's information content ABOVE 1 BIT (a structured assemble-the-answer, never a binary chip with every other quantity held constant). Demote the keyword-classifiable rounds to illustrative scaffold.** The re-center KEEPS the game flagship (scope unchanged; no operator fork — like Game-74). Generalizes to EVERY classify-from-language / match-the-meaning game (audit: is the answer lexically present in the prompt? is the answer-core independently recallable, leaving only a keyword residue?). Cousin of Game-72 [comprehension-gate validity blind spot — there the GOLD was contestable; here the gold is OBJECTIVE but LEXICALLY PRESENT IN THE PROMPT] + Game-73 [cheat in a surface the stimulus-only solvers miss]; distinct mechanism — the cheat reads the PROMPT (adaptive), so a fixed-policy solver is blind. **The four roads to a re-centered/scoped flagship are now: 72 contestable-gold→human-validity-panel, 73 content-yield→mid-size, 74 unassessable-mechanic→produce-the-symbol, 75 lexical-tell→keyword-stripped-inference.**_

_**⭐ OBSERVATIONALLY-UNASSESSABLE-MECHANIC / RE-CENTER-ON-PRODUCE-THE-SYMBOL lesson (Game 74 — PROGRAM-WIDE; a THIRD failure class — the mechanic's observable can't carry the cognition):** a mechanic that maps a CONTINUOUS GESTURE onto a PROPORTIONAL SCALE (set-the-slider / drag-to-the-value / fill-to-the-amount / set-the-clock-hand / place-on-the-number-line / point-on-a-dial) is **OBSERVATIONALLY UNASSESSABLE for the target reading-skill**: a skilled reader and a proportional-EYEBALLER (read only the endpoints, estimate the fraction, let SNAP-to-nearest round it onto the right tick) produce the **IDENTICAL observable** (a position → a value), so NO measured gate solver can ever separate them — an information-theoretic identity, NOT a patchable cheat. The TELL is a **tolerance contradiction**: the tolerance floor needed for a FAIR reading task (a correct reader who snaps must pass) EQUALS the ceiling on how much tolerance can defeat the eyeball (tighter = a MOTOR-precision task that punishes the very readers you want) — when the floor meets the ceiling, that mode cannot carry the assessment. **THE FIX — re-center the GRADED assessment onto modes where the cheat-strategy and the skill-strategy produce DIFFERENT observables: (1) PRODUCE-THE-SYMBOL (read-and-NAME — the child OUTPUTS the number/word, eyeball-proof ONLY if the distractors are within-K-units AND proportionally-INSEPARABLE so coarse fraction-reading can't sort them — else it's the eyeball cheat in a multiple-choice costume, the single most important sub-fix); (2) a RELATIONAL judgment under an ANTI-CORRELATED trap (compare under an anti-monotonic quota — surface-order ≠ value-order AND fraction-order ≠ value-order). The continuous-SET mode survives as UN-GRADED tactile SCAFFOLD — kept for delight + teaching, STRIPPED of assessment weight.** Generalizes to EVERY set/drag/fill/place mechanic: the SET direction is eyeball-able + weakly assessable; the READ/NAME direction (produce the symbol) is the assessable one. Crucially, the FIX KEEPS THE GAME FLAGSHIP — re-centering moves the assessment center-of-gravity, it does NOT shrink scope (Game 74 stayed a flagship; no operator scope-fork was needed). (Companion: an a11y SLIDER reintroduces the exact READOUT the visual design bans — `aria-valuetext` must be MARK-RELATIVE, never the resolvable running value; and SNAP-to-tick + per-attempt feedback enable RETRY-HILLCLIMBING → single-commit grading. Lineage: Game-71's region-reducible read is the volume-eyeball's ancestor; distinct from Game-72 [contestable-gold VALIDITY ceiling, fixed by a human panel] + Game-73 [CONTENT-YIELD ceiling, fixed by mid-sizing] — this is the THIRD road: the mechanic's OBSERVABLE can't carry the cognition, fixed by changing the OUTPUT the child PRODUCES, not the content or the gold.)_

_**⭐ ADJACENCY / CANDIDATE-SPACE lesson (Game 73 — PROGRAM-WIDE; the point-at-the-word game's blind spot):** for ANY "tap the target / point at the right word" game (modifies-which-word, referent/pronoun→antecedent, find-the-evidence, which-word-does-X), the load-bearing cheats live in the **CANDIDATE SPACE, not the labeling** — and they are INVISIBLE to solvers that test the stimulus in isolation or by ABSOLUTE position. TWO survive the obvious no-bins / class-is-the-output / dual-class fix: **(1) ADJACENCY / position-RELATIVE-to-the-cue** — natural language GLUES a modifier/referent next to its target (English attributive-adjective-before-noun "fast CAR", manner-adverb-after-verb "ran FAST"), so "tap the content word adjacent to the cue" passes **~95%+ WITHOUT the target cognition**, and it SURVIVES dual-class/witness structure (adjacency is computed fresh per-round, blind to the witness — fast→car AND fast→ran are both adjacent-correct). A `POSITION` solver that tests absolute index does NOT catch it → you need an explicit **`ADJACENCY_SOLVER`** (tap the cue's nearest content neighbour) forced to FAIL + an **`ADJACENCY_DECORRELATED`** assert (nearest==target ≤ chance, not ~95%), and a corpus authored from **non-adjacency templates** (fronted/comma cue + opposite-POS decoy adjacent; copular/predicate construction putting a decoy between cue and target). **(2) TWO-CANDIDATE BINARY DEGENERACY + SEMANTIC-COLLOCATION** — when the answer reduces to a one-bit choice between ~one-of-each-type, a blind 50/50 guess is **undetectable per-round** and ONE weak correlated cue (adjacency OR **semantic fit** — "which word does this make SENSE next to") pushes 50%→90% with zero grammatical/structural judgment. Fix the candidate SPACE: **≥3 content candidates with a same-type DECOY** (`MULTI_CANDIDATE_PRESENT≥N` so "tap the only noun/verb" can't win) + a **`SEMANTIC_FIT_SOLVER`** (a frozen collocation table) forced to FAIL via **both-plausible** items (the dual-class words, plausible with both candidates). Structural COROLLARY: forcing non-adjacency + multi-candidate + semantic-ambiguity + grade-legality SIMULTANEOUSLY collapses the qualifying corpus to a **FEW sentence templates** — so an OBJECTIVE-gold standard can still be CONTENT-YIELD-thin → MID-SIZE, and the post-fix gate ENFORCES it (it REJECTS the adjacency-trivial padding). Generalizes to every point-at-the-word game: audit position-relative-to-the-cue + candidate plurality + semantic-collocation, not just the label surface. (Distinct from Game-72's SELF-CONSISTENCY-vs-VALIDITY: there the GOLD was contestable [needs a human panel]; here the gold is OBJECTIVE [the gate decides], but the SUPPLY of cheat-proof items is small — two different roads to MID-SIZE.)_

_**⭐ SELF-CONSISTENCY-vs-VALIDITY / TWO-GATE lesson (Game 72 — PROGRAM-WIDE; the comprehension-game gate's blind spot):** for a comprehension/judgment game whose answer is an AUTHORED HUMAN JUDGMENT (author's purpose, main idea, tone, theme, character-trait), a machine SWAP-INVARIANCE gate (the same surface carries ≥2 answers → surface solvers ≤chance on a held-out split, the oracle reads the tag = 100%) proves ONLY **SELF-CONSISTENCY** (no surface feature predicts the gold). It is CONSTITUTIONALLY BLIND to **VALIDITY** (whether the gold is the answer a competent child / native adult would give) — because the oracle reads the very tag it is scored against (a TAUTOLOGY). A semantic authoring LAW ("one dominant answer all reasonable adults agree on") is UNENFORCEABLE by the machine gate (it can't read), so when the answer is CONTESTABLE — and author's purpose is INHERENTLY so (a text can inform AND entertain) — the machine certifies a 50/50 item and the game GRADES the child against a private gold → the Game-30 shame machine + a measurement-validity failure. (The Game-58 circular-gate lineage: a measured gate proves self-consistency, never validity.) **The fix: TWO ORTHOGONAL ship-blocking gates, ANDed — (1) the machine swap-invariance gate (surface-can't-win) + (2) a HUMAN BLIND-AGREEMENT pilot (the non-author validity channel: ≥N independent native reviewers classify each item BLIND to the gold; UNANIMITY required, tiered HARDER for the more-contestable item-classes; ANY disagreement → QUARANTINE; a per-item agreement STAMP the machine gate checks + HALTs on). The human panel IS the executable form of the semantic LAW the machine cannot enforce.** Structural COROLLARY: the MOST contestable item-class (here same-FORMAT divergence) — often the very one the design WANTS as the load-bearing assessment — may be UNGRADEABLE (it can't clear unanimity); the graded assessment must fall to the reliably-unambiguous class (here same-TOPIC divergence, where the CONTENT itself differs), with the contestable class demoted to ungraded scaffold — and that validity ceiling can SCALE THE WHOLE GAME DOWN (a flagship → a mid-size activity; Game 72 is the cohort's first critic-ruled MID-SIZE). Generalizes to EVERY authored-judgment comprehension game: the machine gate proves surface-independence; ONLY a non-author human panel proves the gold is the competent answer; and you can only GRADE the item-classes that clear unanimity. (Companion: an "intent" category whose label is a reader-EFFECT verb ["enjoy"] smuggles the fenced reader-response family back in — label it with the author's MOVE ["the author is playing / telling a fun story"], not the reader's feeling.)_

_**⭐ READ-TAXONOMY / VALUE-PERMUTE-INVARIANCE lesson (Game 71 — PROGRAM-WIDE; the count-visible cheat hiding at the READ step of any data/scale game):** when a data/graph/measurement-display game lumps its "load-bearing" reads into one bucket ("position-requiring" / "uses the scale"), AUDIT each read TYPE: is it REGION-REDUCIBLE (the answer is a spatial sweep that COINCIDES with the value-read on an ordered display — the two algorithms are extensionally IDENTICAL, so the cheat is UN-fixable by tuning data; it's a question-TYPE problem) or VALUE-IRREDUCIBLE (the answer requires locating NAMED values / reading the value AT a position — a spatial region-count gives garbage)? The Game-54 count-visible-objects cheat hides in the region-reducible reads: "how many longer than 6" answered by counting marks in the right region as generic objects, never connecting each mark to its measured VALUE — and "region-counting on an ordered number line GIVES the right answer," so it masquerades as distribution-reading. **Crucially, a "derived-not-stored / not-a-bar-graph" STRUCTURAL proof does NOT cover it** (a region-count USES the ordered scale, so it's blind to the structural mutation) — these are TWO DIFFERENT CHEATS and an ensemble will conflate them, applying the bar-graph fix and thinking the region-count is dead. **The mechanical sorter: the `VALUE_PERMUTE_INVARIANCE` test — permute which VALUE each mark encodes while keeping the marks' spatial ORDER fixed; if the oracle answer is INVARIANT → region-reducible → scaffold-only; if it MOVES → value-irreducible → the real assessment.** The fix: classify reads by this test, make the VALUE-IRREDUCIBLE tier DOMINATE the assessment (≥3/session), DEMOTE the region-reducible reads to ≤1 scaffold, and assert a region-count solver at SESSION granularity (it correctly STILL passes the scaffold reads — that is WHY they're scaffold — and must fail the session because the value-irreducible tier dominates). Generalizes to EVERY data/graph/measurement game (retrofit candidate): "uses the scale" is not enough — the assessment must require reading the VALUE the position encodes, not sweeping a spatial region the ordered scale makes coincide with the answer. (Companion: bind GENERATE→READ — if the child measured the data, the marks are THEIR first-person measurements, not generic objects to count; a load-bearing read over a stranger's pre-built plot re-opens the count-visible cheat. And the empty-interior-position is the sharp anti-bar-graph weapon: a number line keeps the 4-position present-and-labeled at zero count; "place the X between 3 and 5" is ordered-scale reasoning a categorical bar graph cannot express.)_

_**⭐ OPTION-SURFACE LEAK lesson (Game 70 — PROGRAM-WIDE; the cohort's anti-cheat blind spot, hiding in plain sight):** when an answer is a CHOICE among options and the anti-cheat rests on "the options are all variants of ONE thing, so no surface separates them," AUDIT THE OPTION SURFACE — not just the stimulus. The cohort's gates already balance STIMULUS-side cues (the context/sentence/scene) but routinely MISS the OPTION tiles, and a leak lives there: a free FUNCTION WORD ("will" → future), a TOKEN-COUNT difference, an orthographic-NOVELTY outlier (the one irregular form among regulars → "weird = the answer"), or a fixed POSITION/COLOR — each maps to the answer WITHOUT the target cognition, and the gate that only reads the stimulus is blind to all of them. **Worse — an anti-cheat LEVER can MANUFACTURE the leak: making the past irregular to defeat suffix-match makes it the novelty-outlier among regular present/future forms, so the fix for one cheat creates the next.** And it disproves a seductive structural claim ("all 3 options are the same verb, therefore morphology-proof") that an ensemble will trust on its face. **The fix: an `OPTION_SURFACE_NON_DIAGNOSTIC` assert — NO single surface feature of the option set (function-word presence, token-count, orthographic-novelty rank, position, color) correlates with the answer above chance — enforced by a must-FAIL solver PER surface feature, run over the RENDERED option tiles.** Stimulus-side cue-balance is HALF the job; option-side surface-balance is the other half, and the second is the one that hides. Generalizes to EVERY multiple-choice / pick-from-options game in the catalog (and is a retrofit candidate for the older ones). (Companions: a MORPH/TRANSFORM mechanic that produces the answer-form AS A CONSEQUENCE of a SIMPLER choice [tap the time → the verb auto-morphs] PERFORMS the production cognition FOR the child [the Game-65 scaffold-performs lineage] — demote it to a post-correct teaching reveal and make the GRADED act the production itself, never the simpler trigger; a "non-numeric abundance vessel" that fills MONOTONICALLY with progress is a soft score / "X of Y" in disguise → fill it non-monotonically; and a context's own COPULA ("was/is") is a tense-cue the child can read instead of the target verb → convey time via non-verb state-contrast or decorrelate the copula tense.)_

_**⭐ SCAFFOLD-RENDERED-AS-EVALUATED-VERDICT lesson (Game 69 — PROGRAM-WIDE; the deepest scaffold-leak the cohort has found):** a working-memory scaffold that the standard's cognition genuinely REQUIRES (the Bryant-Trabasso finding: transitive inference is only Grade-reachable when the premises are kept PERCEPTUALLY AVAILABLE — the bottleneck is memory, not logic) becomes a CHEAT SURFACE if the premises are rendered as EVALUATED VERDICTS ("A ▸ longer ▸ B") rather than RE-PERCEIVABLE OPERATION-EVENTS — because an evaluated-verdict display is a TRUTH-TABLE the child READS (role-frequency: "the object that is never a loser wins") instead of a relation the child COMPOSES. The lethal property: that role-frequency read is **INVARIANT under the obvious anti-cheat scrambles** (left/right flip, presentation order) — so scrambling the layout (the intuitive fix) kills only the SHALLOW positional read and leaves the DEEPER role-frequency read fully alive. This is the Game-65 scaffold-enables-bypass lineage at the working-memory layer, and the classic Bryant-Trabasso "labeling/end-anchor" critique. **The fix: render the scaffold as the RAW RE-PERCEIVABLE STATE of the operation, never the evaluated result — here, the frozen cord-SEGMENTS the child measured, carrying NO verdict label AND inheriting the SAME answer-hiding render-distortion (scale-inversion) that hides the answer everywhere else, so re-perceiving them is itself the already-killed pixel-cheat. The premises stay perceptually-available (the memory hook the cognition needs) WITHOUT becoming a readable answer-key; the child must recall the qualitative RESULT of each operation-event and compose. Gate with a ROLE-FREQUENCY solver (not merely a positional one) that must FAIL — the positional solver is the shallow skin; the frequency solver is the real test, and its failure is the PROOF the verdict-strings are gone.** Generalizes to ANY game that externalizes premises / intermediate results as a memory aid (transitive chains, multi-step reasoning, held relations): show the EVENTS to re-perceive, never the VERDICTS to read. Companion — the GENUINE-DIFFICULTY principle: a working-memory scaffold for a genuinely-hard cognition either LEAKS the answer or imposes the real load; done right (events not verdicts), the residual difficulty IS the cognition, and the no-fail/warm framing must ABSORB a real miss-rate rather than the design engineering the miss-rate to zero (which would mean the scaffold leaked) — the operator's call for the genuine-difficulty ceiling over a guaranteed-pass garnish is the premium-faithful one. (Also: the PIXEL-INVERSION render trick — true-length × per-object scale so `orderBy(pxExtent) ≠ orderBy(length)`, a bbox-sorter scores at chance — is the reusable way to kill a direct-visual/measure cheat when the answer is a magnitude; pair it with same-size diegetic icons so the lying pixels never read to the child as "drawings lie.")_

_**⭐ CLOSED-ENUM-RENDERED-VERBATIM lexical-collapse lesson (Game 68 — PROGRAM-WIDE; the SET-EQUALITY twin of Game-66's render-a-proxy):** when a comprehension answer is a SET-EQUALITY or SET-MEMBERSHIP over closed enums (Game-66 `canSee`, Game-68 experience-elements {action/feeling/problem/outcome}) AND the enum is RENDERED AS TEXT (forced by SR parity / a text-based standard), the oracle (`A.dim === B.dim`) and a pure LEXICAL-STRING-MATCHER over the rendered words **collapse into the same function** — the measured gate (which asserts over the STORED enum) certifies a comprehension game that PLAYS as string-match, and the surface/prop solvers are BLIND (the cheat is the answer's own description-words, a 4th text surface). WORSE: the construction that best TEACHES the standard — the abstraction-forcer (same experience-CATEGORY, different surface: lost-hat-found vs lost-boat-found → SAME) — is the MOST prone to collapse, because same-category → same-enum → same-WORD, so a lexical-matcher gets the teaching vehicle right WITHOUT abstracting. **The fix: a VARIED-WORDING render layer from a curated per-enum SYNONYM BANK (a FIXED decodable sentence skeleton + a VARIED load-bearing slot — which simultaneously preserves the SR parallel-beat scaffold AND Grade-level decodability) so the rendered text's LEXICAL same/different does NOT track the enum's SEMANTIC same/different; the GATE CONSUMES THE RENDER (a `*_WORD_MATCH_SOLVER` over rendered strings, synonym-set-aware, must score at chance in BOTH directions); and a polarity-symmetry assert (`NO_POLARITY_ONLY_DIVERGENCE` — DIFFERENT cases are contrasting POSITIVES, never positive-vs-negated) closes the negation-scan residue that paraphrase alone leaves.** The COST this surfaces and names: a comprehension game whose answer is enum-equality must budget the paraphrase bank as a FIRST-CLASS, per-locale, NON-PORTABLE artifact (~2× per-locale authoring — synonyms/decodability/case-marking are all language-specific) — paraphrase is the most language-bound layer there is, so its 10-locale fan is a separate later ~2× commission, not folded into a normal fan budget. Generalizes to ANY game where a structured answer-key (vantage, experience, attribute, category) is surfaced as readable comprehension text: vary the wording, gate the render, and budget the bank. (Companions, each reusable: a "re-represent on wrong" that CO-PRESENTS the two compared facts is a REVEAL-in-disguise for a one-bit judgment → re-teach by TEMPORALLY-SEPARATED re-experience, never side-by-side; art for a comprehension judgment must be DECOUPLED [zero mutual information with the verdict], NOT anti-correlated [negative correlation is still exploitable]; BASE-RATE balance — assert the asked dimension does not predict the verdict; and when a standard's honest cognition count is the thinnest yet, the premium move is to ADD a genuinely-distinct 2nd cognition — here "which dimension DIFFERS" identify-the-axis — not to lean entirely on the content-variety waiver.)_

_**⭐ INPUT-SURFACE-AFFORDANCE-ON-A-NO-FAIL-BOARD lesson (Game 67 — PROGRAM-WIDE; the choice of answer-INPUT surface is itself an anti-cheat decision):** the cohort kills "pick from N" cheats by switching to a PRODUCE surface (build/type the answer) — but a produce-BUILDER (number-stepper, keypad, dial, free-text) is a SEARCHABLE CONTINUUM, and on a NO-FAIL board (operator-locked: no fail-state, `ctx.correct()` auto-advance) the search space is NOT the full range but the TINY neighborhood the game HANDS the child. Skip-counting (2.NBT.A.2): the board DISPLAYS the last term and the stamp DISPLAYS the step, so the answer is a 1-2-tap NUDGE off an on-screen number — a child reaches it by local-increment-and-press, never adding the step, and every pattern-solver (digit/rote/interpolate) is BLIND (it's not a pattern, it's a hill-climb off the handed anchor). Switching from MC-tiles to a builder to kill elimination CREATED a cheaper cheat (the Game-46 nudge-to-green + Game-48 enumerator at the input-surface layer). **A "deliberate commit" alone is COSMETIC — a skippable re-show is not a cost.** **The fix is THREE composing locks (+ a grade discriminator): (1) never leave the answer's immediate predecessor as a free on-screen anchor — DOUBLE-BLANK / force a working-memory hold so the child computes the anchor too (the on-screen number must not be answer−step); (2) the commit fires ONLY from a deliberate action, NEVER from an input-change — INTEGRATION-ASSERT that setting the input to the correct value WITHOUT committing does not fire `correct()` (else the press is decorative and the whole anti-search story collapses); (3) a wrong commit costs an UN-SKIPPABLE state transition (clear-input-to-zero + a forced warm teaching beat the child must tap past) so search is strictly slower than computing — and the `*_SEARCH_SOLVER` must be tested against THAT cost-bearing state machine, not a frictionless accept; (+) the grade rejects near-misses (target±1, target±step) so a nudge doesn't land.** Generalizes to EVERY produce-input game on a no-fail board. The operator-acknowledged truth: a FULLY frictionless no-fail board and a search-proof produce-surface CANNOT coexist — the minimum viable friction is a warm forced teaching beat (chosen over weaker clear-only). (Companions: §A.13.60 honest count corrected 3→2 — REVERSE is a PARAMETER on PRODUCE, not a separate cognition; and the place-value-digit-increment-vs-magnitude HONEST CEILING — cross-hundred doesn't fully isolate magnitude from learnable carry-competence, so MANDATE backward-cross-hundred [the borrow has no naive forward-carry analog] and state the ceiling rather than pretend it's closed.)_

_**⭐ GATE-MUST-CONSUME-THE-RENDER / RENDER-A-PROXY-NOT-THE-ANSWER-FIELD lesson (Game 66 — PROGRAM-WIDE; the structured-data twin of the circular-gate):** the cohort's measured gate drives the real core + adversarial solvers — but it feeds those solvers the DESCRIPTOR STRUCT (the fields the oracle reads), while the CHILD plays the RENDERED DOM. If a structured field that DRIVES the answer is RENDERED VERBATIM to the child, the oracle and a pure word-match solver **collapse into the same function**, and the gate certifies a word-matching game as a reasoning game — BLIND, because the cheat lives in a text surface no struct-fed solver reads. RL.1.6 point-of-view: the SR-accessibility requirement forces the vantage into a TEXT label; rendering `canSee` verbatim ("Gull sees FAR; boats look TINY") next to a line that echoes it ("the boat looked TINY, FAR away") lets a child word-match with ZERO position→visibility inference — the keyword-proxy (Games 61/64) in a 4th text surface, and `EXACTLY_ONE_CONSISTENT_HOLDER` makes it WORSE by forcing the very divergence the bridge exploits. This is the Game-58 circular-gate lesson (gate what the child CONSUMES, not the author's model) applied to a STRUCTURED-DATA game, and the Game-64 tuple/render-split twin (render a proxy, not the answer token). **The fix, three locks: (1) the GATE MUST CONSUME THE RENDER — pure `render*()` functions feed the adversarial solvers the ACTUAL RENDERED STRINGS, never the struct (close the struct-vs-DOM gap or every text-surface cheat is invisible forever); (2) a structured field that DRIVES the answer must NOT be rendered verbatim — render a PROXY (here POSITION) from which the child INFERS the answer-driving field (VISIBILITY), and prove the inference is load-bearing by MUTATING the answer-claim and asserting the holder flips while every rendered proxy-string stays BYTE-IDENTICAL; (3) assert the answer-claim's content-words are LEXICALLY ABSENT from the winning proxy-label (`*_NOT_LEXICALLY_IN_LABEL`) so no word-bridge survives.** Generalizes to ANY game where a structured answer-key is surfaced as readable text (vantage labels, definition glosses, attribute tags, clue text): render the PROXY the child reasons FROM, never the KEY the oracle reads. Companion scope-rule: when a standard's reasoning needs an INFERENCE, confirm the inference is GRADE-EMBODIED (high/low→far/small is Grade-1-owned; occlusion/facing-direction is Grade-2) — restrict the deck to the embodied axis and let a SECOND cue-type carry breadth, rather than ship an above-grade inference the child guesses at. (And: §A.13.60 honest count corrected 3→2 — "vantage attribution" and "knowledge attribution" are ONE act over two cue-types.)_

_**⭐ TEACH-TIER-vs-ASSESS-TIER / SCAFFOLD-PERFORMS-THE-STANDARD lesson (Game 65 — PROGRAM-WIDE; the deepest gate-blindness of the cohort, distinct from every prior cheat):** every prior anti-cheat assumes the child is trying to shortcut the ARITHMETIC/answer (single-pass, back-fill, keyword, brute-tap, retrieval, world-knowledge) — and the measured gate kills those by driving the real core with adversarial solvers. But a SCAFFOLD built to KILL one of those cheats can itself PERFORM the standard's load-bearing cognition FOR the child, and the gate is BLIND to it because every solver presumes the PRE-STRUCTURED descriptor the scaffold produces. Two-step word problems (2.OA.A.1): the obvious anti-back-fill fix — SEAL step-2 until the interim commits — also PRE-SEGMENTS the problem (announces "two steps", enforces the order, presents each step as a labeled one-step) AND holds the interim on-screen. So a child does two HANDED one-steps in a forced order and passes, having NEVER self-segmented the situation or held the interim — which is the actual cognition 2.OA.A.1 assesses (and a permanently-collapsed step-1 = the LIVE one-step product = a fence breach). The tier "ladder" was cosmetic (same apparatus, only prose changed); the `SINGLE_PASS`/`BACK_FILL`/`KEYWORD` solvers all see a pre-segmented descriptor and pass it. This is the Game-44/46 cosmetic-fade lineage promoted to the problem-STRUCTURE layer: **the fix for the obvious cheat over-scaffolds and performs the cognition.** **The general rule: if REMOVING the game's apparatus would leave the child unable to do the task, the apparatus is performing the standard — you must ASSESS on a surface that doesn't pre-structure it. Concretely: split the ANSWER SURFACE by tier — (1) a TEACH tier where the scaffold demonstrates the structure (keep its anti-cheat value); (2) an ASSESS tier where the child produces the STRUCTURING as graded output on a NON-LEAKING surface (a plan/segmentation built from inputs that INCLUDE distractors + illegal pairings, NOT a handed labeled step or a 2-option fork — a `*_NOT_SYNTACTIC_RECOVERABLE` assert: the canonical first move ≠ the textually-first tokens ≥40%); (3) FADE the scaffold's externalizations (the displayed interim/structure) on the assess tier so the child carries them (a11y parity: faded = `aria-hidden`+out-of-tab-order, not just visually hidden); (4) a per-round RESETTING ladder so the assess tier keeps being re-presented (a no-fail re-tell must not silently demote a struggling child to a permanent scaffolded experience); and (5) a `SCAFFOLD_DEPENDENT_SOLVER` that MUST pass the teach tier and FAIL the assess tier — the structural proof the split is real, not cosmetic.** Generalizes to ANY game whose mechanic pre-structures the very thing the standard asks the CHILD to structure: multi-step, sequencing, sort-into-GIVEN-bins, fill-the-GIVEN-frame, complete-the-GIVEN-pattern. The tell: a scaffold introduced to kill cheat-X, where a child who can't do the un-scaffolded task still passes. (Companions this run: the §A.13.60 honest count is 2 not 3 — "interim-formation" and "operation-selection" are ONE act observed twice; and the order-load tripwire is DECORATIVE unless narrative-order ≠ operation-order — "baked 20 before gave 15" never tempts subtract-first; decouple reading order from operation order or drop the claim.)_

_**⭐ TUPLE-LAYER / RENDERING-LAYER SPLIT lesson (Game 64 — PROGRAM-WIDE; the keyword-proxy reborn in a COMPUTED answer's own gloss):** for any "pick the MEANING" game whose answer must be DERIVED (the cohort's `*_DERIVED_NOT_STORED` gate spine — to keep the answer un-stored, the oracle COMPUTES it), a templated gloss is the natural computed form (NOT→"not X", WITHOUT→"without X", FULL_OF→"full of X") — and that template RE-INTRODUCES the keyword-proxy (Games 61/62): every correct answer carries a SYNTACTIC FINGERPRINT that maps 1:1 to the operator/affix the child can SEE, so a `PARAPHRASE_TEMPLATE_SOLVER` matches operator→gloss-keyword with ZERO comprehension, and the root-only/wrong-transform/familiar-retrieval solvers are ALL BLIND to it (it's not the root, not a different transform, not a retrieved whole — it's the answer's own gloss-frame). **The trap that makes this the deepest catch: the obvious fix — "vary the phrasing so there's no fixed frame" — DESTROYS the very gate that defines the game.** A varied paraphrase ("open what was shut") CANNOT be computed by `applyTransform(op, base)`; it must be HAND-STORED; and `MEANING_DERIVED_FROM_TRANSFORM_NOT_STORED` rejects a hand-stored meaning. The computed-oracle and the cheat-defense are in DIRECT CONTRADICTION — you cannot keep both naively. **The fix — split the meaning into TWO layers: (1) the oracle computes a SEMANTIC TUPLE `{op, base}`, NOT a gloss string (the gate asserts the TUPLE is derived from structure — stays honest, the tuple IS computed); (2) a separate per-item `phrasings[]` table renders each tuple → surface text with ≥3 DISTINCT NON-FRAME realizations chosen at RANDOM per round (the leading word of the correct phrasing is NOT a function of the operator), and distractors are drawn from the SAME phrasings pool of OTHER tuples so a "not…"/"without…"/"full of…" co-occur across targets and the frame no longer identifies the operator; (3) the de-cheating solver (`PARAPHRASE_TEMPLATE_SOLVER`) runs against the RENDERED SURFACE, not the tuple — the load-bearing detail the naive brief misses — and MUST score at chance.** This severs the operator→keyword map WITHOUT collapsing the computed oracle. Generalizes to ANY game where a computed answer is a MEANING/LABEL that would otherwise carry a structural fingerprint (affix-meaning, definition-pick, function-of-a-part). The tell: a measured gate whose oracle emits a TEMPLATED answer-string — the template is the fingerprint; compute the structure, render it variably, gate the rendered surface. (Companion fences this run: a multi-sense operator [un-=NOT/REVERSE] births a POS-ROUTING cheat [route by word-category, not meaning] — CUT the second sense for the pilot or give it its OWN glyph; and the answer surface must be PURE-TEXT meaning-phrases with the operator-GLYPH as a post-commit reveal [`OPTION_REGION_CONTAINS_NO_SVG_TRANSFORM_GLYPH`] so the Game-63 visual-transform-match can't smuggle the answer through a glyph-to-glyph match.)_

_**⭐ CONTINUOUS-PROXY / GRADED-PER-ROLE lesson (Game 62 — PROGRAM-WIDE; the next rung below the keyword-proxy):** the surface-proxy ladder a child climbs to dodge a comprehension standard is order → specific-keyword → **GENERIC AFFECT/SALIENCE** (most-tense→problem, calmest→setting, biggest→most, etc.). Unlike order and keyword (DISCRETE signals you can fully strip — shuffle kills order; same-content-different-X kills the specific keyword), affect/salience is a CONTINUOUS signal that **cannot be fully stripped** (problems genuinely tend to be tenser) and that the content graph often ENCODES (feeling edges). Three consequences the obvious fix misses: **(1) the adversarial solver must be GRADED + PER-CATEGORY, not binary, not aggregate** — a `*_AFFECT_SOLVER` that scores 64% "fails" a binary gate but is a child winning 2-of-3 by vibes; and an aggregate at-chance score HIDES per-category leakage (85% on the calm category, 15% on the tense one). Assert EACH category's accuracy ≤ chance+ε. **(2) the corpus needs a measured CORRELATION FLOOR, not a "≥1 incongruent item" quota** — one calm-problem in a 7-item pool means affect wins 6/7; assert |rank-corr(proxy, label)| < threshold across the whole pool. **(3) the "same-X-different-Y" load-bearing mechanism LEAKS the continuous proxy unless authored PROXY-MATCHED** — a swing-pair (storm=setting in A, problem in B) authored "naturally" draws the problem-storm scarier, so affect still tracks the label across the very pairs meant to be hardest; force within-ε proxy-parity across each pair so the mechanism defends the continuous proxy too. Reconcile per-category: some categories decouple easily (a tense SETTING, a quiet SOLUTION), some are intrinsically proxy-linked (the PROBLEM is hard to make calm at K) — decouple where easy, defend the hard category via proxy-matched pairs, loosen its band. The human bypass-pilot adds a "vibes-only player ≤ chance" check._

_**⭐ ELIMINATION-via-REJECTION-FEEDBACK lesson (Game 62 — PROGRAM-WIDE):** a no-fail "place-it; if it's wrong it slides off, try the next slot" loop combined with a COMPLETENESS reward (the thing blooms when all N slots are filled) is a FREE ORACLE — with N slots and slide-off feedback a child brute-forces every item in ≤N tries, and place N-1 correctly and the Nth is FORCED by elimination, all with ZERO target comprehension; the gentle "function question" on a wrong placement is DECORATIVE (the child ignores it and tries the next slot). The no-fail design rewards the eliminator identically to the comprehender. **The fix: (a) on a wrong placement the item returns to the tray RESHUFFLED among the others (re-decide, don't mechanically advance to the next slot); (b) add a LOAD-BEARING DECOY — an (N+1)th item that belongs in NO slot — so "place N of N+1" is NOT elimination-solvable (the child must ALSO identify the decoy = comprehension), reframing the decoy from a soft "not everything has a role" nicety into a structural anti-cheat; (c) the win condition is the N TARGETS satisfied, NOT all tiles placed (so over-declining can't win either); (d) add an `ELIMINATION_SOLVER` (try-each-slot-keep-what-sticks + infer-the-decoy-as-leftover) that MUST FAIL.** Any sort-into-slots / fill-the-board game with rejection feedback inherits this — audit for it._

_**⭐ SITUATION-SURFACE / INTERPRET-BY-KEYWORD lesson (Game 61 — PROGRAM-WIDE; DEEPENS Game-52's "grade scene-selection"):** a powerful anti-cheat for a NUMBER-cheat (deal-and-count, fact-recall, count-the-result) is to make the child "interpret the SITUATION" — route/classify by the scenario rather than compute (the partitive-vs-quotitive route; multiplication's vary-the-scene). But the SITUATION ITSELF is keyword/picture-surface-matchable: a child maps trigger-words ("share/each→partitive", "pack/groups-of→quotitive") or scene-pictures (people→share, containers→group) to the answer with ZERO of the target STRUCTURAL understanding — the cohort surface-proxy pattern (rounding's digit-rule, Game-60's sequence-keyword, Game-59's rote-order) in the SITUATION channel. Worse: a "matched-pair / same-expression-different-meaning" fixture that VARIES the situation text is FEEDING the keyword solver, not defending against it. **The fix: grade the STRUCTURE, not the situation-route — make the graded act the identification of which quantity is KNOWN vs UNKNOWN (the role of the divisor: #groups vs group-size), placed into an expression frame; GRAMMAR-NEUTRALIZE the situation text (same verb + noun for both readings, the distinction carried ONLY by known/unknown + what's-asked, so no trigger-word maps to an answer); add SAME-SURFACE-OPPOSITE-STRUCTURE matched pairs (both say "share", one each-meaning one group-meaning); PICTURE-NEUTRALIZE (same imagery for both); and add a `KEYWORD_SOLVER` + `SCENE_PICTURE_SOLVER` that MUST score ≤ chance — the situation-axis analog of the same-number-multiple-meanings axis. The one-line acceptance gate: both surface-solvers ≤ chance.** This is the deepening of Game-52's "vary the scene, grade scene-selection" — scene-selection is itself keyword-matchable UNLESS grounded in structure. (Per-locale corollary: grammar-neutralization WORSENS the §A.13.56 doubled-trap — the single neutral verb may be ungrammatical or re-introduce a keyword per language; run a per-locale `KEYWORD_SOLVER`-must-fail gate BEFORE any fan-out.)_

_**⭐ SCHEMA-RECOVERABLE / SCRIPT-VIOLATING-DETAIL lesson (Game 60 — PROGRAM-WIDE):** for any NARRATIVE / SEQUENCE / ORDERING standard, the obvious comprehension fence — "make the order recoverable only from THIS content's causal logic" — is defeated by a carried-in prior the child already owns: a GENERIC event-SCHEMA (lose→search→find, problem→resolution, want→try→get, cause-before-effect). A `SCHEMA_SOLVER` orders the whole arc correctly WITHOUT engaging the specific content, because the very causal structure that makes order comprehension-load-bearing ALSO makes a GENERIC arc world-knowledge-recoverable (this is the Game-49 world-knowledge cheat reincarnated for sequence). Worse — the natural fix ("center the key DETAILS, not the order") has a HOLE: the key-detail FOILS are themselves schema-recoverable whenever the discriminating detail is the SCRIPT-EXPECTED value (the searcher IS the loser = the generically-expected continuation). **The fix, three locks: (1) the discriminating detail must be SCRIPT-VIOLATING — where THIS instance DEPARTS from the canonical schema (the searcher is NOT the loser; the cause is non-obvious); author-tag each unit's `scriptExpectedValue` and lint `trueValue !== scriptExpectedValue`; (2) the WIN must REST on the script-violating detail (the order is allowed to be schema-recoverable — kids legitimately know story shape; it's necessary-not-sufficient — but the win is decided by the detail the schema gets wrong); (3) gate a `SCHEMA/SCRIPT_SOLVER` (orders by the generic schema + picks the canonical-continuation value) that MUST FAIL — if it passes, the content is too generic → reject.** Companion: variety for narrative content is ≥N DISTINCT causal SCHEMAS (loss/mistake/fear/help/transformation/…), NOT N reskins of one script (the operator's distinct-SHAPES-for-fractions ruling applied to narrative — N reskins of lose→search→find = "≈one script reshuffled"). (Also: a per-locale trap — stripping temporal/sequence keywords to kill the keyword cheat can DESTROY orderability where the causal link is carried by a connective [German weil, Finnish causal-case]; author causality in the EVENT CONTENT, not connective-dependent.)_

_**⭐ OPTIMIZATION-MODE-IS-PROXY-PASSABLE lesson (Game 59 — PROGRAM-WIDE):** for any standard whose mode is an OPTIMIZATION ("make X with the FEWEST coins / steps / pieces"), the natural gate — assert the committed answer is the OPTIMAL one (`FEWEST_COINS_OPTIMAL`) — is BLIND to whether the child reached it via the target cognition or a VALUE-BLIND PROXY. A child who knows only the rank ORDER of the items (quarter>dime>nickel) with ZERO knowledge of their actual values solves fewest-coins by greedy-largest-first + climbing the consequence-free hand-back signal — and the optimal-answer is identical to the value-reasoner's, so the gate passes the proxy. When **greedy-by-proxy ≡ greedy-by-target at the interaction layer** (true whenever the optimization's greedy rule coincides with a cheap surface ordering — rank, size, position), the optimization mode CANNOT carry the cognition alone. **The rule: center the EXACT-value, ORDER-PROOF modes** (here equivalence "5 pennies = 1 nickel needs the exact 5, not the order" + valuation "the exact total, order tells you nothing") **and DEMOTE the optimization mode to a minority/scaffold**, and add a `*_ORDER_SOLVER` (knows the rank, not the values) + a `*_SIZE_SOLVER` (orders by a rendered surface proxy) that MUST FAIL the dominant modes. Bonus weaponization: if the domain has a proxy-INVERSION (the dime is physically SMALLER but worth MORE), render it TRUE-to-life and make every load-bearing item hinge on it → the size/proxy solver scores 0% BY CONSTRUCTION (never scale the rendering BY the value — that BUILDS the proxy). Companion: a `GRADE_BY_VALUE_NOT_IDENTITY` assert (type-disjoint-value-equal both accepted; type-equal-value-different rejected) proves value-grading at the GRADER layer, not the answer-key. (Also this run: a STALE-INDEX gate-check failure — 2.MD.C.8 read "UNOWNED" but a live free activity owned its core; ALWAYS verify candidate standards against the live activities/mini-tools, not the policy-note prose.)_

_**⭐ AUTHORED-CONTENT GATE / THE CIRCULAR-GATE lesson (Game 58 — PROGRAM-WIDE, the deepest gate-catch of the cohort):** the cohort's whole gate philosophy — a MEASURED gate that drives the real core + adversarial solvers that must fail — silently DEPENDS on the answer being DERIVED FROM STRUCTURE (the math games: the gate computes the answer the same way the runtime does, so passing the gate proves the runtime). For an AUTHORED-CONTENT game (a story + questions + a correct answer the author WROTE — and the whole ELA/RL/comprehension family is this), that dependency BREAKS: the gate's "comprehension oracle" reads the author's ANNOTATION and selects the author's flagged answer, so it proves only "the author's annotation selects the author's answer" — VACUOUSLY TRUE BY CONSTRUCTION. The child does not consume the annotation; the child consumes the RENDERED ARTIFACT (the art / the audio / the rendered scene). Every cheat the structural gate "killed" (picture-leak, plausibility, keyword, trope) re-enters through the unverified ARTIFACT-vs-DESCRIPTOR gap, and "recompute the flags rather than trust the author" is THEATER (it recomputes one author field from another author field — still no ground truth but the author). **The general rule for any authored-content game: (1) BIND the rendered artifact to the gated descriptor — make the artifact COMPUTED from the descriptor (the ENTITY-REGISTRY: the renderer draws exactly the declared entities; the art brief is generated from it), so "what the artifact shows" becomes ground truth the gate can actually check; (2) VALIDATE with a NON-AUTHOR channel — a BLIND second-annotator (writes the answer key from the rendered artifact, blind to the author's annotation; agreement = unique-determination) + a HUMAN/native BYPASS-PILOT on the RENDERED output (reviewers see a DIFFERENT same-shape item's artifact then this item's questions; above-chance = the artifact leaks → reject — the ONLY non-circular validity test). Gate what the CHILD CONSUMES, not the author's MODEL of it.** The tell: a measured gate whose "oracle" reads author-written fields rather than computing from primitives — that oracle proves self-consistency, never validity. (This founds the whole RL strand on a re-usable un-circular authored-content gate; it is also the cohort's first HITL ship gate — justified because a strand-opener leak propagates to every game built on its engine.)_

_**⭐ RENDERED-NUMERAL-SET / GATE-WHAT-THE-PAGE-SHOWS lesson (Game 57 — PROGRAM-WIDE):** the cohort's strongest scoring-integrity idiom is `ANSWER_DERIVED_NOT_STORED` (the head-flip / mutate-a-field test) — but it proves only that the SCORING composes from structure, NOT that the RENDERED page withholds the answer. A design can store nothing yet still hand the child everything: if the pre-commit DOM displays enough numerals to compute the answer by a pure function, a `*_COMPARISON_SOLVER` reading only those on-page numbers wins with zero target reasoning — and the derived-not-stored proof is blind to it. Rounding's deepest cheat was exactly this: the pedagogy MANDATED a labeled midpoint every round, so `value` + `midpoint` were both on-page numerals → the task collapsed to `value≥midpoint?hi:lo` (a two-number comparison = the fenced comparison standard), place-value never engaged. **The general rule: gate the RENDERED NUMERAL-SET, not just the descriptor — assert the set of numerals visible BEFORE the child commits is provably INSUFFICIENT to derive the answer by any pure-arithmetic function** (`NUMERAL_SET_INSUFFICIENT`), and add a solver that reads only the pre-commit on-page numbers and MUST score ≤ chance. The fix when it fails: make the missing piece CHILD-DERIVED (re-sequence to construct-then-decide — the child produces the bracket/midpoint from the target skill, so the snapshot doesn't contain it). The sharpest tell: a cheat created by the PEDAGOGY'S OWN mandate (the labeled model the standard requires is the very thing that hands the answer) — the canonical teaching representation and the answer channel are the same artifact, split them by deriving it. (Companion: a to-scale number-line leaks the answer as a PIXEL-GAP — render the model to-scale only on the TEACH beat, SCHEMATIC/uniform-spacing on the DECIDE beat.)_

_**⭐ DUAL-RECALL-SOLVER / GATE-BOTH-RECALL-ROUTES lesson (Game 56 — PROGRAM-WIDE):** the cohort's strongest anti-recall idiom is the WORLD-KNOWLEDGE solver (Game 49) — but it models ONE recall route at a time, and a game can leak the answer to the child's head through MORE THAN ONE channel simultaneously. Shades-of-meaning's same-set invariant kills plain-synonym-match, but leaves the answer recall-reachable by BOTH (a) a memorized RANK [familiar gradients big<huge<gigantic are retrieved, not reasoned — the order/which-stronger modes] AND (b) a memorized context-KEYWORD COLLOCATION ["baby→tiny" learned as a pairing — the pick-for-context mode, which was the proposed fix for (a)]; a single familiar/world-knowledge solver catches at most one. The general rule: when an answer can live in the child's head, enumerate EVERY recall route (rank-table · keyword-collocation · item-retrieval · sight-fact) and add a SEPARATE adversarial solver per route, EACH must score ≤ chance — plus the structural invariant that defeats it (`NON_CANONICAL_ORDER` strips rote ladders from the order mode; `CONTEXT_KEYWORD_AMBIGUOUS` makes the same keyword map to ≥2 answers so no keyword→word lookup is stable). The tell: a "fix" for one recall cheat that is ITSELF a different recall cheat (the pick-for-context fix that's keyword-collocation recall) — gate the fix, not just the original. Companion: for a gradient/degree standard, bar perceptual TIES (`MIN_PERCEPTIBLE_RANK_GAP` + `EXACTLY_ONE_DEFENSIBLE`) — an unperceivable adjacent step (big≈large) ships an unanswerable item. (Also: shades is the MOST language-bound L.5 strand — the keyword-AMBIGUITY corpus must be RE-authored per locale, not just the words; lead with size+temperature, allow size-only locales.)_

_**⭐ STRUCTURALLY-KILLED-OBVIOUS-CHEAT ≠ MECHANICAL-COMPLETION-CHEAT lesson (Game 55 — PROGRAM-WIDE):** when a game makes the OBVIOUS cheat structurally impossible (e.g. the dual-graph "interior is inert, only the boundary is interactive" firewall that kills perimeter-area confusion), it often leaves a MECHANICAL-COMPLETION cheat wide open: the child completes the only-interactive-thing (sweep every tappable edge / fill every highlighted cell) until the task auto-resolves, with zero target reasoning — and the obvious-cheat solvers are blind to it (this is the Game-54 mechanical-fill / object-counting pattern). Any game where the answer auto-derives from completing a constrained interactive set must (a) DECOUPLE the answer-report from the completion act (report by REASONING over labeled values, not by recounting the thing you just completed; no running counter that turns completion into a readout), (b) make the REASONED modes DOMINATE + demote the completion act to a flagged concept-intro, and (c) add a `MECHANICAL_*_SOLVER` (completes-without-reasoning) that MUST FAIL the dominant modes. The answer must be a reasoned report, NOT a byproduct of exhausting the interactive set._

_**⭐ COUNT-THE-VISIBLE-OBJECTS lesson (Game 54 — PROGRAM-WIDE):** when a game's answer is a COUNT of on-screen items (area = N unit squares, etc.), a child can produce the right number by counting the visible items as GENERIC OBJECTS (1,2,3… = K.CC.B.5 counting) without the target cognition (measuring, grouping, the unit-meaning) — and the compute/read/pre-placed solvers are BLIND to it (the count-and-oracle agree by construction). Any count-answer game must (a) SEVER the count from a separately-countable visible array at report time (e.g. the unit tiles KNIT/dissolve into one whole after covering, so the count comes from the ACT not a static tally; or report under occlusion), and (b) add a `COUNT_VISIBLE_OBJECTS_SOLVER` that MUST FAIL the dominant mode. The deepest form of the Game-53 "derived ≠ the-child-understood": even a child-REPORTED count can be the wrong cognition if the report is a visible-object tally. Companion: a "non-numeric abundance vessel" in a COUNTING game is a hidden score — make it non-numeric AND non-content (no integer except the current answer)._

_**⭐ DERIVED-NOT-STORED ≠ THE-CHILD-DERIVED lesson (Game 53 — PROGRAM-WIDE):** the cohort's strongest gate idiom — "the answer is DERIVED from structure, never a stored boolean/index" (the head-flip / mutate-a-field test) — proves the SCORING composes, but it does NOT prove the CHILD composed. If the target meaning can also be RETRIEVED (a familiar item whose known answer equals the composed answer by construction) or read off a leaked channel (a picture of the whole), a RETRIEVAL/shortcut solver lands on the gate-correct answer and the derived-not-stored proof is blind to it. Any game where the answer is memorizable/retrievable must (a) use NOVEL items (composition forced, retrieval-blind) on the load-bearing rounds + strip the leak channel (no whole-item picture), and (b) add a `*_RETRIEVAL_SOLVER` (seeded with familiar answers) that MUST fail. Watch for a vocab/content spec that mandates FAMILIAR items while the standard's verb is PREDICT/COMPOSE (novelty) — that contradiction is the tell. Gate the retrieval route, not just the scoring._

_**⭐ NUMERAL-EXTRACTION / SYNTACTIC-SHORTCUT lesson (Game 52 — PROGRAM-WIDE):** when a game's answer-relevant numbers are rendered as live DOM/SVG `<text>` (the operator-locked 11-language requirement), the numerals are MAXIMALLY machine-legible — so a SYNTACTIC-EXTRACTION solver (read the visible numerals, concatenate/pattern-match them into the answer) can pass WITHOUT the target cognition, and the cohort's count-all/no-text solvers are BLIND to it (it reads glyphs, not objects/context). Any game whose answer is constructible from the on-screen numerals must (a) make the answer **unrecoverable from the visible numerals** — e.g. INVERT the task so the numerals are shared across choices and only the STRUCTURE/MEANING disambiguates (fix the expression, vary the scene; or an inferred/unbadged round), and (b) add a `NUMERAL_EXTRACT_SOLVER` adversary + an `ANSWER_NOT_RECOVERABLE_FROM_VISIBLE_NUMERALS` assert, ranked ABOVE count-all. The live-DOM-text mandate that serves SEO/a11y also arms the cheat — gate it. (And: a "commuted/positional foil" fix can itself be defeated by a positional habit AND collide with a fenced sibling standard — prefer a structure-disambiguation fix.)_

_**⭐ DIMENSION-MATCH / DISTRACTOR-AXIS lesson (Game 51 — PROGRAM-WIDE):** for a discrimination standard (pick the X-related item among choices), a cross-category decoy defeats only ASSOCIATION-matching — it leaves a DISTRACTOR-AXIS heuristic live: if the only same-AXIS choice is always the answer, a child passes by "pick the one on the same axis (size/temperature/dimension)" WITHOUT processing the target RELATION (opposition/equivalence/etc.). The fix is a **same-axis-NON-target decoy** (a second choice on the same axis that is NOT the answer — black→white needs a RED decoy, not just a cross-category one), asserted present + a `DISTRACTOR_AXIS_SOLVER` that MUST fail. Companion ruling: for a partly-RECALL standard (K.L.5.b), do NOT anti-rote-engineer (recall IS the skill) — but a same-axis distractor is still what makes the RELATION (vs bare recall/association) load-bearing; honor recall on the no-third-pole (complementary) rounds, capped. And: an "enact/produce" escape from a pick-cheat is ILLUSORY in a no-physics DOM engine (enact collapses to a same-axis pick) — fix the DECOY STRUCTURE, not the interaction skin._

_**⭐ PER-ROUND-vs-PER-DECK gate granularity lesson (Game 50 — PROGRAM-WIDE):** a content-RATIO constraint (e.g. "≥30% of rounds defeat cheat X") makes the deck-level adversary score ≈ chance OVERALL while the cheat stays LIVE on the majority of rounds the child actually plays — the gate reports "cheat X fails" as one deck boolean and is BLIND to the per-round reality. Any cheat the child can exploit round-by-round must be defeated as a PER-ROUND STRUCTURAL INVARIANT (every round carries a mis-fire decoy) and asserted on 100% of rounds, NOT as a deck ratio. Measure the cheat at the granularity the child plays. Retrofit candidate: any game relying on a "≥N% of rounds" content ratio to kill a cheat. **Companion ruling (Game 50): a SOUND-skill game (rhyme/phonics-audio) is an HONEST audio-first LISTENING game** — do NOT bolt on a non-audio path that silently converts the standard into a visual/print match (and never a printed-answer-highlight); audio is load-bearing, the audience is consciously narrowed to hearing children, and non-audio learners are served by the catalog's non-audio games._
