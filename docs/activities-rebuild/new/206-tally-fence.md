## The skill
Show a count with tally marks grouped in fives, and read a tally as fives and ones.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE FENCE THAT LATCHES AT FIVE. Make mode: a field of identical library animals (one noun per round) waits beside an empty fence line. Tapping an animal walks it through the gate into the pen and drives one upright post into the fence. After four uprights the gate stands open and the next post arrives lying FLAT above the gap: the child must drag it diagonally across the four uprights to latch the bay (it snaps from top-left to bottom-right). If the child taps another animal instead, the gap is still open: that animal trots through and straight back out to the field, and no post is added — a fifth upright cannot be made. A latched bay tints slightly as one 'five'; the next post starts a new bay. When the field is empty the child types how many on the keypad. Read mode: a finished fence (no animals) is shown and the child types the number it shows. Miss 1: the latched bays pulse one after another with '5, 10, 15' rising above them; miss 2: every post gets a small numeral under it. Right: the gate swings shut and that many identical animals settle in the pen.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Drawing five (or six) upright strokes before crossing, and counting every stroke one by one instead of reading a closed bundle as five.
- **Pictures:** farm-animals/sheep, cow, pig, goat, horse, duck (identical instances, one noun per round); fence, posts and latch drawn as apparatus
- **Settings:** Mode: make the tally / read the tally · Numbers up to 10 / up to 20
- **Rounds:** Make: 9 rounds n = 3–10 (to 10) or 6–20 (to 20), including 4 (one before the latch), 5, 10 and 15 exactly; read: 9 fences with distinct n (never 0); keypad answers in both modes so there is no answer position; order-only reshuffle per pass.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** K-014, K-015, K-025
- **Distinct from:** Counting Table / Treat Box count with the picture set itself; no instrument represents a count with a SYMBOL bundle. Not the Ten-Shelf (tens of egg cups): the five-latch is a different grouping and a written-mark representation.
- **Build risk:** None structural; post-and-latch geometry is inline SVG apparatus.
