## The skill
Synonyms are different words with the same meaning: swapping one for the other leaves the picture the same.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE PICTURE THAT DOES NOT CHANGE. A library picture sits under a sentence with a coloured swap slot ("The dog is big."). Word cards lie below. Dropping a card into the slot makes the picture redraw to match the new sentence: "large" leaves it still and the slot locks with a click; "small" shrinks the dog; "brown"/"red" tints it; "happy"/"sad" swaps the emotions face beside it. The task is to find the word that keeps the picture still. Miss 1: the picture changes and stays changed for a moment beside a faint copy of the original, so the change in meaning is seen; then the card returns. Miss 2: the cards that change the picture grey a little. PAIRS mode (G2-373, absorbed Meaning Mirror): left words are dropped on right words; each pair is tested in the same sentence. TWO-WORDS rounds (G1-395): two cards keep the picture still — both must be found.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Picks a word from the same topic (big -> tall, or big -> dog) instead of one with the same meaning.
- **Pictures:** animals, toys, fruits (scale and tint transforms only), emotions (happy, sad, angry, scared, tired) for feeling adjectives.
- **Settings:** Words: size and colour / feelings · Choices: 3 / 4 · Task: find the swap / make pairs
- **Rounds:** 9 rounds from a native-authored bank of picturable synonym pairs (big/large, small/little/tiny, happy/glad, scared/afraid, angry/cross, tired/sleepy…); distractors always one antonym + one same-topic word that redraws differently; the synonym's tray position balanced; one right word (two in two-word rounds).
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G1-395, G2-358, G2-373
- **Distinct from:** Shades of meaning orders by strength; Quill's opposites finds antonyms; this tests meaning-preservation.
- **Build risk:** Some locales have few picturable pairs — the native panel authors ≥ 9 per locale or the setting is refused there; G2-358 synonym worksheet faces that are verb-based go to doing-stage.

## Merged into this instrument (cover each as a setting or round set)
- grade 2 "The Meaning Mirror — Words That Mean the Same" (meaning-mirror): Recognise synonyms by testing whether a swapped word keeps the meaning of a sentence. — A short sentence with one word in a coloured slot ("The dog is big.") and, beside it, a MEANING MIRROR that shows the sentence as a picture (a big dog at large scale). The child drags a candidate word into the slot: if it means the same ("large"), the mirror picture stays still and the slot locks; if it changes the meaning ("small", "brown"), the picture changes to match the new sentence (the dog shrinks) — the child sees that meaning moved. Pairs mode: drag left words onto right words; each pair is tested in the same sentence.
