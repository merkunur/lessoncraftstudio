## Templates from today's worksheet types

**Hear rhymes: rhyming pairs, rhyme or not**

- `K-232` Rhyming Pairs — Draw a line between the two pictures that rhyme. (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-232-rhyming-pairs.js)
- `K-352` Rhyme or Not? — Say both pictures out loud. If the two words rhyme, circle the check mark. If they do not rhyme, circle the X. · screen version (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-352-rhyming-words-rhyme-or-not.js)

**Hear, sort, finish and produce rhymes**

- `G1-309` Rhyming Words: Rhyme and Write — Say the first picture. Circle the picture that rhymes with it, then write its word on the line. · screen version (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/g1/G1-309-rhyming-words.js)
- `G1-343` Sort the Rhymes — Say each picture in the bank. Find the big picture it rhymes with and write its word on a line under that picture. · screen version (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/g1/G1-343-rhyming-words-sort-the-rhymes.js)
- `G1-344` Finish the Rhyme — Read the two lines out loud. Look at the picture, hear the rhyme and write the missing rhyming word on the line. · screen version (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/g1/G1-344-rhyming-words-finish-the-rhyme.js)
- `G1-345` Rhyme Strings — Say each picture. Find the two words in the bank that rhyme with it and write them on its lines. Some words fit nowhere. · screen version (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/g1/G1-345-rhyming-words-rhyme-strings.js)
- `G1-346` Write Your Own Rhymes — Read the word beside each picture. Think of two words that rhyme with it and write one on each line. (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/g1/G1-346-rhyming-words-write-your-own-rhymes.js)
