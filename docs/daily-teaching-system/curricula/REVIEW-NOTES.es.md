# Reviewers' notes: registry `es`

Written by the reviewers of the crosswalk (`scripts/curricula/apply-review.js`). 306 teaching points carry a reviewer's row; the reason for each is in `frontend/content/curricula/es/crosswalk-authored.json`.

## author-math

- Clock reading is a 3° de primaria process in this programme (F4.SPC.18.G3.1: hora, media hora, cuarto de hora y minutos). Fase 3 (1° and 2°) has no clock objective at all, so the grade 1 and grade 2 time sheets all point two years ahead; a Mexican teacher of 1° will look for them under 3°.
- Fractions start in 3° (medios, cuartos, octavos, dieciseisavos); thirds, fifths, sixths and tenths are 4°. The grade 2 halves/thirds/fourths sheets and the grade 3 sheets with thirds or sixths sit a year or two early for Mexico.
- Conventional units arrive in 3° and only as metro, decímetro, centímetro, kilogramo and litro with their halves and quarters. Grams and millilitres are not named in Fases 2 to 4, and inches and feet are never used: the 'choose the unit' sheets must not show in. or ft. in the Mexican edition.
- Perimeter and area (F4.SPC.17) and the faces, edges and vertices of prisms (F4.SPC.14.G4.1) have only a 4° process, and bar graphs are first named in 4° (F4.SPC.19.G4.1); 1° to 3° work with tables and pictograms. Volume, rounding, odd and even numbers, grid coordinates and the 24-hour clock are not named anywhere in Fases 2 to 4.
- In 2° the conventional algorithm is named only for addition with two-digit numbers (F3.SPC.10.G2.3); written subtraction and three-digit sums are 3°. The '<', '>' and '=' signs for comparing numbers are a 2° process, a year after the grade 1 sheets.
- Money has no process in primary Fases 3 and 4 as given here; it appears only in preschool (F2.SPC.4.GP2.7, GP3.8). The coin and shopping sheets were mapped to the addition and subtraction problems 'vinculados a su contexto'; they need Mexican pesos (monedas de 1, 2, 5, 10 pesos and billetes) to count as context.
- Ordinal numbers appear in preschool (GP3) and then again only in 3° (up to ten elements) and 4° (up to twenty); Fase 3 has none. Picture patterns likewise live only in preschool; primary 'regularidades' are about the number sequence.
- Science: life cycles, flower parts, the planets and floating/sinking are not named in Fases 2 to 4 (the sky content is only the Tierra-Luna-Sol system). The 2° programme explicitly observes the Moon 'sin pretender que reconozca los nombres de las fases lunares', and the water cycle (4°) lists evaporación, condensación and solidificación, not precipitation or collection.
- Content of these years that no teaching point covers and that teachers will look for: healthy eating and the Plato del Bien Comer (F3.SPC.2, F4.SPC.3), care of the body and the sense organs, the locomotor and digestive systems (3° and 4°), vertebrates and invertebrates, herbivores/carnivores/omnivores and food chains, forces and movement (push, pull, trajectories), sound and light (shadows, opaque/transparent), states of water and mixtures, eclipses, natural regions of Mexico (desiertos, selvas, manglares), decimals to hundredths (4°), multiplication with three-digit products, the Maya way of writing numbers with dots and bars (F2.SPC.4.GP3.11) and capacity comparison by pouring.
- Several skill lines were too short to place with confidence: '1:sort-three-groups' (what is sorted and by what), '3:magic-squares' and '3:input-output' (number range and operations), and the two '1:days-months-seasons' points share one skill line, so the split between them is my reading (week and month words to the first, months and seasons to the second).
- The wording in PART 2 carries extraction faults that should be checked against the SEP document before any of it is shown to teachers: 'Explica con sus palao bras', 'lo que sabe intuye', 'que tiene al o alcance', 'comparar a el peso', 'más pesado ligero', 'en y su hogar', 'desa rrollo', and F2.SPC.4.GP3.4 looks like two processes run together ('... interpreta los registros de sus pares. y Construye y compara colecciones ...', which repeats GP3.3).

## author-lit

- Preescolar (Fase 2) holds no letter-sound, syllable, rhyme-recognition or handwriting process: it stops at the child's own name ('Reconoce las letras de su nombre', 'Usa grafías para representar su nombre y otras palabras conocidas'). Almost every year-K literacy point therefore lands on a 1° de primaria process (capital and small letters, vowel and consonant sounds, spaces and left-to-right, sound value of letters). A Mexican teacher will see these sheets as 1° material or as 'preparación' in 3° de preescolar.
- The word sílaba does not appear in Fases 2-3 (only once, in a 4° poetry process). The four syllable points are placed under 'Establece correspondencias entre la oralidad y la escritura' and the sound-letter processes; teachers work syllables daily in 1°, but the Programa Sintético leaves the method to the school's programa analítico.
- The programme has no word-formation content at all: familias de palabras, prefijos, sufijos and palabras compuestas are not named in any fase. Six points have no row for this reason (1:root-words, 2:root-words, 2:prefixes, 2:compound-words, 3:person-words, 3:root-words-in-context).
- Grammar arrives late and only through use: adjectives, adverbs, verbs (infinitive, imperative) and pronouns in 3°; verb tenses, gender and number agreement, synonyms and antonyms in 4°. The sustantivo and the article are never named. The 1° and 2° grammar points (word classes, pronouns, articles, plurals, verb forms, opposites, synonyms) can only 'prepare' those processes, sometimes two or three years ahead; the year-K points on plurals, articles and opposites have no row.
- Cursive is not in the programme. The only hook is 1°'s 'variaciones de estilo' of a letter; the three cursive points are marked teaches or extends against it. Orden alfabético is placed in 3° (dictionaries and indexes), so the K and 1° alphabet points only prepare it.
- Several skill lines are English and were judged by their Spanish counterpart: digraphs (ch, ll, rr, qu, gu instead of sh/th), spelling rules (c/qu, g/gu/gü, r/rr, z to c, b/v, h instead of ck/tch/-dge), sight words (Spanish schools have no sight-word list; frequent short words are read by syllable), CVC words (Spanish reading starts with open syllables ma-me-mi; closed words such as sol or pan are few), a/an (el/la, un/una), the irregular verbs, and a synonym for 'said' (dijo).
- Content of these years that teachers will look for and that no point covers: writing and comparing one's own name and classmates' names (F3.LEN.1, the starting point of literacy in 1°); letreros, carteles, avisos and anuncios publicitarios (F3.LEN.10); notas informativas (F3.LEN.11); writing instructivos and recetas (F3.LEN.9); recados, cartas and messages to someone far away (F3.LEN.14, F4.LEN.10); poemas, trabalenguas, adivinanzas, onomatopeyas and calambures (F3.LEN.23); canciones and rondas (F3.LEN.22); words of indigenous origin and linguistic diversity (F3.LEN.16, F4.LEN.15).
- Spanish-specific conventions with a process in the programme but no point: opening and closing question and exclamation marks (F3.LEN.4.G2.4, F4.LEN.7.G4.4), capital letter in proper names (F3.LEN.6.G2.4), the comma in a list (F4.LEN.2.G3.4), the written accent on question words (F4.LEN.5.G4.1), homónimas (F4.LEN.8.G4.4). Acentuación and sílaba tónica are not in Fases 3-4 of this document.
- 3° de primaria is very thin on the site's side: four points, of which two have no row. Fase 4 is built on text types (narración, descripción, textos expositivos, resumen, instructivos, cartas, entrevistas, croquis y mapas, textos discontinuos, cuentos, poemas, texto dramático) and none of them has a teaching point.
- Oddities in the registry data: F3.LEN.2.G1.3 and F3.LEN.4.G1.5 carry the same wording, as do F3.LEN.4.G1.1 and F3.LEN.6.G1.2 (the SEP repeats them under two contenidos); F2.LEN.8.GP1.4 has word for word the text of F2.LEN.8.GP2.1, which looks like a row placed in the wrong grade column; the title of F4.LEN.4 has lost its hyphens ('comparacióncontraste, causaconsecuencia'); F3.LEN.1.G2.1 reads 'y de sus de familiares'. Within a contenido the processes are listed with the grades interleaved, which is harmless but hard to read.
- This part holds only the Campo formativo Lenguajes. Classification, odd-one-out and other thinking sheets filed under literacy (K:category-sort, K:category-odd-one-out) belong to Saberes y pensamiento científico in Mexico and should be crossed there.

## author-soc

- Continents and oceans (2:continents, 3:continents-oceans) have no objective in this registry: in Fases 2 to 4 the territory goes from the place where the child lives to the entidad and to México and stops there. A Mexican teacher expects the world map in 5° and 6° (Fase 5), which this registry does not hold.
- The cardinal points are first named in 3° de primaria (F4.ENS.1.G3.1), one year above the grade-2 worksheets; 1° and 2° speak only of 'referencias básicas' on a croquis.
- Road safety (K:road-safety, 1:road-signs, 2:road-signs) is not named anywhere in the programme: no traffic light, no signs, no 'educación vial'. All its rows are 'prepares' on the general accident-prevention processes, and teachers will look for these sheets under prevention of accidents, not under a road-safety objective.
- Several year-K points are placed by the programme in 2° de preescolar, one year below the worksheets: basic needs (F2.ENS.2.GP2.3), healthy and risky foods (F2.HC.7.GP2.1), naming emotions (F2.HC.4.GP2.1) and identifying jobs and services (F2.ENS.5.GP2.1). The 3° de preescolar processes on the same lines ask for more (obtain information, exchange with classmates, value the impact).
- K:wants-needs: the programme speaks only of 'necesidades básicas' (alimentación, vestido, vivienda) and, in primaria, of needs as part of human rights; the idea of 'wants' is not in the wording.
- 1:food-groups: the wording says 'los distintos grupos' without a number. As a teacher (not from PART 2): Mexican schools use the Plato del Bien Comer, not a four-group model, so the Spanish sheets should use its groups.
- The programme never names the family tree; it asks for 'dibujos y gráficos como la línea del tiempo' (F3.ENS.10.G1.1). The family-tree points fit as a graphic of the family history. 2:family-relationships is in practice a vocabulary point and would sit better against a Lenguajes objective.
- K:transport-flying fits only loosely: transport appears once, in 2° de primaria, as something whose changes over time the child investigates (F3.ENS.17.G2.1).
- Content of these years that no teaching point here covers and that teachers will look for: los símbolos patrios (F2.ENS.3.GP3.3, F3.ENS.16.G2.1, F4.ENS.9.G4.1); los derechos de niñas y niños (F2.ENS.6, F3.ENS.5); cuidado de la naturaleza and ecosystems (F2.ENS.1, F3.ENS.2 and 3, F4.ENS.1 to 4); acuerdos de convivencia, conflict and cultura de paz (F2.ENS.8, F3.ENS.11 and 12); prevention for sismos, huracanes and inundaciones, the mochila de emergencia (F2.HC.8, F3.HC.17, F4.ENS.5); límites corporales and self-protection (F3.ENS.8, F4.ENS.10); pueblos originarios before the first European invasion (F4.ENS.8, 3°); the map of the entidad and of México (F4.ENS.6); alimentos procesados y ultraprocesados and hidratación con agua simple (F3.HC.14.G2.2, F4.HC.17).
- Data: F3.HC.19.G2.2 holds two processes joined into one. Its second sentence ('Considera las reacciones emocionales para la toma de decisiones de forma asertiva …') is about emotions and belongs to F3.HC.20 (Los afectos y su influencia en el bienestar), 2° grado, which in the dump has only one 2° process.
- Data: F3.ENS.16.G2.2 ('Representa por medio de dibujos, líneas del tiempo, croquis … comparando tres generaciones: abuelos, padres y actual') sits under the contenido on cultural expressions and national symbols, but its wording belongs to the next contenido, F3.ENS.17 (Historia de la vida cotidiana), most likely as a 1° process. F3.ENS.4.G2.2 repeats F3.ENS.3.G2.1 almost word for word under a contenido about time and chronological order, and F3.ENS.4 has for 1° only one process, which never mentions the 'antes, durante y después' of its own contenido; the 1° column of that contenido looks incomplete.
- Data: exact or near repeats that may be the source's own but are worth a look: F4.ENS.4.G3.1 = F4.ENS.1.G3.4; F4.HC.19.G4.2 = F4.HC.19.G3.1; F4.HC.19.G4.3 nearly = F4.HC.19.G3.2. Small slips in the text: F2.ENS.1.GP1.4 'deterioran la naturaleza las evita' (a comma is missing) and F4.ENS.5.G4.3 'participa acciones colectivas' ('en' is missing).
- Strand names: 'Educación socioemocional' is the name of the 2017 plan; in the 2022 plan emotions are part of the campo De lo Humano y lo Comunitario (contenidos 'Las emociones en la interacción con diversas personas y situaciones', 'Los afectos y su influencia en el bienestar'). Every teacher still uses and understands the old name, so it is kept. The official spelling of the campo is with capitals: 'Ética, Naturaleza y Sociedades'.

## own-audit

- The builder's own reading after the three reviews: the 36 points left without a counterpart and a sample of rows beside the official wording. Three points had their home in the campo of another reviewer (two picture-sorting points in Saberes y Pensamiento Científico, one writing point in Lenguajes) and are mapped there.
- The reviewers found faults in the registry's first reading of the SEP's PDFs, and they were right: a line's last short word (o, y, a) had slipped into the neighbouring column in 15 processes, two pairs of processes were run together, and the text's own hyphens were lost in one title. The reader is repaired and the framework rebuilt; the reviewers' rows were carried over by wording. Their notes about garbled wording describe that first reading.
- What the reviewers reported and the PDF confirms as the SEP document's OWN print (kept as printed): F2.LEN.8 prints 'Interpreta manifestaciones artísticas y culturales …' under both 1° and 2° de preescolar; F3.ENS.16 (símbolos) prints the three-generations process 'Representa por medio de dibujos, líneas del tiempo, croquis …' in its 2° cell although its wording belongs to F3.ENS.17; F3.LEN.1.G2.1 reads 'de sus de familiares'; F4.SPC.16 has a process for 3° only and F4.SPC.17 for 4° only.
