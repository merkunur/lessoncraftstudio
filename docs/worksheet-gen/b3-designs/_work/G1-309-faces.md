## Deviations from §3 (each with the measurement)

1. **F2 bins stretch to the body, the head pinned at the top and the two lanes spread evenly over the rest** (§3: a fixed 374-px stack, "whitespace stays"). Measured at the shipping chrome (body 766): the pinned stack left ~330 px of every bin empty below the lanes and read as unfinished; a fully even spread floated the head mid-column. The G1-305 sort precedent (columns to the body, rulings spread).
2. **F4 lanes side by side, anchor 100/84, lanes 250×64 glyphH 28** (§3: two stacked 250×56 lanes glyphH 26 beside an 88/72 anchor). Measured: the stacked row used 352 of the 639-px inner and left ~250 px of every row blank; the chain shape uses 628 and reads as a rhyme string. K shape 250×80 glyphH 40 fits the 3-row K page with 26 px to spare under the three-line chrome (lowest 866 vs foot 921).
3. **F2 bank is `rhymeBank`, F4 bank is `stringBank`** (§3 names `numberedBank` / `wordBank`): both re-emit the sibling / b2 markup verbatim plus the class / word / role stamps the gates need; the originals cannot be edited.
4. **F3 carries `unitAxis:{applicable:false}`** (§1: the class set is the fan lever on all six). The couplet pool is the authored list (10 in en, floor 8) and a page draws 6 of them: a class-unit fan would emit up to 10 near-identical decks from one page's worth of content. F3 = one coordinate per locale, seed-fanned (19 distinct pages over 20 seeds).
5. **F2 head / F4 anchor may be ANY member; only the WRITTEN words are `sameSpelling:true`** (§4 en: "write faces use sameSpelling:true members only", read as the words the child writes — the base record's deviation 3 reads it the same way). Measured: with heads writable-only 9/34 en units built on sort/string; relaxed 16/34 (-ee heads with `key`, the child writes bee/tree).
6. **F5 anchors are `productive:true` AND writable** (§3 names `productive` only): the anchor is PRINTED and the child spells by analogy with it, so `key` / `thumb` / `eye` never anchor (the gate's FO5 + the node check caught `key` on the first render).
7. **F1 non-pair rule tightened** (§3: "two members of two different classes"): classes differ, sounds differ, neither is the other's near-miss at d2, off the rhyming set, the same class pair never twice, distinct classes where the bank allows. Also "never 4 in a row" is enforced for `no` as well as `yes`, and both columns must hold a cross too.
8. **F1 cards are numbered** (§3 silent; the K base drops the badge to buy lane width — the judge card has no lane; the badge clears the 188-px centred pair by 57 px each side).
9. **F3 text on the base line** (§3 silent): see above; a verify assertion + poison FC6 hold it.
10. **F3 caps**: pre + post ≤ 40 together (§5 rule 9: "≤ 40 before the blank" — a panel may put the blank mid-line); over any cap the couplet is unusable, never shrunk.
11. **`maxLetters` per face**: sort 8 (K 6), couplet 7 (a 170-px lane at 26), string 10 (K 7), open 12 (the printed word ≤ 208 px at Baloo 24 by the 0.6·px estimate), judge unused (no writing).
12. **Judge chip size stamp is `data-lcs-chip-px`** — `data-lcs-chip` is the chips' own stamp (the first gate run counted 17 chips: a selector collision, fixed at the stamp).
