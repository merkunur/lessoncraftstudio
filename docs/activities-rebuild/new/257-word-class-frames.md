## The skill
Sort words into nouns, verbs and adjectives by testing them in a frame: "a ___", "I can ___", "the ___ dog".

## The apparatus and its invention
TEST FRAMES. A sentence-strip machine holds two or three frames, each a short sentence with a gap and a little library picture beside it: "Look at the ___." (picture: an empty stand), "I can ___." (a library friend from animals), "a ___ ball" (a library ball). The child drops a word card into a frame; the frame READS itself (written; TTS bonus). If the word fits, the frame acts: the noun's library picture appears on the stand, the friend does the action (activities picture), the ball changes size or colour. If it does not fit, the strip crumples and the card drops out. The child may test the card in every frame, then drops it into the bin under the frame that worked. Miss (wrong bin): the card flies back to the frame that works and that frame replays. Success: all cards binned, the three bins glow in turn.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Calls every word for something you can see a noun ("run" because there is a picture of running), or treats colours and sizes as nouns.
- **Pictures:** Nouns: any colour theme; verbs: activities (dancing, running, reading, writing, jumping, baking…); adjectives: scale/colour transforms of toys/ball.
- **Settings:** Classes: noun or verb / noun or adjective / all three.
- **Rounds:** 10 cards per pass, classes balanced (40–60 % for two, 25–40 % for three); only words that belong to one class in that locale (no brush/run/play); one right bin.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G1-293, G1-300, G2-275, G2-285, G2-286, G2-287
- **Distinct from:** Glim's Describing Words separates adjectives from adverbs (grade 2); no activity makes the child TEST a word in a frame.
- **Build risk:** de: every noun is capitalised — in de the noun class carries a visible cue the native panel accepts as part of the skill (Nomen groß), and the real challenge is verb vs adjective; fi: frames authored with the right case so the gap takes a base form; native review of every card.

## Merged into this instrument (cover each as a setting or round set)
- grade 2 "The Slot Test — Noun, Verb or Adjective?" (slot-test): Classify words as nouns (naming), verbs (doing) or adjectives (describing). — Three test-frame slots sit above three bins: "I see a ___." · "I can ___." · "It is ___." A word card is dropped into each frame in turn and the frame READS the sentence (text + optional TTS); the frame where it makes sense lights and the card drops into that bin. The child decides which frame to try first and makes the final drop; a word that fits two frames (e.g. "run") is excluded from the pool so there is one answer. Picture help shows a library picture on noun cards.
