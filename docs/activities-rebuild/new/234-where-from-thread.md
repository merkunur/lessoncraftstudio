## The skill
Tell things found in nature from things made by people, and trace a made thing back to its natural material.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE THREAD BACK TO THE SOURCE. The stage has two sides: NATURE (a strip of library sources: tree, sheep, beach rock, sea water, cow) and MADE (a workbench of apparatus geometry). Each thing in the tray trails a loose thread. The child drags a thing to a side. Then its thread pulls tight: a natural thing's thread ends right where it lies (a pinecone, a seashell, a leaf, a rock — it IS nature); a made thing's thread runs across the workbench and back to the natural source it was made from (wooden chair → tree, wool scarf → sheep, glass jar → sand/rock, milk carton? refused). Right: the thread draws in one smooth line. Miss 1: a made thing put on the nature side shows its thread running out across the workbench to its source; a natural thing on the made side shows its thread ending at itself. Miss 2: the side glows. Success: all threads drawn, the strip of sources gives a small bounce.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Thinking made things have nothing to do with nature, and that anything wooden is natural.
- **Pictures:** Natural: miscellaneous (leaf, acorn, pinecone), beach (seashell, rock, water), camping (log), flowers (rose), easter (feather). Made: furniture (chair, table), kitchen-tools (jar, glass, spoon), accessories (scarf), toys (ball, car, robot), at-the-supermarket (can, bag). Sources: tree theme (apple tree), farm-animals (sheep, cow), beach (rock).
- **Settings:** Mode: sort / pull the thread · Things: everyday / tricky (paper, wooden spoon, wool)
- **Rounds:** 9 things per pass, natural and made balanced (4–5 each), ≥ 2 wooden lures (log natural / chair made) per pass in the tricky setting; one right side from a reviewed table (refuse contested items: paper, cotton, honey).
- **Worksheet types it covers:** K-214
- **Distinct from:** No materials activity exists.
- **Build risk:** Plastic has no picturable natural source for K — plastic items are made things whose thread ends at the workbench (no source shown), stated honestly. Code: K-214 natural vs made is closest to NGSS K-2-ETS1/2-PS1-1 (materials); keep 2-PS1-1.
