## The skill
Skip counting by 2s, 5s and 10s is counting equal groups: each peg holds one group and its tag is the running total.

## The apparatus and its invention
A LINE WITH PEGS. A washing line with pegs; each peg holds one equal group in a little net bag: a pair of socks (2s), 3 or 4 identical fruits (3s, 4s), 5 strawberries in a row (5s), 10 crayons as two rows of five (10s). Under each peg hangs an empty tag. The child hangs the next bag (drag from the basket) and drags the running-total numeral onto its tag from a small tray. A tag only takes the total of everything hung so far: a wrong number makes the line sag at that peg and the groups behind it slide together into one heap with its count in ones, so counting the groups (1, 2, 3) or adding 1 after a ten is seen to be wrong. Ticks setting: the bags vanish and the line becomes a number line with pegs 2, 5 or 10 apart, starting at 0, 25 or 50, with only some tags printed; the child pegs the missing ones. Miss 2 (both settings): the step is drawn as a bracket between two neighbouring pegs. Right: the line pulls tight with a little bounce and the finished tags are read as the count-by chant.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Counts the groups (1, 2, 3) instead of the objects, or adds 1 instead of the step after a ten (10, 20, 21).
- **Pictures:** accessories/sock (2s); fruits/strawberry, cherry, plum (3s, 4s, 5s); classroom/crayon (10s) — identical instances per bag; line, pegs and bags drawn as apparatus
- **Settings:** Count by: 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 10 · Line: groups on pegs / number line with ticks (starts 0, 25 or 50; 3s and 4s start at 0)
- **Rounds:** 7+ lines per step: missing tags at random positions (never only the last), starts vary; the tray offers the right total, total ± 1, total ± step and the count-of-groups distractor, reshuffled every tag so the right numeral has no fixed place.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G1-123, G1-124, G1-125, G1-126, G1-278, G1-279, G1-280, G1-281, G1-285, G2-207, G2-208
- **Distinct from:** skipcount.fill hops a frog over numbered pads (5/10/100) with no objects; Posy's Egg Cartons count tens only; the Counting Necklace (#9) counts by ones. This links the count-by to equal groups and to interval ticks.
- **Build risk:** Need a clear identical "group of 2" and "group of 5" picture from the library (socks/gloves exist in clothing; check).

## Merged into this instrument (cover each as a setting or round set)
- grade 2 "The Stepping Stick — Count by 3s and 4s" (stepping-stick): Skip count by 3s and 4s from 0 (and from another start) and name the missing numbers in the sequence. — A long number track (0–50, ten stones a row, drawn apparatus) and a wooden STEPPING STICK exactly 3 (or 4) stones long. The child drags the stick so it starts where the last one ended; it snaps only end-to-end (no gap, no overlap) and the stone under its far end lights. Some landing stones are blank; on a blank one a keypad asks the number. Miss 1: the stick turns translucent so the child can count its stones; miss 2: dots under each stone the stick covers.
