## The skill
Sort vehicles by where they travel — on land, on water, in the air — and tell which things can fly.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE ROAD, THE SEA AND THE SKY. The stage is three bands: sky on top, sea in the middle, road at the bottom. The child drags a vehicle into a band and lets go: in the right band it moves off (the boat sails, the car drives, the plane flies); in the wrong band it cannot — a car on the sea bobs and drifts back, a boat on the road scrapes to a stop, a train in the sky drops gently down to its band. Fly mode: the bands are "can fly" (sky) and "cannot fly" (ground) with animals and things (a bee, a penguin, a kite, a balloon).

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Every bird flies (penguin), anything with wings flies, a seaplane / hovercraft (shown only in tricky rounds as "two lanes").
- **Pictures:** vehicles theme (car, bus, train, boat, ship, plane, helicopter, bicycle, tractor), animals and insects (bird, penguin, bee, butterfly), toys (kite, balloon)
- **Settings:** Mode: land–water–air / can fly or not · Things: everyday / tricky
- **Rounds:** 9 things per pass, bands balanced, ≥ 2 tricky things in the tricky setting; one right band (two-lane vehicles accept either).
- **Worksheet types it covers:** K-206, K-210
