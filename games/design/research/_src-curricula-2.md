# Curricula research, part 2 — Spain · Brazil · Italy · Sweden · Denmark · Norway (ages 5–9)

Scope: brief §4.2 (`GAME-DESIGN-BRIEF.md`). Six systems, mathematics + first-language literacy + science/world-knowledge, ages 5–9.
Method: official curriculum texts fetched and read (BOE consolidated PDFs, BNCC habilidade codes, DM 254/2012 PDF, Skolverket Lgr22 pages + PDF, UVM Fælles Mål PDFs, Udir LK20 pages + PDF), supplemented by official agency pages for school-start rules and national tests. Every claim carries an inline URL. Quotes are verbatim from the source language; my paraphrases are marked "≈".
Date of research: 2026-09-05.

Age → year mapping used throughout (age in the year the child is *mostly* that age; all six start the school year in Aug/Sep, except Brazil which starts in February):

| Age | Spain | Brazil | Italy | Sweden | Denmark | Norway |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Infantil "5 años" (3rd yr of 2º ciclo) | Pré-escola (last yr) | Scuola dell'infanzia (last yr) | Förskola (last yr) | Børnehave (last yr) | Barnehage (last yr) |
| 6 | 1º Primaria | 1º ano EF | Classe prima | **Förskoleklass** | **Børnehaveklasse (0. kl)** | **1. trinn** |
| 7 | 2º Primaria | 2º ano | Classe seconda | Årskurs 1 | 1. klasse | 2. trinn |
| 8 | 3º Primaria | 3º ano | Classe terza | Årskurs 2 | 2. klasse | 3. trinn |
| 9 | 4º Primaria | 4º ano | Classe quarta | Årskurs 3 | 3. klasse | 4. trinn |

The single most important structural fact for game design: **Spain, Brazil, Italy and Norway begin the first formal school year at 6; Sweden and Denmark begin at 6 too, but that first year (förskoleklass / børnehaveklasse) is a pre-school-shaped year, and "year 1" proper is at 7.** So a "grade 1 / year 1" label means age 6 in four systems and age 7 in two. Never key content to a grade label; key it to age and to the concept tables below.

- "Leggere e scrivere i numeri naturali in notazione decimale, avendo consapevolezza della notazione posizionale; confrontarli e ordinarli, anche rappresentandoli sulla retta." (**no explicit range**; INVALSI classe 2 and textbooks work to 100 in seconda, 1000 in terza — practice, not text)
- "Eseguire mentalmente semplici operazioni con i numeri naturali e verbalizzare le procedure di calcolo."
- **"Conoscere con sicurezza le tabelline della moltiplicazione dei numeri fino a 10. Eseguire le operazioni con i numeri naturali con gli algoritmi scritti usuali."** → all tables 1–10 secured by age 8–9; written column algorithms for all four operations.
- **"Leggere, scrivere, confrontare numeri decimali, rappresentarli sulla retta ed eseguire semplici addizioni e sottrazioni, anche con riferimento alle monete o ai risultati di semplici misure."** → decimals via euro/cent by classe terza; **fractions proper are a classe-quinta objective** (p. 51).

**Spazio e figure**
- position words "(sopra/sotto, davanti/dietro, destra/sinistra, dentro/fuori)"; follow/describe a route from verbal description or drawing;
- "Riconoscere, denominare e descrivere figure geometriche." · "Disegnare figure geometriche e costruire modelli materiali anche nello spazio." (no named shape list at terza; names come at quinta)

**Relazioni, dati e previsioni**
- classify numbers/figures/objects "in base a una o più proprietà"; "Leggere e rappresentare relazioni e dati con diagrammi, schemi e tabelle."
- **"Misurare grandezze (lunghezze, tempo, ecc.) utilizzando sia unità arbitrarie sia unità e strumenti convenzionali (metro, orologio, ecc.)."** → clock is an instrument at terza; no granularity stated.

Money: **EUR**, via "monete" in the decimals objective above. Patterns: not a separate objective at terza (appears via "relazioni"). Probability: none until quinta.

- Lettura: "Padroneggiare la lettura strumentale (di decifrazione) sia nella modalità ad alta voce, curandone l'espressione, sia in quella silenziosa." · predict content from title/images; read narrative/descriptive/informative texts and "individuando le informazioni principali".

## 7. Notable divergences (with evidence)

1. **Two different meanings of "the first school year".** SE förskoleklass and DK børnehaveklasse (age 6) are play-based bridge years with light content (letters + sounds, numbers 0–10, shapes) — https://www.skolemaelk.dk/dit-skolebarn/boernehaveklassen/boernehaveklassens-seks-faglige-maal/ ; Lgr22 p. 23. ES/BR/IT/NO start full subject teaching at 6.
2. **Reading method families.** Romance systems are phonic-*syllabic*: BNCC 1º-ano codes segment words into syllables (EF01LP06/08/09); Italy's mainstream *metodo fono-sillabico*; Spanish *método silábico* is common practice. Nordic systems are letter-sound synthetic: NO "combine letter sounds into words", SE "Sambandet mellan ljud och bokstav", DK "bogstav-lydforbindelser" + explicit irregular-word reading because Danish orthography is deep.
3. **Cursive.** Brazil requires print *and* cursive by 2º ano (EF02LP07). Italy 2025 text promotes cursive; practice introduces it in classe prima/seconda. Spain: no rule (practice varies). Sweden/Norway/Denmark: **no script prescribed**, legibility only; NO removed løkkeskrift in 2020.
4. **Multiplication tables.** Italy is the strictest: "tabelline... fino a 10" secured by classe terza (age 8–9). Brazil sequences tables 2,3,4,5 in 2º and adds 10 in 3º. Spain builds tables in 3º–4º. SE/DK/NO: "the four operations" / "methods for multiplication and division" by age 9 with **no table list**; NO reaches division strategies only after 4. trinn.
5. **Fractions.** SE introduces "del av helhet" at age 6 (förskoleklass) and "enkla bråk" by åk 3. DK: "genkende enkle decimaltal og brøker" fase 3. BR: halves/thirds via dobro/metade. ES: proper fractions with denominator ≤12 in 3º–4º. IT: **decimals (via euro) before fractions**; fractions at classe quinta. NO: nothing until 5. trinn.
6. **Money.** Only ES (EUR coins 1–2 € and notes 5–100 € in 1º–2º; cents in 3º–4º), BR (BRL every year 1º–3º) and IT (monete with decimals by classe terza) teach currency at 5–9. **SE, DK, NO do not mention money before age 10–12.** So money games are Romance-market games; a Nordic version must be optional.
7. **Number ceilings.** Explicit only in ES (999 by age 8; 9,999 by 10), BR (100 → 1000 → milhar), DK (trecifrede = 3-digit by age 9). IT, SE, NO state none.
8. **Clock.** BR is the most specific (digital in 2º, analogue+digital with minutes in 3º); ES names "reloj analógico y digital" and minutes only in 3º–4º; NO "clocks and calendars" by year 2; SE/DK "tid" with instruments, no granularity; IT "orologio" as instrument by terza.
9. **Data & probability.** BR has explicit probability vocabulary from 1º ano ("com certeza / talvez / impossível") and surveys/graphs every year; SE and DK have chance + tables/diagrams by age 9; ES pictograms/bar charts by age 8; IT tables/diagrams by terza; **NO has no data aim before year 5**.
10. **Science.** All six put seasons/weather, living things in the local environment, materials-by-property and the senses/body in ages 6–9; explicit **life cycles** appear in SE (åk 1–3), IT (classe terza), BR (EF03CI05), ES (ciclo vital) but not in NO/DK before age 9–10. Water states appear in SE and DK at this age.
11. **Assessment pressure.** Age-7/8 national instruments exist in IT (INVALSI classe 2, incl. reading speed), NO (3. trinn kartleggingsprøve), DK (færdighedstest 2. klasse reading/maths from 2026/27), BR (SAEB 2º sampling), SE (åk 3 nationella prov, to be replaced 2028); ES none before age 9–10.
12. **Curriculum granularity.** Per-year detail: BR (every year, coded). Per-two-years: ES ciclos. Blocks of three: SE åk 1–3, IT classe 1–3, DK efter 2./3. NO is per-year in maths only. Design implication: only BR and NO can be aligned per grade; the others must be aligned per *band*.
13. **Living change.** Italy is mid-transition (2012 → 2025 Indicazioni, phased 2026–2030); Sweden is replacing its åk-3 tests (2028); Denmark's tests changed in 2026/27; Denmark made færdigheds-/vidensmål merely advisory in 2025. Re-check IT and SE annually.

## 9. Common across all six (ages 5–9) — safe "everywhere" content

Concepts named in every one of the six curriculum texts (not just practice), so a game on them is useful in all six markets:

1. Counting and ordering natural numbers; number as quantity and as order (rows 1–2).
2. Positional (base-ten) place value with tens and ones (row 4).
3. Addition and subtraction — facts, mental strategies, then multi-digit (rows 5–7).
4. Skip counting / number sequences and repeating patterns (rows 8, 16).
5. Multiplication and division *as concepts* by age 8–9 (rows 10, 12) — but table mastery differs (row 11).
6. Naming and sorting 2-D shapes; building 3-D shapes; position words (rows 17–18, 20).
7. Measuring length (and mass/capacity/weight) with non-standard then standard units (rows 22–23).
8. Calendar (days, months) and telling time with a clock at least to hours (rows 24–25).
9. Reading simple tables / diagrams — five of six (all but NO, row 28).
10. Letter names and letter–sound correspondence; phonological awareness (rhyme, syllables, initial sounds) — every system, ages 5–7 (rows 31–32).
11. Blending letter sounds into words (decoding) — every system, ages 6–7 (row 33).
12. Reading short texts with comprehension and writing short texts with capital letter + full stop/question mark (rows 35, 37, 39).
13. Spelling of common/regular words (row 36) — all but NO (which has no spelling aim by year 2).
14. Science: seasons/weather; local plants and animals and their adaptations; sorting materials by observable properties; the senses/body (rows 41–42, 45–46).

## 11. Confidence and gaps

**High confidence (official text read in full):** ES RD 157/2022 + RD 95/2022 (BOE consolidated PDFs); IT DM 254/2012 (MIM PDF); SE Lgr22 (Skolverket subject pages + full PDF); NO LK20 MAT01-05, NOR01-07, NAT01-04 (Udir pages + PDF); DK Fælles Mål matematik 2019/2024 PDF (UVM); BR BNCC habilidade codes (reproduced verbatim on tudosaladeaula/atividadesaulas/professoresmestres — these mirror the MEC text; I did not fetch the MEC master PDF).

**Medium confidence:**
- DK dansk after 2. klasse: read from an official table reproduced by skoven-i-skolen.dk and the odder.dk summary; the UVM dansk måloversigt PDF fetched was the *teknologiforståelse* overlay, not the base fagmål. Wording for "lydrette/ikke-lydrette ord" is from a secondary summary.
- DK børnehaveklasse content: retsinformation.dk returned 403; opmærksomhedspunkter quoted via search summary + skolemaelk.dk.
- DK natur/teknologi: extracted from the UVM måloversigt PDF (fragmented layout); goals are correct in substance but I could not reconstruct every fase.
- IT "number range to 100/1000 by year" and NO/SE number ceilings are **practice** (textbooks, INVALSI, kartleggingsprøver), not curriculum text — marked "(p)" in the matrix.
- ES cursive/"letra ligada" and IT script sequence are practice claims from teacher sources, not regulation.

**Gaps / not verified:**
- Brazil: per-year *Língua Portuguesa* comprehension/production codes (EF12LP/EF15LP) only skimmed; Educação Infantil "crianças pequenas" list is complete for the two campos quoted.
- Spain: autonomous-community curricula (Andalucía, Cataluña, Madrid, etc.) add detail and can set number ranges per year; only the state *enseñanzas mínimas* were read. Catalan/Basque/Galician co-official-language literacy not covered.
- Italy: the 2025 Nuove Indicazioni text itself (DM 221/2025) was not read — only press summaries; if the product ships after 2027, read the 2025 matematica/italiano objectives for classe prima–terza.
- Sweden: "Kommentarmaterial till kursplanen i matematik" (which may discuss tables) not read.
- Norway: nynorsk vs bokmål literacy differences ignored; Sami curriculum ignored.
- Denmark: whether the 2025 "vejledende" status of færdigheds-/vidensmål changes classroom practice is unknown.
- No system's *textbook* sequencing was sampled; where the matrix says "(p)", a 30-minute textbook check per market would firm it up.
