## The skill
Wash hands in the right order (wet, soap, scrub, rinse, dry), and know which tool is for which care job.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE GERMS THAT ONLY THE RIGHT ORDER LIFTS. Two library hands (body-parts/hand, one mirrored) rest over a library sink with a library faucet, dusted with glittery germ dots (apparatus geometry). The step tools stand on a shelf: faucet (wet), soap, a scrub control (the hands rub), faucet again (rinse), towel. The child drags tools onto the hands. Each step does its real job only in its place: soap on dry hands will not lather; rinsing before the scrub washes a few germs away but most cling; the towel before rinsing leaves soap smears. Miss 1: the step happens, the germs stay, and the empty step slot before it pulses. Miss 2: the step strip shows the next tool's outline. Success: the last germ fades in a sparkle and the hands shine. CARE-TOOLS mode (buildable now, K-380): a library body part lights (tooth, nose, hand, head); the child picks the library tool (toothbrush, tissue, sponge, comb); a right tool performs a small motion over the part. HABIT-SHIELD mode (G2-379): habit pictures (toothbrush, bed, apple, ball, sunscreen) dragged onto what they protect. GERM-TRAIL mode (G1-404, G1-403): two action pictures per moment; the glitter spreads or vanishes on the next frame.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Skipping the scrub, or thinking a quick rinse cleans.
- **Pictures:** body-parts (hand, tooth, nose, head), around-the-house (faucet, sink, toothbrush, toothpaste, comb, brush, bed), hospital (tissue), kitchen-tools (sponge), fruits (apple), toys (ball), beach (sunscreen). MISSING: soap, towel, elbow (label).
- **Settings:** Mode: wash the hands / which tool · Steps: 4 / 5
- **Rounds:** hand-washing: the step order is fixed, so rounds vary which step is pre-placed and the shelf order (each tool in each shelf slot across a pass); tools mode: 9 rounds, body parts balanced; one right sequence / tool.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G1-403, G1-404, G1-405, G2-379, K-380, K-382
- **Distinct from:** No health activity exists.
- **Build risk:** Blocked on art: soap (labelled), towel; elbow needs an lcs-pictures label (art exists). The care-tools mode alone would cover only K-380. G1-405 (habit week chart) is self-report and stays a worksheet.

## Merged into this instrument (cover each as a setting or round set)
- grade 1 "The Germ Trail" (germ-trail): Healthy habits stop germs and keep teeth clean: wash hands, cough into your elbow, brush before bed. — GLITTER GERMS: a short scene strip (lunch table, playground, bathroom) shows germs as glitter dots on a library friend's hands. The child chooses an action card for each moment (wash hands / wipe on shirt; cough into elbow / into hand); the glitter then spreads or vanishes along the next frames of the strip. Brushing setting: before / during / after cards are ordered on a toothbrush timeline.
- grade 2 "The Body Shield — Why We Do Healthy Habits" (body-shield): Explain why we do healthy habits by matching each habit to what it protects (hands → germs, teeth → cavities, sleep → energy, food → growing, exercise → heart, sun cream → skin). — A child figure built from library body-part pictures stands in the middle; habit cards (library pictures: toothbrush, soap, bed, apple, ball, sunglasses) are SHIELDS. The child drags a shield onto the body part it protects; the right part glows and a short "because" line appears under it. Reason mode: the reason cards arrive and the child links each to its habit.
