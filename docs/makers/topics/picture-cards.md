## Templates from today's worksheet types

**Match a word to the picture it names (picture-word cards, memory pairs)**

- `K-225` Match Word to Picture — Draw a line from each word to the picture it names. (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-225-match-word-picture.js)
- `K-324` Picture Word Cards — Cut along the dotted lines to make the cards. Say the name of each picture, then read its word. · picture themes (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-324-picture-word-cards.js)
- `K-347` Picture Cards and Word Cards: Matching Pairs — Cut out the sixteen cards. Lay the word cards next to the pictures they name, or turn them all face down and play Memory. · picture themes (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-347-picture-word-cards-twin-set.js)

**Choose the article for a noun (en a / an; de der / die / das; fr le / la …)**

- `K-348` A or An: Word Cards with the Article — Cut out the eight cards. Say each word with its article, just as it is written on the card. · picture themes (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-348-picture-word-cards-article-cards.js)

**One and many: singular and plural nouns**

- `K-349` One and Many: Singular and Plural Cards — Cut out the cards. Say the word for one and the word for many, then match each pair. · picture themes (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-349-picture-word-cards-one-and-many.js)

**Bilingual picture word cards for a word wall**

- `K-350` Bilingual Picture Cards: English and {U} — Cut out the eight cards. Say the word in English, then in {U}, and keep the cards for your word wall. · picture themes (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-350-picture-word-cards-bilingual-cards.js)

**Clap and split words into syllables, missing syllable, syllable scramble, 2 or 3 syllables, syllable cards**

- `G1-324` Syllable Cards: Read the Word in Parts — Cut out the eight cards. Read each word one syllable at a time, then say the whole word. · picture themes (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/g1/G1-324-picture-word-cards-syllable-cards.js)
