## The skill
Estimate a sum by rounding each addend, then judge whether an exact answer is reasonable.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE ESTIMATE FLAGS: two cargo numbers (e.g. 48 and 273) each sit on their own number line; the child drags a flag from each to its nearest ten/hundred (the snap is the rounding). The two flags then slide onto one long line and stack end to end, planting an estimate flag (50 + 270 = 320). Then a 'friend's answer' card drops onto the line at its own value — the child taps 'close' or 'far off' by seeing how near it lands to the estimate flag. Miss on a rounding: the halfway mark lights on that line.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Rounding then forgetting to add, rounding only one addend, and accepting an exact answer that is far from any sensible estimate.
- **Pictures:** none (number lines and flags drawn tool-grade)
- **Settings:** Round to: nearest ten / nearest hundred · Ask: estimate only / estimate then judge an answer
- **Rounds:** Generated addend pairs within 1000, ≥ 9 rounds, the friend's answer is reasonable in ~50% of rounds and the unreasonable ones err by place value (a dropped carry) not random; positions of close/far choices fixed but balanced 4/5.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G3-389
- **Distinct from:** Halfway Harbors rounds a single number; the flags combine rounding with an estimated sum and a reasonableness check.
- **Build risk:** Low.
