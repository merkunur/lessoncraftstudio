# Reviewers' notes: registry `de-nw`

Written by the reviewers of the crosswalk (`scripts/curricula/apply-review.js`). 306 teaching points carry a reviewer's row; the reason for each is in `frontend/content/curricula/de-nw/crosswalk-authored.json`.

## author-all

- All year-K points are marked 'prepares': the NRW Lehrpläne begin with Klasse 1, and the year before school is covered by the Bildungsgrundsätze for day-care, not by these plans.
- Zahlenraum: NRW ends Klasse 2 at 100. The grade-2 sheets with three-digit numbers (place value, charts to 1000, comparing, blocks within 1000) are Klasse 3 work in NRW and are mapped as 'extends' the s-expectation and 'prepares' the k-expectation, whose wording goes to 1.000.000. A German Klasse 3 teacher will look for them under Klasse 3.
- Written column methods appear only in the Klasse 4 column (M.1.5). NRW schools start written addition and subtraction in Klasse 3, so 2:column-method sits a year early; written multiplication and division (Klasse 4) are not covered by any point.
- Multiplication and division start earlier in NRW than on the site: by the end of Klasse 2 children know the Kernaufgaben of the Einmaleins and derive the rest (M.1.3.s3) and match sharing and grouping with division tasks (M.1.2.g4). The site's grade-3 points on equal groups, arrays, times tables and sharing will be searched for by Klasse 2 teachers.
- Addition and subtraction to 100 in the head or half-written (M.1.4.s1) is Klasse 2 work in NRW; the site has it in grade 1 (mental-math-to-100, bonds-to-100, open-number-line). In NRW Klasse 1 stays within 20.
- Fractions: the NRW primary plan has no fraction strand. Its only wording is M.3.1.k6, everyday fractions ¼, ½, ¾ with measures. All grade-2 and grade-3 fraction points hang on that one expectation as 'prepares' or 'extends'; number-line, comparing and equivalent fractions are secondary-school content in NRW.
- Measures: weight (g, kg) and volume (ml, l) are named only for classes 3/4, so the grade-2 scale and jug sheets are early. Clock times beyond full, half, quarter and three-quarter hours are also Klasse 3/4. Rounding is named only in the Klasse 4 column. Inches and feet in 2:choose-units have no German use.
- Not named in the content wording, so placed on the nearest expectation or Schwerpunkt: ordinal numbers (M.1.1), odd and even numbers (M.1.1.g1), estimating a number of things (M.1.1), the meaning of the equal sign (M.1.3.s2, M.1.2.g7). These rows are the weakest in the file.
- Mathematics content of classes 1 to 3 that no teaching point covers and NRW teachers will look for: Würfelgebäude and building plans (M.2.3.s2, k2), nets of the cube (M.2.3.k3), chance words sicher / möglich / unmöglich (M.4.2.g2), simple combinatorics (M.4.2.g1), half-written multiplication and division (M.1.4.k1), drawing with ruler and Geodreieck, parallel and senkrecht (M.2.5.k1), Bandornamente and Parkettierungen (M.2.2.k2), enlarging on grid paper (M.2.2.k4), mm / km and money with comma (M.3.1.k5, k7), telling a number story for a given equation (M.3.2.s3).
- Deutsch content no point covers: the spelling strategies Verlängern (Hund / Hunde for d / t, b / p, g / k) and Ableiten (ä / äu from a / au), Großschreibung of nouns, doubled consonants and ie as German rule sheets, Satzglieder (Subjekt, Prädikat), wörtliche Rede, the three sentence types with !, dictionary work with a real Wörterverzeichnis, reading non-continuous Sachtexte (tables), poems, and everything on media (D.3.3.s5 to k10). All of Sprechen und Zuhören (D.1) can only be prepared by sheets.
- English-shaped skill lines: 'digraphs sh / ch / th' is taken as German sch / ch (th does not exist); 'spelling rules tch, dge, ai / ay, -ies' has no German counterpart and is taken as ck / k, tz / z, ie; 'a / an' as der / die / das; 'CVC words' as short sound-true words; 'sight words' as Häufigkeitswörter; 'person words' as backen - Bäcker; 'said' as the Wortfeld sagen; line plots are not a usual German diagram.
- Handwriting: NRW asks for Druckschrift by the end of Klasse 2 and for joined handwriting only by the end of Klasse 4 (D.2.1.k2). Many schools start the joined script in Klasse 2, so the grade-2 cursive sheets fit school practice although the plan's target is later.
- Pronouns and verb tenses are usually Klasse 3/4 in NRW; the plan lists Wortarten across all four years with 'u. a.', so the grade-1 and grade-2 points are mapped there with a note.
- Sachunterricht: the NRW plan does not name weather, the moon, day and night or the planets. Weather, moon phases and day / night are placed under 'Naturphänomene' (SU.3.2.s3), whose only printed example is Magnetismus; 3:planets has no row. Animal life cycles are covered only by the 'u. a.' in SU.3.1.s1.
- Compass directions and continents are Klasse 3/4 in NRW (SU.4.1.k1, k3) and the plan starts from the home area: NRW, Deutschland, Europa, then Welt. Teachers will look for maps of NRW and Germany, which no point covers.
- Sachunterricht content of classes 1 to 3 with no teaching point: Magnetismus, simple circuits and electricity safety, energy sources, fire and burning, air and drinking water, forest as habitat, life then and now and timelines (SU.6), advertising and pocket money, class rules and votes, children's rights, media use and data protection, body boundaries and sex education, building and construction, programming a sequence, the bicycle training.
- Puzzles with no row (16 points): mazes, sudoku, logic grids, analogies, matrix puzzles, spot-the-difference, shadow matching, drawing by steps and the planets. Mathematics process competences (Problemlösen, Argumentieren) and the Kunst plan are not in the list, so these have nothing to attach to.
- Three ids are split copies of another point (1:days-months-seasons~season-wheel, 2:synonyms~doing-stage, 3:two-attribute-sort~logic-cabinet): their skill lines are the same as the original, so they carry the same rows, the season one reduced to Sachunterricht.
