## 2. Concept & creative hook
**Rosa the carnival raccoon runs a gumball wagon whose machine is a "rhyme-EAR" — it doesn't care what the picture-tokens LOOK like or what they ARE, only whether their TAILS sound the same.** Feed it tokens whose names ring alike → it clinks, spins, and a gumball rolls into the big glass globe (the non-numeric abundance vessel).
- **THE MACHINE IS A RHYME-EAR (the pedagogical wedge):** every token, when tapped, SPEAKS its own name (TTS, whole-word) + a soundwave ripple; the child HEARS each name. The machine dispenses only when two names share their END-RIME. Looking is useless (the rhyme is a sound); the child becomes Rosa's ears. **AUDIO-FIRST** — "headphones recommended" (the operator-ruled honest listening game); print is an inert label, never a cue.
- **THE RHYME RINGS BACK (the echo-confirmation, Designer B):** on a correct feed the machine CHIMES the rhyming pair back-to-back (staaar … caaar) so the child hears the end-rime RING — the win is literally the sound matching.
- **THE CONTENT IS THE STANDARD (the 5 constraints, every round):** (1) **cross-category** rhymes (star/car, bee/key, snail/whale — rhyme cuts across visual/semantic groups); (2) a **same-category NON-rhyme decoy** (cat → dog, so category-matching MIS-fires); (3) **novel/non-nursery** pairs (memory can't supply); (4) **≥30% same-sound/different-spelling** (bee/key — print can't carry it); (5) **per-round print-defeat** — different-spelling correct OR a same-spelling-tail non-rhyme decoy (comb→bomb), so a letter-tail matcher MIS-fires on EVERY round (the critic's deepest fix). Plus a **near-miss decoy** (cap for cat — shared onset+nucleus, different coda) on a portion of rounds to sharpen terminal-rime isolation.
- **BOTH VERBS (recognize + produce):** RECOGNIZE = judge/pick/odd-one-out/sort by the ring; PRODUCE = complete Rosa's carnival chant (a sung couplet with the last word missing) / generate from an open field / build a rhyme chain — selection-based cued production (graded ASR is OUT), CAPPED with an **ungraded oral invitation** every round ("Can you say another word that rhymes with star? Say it out loud!").
- **THE PAYOFF:** a correct ring → gumballs fill the glass globe (abundance, never a count); when full, Rosa cranks the handle and a cascade pours out for the fair (the celebration). `ctx.correct()`. A wrong feed → no fail; the token floats back and Rosa **replays the two tails** ("staaar … sock … not the same tail — listen again"); the wrong tile becomes non-confirmable that round (kills cycle-elimination) but stays hearable.
- **Anti-generic statement:** NOT match-the-rhyming-pictures — (a) the machine is a SOUND judge (audio-first; pictures are referents, print is inert); (b) the 5 content constraints make end-rime attention the ONLY winning path (cross-category + same-category-non-rhyme-decoy + novel + ≥30%-different-spelling + per-round-print-defeat); (c) the **per-round print-matcher gate assert** kills the cheat the cohort's deck-level gates miss; (d) BOTH verbs (the chant-completion + open-field generate carry produce); (e) the gate proves visual/semantic-match + familiar-pair + print/spelling-match + first/random solvers ALL fail (on EVERY round) while the rimeKey oracle passes.

## 3. Core loop & the ≥7 experiences (RECOGNIZE + PRODUCE; distinctness in the INTERACTION SHAPE)
**Loop (HEAR → judge/produce the rime → feed):** Rosa sets a target (picture + speaks its name) → the child taps tokens to HEAR them (tap = hear, re-hear is free) → feeds/selects the rhyming token with a DELIBERATE commit → a correct feed → the pair chimes + a gumball drops + `ctx.correct()`; a wrong feed → Rosa replays the two tails (guided re-listen), the wrong tile non-confirmable that round → re-try. **No timer/score/streak/countdown/fail/attempts/"X of Y".** `nextTask` reshuffle (order + distractor SET).

**The ≥7 distinct experiences (4 RECOGNIZE + 3 PRODUCE; distinctness in the child's INTERACTION SHAPE, NOT word-pair-swap padding — F4: ≥7 shapes × ≥7-deep pools each):**
| # | Experience (`cog`) | Verb | The child's act | Why genuinely distinct |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ★ | **judge-the-pair** (`judge`) | RECOGNIZE | hear TWO names; decide ring / not-ring (yes/no) | the atomic binary rhyme judgment on one pair |
| 2 ★ | **pick-the-rhyme** (`pick`) | RECOGNIZE | hear a target; from 3-4 cross-category tokens pick the one whose tail matches | selection among competitors; the same-category non-rhyme decoy lives here |
| 3 ★ | **odd-one-out** (`odd`) | RECOGNIZE | 3-4 tokens mostly ring; eject the ONE that doesn't | inverted logic — hold a rime-family + detect the violator |
| 4 ★ | **sort-rhyme-families** (`sort`) | RECOGNIZE | route a pile into 2-3 machines, each a different rime family (anchored by a HEARD word, not a spelled `-at`) | multi-bin classification by sound (two-tap place, NOT drag) |
| 5 ★ | **complete-the-chant** (`chant`) | PRODUCE | Rosa hums a carnival couplet with the last word missing; supply the rhyme that completes it | cued production scaffolded by prosody (classic K rhyme cloze); near-miss decoy belongs here |
| 6 ★ | **generate-from-open-field** (`field`) | PRODUCE | a large scattered field (6-9 tokens); FIND/supply any (or all) that rhyme with the target | generative scan-and-supply — the production spine, not verify-a-pair |
| 7 ★ | **build-a-rhyme-chain** (`chain`) | PRODUCE | start with a word; add a rhyming token, then extend (sun→bun→run…) — a moving target each step | iterative production; working-memory of the running rime |
- **Honest framing (pedagogy + critic F1/F4):** counted by the child's INTERACTION SHAPE (judge a pair / pick 1-of-N / find the non-member / sort N into families / complete a couplet / scan-and-supply from an open field / extend a chain) — 7 genuinely different acts, balanced **4 RECOGNIZE + 3 PRODUCE** (covering BOTH verbs), with ZERO word-pair-swap padding. "Produce" is honestly cued-SELECTION (graded ASR is OUT) PLUS an **ungraded oral invitation** every round (the only place real oral production lives). The gate (`audit-game-variety.js`) confirms ≥7 live shapes × ≥7-deep pools each (the 7×7 intersection, not 7 thin shapes). §A.13.60 reshuffle (order + distractor set).
- **Free slice = pick-the-rhyme + complete-the-chant + generate-from-open-field** — proves BOTH verbs, the audio-first sound judgment, AND the 5 content constraints; the visual/semantic + familiar-pair + print-match + first/random solvers all fail on every round.

## 6. Interaction & UX
- **Runtime: HEAR → judge/produce → feed (deliberate commit).** `rhyme-shop-core.js` holds the round `{ id, cog, type, target:{word,picture,rimeKey,category,spelling}, choices:[{word,picture,rimeKey,category,spelling}], coupletLine? }` + `oracle(round)` (DERIVES the answer set as `choices.filter(c=>c.rimeKey===target.rimeKey)`; **no `isCorrect` boolean exists** — a hand-authored flag is IGNORED). **The answer = `f(hear + match the rimeKey)`, never `f(picture)` / `f(category)` / `f(memory)` / `f(letter-tail)` / `f(printed-rime-highlight)`.**
- **State separation (anti-cheat):** `snapshot()` exposes `{ cog, type, target (picture+word, no rimeKey), choices (picture+word, no rimeKey), coupletLine }`. `facts()` exposes per-round `{ answerDerivedFromRimeKey, correctIsCrossCategory, sameCategoryNonRhymeDecoyPresent, notFamiliarPair, atLeast30pctDifferentSpellingDeck, printMatchFailsThisRound (===true EVERY round), nearMissDecoyPresentSomeRounds, categoryIsNonPredictive, perfectRhymesOnly, multiplePlausibleChoices, correctNotIndex0, dialectPinnedGenAm, voiceRimeAudited }`.
- **Audio + deliberate commit (no Check button):** **NO audio-on-load** (the inventory-404/startle hazard); a target 🔊 + every tile is **tap-to-hear**. Deliberate hear-before-commit: first tap = SPEAK + select-pending (`aria-selected`, no grading); a confirm = commit (the only feedback is the warm ring or the guided re-listen — NOT a retry-checker; this is the Games-38/41/46 deliberate-commit, not a forbidden Check button). A wrong commit → guided re-listen (replay target + wrong tail) + the wrong tile non-confirmable that round (F3). TTS speaks WHOLE WORDS only (isolated rimes mis-speak).
- **Mobile @280px (F4/§A.13.55):** `[ target picture + 🔊 ] [ plain word label, no rime highlight ] [ choices = auto-fit grid minmax(120px,1fr) → 2-col@280 ] [ gumball globe ]`; ≥44px tiles + speakers; `overflow-x:hidden`, widths in vw/%/fr (NOT iframe-relative vmin, §A.13.47). complete-the-chant: couplet ≤8 words wrapping above the grid. sort-families: stacked family rows + a pile strip, **two-tap place (NOT drag)**, 2 bins max @280 (3+ only at tablet). `audit-game-mobile.js` empty/mid/answered at 280-768.

- **Round descriptor (the rimeKey + the 5-constraint structure; answer DERIVED from rimeKey, NEVER stored):** `{ id, cog, type, target:{word,picture,rimeKey,category,spelling}, choices:[…], coupletLine? }`. `makeRounds`: every recognition round satisfies the 5 constraints — cross-category correct + a same-category non-rhyme decoy + novel pairs + (deck) ≥30% different-spelling + (EVERY round) a per-round print-defeat (different-spelling correct OR a same-spelling-tail non-rhyme decoy); a near-miss decoy on a portion; perfect-rhymes-only; ≥3 choices, correct ∉ index-0; General American pinned, dialect-variable rimes quarantined; ≥7 cognitions × ≥7-deep pools; reshuffle order + distractor set.
- **`verify-rhyme-shop-core.js` (MEASURED — drives the REAL core under the window shim, the `verify-array-core` idiom; HALTS build) — the critic-armed solver set:**
  1. **RIMEKEY ORACLE PASSES 100%:** picks the choice(s) whose `rimeKey === target.rimeKey` → correct on every round.
  2. **VISUAL/SEMANTIC-MATCH solver FAILS:** argmax category-similarity to the target → MIS-fires on the same-category non-rhyme decoy; assert `CORRECT_IS_CROSS_CATEGORY` + `SAME_CATEGORY_NONRHYME_DECOY_PRESENT` + `CATEGORY_IS_NON_PREDICTIVE` (mutual info between category-relationship and correctness ≈ 0 — F6).
  3. **FAMILIAR-PAIR solver FAILS:** a nursery-pair table → scores ≤ chance on the novel/cross-category pairs; assert `NOT_FAMILIAR_PAIR`.
  4. **PRINT/SPELLING-MATCH solver FAILS — PER ROUND (the critic's DEEPEST fix):** argmax letter-tail-overlap → assert **`PRINT_MATCH_FAILS_THIS_ROUND` on 100% of rounds** (every round has a different-spelling correct OR a same-spelling-tail non-rhyme decoy), NOT a deck-level ratio; plus `RIME_NOT_SPELLING_DERIVABLE` (≥30% same-sound/different-spelling deck-wide). **The gate now measures the cheat at the granularity the child plays — per-round, not per-deck (the cohort blind-spot closed).**
  5. **FIRST/RANDOM solver FAILS:** ≥3 choices, correct ∉ index-0, distribution spread.
