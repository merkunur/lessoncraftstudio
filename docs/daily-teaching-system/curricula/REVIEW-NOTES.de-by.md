# Reviewers' notes: registry `de-by`

Written by the reviewers of the crosswalk (`scripts/curricula/apply-review.js`). 306 teaching points carry a reviewer's row; the reason for each is in `frontend/content/curricula/de-by/crosswalk-authored.json`.

## author-math

- Year K is the Vorschuljahr, so K points are marked 'prepares' for M1/2 expectations. Two exceptions are marked 'teaches' because their range is plain Jahrgangsstufe 1 work in Bavaria: K:fact-fluency-colour-code (within 20) and K:number-bonds-20. For a Bavarian teacher these two sets, and also K:teen-numbers and K:number-bonds-k, are first-year school material rather than pre-school.
- Bavaria ends Jahrgangsstufe 2 at 100. Every grade-2 point with three-digit numbers (2:read-build-3-digit, 2:expanded-form-digit-value, 2:chart-within-1000, 2:compare-order-3-digit, 2:hundred-more-less, 2:add-with-blocks, 2:subtract-with-blocks) belongs to M3/4, in practice Jahrgangsstufe 3. I marked those that do the Jahrgangsstufe 3 work itself as 'teaches' (a part: range to 1000 of a band that runs to the million) and the block models as 'prepares'.
- Written column addition and subtraction (2:column-method, 3:column-regrouping) is M3/4.1.2.6 only; there are no written methods in Jahrgangsstufen 1/2. The plan prescribes the 'Abziehverfahren' for subtraction, so the German sheets must show taking away with regrouping in the Bavarian notation.
- Mass and capacity (2:mass-grams, 2:read-jugs, half of 2:choose-units, K:heavy-light) are M3/4 in Bavaria. Jahrgangsstufen 1/2 know only money, length and time as quantities.
- Several grade-3 points meet M1/2 expectations, because Bavaria places the content in Jahrgangsstufe 2: the meaning of multiplication and division (3:mult-equal-groups, 3:mult-number-line, 3:division-sharing, 3:division-repeated-subtraction), the Tauschaufgabe (3:commutative), the clock to the minute and 24-hour times (3:time-to-minute, 3:time-12-24), area with unit squares and comparing perimeters (3:area-model, 3:same-area, 3:perimeter, 3:same-perimeter) and 'Quadrate als besondere Rechtecke' (3:classify-quadrilaterals).
- Division with a remainder is already worded in M1/2.1.2.1 ('Division – auch mit Rest – als Aufteilen oder Verteilen'); M3/4 names the remainder again only in the written method. 3:division-remainder is therefore 'teaches' for M1/2.1.2.1 and 'prepares' for M3/4.1.2.6.
- Fractions are almost absent from the Bavarian Grundschule: the only expectation is M3/4.3.2.3 (half, quarter, three quarters used with quantities, such as half a litre or a quarter of an hour; the fraction signs are missing in the served wording). The nine fraction points are tied to it as 'prepares' or 'extends'; teachers of Jahrgangsstufen 1 to 3 will not look for unit fractions, fraction number lines, comparing or equivalent fractions.
- Rounding is not named anywhere in the plan; it appears only through 'Überschlag' (M3/4.1.2.7). 2:round-to-ten and 3:rounding are tied to that and to orientation on the number line.
- Calendar knowledge (K:days-of-week, 1:days-months-seasons and its season-wheel part) and the thermometer belong to Heimat- und Sachunterricht in Bavaria. Only 2:calendar, which reads dates and time spans from a calendar, is mathematics (M1/2.3.1.2, M1/2.4.1.2).
- The German sheets must use Bavarian units and forms for the rows to hold: euro and cent coins (1:coins, 2:shopping-money), centimetres and metres instead of inches and feet (2:measure-with-ruler, 2:choose-units, 3:line-plot-halves), 15 Uhr instead of 3 p.m. (3:time-12-24). The line plot (2:line-plots, 3:line-plot-halves) and the bar model (1:bar-model) are not usual forms in Bavarian books.
- Bavarian content of Jahrgangsstufen 1 and 2 that no teaching point covers: chance ('sicher, möglich, unmöglich', simple Zufallsexperimente, M1/2.4.2), simple combinatorics (2 T-Shirts and 3 Hosen, M1/2.1.3.6), describing and following routes and views from different sides (M1/2.2.1.2, M1/2.2.1.3), changing money and showing an amount in different ways, mixed notation such as 9 € 30 ct or 2 m 15 cm (M1/2.3.2.3, M1/2.3.1.4), estimating lengths with reference sizes (M1/2.3.2.1), checking whether an answer with quantities is plausible (M1/2.3.3.3), and cuboid and cube compared (M1/2.2.2.6).
- Bavarian content of Jahrgangsstufe 3 that no teaching point covers: mental and half-written calculation to 1000 with transfer of the tables (60 ∙ 4, 120 + 30; M3/4.1.2.2, M3/4.1.2.5), nets of cubes and cuboids (M3/4.2.2.3, M3/4.2.2.4), plans, sketches and scale, enlarging and reducing on a grid (M3/4.2.1.1, M3/4.2.1.2, M3/4.2.3.1), tilings and band ornaments (M3/4.2.4), drawing with ruler, Geodreieck and compasses (M3/4.2.2.5), kilometres, millimetres, seconds, converting units and comma notation (M3/4.3.1.1 to M3/4.3.1.3), timetables and price lists (M3/4.4.1.2, M3/4.4.1.4), combinatorics with tree diagrams (M3/4.1.3.7), chances at the wheel or dice (M3/4.4.2) and Fermi tasks.
- Skill lines too vague to place exactly: 1:sort-three-groups (what is sorted), 1:number-walls and 3:magic-squares (no number range), 2:skip-count-5-10-100 (how far the counting goes), 3:perimeter (grid units or given side lengths; marked 'extends', read as 'teaches' if the sheets only count grid units), 2:shopping-money (prices with or without comma).
- Logic grids (1:logic-picture-clues, 2:logic-grid, 3:logic-grids) are tied to the problem-solving expectations (M1/2.1.3.4, M3/4.1.3.5) because ruling out from clues with a table is a strategy the wording covers; this is a judgement call. Sudoku, mazes, picture analogies and find-the-differences have no rows: the plan names nothing like them.
- Process skills (Argumentieren, Kommunizieren: 'begründen', 'im Austausch mit anderen', 'Rechenkonferenzen') run through many expectations. A worksheet can carry the content part only; where a row says 'teaches', the talking and explaining the wording asks still has to happen in the lesson.

## author-rest

- The Deutsch list for Jahrgangsstufen 1/2 has no expectation that names single letters, letter-sound matching, the first sound or rhyme. All the pre-school letter and sound points therefore hang on the nearest reading, handwriting or 'lauttreue Wörter' line as 'prepares'; rhyme could only be tied to the part 'Phonologisches und silbisches Prinzip nutzen' as a whole.
- Five points have no row: the two category points (K:category-sort, K:category-odd-one-out; category words are named nowhere in Deutsch or HSU), K:how-to-draw and 1:how-to-draw (Kunst), and 3:planets. 2:drawing has a row only for its writing half.
- Bavaria has no astronomy in HSU. 1:sun-earth-moon, 2:moon-phases and 2:day-and-night are tied only as 'extends' to the line on natural rhythms and the time spans day, month and year; a Bavarian teacher will not look for them in the plan.
- Language content that Bavaria places in 3/4 although the worksheets sit in years 1 or 2: pronouns as a word class, past tense forms, recognising adjectives as a class, the joining letter in compounds (Fugenelement), dividing words at the line end, sentence kinds with ? and !, and the question test for sentence parts. These rows are 'prepares'.
- HSU content that Bavaria places in 3/4 although the worksheets sit in years 1 or 2: animal development (life cycles), compass directions, map key and map reading, continents, buoyancy (Auftrieb). These rows are 'prepares'. The water cycle and the fair test at Jahrgangsstufe 3 sit in the right band.
- Bavarian HSU names its habitats: meadow or hedge in 1/2, forest and water in 3/4, always native species ('heimische Tier- und Pflanzenarten'). Animal sheets built on farm, zoo, desert or polar animals teach the idea but not the species a Bavarian class must know; sheets on Wiese, Hecke, Wald and Gewässer animals and plants would fit the plan much better.
- Joined handwriting: the plan names only the 'Vereinfachte Ausgangsschrift' or the 'Schulausgangsschrift', and places learning the joined script in 1/2. Cursive sheets in any other script (for example Lateinische Ausgangsschrift) do not serve a Bavarian class, and the Jahrgangsstufe 3 tracing sheets are a year later than the plan expects.
- Points whose skill line is English and was placed by its German counterpart: 2:spelling-rules (German: ck / k, tz / z, doubled consonants, ie; tch, -dge, ai / ay have none), the digraph points (German: sch, ch, st / sp, ei, au, eu; th has none), K:cvc-build (short words spelled as they sound), K:articles and 1:articles (der / die / das), 1:food-groups (German classes use the Ernährungspyramide or Ernährungskreis, not four groups).
- Deutsch content of Jahrgangsstufen 1 to 3 that no teaching point covers and teachers will look for: words with ie, doubled consonants, final hardening and Umlaut derivation (Hund – Hunde, Haus – Häuser), unstressed endings -e / -el / -er / -en, St / Sp and Ei, the Grundwortschatz, copying texts without mistakes and checking against the model, looking words up in a word list, capital letters for nouns, adjective comparison, direct speech and sentence parts (3/4), text kinds (poem, fairy tale, factual text) and reading diaries.
- HSU content of Jahrgangsstufen 1 to 3 that no teaching point covers: time (daily routine, calendar, months, time line, festivals in the year, the child's own life story and its sources), ripening times of native fruit and vegetables, food chains in the meadow or hedge, care of pets, poisonous plants, air and its effects, fire and fire safety, media in everyday life, buying and selling with pocket money, class rules and solving conflicts, plans of the classroom and the way to school, tools and how they work, building towers and rolling vehicles; in Jahrgangsstufe 3 also electricity and circuits, the eye, first aid, forest and water habitats, the municipality and democratic decisions, children's rights, local history and bridges.
- Many HSU expectations ask children to examine, measure or observe for real ('untersuchen', 'beobachten', 'dokumentieren'). For weather, materials and sink-or-float I gave 'teaches' to the content line the worksheet covers and only 'prepares' to the hands-on expectation.
- Family points: the Bavarian wording stresses 'Achtung und Respekt vor unterschiedlichen Lebensbedingungen und Familienformen'. A family tree with one fixed shape (mother, father, grandparents) can sit badly with that; the 'my own family tree' face fits best.
- 2:synonyms and 2:synonyms~doing-stage carry the same skill line and received the same row.
- Skill lines too vague to place exactly: 2:healthy-habits-why (which habits; sleep and exercise would add HSU1/2.2.2.3), K:natural-made (objects or places), 2:mystery-animal and 2:animal-fact-file (which animals; native ones would add the HSU animal content).
