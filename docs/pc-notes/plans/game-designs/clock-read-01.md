## 2. Concept & creative hook
**Owl's Cuckoo Cottage** — a cozy cottage cross-section (kitchen, window with a sky, bed, garden) with a big wall clock + a cuckoo door. Owl's whole day unfolds across a session — dawn tea → breakfast → lunch → garden → lamp-light → bedtime — and the child is the cottage's **timekeeper-and-time-reader**. The cuckoo is the star: it bursts out and ACTS OUT each moment.
- **THE READ SPINE (the premium-distinct cell E10 can't hold — the critic's rescue):** most rounds show a clock **already set** to a time, and the cuckoo asks *"What's Owl doing now?"* → the child READS the analog face (interpreting the coupled hour-hand position) and taps the matching event (breakfast / lunch / bedtime). This is the everyday "look at the clock and tell the time" act — receptive analog reading, the inverse of set, the skill the free E10 omits. **The coupling interpretation is load-bearing: reading half-past requires "the hour hand is PAST 3 but not yet 4 → half past 3" (defeating the "is it 3:30 or 4:30?" misconception).**
- **THE SET ON-RAMP (honest 1.MD.B.3, reuses E10):** some rounds name a time in WORDS ("set the clock to half past seven so breakfast begins") → the child radial-drags the coupled hands (reuse E10's atan2/snap/coupled render) → commits.
- **THE COTTAGE IS THE ENGAGEMENT WRAPPER, NOT A COGNITION (the critic's demotion — honest):** the day-walk + the cuckoo acting out events is the motivating frame; it is NOT a claimed "sequence cognition" (the game sequences the rounds for the child — render-order, not cognition-order). The premium value is the READ act, themed as Owl's day.
- **THE COTTAGE IS FROZEN DURING THE SOLVE (the critic's 1b fix — no proximity cheat):** during a read or a drag, the cottage state (sky/lamp/Owl) does NOT react — there is NO continuous perceptual signal to chase (the G28 cheat killed). The cottage advances ONLY after a CORRECT commit (the cuckoo bursts out + acts out the event). The only live feedback during a SET is the digital readout mirroring the child's CURRENT hands (a tell-time mirror, not a target).
- **NO DIGITAL TARGET, EVER (reuse E10's anti-cheat):** SET prompts are named-WORDS + audio; READ choices are events (not a digital-time copy-cue). The only number on screen is the dial numerals + the child's own current-state readout. A copy-the-readout solver has nothing to copy.
