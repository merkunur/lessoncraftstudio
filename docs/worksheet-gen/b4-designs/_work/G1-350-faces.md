## Deviations from §3 (each with the measurement)
1. **F1 letter boxes are 44 px, not the design's 26; 7 rows, not 8; maxLetters 11, not 12.** The face brief's G1 element floor is 44 (the K-354 G1-356 precedent ships 44) and a 26 px box is not a box a 6-year-old writes a letter in.
