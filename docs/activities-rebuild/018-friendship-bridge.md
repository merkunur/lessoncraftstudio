## The skill
K.CC.C.6 by COUNTING: which of two groups has more, fewer, or the same — and "the same" made by hand.

## The apparatus and its invention: THE BRIDGE
- Two plates of one library toy (ball, car, boat, robot, truck, rocket, kite, dinosaur, train — contact sheet: crayons/blocks/cards/dominoes are sets, the dolls and the baby are people), a plank bridge between them. Plates hold 12, so their size never tells; toys scattered by default.
- Step 1: the plate itself is the answer ("this one has more"), or "The same" on the bridge. A wrong judgment shakes, never a miss.
- Step 2: a tap on a toy sends it walking across the bridge (either way) until both plates hold the same; Check. Totals are always even, so fair is always possible. Seven and three is two toys across: the child counts both plates again and again (#17 owns matching).
- Check: before judging → hint; not the same → miss 1: the plate with more named, both plates numbered (until a move); miss 2: one toy on the fuller plate glows. Done → "Fair! 5 and 5.", both numbered.
