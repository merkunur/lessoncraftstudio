## The skill
Decide whether a line is a line of symmetry, complete a half-painted grid picture so both halves match (vertical and horizontal lines), and count how many mirror lines a shape has.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE WET-PAINT PRESS. A square-grid sheet with a crease line; one half is painted in WET paint squares. COMPLETE mode (G2-253, G2-264–268): the child paints squares on the blank half, then pulls the press lever — the sheet folds along the crease and presses the wet half onto the other side like a butterfly blot. Right: every painted square lands on a painted square and the halves lock with a shine. Miss 1: a wrong square shows as a smudge, a missing one as a pale gap, and the sheet unfolds with those squares marked. Miss 2: the mirrored partner of the first wrong square glows. IS-IT-A-MIRROR-LINE mode (G2-247): shapes (shapes theme) and grid pictures carry a dashed line; the child predicts tick or cross, then presses — overhangs show in a second shade if it is not a mirror line. HOW-MANY-LINES mode (G3-342): the child drags a fold line through a shape (it snaps through the centre at 8 angles) and presses; a true mirror line is kept as a crease; when the child taps the count, a not-yet-found line that works is shown on a miss.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Copying the half picture without flipping it (translating instead of reflecting), counting squares from the wrong edge, and thinking every shape has exactly one (or a diagonal) mirror line (a rectangle has two, not four).
- **Pictures:** shapes theme (heart, star, square, rectangle, triangle, hexagon, circle) for line and count modes; grid pictures are painted squares (apparatus); no other art.
- **Settings:** Mode: complete the picture / is it a mirror line / how many lines · Line: vertical / horizontal · Grid: 6×6 / 8×8
- **Rounds:** 9 half-pictures per grid size (asymmetric halves, 6–14 painted squares, never symmetric about the crease already); line rounds: tick and cross 40–60 %; count rounds cover 0, 1, 2, 4 and many (circle) lines; one right answer.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G2-247, G2-248, G2-253, G2-264, G2-265, G2-266, G2-267, G2-268, G3-342
- **Distinct from:** Split out of fold-mirror (K reflection of pictures). The folding-sheet TOOL lets a teacher fold freely with no task; here a given half must be completed or a given line judged, each with one right answer.
- **Build risk:** Low. Grid cells ≥ 34 px at 320 (8×8 stacked); the 8-angle snap for the count mode must hit true mirror lines exactly for regular shapes.
- **Grade span (settings):** 2 (mirror-line count: 3)
