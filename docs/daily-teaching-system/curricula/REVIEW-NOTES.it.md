# Reviewers' notes: registry `it`

Written by the reviewers of the crosswalk (`scripts/curricula/apply-review.js`). 306 teaching points carry a reviewer's row; the reason for each is in `frontend/content/curricula/it/crosswalk-authored.json`.

## author-all

- The Indicazioni 2012 state objectives only for the end of classe terza and of classe quinta, so nothing here places a worksheet in a single year; every year judgement in the 'why' lines is mine as a teacher. From 2026/27 classe prima already follows the new text (D.M. 221/2025), so the grade-1 rows rest on a text that is being replaced.
- Fractions: the classe-terza objectives do not name them (only MAT.5.5 and MAT.5.6), so all 9 fraction points are 'prepares', although Italian schools start fractions in classe terza. The same holds for perimeter, area, angles, symmetry and the grid/cartesian plane: all are classe-quinta objectives.
- A classe-terza objective no teaching point covers: MAT.3.5, decimal numbers 'con riferimento alle monete o ai risultati di semplici misure' (euro and cents written 2,50 €; cm and mm). Italian teachers of classe terza will look for it. The shopping and coin points would teach it if prices were written as decimals.
- Problem solving has no objective in this registry (it sits in the traguardi per lo sviluppo delle competenze, which PART 2 does not hold), so every word-problem point is tied only to the calculation objective MAT.3.3 / MAT.3.4. The same is true of the scientific method (3:investigation).
- Grammar: 'Riconoscere ... le parti del discorso' exists only as ITA.5.36 (classe quinta), so articles, nouns/verbs/adjectives, pronouns, verb forms and singular/plural are all 'prepares', while Italian schools teach nome, articolo, aggettivo and verbo in classe seconda and terza.
- Italian orthography of classi 1-3 that no teaching point clearly covers and teachers will look for: le doppie, l'accento, l'apostrofo, l'uso dell'h (ha/a, hanno/anno), è/e, c'è/ci sono, cu/qu/cqu, gn/gl/sc, mb/mp, la divisione in sillabe. The points 2:spelling-rules, 1:digraphs, 2:digraphs and K:digraphs-k are described with English patterns (ck, tch, sh, th) and need these Italian contents to be true for Italy.
- Points whose skill line is English in a way that changes the content in Italian: K:cvc-build (Italian has almost no CVC words), K:sight-words-reading (not an Italian method), K:articles and 1:articles (il/lo/la, un/uno/una, with l' and gli), 1:pronouns (lui/lei/loro), 2:verb-forms (passato prossimo and imperfetto, not one past tense), 2:compound-words (no joining letter), 2:choose-units (no inches or feet), 1:food-groups (Italy teaches five groups or the piramide alimentare), 3:continents-oceans (Italian books usually count six continents, America as one, and three or five oceans).
- Calendar, clock, days, months and seasons are storia in Italy (STO.3.4, STO.3.5) as well as measurement (MAT.3.14); the site files them under math. Corsivo is taught in classe prima, not seconda; the 24-hour clock is the normal written form.
- Road safety (3 points), wants and needs, community helpers, feelings and most of the health points have no home in PART 2: in Italy they belong to educazione civica (law 92/2019) and to educazione fisica (nucleo 'Salute e benessere, prevenzione e sicurezza'), neither of which is in the registry. Musica, lingua inglese and educazione fisica are also absent, as are the campi di esperienza of the scuola dell'infanzia, which is why every year-K point can only 'prepare'.
- Science: the Italian objectives describe ways of working (osservare, sperimentare, descrivere) and name few contents. Parts of the body, parts of the plant and of the flower, animal classes and the planets are matched only as 'prepares'; the solar system and animal/plant classification are classe-quinta matter.
- Content of classi 1-3 that teachers plan for and no teaching point covers: MAT.3.8 (percorsi: following and describing a route on a grid with arrows), MAT.3.6, the linea del tempo and sources of the past (STO.3.1, STO.3.2, STO.3.7), the landscapes of geografia (GEO.3.4, GEO.3.5: montagna, collina, pianura, mare; elementi fisici e antropici), the plan of the classroom (GEO.3.2), dictation (ITA.3.14) and subject/predicate in the sentence (ITA.3.22).
- Two oddities in PART 2 are in the official text itself, not reading errors: MAT.5.16 lists 'parallelismo' twice and GEO.5.5 begins 'Localizza' instead of 'Localizzare'. I found no objective missing from the seven disciplines listed.
