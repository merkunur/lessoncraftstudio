## Templates from today's worksheet types

**Recognise feelings in faces, match face to feeling word, comfortable vs uncomfortable feelings, name how I feel**

- `K-319` Feelings: Match the Face to the Word — Draw a line from each face to the feeling word that says how it feels. · screen version (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-319-feelings.js)
- `K-331` How Do You Feel? Circle the Face — Look at the picture, think how you would feel, and circle the face that matches. · screen version (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-331-feelings-how-do-you-feel-circle-the-face.js)
- `K-332` Draw the Feeling Face — Read the feeling word and draw a face that shows it in the empty circle. (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-332-feelings-draw-the-feeling-face.js)
- `K-333` Feels Good or Feels Bad? Sort the Faces — Draw a line from each face to the box that says whether the feeling feels good or bad. · screen version (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-333-feelings-feels-good-or-feels-bad.js)
- `K-334` Which Face Shows the Feeling? — Read the feeling word and circle the one face in the row that shows it. · screen version (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-334-feelings-which-face-shows-the-feeling.js)
- `K-335` How Do I Feel Today? — Circle the face that shows how you feel today, then draw your own face. (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-335-feelings-how-do-i-feel-today.js)

**All about me: self-portrait, my favourite things, things I can do (personal expression)**

- `K-323` All About Me — Write your name and your age, draw yourself and your family, then draw your three favorite things. (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-323-all-about-me.js)
- `K-342` My Favorite Things: Circle and Write — In each row, circle your favorite picture and copy its word onto the line. (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-342-all-about-me-my-favorite-things.js)
- `K-345` I Can: Tick What You Can Do — Read each sentence and tick the things you can do. Then write one thing you want to learn. (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-345-all-about-me-i-can.js)

**Count a set (scattered, framed, in an array) and name / write / match the number, to 10–20**

- `K-343` All About My Family: Draw and Count — Draw your family. Then show how many people, brothers, sisters and pets on the ten-frames and write the number. (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-343-all-about-me-my-family.js)

**Compare two groups: more, fewer, equal (incl. comparing the letter counts of two names)**

- `K-346` My Name: Write, Count and Compare — Write your name with one letter in each box and count the letters. Then do the same for a friend and circle who has more. (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-346-all-about-me-my-name.js)

**Family members and family words (mother, grandfather, brother …) on a family tree**

- `K-370` Family Members: Who Is Who? — Find each person on the family tree and write their number in the box next to their family word. · screen version (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-370-family.js)
- `K-375` Family Words: Trace and Write — Find the person with the same number on the tree, trace their family word and write it again on the line. (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/k/K-375-family-words-trace-and-write.js)

**Family generations oldest to youngest; read clues to fill a family tree; my own family tree**

- `G1-385` Family Generations: Oldest to Youngest — In each row, write 1, 2 and 3 in the boxes to put the family words in order from the oldest generation to the youngest. · screen version (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/g1/G1-385-family-generations-oldest-to-youngest.js)
- `G1-386` Family Tree: Read the Clues — Read the clues and write each name in the right name box on the family tree. · screen version (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/g1/G1-386-family-tree-read-the-clues.js)
- `G1-387` Family Tree Template — Draw the people who are important to you in the frames and write their names on the lines. (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/g1/G1-387-family-tree-template.js)

**Family words for relationships (aunt, cousin, grandmother) from riddles**

- `G2-362` Family Relationships: Riddles — Read each riddle and write the right family word from the box on the line; one word can fit more than one riddle. · screen version (scripts/worksheet-gen/types/g2/G2-362-family-relationships-riddles.js)
