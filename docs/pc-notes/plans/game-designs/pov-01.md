## §1 — Concept / world
A lighthouse of creature-windows over ONE harbor (Pim the keeper-owl). A gentle event happens (third-person caption: "A red sailboat comes into the harbor"); a first-person LINE appears ("The boat looked so tiny — just a red dot far away"); the child taps the WINDOW whose point of view the line is told from. The **vantage lives in a POSITION-ONLY text label** ("Gull is high on the top balcony"; "Mouse is down in a rowboat on the water") — the child INFERS what each can see. Warm, no-shame: a wrong tap RE-REPRESENTS the chosen creature's vantage (why they can't be the teller), never a beep/score. A non-numeric lit-window lighthouse fills as ambient abundance (never a count).

## §2 — The standard + the load-bearing cognition
**RL.1.6 (clean SOLE primary)** — identify who is telling the story at various points in a text. **FENCES:** RL.K.6 authorship (NO "author/illustrator/who wrote this" language EVER — answer set = story characters only); RL.2.6 contrast-two-viewpoints (Grade-2 drift — this is SINGLE-attribution only, NOT also_teaches — the catalog's RL.2.6 is STRIPPED); feeling-naming (RL.K.3 / the live `social-fairness-12` + Game-62 — emotion NEVER decides; the `feels` field is never consulted by the grader). **Grade-1 meaning:** identify the first-person SPEAKER by reasoning about which character's POSITION makes the line's perception/knowledge possible; notice when the teller shifts. NOT 1st-vs-3rd-person labels; NOT speaker-TAG reading (every load-bearing line is TAG-STRIPPED — no "said Pim"). **Cue ranking (critic-locked to what a 6-year-old owns embodiedly):** VANTAGE on the **HIGH/LOW (far/near, tiny/big) axis ONLY** ≥60% dominant > KNOWLEDGE ~30% (with a visible TEXT cause) > FEELING 0% as the deciding cue. Occlusion / facing-direction vantages are **CUT** (Grade-2 spatial reasoning — a 6-year-old robustly owns "high up → things look small/far" but not "facing the rocks → can't see the water behind me"); "same-event-two-views" survives RE-CAST on high/low (the gull: "it looked tiny" vs the mouse: "it filled my whole window").

## §3 — THE ARCHITECTURE REWORK (the heart; the deepest flaw + fix)
**The critic's deepest catch — the gate validates the STRUCT while the child plays the rendered DOM.** The oracle solves a SET-MEMBERSHIP problem (`line.asserts ⊆ canSee ∪ knows`); the child solves a WORD-OVERLAP problem over the rendered label. If `canSee` is RENDERED VERBATIM into the label ("Gull sees FAR; boats look TINY") and the line echoes it ("the boat looked TINY, FAR away"), the perspective oracle and a pure `VANTAGE_KEYWORD_SOLVER` **collapse into the same function** — a child word-matches with ZERO position→visibility inference, and the locked solvers (content-match=named-noun, affect-match=emotion, bubble-restate=option-echo) are ALL BLIND (the cheat lives in a fourth text surface no gate solver reads). `EXACTLY_ONE_CONSISTENT_HOLDER` makes it WORSE (it forces the very divergence the keyword bridge exploits). This is the Game-58 circular-gate lineage (gate what the child CONSUMES, not the author's model) + the Game-64 render-a-proxy twin.

**THE FIX — split position (rendered) from canSee (oracle-only), and make the gate consume the RENDER:**
1. **`character.position`** = a spatial-only string from a CLOSED high/low POSITION vocab with NO perceptual/visibility words (`top_balcony/high/mast` vs `rowboat/tide_pool/low/on_the_water`). The label renders ONLY this. **`character.canSee`** is ORACLE-COMPUTED via a pure `visibilityFrom(position) → Set<assert>` (high → {far, whole_harbor, things_look_tiny}; low → {close, big, right_beside_me}) and is **NEVER rendered to any DOM node** (`NO_CANSEE_IN_DOM` lint). The child performs the **position→visibility inference** (Grade-1-embodied on the high/low axis) to bridge the position-label to the line's perception-claim.
2. **The gate CONSUMES the RENDER, not the struct:** `renderLabel(character)` + `renderLine(line)` pure functions feed the solvers the actual rendered strings — closing the struct-vs-DOM gap that makes every text-surface cheat invisible.
3. **KNOWLEDGE tier (~30%) carries breadth** (the high/low cut narrows vantage): exclusive-vantage / who-knows-the-secret, the cause stated as a TEXT premise (closed `knows` vocab, "Hen put the egg under the basket"), recency-decoupled (the causing character is NOT the most-recently/saliently rendered).

## §4 — Anti-cheat architecture (the gate; consumes RENDERED strings)
- **VANTAGE-LABEL KEYWORD-MATCH (the deepest cheat) — killed** by §3: `PERCEPTION_NOT_LEXICALLY_IN_LABEL` (tokenize the correct character's RENDERED label + the RENDERED line; perceptual/spatial content-word intersection MUST be empty — HARD FAIL if `far`/`tiny`/`red dot` is in both) + `VANTAGE_KEYWORD_SOLVER` MUST FAIL (real solver over rendered strings, max content-overlap → degenerates to random with position-only labels) + `POSITION_VISIBILITY_INFERENCE_REQUIRED` (mutate the line's perception `far→close` → the holder FLIPS to the low character while EVERY rendered label string stays byte-identical; if a label change is needed to flip, perception leaked) + `VANTAGE_AXIS_IS_HIGH_LOW_ONLY` + `NO_CANSEE_IN_DOM`.
- **PICTURE-DETECTIVE / VISUAL-CONTAINMENT (both designers' + pedagogy's #1 risk) — killed:** the window-swap "try on the eyes" preview is a **POST-COMMIT reveal ONLY** (no pre-tap/hover render path); `VISUAL_CONTAINMENT_SOLVER` MUST FAIL (the preview is not a pre-commit decision input) + `SOLVABLE_WITH_ART_STUBBED` (oracle + a TEXT-ONLY human pass both still solve — if removing art breaks solvability, the art was load-bearing → FAIL).
- **KNOWLEDGE-tier recency cheat — killed:** `KNOWLEDGE_NOT_RECENCY_SOLVER` (picks the last-mentioned/last-acting character) MUST FAIL; the cause is a TEXT premise, not a watched animation.
- **The locked surface solvers** (`POV_CONTENT_MATCH` / `POV_AFFECT_MATCH` / `POV_BUBBLE_RESTATE` / `POSITION_BIAS` / FIRST / RANDOM) all FAIL; `feels` never consulted (`AFFECT_DECOUPLED_FROM_HOLDER`); `HOLDER_POSITION_RANDOMIZED`.
- **Structural asserts:** `EXACTLY_ONE_CONSISTENT_HOLDER` (0 or ≥2 = hard build failure); **3-character scenes for Rung-2+** (2-char only for Rung-1 uncontested) + `SINGLE_HOLDER_VIA_POSITION_NOT_LEXEME` (single-holder survives deleting all shared content-words); `ANSWER_DERIVED_NOT_STORED` (mutate canSee/asserts → holder flips; mutate mentionsName → unchanged) + `NO_FIELD_BIJECTS_TO_HOLDER` (generalized no-`speaker` lint: no single rendered field alone picks the holder across the deck); `PERSPECTIVE_IS_LOAD_BEARING` (holder ≠ mentionsName not always true); `DIVERGENCE_ROUNDS_PRESENT ≥3`; vocab-closure; ≥7 variety floor.
- **TELLER-SHIFT (Rung 3) rebuilt as genuine shift-DETECTION** (not two independent attributions): a 2-3-line story; one line's "I" asserts something OUTSIDE the running speaker's `visibilityFrom(position)`; the child taps **THAT line** ("the teller changed here"). `SHIFT_IS_VANTAGE_INCONSISTENCY` + `SHIFT_NOT_NAME_CUE`.

## Cohort doctrine this game adds
**The GATE-MUST-CONSUME-THE-RENDER lesson (render a proxy, not the answer-bearing field) — PROGRAM-WIDE.** A MEASURED gate that validates the STRUCT (the descriptor fields the oracle reads) is BLIND to a cheat that lives in the RENDERED TEXT SURFACE the child actually plays — and if a structured answer-driving field is RENDERED VERBATIM to the child, the oracle and a pure word-match solver **collapse into the same function**, so the gate certifies a word-matching game as a reasoning game. (The Game-58 circular-gate lineage — gate what the child CONSUMES — applied to a structured-data game; the Game-64 tuple/render-split twin — render a proxy, not the answer token.) **The fix, three locks: (1) the gate must CONSUME the RENDER — pure `render*` functions feed the adversarial solvers the actual rendered strings, never the struct; (2) a structured field that DRIVES the answer must NOT be rendered verbatim — render a PROXY (here POSITION) from which the child INFERS the answer-driving field (VISIBILITY), and assert the inference is load-bearing by mutating the answer-claim and checking the holder flips while every rendered proxy-string stays byte-identical; (3) assert the answer-claim's content-words are LEXICALLY ABSENT from the winning proxy-label (`*_NOT_LEXICALLY_IN_LABEL`) so no word-bridge survives.** Companion scope-rule: when a standard's reasoning requires an INFERENCE, confirm the inference is GRADE-EMBODIED (here high/low→far/small is Grade-1-owned; occlusion/facing-direction is Grade-2) — restrict the deck to the embodied axis and let a SECOND cue-type (knowledge) carry breadth, rather than ship an above-grade inference the child guesses at.
