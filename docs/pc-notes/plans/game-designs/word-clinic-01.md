## 2. Concept & creative hook
**A gentle word-clinic.** A "sick" word-patient (a picture-creature — a frog, a boat) shuffles onto the exam cot, sniffling, feverish. The X-ray viewer shows its **sound-skeleton** — one slot per PHONEME (split from the gated `vocabulary-phonics.json`, never invented). Most slots are **healthy** (scaffolded with their letters — the stable frame); ONE (sometimes a 2-letter cluster) is **sick** — faded/empty. The child is the caring **word-doctor who heals by EAR**.
- **THE LISTEN LAMP (the phonemic channel):** the child taps the lamp → the patient says its WHOLE word (TTS, replayable; tapping the SICK slot re-voices the whole word SLOWER — supporting the child to re-listen for the target sound WITHOUT a fake isolated phoneme). The child must hear the word, isolate the SICK sound in its framed position, and choose the grapheme that spells THAT sound.
- **THE ATOMIC EAR-DECISION (the critic's reframe — the keep-condition):** sickness is ONE encoding decision — one phoneme's grapheme (1 letter for a vowel, 2 for a digraph/team), chosen by ear. NOT N loose scattered slots (impossible to segment + maximally brute-forceable); NOT a single forced letter in a fully-shown word (the rejected recognition shape). The child GENERATES the sick sound's spelling — production, even when it's one phoneme — because the spelling is never shown + the grapheme is chosen by ear from different-sound competitors.
- **THE MEDICINE TRAY (DIFFERENT-sound competitors — the refinement):** the tray holds the grapheme that spells the heard sick sound + **phonetically-confusable DIFFERENT-sound competitors** (the near-confusion graphemes a Grade-1 speller mixes up — short-/i/→{i,e,a}; /sh/→{sh,ch,s}; long-/oa/→{oa,o,ow... but distinct SOUNDS only}). The EAR decides (pick the grapheme matching the heard sound) — NOT same-sound homophones (which the ear can't distinguish; those are L.1.2.d convention-choices, DROPPED).
- **THE VOICE-BACK LOOP (the signature — anti-brute-force AS learning):** when the child places a grapheme, the patient **"tries to say" the resulting word** — a wrong (different-sound) grapheme makes the patient voice a DIFFERENT, wrong-sounding word ("ren" when the child put short-e in "rain") → the child HEARS the mismatch against the target word → self-corrects. Brute-force becomes a self-correcting listening loop (a wrong guess TEACHES); the patient never says "wrong."
- **THE HEAL (binary, earned by encoding):** the correct grapheme → the patient voices the RIGHT word → the fever cools (binary 🤒→😄, NEVER a number) → the creature pinks up, hops off the cot, and says its NAME. `ctx.correct()`.
- **NO-SHAME:** a wrong tile springs back atomically (the sick grapheme returns; the rest of the frame is untouched — no per-slot green to climb), fever HOLDS (never rises), the lamp re-voices; after ~3 wrongs a **"nurse's hint"** gently dims ONE clearly-impossible competitor (narrows without revealing; still ≥2 plausible). No buzzer, no red, no score. Non-numeric vessel = the **waiting-room bench** of healed friends.
- **Anti-generic statement:** NOT a spelling worksheet with a stethoscope — (a) the spelling is shown NOWHERE → generative encoding, not visual-diff; (b) the atomic ear-decision + different-sound tray make the EAR load-bearing (phonemic L.1.2.e); (c) the voice-back loop turns brute-force into self-correcting listening (the premium delight); (d) the gate proves a no-spelling + a no-sound-constraint + a picture-recall solver all FAIL while a spelling oracle passes.

## 3. Core loop & the honest ear-decision cognitions
**Loop (`stage`: listen → encode):** the sick patient arrives → the child taps the listen lamp (whole word; sick-slot tap = slower re-voice) → isolates the sick sound → drags the matching grapheme from the tray → the patient voices-back the resulting word (right → heal; wrong → a different word heard, self-correct) → correct → fever cools, the creature says its name → `ctx.correct()`. **No fail, no timer, no score, no Check button, no attempts-counter.** Wrong → atomic spring-back + voice-back + (post-3) nurse's-hint dim. First-attempt-correct is the internal signal (never shown).

**The genuine ear-driven ENCODING cognition-classes (the critic's must-fix F — DIFFERENT-sound, ear-decides; pure-orthographic conventions DROPPED as L.1.2.d). Each round carries a `cog`:**
| # | Cognition (`cog`) | The child's ear-decision | Why honest L.1.2.e + voice-back-compatible |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-5 ★ | **short-vowel choice** (`vowel-a/e/i/o/u`) | hear the framed short vowel → pick a/e/i/o/u | the hardest K-1 encoding act (Treiman vowel-confusion); 5 DISTINCT decisions (each a different sound + competitor set + error pattern); a wrong vowel voices a different word |
| 6 ★ | **consonant-digraph SOUND** (`digraph`) | hear /sh/ /ch/ /th/ → pick the 2-letter digraph (one sound) | phoneme≠grapheme-count (the core L.1.2.e leap); competitors = different-SOUND digraphs/consonants → ear decides; wrong = different word |
| 7 ★ | **long-vowel / vowel-team SOUND** (`vowel-team`) | hear the long vowel /oa/ /ai/ /ee/ → pick the team spelling | competitor = the SHORT-vowel grapheme (a DIFFERENT sound → a different word voiced: "rain" vs "ran") → ear decides, loop works |
| 8 | **initial/final blend — don't-drop-a-sound** (`blend`) | hear /fr/ /st/ → encode BOTH consonants (the framed cluster) | the segmentation K-1 kids collapse ("fog" for "frog"); each consonant a different sound |
- **Honest framing (the critic's must-fix F):** **≥7 genuinely-distinct ear-decisions** (5 short-vowel choices + consonant-digraph + vowel-team + blend = 8 distinct DECISIONS, each a different sound/competitor/error-pattern — NOT one "pick a grapheme" act × words). **DROPPED from L.1.2.e (pure orthographic, ear-CAN'T-decide → L.1.2.d, breaks the voice-back loop):** consonant DOUBLING (bb), c/k/ck same-sound choice, same-sound vowel-team homophones (ai/ay/a_e). These are a future L.1.2.d game's lane. **The ≥7 distinct EXPERIENCES = the 8 ear-decisions × the (untaught-word) pool** — a fresh word recruiting a different sound-decision is genuinely distinct (the Game-30 paragraph-pool precedent; NOT the fraction-shape padding, because each decision is a different orthographic sound-target). Order-only reshuffle.
- **Free slice = vowel-i + digraph + vowel-team** — the three sharpest ear-decisions (a no-sound solver + a picture-recall solver both fail immediately).

## 5. Pedagogy
- **L.1.2.e instantiation (positionally-scaffolded phonemic encoding):** the child hears the word, isolates the sick sound in its framed position, and PRODUCES the grapheme that spells it (generative encoding) — choosing by EAR among different-sound competitors. The frame assists segmentation (positional), but the grapheme-CHOICE (where the standard lives) is genuine + unscaffolded.
- **THE KEYSTONE RULES (the critic's reworks + the refinement):** (i) **ATOMIC ear-decision** (one phoneme's grapheme; not loose slots, not a forced single letter). (ii) **DIFFERENT-sound competitor tray** (the ear decides; same-sound homophones are L.1.2.d, dropped). (iii) **the VOICE-BACK loop** (a wrong sound voices a different word → self-correcting; brute-force becomes learning). (iv) **the correct spelling shown NOWHERE** (encoding, not visual-diff). (v) **whole-word TTS only + slower-re-voice** (the safe phonemic channel; never isolated phonemes). (vi) **untaught/non-sight-spellable pool** (L.1.2.e = "untaught"; not memorized-spelling words). (vii) **NO pure-orthographic conventions** (doubling/c-k-ck = L.1.2.d, ear-can't-decide).
- **§20.8 demonstrate-don't-touch:** the catalog's diagnose-and-replace TOUCHED spelling (recognition); the generative atomic-ear-decision build DIRECTLY INSTANTIATES L.1.2.e (production by ear). The line is exactly the keystones — the moment the spelling is shown, or the tray is same-sound, or the scaffold forces the answer, it collapses to recognition/recall/narrowing. The gate (no-spelling + constraint + picture-recall solvers all fail; oracle 100%) is the structural proof.
- **The cohort cheat one level deeper (the critic's catch):** the obvious fixes (no-spelling-shown, confusable-tray, child's-diagnosis, no-per-slot-green) leave the SCAFFOLD-AMOUNT cheat — too much scaffold degrades to "fill one letter" (recognition); too little is K-1-impossible. The atomic ear-decision resolves it (one decision, framed rest). And the picture-RECALL cheat (sight-spelling a memorized common word) — resolved by the untaught-word pool + the picture-recall gate-solver.
- **also_teaches:** **RF.1.3** (apply Grade-1 phonics/word-analysis — strand-level, honest for the grapheme-choice). **DROPPED: RF.K.3.c** (read common HFW by SIGHT — the OPPOSITE word-class from L.1.2.e's "untaught words"; drift) + **RF.1.3.b** (DECODE/read = the inverse direction of ENCODE/spell; drift).
- **Grade-1 fit:** 3-6-letter words; ONE sick ear-decision per word (a 2nd only in a stretch tier, and only if both are atomic + framed); start short-vowel + digraph; vowel-team + blend harder. The pool skews to decodable-but-not-yet-memorized-spelling convention words.
- **No-shame:** the served-creature (healed, named, on the bench) is the intrinsic reward; wrong → the voice-back (hear why) + atomic spring-back + the nurse's-hint floor; never reveal, never buzz.
