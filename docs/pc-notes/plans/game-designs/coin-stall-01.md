## 2. Concept & creative hook
**Pip the otter's seaside market stall.** A driftwood counter, a kelp basket, a swinging chalkboard price. Pip sells snacks + toys; the child pays EXACT change from a coin purse.
- **THE COIN = A RECOGNIZABLE TOKEN, value INVISIBLE-ON-THE-COIN (the pedagogy's core + the critic's A):** coins render as real engraved FACES — distinct by size, metal-color, and minted design (penny small-copper, nickel larger-silver, **dime SMALL-silver**, quarter largest-silver) — with **NO value-numeral and NO countable pips/dots ON the coin**. The value lives in the child's head. An **off-coin REFERENCE LEGEND** (a "coin values" strip) scaffolds EARLY: Tier 1 shows coin-image → "10¢"; **Tier 2 withholds ONE channel** (images-only OR ¢-values-only — never both, so the child supplies the other half); **Tier 3+ has NO legend** (retrieval from memory). **≥60% of a session is legend-FREE** (where the standard is genuinely exercised).

The running sum stays in the CHILD's head (the unitizing mental count-on IS the cognition). The trade SEALS on exact; on over, **adjust-don't-wipe** — Pip says "a bit too much — take one back" and the CHILD removes a coin of their choosing (no system-leak of which/how-much; no rage-wipe of careful work).
- **PENNY-LIMITED + ≥2-DENOMINATIONS (the anti-all-pennies):** the purse holds ≤4 pennies + every round's solution requires ≥2 distinct denominations → you CANNOT pay 47¢ in pennies; you must reach for a nickel/dime/quarter + KNOW its value.
- **THE DIME CELEBRATED (value-over-size, the critic's E):** mid+ tiers include a fewest-coins round where the small dime BEATS a larger nickel-pair; Pip celebrates "the tiny dime is worth MORE than the big nickel — sneaky-strong!" — a value-over-size ACT, not an appearance lookup.
- **REAL WORD-PROBLEMS (the critic's D):** Pip narrates a scenario ("3 acorns, each 15¢ — pay the exact price" / "you have these coins — can you afford the basket?"); ≥1 round crosses $1 (the $ symbol). The narration carries the 2.MD.C.8 word-problem; the coin act answers it.
- **THE TRADE-UP (the collect-trade family promise + a cognition):** Pip offers "5 pennies for 1 nickel?" — the child trades up equal value across denominations (the unitizing insight made a barter act).
- **THE PAYOFF (earned by value-composition):** exact change → cha-ching + the snack arcs into the kelp basket + Pip claps. `ctx.correct()`. Wrong → no red; under won't release; over → adjust-a-coin (no wipe); reshuffle-on-wrong re-randomizes the purse (anti-brute-force).
