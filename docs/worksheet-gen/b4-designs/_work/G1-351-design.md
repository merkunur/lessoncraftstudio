## 1 Page concept (base)

Nine round numeral chips in a strip; below them two houses, one for odd, one for even, each with its word on a sign and eight empty dashed boxes inside. Three numbers have already moved in: they sit in a white tile in their house with their pairs drawn beside them (a hollow ring on the one left over), the worked examples. The child reads a chip, decides, and WRITES the numeral into a box in that house. Pencil first, one apparatus, whitespace around the houses, no legend, no arrows. Quality comes from the house drawing (clean 3 px teal outline, a chimney so it never reads as an arrow, roof colour as a redundant cue only, the word sign as the mono cue) and from the answer never being on the page for the nine open chips: the cue lives ONLY next to the three solved numbers, inside their houses.

## 4 Answer-hiding + uniqueness

The chips print numerals only (`data-lcs-val`); NO parity attribute is stamped on a chip: verify re-derives `val % 2`. The houses stamp `data-lcs-house="odd|even"` on their root; the worked tiles stamp `data-lcs-given="<n>"` and sit INSIDE a house, so the only place a house name meets a numeral is a solved example. The pairDots cue is rendered ONLY inside a worked tile (the component throws if `pairDots` is called from the chip strip; verify asserts `[data-lcs-pairdots]` count === worked count and every one has a `[data-lcs-given]` ancestor). Every open box is `blankNumeralBox` with `data-lcs-answer=""` and `data-lcs-house` inherited from its ancestor; nothing on the page says where a chip goes. Each open chip has exactly one correct house (parity is total on the integers). A wrong answer is visibly wrong: the teacher reads a numeral inside the wrong house's walls; a numeral written twice or a chip never written is visible against the strip. Verify (base): 2 `[data-lcs-house]` = {odd, even} in the bank's `houseOrder`; chips distinct, in `range`, count === `chips`; worked numerals distinct from the chips, <= 10, parity === the house they sit in, `pairDots` pairs === `floor(n/2)` and single === `n % 2` (poison: a worked 7 with 4 pairs -> FAIL; a worked 8 in the odd house -> FAIL); chips + worked = 6 odd + 6 even at d2; per house: blank boxes === `boxes`, every `data-lcs-answer` is the empty string, `boxes` !== open chips of that parity; no `<img>` on the page; no text node equals any `doublesHalves` literal of `data/b2/labels.js` for the locale (poison: a sign reading "dubbelt" -> FAIL); the signs' text === `chips.odd` / `chips.even` from the locale bank.
