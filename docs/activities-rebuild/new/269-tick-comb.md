## The skill
Locate and read numbers on a number line that does not start at 0 and labels only every 5th or 10th tick.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE TICK COMB. A number-line window (40–60, 60–100, or a random 20-wide window up to 100) labels only every 5th or 10th tick. A dot sits on an unlabelled tick. The child drags a COMB out of any label: each tooth drops onto the next tick and shows +1, +2, +3 …; the comb pulls right or left, so counting back from the next label works too. The child types the number at the dot. Place mode: a number is given and the child drags the dot onto a tick; the comb then comes out from the nearest label and shows how far off it landed. Miss 1: the nearest label pulses and the comb snaps out from it to the dot; miss 2: every tick between the two neighbouring labels gets a faint numeral. Right: the dot becomes a labelled tick that stays on the line for the rest of that window's rounds.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Counting from 0 or from the left end of the window, counting the label tick itself as 1, or treating each tick as 10 because the labels go up in tens.
- **Pictures:** none
- **Settings:** Window: 40–60 / 60–100 / random 20-wide window up to 100 · Labels: every 5 / every 10
- **Rounds:** ≥ 8 rounds: dot positions never on a label, spread left/right of the nearest label (≥ 3 rounds where counting back is shorter); windows shuffled.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G2-305, G2-310
- **Distinct from:** Track Repair numbers every tie from a start; Hopper adds by hops from 0; the Landing Strip and Open Number Line are tools. The comb counts ticks from a sparse label, which is the 2.MD.B.6 reading skill.
- **Build risk:** Low.
