# nt20-C panel findings per type (consolidated 2026-09-13 from four expert panels: pedagogy ×11 curricula; native SEO en/de/nl · es/pt/fr/it · sv/da/no/fi)

Tier A = a genre teachers search by name every week · B = real, regular · C = fringe · dead = no native genre. "Head" = the native term a teacher types. Where a panel ruled a REBUILD (a different teaching point in that locale), it is stated; the locale panel in the build phase may still refine it — but must not contradict the code facts below.

## 1 K-317 `letter-of-the-week` — Letter of the Week / letter hunt (K)
Heads: en "letter of the week printables", "letter a worksheets preschool" (per-letter packs; WINNABLE) · de NO "Buchstabe der Woche" genre — real heads are **Anlaute hören Arbeitsblatt**, **Buchstabeneinführung**, "Buchstabe M Arbeitsblatt", **Anlaut Inlaut Auslaut** (letter anywhere) (A; heads HARD, per-letter pages WINNABLE) · nl **letter k werkblad** (per-letter), **beginklank kleuters** (A; the biggest NL literacy deck gap) · fr **fiche son [a/ou/on/oi] CP**, "fiches des sons CP à imprimer" (A; Bout de Gomme owns the head, per-son tail large; 39 gap queries) · es **fichas letra M** / la letra M primer grado / palabras con la letra m / ma me mi mo mu; preschool face **sonidos iniciales** (A; 178 per-letter hits) · pt **atividades com a letra M** / atividades de alfabetização letra M / som das letras (A; 287 hits) · it **schede lettera M classe prima** / MA ME MI MO MU / parole con la M (A; PianetaBambini) · sv **veckans bokstav**, bokstavsarbete (A; skolmagi/teachacademy paid) · da **ugens bogstav**, bogstavlyde, bogstavkendskab (A) · no **ukens bokstav**, bokstavinnlæring, "arbeidsark bokstaven s", bokstavhus (A) · fi **viikon kirjain**, kirjaintuntemus, kirjainjahti (A).

Traps: fi "kirjain" collides with "iso vai pieni alkukirjain" (capitalisation); de per-letter fan is the winnable tail.
