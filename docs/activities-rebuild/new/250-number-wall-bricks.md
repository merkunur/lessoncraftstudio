## The skill
In a number wall each brick holds the sum of the two bricks under it; a missing lower brick is found by subtracting.

## The apparatus and its invention
BRICKS THAT POUR UP. A wall of 3 (or 4) rows; each brick is a small open crate. A filled bottom brick shows its numeral and, in the counter setting, that many identical library objects. The child taps two side-by-side bricks; their objects pour up into the empty brick resting on both and settle there — but its numeral stays blank: the child types the total, and only a right number lets the brick take its weight (a wrong one wobbles and the objects stay heaped; miss 2 numbers the objects). A pour is refused unless the two bricks are the pair that brick actually stands on: a diagonal or far pair makes the target brick tilt and shake. Missing-bottom face (and the start-at-the-top face): the top brick shows its total as a crate with that many slots; the child pours the known neighbour up into it and the slots it fills light, so the slots left empty ARE the missing brick — the child types that number. Numbers setting: bricks show numerals only and a pour rises as a filling bar; miss 2 brings the objects back. Right: the brick settles with a solid thunk.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Adds the wrong pair (diagonal or non-adjacent bricks), or adds instead of subtracting when the top brick is known.
- **Pictures:** fruits / vegetables (identical instances) for the counter setting; none for the numbers setting
- **Settings:** Wall: 3 rows to 10 / 3-4 rows to 20 · Missing: top bricks / one bottom brick / start at the top.
- **Rounds:** 8+ walls from random base triples/quads with every sum within the cap; the missing-brick position rotates across left, middle and right (measured 30-40% each); no wall repeats in a pass.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G1-246, G1-266, G1-267, G1-268, G1-289
- **Distinct from:** No existing activity uses the additive pyramid; number bonds use one whole and two parts with no stacking or chaining.
- **Build risk:** Four-row walls at 20 must fit a phone at 360 px; counters give way to numerals above 10 per brick.
