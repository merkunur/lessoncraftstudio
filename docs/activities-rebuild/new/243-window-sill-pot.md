## The skill
Know what plants need to grow: water and light (and soil and air).

## The apparatus and its invention
THE PLANT THAT ANSWERS WHAT YOU GIVE IT. A pot (apparatus) on a window sill holds a library flower at small scale; a watering can (apparatus control), a curtain (open/closed) and a cupboard door wait. The child decides what the plant gets for a week — taps the can to water, opens or closes the curtain, or drags the pot into the cupboard — then pulls the week lever: the plant grows (scales up and stands tall) only with both water and light; with water but no light it grows pale (desaturated) and does not grow; with light but no water it droops (a slow lean). COMPARE mode (K-376): three pots get different treatment shown by icons (drop, sun, both); the child picks the one that will grow best, then the week lever runs all three. Miss 1: the chosen pot droops or pales while another grows; Miss 2: the missing need's icon flashes on the chosen pot.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Plants need only water (or food); light does not matter.
- **Pictures:** flowers (sunflower, tulip, daisy-like zinnia) — one flower per round; pot, can, curtain and cupboard are apparatus.
- **Settings:** Mode: grow your pot / which pot grows best · Needs: water and light / plus soil
- **Rounds:** 9 rounds; compare-mode pots shuffled with the right one in each slot ≤ 40 %; grow-mode rounds alternate the starting state (in the cupboard / dry / both missing); one right answer.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** K-376
- **Distinct from:** No plant activity exists.
- **Build risk:** The transforms (scale, desaturate, lean) must not look broken — the reviewer reads the three states at 360 and 704. Soil/air setting dropped (not picturable).
