## 2. Concept & creative hook
Big and Bit, two round fuzzballs, run a little **friendship bridge** over a stream. The bridge has a secret: friends can only cross if **everyone has a hand to hold** — each fuzzball on Big's side reaches across to one on Bit's side and they **clasp paws** with a soft "boop." The child is the matchmaker: tap a Big-fuzzball, a glowing yarn-ribbon shoots from its paw, drag it to a Bit-fuzzball — they giggle, link arms, and **settle into a tidy rung** of the bridge-ladder. When no more pairs can form, the friends **left standing alone with an empty paw** are the "MORE." But the bridge keeps its truth hidden until the child decides: it sits **flat and patient** while the child matches and judges — the tilt is the *applause* after, never the answer.
- **The LADDER OF RUNGS (the critical structural fix — closes the pedagogy + mobile + gate holes at once):** each matched pair slides into an **aligned row** — Big directly beside its Bit, the yarn-rope short and vertical — so the bridge becomes a tidy **ladder of rungs (pairs), NOT crossing diagonals.** The unpaired friends sit **alone at the end of the ladder** with empty paws. The leftover is legible as a **pure consequence of the child's own pairing** (no partner = an empty rung-slot), with **NO glow, no pulse, no wave** during matching/judging. The child READS the matched state (the matching strategy) — the engine never points at the answer.
- **The beam is LOCKED FLAT through match + judge (a wooden peg holds it):** there is no tilt to read; the tilt releases ONLY after the child commits a relation (MORE/SAME/FEWER), tipping toward the leftover side as *confirmation/applause*. A child (or solver) inspecting the judge stage sees a level beam + a ladder — the only way forward is matching or counting.
- **The misleading-bust is the EMOTIONAL PEAK (not a quota):** the default first impression LIES — a spread-out row of 5 *looks* longer/more than a tight cluster of 6. The child **predicts** ("the long line has more"), pairs them into the ladder, and **watches the lie collapse**: Big's row runs out at 5 rungs, and TWO Bit-friends are still standing alone. *"Wait — the SHORT one had MORE?! Our eyes tricked us, but our hands found the truth."* The misconception isn't avoided — it's **named, dramatized, and busted by the child's own action.**
- **Counting is supported alongside (the standard names both):** each pan has a tap-to-count affordance (tap each fuzzball → it lights + speaks the number word + a per-round tally fills). Some rounds foreground counting (9-vs-9 — pairing 9 ropes is tedious → counting is the *smart* strategy → the game teaches strategy-selection). Both strategies converge on the same MORE/FEWER/SAME word — **but the comparison is always the child's** (the engine never renders or resolves "6 > 5").
- **Anti-generic statement:** NOT the free which-more + fuzzballs — (a) the child ENACTS the matching strategy (pairs 1-to-1 into a ladder; the leftover is their residue, NOT an engine glow); (b) the beam is locked flat (no tilt-read) + NO pre-commit emphasis on the leftover (the glow-cheat killed); (c) the misleading-perceptual / conservation cases (the eye points wrong); (d) relation words only (no symbols); (e) a board ships only if a **perceptual solver (tilt/length/spread/height/area/density/glow)** FAILS on the misleading subset while a **count/match solver** passes — AND no pre-commit visual emphasis exists during the judge stage (§8).

## 3. Core loop & the ≥7 distinct experiences
**Loop (`stage`: predict? → match → judge → confirm):** the two groups are GIVEN → (optional) the child PREDICTS who has more → the child PAIRS them 1-to-1 (drag yarn-yokes; each settles into a ladder rung; no auto-pair) → the beam stays LOCKED FLAT; when no more pairs form, the unpaired friends sit alone at the ladder's end (no glow) → the child JUDGES (taps **MORE on Big's side / SAME / MORE on Bit's side** — words) → on commit: the peg releases, the beam tips toward the leftover side (applause), the misleading-bust reveal plays → `ctx.correct()`. **Wrong → the beam stays FLAT + (NOW, post-commit-of-a-wrong-answer) the lonely friend waves "you forgot me!" → a gentle guided-pairing re-teach** (the re-teach IS the matching strategy demonstrated). **No fail, no timer, no score, no Check button.**

**The ≥7 distinct experiences — HONEST re-derivation (the critic's catch: the misleading-bust is a MODE of match-to-judge, not a separate act): distinct = the CHILD'S ACT, NOT a different pair or a layout-knob.** Each carries a declared **`act_type`** (gate-keyed, §8):
| # | Experience (the distinct ACT, `act_type`) | Render / act | Why it's distinct & instantiates K.CC.C.6 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ★ | **Match-to-judge** (`match-judge` — the core; misleading layouts are the INTERNAL variety mode) | pair 1-to-1 into the ladder → read the leftover (empty-paw friend) → judge the relation | the matching strategy; the misleading-spread/height/size/density layouts are the variety knob WITHIN this act (NOT separate experiences) |
| 2 ★ | **Count-to-judge** (`count-judge`) | tap-count each pan → the CHILD compares (no "6>5" rendered) → judge | the counting strategy (distinct from pairing); strategy-selection (smart when pairing is tedious) |
| 3 ★ | **How-many-more** (`how-many-more` — promoted to co-headline) | after pairing → count the leftover lonely friends → "Bit has TWO more" | quantify the DIFFERENCE (count the unmatched) — a distinct act from judging the direction; the richest premium act the free tile can't touch |
| 4 ★ | **Conservation-confront** (`conservation` — the SAME-relation discovery) | a MISLEADING-spread layout (5-spread vs 5-tight, the spread LOOKS more) → predict → pair → everybody matches → "SAME — just spread out!" | the EQUAL endpoint (no leftover) + overriding the prediction; the conservation hard case (rearrangement doesn't change number) |
| 5 ★ | **Make-it-fair / commit-the-count** (`make-equal` — the K.OA.A.4 sub-mode) | two friends are alone → the child COUNTS how many more are needed ("3 more") → adds exactly that → all pair | construct-to-equalize with the QUANTIFICATION load-bearing (count-then-add, not blind brute-force) |
| 6 ★ | **Build-the-equal-group** (`build-equal`) | a group is GIVEN → the child builds a matching group from scratch (count-and-match production) to have the SAME number | a PRODUCTION act (build to match), distinct from judging given groups |
| 7 ★ | **Predict-then-verify** (`predict-verify` — the stance) | guess who has more BEFORE evidence → then match/count to check (your guess can be wonderfully wrong) | the metacognitive stance (commit before evidence) — builds the disposition to match/count, not eyeball |

- **Honest framing (the §A.13.60 fix):** seven distinct ACTS (declared `act_type` enums), NOT layout/pair variants. The **misleading-perceptual-bust is the variety MODE within #1** (spread/height/size/density as the knob) — never counted as a separate experience. Different pairs ride within each. The variety gate asserts ≥7 distinct `act_type` enum VALUES present (not 7 rounds); #4 (conservation) + #7 (predict-verify) flagged for playtest (the predict-then-verify stance can wrap others).
- **Free slice = match-to-judge (1) + count-to-judge (2) + how-many-more (3)** — proves both strategies + the quantify.

## 4. Win-states & juice (no competition)
No timer/score/streak/countdown/**fail-state**/attempts-counter/"X of Y". **THE MISLEADING-BUST REVEAL (the emotional peak, choreographed):** predict → pair → **watch the long line shrink as pairs settle into rungs** → the lonely friend pops out on the SHORT-looking side → `ctx.speak()` "the short row had MORE — six is more than five — longer isn't always more!" + the beam tips toward the leftover side (applause). **THE CONSERVATION REVEAL:** zero leftovers → the beam settles PERFECTLY FLAT + "everybody found a friend — same number, different shapes!" + a symmetric glow — **post-commit only** (so the equal case lands emotionally without handing the answer). **Per matched pair:** a soft "boop" + the rung settles. **Non-numeric vessel = the Friendship Garland** (`GameCollection`) — each completed round ties one more bloom into a garland strung between two trees; the full garland lights up + all the pairs swing on it. No "3 of 8", no bar; the per-round count tally RESETS each round (never cumulative — no score-like artifact). **Negative signal = no-shame re-teach ONLY:** a wrong judgment → the beam stays FLAT + (post-commit) the lonely friend waves "you forgot me!" + a guided-pairing demonstration (the re-teach IS the matching strategy); never a buzzer. **NO pre-commit emphasis** on any unpaired/leftover element during match+judge (the glow-cheat killed — the leftover is read from the ladder, not a cue). `ctx.correct()` fires on the correct judgment → auto-advance. Full-pass keepsake = the Friendship Garland → cross-game `GameCollection`. CSS/SVG/WebAudio; reduced-motion (the tilt is an instant settle, not a spin); mute-safe (the ladder + the relation text carry it).

## 5. Pedagogy
- **K.CC.C.6 instantiation:** the child IDENTIFIES the relation (more/fewer/same) by MATCHING (pair 1-to-1 → the leftover side is more) or COUNTING (count each → the child compares the cardinals), NOT by any perceptual cue. The misleading cases (the eye points wrong) + the conservation case (same N, different arrangement → equal) are the load-bearing content.
- **THE KEYSTONE RULES (non-guessability):** (i) **NO PERCEPTUAL PROXY** — the beam is locked flat during judgment (no tilt) AND there is NO glow/pulse/wave on the leftover pre-commit (the leftover is read from the pairing graph — empty paws on the ladder); a perceptual solver (tilt/length/spread/height/area/density/glow) must FAIL on the misleading subset. (ii) **THE MATCHING IS THE CHILD'S ACT** — no auto-pair; the leftover emerges from the child's 1-to-1 pairing (the standard's matching strategy). (iii) **THE COMPARISON IS THE CHILD'S** — count-to-judge never renders or auto-resolves "6 > 5"; the child commits a relation, the engine checks against ground truth, never displays the numeric comparison. (iv) **MISLEADING TWIST REQUIRED ≥⅓** — spread-vs-tight near-pairs + the conservation equal case; NEVER an easy far-pair in a congruent layout (the pure cheat trial). (v) **RELATION WORDS ONLY** (no >/</= symbols — Grade 1).
- **Number ranges (pedagogy):** groups ≤10; **NEAR-PAIRS (differ by 1: 5/6, 6/7, 4/5) are load-bearing** (perceptually ambiguous → force match/count); far-pairs (3 vs 8) only in MISLEADING layouts (spread-4 vs tight-8 so "looks longer = more" still misleads); never an easy far-pair in a congruent layout. Arrangements: aligned (good for matching) / scattered (force counting) / the misleading spreads (spread-thin vs tight-cluster; tall-thin vs short-fat). Conservation cases use the misleading-spread arrangement (same N, one spread one tight).
- **Distinctness from the FREE which-more:** the free tile is a single tap on the bigger group (perception allowed to win); this enacts the MATCHING strategy + busts the perception + owns the conservation hard case. If the game could be completed by a perceptual tap, it's the free tile — REJECTED.
- **No-shame:** the wrong-answer re-teach is a guided pairing (the matching strategy demonstrated), never a buzzer; the conservation case is celebrated; the misleading-bust is the intrinsic delight ("our hands found the truth"). **§20.8 demonstrate-don't-touch:** the weight-see-saw was the textbook violation (it trained the misconception); the matching-model ENACTS the standard — the gate (perceptual-solver-fails + no-pre-commit-emphasis) is the structural proof.

## 6. Interaction & UX
- **Runtime: the pairing graph + the locked beam (the one primitive).** `compare-balance-core.js` holds `{ left:{count, layout}, right:{count, layout}, pairs (a partial bijection Map<leftId,rightId>), stage }`. Everything else DERIVED: `relation()` (from counts), `leftovers()` (the unpaired ids), `facts()` (per-side count + spread + length + density, read from the ACTUAL placed geometry). Mutators: `addPair(L,R)` (validates each endpoint unpaired; no auto-pair), `removePair(L)`, `judge(choice)` (checks `choice` against `relation()` — the ONLY answer-check; never renders a numeric comparison). Pair interaction = drag a yarn-yoke (the web designer's single full-card SVG overlay; `pointer-capture`, `touch-action:none`, `getScreenCTM().inverse()`, rAF) OR tap-block-then-tap-block (pointer parity ≤360px); ≥44px hit-halos.
- **The LADDER (the no-crossing layout):** each `addPair` slides the pair into the next aligned RUNG (Big beside its Bit, rope short+vertical) — NO crossing diagonals. Unpaired friends accumulate at the ladder's end (empty rung-slots). The leftover is legible from the ladder structure alone — NO glow/pulse/wave during match+judge.
- **State separation (anti-cheat):** `snapshot()` exposes `{ left, right, pairs, leftovers, stage, judged }`; `facts()` exposes per-side count/spread/length/density + `perceptualSide` (the eyeball answer) + `matchSide` (the leftover-truth) for the gate; the beam's `transform` is `rotate(0)` + `data-locked` during match+judge (no `data-tilt` exists pre-commit).
- **Counting:** tap-to-count each pan (each tap lights the fuzzball + speaks the number word via `_NUMBER_WORD_HELPERS` + fills a per-round dot-tally); the tally RESETS each round. For count-to-judge, the child commits a relation after counting — the engine NEVER shows "5 vs 6" or resolves the comparison (option: a count-track where the higher count lands further along, so "further = more" is the child's read of their own counting).
- **The see-saw:** locked flat (peg) through match+judge; on commit, the peg releases + the beam runs ONE deterministic `rotate(clamp(delta*4, ±16deg))` settle (CSS transition + one gentle bounce; NOT a physics loop) toward the leftover side.
- **Mobile @280px (truth-tested, the ladder solves the tangle):** vertical stack — prompt / the two groups (fuzzballs shrink to fit 10, or a 5×2 sub-grid per pan; ≥44px hit-halos) / the LADDER of rungs (no crossing yokes — the fix) / the relation pills (MORE/FEWER/SAME, stacked full-width ≤480px, ≥44px) / the see-saw beam (small ±16° clamp, pivots at the fulcrum, never overflows) / the Friendship Garland vessel. `audit-game-mobile.js` 280-768 empty + filled (the 9-vs-10 worst case): assert ≥44px targets, no overflow/clip/overlap, AND a **yoke-legibility assertion** (no rope crossings, no overlap of the unpaired indicator) at 280px.
- **Accessibility:** TTS speaks "five is fewer than six" (`_NUMBER_WORD_HELPERS` + the relation lexicon); `ctx.announce()` mirrors the matching ("matched — two friends left over on Bit's side"), the judgment, the bust reveal; keyboard path = pick-a-friend (Enter) → pick-a-partner (Enter) pairs them, the relation pills are buttons (no pointer needed); ≥44px; reduced-motion (instant tilt settle); mute-safe (the ladder + text carry it); not color-only (the leftover is an empty rung + the relation is matching + real text, never hue alone).
