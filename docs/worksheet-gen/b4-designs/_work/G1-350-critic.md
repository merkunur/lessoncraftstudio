## 5 Quality verdict

I would print the base and the letter-box page for a first grade tomorrow: one picture, one sentence, one coral box, a bank a child can actually read across, and the pictures big enough (56, not 44) that a hippopotamus is not a grey blob. The two rulings I fought hardest for are the ones a teacher would have caught on the photocopier: the plural page must not print the singular in a little chip beside the sentence (the answer minus one letter), and the story strip must not sit in sentence order (the answer's position printed). What would embarrass me: a "Der ___" over a cow (ruled out by the article table and its poison), a bank pill reading "banan" on a Swedish sheet about a running track (ruled out by an equality check against the whole vocab, not a note), a Finnish word dropped into a sentence in the wrong case (ruled out by making the case the frame's declared form), and a glass of red wine among the household pictures (opened, excluded). The residual risk no gate sees is the one the lock names: a frame two bank nouns satisfy. `fits` and `signedExclusive` make it the panel's signature, `predicateKind:'copula'` makes the classic "the ___ is red" a refusal unless justified, and the critic reads three frames per theme per locale; that is the whole defence, and it is a human one.
