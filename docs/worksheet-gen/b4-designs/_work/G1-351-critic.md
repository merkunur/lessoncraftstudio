## 5 Quality verdict

I would print this for a class. The base page is the one a first-grade teacher pins on the wall: two houses, three numbers already living in them with their pairs drawn, nine numbers waiting on the doorstep, and nothing on the page that tells a child where a number goes except the child's own pairing. The pair proof writes 2.OA.C.3 as the standard actually reads, the ones-box page makes the last-digit rule an apparatus instead of an instruction, and the sums page is honest about "do not add" only because I kept the pedagogy's three-digit addends. What would embarrass me: F2 and F5 look alike at arm's length (a pile of pictures, boxes, two pills), and both sit 11 px under the fi budget on a 4 px lane padding, which is calm on a normal title and tight under a four-line one; the `+ 0` on even pair-proof cards will draw a raised eyebrow from a teacher who reads the standard literally, and the landing must say why it is there; and the sv pill reads "jämnt" while the title says "jämna", which is correct Swedish and will still get a question. None of that is a wrong answer on a page; all of it is a render the panel and I must open before the wave.
