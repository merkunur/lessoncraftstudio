## 2. Concept & creative hook
A jar of glowing **moon-beads** spills on the floor. **Pim** (small, hopeful) and **Bo** (tall, gentle) scoop them up fast, not counting — and one ends up with MORE. Nobody did anything wrong; *it just happened.* The child sees it instantly: one friend's jar **glows warm and full**, the other's is **dim and a little droopy** — and the friend with fewer doesn't cry, they just go **quiet, ears down.** The child's job: **make it fair** — figure out how many more the quiet friend needs, and give exactly that many from the **spilled pile on the floor** (a neutral shared source — so making it fair never means taking from a friend). The agency hook: **you're the peacemaker — you say the kind number, and two friends lean their heads together.**
- **THE PRODUCE-THE-NUMERAL-FIRST SPINE (the critic's binding fix — `viaNumber` made literal):** the two jars sit frozen, their counts shown as real numerals (Pim 3, Bo 5). Mama-less; the question is the friends': *"Bo has more. How many MORE beads does Pim need to have the SAME as Bo?"* The child **determines the gap and produces the numeral** — taps **2** on a row of carved number-tiles (0-10). **That tap IS the answer** (the unknown addend `3 + ? = 5`). There is **NO basket to fill one-bead-at-a-time against the visible target** — so a child cannot increment-and-watch their way to fairness; they must DETERMINE m (by counting on 3→4→5, or by the difference) and produce it.
- **THE GIVE IS THE CELEBRATION (not the input):** only when the produced numeral is correct do exactly m beads **lift from the floor-pile and float into Pim's jar** in one warm arc; Pim's jar brightens to match Bo's; the **"=" bridge** glows between two equal jars; the friends lean together. (The child MAY tap each floating bead to send it — counting along, `ctx.speak()` — but the SOLVE already happened via the numeral; this is joy, not the gate.)
- **Why this is genuinely load-bearing + non-cheatable:** (a) the numeral is produced BEFORE any bead moves → no increment-against-a-visible-target loop (the mutated perceptual cheat is structurally gone); (b) a wrong numeral → NO bead animation (no perceptual confirmation to climb toward) + a fresh non-directional re-pose; (c) on the CEILING tier the bigger jar's count is HIDDEN (the child must hold/derive it) → the missing-addend does real work; (d) the give-from-the-floor (neutral source) keeps the social tone (level-up, never rob).
- **THE 0-CASE (required edge — woven in, not a separate experience):** sometimes the jars are **already equal** (Pim 4, Bo 4). The question still comes — *"how many more does Pim need?"* — and the child must recognize **"none — it's already fair!"** and produce **0** (or tap an "It's already fair" affordance). Recognizing fairness = giving nothing; defeats the always-give-at-least-one habit; interleaved unpredictably.
- **THE FRIENDSHIP-STRING (the keepsake + non-numeric vessel):** every fair moment adds one warm glowing bead to a **friendship-string** strung across the top (`GameCollection`) — never "5 of 9," just a growing garland of moments-of-kindness the friends hang up at the end. The repaired friendship (the quiet friend's ears come back up; heads lean) is the win — **SOCIAL ("now it's fair"), never "correct."**
- **Anti-generic statement:** NOT a missing-addend drill with a sad face — (a) the child PRODUCES the numeral first (gate-proven; the increment-watch cheat structurally gone); (b) **5 distinct CGI schemas** give the family a real math spine (not one skill in costumes); (c) the neutral-shared-bag keeps the social tone honest; (d) the 0-case "recognize it's already fair"; (e) a board ships only if an **increment-and-watch solver cannot exist** (no fill-against-target surface), an **off-by-one solver FAILS**, a **constant-answer stub FAILS the discriminating grade**, while a **produce-the-unknown solver PASSES** across all 5 schemas + the 0-case (§8).

## 3. Core loop & the 5 distinct CGI schemas (+ honest tiers)
**Loop (`stage`: read → decide → give):** the friends + their (frozen) jars appear → the question is posed (a schema, §below) → the child **produces the unknown numeral** on the number-tile row → IF correct, the give animates from the floor-pile + the friends reconcile → repeat until the friendship-string is full → `ctx.correct()`. **No fail, no timer, no score, no Check button.** A wrong numeral → **non-directional re-pose:** the tiles clear, the friends stay patient (no sad reaction at the child), the question re-poses *"look again — how many more does Pim need?"* — **no bead animation, no high/low, no by-how-much** (the critic's no-leak + no-binary-search rule); a fresh determination. First-attempt-correct is the internal signal (never shown).

**The distinct experiences — 5 genuinely-distinct CGI SCHEMAS (the math-distinct spine), each a different single-unknown structure + a different felt social wrong. distinct = a different MATH SCHEMA / reasoning, NOT a bigger number / a different friend / a magnitude (BLOCKLISTED §8).** Each carries a declared **`schema`**:
| # | Experience (the distinct SCHEMA, `schema`) | The felt wrong → the produced unknown | Equation / why distinct |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ★ | **Equalize, add-to** (`equalize-add` — the SPINE) | a quiet friend has fewer → produce how many MORE to match the other jar | `b + ? = a` (reference = the other live pile) — the unknown ADDEND; the family's core act |
| 2 ★ | **Compare, difference-unknown** (`compare-diff` — a static judgment, NO give) | "how UNFAIR is it?" → produce the SIZE of the gap (`a − b`), the child names the unfairness, no transfer | `a − b = ?` — the difference itself as a static judgment (distinct cognition: judge, don't act) |
| 3 ★ | **Separate, change-unknown / RESTORE** (`restore` — witnessed a removal) | the child SAW a friend take k from another (5 → take ? → 3) → give exactly k back to restore fairness | `s − ? = r` (find the change) — the child witnessed the CHANGE event; backward from a seen action |
| 4 ★ | **Equalize, remove-to-a-fair-target** (`reduce-to-target` — the subtraction direction) | a friend HOARDED (has 6, fair is 4) → produce how many to put BACK in the floor-jar to reach fair | `a − ? = T` (put-back to a stated fair number; goes to the NEUTRAL bag, not the other friend) — the SUBTRACT-unknown, give-away direction (distinct from #1's add) |
| 5 ★ | **Start-unknown** (`start-unknown` — reason backward to the start) | a GENEROUS friend gave some away and now has r (gave k, has 3) → how many did they START with? | `? − k = r` — the START-unknown, the hardest 1.OA.D.8 schema; reason backward |

- **Honest framing (the critic's de-padding + §A.13.60):** **5 genuinely-distinct CGI schemas** (add-unknown / difference / change-unknown / subtract-to-target / start-unknown) — the math-distinct spine. **CUT** (the critic): match-to-a-stated-target (= Game 23 make-a-total), the 3-friends identify-the-left-out (a pick + a 280px-clipping third pile). **FOLDED as a required edge, NOT a counted experience:** the 0-case (recognize already-fair → produce 0), woven across schemas. **TIERS (honest difficulty, NOT counted toward distinct):** FLOOR = both counts visible (the child computes `a−b` / counts-on — legit Grade-1 entry, but acknowledged as easier-than-missing-addend); CEILING = the bigger count HIDDEN (true missing-addend — the child must hold/derive the target); within-10 → within-20 magnitude. The variety gate keys on `schema` with a **BLOCKLIST**: "differs only in the numbers / friend-skin / magnitude / visible-vs-hidden tier" do NOT count as a distinct schema. Order-only reshuffle.
- **The 5-schema spine IS the family-head proof (the critic's mandate):** the head demonstrates 5 distinct math schemas so social-fair is born with a math spine, not "one missing-addend skill in seven emotional costumes." Future social-fair games inherit: **vary the schema, keep the felt-wrong as the through-line.**
- **Free slice = equalize-add (1) + compare-diff (2) + restore (3)** — three distinct schemas, the family's range visible immediately.

## 4. Win-states & juice (no competition)
No timer/score/streak/countdown/**fail-state**/attempts-counter/"X of Y". **THE RECONCILE (signature — the math heals the friendship):** the child produces the correct numeral → exactly m beads float from the floor-pile into the quiet friend's jar (ONE warm arc) → the jar brightens to match → a soft **"=" bridge** glows between two equal jars → the friends **lean their heads together** + `ctx.speak()` the warm number-word ("Two! Now they both have five — fair!"). **THE 0-CASE reveal:** already-fair → produce 0 → "They're already the same — that's fair! Give none" (a quiet shared hum). **Non-numeric vessel = the friendship-string** (`GameCollection` garland — each fair moment a warm bead) — NO "5/9", no bar; the friends hang it up at the end (a soft close). **Negative signal = a NON-DIRECTIONAL re-pose ONLY:** wrong numeral → tiles clear, NO bead animation (no perceptual climb), the friends stay patient, *"look again — how many more?"* — **no high/low/by-how-much**, a fresh determination (the critic's no-binary-search + no-leak rule). Never a buzzer/sad-at-the-child. **`ctx.wrong()` is a soft "not yet."** `ctx.correct()` fires on the FULL string → auto-advance. Full-pass keepsake = the hung garland + the repaired friendships → cross-game `GameCollection`. CSS/SVG/WebAudio; reduced-motion (instant give/brighten); mute-safe (the visible float + the brightened jar + the "=" carry it).

## 5. Pedagogy
- **1.OA.D.8 instantiation (5 schemas):** the child DETERMINES + PRODUCES the unknown number in a single-unknown add/sub relation of three numbers — across equalize-add (`b+?=a`), compare-difference (`a−b=?`), change-unknown (`s−?=r`), subtract-to-target (`a−?=T`), and start-unknown (`?−k=r`). The produced numeral is the load-bearing act; the give is its celebration.
- **THE KEYSTONE RULES (non-cheatability — the critic's fixes):** (i) **PRODUCE-THE-NUMERAL-FIRST (`viaNumber` literal)** — the child produces the unknown as a numeral BEFORE any bead moves; there is NO fill-against-a-visible-target surface → the increment-and-watch loop is structurally impossible (the mutated perceptual cheat is gone). (ii) **NO WRONG-ANIMATION + NON-DIRECTIONAL RE-POSE** — a wrong numeral gives no perceptual confirmation + no direction + a fresh determination → no binary-search/guess-and-check profit (and the visible piles let a determined child just COMPUTE, making guessing slower than solving). (iii) **TIER THE VISIBILITY HONESTLY** — FLOOR (both counts visible) genuinely grades the difference/count-on (a legit Grade-1 entry, NOT pretended to be missing-addend); CEILING (bigger count hidden) is where the missing-addend does real work (the child holds/derives the target). (iv) **NEUTRAL SHARED SOURCE** — give from the floor-pile, never from the other friend (the social tone of level-up; aligns with the pedagogy's add-to). (v) **0-CASE woven in** (recognize already-fair → produce 0; defeats always-give-one). (vi) **5 DISTINCT SCHEMAS** (the family math spine).
- **Count-on is the target strategy, NOT a leak (the critic's ruling):** producing m by counting on (3→4→5 = "two") is the canonical Grade-1 unknown-addend strategy (1.OA.C.5/D.8) — legitimate. The leak was *increment-a-basket-and-recheck-against-a-visible-target*, eliminated by produce-the-numeral-first. The two looked identical in taps; removing the fill-against-target surface is what separates "counted on to FIND m" from "climbed until it matched."
- **also_teaches (re-based to honesty per the pedagogy ruling):** keep **K.CC.C.6** (compare groups — the honest readiness predecessor: compare-groups → find-the-unknown-that-equalizes); **DROP 2.OA.A.1** (within-100 — a marbles game is within-20; range inflation), **K.MD.A.2** (measurable attribute, not discrete sets — DRIFT; discrete-set comparison is K.CC.C.6), **2.MD.B.5** (length — DRIFT).
- **Number ranges (pedagogy):** **within 10 base** (larger jar 3-6, gap 1-3 entry; 6-9, gap 1-4 mid), **within 20 ceiling** (larger up to ~10-12, gap up to 5; a magnitude knob WITHIN a schema, NOT a distinct experience). The 0-difference edge required (~1 in 8, never first). No negative differences (always level the smaller up / the hoarder down-to-target — consistent direction).
- **Distinctness from Games 5 & 23:** Game 5 = repair a SYMBOLIC equation (symbol manipulation); Game 23 = make-a-total (assemble addends to a STATED total). This = the Compare/Equalize SOCIAL corner referencing a LIVE second quantity + the give/reconcile resolution, across 5 schemas. If the game could be completed without DETERMINING the unknown (the increment-watch loop), it's hollow — REJECTED.
- **No-shame + §20.8 demonstrate-don't-touch:** the STRUCK move-between only TOUCHED fairness while instantiating off-band averaging (it would be removed under the "Decompose 10" precedent); the produce-the-numeral level-up DIRECTLY instantiates finding the unknown addend across 5 schemas — the gate (no increment-watch surface; off-by-one + discriminating-grade fail; produce-the-unknown passes) is the structural proof. Wrong → a warm non-directional re-pose; the 0-case celebrated.

## 6. Interaction & UX
