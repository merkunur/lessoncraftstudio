## The skill
A fraction a/b is a point on the number line: fold 0-1 into b equal spaces and count a spaces from 0.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE FOLDING STRIP: a paper strip from 0 to 1 lies on the line. The child taps the fold button b times-worth (or drags a fold-count dial) and the strip folds and unfolds, leaving b-1 creases that become tick marks — equal by construction. Then the child drags a bead from 0 along the creases to a/b; the bead counts SPACES as it passes (a small arc appears over each space crossed). Miss 1 (bead on a tick counted by marks, e.g. 1/4 placed at the second mark) lights the spaces already crossed; miss 2 numbers the spaces.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Counting tick marks (including 0) instead of the spaces between them, and making unequal parts.
- **Pictures:** none (strip, creases and bead drawn tool-grade)
- **Settings:** Denominators: 2, 3, 4 / 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 · Line: 0 to 1 / 0 to 2
- **Rounds:** Generated a/b with b from the setting and a ≥ 1, ≥ 9 rounds, no fraction repeated, includes a = b (1 whole) at least once and a/b > 1 on the 0-2 setting; answer is a position, balanced across the line by thirds.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G3-317
- **Distinct from:** No number-line fraction activity exists; Fox Forge and fraction-equiv are area models. Not Halfway Harbors (whole-number rounding).
- **Build risk:** Low.
