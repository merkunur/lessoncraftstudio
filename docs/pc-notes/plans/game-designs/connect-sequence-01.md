# GAME 13 DESIGN — "The Star-Stitcher" / Count the Stars Awake (K.CC.A.2)

> 6-expert ensemble (2 designers + math-pedagogy + web → creative-director synthesis → adversarial premium critic). Critic returned **NEEDS-REWORK** (1 structural + 6); all baked into the spine below. **The cohort through-line caught a FOURTH time — in a new costume:** the synthesized canonical input was a 4-option **candidate-band** (tap which numeral comes next) — and with the answer always present in a 4-set + no fail-state + neutral reject + infinite retries, a non-counter clears the whole game at ~2.5 taps/dot of pure guessing. Every other defense (blank dots, produce-then-arm, anti-proximity) defends against *information-leak* while the real exploit is *brute-force exhaustion of a small key-containing set* (the Game-12 nudge-hill-climb / Game-7-beam / Game-10-thread family). **The structural fix (also the most premium): production is GENERATIVE, not selective** — the canonical input is a **count-on beat** (tap "count on" → the firefly flies one star forward as the numeral climbs 7→8→9 and her luminous thread draws behind her; she stops at the target). *The only way to produce a number is to count up to it from the given star* — there is no set to pick from, mashing overshoots, and every production attempt IS an enactment of count-on. This also fuses the chopped produce-then-draw rhythm into ONE flowing beat (count = forward-flight = the thread drawing itself). Build-ready, design-only. ## 1. Header
- **Standard-facing:** Connect the Dots: Count to Reveal · **kid-facing:** Count the Stars Awake · **id:** connect-sequence-01 (catalog draw-trace-06) · **family:** draw-trace (a NEW engine — head of a "produce-the-successor-to-reveal" ordered-sequence sub-family; sibling to Game 11's `engine-draw-partition.js`)
- **PRIMARY standard:** **K.CC.A.2 (Kindergarten) — COUNT FORWARD beginning from a GIVEN NUMBER within the known sequence (instead of having to begin at 1).** Load-bearing cognition = the **successor function from an arbitrary entry point**: enter the sequence at k and produce k+1, k+2… WITHOUT rebuilding 1-2-3…-k. The diagnostic that separates it from K.CC.A.1: asked "what comes after 7," the rote child restarts "1,2,3…8"; the count-on child says "8" directly. This game targets the second child.
- **also_teaches:** K.CC.B.4 (cardinality — earned ONLY via the quantity-start experience, where the counted set's cardinal IS the firefly's perch; struck from any digits-only round per the pedagogy ruling) · K.CC.B.4.C (one more — the +1 beat is literally "one more"). **A SIBLING alphabet A→B→C variant** ships as its OWN activity aligned **L.K.1.a + RF.K.1.d** primary (K.CC.A.2 is only the structural analog — neither standard launders the other); it is NOT counted toward this game's K.CC.A.2 experiences.
- **OUT OF SCOPE (pedagogy rulings, applied):** **skip-counting by 2s/5s/10s = 2.NBT drift — DROPPED.** **count-back — DROPPED** from the gated set. Both designers proposed these; the pedagogy authority struck them; not in the spine.
- **engine:** new `engine-connect-sequence.js` + `connect-sequence-core.js` (0 lines to any protected core; the reusable primitive = *a count-on beat that produces the successor by counting + a reveal-after-produce ordered trail*) · **runtime:** SIMPLE · **per-locale CHEAP for the number variants** (numerals universal; reuses `_NUMBER_WORD_HELPERS` TTS) — the alphabet sibling localizes its (per-locale) alphabet + the firefly's lines (NSR per locale).
- **The instantiation-fidelity call (the pedagogy's central ruling):** **blank dots ARE the standard.** A fully-labeled connect-the-dots is a numeral-matcher (read "8" → find the dot stamped "8" → zero counting). The given START is lit + labeled (k≠1); every other dot is DARK + unlabeled; the only way to know which dark dot is next is to count on. **The dot's number is an OUTPUT of the child's count, never an input.**
- **Distinctness — spine = COUNT ON FROM A GIVEN STAR to wake the next dark star (generative production; the thread draws as you count) → the constellation reveals.** vs the LIVE catalog neighbors: **collect-trade-08 Set-Completer** (K.CC.A.3 — place an ordered set into numbered frames, tap/drag); **hide-reveal-12 Treasure Map** (K.CC.A.2 — tap to reveal a hidden path via ordered clues; no drawing, no count-on production); **rhythm-sound-02 Echo the Beat** (K.CC.A.1 — Simon echo); Game 11 `draw-trace-09` (2.OA.C.4 partition — different engine); Game 14 Copy-the-Bead-String (K.CC.B.4 — replicate ORDER, no count-on production). Here the child PRODUCES each successor by counting + the thread draws — unique.

## 2. Concept & creative hook
A firefly — the **Lantern-Keeper** — has forgotten how to count past where she's standing, so the constellation animals sleep in the dark as unlit stars. **She can only fly where the child has already counted.** She perches, trembling, on the last lit star, looks out into the dark where the next star should be, and **waits.** The child counts her forward — one star at a time — and her luminous thread draws behind her. When the last star lights, the constellation wakes: the creature turns its head to look at the firefly, and the anxious little light **nestles onto it** (rides the deer's antler, sits in the owl's ear) — *you didn't draw a picture, you gave a lonely light someone to fly with.*
- **The blank dark is the pedagogy (the convergent spine):** only the given start is lit + labeled (k≠1; e.g. a glowing "**7**"). The dark stars carry NO numeral. The firefly's behavioral truth — *she's stuck; I free her one star at a time by counting* — is what a 5-year-old actually grasps (the "firefly is your memory" narration is garnish for the parent reading over the shoulder; the SPATIAL truth carries the game).
- **GENERATIVE production — the count-on beat (the structural rework, fix #1):** the child taps a big coral **"count on"** beat (or taps the firefly herself). Each tap **advances the count by one** — the numeral climbs and speaks ("…eight!"), the firefly **flies one star forward** along the path, and her thread draws behind her. To wake a target star the child counts the firefly up to it and **STOPS**. *Producing any number requires counting up to it from k* — there is no 4-set to pick from, **mashing overshoots** (the firefly flies PAST the target; she must be stopped AT the right star), and every production attempt is itself an enactment of count-on. This is simultaneously the anti-cheat AND the most premium, flowing input (count = forward-flight = the thread drawing itself — the beloved single-line dot-to-dot feel restored).
- **Wordless worked example FIRST (fix #6):** the very first screen teaches the rule with zero pressure — the firefly looks at the lit star, the count-on beat pulses, the child taps once, and a dark star **wakes "over there"** with a chime + the firefly flies to it, BEFORE any target is asked. The child FEELS "my count wakes a star" before it's ever tested. (Without this, the dark field reads as a broken connect-the-dots — "where are the numbers?")
- **Anti-generic statement:** NOT a numbered dot-to-dot worksheet — (a) the dots are DARK; the number is the child's count, not a label to match; (b) production is GENERATIVE (count the firefly forward; no set to brute-force; mashing overshoots); (c) **anti-proximity placement** (sequence order ≠ spatial order; the picture geometry does NOT leak the path — the silent second cheat); (d) start k≠1, always; (e) a board ships only if a **numeral-matcher solver**, a **nearest-unconnected (proximity) solver**, and an **exhaustion/overshoot solver** ALL fail the gate while a **successor-producing solver** passes (§8).

## 3. Core loop & the ≥7 distinct experiences
**Loop:** the firefly perches on the lit given star (k≠1) → the **count-on beat** pulses → the child taps to count the firefly forward (numeral climbs + speaks; firefly flies one dark star per correct beat; thread draws behind her) → on reaching the target star she locks it (it lights + twinkles); the next dark star is the new edge → repeat across the trail → on the final star the outline closes, color floods, the creature wakes + the firefly nestles onto it → `ctx.correct()`. **Overshoot/wrong stop = gentle non-stick** (the firefly drifts past into the dark, then gently glides back to the last lit star; soft non-buzzer tone; NO hot/cold, NO penalty, NO counter); the child counts again. **Fused, not stop-start:** the count IS the forward motion IS the drawing — one beat, no separate "now trace" chore.

**The ≥7 distinct experiences — HONEST re-count (fix #5): distinct = a different CHILD-ACT/REASONING, NOT a harder number, a different animal, or a re-skin.** The old "small-k / decade / audio-given" are ONE parameterized act (merged), not three. The genuinely distinct count-on child-acts:
| # | Experience (the distinct ACT / reasoning) | Render / act | Why it's distinct & non-guessable |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ★ | **Count on +1 from a given star** (parameterized: small k≠1 / **decade-crossing** 9→10, 19→20 / **audio-given** k the firefly hums) | one count-on beat from the lit k → wake the next star | the base successor act; internal variety (the decade prosody-break + the auditory-given modality are PARAMETERS, not separate experiences) |
| 2 ★ | **Count on by N to a named far star** ("wake the owl — 3 stars on") | tap the beat N times (8,9,10) and STOP at the target | produce a RUN + stop at N — a non-counter who mashes overshoots; the stop is the cardinality act |
| 3 ★ | **Fill the gap** (lit anchors on BOTH sides: 6 lit … 9 lit; wake the 7,8 between) | count on from the floor anchor; the count self-terminates at the ceiling anchor | count-on BOUNDED by a ceiling — a different reasoning than open-ended successor; the stop is self-determined by the count, not a label |
| 4 ★ | **Continue the firefly's count** (she counts "4… 5… 6…" aloud + stalls mid-run; the child continues 7, 8) | pick up an ONGOING count and extend it | the literal K.CC.A.2 verb — enter a running sequence and carry it forward |
| 5 ★ | **Read-k / mid-sequence resume** (the firefly starts OFF the anchors; several stars are pre-lit) | FIRST land her on the correct lit star (read which number she's resuming from), THEN count on | forces reading-k-from-state (fix #2c/#3) — the off-anchor start makes "which number do I count from" load-bearing; without it this collapses into #1 |
| 6 ★ | **Quantity-start** (the start is a SET of ••• pips, not a digit) | count the pips, STATE how many (the cardinal), that cardinal IS the firefly's perch → count on from it | earns K.CC.B.4 honestly — the counted cardinal is load-bearing (the woken-star count == the pip count), not a bolt-on (fix #3) |
| 7 ★ | **Path discrimination** (two threads leave one star; only the in-sequence next wakes) | count on; the correct next star lights, the decoy thread stays dark | count-on as CHOOSING the correct continuation — a different act than producing into open space |
| — | **Alphabet A→B→C** (sibling) | produce the next LETTER from a given letter | ships as its OWN activity, aligned L.K.1.a/RF.K.1.d primary — **NOT counted toward this game's 7** (different standard; per-locale alphabet, NSR) |

- **Honest framing (fix #5):** seven genuinely-distinct count-on child-acts (single-successor / by-N-run / bounded-fill / continue-a-running-count / read-k-first / count-a-set-then-on / discriminate-the-continuation), NOT difficulty/modality/animal re-skins. The variety gate asserts distinct by **child-act** and **REJECTS "harder N / different animal / audio-vs-visual-given alone" as a distinctness source** (§8, §A.13.60). The keypad-numeral input is a per-child config layer (numerate children type the successor), NOT a separate experience.
- **Free slice = wordless worked-example (intro) + count-on +1 (1) + count-on by N (2) + fill-the-gap (3)** — proves generative count-on + the bounded/run reasoning.

## 4. Win-states & juice (no competition)
No timer/score/streak/countdown/**fail-state**/attempts-counter/"X of Y". **Per-star wake:** the firefly flies forward + the star lights with a rising pentatonic pip (the count is audible AND musical — a child who can't read numerals HEARS the count climb), the thread draws its segment (`stroke-dashoffset` 1→0). **The reveal payoff (signature):** the closed outline floods with color from the firefly outward; the creature wakes, turns to look, and the firefly **nestles onto it**; `ctx.speak()` names the creature in-language. **Non-numeric vessel = the FILLING NIGHT SKY:** each woken constellation joins a cumulative night-sky **scrapbook** (`GameCollection`) — the sky gains a star-friend per creature; when full, **dawn breaks** softly. No bar, no "3 of 7." **Negative signal = ABSENCE + a gentle process-nudge ONLY:** an overshoot/wrong-stop → the firefly drifts into the dark + glides back to the last lit star, a soft "…not yet" shimmer on HER (not on the next dot — fix below), no buzzer/red/counter. **`ctx.wrong()` is a soft, information-free state.** **The post-error scaffold re-teaches the PROCESS, never the product (fix #4 — the cohort leak in audio form):** it models the count-on ACTION ("let's count on together — seven…") and **STOPS before the target**, handing the next beat to the child — it MUST NOT speak `symbols[expectedNext]` while that token is the live target (no "after seven comes eight" that the child parrots). It escalates *support* (slower, more modeling of the +1 beat) but never the answer. **Produce-then-glow ordering:** before the child has counted, NO dark dot glows (a pre-glow would re-create the numeral-matcher); only the count-on beat pulses; any dot-locating hint is delayed (long idle) + surfaces the *next number as a question* ("…seven?"), never spotlights the next star. Full-pass keepsake = the filled night-sky scrapbook → cross-game `GameCollection`. CSS/SVG/WebAudio; reduced-motion; mute-safe.

## 5. Pedagogy
- **K.CC.A.2 instantiation:** the child enters the sequence at an arbitrary given k (≠1) and produces successors by COUNTING ON — the generative count-on beat makes "produce 8" inseparable from "count 7→8." Blank dark dots remove the numeral-matcher's input entirely.
- **THE KEYSTONE RULES (non-guessability):** (i) **BLANK DOTS** — only the given start is labeled (k≠1); every other dot is dark + unlabeled; the next dot's identity is recoverable ONLY by counting on. (ii) **GENERATIVE PRODUCTION (fix #1)** — the count-on beat produces each number by counting up from k; there is NO finite key-containing set to exhaust; mashing overshoots (the child must STOP at the target); every production attempt enacts count-on. (iii) **ANTI-PROXIMITY PLACEMENT (the silent second cheat, fix #2)** — sequence order ≠ spatial order; the picture geometry must NOT make "next in sequence" = "nearest unconnected dot"; a pure nearest-dot solver must complete **0% of decks**. (iv) **START k≥2, NEVER 1** — a start of 1 is K.CC.A.1 (rote); arbitrary entry is the whole standard. (v) **PRODUCE-THEN-GLOW** — no dark dot glows before the child has counted to it (a pre-glow IS the matcher cheat).
- **Number ranges + rigor (pedagogy):** known-sequence ceiling **20**; count-on within **1–20**; given-starts ≥2 with deliberate spread (small 3–4 AND larger 11–14); **FEATURE the decade crossings (9→10, 19→20) — the prosody break where genuine successor knowledge is tested** (experience #1's decade parameter forces the crossing mid-trail by construction, fix #3); **5–8 count-on steps per trail**; sparse re-anchoring every ~5 dots allowed (a scaffold to recover place without shame, NOT the path; between anchors the child counts on across ≥4 blank dots; never >10 consecutive blank for K).
- **Cardinality (K.CC.B.4):** earned ONLY via experience #6 — the child counts a pip SET, STATES the cardinal, and that cardinal is the firefly's perch (count-on then proceeds from it). A digits-only board does NOT instantiate B.4 (struck).
- **Alphabet sibling:** "produce the next letter from a given letter" is a genuine structural parallel (successor-from-an-arbitrary-entry-point over the alphabetic ordering) — aligned to **L.K.1.a/RF.K.1.d as PRIMARY**, same blank-successor + generative defenses; NOT laundered through K.CC.A.2.
- **No-shame:** the overshoot non-stick is neutral + silent (also the trial-and-error defense — no hot/cold oracle); the reveal is the intrinsic reward (no score/timer/streak); the post-error scaffold re-runs the COUNTING PROCESS, never hands the answer.

## 6. Interaction & UX
- **Runtime: the GENERATIVE count-on beat (fused with the draw).** Canonical = a large coral **"count on"** button (or tap the firefly): each tap → `commitCountOn()` advances the internal count by one, renders+speaks the numeral, flies the firefly one dark-star-position forward along the path, and extends the SVG thread (`<polyline>` snaps dot-center→dot-center; live ink trails between). Reaching the target star (produced value === target order) locks it. **Mashing overshoots** (the firefly flies past; she must be at the target value when the beat lands). The **keypad** (`answerType:number`) is the numerate-child alternative (type the successor → same `commitProduction` path). A **drag-the-firefly** path is a secondary input (drag forward; she advances a star only as the count advances).
- **State separation (the anti-cheat mechanism):** `order` is INTERNAL-only — dark dots carry a random `data-dot-id`, NEVER `data-order`; the engine holds a private `Map<domId,order>`. `getRenderableState()` deliberately OMITS `armedOrder` + the order map + the unlabeled dots' values. The thread connects a star ONLY when the produced count equals its order. A solver reading the DOM/pixels finds no answer.
- **The continuous-trail feel (finger-robust @280px):** SVG `viewBox 0 0 1000 1000` CSS-scaled; each star = a visual `<circle r≈22>` + an invisible hit-halo `<circle>` sized from the LIVE css width so the effective target stays **≥44px** at every breakpoint (recompute on `ResizeObserver`); `getScreenCTM().inverse()` for screen→viewBox (guard `if(!ctm)return`); `setPointerCapture`; rAF-throttled ink; `touch-action:none` + `overscroll-behavior:contain`. The thread trails the firefly with a 1-frame lerp + slight sag (pulled luminous string, not a ruler).
- **The gentle non-stick + the hint discipline:** overshoot/wrong-stop → the firefly glides back to the last lit star (~180ms), soft non-buzzer tone, no line drawn to the wrong place, NO hot/cold. Before production: only the **count-on beat** pulses (never a dark dot). After correct production: the matching star may glow as a "you found it" affordance (it's already earned — not a cheat). Idle-hint (long idle) surfaces the next number as a QUESTION, never the dot's location.
- **Two-stage where needed (shell `stage`, no inter-stage auto-advance):** for read-k (#5) the firefly-landing is a `stage:'pick-anchor'` before `stage:'count-on'`; for quantity-start (#6) a `stage:'count-set'` (state the cardinal) precedes count-on. The single-successor experiences are ONE fused beat (no chop).
- **Night panel = a framed deep-teal inset INSIDE the cream Direction-A card** (reconciles the constellation's dark field with Direction-A — not a full dark theme).
- **Mobile @280px (truth-tested, fix #6):** vertical stack — prompt strip / **the night-panel SVG is the HERO (most height), aspect-1** / count-on beat row (real-DOM numeral, ≥44px) / firefly vessel (a THIN strip, not a square). **Dot budget is responsive + generation-enforced:** **≤9 dots at ≤360px, ≤12 at ≥600px** (the 240×240 night-panel at 280px holds ~8–9 stars with non-overlapping ≥44px hit-halos AND the anti-proximity geometry — 12 would overlap or clip; the constellation art is authored to the budget so the reveal is legible, never a postage stamp). **Non-overlapping ≥44px halos are a GENERATION-TIME constraint** (the generator FAILS to place a field it can't satisfy — not just an audit). Pass `audit-game-mobile.js` 280–768 empty (no stars lit) + filled (all lit + reveal): assert no two hit-halos overlap AND every halo ≥44px at every width.
- **Accessibility:** TTS speaks the count-on ("…eight!") reusing `_NUMBER_WORD_HELPERS`; `ctx.announce()` mirrors every state ("you counted to eight — a star woke up!" / "not yet — count on from seven" / "you woke a fox!"); keyboard path = the count-on beat is a button (Enter/Space = count on), the keypad types the numeral, the armed star is focusable (Enter connects) — full no-pointer path that still requires counting; not color-only (lit stars differ by shape-state + glow, not hue alone); ≥44px; reduced-motion (instant segments, no twinkle loop) + mute-safe (visual draw carries it).

## 8. Runtime mapping + MEASURED build-gate + PER-LOCALE
