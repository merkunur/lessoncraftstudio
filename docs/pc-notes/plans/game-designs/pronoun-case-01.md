## §1 — Concept / world
**The Borrowed Hat.** Pim, a tiny round shopkeeper-owl, runs a Lost-and-Found Hat Shop and can never remember who-did-what. Each character is ONE puppet with its identity (gender/number) FIXED and visible — the puppet IS "Fox" (a he), "the twins" (a they), "you" (the player). A sentence-strip slides onto Pim's counter with a hat-shaped blank ("Mom hugged ▢ at the door."). The child gives Pim the right pronoun-hat to send the borrowed character home. The whole point made physical: **the same Fox needs a DIFFERENT hat depending on his JOB in the sentence — "he" when he's DOING, "him" when it's DONE to him, "his" when he OWNS** — and the child must READ the sentence to figure out Fox's job (the puppet's identity tells you nothing about which hat). Non-numeric abundance vessel: a tall **Hat Wall** behind Pim fills bottom-up across a session with a cheerful gallery of uniquely-styled returned hats (never a count/score). `ctx.correct()` auto-advances; no Check button.

## §2 — The standard + the load-bearing cognition
**L.1.1.d (Grade 1, SOLE) — use personal/possessive pronouns correctly.** Pedagogy ruling: NOT thin but SINGLE-CORED — the genuine cognition is **personal-pronoun CASE by SYNTACTIC ROLE** (subject I/he/she/they/we vs object me/him/her/them/us vs possessive my/his/her/their), parsed from who-is-DOING vs who-is-DONE-TO. Case IS Grade-1-real — the canonical persistent errors are objective-for-subjective ("Me and him went," "Her is nice"), esp. in compounds (Rispoli). **The critic's re-center (what is actually GRADEABLE):** simple-SVO case ("___ runs" → he) is solved by native EAR ("him runs" sounds wrong) with ZERO role cognition → SCAFFOLD, not graded. The genuine non-ear, non-position, non-gender grade lives in THREE veins: **(1) compound SUBJECT case** (Sam and ___ → I), **(2) compound OBJECT case** (hugged Sam and ___ → me) [the ear FAILS on compounds — that's why the standard is teachable], and **(3) 3rd-person FORM-BY-FUNCTION** (him↔his, them↔their) [the ONLY axis that is BOTH non-ear AND drop-trick-immune — the function, not a conjunct, sets the form]. All words = real DOM/SVG `<text>`. **FENCES:** gender/number AGREEMENT (the clever-hans — SCAFFOLD); the ROLE-CUE pre-reveal (the affordance-leak — post-commit only); simple-SVO (ear-solvable — scaffold); predicate-nominatives ("It was he/him" — too hard, even adults say "him" — EXCLUDE); gender-readable 3rd-person possessives as load-bearing (gender leak); anaphora (wrong standard — RL/RI; EXCLUDE); indefinite-pronoun agreement (above Grade 1). Do NOT penalize singular "they". **TIGHT scope (operator-ruled): the 3 veins + ear/scaffold warmup, NO padding** ("ship-tight beats ship-thin-padded" — padding with ear-solvable items IS the Game-78 thinness failure).

## §3 — THE ARCHITECTURE REWORK (the heart; the fatal leak + two cheats + the re-center)
**FATAL — the ROLE-CUE is an affordance-leak that ALSO collapses to the gender clever-hans.** The load-bearing cognition is PARSING the sentence to determine doer-vs-done-to. If the game PRE-RENDERS the role — a cape-hook/mitt-hook, a doer→done-to ARROW, a labeled doing-stage/getting-chair, or any pre-glowing "doer slot" — it does that parse FOR the child (transcription, not cognition). Worse: role is one bit, features the other, and together they DETERMINE the pronoun; **give the role and the ONLY remaining decision is the FEATURES = pure gender/number AGREEMENT** (the exact clever-hans we demoted). The gender-mutation probe still "passes" in the abstract (subject↔object distance is gender-independent) but the CHILD never traverses that distance — they were handed the role and walked only the gender step. A gate that doesn't model "role pre-rendered on screen" scores this clean while children solve by gender.
**CHEAT 2 — simple-SVO is native-EAR.** "___ runs" → he, "Pat sees ___" → him are acoustically obvious to a native 6-year-old without any role concept → grading them measures EAR, not cognition.
**CHEAT 3 — binary-place FLAIL (Game-77/80).** Placing the character into stage(subject)/chair(object) is a 50% guess (33% with possessive); and ANY pre-commit differential response to a wrong drop (glow, snap-back, won't-latch) lets the child run the motor loop (try stage, if rejected try chair).

**THE FIX — RE-CENTER onto the role the child DERIVES, grading only ear-resistant items:**
- **WITHHOLD the role-cue until POST-COMMIT.** Pre-commit the child sees ONLY: the sentence, the fixed-identity character, the case-form choice, and **visually-NEUTRAL identical placement targets** (a plain platform — NOT a doer-shaped hook). No arrow, no labels, no glow. The role re-teach (the doing-stage/getting-chair, the "Mom is doing it; Fox is getting hugged" arrow) appears ONLY post-commit. If the child must SEE the role to act, the role is scaffold; if they must INFER it, the role is the grade.
- **HARD DISCRETE single commit.** Placement is one irreversible commit — NO drop-rejection, snap-back, hover-preview, or per-posture pre-commit feedback (any differential response is a flail-tell). Feedback + role-reteach come AFTER.
- **GRADE ONLY EAR-RESISTANT ITEMS.** Load-bearing = the 3 veins (compound-subject, compound-object, 3rd-person form-by-function). Simple-SVO + 1st/2nd-person possessive = SCAFFOLD tier (ungraded warmup, ear-solvable by design). **The gate FAILS a build that grades ear-solvable simple-SVO.**
- **TWO-TILE SAME-REFERENT BOARD.** Exactly {correct-case, wrong-case} of ONE referent, gender/number/person CONSTANT across the two tiles (differ ONLY in case: {I, me}, {he, him}, {they, them}, {his, him}). Any third different-gender/number tile reopens the gender cheat → forbidden on graded items. The discriminating axis is CASE alone.
- **PROMOTE form-by-function (him↔his, them↔their) to a PRIMARY graded vein** — the compound-drop trick ("drop the other person: 'I went' not 'me went'") is the standard's OWN taught strategy (an ACCEPTABLE path, do NOT block it) but it re-collapses to ear-on-the-isolate, so compound-case can't be the SOLE grade; form-by-function is non-ear AND drop-immune (the function sets him-vs-his, no single-pronoun-drop disambiguates).
- **Defeat COMPOUND_SLOT_ECHO + PERSON-PRIMING (critic-added):** vary conjunct ORDER ("Sam and ___" vs "___ and Sam") + randomize which member is the blank (else the child learns "blank-after-and → I" = a compound-level position-heuristic); + referent VARIETY so case isn't trackable by rhythm.
- **child-output STRICTLY before game-output** (`ANSWER_NOT_PRE_REVEALED`); the referent is NAMED in the sentence but NEVER highlighted/boxed/linked (no spotlight); the child commits before any feedback.

## Cohort doctrine this game adds
**The ROLE-CUE-LEAK-COLLAPSES-TO-THE-DEMOTED-CHEAT + GRADE-THE-GATE-ON-THE-RENDERED-SCREEN + the NATIVE-EAR-IS-NOT-COGNITION lesson — PROGRAM-WIDE.** Three linked findings. **(1) A pre-rendered "what kind of slot this is" CUE collapses the grade back onto the very cheat you demoted.** When the answer = ROLE × FEATURES and you demote FEATURES (gender-agreement) to scaffold, a cue that hands the ROLE leaves FEATURES as the only remaining decision — so the child solves by the demoted cheat. The hard, gradeable half is DERIVING the structural classification (here: parse doer-vs-done-to) from the stimulus; any UI element that pre-shows it (an arrow, a role-shaped slot, a labeled bin, a pre-glowing target) is an affordance-leak that REINSTATES the demoted axis. → WITHHOLD the structural cue until POST-COMMIT (re-teach only); pre-commit the child sees only the raw stimulus + the choice + NEUTRAL targets. **(2) NEW gate-design law — model the RENDERED PRE-COMMIT SCREEN, not just the descriptor.** A `ROLE_AFFORDANCE_READ` solver must consume what's ON SCREEN before commit; a gate that only reasons over the data structure scores a screen-leaked game CLEAN while children read the leak. (Beside Game-81's SMART-false-pass guard + Game-83's misconception-foil-must-be-present — the gate's honesty depends on arming it with the cheat the child can actually run, including PIXELS.) **(3) A native-EAR-solvable item is NOT cognition — grade only where the ear FAILS.** For a language standard, the cases the native child already gets by ear ("him runs" sounds wrong) measure the ear, not the target skill; the genuine grade lives exactly where the ear is unreliable (compounds break the acoustic pattern → "Sam and me went" is the common error → the teachable target) and on the axis the standard's OWN taught shortcut can't crack (form-by-function him↔his is drop-immune; the drop-and-test trick is a sanctioned path but re-collapses to ear, so it can't be the sole grade). The sub-lesson: a standard's own pedagogical TRICK (drop-the-conjunct) is an acceptable solve PATH — don't block it — but if the trick re-collapses to a demoted cheat (ear), you need a second load-bearing vein the trick can't reach. Generalizes to EVERY classification/structural-role standard (grammatical case/role, part-of-speech-by-function, syntactic-bracketing, sentence-element labeling) and every language standard with an ear/intuition component: withhold the structural cue to post-commit, arm the gate on the rendered screen, grade only where the native intuition fails, and find the axis the standard's own shortcut can't crack. (Lineage: the role-cue-collapse sharpens Game-82's positional-transcription [there the symbol's token-order leaked; here the slot-TYPE leaks] + Game-76/80/83's affordance-leak [the cue hands the answer] — and uniquely shows the leak RE-INSTATES a demoted cheat; the rendered-screen gate is a NEW assert; native-ear-is-not-cognition is the language-standard analog of Game-78's owned-op/Game-81's irreducibility [the cheap path is INNATE, not owned].)
