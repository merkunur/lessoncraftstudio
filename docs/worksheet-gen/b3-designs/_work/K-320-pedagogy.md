**Boundary (load-bearing).** The child reads or writes an ordinal NUMERAL (`3rd` / `3.` / `3e` / `3:e` / `3.º`) against a LINE-UP of distinct pictures whose START and DIRECTION are marked by a flag. K-064..067 (`position-words.js`) own spatial RELATIONS (on/under, left/right, rows, inside) where the arrangement is the answer and no order exists; G1-308 F3 owns the ordinal WORD inside a full imperative over a MIXED strip with repeats ("cross out the second dog" = the 2nd of the dogs); G2-277 owns date ordinals on a month grid; K-019..023 (`number-strip.js`, `neighbors.js`) own CARDINAL sequences; K-052 owns an up/down pattern. No face here prints a relation word, a full noun sentence, a date or a cardinal strip.

**Gender rule (locks the whole type).** An ordinal word or numeral NEVER stands in the same phrase, tile or chip as a picture noun. Base/F4: the cue chip carries the numeral only. F2: words are citation-form position labels (masc. sg in es/pt/it/fr) in a bank row, never beside a picture. F3/F5: the child's numeral answers a frame whose HEAD noun is fixed and panel-chosen with a stable gender (es/pt `lugar` m, it `posto` m, fr `rang` m, de `Stelle`, nl `plaats`, sv `plats`, da `plads`, no `plass`, fi `monesko`), so the correct notation is one string per n (es `3.º`, fr `1er`); the picture noun's gender never enters. 11-20 words are NOT stored (no face needs them).

## B. The six faces

Reading direction: all 11 locales LTR; the coral flag is the ONLY start cue, drawn on the row (never a printed "1"). Reused: `cardGrid`, `answerBox` (`components.js:105`), `.ws-lane .ws-icon .ws-achip .ws-answerbox .ws-card`, `fileUri`, `entriesFor`. NEW in `components-b3.js`: `lineUp({theme, nouns[], tile=80, pic=64, gap=6, start:'left'|'right', target?})` (the G1-308 `pictureStrip` idiom + a `start` side; NO band, NO numerals) · `ordinalChip({text, px=30})` (teal-bordered white chip 84x56, Baloo 2 700, `data-lcs-ask`) · `raceLane({noun, x, laneW, finish:true})`. NEW `lib/b3-common.js ordinalFor(loc, n)` (README names it) reads `data/b3/ordinals.js` tables.

### Base: Ordinal Numbers: Circle the Picture in Its Place (K-320, PARAM ladder)
**Move:** READ the ordinal numeral and count from the flag to the matching picture (the notation is the teaching point; the words stay oral, the teacher reads). **Child:** 5 rows; each = `[chip 84][12][flag 22][6][6 tiles 80, pic 64, gap 6]` = 634 <= 675 *est.*; circles the picture at the chip's place. Rows `minmax(108px,1fr)` x 5 + 4 x 12 = 588 <= 722 (README floor) *est.*; pic 64 >= K 56 (`_tokens.js:68`); 5 items in [4,8]. Instruction: "Start at the flag. Circle the picture that is in the place the number shows." **d2 params:** `{rows:5, len:6, start:'left', ask:'distinct'}` (5 distinct of 1..6 per page, so 1st or 6th is always asked); d1 `{rows:4, len:5}`; d3 `{rows:5, len:8, pic:56}` (8 x 62 + 7 x 5 = 531 *est.*). Pictures per row = a distinct sample of the theme (may repeat ACROSS rows). **verify():** every row stamps `data-lcs-lineup="a,b,c,..."` (all distinct), `data-lcs-start`, `data-lcs-ask` (1 <= ask <= len); the tile at `start==='left' ? ask-1 : len-ask` carries `data-lcs-target` and no other does; asked values distinct across rows; no tile carries text or `alt`; chip text === `ordinalFor(loc, ask)` (node-side re-derive); every `img.complete && naturalWidth > 0`; no localized BW marker in any path. **Query face:** the bare head.

### F1: Write the Ordinal Numbers 1st to 8th (G1-3xx, CODE `mode:'write'`)
**Move:** PRODUCE the notation in sequence (the de "Ordnungszahlen bis 10 schreiben" / es "1º al 10º" head); the suffix system (st/nd/rd, 1:a/3:e, º) is the learning. **Child:** 3 blocks; block = line-up of 8 (tile 76, pic 60 >= 44) with the flag + a box row under it (`answerBox` 60x44 x 8, gap 12 = 564); 4 boxes per block PRE-PRINTED with the notation (inkSoft, the pattern) and 4 blank; blank never at position 1 (anchor) and never 4 in a row. 3 x 148 + 2 x 16 = 476 <= 722 *est.*; 12 blanks = G1 ceiling. d1 2 blocks x 6, 3 blanks; **d2 3 x 8, 4 blanks**; d3 2 x 10 (tile 60, pic 48), 6 blanks (1st..10th). **verify():** each blank `data-lcs-answer === ordinalFor(loc, idx+1)`; pre-printed text likewise; blanks count per block; position 1 never blank. **Query face:** "write" + "1st to 10th".

### F2: Ordinal Words: Match the Word to the Picture (G1-3xx, CODE `mode:'words'`)
**Move:** DECODE the ordinal WORD (first/third) and locate its picture: word -> position -> picture. **Child:** 2 blocks; block = line-up of 8 (flag) + 60 px line zone + a shuffled row of 5 word chips (`.ws-achip` h 44, Baloo 2 700 22, `data-lcs-word-n`); the child draws a line from each word UP to its picture. 5 chips x <= 110 + 4 x 8 <= 647 *est.* (widest words fi `seitsemäs`/`kahdeksas` 9 glyphs, pt `primeiro` 8; validator caps 10 glyphs); 2 x ~200 + 16 <= 722; 10 lines in [6,12]. d1 2 x 6, 4 words; **d2 2 x 8, 5 distinct words of 1..8, >= 1 of 7th/8th per page**; d3 2 x 10, 6 words. **verify():** each word chip's n <= len, distinct per block, chip text === `words[n].m`; target = tile at n-1; chips never adjacent to a tile (separate row, `data-lcs-bank`); >= 1 crossing pair per block (chip order != position order). **Query face:** "ordinal words" / "Ordnungszahlen Wörter" / "palabras ordinales" / "rangtelwoorden lezen" / "ordningstal ord".

### F3: Who Is Where? Write the Place (G1-3xx, CODE `mode:'where'`)
**Move:** the INVERSE of the base: a picture is named, the child writes its position. **Child:** one line-up of 8 (tile 76) at the top; below, `cardGrid({cols:2, rows:3})` 6 cards, card = the locale frame with an INLINE picture (48 px, the G2-277 sticker idiom: no noun, no article) + `answerBox` 72x44: en "Which place is [pic] in? [ ]" · de "An welcher Stelle steht [pic]? [ ]." · fi "Monesko [pic] on? [ ]" · fr "Quel est le rang de [pic] ? [ ]" · es "¿En qué lugar está [pic]? [ ]". 6 of 8 positions asked (distinct); cards 3 x ~190 + line-up 100 <= 722 *est.* d1 line-up 6, 4 cards; **d2 8, 6 cards**; d3 two line-ups of 8 (flag left/right), 6 cards each referencing its line-up by a colour tag. **verify():** card `data-lcs-noun` occurs exactly once in the line-up; `data-lcs-answer === ordinalFor(loc, idx+1)`; asked positions distinct; frame contains exactly one `{pic}`; es/pt/it/fr answer uses the frame's `headGender` form. **Query face:** "which place" / "an welcher Stelle" / "en qué lugar" / "monesko".

### F4: Start at the Right: Ordinals from the Other Side (K-3xx, PARAM `start:'mixed'`)
**Move:** the REFERENCE POINT is the whole task: the same chips, the flag now sits on the RIGHT with the arrow pointing left, and rows alternate sides so the child must read the flag before counting (the de "von rechts / von links" and nl "van links / van rechts" teaching point). **Config** `{...base.difficulty[2], start:'mixed'}`: 5 rows, >= 2 rows each side, never the same side 3 times running; chip column stays left. d1 `start:'right'` all rows (4 x 5); d3 8-tile rows mixed. **verify():** base rules + `start` takes both values with count >= 2, target index = `len - ask` on right rows. **Query face:** "from the right" / "von rechts" / "desde la derecha" / "van rechts" / "från höger". K band: same receptive act as the base; the panel may note G1 in its landing prose but the level key is K.

### F5: Who Wins the Race? Write the Place (G1-3xx, CODE `mode:'race'`)
**Move:** ORDER BY DISTANCE, not by left-right position: five runners on five lanes at different distances from a chequered finish line on the right; the closest is 1st (de "Wettlauf / Wer gewinnt?", the universal race page). **Child:** 5 lanes h 100 (`raceLane`): `[answerBox 60x44][12][dotted track 500][finish strip 20]`; the runner (pic 64) at x drawn from 5 bins with >= 40 px separation; the child writes 1st..5th in each lane's box. 5 x 100 + 4 x 12 = 548 <= 722 *est.* d1 4 lanes, gaps >= 80; **d2 5 lanes**; d3 6 lanes. Themes = `racers` only. **verify():** `data-lcs-x` distinct with pairwise gap >= 40; rank by descending x; `data-lcs-answer === ordinalFor(loc, rank)`; exactly one finish strip; no two lanes share a noun. Art facing is NOT gated (no `facing` table exists, m; `image-cache/silhouette.js` is shape-only); the critic reads the contact sheet; a `facing:{noun:'left'}` mirror override is an additive knob. **Query face:** "race" / "Wettlauf" / "carrera" / "course" / "tävling" / "kilpailu".

**Rejected non-moves.** Theme swap · "colour the 3rd" on colour art (cannot be coloured; a `bwOnly` twin of the base on the 9 cached BW themes is the same move on other art, NOT a face) · ordinals to 20th (a range; 20 pictures cannot reach the 44 px floor) · calendar ordinals (G2-277) · "the second cat" over repeats (G1-308 F3) · left/right/between (K-064..067) · a podium with numbered steps (the numerals print the answer) · "3" <-> "3rd" symbol match (no line-up) · counting backwards from the flag (K-023) · d1/d3 relabelled.

## D. Data + gates

`data/b3/ordinals.js`, GENERATED from `i18n/.draft-b3-<loc>.json` by `tools/apply-b3-locale.js` (the `apply-b2-locale.js` pattern; `data/` gitignored, `git add -f`):
```
ORDINALS[loc] = {
  notation: { 1:'1.º', 2:'2.º', ..., 10:'10.º' },          // explicit table, never a pattern
  notationF: { 1:'1.ª', ... } | null,                       // es pt it fr(1:'1re' only) else null
  alt: { 1:['1.er'] },                                      // accepted alternates, never printed
  words: [{ n:1, m:'primero', f:'primera' }, ... n:10],     // f null where invariant
  where: { frame:'¿En qué lugar está {pic}?', head:'lugar', headGender:'m'|'f'|null },
  strings: { 'K-320':{title,instruction}, F1..F5:{title,instruction} } }
```
`lib/b3-common.js ordinalFor(loc, n, gender='m')` returns `notation[n]` (or `notationF[n]` when `gender==='f'`); throws outside 1..10. `calendar.js ordinal()` is NOT called by this type; where the two coincide (en, dot, nl) the validator cross-checks them and reports every divergence (expected: es pt it fr sv).

**`tools/validate-b3-draft.js` (ordinals block; exit 1 on any):** (1) `notation` has exactly keys 1..10, distinct, each contains `String(n)`, none contains U+00B0 (degree) or `ème`/`ième`; sv 1 and 2 end `:a`, 3..10 `:e`; nl end `e`; dot locales end `.`; en end st/nd/rd/th correctly; es/pt/it contain U+00BA (and `notationF` U+00AA). (2) `words` 1..10, `/^\p{L}+$/u` (de may carry a capital only with `capital:true`), distinct, <= 10 glyphs (F2 chip width), `f` present for all 10 in es/pt/it and for 1 in fr, null elsewhere; fr `words[2].m === 'deuxième'`. (3) `where.frame` has exactly one `{pic}`, ends `?`, `head` non-empty; es/pt/it/fr `headGender` declared. (4) titles <= 70, no worksheet-word, unique in band; instructions <= 150, no `{noun}`, no relation word from the position-words bank. (5) theme fan set: every theme has >= 10 safe nouns in the locale (m: all 10 pass; floor is pets 19).

**`qa/verify-b3-ordinal-numbers.js`:** renders face x 11 x fan themes at d2 under a 3-line title + 150-char instruction (722); `verify()` empty; `qa/lints.js` clean; icons >= 56 (K faces) / 44 (G1); boxes/chips >= 44 high; 20-seed sweep: base asked set covers 1..6 across seeds, F4 both sides every seed, F2 chip order never equals position order, F5 ranks not constant. **Poison (each must FAIL; the correct draft is the control):** P1 a line-up `cat,dog,cat` (duplicate picture) · P2 `ask:7` on a 6-picture row · P3 a row with `data-lcs-start` removed / no flag element · P4 F4 page with every flag on the left · P5 F5 two runners 24 px apart · P6 F2 a word chip placed inside a tile · P7 sv notation `1:e` · P8 es notation `3°` (degree sign) · P9 theme `zoo animals bw` · P10 base with two rows asking `3` · P11 F1 position 1 blank · P12 F3 a card naming a noun absent from the line-up. Page reads at render: `data/b3/ordinals.js[loc]`, `fileUri`, `entriesFor`; never `image-vocabulary.js`, `calendar.js` or `approved-words-*.json`.
