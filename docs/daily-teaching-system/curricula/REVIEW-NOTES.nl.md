# Reviewers' notes: registry `nl`

Written by the reviewers of the crosswalk (`scripts/curricula/apply-review.js`). 306 teaching points carry a reviewer's row; the reason for each is in `frontend/content/curricula/nl/crosswalk-authored.json`.

## author-all

- Kerndoelen describe the end of primary school and place nothing in a group, and most parts are broad. Nearly all 160 math points therefore land on six components (K10.A, K10.B, K11.A, K12.A, K13.A, K14.A); the point letter that decides is always given in the 'why'. A Dutch teacher will expect a finer grain (leerlijnen, tussendoelen per groep, e.g. the SLO aanbodsdoelen), which this registry does not hold.
- The new kerndoelen Nederlands in PART 2 do not name aanvankelijk lezen, klank-letterkoppeling, technisch lezen or fonemisch bewustzijn as such. I placed groep-2 sound and letter work as 'prepares' K2.A (point a 'aandachtig vloeiend lezen') and groep-3 decoding as 'teaches' K2.A; spelling goes to K6.B, word build and grammar terms to K6.A. Vocabulary has no kerndoel of its own, only K2.A point b.
- Placed in another year than Dutch schools do: reading and building words, sight words, addition and subtraction facts to 20 and number bonds (your K) are groep 3, not groep 2 (groep 2 does sounds, letters and counting only). Mental sums to 100 and bonds to 100 (your grade 1) are groep 4. Numbers and sums to 1000 and the column method (your grade 2) are groep 5-6; Dutch methods teach rijgen, splitsen and kolomsgewijs rekenen before cijferen. Written fractions (your grades 2-3) start in groep 6; groep 4-5 only say 'de helft' and 'een kwart'. Right angles, moon phases, planets and continents come in groep 6-8.
- The opposite also occurs: verbonden schrift (cursive) is taught from groep 3 in the schools that use it, so cursive at your grade 2-3 is late; and the tables of multiplication are groep 4-5 (your grade 3 is groep 5).
- English-only content that needs a Dutch counterpart, not a translation: sh/ch/th digraphs (Dutch: sch, ch, ng, nk and the tweetekenklanken oe, ie, eu, ui, ei, ij, au, ou); the spelling rules ck/k, tch/ch, -dge/-ge, ai/ay (Dutch: open and gesloten lettergreep, ei/ij, au/ou, -d/-t and -g/-ch by lengthening the word, verkleinwoorden, meervoud -en/-s/-'s); a/an (Dutch: de/het); 'sight words' (not a Dutch notion; nearest are hoogfrequente woorden / flitswoorden); inches and feet in 2:choose-units; the four food groups (Dutch: Schijf van Vijf with five vakken).
- Clock reading in Dutch differs in substance, not only in words: 'half vier' is 3:30, and five-minute times are read around the half hour ('tien voor half vier' = 3:20, 'vijf over half vier' = 3:35). The clock points (1:time-read, 1:time-set-hands, 2:time-5-min-quarter) must be made that way for Dutch schools.
- Content of groep 2-5 that teachers will look for and no teaching point covers: verkleinwoorden; the open/gesloten-lettergreep spelling rule and the long/short vowel; werkwoordspelling in the present tense (stam + t); begrijpend lezen of zakelijke teksten beyond the animal sheets; topografie of the Netherlands (provinces, the map of the own region, groep 5-6); the Tijd domain of groep 5 (tijdvakken 'jagers en boeren', 'Grieken en Romeinen', KD52/KD53) and the Dutch water theme (KD48); money sums in euros and cents as a line of its own; verhoudingen (K10.C) has no point, which is right for these years.
- Kerndoelen in PART 2 with no teaching point at all (rows were always put on a part, so a whole kerndoel such as K10 counts as used when one of its parts is): K1, K4, K5, K7.A, K8 (school duties, conversation, reflection, reading preference — not worksheet content), K2.C (reliability of sources), K10.C, K15.C (algoritmes), K17, K18.B, KD36, KD37, KD38, KD45, KD48, KD49, KD52, KD53, KD55, KD56.
- Points whose skill line was hard to place: K:family and 2:family-relationships (no kerndoel names the family; placed on vocabulary/reading only); K:transport-flying and K:community-helpers (only a thin 'prepares' on KD47); 2:line-plots and 3:line-plot-halves (the line plot is hardly used in Dutch methods; placed as a diagram under K12.A); 2:mystery-animal (reading task with animal content). Five puzzle points have no rows: K:mazes, 1:mazes, 1:find-the-differences, 1:picture-analogies, 1:secret-code.
- What looks incomplete in the registry itself: (1) the Besluit kerndoelen 2026 as I know it also lays down kerndoelen burgerschap and digitale geletterdheid; neither is in PART 2, and the feelings, wants-and-needs and community-helper points would sit more naturally there than on KD34/KD35/KD47 — please check also whether KD36-KD38 of 2006 are still in force beside new burgerschap kerndoelen. (2) Of the 2006 set only Oriëntatie op jezelf en de wereld and Kunstzinnige oriëntatie are present; Engels (kerndoelen 13-16), Friese taal and Bewegingsonderwijs (57-58) are absent, which matters for the bilingual word cards. (3) The source line reads 'Staatsblad 2026, 198 (Besluit van 10 juni 2026,)' with a stray comma, so the citation looks cut off; I could not check the Staatsblad number. (4) Every objective carries 'years 1-8', which is correct for kerndoelen but means the registry gives no year for anything.
- Strand names: besides the three changes in strandNames, two shown names are translation calques a Dutch teacher would not use, though PART 2 has no heading to prove another: 'Getallen en het tientallig stelsel' (teachers say 'Getallen en bewerkingen' or 'Getalbegrip') and the bracket in 'Lezen: technisch lezen (fundamentele leesvaardigheden)' ('Technisch lezen' alone is the Dutch name). 'Begrijpend lezen: informatieve teksten' is common usage; the new kerndoelen say 'zakelijke teksten' (K2.A).
