# What a Swedish teacher found, language `sv` (Part 4.6)

The answer of a Swedish lågstadielärare (reviewer agent, 2026-11-11) to the placement sheet of the 200 lessons (`scripts/units/placement-sheet.js --loc=sv`), beside the placement itself (`scripts/units/lists/sv-placement.txt`). Kept as written. Section (a) is the list of what a Swedish teacher expects in each year and does not find (input for new worksheet types and for the curriculum pages of Part 6). Section (b) is printed wording on Swedish worksheets that is wrong or un-Swedish: those are deck fixes for the dedicated content-fix session; the teacher notes of the units already use the school words. Sections (c) to (e) are the rulings the Swedish units follow.

### (a) What a Swedish teacher expects and does not find

**Förskoleklass**
- Matematik: practical measuring (length, volume with sand and water, as in Hitta matematiken); longer/shorter; the day and time words; simple problem solving in play; step-by-step instructions (early programming).
- Svenska: listening and talking, read-aloud with text talk, and telling/describing (three of the four areas of Hitta språket); splitting sentences into words, long and short words, compound words orally; blending sounds orally; the child's own name. The material is nearly all paper-and-pencil, while F teaching is mostly oral and play-based.

**Åk 1**
- Matematik: the biggest gap is addition and subtraction 11–20, with and without crossing ten (8 + 5, 13 − 5). Also: the numbers 0–10 themselves in åk 1 (number neighbours, >, <, = within 0–20); subtraction as difference; length in metres and centimetres; comparing mass and volume; chance; programming.
- Svenska: the core of åk 1 is missing, namely letter-by-letter work (each letter's sound and form, vowels and consonants, long and short vowels). Also: short connected reading texts with comprehension; writing own sentences with spaces between words; handwriting on guide lines.

**Åk 2**
- Matematik: mental strategies 0–100 with crossing ten (47 + 8, 52 − 7, talsortsvis), which is the main content of the year. Also: 10:ans tabell; halves and quarters as its own area; metre/centimetre, kilogram, litre/decilitre; digital time; change when shopping; chance; programming.
- Svenska: only two reading texts exist. Missing: spelling areas as a sequence (dubbelteckning, ng, sj, tj, j-ljudet, e/ä, o/å); instructions and letters as text types; the structure of a story (beginning, middle, end); handwriting.

**Åk 3**
- Matematik: the tables 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10 (only 2 and 5 exist) and division facts. Also: mental arithmetic 0–200 and 0–1000 without uppställning; length units mm, cm, dm, m, km; mass kg, hg, g; volume l, dl, cl; older units; scale (förstoring/förminskning); chance; programming; problem-solving strategies; doubling/halving as proportionality.
- Svenska: almost everything a teacher needs before the national tests. Missing: narrative writing with a clear thread; descriptive and instructional text; reading comprehension of narrative and factual texts; punctuation of dialogue; exclamation mark; comparison of adjectives; oral presentation; searching for information and source criticism. The 11 åk 3 lessons are word and grammar work only.

### (b) Swedish wording that is wrong, un-Swedish or inconsistent

### (c) What the thing is called

- The short sequence of lessons: **arbetsområde** is the everyday word. **Tema** is used for cross-subject work. **Lektionsserie** is understood and is the usual word for ready-made sequences from publishers. **Pedagogisk planering** (often "LPP") is the written plan for an arbetsområde tied to the curriculum, not the lessons themselves. **Undervisningsenhet** is not used by Swedish teachers; it reads as a translation.
- The plan over a term or year: **årsplanering** and **terminsplanering**. **Grovplanering** is staff-room jargon for the same thing and is typed less.
- What a teacher types into a search engine: the topic plus the year plus a material word, for example "multiplikation åk 2 arbetsblad", "klockan åk 1 lektionstips", "årsplanering matematik åk 2", "pedagogisk planering svenska åk 3", "lektionsplanering". "Arbetsområde" is said but seldom typed; "undervisningsenhet" is never typed.
- Recommendation: call the unit **arbetsområde** on the page, describe it as "en lektionsserie med arbetsblad", and call the plan **årsplanering** (with terminsplanering as a secondary word).

### (d) Address and words for the child

- Yes, **du**, always. Not "ni", not "läraren" in the third person.
- **Eleven / eleverna** for åk 1–3. Skolverket also says elever in förskoleklass; teachers there often say **barnen**, so "barnen" is acceptable in F notes only. "Klassen" and "elevgruppen" for the group. Never "studenterna".
- Say "förskoleklass" (not "F-klass" or "förskolan", which is a different school form) and "åk 1" / "årskurs 1".

### (e) What misleads if the American order is kept

- **US K is not förskoleklass.** US K reads words, writes letters on lines and does written plus and minus. In Sweden formal reading and written arithmetic start in åk 1; F is oral and play-based.
- **Number range runs one year later.** US grade 1 reaches 100 and grade 2 reaches 1,000; Sweden has 0–20 in åk 1, 0–100 in åk 2, 0–1000 in åk 3.
- **Multiplication runs earlier relative to the number range.** US starts it in grade 3; Sweden starts in åk 2 (2, 5, 10), with division in åk 3.
- **Column method.** US grade 2; Sweden åk 3, after mental and written talsortsvis methods.
- **Fractions.** Number line and equivalence are US grade 3 but Swedish åk 4–6. Halves and quarters, on the other hand, are met already in åk 2.
- **Area and perimeter.** A full US grade 3 unit; in Sweden omkrets is touched in åk 3 and the rest is 4–6.
- **Data.** Line plots do not exist in Swedish lågstadium; tables, pictograms and bar charts do.
- **Units.** Sweden reads decilitres before millilitres, kilograms before grams, and centimetres from åk 1–2 rather than unnamed units.
- **Time.** No am/pm; the Swedish distinction is analog versus digital (24-hour) time.
- **Phonics.** English digraphs, sight words and word families have no Swedish counterpart. The Swedish sequence is sound-by-sound blending in åk 1, then spelling areas (dubbelteckning, ng, sj, tj, j) in åk 2–3.
- **Grammar.** US grade 1 teaches nouns, verbs, adjectives and pronouns; Sweden does word classes in åk 2–3 and pronouns as a class in 4–6. "A or an" maps onto en/ett, which native speakers do not need to practise.
- **The school year.** It starts in August with höst- and vårtermin; units should be ordered by termin.
