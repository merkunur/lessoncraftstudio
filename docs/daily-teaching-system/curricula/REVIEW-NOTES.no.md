# Reviewers' notes: registry `no`

Written by the reviewers of the crosswalk (`scripts/curricula/apply-review.js`). 306 teaching points carry a reviewer's row; the reason for each is in `frontend/content/curricula/no/crosswalk-authored.json`.

## author-all

- The Norwegian plan gives aims per set of years, without number ranges, units or lists of words. Where a skill line names a range (to 20, within 1000) I judged by the wording first and by usual practice second, and said so in the reason: three-digit numbers are usually 3. trinn work, although the positional-system aim after 2. trinn sets no range.
- Fractions and symmetry have no aim through 4. trinn in PART 2; in Norway they belong to the aims after 5. to 7. trinn, which this registry does not hold. The site places them in grade 2 and 3, so nine of those points (and K:mirror-image) have no rows and three more only rest on halving, sharing or the number line.
- Mathematics has no aim on tables and diagrams through 4. trinn in PART 2. The seven graph points are tied to naturfag's aim KM13817 ('bruke tabeller og figurer til å organisere data', after 4. trinn) as 'prepares' only. Line plots are not a diagram type Norwegian teachers use at this age.
- The signs for greater than and less than are first named after 3. trinn (KM13249), multiplication after 3. trinn and division with its two meanings after 4. trinn. The site's grade 1 and 2 comparing sheets, grade 2 equal groups and arrays, and grade 3 division therefore come out as 'prepares'.
- The only aim on time is after 2. trinn (KM13238) and names no precision. Reading to the minute, time passed and 12/24-hour time in grade 3 are marked 'extends'; no aim after 3. or 4. trinn in PART 2 names time. Norwegian sheets need the 24-hour clock and the spoken forms 'kvart over', 'halv tre' (= 2:30), 'fem over halv'.
- Money, ordinal numbers, rounding, perimeter and capacity before 4. trinn are not named in the mathematics aims. Money sheets must use kroner (coins of 1, 5, 10 and 20; no øre in cash); inches and feet in 2:choose-units have no place in Norway.
- Norsk after 4. trinn: PART 2 holds 16 aims and none on spelling. In the version of the plan I have taught from there was an aim 'skrive tekster med tydelig struktur og mestre sentrale regler for rettskriving, ordbøying og tegnsetting' and one on giving and using feedback on texts ('gi tilbakemelding på andres tekster ...'). PART 2 instead has KM14157 'bruke komma og andre skilletegn i tekster'. This may be a real change in NOR01-08, but please check the source, because spelling-rule and plural sheets now have no aim after 4. trinn to rest on.
- Naturfag after 2. trinn: in the version I have taught from there was an aim on naming the outer and inner parts of the human body and their functions ('sette navn på og samtale om funksjonene til noen ytre og indre deler av menneskekroppen'). It is not in PART 2, where KM13814 (common illnesses) stands. Please check the source; the two body-part points could only be tied loosely to samfunnsfag's KM14727.
- Traffic and road safety belong to kroppsøving in Norway, and the registry holds no kroppsøving, KRLE, musikk or engelsk. The three road points have no rows for that reason, not because Norwegian schools do not teach it.
- Language points written from English need Norwegian content: digraphs are sj, skj, kj and ng (th has none); spelling rules are double consonant, silent d, g and h, o or å, and kj or skj; articles are en, ei and et; the alphabet has 29 letters. Alphabetical order and pronouns are not named in any aim in PART 2 and have no rows.
- Handwriting: the plan asks only for writing by hand (after 2. trinn) and 'funksjonell håndskrift' (after 4. trinn). A joined script is not required, and many schools no longer teach løkkeskrift, so the three cursive points are a choice of the school.
- Plant parts, flower parts, life cycles, animal groups and the planets are not named in any naturfag aim in PART 2. I tied the first four loosely to the aims on adaptation and on nature changing and said so in each reason; the planets have no rows.
- Content of these years that teachers will look for and no teaching point covers: the Sami content (the Sami division of the year in KM13811, the Sami people in KM14728 and KM14671), nynorsk beside bokmål (KM14139, KM14161), children's rights and democracy (KM14724, KM14725), digital judgement (KM14726, KM14675), friendship and boundaries (KM14722, KM14727), and algorithmic thinking with coordinates and programming ideas (KM13252, KM13263).
- In the dump the sets MAT.3 and MAT.4 carry no '[years …]' tag and stand under 'grade 3' and 'grade 4' headings, while MAT.2 has the tag and no heading. I read them as the aims after 3. and after 4. trinn.
- Strand names: PART 2 only has subject headings and 'Kompetansemål etter N. trinn', so it proves no other name and I kept all. Two could mislead a Norwegian teacher: 'Lesing: grunnleggende ferdigheter' (in the plan 'grunnleggende ferdigheter' means the five basic skills across all subjects, not early reading; teachers say 'begynneropplæring' or 'den første leseopplæringen'), and 'Visuell oppmerksomhet' (the usual term is 'visuell persepsjon').

## own-audit

- The builder's own reading after the review. The reviewer found no fraction in the aims through 4. trinn and said that Norway places fractions after 5. trinn, outside the registry. The registry now also holds the mathematics aims after 5. trinn (ten aims), and the nine fraction points are mapped there by the builder, each with the wording that decides; a Norwegian teacher should read them as 'taught two years later than these worksheets'. Symmetry stays without a counterpart: no aim through 5. trinn names it.
- The reviewer expected two aims from earlier versions of the plans (a spelling aim after 4. trinn in norsk, the body-parts aim after 2. trinn in naturfag) and did not find them. The registry was checked against Utdanningsdirektoratet's data once more: every aim of every set after years 2, 3 and 4 of the plans in force (NOR01-08, NAT01-05 and the others, each the successor the service itself names) is in the registry, 148 aims of 148. The plans changed; the registry holds what is in force.
