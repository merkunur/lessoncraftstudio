## The skill
Tell vowels from consonants, and see that every word needs a vowel.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE ARCH THAT HOLDS ONLY ON A VOWEL. A stream with three stone arches, each spelling a CVC picture word with its middle stone missing (c _ t under a library cat). Letter stones lie on the bank; two carts wait: the KEYSTONE cart (arched top) and the WALL cart (flat top). The child loads each stone into a cart. Check: the carts roll onto the bridge row; every stone in the keystone cart is tried in the middle of the next arch — a vowel locks the arch and it rises (a real or a nonsense word, both stand); a consonant makes that arch sag and the stone drops back to the bank. Then any vowel hiding in the wall cart pops up out of it and glows. Miss 1: only the wrong stones return to the bank. Miss 2: vowels on the bank shimmer when held over an arch gap. Success: all arches stand and the picture animal walks across. FIX-THE-ARCH mode: the child drags the one stone that makes the picture's word.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Thinking any letter can go anywhere; not knowing which letters are vowels (y as a vowel in sv/da/no/fi; å ä ö æ ø as vowels).
- **Pictures:** one library picture per CVC arch word (approved-words-<locale>.json)
- **Settings:** Mode: load the carts / fix the arches · Vowels: core (a e i o u + locale core) / full locale set (sv/da/no/fi y; å ä ö æ ø; de ä ö ü; accented forms count as the same vowel)
- **Rounds:** 9 rounds of 6–8 stones with 2–4 vowels; stone order shuffled; the carts swap sides every other round (cart SHAPE, not side, is the cue); one right sort.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** K-230
- **Distinct from:** No vowel-class activity exists; the CVC Builder spells words but never names vowels as a group.
- **Build risk:** Per-locale vowel set authored by the reviewer; en y excluded from the core setting. Arch words from approved-words-<locale>.json CVC entries only (fi/it/es CVC words are rare — CVCV allowed with the middle vowel missing).
