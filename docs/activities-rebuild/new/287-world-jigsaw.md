## The skill
Name and place the seven continents on a world map.

## The apparatus and its invention
A world map outline (oceans blue) with seven empty continent sockets. Continent pieces in a tray snap only into their own socket (shape-locked). After placing, name tags (native names) drop onto the continents; Name mode: all pieces placed, a numbered continent glows and the child picks its name from a bank.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Confusing North/South America and Asia/Europe; thinking Australia is a country only.
- **Pictures:** space (earth) as cue; library animals (kangaroo, penguin, lion…) as optional continent hints
- **Settings:** Mode: place the pieces / name the continents · Hints: animal on each piece / none
- **Rounds:** ≥ 7 rounds (each continent asked once per pass), order shuffled.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G2-370, G3-396
- **Distinct from:** No geography activity exists.
- **Build risk:** Blocked on art: a world outline with seven continent pieces and five ocean basins. Locale content: es/pt/it/fr schools often use one "América" (6 continents) and the Olympic 5 — the native panel decides the set per locale. Only 7 + 5 items: rounds must vary which pieces are pre-placed.

## BLOCKED — needs an operator decision before building
Continent outline art and ocean basins are not in the library (apparatus from public-domain map data needs an operator ruling), and the continent count differs by locale (es/pt/it/fr schools often teach 5–6 continents with one America).

## Merged into this instrument (cover each as a setting or round set)
- grade 3 "The World Jigsaw" (world-jigsaw): Locate the seven continents and five oceans on a world map. — THE WORLD JIGSAW: an empty world with ocean blue and continent-shaped holes. The child drags each continent piece into its hole; pieces only seat in their own outline, and a piece held over the wrong hole shows its outline does not fit. Then ocean labels are dropped onto the water: the label floats until it is in the right ocean basin, where it docks. Find mode: a name is given and the child taps the place.
