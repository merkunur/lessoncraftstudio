## The skill
Read and name weather symbols, match the sky to its symbol, tell cold / warm / hot on a thermometer, and tell day from night.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE WINDOW AND ITS THERMOMETER. A big window frame (apparatus) whose pane shows the sky as a colour and one library weather picture outside (sunny, cloudy, rainy, stormy, snowflake, rainbow); a weather card hangs on the frame and a band thermometer (cold blue · warm yellow · hot red, no numerals) on its side. WEATHER mode (K-356, G1-362): the child drags the matching symbol card onto the frame's card holder; a wrong symbol sits on the holder while the sky stays different, so the two are compared, then drops off (miss 1); miss 2: the sky's picture pulses. TEMPERATURE mode (K-211, K-365): the child drags the thermometer liquid up or down and the scene outside changes its clothing pictures (coat → t-shirt → swimsuit); or a scene is shown and the child sets the liquid to its band. DAY/NIGHT mode (K-208): the pane is split day | night under a sun–moon dial; things (owl, rooster, breakfast foods, moon, bed, sunglasses) are dragged into a half. WEEK mode (K-364, G1-363 + absorbed wardrobe): seven small windows, each with a symbol; questions ("which day can we fly a kite?") answered by tapping a window; WARDROBE: a library animal is dressed for the asked day (raincoat, boots, scarf, mitten, sunglasses) and walks through that window — rain lands on it without the raincoat.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Thinking sunny always means hot (sunny winter day), reading the thermometer by colour alone.
- **Pictures:** weather (sunny, cloudy, rainy, stormy, snowflake, rainbow, raincoat, scarf, sunglasses, umbrella, thermometer), clothing (coat, t-shirt, swimsuit, mitten), space (moon), animals (owl), farm-animals (rooster), breakfast foods, furniture (bed). MISSING: wind (refused), pyjamas.
- **Settings:** Mode: weather symbols / thermometer / day and night · Week chart: off / on (a 7-day strip to fill)
- **Rounds:** 9 rounds per mode, each symbol or band used ≥ once, tray order shuffled with the answer in each slot ≤ 40 %; one right answer.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G1-362, G1-363, K-208, K-211, K-356, K-364, K-365
- **Distinct from:** No weather activity exists; the Season Wheel is the year cycle, this is today's sky.
- **Build risk:** No numerals on the thermometer, so °C/°F never arises; symbol conventions per country checked by the reviewer; wind is absent from all modes until art exists.

## Merged into this instrument (cover each as a setting or round set)
- grade 1 "The Weather Wardrobe" (weather-wardrobe): Read weather symbols and a weekly forecast, and dress for the weather. — A WEEK OF WINDOWS: seven day windows each show a weather symbol (library weather pictures). A library friend stands at the wardrobe; the child dresses it for the asked day (raincoat, boots, sunglasses, scarf, mittens) and the friend walks through that day's window: in the rain without a raincoat the drops land on it, in the snow without mittens it shivers. Forecast setting: questions "which day is rainy / when can we fly a kite" are answered by tapping a window.
