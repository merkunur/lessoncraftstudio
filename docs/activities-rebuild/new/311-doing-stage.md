## The skill
Group verbs into word fields by meaning (ways to move, ways to look) and tell close words apart by what they show.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE DOING STAGE. A library animal stands on a little stage with a path to the left, a window to the right and a speech spot at the front. Each word card (stroll, dash, tiptoe; glance, stare, peek; whisper, shout, ask) is dragged onto the animal: a go-word makes it travel the path in that manner (dash = fast, tiptoe = small slow steps), a look-word turns it to the window for a short or long look, a say-word puts up a speech bubble (whisper = small dotted bubble, shout = big jagged bubble, ask = bubble with a question tail). SORT mode: the child drops each card on the path, the window or the speech spot. Right: it acts there and the card settles in that field. Miss 1: the animal acts the word in the wrong place, then the card floats back. NUANCE mode (and G2-374): two close words act side by side and the child picks the one that fits a sentence.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Grouping words by spelling or sound instead of meaning; treating all words in a field as the same (glance = stare).
- **Pictures:** one library animal or person per round (forest creatures, animals); stage, path and window drawn tool-grade
- **Settings:** Fields: go + look / go + look + say · Mode: sort into fields / pick the closer word
- **Rounds:** Native word banks per locale (≥ 12 verbs per field), 9 rounds, field answers balanced; nuance pairs never repeated; one right field or word.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G2-374, G3-397
- **Distinct from:** Roary's Roar Meter and Pesto's Soup Stall rank strength within one field; this sorts across fields by meaning and acts each word out.
- **Build risk:** Medium: the manner animation is the meaning — needs per-verb motion specs; native word fields ×11 (reviewer).
