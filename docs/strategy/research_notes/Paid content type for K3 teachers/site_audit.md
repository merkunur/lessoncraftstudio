## 2. Exact free tier vs Teacher tier, and which code enforces it

### Takeaway
The code confirms the operator's statement: free = 10 plays/day (anonymous or free account) across interactive decks, activities and tools, + 3 PDF/export downloads per UTC month with a free account (anonymous = 0 downloads); Teacher = $69/yr or $8.99/mo, unlimited, plus hosted worksheets, collections/workspace, play links, activity share links, topic-pack ZIPs and "full depth" in ~50 of 62 tools. `METERING_ENABLED=1` and `PRICING_PUBLIC=true` are ON in production.

### Cited Findings
- **Quota constants:** `DOWNLOADS_PER_MONTH = 3` (per UTC month, "FREE ACCOUNT REQUIRED — anonymous download attempts return reason 'signup_required'"), `PLAYS_PER_DAY = 10` ("for both anonymous and free accounts (the IXL model)"); subscribers and crawler UAs bypass; kill switch `METERING_ENABLED` env ("anything but '1' makes every check return allowed"); fail-open on errors — [frontend/lib/quota.ts](frontend/lib/quota.ts).
- **Production flags:** "METERING_ENABLED=1 in frontend/.env.production" (2026-07-12) — [project_subscription_launch_2026_07.md:16](docs/pc-notes/memory/project_subscription_launch_2026_07.md); re-verified "METERING_ENABLED=1 verified live on Hetzner (env var exists ONLY there)" — [feedback_tier_truth_before_marketing_copy.md:18](docs/pc-notes/memory/feedback_tier_truth_before_marketing_copy.md). `export const PRICING_PUBLIC = true; // FLIPPED 2026-07-12` — [frontend/config/subscription-launch.ts](frontend/config/subscription-launch.ts). `worksheet-host.js` `var LAUNCHED = true;` — [REFERENCE TRANSLATIONS/worksheet-host.js:24](REFERENCE%20TRANSLATIONS/worksheet-host.js). Hosted-worksheet kill switch `HOSTED_WORKSHEETS_DISABLED` exists — [project_subscription_launch_2026_07.md:46](docs/pc-notes/memory/project_subscription_launch_2026_07.md) (no note says it is set; "not recorded").
- **Entitlement predicate:** `isSubscriptionUsable` = admin bypass, or LS-originated row with status `active`, or `past_due` within 14 days (`SUBSCRIPTION_GRACE_DAYS = 14`), or `canceled` with `currentPeriodEnd` in the future — [frontend/lib/entitlements.ts](frontend/lib/entitlements.ts). The stricter billing predicate `isLcsSubscriptionActive` lives in `lib/subscription-helpers.ts`.
- **API gate:** `requireActiveSubscriber` (auth + `isSubscriptionUsable`, 403 `subscription_required`) — [frontend/lib/subscriber-api-gate.ts](frontend/lib/subscriber-api-gate.ts); used by 12 routes: `/api/worksheets/hosted` (+`[id]`), `/api/workspace`, `/api/activities/share` (+`[id]`), `/api/play-links` (+`/bulk`), `/api/collections` (+`[id]`, `/decks`, `/decks/[deckId]`, `/decks/bulk`) (grep, 2026-09-26).
- **Client meter:** `meterAction(kind)` POSTs `/api/quota/check-and-increment` with the localStorage Bearer token, shows the wall on 401/402, tracks Umami event `meter-wall-shown`, fails open — [frontend/lib/client-meter.ts](frontend/lib/client-meter.ts). The deck action strip (Download PDF · Answer key · Make your own) baked into every interactive deck uses `/api/quota/dl` — [CLAUDE.md §21.2 step 6d](CLAUDE.md).
- **Deck play wall:** `lcs-meter.js` injected into every deck.html; does nothing when embedded (`window.top !== window.self`); POSTs a play on load; on 402/401 shows a full-cover overlay linking to `/<loc>/pricing?from=deck-wall` — [REFERENCE TRANSLATIONS/lcs-meter.js](REFERENCE%20TRANSLATIONS/lcs-meter.js). "Optimistic by design (IXL-style: you play, then meet the wall)".
- **Generators:** `access-guard.js` calls `/api/verify-app-access`; the tier oracle returns `tier: 'anonymous'|'free'|'subscriber'|'admin'` and `features: { saveInteractive: subscriber }` — [frontend/app/api/verify-app-access/route.ts:24-28,80-86](frontend/app/api/verify-app-access/route.ts). - **Doc/code disagreement:** `docs/SUBSCRIPTION-SCOPE.md` (v3/v4 amendments, 2026-05-17) says quota infrastructure is deleted and "The website reads as flat-free-forever"; CLAUDE.md §7 says "Free for everyone.
