## 1 Page concept (base, G1)

"Personal Pronouns": eight numbered cards, two by four. Each card is one person (a 64 px portrait) or two people side by side (52 px each), a white name plate under them ("Mia", "Francesco e Leonardo"), and three (fr/es/pt four; fi two) identical white pill chips: `he  she  they`. From across the room: eight little faces, eight name tags, eight rows of three white pills, and nothing else. The child reads the NAME (primary cue), glances at the portrait (secondary, confirms), and circles ONE chip. Top quality = one focal apparatus per card (portrait + plate + chips, nothing competing), the chips identical on every card (no position leak), generous air (the stage centres in a 170 px card at worst chrome and opens to 193 at one-line chrome), pencil-first (a circle is the only mark), and the occupation noun NEVER printed (the portraits are people, not vocabulary). de/nl: three of the eight cards are captioned OBJECTS (`Flugzeug`, `vliegtuig`) whose chips are er/sie/es and hij/zij/het, the Klasse 2 / groep 4 "das Kind -> es" move, on the same card.

## 4 Answer-hiding + uniqueness

- **The name plate is the key, the portrait the confirmation, the chip table the lock.** Nothing on a card differs by answer: chips are identical white pills in the canonical order on every card (the K-288 position-leak check: `data-lcs-label` sequence equal on all cards; identical computed style). The correct chip is marked ONLY by `data-lcs-correct`. Wrong = the circled pill is not the tagged one; the teacher sees it at a glance because the plate sits 3 px above the pills.
- **Uniqueness by construction:** `keyOf(refs)` from the OPENED `depicted` tags + the panel's name `gender` (the composer pairs a name only with a portrait of the same tag; the gate refuses a mismatch); one chip per key per face (`map[face][key]`); a key that maps to `null` (de/nl pairs on the base: sie/zij would be two answers) never reaches a card. fr/es/pt: the mixed pair is masculine (ils/ellos/eles) and the landing says so.
- **F1:** the gap is sentence-initial; line 2 = line 1 minus the subject phrase; the answer literal (`initial`) is nowhere in the text (Unicode word test, never `\b`); the bank is the WHOLE table so its order cannot leak (it is still shuffled, `!==` the chip order on >= 1 of 10 seeds). Wrong = a pronoun that does not match the name's sex/number, visibly beside the portrait.
- **F2:** the two pronoun boxes on a card carry DIFFERENT literals (Él / Ella; fi Hän / He); every sentence is `contentNeutral:true` (panel-signed; poison "{P} trägt ein Kleid."); the two referents have different keys. Wrong = a line from "Ella" to the boy's plate, or crossed lines where they should be parallel.
- **F3:** the owner's name is IN the frame ("This is Mia."), the thing's name is IN the frame with its article where the article is the cue (it/fr), the chip literal never occurs in the text; de prints no article before the blank. Wrong = a circled possessive that does not match the owner (or, it/fr, the thing's gender).
- **F4:** heads = the chip table; every bin's ruled lines >= its card load (a pair card counts 2 lines); no card is pre-placed; no card text equals a head. Wrong = a name under the wrong head.
- **F5:** the printed sentence starts with the name(s); the answer sentence is NOT printed anywhere (no starter); the writing row is empty. Wrong = the name copied instead of a pronoun, or the wrong pronoun.
- Every face root stamps `data-lcs-layout` (base: none); `verify()` branches on the stamp, never on the level.
