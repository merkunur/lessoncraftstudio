# b9 Picture Tracing — the studio brief (read before designing anything)

## The commission (operator /goal 2026-10-21, essentials)
Design and build a "Picture Tracing" worksheet type: **20 top-quality, pedagogically meaningful variations, PDF only**, the new
**flagship** of the site. Not "good" — top quality. **Original scenes, not similar to each other**; premium composition and
template design for every page. Use the image library's **black-and-white drawings** as much as possible; where a scene
needs an element the library lacks, it is **drawn in the library's style** (thick even black outline, rounded, cute, flat,
no shading). Perfectly native in 11 languages (en de fr es pt it nl sv da no fi). Every page visually analysed before
publishing. Strong SEO (≥ 500 clicks/day from the 220 pages). The method documented as the template for future expansions.

**The inspiration samples** (competitor pages the operator showed) are typical of the genre: a heading band + one subtitle,
then dashed outlines of clip-art: four snails; a rainbow with clouds, a turkey and grass; food items with their names; four
insects with names; one big strawberry with the word; a palm tree + deer; Christmas snowflakes + snowman + tree; New Year
fireworks + people; a summer beach umbrella + chair with outlined bubble words "Hello Summer"; two maple leaves (one dashed
one solid); "Look at the image" (solid kangaroo) / "Trace it" (dashed kangaroo in a box); one big star; weather (clouds,
rain, rainbow); sea animals; butterflies of different sizes; lemonade + ice cream. **Their weaknesses are what we must beat:**
items scattered with no real scene, uneven dash rhythm, no progression or teaching intent, English only, generic titles.

## The engine (built and measured — design to it)
- **Any B&W library drawing can be turned into a tracing guide** that lies exactly on the drawing's own lines (the
  drawing's ink is thinned to its centre line and redrawn dashed; filled black parts such as pupils stay solid so a face
  still reads as a face). 1,288 drawings are traceable; the list with a simplicity grade is `_SUBSTRATE-POOL.md`
  ((1) = PreK-simple: few long lines; (2) = K; (3) = detailed, for G1/G2). The tracer cannot invent lines and cannot
  merge two drawings into one drawing; it can place, scale and mirror drawings, and decide per drawing whether it prints
  dashed, dotted, solid (a model to look at) or partly solid.
- **Scene elements I draw myself as vectors** (they trace perfectly by construction), in the library style: ground line,
  hills, a path, waves, water line, a pond, sun, moon, stars, clouds, rain, snowflakes, raindrops, bubbles, grass tufts,
  flowers-in-grass, a fence, a hedge, mountains, a rainbow, a table edge, a shelf, a window, a frame, a road, rails, a
  kite string, balloons, a picture frame, bunting, music notes, simple geometric shapes. Anything bigger (a house, a tree,
  a boat, an animal) must come from the library list.
- **Guide styles (the ladder, in page px on an A4 page body ~640 × 730 px):** P1 thick grey dashes for big pictures
  (PreK); P2 standard dashes (K); P3 fine dashes (G1); P4 dots (precision). Optional per line: a green start dot + a
  small direction arrow. A dashed line can also be partly solid (the child finishes the picture).
- **The page:** the site's worksheet shell (title, one instruction line, the body, a small footer). PDF, printed in
  black and white at home or school — **no colour may carry meaning**; grey/black only, any colour hint must also work in
  grey. A page must fill its body (no big empty band at the bottom). Text on the page is native per locale; words a child
  traces (e.g. a picture's name) come from the vocabulary of each language, so a face that prints words must work for
  11 languages (word lengths differ: "Schmetterling", "perhonen", "borboleta").

## The neighbours (never duplicate them)
- `pre-writing` (shipped): ABSTRACT strokes — lines, zigzags, waves, loops, spirals — and "draw a path from picture to
  picture". Picture Tracing always traces the outline of a PICTURE.
- `how-to-draw` (shipped): a step-by-step ladder (shapes → finished drawing), incl. "trace then draw". Picture Tracing
  has no step ladder.
- dot-to-dot (Level Set): numbered points. Picture Tracing has no numbers on a guide.
- `color-by-number`, `read-and-color`: colouring by a code. A "trace and colour" face is free colouring after tracing.
