## The skill
Match each experience to the sense and body part we use for it (see, hear, smell, taste, touch).

## The apparatus and its invention
THE SOCKET THAT LIGHTS ITS SENSE. Five large library body-part pictures sit in a row as sockets: eye, ear, nose, mouth, hand (body-parts). A library thing slides in on a cable from the left (bell, drum, rainbow, rose, onion, lemon, honey, sponge, icicle…). The child drags its plug into a socket. Right: the socket responds with an apparatus effect (rings of waves round the ear, a swirl into the nose, a sparkle round the eye, a little shake of the hand, the mouth pulses) and the thing docks beside it. Miss 1: the socket stays still and the plug drops back. Miss 2: the right socket's ring of effect shows faintly. Two-sense rounds (setting 2) show two empty plug-holes on the thing (lemon: taste + smell). ODD-ONE-OUT mode (G1-359): three things docked on one sense plus one that does not belong; the child unplugs it. FIND-ALL mode: one socket lights and the child plugs in every thing for it.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** One sense per thing; thinking we taste with the nose only or hear with the eyes on picture books.
- **Pictures:** body-parts (eye, ear, nose, mouth, hand); things: christmas (bell), music (drum, trumpet, whistle from accessories), weather (rainbow), flowers (rose, lavender), vegetables (onion), at-the-supermarket (garlic, lemon, popcorn), breakfast (honey), kitchen-tools (sponge), winter (icicle), farm-animals (rooster).
- **Settings:** Senses per thing: one / one or two · Mode: drag to the sense / find all things for one sense
- **Rounds:** 9 things per pass, each sense the answer ≥ once and ≤ 3 times; two-sense things only in setting 2 and the plug-holes say how many; socket order fixed (the organ is the content); one right set per thing from a reviewed table.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G1-357, G1-358, G1-359, G1-360, G1-361, K-355
- **Distinct from:** Not the body map (names of body parts): this is what each organ does.
- **Build risk:** Each thing must have ONE obvious sense in setting 1 (refuse cake, cat, apple — several senses). Taste is shown by the mouth picture (lips); tongue is in the cache, not labelled.

## Merged into this instrument (cover each as a setting or round set)
- grade 1 "The Sense Switchboard" (sense-switchboard): We learn about the world with five senses, each through one body part: eyes see, ears hear, nose smells, tongue tastes, skin touches. — A FACE WITH FIVE SOCKETS: a large library face/body part set (eye, ear, nose, tongue, hand) as plug sockets. A picture of a thing to sense (bell, rainbow, flower, lemon, cactus) slides in on a cable; the child plugs it into a socket. The right socket makes the sense "light up" (the eye opens, the ear wiggles) and a short effect plays (sound waves, a smell swirl drawn as tool-grade lines). A wrong socket shows nothing happening. Odd-one-out setting: three things for the same sense plus one other.
