**What this type therefore owns:** the **FAMILY RELATION** across several words that share one stem and one meaning, judged or produced by the child: (1) sorting words into their stem families, (2) recognising the one real family member among look-alikes from a picture, (3) extracting the shared root from a family, (4) choosing a NON-negating prefix by its MEANING, (5) building the PERSON word (agent noun) from what the person does, (6) placing each member of one family in the sentence its word class fits. Derivation only: an inflected form (plural, tense, person) is never a family member and never an answer. In es/fr/it/pt the type additionally never builds "picture + given suffix → word", never splits one word at its root boundary, never asks "built or simple word?", never diminutive/augmentative (all shipped).

### Base: Sort the Words into Their Families (G2, G2-359, CODE `mode:'base'`)
- **Move:** two family "houses", each roofed by its ROOT (picture where one exists + the root word); a bank of mixed members; the child writes every bank word under the house of its stem. Membership by shared stem + meaning (the Wortfamilien-sortieren / classer par famille / clasifica routine).
- **Child:** en "Read each word in the bank. Which family is it from? Write it in the house with the same root word." (98 chars)
- **Params:** d1 `{houses:2, perHouse:3, roofPic:true}` · **d2 (ships) `{houses:2, perHouse:5, roofPic:true, bankOrder:'shuffled', memberKinds:['derived','prefixed','compound']}`** (10 answers, G2 floor 8-16) · d3 `{houses:3, perHouse:4, roofPic:false}`. Layout: `wordBank` strip (59 px + 10) over 2 × `ruleBins({w:300, rows:5, rowH:56, glyphH:26})` with a 72 px roof tile (picture 64 px + root word 22 px Baloo). Stack (e) 69 + 26 + 72 + 5×56 + 40 ≈ 490 ≤ 722.
- **verify():** stamps `data-lcs-face="base"`, per house `data-lcs-family`, per bank chip `data-lcs-family-of`; re-derive: each chip's family = the unique house whose `stem` occurs (case-folded, NFC) in the chip AND whose `members` list contains it; FAIL on 0 or 2 houses; each house receives exactly `perHouse`; the two stems are not substrings of each other; bank order ≠ grouped (no 3 consecutive same-family chips).
- **Refusals:** none (m: every locale has ≥2 families × 5 transparent members plausible; (p) panel signs). es/fr/it/pt: members are chosen so that NO house is a pure "stem + diminutive/augmentative" set and ≤1 member per house is a "stem + suffix" word already printed in that locale's `compound-words` bank (m lists above).
