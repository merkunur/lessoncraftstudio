## 1 Page concept (base)

Six round weather badges down the left, six cream word signs down the right, a straight pencil line per pair. A "weather board": the symbols a K class reads off the morning chart, each tied to its word. One apparatus, pencil first, nothing else printed. Quality = the pictures chosen for mono legibility (section 5), 72 px art in 96 px badges, words at Baloo 2 700 24, generous 12 px rows with 213 px of clear line space between the columns.

## 4 Answer-hiding + uniqueness

Both columns print; the MAPPING is the answer and lives in `data-lcs-concept` on each badge and each tile (never text, never order: the derangement rule makes row i never its own answer).
