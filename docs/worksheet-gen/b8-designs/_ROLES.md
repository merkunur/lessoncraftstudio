## PEDAGOGY + CONTENT (`_work/K-405-pedagogy.md`)
For the base and each of the 19 faces: **Move** (what the child does that differs) · **Child** (the instruction candidate, ≤ 150 chars, names only what is on the page, includes the story frame) · **Skill + honest standard** (or readiness) · **Band** (PreK / K / G1 / G2 with the reason) · **d1 / d2 (ships) / d3** as concrete parameters (corridor count, dead ends, route length, letters / numbers range, items) · **What makes it pedagogically right** (the developmental research in one or two lines: e.g. visual-motor integration, alphabet-order fluency) · **Misconceptions and tells to design out** (e.g. the letter path must not also be the shortest-looking path; a number path whose numbers are visible from the goal) · **Per-locale refusals / re-targets** (alphabets, initial-sound pools measured against `REFERENCE TRANSLATIONS/image-vocabulary.js`, number words) · **Query face**. Then: **Rejected non-moves**, and a **native rebuild table** ×11 (what each panel authors: title, instruction, story nouns via vocab, alphabet, sound pools, number words, traps). Also read `../b7-designs/K-395-find-the-differences.md` for the house standard of a final.

## DESIGNER A: SCENES (`_work/K-405-design-A-scenes.md`)
Twenty ORIGINAL scenes, one per face, all different settings and heroes. For each: setting, the start hero and the goal (exact library `theme/noun` you OPENED on the contact sheets), 4-10 supporting library drawings placed on the islands between corridors (exact `theme/noun`), the drawn scenery structure (only structure: sky, hills, water, soil, road, lily pads … with its flat colours as hex), the ONE style family of the cast, where start and goal sit, the corridor's look (colour of road / water / tunnel / vine; outline tone), the mood. Prove dissimilarity: a table of setting × hero × palette × corridor look across the 20. Flag any face where the library lacks what the scene needs and propose a scene the library CAN carry. Look at `%TEMP%\hm\proto\p7.png` (what the corridor engine draws today).

## DESIGNER B: MAZE TOPOLOGY + DIFFICULTY (`_work/K-405-design-B-topology.md`)
For each face: the graph shape (tree over a jittered grid / organic Poisson field / street lattice / forest of k routes / crossing lines / hop grid / arrow cells), node count, corridor width W (page px), solution length range, number and depth of dead ends, branch degree, the islands' minimum size for art, start/goal positions, and d1 / d2 / d3. The quality rules as measurable thresholds: no position tell (correct branch side distribution), wall-follower share, no dead end visible as a stub, goal not visible along a straight line, dead ends that are tempting (they lead toward the goal before failing). For the letter / number / math / sound faces: how labels sit on corridors or stones, how distractor labels are chosen so the true path is unique AND not guessable by shape, and the search that proves uniqueness. The raster oracle and the poisons that prove the gates. The `trace-path` screen's graph contract (nodes, edges, snap radius ≥ 90 page px, per-face Check rule).

## DESIGNER C: PAGE, SCREEN AND KEY (`_work/K-405-design-C-page.md`)
The page composition for the base and every face at d2: ASCII layout with px arithmetic (620 ≤ stack ≤ 740; page inner 675), where the scene frame sits, start/goal markers (an arrow, a "start" flag drawn as apparatus, never a word that needs translating unless it is in the strings), boxes for written answers (face 8 numbers, face 19 code), the legend strip (face 9 items to collect, face 10 things to avoid, face 18 the sound), print legibility (greyscale, ink use: corridors light, scene colours soft), the fonts and floors. The SCREEN: how `trace-path` looks and behaves (ink colour, route colours, celebration, the toolbar, undo by retracing), its strings. The KEY: the coral route, numbered or coloured routes. Two alternative page concepts considered and why yours wins.
