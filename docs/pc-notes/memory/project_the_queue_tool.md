**REBUILT + SHIPPED 2026-08-16.** The operator called the shipped #58 "a piece of shit… far from a
premium teaching tool… not creative," and commissioned a full transformation. Four expert panels
(pedagogy / interaction / art / fence) drove the rebuild. **This supersedes [[project_the_queue_rejected]]**
(the old ordinal framing is intentionally retired — the rebuild INVERTS its refuse-list).

## The retarget (the fix for every weakness)
The old tool taught **ordinal position** — a word the design law §23.2 forbids naming, with NO CCSS
standard, a blank-line printout as its only "premium," austere teal blobs, and nothing for the child to
touch. Retargeted to **Counting & Cardinality** using the fact NUMERALS are allowed on the apparatus:

> A warm line of distinct "counting friends" on a shelf. A genuine-coral **hand** sweeps from a **chosen**
> end; each friend it passes wears its **running-count numeral** (1,2,3,4). The last number = HOW MANY.
> Count the SAME line from the OTHER end: each friend wears a different number, but the **total is
> identical** — cardinality invariant, position not (**CCSS K.CC.B.4b, the order-of-count clause**). The
> odd middle friend wears the same number both ends.

**Fence = CLEAR** (unowned on all 4 surfaces): the both-ends / order-of-count invariance on a projected
line of discrete members is nowhere. Subtract in copy: NOT "count a set" (owned), NOT
conservation/rearrangement (that's `necklace` — claim ORDER-OF-COUNT only).

## The three binding trap-defeaters (the running numeral is curative ONLY with all three)
1. **Transience** — badges are a pure function of `k` (drag back un-accretes); none at rest.
2. **The total is a WHOLE-LINE span** (a bracket + pill beneath the line), never the last friend's badge
   (the last-word-rule trap). Adjudicated pedagogy-over-art on this exact conflict.
3. **The reversal is mandatory and IS the cure** — a still friend visibly wearing a different number
   from the other end refutes "the number is its name."

## Engineering lessons bought (each caught by a gate, in both directions)
- ⭐ **The old "coral is banned" was a BARE-FILL problem, not a coral problem** (art panel): outline any
  warm fill with an `#A34122` stroke that itself measures ≥3:1 and the fill is free to be warm — this is
  what turns the joyless monochrome into a warm crowd AND lets the hand be genuine coral (`#F2784B` fill
  + `#A34122` stroke, the number-hotel is-here precedent). CAP lifted 4→6 (the old 4 was a *monochrome*
  ceiling; colour is the strongest back-of-room discriminator).
- ⭐ **A front-facing symmetric face SOLVES "a facing asserts an end"** — looking straight out faces
  NEITHER end (the old build banned faces entirely and got sterility).
- ⚠ **`\bvh\b` can never match `50vh`** — the boundary before `v` fails against the digit; a mutation
  survived until the vh-check became `[0-9.]\s*vh\b`. (The `\b`-is-brittle trap, in miniature.)
- ⚠ **`createElement('button')` reads as a `t('button')` call** — the string-key-reachability regex
  needed `(?<![A-Za-z])t\(` or it flagged phantom keys.
- ⚠ **`manipulatives.ts` is CRLF, the tool-content JSONs are LF** — the landing apply script had to
  detect the line ending per file or the card-replace matched nothing.
- ⚠ **The travel arm must sweep between two VISIBLE hand positions** — the hand's first appearance from
  `display:none` correctly does NOT slide (no before-state), so a Home→End from a hidden hand shows
  "teleport" for a correct tool; establish a visible hand first.
- The 5th…10th native panels ALL validated my authored in-tool strings (`inToolReview` empty ×10) —
  authoring EN carefully + native-review-per-locale (hybrid §A.13.48) held.
