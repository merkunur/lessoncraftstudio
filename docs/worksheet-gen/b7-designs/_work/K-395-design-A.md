## 1 Page concept (base)

**"The Spotter's Ladder."** The pictures are the hero and they get the page: stacked, not side by side, because the scene is nearly square (600 × 560) on a portrait page, so stacking gives 0.575 px/unit against 0.543 side by side inside 675 (and 0.525 in a 639 lane): +12 % linear, +25 % area, at exactly the geometry the screen version uses (pictures one above the other, picture 2 the one you mark). The ONE apparatus is the ladder: N dashed coral rings (Ø40) in a column beside picture 2. It is the self-monitoring a commercial spot-the-difference book prints as a row of little circles ("5 found: oooo o"): the child circles the thing in picture 2 AND fills a ring, so at any moment the page says how many are left, without a number being written. It carries no information about WHERE (the ladder is the same for every seed), only how many, which the title already says. Pencil-first: a pencil circle on picture 2, a pencil fill in the ladder. No printed text in the body except the two numerals (apparatus) and nothing coral on the pictures. Whitespace: 96 px margins each side, air above and below the stack, no cream lane (the house's habitats page G1-398 is the precedent for frames directly on white). Top quality = the biggest picture the engine can be given, one calm apparatus, nothing that competes with the line art.

## 4 Answer-hiding + uniqueness

The printable shows no ring, no hotspot, no numeral, no tick: `data-lcs-fd-ring` count on the print and screen pages = 0 (gate d).
