## 2 The base page

**Concept.** "Read the rule, look at the picture, write the rule letters in the gap." A rule box on top (one coral chip with the rule grapheme + two MODEL words with their rule letters in coral) and eight cream cards (2x4): a picture above the word printed in equal LETTER CELLS with ONE dashed coral gap box where the rule grapheme belongs; the child writes the grapheme (1-3 letters). The answer is never printed. Distinct from K-224 (one arbitrary CVC letter at K), G1-244 d2 (whole word, no rule), G1-305 Missing Syllable (a syllable, no rule), K-317 F5 (a SOUND unit traced + circled), K-318 (no letters printed).
