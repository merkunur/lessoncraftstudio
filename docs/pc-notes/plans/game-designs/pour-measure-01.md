## 2. Concept & creative hook
**Pippa** is a small round frog who runs a cozy roadside **pond-juice stand** for thirsty pond creatures — a tippy old watering-can pitcher, a tall **frosted measuring-beaker with numbers painted down the side**, bubbling juice in soft pond-greens and berry-pinks, a hand-lettered MENU clipped to the stand. A customer hops up: *"A dragonfly would like about SIX, please!"* Pippa can't read the fancy numbers — **only the child can.** The child helps Pippa pour + read the measure. The hook: **you're the lab-scientist running the stand — you pour by feel, then read exactly how much you made.**
- **THE FROSTED HIDDEN-POUR (the critic's #8 fix — the perceptual-cheat-killer + the family identity):** the beaker is **frosted/opaque** — while the child **holds the pitcher to pour**, the juice rises but **the level is HIDDEN** (you can't see where it's reaching). So the child pours **by feel, toward their ESTIMATE** of the order ("about six"). There is NO visible line to chase, NO level to eyeball → perceptual-line-matching is **structurally impossible** (there's nothing to watch). The pour is genuinely CALIBRATED by the child (the experiment-measure family identity — you tinker the pour), NOT an auto-animation.
- **THE REVEAL + READ (the scale-reading is load-bearing — no numeral to match):** on RELEASE, the beaker turns **CLEAR**, revealing where the juice actually landed against the **numbered graduated scale.** The child must **READ the revealed level** (e.g., it's at 5) and **report it** (tap the matching number on a ≥44px strip / confirm). **There is NO given numeral to match** — the child poured BLIND, so the answer is whatever the LEVEL reads (the order "about six" ≠ the actual 5; matching "6" is WRONG; only READING the revealed level works → numeral-matching defeated, gate-proven §8).
- **ESTIMATE-THEN-VERIFY is the DEFAULT LOOP (the 3.MD.A.2 estimate verb, the critic's #5 fix — not a token):** every pour opens with the order as an ESTIMATE ("about six") → the child predicts + pours-by-feel → reveal → reads the actual → compares ("ooh, five — almost! that's plenty"). The estimate is load-bearing across the game, NOT one round.
- **NO-SHAME (the pour accuracy doesn't matter — the READING does):** the customer is happy with CLOSE (over/under is fine — "a little over, that's lovely!"); the assessed act is the **READING** (did you correctly read it's 5?), never the pour precision → no motor-punishment, no spill-fail, no dexterity test. A wrong READING → the customer re-asks "what does it say?" + a soft finger-glow traces up to the juice's top (shows WHERE to look, NOT which number — non-leaking); re-read. No buzzer. Non-numeric vessel = a tray of served-glasses filling.
- **THE PAYOFF (earned by the READING):** correct reading → the customer drinks happily + says the number back ("Five! Just right — thank you!"), a glass fills on the tray, Pippa hops. `ctx.correct()`.
- **Anti-generic statement:** NOT a vertical readruler tile-select — (a) the frosted hidden-pour makes the perceptual cheat structurally impossible WHILE keeping genuine child-controlled calibration (the family identity); (b) the read-the-revealed-level has NO numeral to match → scale-READING is load-bearing (gate-proven, not numeral-matching); (c) the conservation cognitions (compare / different-graduation — read the scale, not the height) are cognitions the ruler lacks; (d) estimate-then-verify is the loop (the Grade-3 verb); (e) a board ships only if a **perceptual-pour solver** (no line to watch) + a **NUMERAL-MATCH solver** (no given numeral on the read-the-level rounds) + a **motor-timing solver** all FAIL while a **scale-reader** passes (§8).

## 3. Core loop & the honest ~4 cognition-classes
**Loop (`stage`: estimate → pour-blind → reveal → read):** the customer orders an ESTIMATE ("about six") → the child pours-by-feel into the frosted beaker (no level visible) → release → the beaker turns clear, revealing the actual level against the scale → the child READS the revealed level + reports it → correct → the customer drinks + says the number; the tray-glass fills → repeat → `ctx.correct()`. **No fail, no timer, no score, no Check button, no dexterity/pour-precision assessment.** A wrong reading → the customer re-asks + the non-leaking finger-glow trace; re-read. First-attempt-correct is the internal signal (never shown).

**The genuine scale-reading COGNITION-CLASSES (the critic's honest de-padding — read-labeled / read-between / 0-start collapse into difficulty-TIERS of "read a level"; "pour to 4 vs 6" = number-variant BLOCKLISTED). Each round carries a `cog`:**
| # | Cognition-class (`cog`) | The child's act | Why distinct + reading-load-bearing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ★ | **Estimate-and-pour-then-read** (`estimate-pour` — the SPINE) | pour blind toward an estimate → reveal → READ the actual revealed level | continuous-pour CALIBRATION + the ESTIMATE verb + reading-a-revealed-level (NO numeral to match — the family identity + the Grade-3 core) |
| 2 ★ | **Pour-then-read** (`read-level` — pure read, the frog pours) | the frog pours a mystery amount → reveal → READ what it reached | pure scale-reading, no given numeral (the read isolated; reading-between-marks rides here as a difficulty tier) |
| 3 ★ | **Compare two cups** (`compare` — conservation: the NUMBER decides) | two revealed cups → which has MORE? read BOTH scales | defeats taller-looks-more (volume-conservation) — a cognition the ruler LACKS; the number, not the height |
| 4 ★ | **Different-graduation scales** (`diff-scale` — read the scale, not the height) | the same amount at a different physical height (tall thin vs short wide cup, different marks) → read the SCALE | the SCALE not the visual column gives the measure — the sharpest anti-perceptual + anti-numeral-match cognition |
- **Honest framing (the critic's de-padding + §A.13.60):** **4 genuine scale-reading cognition-classes** — estimate-pour (spine, the family identity + estimate verb), pure-read, compare (conservation), different-graduation (scale-over-height). **CUT/COLLAPSED** (the critic): read-labeled-mark + read-between-marks + 0-start = difficulty-TIERS of "read a level" (interpolation / non-zero-start), NOT distinct cognitions; the pour-TO-a-given-printed-number tile-select = the readruler re-skin + numeral-matching → STRUCK (when a target IS given, it's spoken-only, numeral suppressed on screen, so the child reads the scale to find it). The **≥7 distinct ROUNDS** (DATA requirement) = the 4 cognition-classes × the difficulty-tiers (labeled vs between-marks, exact vs interpolate, near-mark vs mid-interval estimate) × the cup/graduation/context — honestly, fanned by GRADUATION/cup/context, **NEVER by the target number alone** (blocklisted). Order-only reshuffle.
- **Free slice = estimate-pour (1) + compare (3) + different-graduation (4)** — the calibration + the conservation cognitions (a numeral-matcher + a perceptual-pourer + a height-reader all fail immediately).

- **THE KEYSTONE RULES (the critic's fixes — scale-reading not numeral-matching):** (i) **FROSTED HIDDEN-POUR** — the level is invisible during the pour → no line to chase (perceptual-matching structurally impossible) + the pour is genuinely child-controlled (calibration, the family identity). (v) **NO POUR-PRECISION ASSESSMENT** — the reading is judged, never the pour accuracy → no motor-punishment, no-shame. (vi) **NO HARD-CODED UNIT** (per-locale).

- **The family identity vs the cheat (the critic's #2):** killing the perceptual cheat by auto-pouring (the synthesis) turned it into a quiz (lost the calibrate/tinker family identity). The frosted-pour resolves the dilemma: child-controlled pour (calibration restored) + invisible-level (perceptual cheat gone) + reveal-and-read (scale-reading load-bearing).

- **Distinctness from readruler/Ruler Lab + Game 15:** readruler = tile-select read of an object's LENGTH (no calibration, no conservation); this = child-controlled volume CALIBRATION + conservation + read-a-revealed-level. Game 15 = estimate-then-COUNT discrete; this = estimate-then-READ continuous. If the game were a tile-select read-the-scale (no calibration, no conservation), it's a vertical readruler re-skin — REJECTED (the frosted-pour + compare/different-graduation are the guard).
- **No-shame + §20.8 demonstrate-don't-touch:** the synthesized quiz only TOUCHED measurement (numeral-matching); the frosted-pour + read-the-revealed-level DIRECTLY instantiates volume scale-reading + estimation — the gate (perceptual + numeral-match + motor solvers all fail; scale-reader passes) is the structural proof.
