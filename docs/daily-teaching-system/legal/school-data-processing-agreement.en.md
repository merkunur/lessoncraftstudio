# Data processing terms for classes (classes and results)

> The text after the legal decision of 2026-11-14 (`DECISION-2026-11-14.md`, section D), with the operator's facts filled in (PART-9-DESIGN.md section 13). **Not published**: it goes live with condition E7, in eleven languages, and only when the feature does what every sentence says. It follows the structure of GDPR Article 28(3). The technical statements must match `FACTS.md`; where they differ, `FACTS.md` is corrected or the feature is, before this is used.

**Between**

the school or school authority named at acceptance, represented by a person authorized to sign for it, or a private tutor or home educator who decides alone about the data ("the School", the controller), and

Riza Kunur Genc (LessonCraftStudio), Björkvägen 15D, 459 31 Ljungskile, Sweden, who operates LessonCraftStudio as a private person ("LessonCraftStudio", the processor).

## 1. Subject and duration

LessonCraftStudio provides the "classes and results" feature of its website to the School's teachers. This agreement applies for as long as a teacher of the School uses that feature, and until the data has been deleted under section 9. For classes that work with numbers only, these terms apply from the moment a teacher makes a class; a school can have them countersigned at any time.

## 2. Nature and purpose of the processing

Storing the classes a teacher makes, receiving the results of worksheets that children do through a class link, and showing those results to that teacher. The purpose is teaching: letting a teacher see what each child has understood. No other purpose.

## 3. Types of personal data

- Of children: a seat number and a picture; a display name (first name or nickname) only if the School has switched first names on; per worksheet: which worksheet, when it was opened, the number of right answers at the first Check and when that was, the number of answers, which answers were right, the number of Check presses, whether the page was finished; for a set with levels, the level of the page. Of the class: its name and language. In transit only, not stored with any of the above: the internet address of the device.
- Of teachers: the account data described in the general privacy policy.

No special categories of data are requested. The School instructs its teachers not to enter a child's name or other personal details in the class name, and, where first names are switched on, to enter nothing other than a first name or nickname as a display name.

## 4. Data subjects

Children in the School's classes, typically 4 to 10 years old. The School's teachers.

## 5. Instructions

LessonCraftStudio processes the data only on the School's documented instructions. The School gives its instructions by using the feature: making, changing and deleting classes, seats and assignments. LessonCraftStudio tells the School if it believes an instruction breaks data-protection law. These terms and the settings of the feature are the School's complete instructions. LessonCraftStudio does not process class data for purposes of its own and does not transfer it outside the European Economic Area except as described in section 14, unless Union or Member State law requires it; in that case it informs the School first, unless that law forbids it.

## 6. Confidentiality

Everyone at LessonCraftStudio who can access the data is bound to confidentiality. Access is limited to what is needed to run and repair the service.

## 7. Security

LessonCraftStudio keeps at least these measures:

- Transport encryption (HTTPS) for every page and request.
- Results can be read only by the teacher who owns the class, after signing in; every request is checked against the owner on the server.
- A class link contains a random key of 10 characters from 32 symbols; the teacher can replace it at any time. A result is tied to a seat by a random one-time token of 20 characters.
- A child's result request carries no cookie; no device identification is stored.
- The database is on a single server in Finland, reachable for administration only with a personal key. Backups of the database are kept on that server for 7 days; a copy that leaves the server holds no class data.
- Pages a child opens through a class link load nothing from other companies and run no analytics.
- Requests of the class page are logged without the device's internet address.
- The software that enforces these rules is covered by automated tests that are run before every release.

## 8. Sub-processors

The School agrees to these sub-processors:

| Sub-processor | Purpose | Place |
|---|---|---|
| Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany) | Server hosting | Finland, EU |
| Cloudflare, Inc. | Delivery of the website; requests pass through, class data is not stored | United States and its worldwide network; EU-US Data Privacy Framework certification of Cloudflare, Inc., and the EU standard contractual clauses (Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914) in Cloudflare's data processing addendum |

LessonCraftStudio informs the School 30 days before adding or replacing a sub-processor, by e-mail to the account address of each teacher of the School and on the page www.lessoncraftstudio.com/en/class-terms. LessonCraftStudio binds every sub-processor by contract to the same data-protection duties as in this agreement and remains fully liable to the School for what a sub-processor does. The School may object; if the objection cannot be met, the School may stop using the feature and its data is deleted.

## 9. Deletion and return

- A teacher can delete a child's results, a child, an assignment or a class at any time, and can print the results or save them as a PDF from the dashboard before doing so.
- A class not used for 14 months is deleted automatically. When the teacher's plan ends, their classes are deleted 60 days later.
- Every single result is deleted at the latest 14 months after it was made.
- When this agreement ends, all class data is deleted within 60 days, and from backups within a further 7 days, unless a law requires it to be kept.

## 10. Helping the School

LessonCraftStudio helps the School answer requests from children and parents (access, correction, deletion, objection), with data-protection impact assessments, and in consultations with the supervisory authority, as far as the feature is concerned. Most requests can be met by the teacher directly in the feature. Requests that reach LessonCraftStudio are passed to the teacher without delay. LessonCraftStudio does not answer such requests itself. It also helps the School meet its duties on security, breach notification and impact assessment (GDPR Articles 32 to 36).

## 11. Personal data breaches

LessonCraftStudio tells the School without undue delay, and no later than 48 hours after becoming aware of it, of a breach that affects class data. The notice says what happened, which kinds of data and roughly how many children and records are affected, the likely consequences, what has been done, and whom to contact.

## 12. Audits

LessonCraftStudio gives the School the information needed to show that this agreement is kept, and allows an audit by the School or an auditor it names, with reasonable notice, at most once a year unless there is a concrete reason.

## 13. No other use

LessonCraftStudio does not use class data for advertising of any kind, does not sell or rent it, does not use it to train any system, does not combine it with other data, and builds no profile of a child except what the feature shows the teacher for teaching (the result bands and the automatic choice of the next page in a set with levels). Statistics that contain no names and no results of single children are not personal data and are outside this agreement.

## 14. Transfers outside the EU / EEA

Class data is stored in Finland. It passes through the network of Cloudflare, Inc., which may process it in the United States; this transfer rests on Cloudflare's certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and, should that fall away, on the EU standard contractual clauses in Cloudflare's data processing addendum.

## 15. Law and precedence

Where the School is in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, this agreement is governed by the law of the School's country and the courts at the School's seat decide. Otherwise it is governed by the law of Sweden. A public school in the United States is governed by the law of its own state. Where this agreement and the general terms differ on personal data, this agreement applies.

## 16. Schools in the United States

LessonCraftStudio acts as a school official with a legitimate educational interest (34 CFR 99.31(a)(1)(i)(B)): it performs a service the School would otherwise perform itself, is under the School's direct control as to the use and keeping of education records, uses them only for the purpose in section 2 and does not disclose them further (34 CFR 99.33(a)). The School authorizes the collection described in section 3 for its educational purpose only, and can review the data, have it deleted and stop further collection at any time. LessonCraftStudio does not use student data for targeted advertising, does not sell it, and builds no profile of a student except for the School's purposes. On request LessonCraftStudio signs the School's or its state's own student data privacy agreement (for example the National Data Privacy Agreement, the New York Parents' Bill of Rights supplement with a data security and privacy plan, or an Illinois SOPPA agreement); first names are switched on for a school in a state that requires such an agreement only after it is signed.

## How a school accepts it

Decided: (1) For numbers-only classes these terms apply through the teacher's confirmation when she makes her first class. (2) To switch first names on, a person authorized to sign for the school accepts this agreement on a page of the site that is opened when first names are offered, giving name, role, school, the school's official e-mail address and the confirmation "I am authorized to conclude this agreement for the school named above"; LessonCraftStudio confirms by e-mail to that address and only then switches first names on for the teachers the school names. (3) A countersigned PDF is sent on request. A teacher cannot accept for a school.
