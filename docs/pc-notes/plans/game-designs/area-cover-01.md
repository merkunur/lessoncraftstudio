## §1 — Concept / world
**The Mosaic Menders of Tessella.** Tessa the glass-fox; the child mends mosaics by covering a BLANK region with Unitiles (the lattice is INVISIBLE until a tile occupies a cell → the grid is REVEALED BY the tiling, never pre-drawn). Overlaps physically impossible (a Set); overhang bounces; gaps detectable. The covering is the felt MODEL of "area = how many unit squares cover, no gaps/overlaps" — BUT the area-COUNT is the child's to OUTPUT (the game NEVER names it first). Non-numeric abundance vessel: a Glass Kiln glows fuller across a session (never a count). `ctx.correct()` auto-advances.

## §2 — The standard + the load-bearing cognition
**3.MD.C.5 + 3.MD.C.6 (Grade 3, SOLE primary)** — area is an attribute; a unit square (side 1) has area 1 square unit; area = the number of unit squares that cover with no gaps/overlaps; measure area by counting unit squares. **3.MD.C.7 (area = L×W) = SIBLING, rides as the produced-from-the-tiling capstone rung.** **Cognition (pedagogy + critic, AS RE-CENTERED):** the covering-by-the-unit-square is the MODEL/teaching; the GRADED cognition = the child PRODUCES/USES the area as a COUNT-OF-THE-UNIT (a magnitude the CHILD determines) — output-the-count, area-conservation-across-shape, irregular-covering-count, area-vs-perimeter (referent, not word). **Single unit square (1×1 = 1 sq unit) ONLY — NO vary-the-unit (operator-ruled TIGHT MID-SIZE; vary-unit is the critic's flagship-maker but 4th-grade-adjacent unit-reasoning, out of Grade-3 scope per pedagogy).** Numbers: rect dims 1-10 (bias 2-8), areas ≤30 (≤48 for L×W), irregular 6-24 cells, area-vs-perimeter where area≠perimeter clearly. **FENCES:** the OWNED 2.G.A.2-count (never a pre-drawn-grid "how many squares"), E13-array (multiplication-of-objects), perimeter COMPUTATION (3.MD.D.8 — area-vs-perimeter is fenced to covering-IDENTITY only; lexicon fence: cover/inside/square-units for area, border/edge/around for the perimeter foil), vary-the-unit (out of grade). Numbers/labels = real DOM/SVG `<text>`.

## §3 — THE ARCHITECTURE REWORK (the heart; the deepest flaw + fix — RE-CENTER onto produce-the-area)
**The critic's catch — the blank-region-cover-it pre-empts the affordance-leak (no pre-drawn grid → COUNT_PRE_DRAWN_GRID fails), but the TILE-TO-COVER is a spatial-PACKING motor act where the GAME (not the child) measures, AND the produce-the-area rounds have their own floor-cheats:**
- **(A) TILE-TO-COVER = FILL-EVERY-CELL + THE GAME COUNTS.** The child taps every cell until full (the jigsaw "is-it-full-yet" perception — the missing-pieces/picture-path family, pre-K-ish); the GAME counts + reports the area. The child performs the FILL (spatial-packing), NEVER the MEASUREMENT (never determines/produces the magnitude). "Beats 2.G.A.2 only on STIMULUS, not cognition." → tile-to-cover is the MODEL/scaffold (the standard's "cover" is the model); the GRADED cognition is "measure area by counting unit squares" — the child OUTPUTS the count-of-the-unit-as-the-area.
- **(e) THE LETHAL ONE — HOW-MANY ECHOES THE FUSE NUMBER.** If the covering fuses + names "12 square units" THEN asks "how many?", the child ECHOES the game's number (reads the answer back — invisible in QA, guts the headline round). → child-output STRICTLY before game-output, ALWAYS (the fuse confirms covering-complete with NO number; the child produces the count; THEN reveal). `ANSWER_NOT_PRE_REVEALED`.
- **(c) BUILD-TO-TARGET = COUNT-TO-N.** "Make a region of exactly 8 sq units" → lay tiles until the counter hits 8 (= counting to 8, not area-as-magnitude; "stickers" would be identical; survives the (A) re-center). → HIDE the live counter during placement; commit-then-report; prefer a dimension-constraint OR "build TWO DIFFERENT regions both of area 8" (area-conservation-across-shape, uncounted-to). `COUNT_TO_N_SOLVER` fails.
- **(B) AREA-VS-PERIMETER = KEYWORD-MATCH (Game-75 clever-hans).** The prompt-word ("cover the INSIDE"→area-tool / "trace the EDGE"→perimeter-tool) telegraphs the answer; the two-tools mechanic COMPLETES the discrimination FOR the child. BUT area-vs-perimeter is a REFERENT distinction (NOT irreducibly lexical like Game-78's compare-op) → assessable via a referent-NEUTRAL prompt ("this region needs a number — what does it count, the squares that FILL it or the steps AROUND it?") + the child produces the count of the right referent (numbers deliberately different: area 12, perimeter 14 — a wrong-referent child produces the wrong number). `KEYWORD_TOOL_SOLVER` fails.
- **(d) SAME-AREA = 2-WAY GUESS** (50% floor). → 3+ regions, same-area-DIFFERENT-BBOX (the bbox≠cells trick), close numbers (10/10/12 not 4/4/20), produce-the-matching-count. `VISUAL_SIZE`/`BINARY_GUESS` fail.

**THE FIX — RE-CENTER the GRADED core onto PRODUCE-THE-AREA (the house pattern: the covering is the MODEL/teaching, the area-COUNT the child OUTPUTS is the assessment; TIGHT MID-SIZE, Grade-3-honest):**
- **GRADED CORE = the child OUTPUTS/USES the area-count:** how-many (output the count, BEFORE any game-reveal) + L×W-from-tiling (produce rows×cols from the child's own tiling) + build-to-target (produce a covering of a TARGET area, counter HIDDEN, or two-different-regions-of-area-N) + same-area (3+ regions, produce-the-matching-count) + irregular-count (count the covering where rows×cols fails). **TILE-TO-COVER = the MODEL/SCAFFOLD** (the felt covering that teaches "area = covering by the unit, no gaps/overlaps"), ungraded/lightly-graded. **AREA-VS-PERIMETER = referent-neutral, decorrelated.** Child-output ALWAYS precedes game-output.

## §4 — Anti-cheat architecture (the measured gate)
NEW `verify-area-cover-core.js` (window-shim; descriptor geometry-only — `rect{w,h}` | `composite{cells}` | `pair` — NO stored area, NO pre-drawn countable grid, NO pre-revealed area-string; `deriveArea` rect=w*h, composite=cells.length [bbox DELIBERATELY ≠ cells.length]). Solvers — ALL must FAIL:
- **`FILL_WITHOUT_OUTPUT_SOLVER`** (NEW) — completes the covering (perfect packing) but never OUTPUTS a number → must FAIL the graded rounds (every graded round requires a child-PRODUCED numeric/comparative output the gate can withhold from the covering act).
- **`ECHO_REPORTED_COUNT_SOLVER`** (NEW) — reads the game's reported area + echoes it → must FAIL via `ANSWER_NOT_PRE_REVEALED` (no area string rendered before the child's answer event; child-output strictly precedes game-output).
- **`COUNT_TO_N_SOLVER`** (NEW) — place-until-counter===target → must FAIL via the HIDDEN live counter + commit-then-verify (build-to-target).
- **`KEYWORD_TOOL_SOLVER`** (NEW) — pick the tool/answer from prompt tokens {inside, edge, cover, trace, fence, border} → must FAIL via the referent-NEUTRAL prompt (no token maps to the answer) + perimeter≠area.
- **`VISUAL_SIZE_SOLVER` / `BINARY_GUESS_SOLVER`** (NEW) — bigger-bbox→bigger-area / coin-flip same-different → must FAIL via 3+ regions, same-area-different-bbox, close numbers, produce-the-matching-count.
- **`COUNT_PRE_DRAWN_GRID_SOLVER`** (no payload field === area, no pre-filled cover) + **`PERIMETER_SOLVER`** (fails where perimeter≠area) + **`LW_NO_COVERING_SOLVER`** (bbox formula fails on ≥3 composite rounds) + **RANDOM** — all FAIL.
- Structural asserts: `GRADED_IS_PRODUCE_NOT_FILL` (every graded round has a child-produced output the gate withholds from the covering), `ANSWER_NOT_PRE_REVEALED` (child-output before game-output), `BUILD_COUNTER_HIDDEN`, `AREA_VS_PERIMETER_REFERENT_NEUTRAL` (no prompt-token maps to the answer; perimeter≠area), `SAME_AREA_DIFFERENT_BBOX ≥N` + close-numbers, `IRREGULAR_COVERING ≥3` (bbox≠cells), `NO_PRE_DRAWN_COUNTABLE_GRID`, `ANSWER_DERIVED_NOT_STORED` (mutate w/h/cells + composite-area≠bbox + frozen-descriptor probe), VARIETY ≥7.
- **Wrong-answer re-show (no-reveal):** re-show the COVERING/unit (the gap, the unit squares re-counted one-by-one) WITHOUT revealing the count; area-vs-perimeter → re-show the inside-covering vs the border (the referent, not the word). The covering is the source of truth the child re-counts.

## Cohort doctrine this game adds
**The FILL-vs-MEASURE / WHO-PRODUCES-THE-ANSWER lesson — PROGRAM-WIDE (a concrete-act variant of the affordance-leak + the house pattern).** For a "perform-the-concrete-act" game (cover-a-region / fill-a-shape / build-a-model) where the act produces a result the GAME then measures and REPORTS, the child's cognitive act is the SPATIAL/MOTOR performance (jigsaw "is-it-full-yet" packing), NOT the MEASUREMENT — **the GAME determines the magnitude, the child never does** — so the headline number is HANDED (it beats a pre-drawn-grid count only on STIMULUS, not cognition). **THE FIX (the house pattern, again): re-center the GRADE onto the child PRODUCING the result (output the count/magnitude), with the concrete act as the MODEL/scaffold; and — crucially — CHILD-OUTPUT MUST STRICTLY PRECEDE GAME-OUTPUT (if the game reports the number first [the fuse names "12"], the child ECHOES it — an `ANSWER_NOT_PRE_REVEALED` violation, invisible in QA, that guts the headline round).** Plus two sub-cheats that survive the re-center: (1) **COUNT-TO-N** — a "build-to-target-N" round with a LIVE counter is counting-to-N (swap the unit-noun for "stickers" and it's identical), NOT magnitude-production → HIDE the counter, commit-then-verify, or constrain by a non-count property (two-different-shapes-of-area-N); (2) **the BINARY/keyword discrimination floor** — a 2-way same/different is a 50% guess (→ 3+ options, produce-the-matching-value), and a referent-discrimination (area-vs-perimeter, inside-vs-border) whose PROMPT-WORD names the referent is a Game-75 clever-hans (→ referent-NEUTRAL prompt + deliberately-different values; but note: a referent distinction is NOT irreducibly lexical [unlike a true word-operation], so it decorrelates cleanly). Gate solvers: `FILL_WITHOUT_OUTPUT` / `ECHO_REPORTED_COUNT` / `COUNT_TO_N` / `KEYWORD_TOOL` / `BINARY_GUESS` all must FAIL. Generalizes to EVERY perform-an-act-and-the-game-scores-it game: ask WHO produces the answer (game→re-center onto the child), CHECK the output ordering (child-before-game), and DEFEAT the count-to-N + binary/keyword floors.
