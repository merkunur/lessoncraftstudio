## The skill
Tell everyday foods from sometimes foods and build a balanced plate.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE PLATE THAT FILLS BY HALVES. A big divided plate (apparatus): half for vegetables and fruit, a quarter for grains, a quarter for protein (plus a side cup for dairy where the locale model has one), and a small side saucer with a star for sometimes-foods that holds ONE thing. Library foods tumble onto a tray. The child drags a food to a section; a right drop settles and that section's fill line rises. Miss 1: the food slides back to the tray and the section it belongs to breathes once. Miss 2: the food's section shows a faint outline of that same food. A second sweet on the saucer slides off. Success: when every section reaches its line the plate turns a slow quarter turn and steam curls rise — balance is the goal and a treat on the saucer is allowed. EVERYDAY/SOMETIMES mode (K-203): two places only, the plate and the saucer.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Some foods are "bad"; any plate with one vegetable is healthy.
- **Pictures:** vegetables, fruits, at-the-supermarket (bread, rice, pasta, cheese, milk, fish, ham, sausage, candy, soda, popcorn), breakfast (cereal, oatmeal, porridge), desserts-and-sweets (cake, cookie, lollipop, donut). Refused as ambiguous: pizza, pancake, juice, butter, pretzels, jam.
- **Settings:** Mode: everyday or sometimes / build the plate · Foods: 6 / 10
- **Rounds:** 9 rounds of 6 (or 10) foods; every section is an answer in every round; sweets ≤ 2 per round; one right section per food from a per-locale reviewed table.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G1-207, K-203
- **Distinct from:** Grow-Where is botany; this is nutrition.
- **Build risk:** Per-locale model: en MyPlate (dairy cup), sv Tallriksmodellen, fi lautasmalli, no/da/de/nl/fr/es/it/pt national guides; G1-207 says FOUR groups — align with each locale's worksheet bank. Wording "everyday / sometimes", never good / bad.

## Merged into this instrument (cover each as a setting or round set)
- grade 1 "The Food Group Plate" (food-group-plate): Foods belong to food groups (fruit, vegetables, grains, protein, dairy) and a healthy plate has them all. — A DIVIDED PLATE: the plate has sections sized like the plate model (vegetables biggest), each with a food-group icon. Foods tumble onto a tray; the child drags each to its section and it settles; a wrong section rejects it with a gentle slide and the section's icon pulses. A "build a balanced lunch" setting asks for one of each group: the plate glows when every section has food.
