## 2. Concept & creative hook
**Skip the squirrel runs a word-welding yard, and a ticket clatters down the chute — "a HOUSE for BIRDS." Skip welds the bird-block and the house-block into a `birdhouse`, and a tiny birdhouse pops into the yard's diorama. The success condition is the MEANING (does this build satisfy the ticket?), NEVER "is this a real word."**
- **THE TICKET IS A MEANING, NOT A WORD-VALIDITY CHECK (the rote-pair kill — Designer A's inversion):** Skip never asks "is sun+flower a real word?" — Skip asks "what should I BUILD to satisfy this MEANING?" Word-validity falls out of meaning-correctness, never the reverse. A rote-pair memorizer who knows all the real compounds STILL can't pick WHICH one the ticket means.
- **PREDICT THE MEANING ON NOVEL COMPOUNDS (the familiar-retrieval kill — the critic's deepest fix):** the form→meaning rounds use **NOVEL** transparent compounds (both PARTS familiar, the WHOLE novel — e.g. a never-memorized "sun-hat" / "milk-jug" / "snail-shell") + show only the two PART pictures (a sun + a hat), NEVER a picture of the whole (which would leak the answer). The child has NOT memorized the whole's meaning → they MUST compose it from the parts. A `FAMILIAR_RETRIEVAL_SOLVER` (a dictionary of familiar compound meanings) provably fails these rounds.
- **THE RELATION IS THE HARD FOIL (the blind-concatenation kill):** the primary foil is **same-head-WRONG-RELATION** ("a house MADE OF birds" vs "a house FOR birds" — same head, same parts, wrong relationship) — a blind-concatenation solver (glues the two meanings) and a relation-blind child both MIS-fire. (Head-reversal "housebird" is transparently silly → a giveaway, demoted.)
- **THE TWO DIRECTIONS:** **form→meaning** (NOVEL compound + part-pictures → pick the composed meaning) AND **meaning→form** ("the ticket": a meaning → weld/pick the compound form; FAMILIAR ok because picking the right head+modifier+order is retrieval-resistant — birdhouse vs housebird vs doghouse).
- **THE PAYOFF:** a correct meaning-build → the weld glows, the THING pops into the yard's diorama, a built model parks on the Build Board (`GameCollection` — abundance, never a count). `ctx.correct()`. A wrong tap → no fail; Skip warmly re-reads the ticket, DIMS the wrong choice, and the head/modifier PART-pictures highlight (a scaffolded re-compose, not blind elimination), then reshuffle.
- **Anti-generic statement:** NOT weld-real-words — (a) the ticket-means-it inversion kills rote-pair (validity falls out of meaning); (b) NOVEL compounds + part-pictures-only + the `FAMILIAR_RETRIEVAL_SOLVER` kill the familiar-retrieval / picture-leak cheat (the critic's deepest fix); (c) the same-head-wrong-relation primary foil kills blind-concatenation; (d) head-modifier reasoning is implicit ("is it a house or a bird?"); (e) opaque is CUT (no "composition is unreliable" message); the gate proves rote-pair + blind-concat + familiar-retrieval + first/random solvers ALL fail while the composeMeaning oracle passes.
