# SEO demand evidence — second batch of printable worksheet genres (11 markets)

Method: ~180 WebSearch SERP probes (2026-09-02), native phrasing per market, judged on (i) dedicated competitor pages on the market's own worksheet portals, (ii) long-tail phrasing visible in titles, (iii) Pinterest boards, (iv) 2025/2026-dated pages (= still-live demand). No GSC data. Demand tiers: HIGH = ≥4 dedicated native pages incl. a top portal; MED = 1-3 dedicated pages or generic portal coverage; LOW = only generic/marketplace hits; none = nothing native.

Click model: house baseline 1-5 clicks/page/day at maturity; 22 pages per genre (landing + deck × 11). Expected clicks/day = Σ_locale P(rank) × per-page clicks × 2. HIGH-tier locale ≈ 1.5-4/day for the pair; MED ≈ 0.5-1.5; LOW ≈ 0.1-0.4.
