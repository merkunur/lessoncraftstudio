# Reviewers' notes: registry `de`

Written by the reviewers of the crosswalk (`scripts/curricula/apply-review.js`). 306 teaching points carry a reviewer's row; the reason for each is in `frontend/content/curricula/de/crosswalk-authored.json`.

## author-all

- The KMK Bildungsstandards say what a child can do at the end of Klasse 4 and place nothing in a single year. 'teaches' therefore means: the worksheet content belongs to the standard, at the level of its year. For the year before school (K) it was given only where the content is what Klasse 1 itself starts with (counting, numerals, number bonds, tracing letters); pure readiness was marked 'prepares'.
- Fractions: the only fraction standard of the Primarstufe is M.2.1.4, everyday fractions tied to quantities (1/2 m, Dreiviertelstunde, 1/4 l). The seven Klasse 3 fraction points (unit fractions, a/b, number line, comparing, equivalent fractions) go beyond it and are marked 'extends'; in Germany this is Klasse 5 and 6. A German teacher would find a full fraction strand in Klasse 3 unusual.
- The written column method (2:column-method) sits in Klasse 2 on the site; in German schools written addition and subtraction come in Klasse 3. The content is M.1.2.7, only the year is early.
- Temperature is not a quantity the mathematics standards list (Geldwerte, Längen, Zeitspannen, Massen, Hohlmaße, Flächeninhalte, Volumina), so 3:thermometer is only 'prepares'. Inch and foot in 2:choose-units and the X-mark line plot (2:line-plots, 3:line-plot-halves) are American forms; German books use cm / m and Strichliste, Säulendiagramm.
- Odd / even and ordinal numbers have no standard of their own; they were placed under the group M.1.1 (Zahldarstellungen und Zahlbeziehungen verstehen). Reading the time of day was placed under M.2.1.2 and M.2.2.2, although the wording speaks of Zeitspannen.
- Mathematics standards that no point of years K to 3 works toward: all of M.5.2 (chance: sicher, möglich, unmöglich; comparing chances); real combinatorics tasks (M.5.1.4 is only prepared); functional relations in real situations such as amount and price and proportionality (M.3.2.1, M.3.2.3); written multiplication (part of M.1.2.7); numbers beyond 10 000 up to one million (M.1.1.2, M.1.1.3, M.1.2.3 ask for it); writing quantities in several units and with a comma (M.2.2.3); estimating quantities with reference objects and calculating with approximate values (M.2.3.1, M.2.3.2); enlarging and reducing figures (part of M.4.3.1); nets of solids and building plans (examples in M.4.1.2); planning a survey (M.5.1.1) and judging data displays critically (M.5.1.3).
- Process standards of mathematics: explaining and discussing solution paths (M.P.2), giving reasons (M.P.1.3) and making up a word problem for an equation or picture (M.P.4.3) are not reached by any point; a worksheet a child works alone can hardly do the first two.
- Deutsch standards that no point works toward: almost all of D.1 Sprechen und Zuhören (a worksheet cannot do it; only K:story-sequencing prepares telling); reading strategies and reading aloud (D.3.1.5, D.3.1.6, D.3.2, D.3.4); literature and media (D.4.1, D.4.2, D.4.4, D.4.5 and most of D.4.3: genres, children's books, library, digital formats, presenting texts); revising texts (all of D.2.5); checking and correcting spelling in a text and using a dictionary (D.2.2.6, D.2.2.7 only prepared, D.2.2.8, D.5.8.1 only prepared); frequent foreign words (D.2.2.3); language use and variety (D.5.1, D.5.2, D.5.3 except the bilingual cards); sentence parts and the place of the verb (D.5.4.4 is only prepared).
- The most German spelling topics have no point of their own: capital letters for nouns (D.2.2.4 is reached only through sentence beginnings and through articles and word classes), lengthening a word to hear its last letter (Hund / Hunde, Auslautverhärtung), deriving ä / äu (Hand / Hände), double consonants, ie and the silent h, and Merkwörter. A German teacher would miss these in Klasse 2 and 3.
- The 2022 Deutsch standards contain no standard for sound-letter work in beginning reading and writing (Anlaute, sound analysis, blending, letter knowledge). The phonics points of K and Klasse 1 were therefore tied as 'prepares' to D.2.2.2 (schreiben Kernwörter richtig) and to the group D.3.1 (Lesefertigkeiten), which is the honest reading of the wording.
- Points whose skill line is English and was read in its German form: digraphs (K:digraphs-k, 1:digraphs, 2:digraphs) as sch, ch, ei, au, eu, with no counterpart for th; 2:spelling-rules as ck / k, tz / z, ie, ss / ß, with no counterpart for tch, dge, ai / ay; K:cvc-build as short sound-true words; K:sight-words-reading as häufige Wörter; a / an in the article points as der / die / das; 3:person-words as backen to Bäcker.
- Cursive (2:cursive-writing, 2:read-cursive, 3:cursive-sentences): the KMK asks for fluent and legible handwriting but prescribes no joined script; the Länder and schools choose (Grundschrift, Schulausgangsschrift, Vereinfachte Ausgangsschrift, Lateinische Ausgangsschrift).
- The KMK has no Bildungsstandards for Sachunterricht, Kunst or social learning. Of the science, social, health, arts and feelings points, 47 have no rows; rows were given only where a Deutsch or Mathematik standard is really practised (reading a forecast table or a map key, compass directions on a map, views from above, recording a test, reading clues, writing about a picture).
- Three ids carry a '~' (1:days-months-seasons~season-wheel, 2:synonyms~doing-stage, 3:two-attribute-sort~logic-cabinet) and have the same skill line as their parent; they received the parent's rows, except the season wheel, whose season content is Sachunterricht and which is therefore only 'prepares'.
- Mazes, spot-the-difference, shadow matching, picture analogies and step-by-step drawing have no counterpart; a row was given only for a single face that practises a standard (the shortest way in a town, writing a sentence about each difference, writing about one's own drawing).
