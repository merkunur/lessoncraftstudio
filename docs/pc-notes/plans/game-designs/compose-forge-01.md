## 2. Concept & creative hook
**Mim the glow-smith forges LIGHT.** Mim fuses translucent glowing glass shards (pattern-block primitives) into bigger lit shapes — **"lanterns"** — that flare warm and float to a glowing shelf.
- **THE COMPOSITION IS VISIBLE (Designer A's winning insight):** shards are **translucent stained-glass**, so the child SEES the big glow is literally MADE OF the little glows — the seams of their OWN tiling glow through the finished lantern. Part-to-whole is perceptual, not abstract.
- **BLANK SILHOUETTE (the anti-seam-read lock):** the target is a single **dark outer outline only** — NO internal piece-lines, ever. The tiling is the child's to DISCOVER.
- **INTERCHANGEABLE SHARDS on a shared lattice (the anti-jigsaw lock):** every triangle is identical and fits anywhere a triangle-cell is empty + inside the boundary; no shard is "keyed" to one hole. The child MUST reason which COMBINATION covers the whole.
- **COMPOSITION MADE LOAD-BEARING BY GEOMETRY (the critic's F1):** targets are shaped so a pure **rim-walk of all-triangles is NOT the easy solve** — interior regions are only cleanly coverable by composing LARGER shards (a trapezoid where 3 loose triangles would leave an unfillable sliver given the rationed supply), so the child must think in composite units, not perimeter-accrete. Tiny 2-triangle / single-rhombus targets are **explicitly labeled K on-ramps** (the one place boundary-following is fine — composition there is trivial anyway).
- **SNAP IS NOT A CORRECTNESS SIGNAL (the critic's F1b):** a dropped shard that is on-lattice + inside the boundary + non-overlapping **stays as translucent-GREY un-fused glass** with NO pre-commit warmer/colder feedback; legality ≠ being part of a valid final tiling. Evaluation is **whole-silhouette, at-commit-only** — there is no per-piece "you're right so far" leak, and no late-round snap-elimination oracle (illegal anchors give nothing; legal-but-doomed placements are allowed, so "it stayed" tells the child nothing).
- **THE RECOMPOSE BEAT (the deep clause, mandatory):** a finished lantern can **crack back into its shards** and become the parts of a NEW, different lantern — the whole becomes a part. Mim: *"This same light wants to become something new — re-forge it."*
- **THE PAYOFF:** exact coverage → the lantern flares warm, Mim hops + cheers (`ctx.speak`), it floats to the shelf. `ctx.correct()` auto-advance. Wrong → no red; an illegal shard drifts back ("try turning it"); removing a shard is always free.
- **Anti-generic statement:** NOT a perimeter-jigsaw in a glow costume — (a) interchangeable shards + blank silhouette + region-coverage validity kill jigsaw/seam-read; (b) **interior-only-by-composite target geometry + commit-only eval + grey un-fused legality** kill boundary-following AND snap-elimination (the critic's deepest catch); (c) the multiplicity "ways gallery" + multiset-signature acceptor make "same whole, many ways" load-bearing; (d) the recompose beat delivers the standard's second clause; (e) the gate proves a jigsaw + seam-reader + brute-snap + **boundary-follower + snap-elimination** solver ALL fail while a real composer passes.

## 3. Core loop & the 6 genuinely-distinct cognitions
**Loop (`stage`: discover the tiling → fuse → flare):** Mim shows a blank lantern outline → child picks a shard (tap/keyboard), rotates it (↻ / `R`, fixed 60°), places it (snaps on-lattice inside the boundary, stays grey un-fused) → keeps composing → the instant the union exactly covers the outline (no gap/overlap/spill) it flares → `ctx.correct()`. **No timer/score/streak/countdown/fail/attempts/"X of Y".** Wrong → illegal shard drifts back (warm nudge); free removal; per-pass order-only reshuffle.

**The 6 pedagogy-locked cognitions (each a different reasoning ACT — NOT a shape re-skin; the ≥7 backbone per critic F3). Each round carries a `cog`:**
| # | Cognition (`cog`) | The child's act | Why genuinely distinct |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ★ | **build-the-target** (`build` — also the K.G.B.6 act) | fill a blank silhouette from scratch | foundational part→whole synthesis |
| 2 ★ | **build-it-another-way** (`reway` — the multiplicity flagship) | re-tile the SAME whole a structurally-different way; the game remembers the first **piece-MULTISET** and accepts only a different one | reasons about EQUIVALENT decompositions (hexagon = 6 triangles OR 3 rhombi OR 2 trapezoids); confronts the "one right answer" misconception |
| 3 ★ | **decompose-then-recompose** (`recompose` — the deep clause) | a finished composite cracks into its shards → build a NEW different target from exactly those shards | runs composition in reverse then forward; the target CHANGES |
| 4 ★ | **substitute-an-equivalent** (`substitute` — Grade-1 stretch, region-coverage framing ONLY) | swap a sub-region in place — replace 1 rhombus with the 2 triangles that **cover the same space** (never "half"/"same amount") | local part-for-part equivalence; bridge to later unit-substitution |
| 5 ★ | **make-a-new-shape-from-the-composite** (`extend`) | extend/join finished composites into a LARGER named shape (rhombus + triangles → trapezoid → hexagon; square + triangle → house) | the whole-becomes-a-part direction; iterative composition |
| 6 ★ | **match-a-named-composite** (`named` — outline-first, universal shapes) | build to a spoken/named target with a faint outline guide; judge when it IS that shape | language→shape mapping + completion judgment |
- **Honest framing (pedagogy + critic F3/F4):** **6 genuinely-distinct cognitions** — NOT padded to a fake 7th. **predict-the-melt was DROPPED** (it's recognition; 1.G.A.2 is the production verb *compose*). **"fewest-pieces" was DROPPED** (padding + it re-introduces the "bigger=better" misconception — the 6-triangle and 2-trapezoid hexagons are EQUALLY celebrated). **Mirror/symmetry rounds HARD-BANNED** (the original 4.G.A.3 bug).
- **§A.13.60 ≥7 distinct-to-the-child experiences:** met by the **6 distinct cognitive ACTS × genuinely-different target composites** (hexagon, rectangle, big-triangle, trapezoid, parallelogram, house, star, circle-from-half-circles) — different *recognizable shapes* = the §A.13.60 different-shapes standard (the fractions-case fix), NOT "one plain shape reconfigured." A session draws ≥7 rounds each distinct by ACT and/or by recognizable composite. Within-cognition shape variety is freshness, never the ≥7 backbone. Order-only reshuffle + the §20.2 per-session Fisher-Yates.
- **Free slice = build (on-ramp + one interior-by-composite target) + build-it-another-way + match-a-named** — proves composition, multiplicity, and naming; the boundary-follower + snap-elimination + jigsaw + brute solvers all fail on the interior-by-composite target.

## 4. Win-states & juice (no competition)
No timer/score/streak/countdown/**fail-state**/attempts/"X of Y". **THE FLARE (signature):** exact coverage → the lantern blooms warm (CSS opacity/scale; reduced-motion → instant soft glow), Mim hops + `ctx.speak("You forged it!")`, the lantern floats up. **Two non-numeric vessels:** (a) **the Lantern Shelf** (`GameCollection` — a growing wall of glowing lanterns; abundance, never a count); (b) **the "ways gallery"** (pedagogy's discovery log — little thumbnails of each DISTINCT tiling the child found of a target; celebrates divergence, **never a quota / rank / "3 of 5 — find the rest" / piece-count**). **Warm wrong-handling (critic F9):** an illegal placement (off-lattice / overlap / spill) → the shard gently drifts back, soft chime, optional `ctx.speak` nudge ("try turning it" / "what if two small ones go here?") — NO red, NO X, NO counter; legal-but-grey placements never buzz; removal is always free. **Glow is celebration ONLY, never a legality/correctness channel** — legality is shown by the shard seating on-lattice + ARIA, so a low-vision / reduced-motion / mono child never depends on the glow (not-color-only). The smith never "wrong"-s the child. `ctx.wrong()` suppressed to a warm nudge; `ctx.correct()` on exact coverage → auto-advance (no Check button). CSS/SVG/WebAudio + TTS.

## 5. Pedagogy
- **1.G.A.2 instantiation:** the child reasons which shards' edges/angles MATE to tile a blank target, holds the whole in mind, and treats the assembled region as a new shape — then RE-composes it (the deep clause). Validity = region-coverage, the structural proof of composition over jigsaw.
- **THE KEYSTONE RULES (pedagogy + critic):** (i) **region-tiled-by-edges, never piece-in-keyed-slot** (anti-jigsaw). (ii) **blank silhouette, no internal seams** (anti-seam-read). (iii) **interior-only-by-composite target geometry + commit-only eval + grey un-fused legality** (anti-boundary-follow + anti-snap-elimination — the critic's F1). (iv) **both clauses** — build AND recompose/extend (recompose mandatory). (v) **multiple tilings celebrated, never ranked/quota'd; no fewest-pieces** (anti "bigger=better"). (vi) **orientation-invariance** — rotated trapezoid valid + still named trapezoid. (vii) **gap/overlap intolerance, KINDLY** — a sliver just doesn't fuse; no shame.
- **§20.8 demonstrate-don't-touch (the critic's headline):** as a perimeter-accretable / snap-leaking design it TOUCHES 1.G.A.2 (a child rim-walks pieces without composing). As an interior-by-composite + commit-only + multiset-multiplicity design it DIRECTLY INSTANTIATES composition (reasoning about combinations is the only path). The line is exactly: a pure all-triangle rim-walk must NOT be the easy solve, and "a piece stayed" must carry no correctness. The gate (boundary-follower + snap-elimination + jigsaw + seam-reader + brute-snap all FAIL; the composer oracle PASSES) is the machine-checked proof.
- **also_teaches:** `1.G.A.1` LIGHT (naming + orientation-invariance). **Hard NO:** `4.G.A.3` (symmetry — original defect), `1.G.A.3`/`2.G.A.2` (equal shares — the substitute cognition is region-coverage ONLY, lexicon-linted across 11 locales), `3.MD` (area-count), `2.G.A.1`.
- **Grade fit + piece set:** **K (K.G.B.6):** 2-3 straight pieces (triangle/square/rectangle), build-the-larger-shape, NO curves, light/no recompose. **Grade 1 (1.G.A.2):** full named set incl. half/quarter-circles (a separate square/curve lattice round-family) + hexagon multi-tiling + recompose. The ladder: K on-ramp (2-3 pieces) → G1 hexagon multi-way → recompose/extend → substitute + named.
- **No-shame:** the forged light (shelf fills) is the intrinsic reward; illegal shards drift back, never a red-X; the smith never fails the child.

## 6. Interaction & UX
- **Runtime: discover → fuse → flare.** `shapeforge-core.js` holds the round `{ id, cog, lattice ('tri'|'square'), target:{cells[(r,c,up)], outline (single closed subpath, NO seams)}, palette[{pieceId,count}] (ABUNDANT + interchangeable + rationed so interior needs composites), cognitionId }` + `cellsOf(placement)` (integer, via rotation permutation table) + `isComplete(placements,target)` (= integer set-equality: union == target cells, no cell twice, none outside) + `place`/`remove`/`rotate`. **The answer = `f(which shards COMBINE to tile the region)`, never `f(piece in its hole)` / `f(rim-walk)` / `f(piece stayed)`.**
- **State separation (anti-cheat):** `snapshot()` exposes `{ cog, palette (interchangeable types + abundance, NO keyed slots), targetOutline (boundary only — no seam data, no cell list in the render payload), placedShards (grey-un-fused vs fused), waysGallery }`. `facts()` exposes per-round `{ blankSilhouette (single subpath, seams===null), interchangeablePieces (≥1 type fits >1 anchor), multipleValidTilings (≥2 distinct piece-MULTISETS), interiorByComposite (true for non-on-ramp; rim-walk-all-triangles NOT a solution), commitOnlyEval (true; no pre-commit correctness), snapNotCorrectness (legal placements include doomed ones), overlapRejected, spillRejected, partialRejected, fewestPiecesValued (===false), symmetryTask (===false), equalSharesLexicon (===false) }`.
- **Drag + rotate:** pointer-capture drag (one path for mouse/touch/pen) → snap to nearest integer lattice anchor on release (snap radius = half a cell, UX only); tap-to-rotate +60° (`rot=(rot+1)%6`; square family %4); `R` key rotates focused piece. NO Check button — auto-fire on exact coverage.
- **Mobile @280px (critic F8):** vertical grid — prompt (real DOM text) → build-area `<svg>` (`aspect-ratio:1; max-height:48vh`; viewBox scales the lattice, never overflows) → rotate control + selected-piece preview (≥44px) → palette (one button per TYPE + abundance dot-strip, `flex-wrap`, ≥44px; small pieces get a transparent ≥44px hit-rect) → ways-gallery + shelf (thin). **Target cell-count CAPPED <360px** so the smallest shard stays ≥44px + legible; `audit-game-mobile.js` runs the LARGEST target × {empty, mid, full} at 280-768.
- **Accessibility (critic F7 — explicit, not a drag-afterthought):** the **select-piece-then-select-region** path IS the keyboard/SR path: pick a palette piece (Enter; "triangle selected") → cycle its 6 orientations (`R`; announced) → Tab through engine-exposed **named candidate anchor REGIONS** ("top-left corner, empty" — NOT raw row/col/up) → Enter places (aria-live: "trapezoid placed; the bottom corner is still open"). Completion announces + `ctx.correct()`. A **keyboard-only oracle solve is gate-asserted**. Reduced-motion → instant seat/flare. Mute-safe (shards + outline + ARIA carry everything). Real-text prompts/shape-names (never baked art). Not color-only (legality = seating + ARIA; glow is celebration only).

## 8. Runtime mapping + MEASURED build-gate + PER-LOCALE
- **Round descriptor (DATA; answer emergent from coverage, NEVER per-piece slot):** `{ id, cog (build/reway/recompose/substitute/extend/named), lattice ('tri'|'square'), target:{cells, outline}, palette[{pieceId,count}] (abundant+interchangeable+rationed-for-interior-composites), cognitionId }`. `makeRounds`: ≥7 distinct rounds spanning all 6 cognitions × genuinely-different composites; ≥1 K on-ramp tagged K.G.B.6; non-on-ramp targets are interior-by-composite; reway/recompose targets have ≥2 distinct piece-MULTISET tilings; order-only reshuffle.
- **`verify-shapeforge-core.js` (MEASURED — drives the REAL `cellsOf`/`isComplete`/`rotate`; HALTS build) — the critic-armed solver set:**
  1. **COMPOSER ORACLE PASSES 100%:** backtracking composer using only `isComplete`/`cellsOf` → finds a valid complete tiling for every shipped target.
  2. **JIGSAW solver FAILS:** assumes one unique slot per piece → ill-defined (interchangeable pieces) → cannot converge; assert `interchangeablePieces` (≥1 type fits >1 legal anchor) + the region is solvable by ≥2 distinct piece-TYPE combinations.
  3. **SEAM-READER solver FAILS:** given only the outline, places along internal seams → finds ZERO (blank silhouette); assert `blankSilhouette` (outline = single closed subpath; `target.seams===null`; render payload carries NO cell list / internal edges).
  4. **BRUTE-FORCE-SNAP solver FAILS:** drops all pieces snapped to first legal anchors → overshoot/overlap → `isComplete` false; assert `overlapRejected` + `spillRejected` + `partialRejected` (strict commit eval).
  5. **GREEDY-BOUNDARY-FOLLOWER solver (the critic's F1 — NEW) FAILS on every non-on-ramp target:** lays pieces inward along the outer boundary by local edge-match, never reasoning about the whole → leaves an unfillable interior sliver given the rationed supply → never `isComplete`; assert `interiorByComposite` (a pure all-triangle rim-walk is NOT a solution; any boundary-SOLUBLE target is REJECTED at build, except labeled K on-ramps).
  6. **SNAP-ELIMINATION solver (the critic's F1b — NEW) FAILS:** treats "the piece stayed (legal)" as a correctness signal and greedily keeps any legal placement → reaches dead-ends (legal-but-doomed placements exist) → never reliably `isComplete`; assert `snapNotCorrectness` (legal placements include doomed ones) + `commitOnlyEval` (no pre-commit correctness rendered).
  7. **MIRROR/SWAP solver (the critic's F2) FAILS on `reway` rounds:** produces a "different" tiling by mirroring or swapping two identical triangles → REJECTED by the **piece-MULTISET signature** acceptor → not counted as a new way; assert `multipleValidTilings` (≥2 distinct piece-MULTISETS) per reway/recompose target.
  - **STRUCTURAL asserts:** `ROTATION_CLOSES` (6× tri / 4× square rotate = identity); `fewestPiecesValued===false`; `symmetryTask===false` (no round scored on halves matching); `equalSharesLexicon===false` (substitute prompts lint-clean of "half/fourth/equal share/same amount" across all 11 locales); **≥7 distinct experiences anchored to the 6 COGNITIONS × different composites** (NOT outline-count); `ABUNDANT_SUPPLY` (palette total > minimal solution); a **keyboard-only oracle solve** passes (a11y path is real).
  - Plus standing gates: `audit-game-mobile.js` (largest target × {empty,mid,full}; ≥44px; 280-768; cell-floor), `audit-game-variety.js` (≥7 distinct cognitions/composites + per-pass reshuffle), `audit-no-competition.js` (lcs-shell 0-line; no timer/score/streak; warm wrong-handling; first-attempt internal-only), SEO/JSON-LD §21.
- **PER-LOCALE — NORMAL math fan-out (NOT currency-bound):** geometry is universal; only the chrome + shape NAMES localize. **EN pilot; full 11-locale eligible.** Shape names (trapezoid/rhombus + any spoken named-targets) + named-composite list (restricted to **culturally-universal** shapes — outline-first everywhere, critic F6/A1) authored via the **§A.13.48 native ensemble** when each locale lands (NOT auto-translate; "trapezoid/trapezium" + no clean K-1 "rhombus" in several locales is a real per-locale call). Framework NAME localizes (§20.10/§A.13.49); CCSS `1.G.A.2`/`K.G.B.6` the machine/SEO anchor. 0 lines to any protected core — a NEW clean `engine-shapeforge.js`/`shapeforge-core.js` (construct-build sibling), reusing `game-shell.js`, `LCSAudio.speak`, `GameAssets`, `GameCollection`.
- **Feasibility:** SIMPLE — DOM/SVG/CSS/WebAudio/TTS with an **integer geometry kernel** (no canvas, no physics, no polygon-union lib; float lives only in the render layer, recomputed from integers each frame → no drift). Risks + mitigations: boundary-following (interior-by-composite geometry + the boundary-follower gate adversary — the headline); snap-elimination (commit-only eval + grey legality + the snap-elimination adversary); jigsaw/seam (interchangeable pieces + blank silhouette); fake "another way" (multiset signature); a11y on a tri lattice (named-region keyboard path + keyboard-only oracle); 280px cell-floor; square/curve family kept on a SEPARATE lattice (ship tri first). **Build the boundary-follower + snap-elimination + jigsaw solvers FIRST** — they reject a perimeter-soluble / snap-leaking / keyed-slot build; that's the gate working.
