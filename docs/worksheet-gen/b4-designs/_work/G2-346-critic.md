## 5 Quality verdict

I would print this for a third-grade class, and I would pin the base page up. The rule box says the whole rule with ten digits and two arrows and no sentence a child has to read, the 7 in the example sits ringed in the up group, and then there are twelve numbers and twelve boxes and nothing else: no line to lean on, no chip to guess between, the half present where the number-line pages refuse it. The sort page is the it / fr teachers' "per eccesso o per difetto" as they actually say it, with more cells than numbers so the last two cannot be inferred; the hundred page is the same page one digit to the left; the estimate page is the German and Norwegian genre sheet ("runden und überschlagen") with the exact sum never printed and the `=` only where it is true; the field page asks the real inverse question with one target, both near-misses on the page and 45 always among the members; the two-box page finally has its headers where they cannot overlap. What would embarrass me: a `≈` that fell back to a system font because someone typed it instead of drawing it (the gate bans the codepoint page-wide); a meta promising an answer key the ZIP does not contain (banned in every string); a French "arrondi CP"; an sv F2 title that is G3-323's title with one word respelled; and, on the field page, a worked example that quietly gives away a member, which the design would have shipped and this merge moves to 83.
