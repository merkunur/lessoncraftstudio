## The skill
Compose a shape from tangram pieces to fill a shadow exactly, and tell a turned or flipped arrangement from the matching one.

## The apparatus and its invention
A dark SHADOW on a light table and the seven tangram pieces in a tray. The child drags pieces into the shadow, tapping a piece to turn it 45° and a flip handle to flip it; a piece only settles when it lies fully inside the shadow without overlapping another — a piece that sticks out casts its own little shadow outside the dark area. Match mode (worksheet face): three finished solutions sit beside the shadow; the child drags each over the shadow — only the exact one lies flat inside; turned and flipped ones stick out.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Accepting a flipped or turned arrangement as "the same" and thinking a shape only fits one way round.
- **Pictures:** none (tangram pieces are apparatus geometry)
- **Settings:** Mode: build the shadow / pick the matching solution · Help: piece outlines shown / no outlines
- **Rounds:** ≥ 8 shadows (animals, house, boat, rocket… made of 5–7 pieces) per mode; in match mode the right solution's position is balanced across the three slots.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G2-347
- **Distinct from:** Shapeforge (Mim's Glow Workshop) fuses triangles/rhombi without orientation demands; no activity uses tangrams or tests flips.
- **Build risk:** Low. Tangram outlines must not read as hand-drawn pictures — they are silhouettes made of the pieces.
