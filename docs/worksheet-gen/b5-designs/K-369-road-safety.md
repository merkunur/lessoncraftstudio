## 2 The base page

**Concept.** "Stop or go? Six little streets." (K). Two columns of three side-view street strips. In the middle of each strip stands ONE traffic light on its pole with exactly one lamp lit; on its left the waiting outcome (a person standing on the kerb, or a car waiting at the stop line), on its right the going outcome (a person walking on the zebra, or a car driving on with motion lines), each under its word pill. The child circles the side the lit lamp tells that road user to take. The light sits BETWEEN its two outcomes, so the decision is a fork the eye travels; stop-left / go-right never changes, so a five-year-old spends the page reading lamps, not hunting for choices. The outcome pictures say whose light it is before a word is read (walkers = the pedestrian's light, cars = the car's light), backed by lamp count (3 vs 2), lamp shape (circle vs rounded square) and height (the pedestrian head sits ≥ 40 px lower, A's cue). The only colour on the page is six lit lamps.
