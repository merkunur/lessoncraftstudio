## The skill
Name the number before, after and between, and count backward from 10 to 1.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE HOPPER THAT HAS TO TURN AROUND. A hopscotch court of ten numbered squares (1–10; 11–20 on a second court above it in the to-20 setting) with one library animal standing on a square, facing forward (toward bigger numbers). A sign card above the court shows the task by icon only: an arrow forward = the number after, an arrow back = the number before, two flags on two squares = the number between them (the hopper then stands on the lower flag). The child taps the square where the hopper must land. Facing is physical: tapping a square BEHIND a forward-facing hopper makes it bump in place (a small shake, its facing arrow flashes) — the child must first tap the hopper to TURN it round, so 'before' is a turn plus one hop. A tap two squares away makes it hop there and leave a dotted trail of two arcs, so 'one before / one after' is seen as ONE hop, not two. Right: it lands, the square gets a chalk mark, bubble 'Yes — 6 comes before 7!'. Miss 1: the trail and the facing arrow stay lit; miss 2: the start square and the target square pulse. Countdown setting: every square is blank except the start (10, 12, 15 or 20); the hopper already faces back; the child hops square by square, choosing each landing number from a numeral strip under the court (the strip is reshuffled every hop), and the chosen numeral is stamped on the square — so the backward sequence is SAID by the child, never read. A wrong numeral bounces off; miss 2 reads the stamped run so far. The run ends on 1 and a library rocket (space/rocket) lifts off from square 1 — never 0.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Confusing before with after (giving n+1 for "before"), giving n−2 / n+2, and being unable to count backward without restarting from 1.
- **Pictures:** the hopper, one per round set, CSS-mirrored for the turn: farm-animals/rabbit, pets/frog, forest-creatures/squirrel, farm-animals/duck, animals/cat; space/rocket for the countdown finish; court, sign card, flags and trail drawn as apparatus
- **Settings:** Range: up to 10 / up to 20 · Task: before–after–between mixed / countdown only
- **Rounds:** Mixed: 9 rounds, ≥ 3 before, ≥ 3 after, ≥ 2 between; the hopper's start spread across the court (on an end square in ≤ 2 rounds; 'before 1' and 'after 10/20' never asked); between-flags are always two apart. Countdown: 8 runs (to 10: starts 10, 9, 8, 7 with ≥ 5 hops each; to 20: 20, 15, 12, 11 …). The answer is a square on a fixed court — its position IS the number, which is the skill, not a tell; the countdown numeral strip is reshuffled every hop so the right numeral has no fixed place. Order-only reshuffle per pass.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** K-021, K-022, K-023, K-308
- **Distinct from:** Peek Path (#2) and Constellation Thread (#3) only go forward and hide the run-up; the Number Elevator (#19) is magnitude. This is the first instrument where DIRECTION is the content.
- **Build risk:** At 360 a row of ten squares is < 34 px: on phones each court wraps into two left-to-right rows of five (reading order, as on a hundreds chart) with squares ≥ 44 px, and the hop from 5 to 6 draws its arc down to the next row. The mirrored hopper must face the way it hops (check per noun).
