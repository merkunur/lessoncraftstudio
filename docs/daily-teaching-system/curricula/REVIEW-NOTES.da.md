# Reviewers' notes: registry `da`

Written by the reviewers of the crosswalk (`scripts/curricula/apply-review.js`). 306 teaching points carry a reviewer's row; the reason for each is in `frontend/content/curricula/da/crosswalk-authored.json`.

## author-all

- Since 2025 only the kompetencemål and opmærksomhedspunkter are binding, and they are very broad. Almost all mathematics lands on MAT.3.K2 or MAT.3.K3 and almost all science on NT.2.K3 or NT.2.K2. This is true to the curriculum but tells a teacher little; Danish teachers still plan from the guiding færdigheds- og vidensområder (for example Regnestrategier, Geometriske egenskaber, Afkodning, Sproglig bevidsthed), which this list does not hold.
- No binding wording names fractions. MAT.3.K2 says 'beregninger med naturlige tal', so all fraction points of grades 2-3 are marked 'extends'. In Denmark simple fractions (halve, kvarte) are met in 3. klasse, and comparing or equivalent fractions in 4.-5. klasse.
- Placed later in Denmark than on the site: fluent adding and subtracting within 20 and number bonds to 20 (K on the site, 1. klasse in Denmark); mental addition to 100 (1 on the site, 2. klasse); the column method (2 on the site, often 3. klasse, after the pupils' own methods); rounding (2 on the site, 3. klasse); division with remainder and the rhombus (3 on the site, often 4. klasse); the solar system and continents (1-2 on the site, 3.-4. klasse).
- Grammar and vocabulary (ordklasser, stedord, synonymer, modsætninger, forstavelser, rodmorfemer) are named by no binding goal. They are marked 'prepares' on DAN.2.K4 or DAN.2.K1, because those goals are about communicating and reading, not about knowing grammar. Teachers will still look for these sheets under 'Sproglig bevidsthed'.
- Too English to place as written: K:digraphs-k, 1:digraphs, 2:digraphs (sh/ch/th do not exist in Danish; use ng, sj, hv, hj, nd, ld), 2:spelling-rules (ck/k, tch/ch, -dge, ai/ay; the Danish counterparts are dobbeltkonsonant and stumme bogstaver), 2:prefixes (un-, dis-, re-; Danish u-, mis-, gen-), K:articles and 1:articles (Danish en / et). The rows given hold only if the sheets are rebuilt with the Danish patterns.
- Danish letters: the alphabet has 29 letters ending in æ, ø, å, and nine vowels (a, e, i, o, u, y, æ, ø, å). The point of attention BH.0.O1 leaves out q, w, x and z, so a b/d/p/q sheet asks for a letter that børnehaveklassen is not held to.
- Measurement and money: Danish sheets must use cm, m, g, kg, ml (or dl) and l only, never inches or feet; decilitre (dl) is the everyday Danish unit for jugs and is missing from the skill lines. Money is in kroner with no cents. Time is written on the 24-hour clock and there is no a.m./p.m.
- Content of these years that teachers will look for and no teaching point covers: chance ('udtrykke intuitive chancestørrelser', MAT.3.K4), use of the calculator (MAT.3.O2 'hovedregning og lommeregner'), reading speed and fluency (DAN.4.O1 'ca. 120 ord/minuttet'), history with sources and old-days-and-now (HIS.4.K1-K3), first aid (FAE.3.K2), body and gender diversity (SSF.3.K2), talking about pictures (BIL.3.K2), and cycling in traffic.
- Thinking-skills puzzles (mazes, sudoku, logic grids, spot the difference, analogies, secret code) have no goal and are left without rows, as are wants-and-needs and community helpers: the Danish primary years have no civics subject (samfundsfag starts in 8. klasse).
- Health: 'four food groups' is not a Danish model; schools use De officielle Kostråd (and often the Y-tallerken). Joined handwriting (sammenbundet skrift) is not named in the binding text and many schools teach little of it, so the cursive points are only 'prepares'.
- Things in the objective list that look wrong to me: MAT.3.O1 reads 'Elevene kan' where the official text has 'Eleven kan'; historie is shown as years 1-4 but the subject is taught from 3. klasse; færdselslære and sundheds- og seksualundervisning og familiekundskab are shown as years 1-3 but are obligatory topics from børnehaveklassen; billedkunst is shown as 'efter 3. klassetrin', where I expect the first goals efter 2. klassetrin; and the validity period ends on 2026-10-09, so it should be checked whether a newer bekendtgørelse has replaced BEK 1715 and 1716.
