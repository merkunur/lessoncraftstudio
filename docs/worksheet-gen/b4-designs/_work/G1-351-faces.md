## Deviations from §3 (each measured or ruled)
1. **F3 title "Under 100", not "1 to 100".** §3 / §6 title the face "1 to 100" while the page draws 10-99 (100 needs a third box, one-digit numbers leave the tens box empty: both refused as items by the design). The brief's rule "a title that names a range names exactly what the d2 page shows" wins: "Odd and Even Numbers Under 100: Look at the Ones Box" names a space every number on the page lives in, keeps the "100" head (rule 7's 100-idiom check passes) and the gate asserts no row title says "1 to 100". OPEN for the panels / reviewer: the en harvest head is "1 to 100"; the ten locales choose their idiom ("bis 100" / "tot 100" / "sataan asti" name a ceiling, not a start, and pass as they are).
2. **F1 equation row ABOVE the dots panel.** §3 draws the panel first. Measured: the `.ws-card-badge` (30 x 30 at the card's top-left) covered 18 x 18 px of the white panel's corner, and at the 677 chrome the card has 2.75 px to spare so no margin can push the panel down.
