## Why it was rebuilt, and the finding that decided it

⭐⭐ **`lids.js:47` had already ruled this tool's entire mechanic DEAD, by name** —
*"cover part of a known total and ask what is under it"* — because
`part-whole-frame` ships three cloths over whole/a/b **in its FREE tier**
(`part-whole-frame.js:151`). #55 fenced diligently against `lids` and
`vet-diagnosis` and **never fenced against the third neighbour**, then shipped a
strictly weaker version of a free tool. Verified before acting.

**The remainder, after subtraction rather than negotiation:** nothing on any of
the four surfaces reveals information about a fixed situation ONE PIECE AT A
TIME. Sixteen tools carry a phase field and not one accretes (`the-gap` conceals
then REPLACES — `:873` returns 0 marks during the gap). There is no word-problem
type among the 240 printables. Teachers hand-build the "Numberless" reveal as
PowerPoint slides.

**So the verb changed: it ACCRETES, it never covers.** At stage 0 the quantities
do not exist on screen — nothing has been said. Each press adds one fact.
THE STAND · THE NICHE · THE SLIP (⚠ RAIL, SEAT and TAGS — the obvious names —
are all already named parts of other tools).

## What the ten native panels found by reading the MODEL

They were handed the English as a **source to audit**, not a target to translate.
Nine real bugs no gate on this shelf could see:

- ⭐⭐ **the told numerals REWROTE THEMSELVES** on an arrangement switch, and in
  one direction the new answer was the number on screen a second earlier — the
  tool showed the class the answer then asked for it (it, fr, pt, independently)
- ⭐⭐ **the band stopped governing** after that switch: 138 cases, worst w=19 on
  a ten-band, so the class would count TEN wells while the tool announced
  NINETEEN (sv)
- ⭐⭐ **the counting strip named the question in frame one** at opacity .45,
  before anything was linked — defeating stage zero entirely (pt)
- ⭐⭐ **a free user's Ctrl+P printed the whole page** — `beforeprint` returned
  without adding the class every print rule is scoped to. The #40/#41 defect,
  introduced BY the scoping fix meant to prevent it (fi)
- two dead strings, the refusal shake landing on the wrong control, the active
  ask button being the faded one, and an inert motion constant

⚠ **The English moved eleven times as a result, and a SECOND delta round found
more** — `saidAtFloor` said "the two parts", family-specific in exactly the way
"altogether" was (in `compare` the other two are the smaller and the difference).
