## 2. Concept & creative hook
**Sprout the bunny gardener carpets IRREGULAR garden beds (an L-shaped flower bed, a staircase pond) completely with moss-patch unit squares — no bare gaps, no overlapping patches — and when the bed is fully covered the patches KNIT into one blooming meadow whose AREA is the count of patches it took.**
- **THE BED IS IRREGULAR — NO L×W (the multiply kill — pedagogy):** the beds are L-shapes / staircases (NOT clean rectangles), so there is no single length and width to multiply (L×W is undefined) and no rows×columns to read as an array. The data is a cell-SET, never dimensions. Covering + counting is the only path.
- **THE PATCHES KNIT INTO A MEADOW (the object-counting kill — the critic's deepest fix):** once the bed is fully covered (no gaps, no overlaps), the individual moss-patch outlines DISSOLVE into one continuous bloomed meadow — so the child CANNOT count separated tiles like 12 apples; the AREA is what the COVERING took (reported from the covering act, or by re-entering cover mode to re-lay the patches). The number is the MEASURE of the space, not a tally of objects. A `COUNT_VISIBLE_OBJECTS_SOLVER` provably fails (no separated tiles to count at report time).
- **COVER COMPLETELY, NO GAPS/NO OVERLAPS (the conceptual heart):** the find-and-fix mode hands the child a flawed covering with a hidden bare gap AND a hidden double-stacked patch (the placements-MULTISET makes overlaps real); the child spots + repairs both before the area is trustworthy. No-gaps/no-overlaps is the load-bearing measurement constraint.
- **SAME GROUND, DIFFERENT SHAPE (conservation — a core mode):** the fern bed is an L of 8 patches; it re-grows downstream as a plus-shape; cover it → also 8. "Different shape, SAME amount of ground." Area is a conserved attribute of the SPACE — the strongest proof the child measures space, not shape or tile-arrangement.
- **THE PAYOFF:** a correct area → the bed blooms into a flowering meadow, a flower (varied species) joins Sprout's growing meadow panorama (`GameCollection` — non-numeric, non-area abundance; NEVER a count). `ctx.correct()`. A wrong covering → no fail; bare cells shimmer ("a little ground still needs a patch"), overlaps slide back ("that bit's already cared for"); a wrong area-pick re-highlights the uncovered corners ("those corners are empty — count only the moss").
- **Anti-generic statement:** NOT count-the-pre-drawn-squares — (a) the irregular beds + no-L/W kill multiply-dimensions; (b) cover-from-empty kills count-pre-placed; (c) no-stored-area kills read-total; (d) **the patches knit into a meadow + the `COUNT_VISIBLE_OBJECTS_SOLVER` kill the object-counting cheat** (the critic's deepest fix — the count is the measure of the space, not a tally); (e) conservation proves area-as-space; the gate proves multiply + count-pre-placed + read-total + **count-visible-objects** + first/random solvers ALL fail while the cover-and-count oracle passes.
