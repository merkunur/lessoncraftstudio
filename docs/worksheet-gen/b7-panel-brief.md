## Load-bearing rules (every one cost a defect in an earlier batch)
- **Titles = the genre name a teacher in your country types into Google** (the heads in `_PANEL-FINDINGS.md` and each final's §1 / §6), ≤ 70 chars, never your language's worksheet word (the engine appends it), unique in the band against ALL existing titles, never a neighbour's title: the live K-061 "spot the differences" bare head in your locale (Unterschiede entdecken · Halla las diferencias · Ache as diferenças · Trouver les différences · Trova le differenze · Zoek de verschillen · Hitta skillnaderna · Find forskellene · Finn forskjellene · Etsi erot — EVERY find title carries its count or its move instead), K-063 "Mirror, Mirror", the tracing families' "tracing" / "nachspuren" / "Schwungübungen" words, the colouring families' "malen / colorear" heads, K-286's grid words ("Raster / rutnät / ruudukko" only WITH the animal first), K-379's story words. A find title with a count carries the count your market types (digits 3 / 5 / 7 / 10, or the Nordic number words "fem fel / fem fejl / fem feil / viisi virhettä"); the how-many face carries NO number anywhere. A how-to-draw title names its ANIMAL through the unit token: `{U}` / `{L}` = your title form with its article ("a Cat" · "eine Katze" · "un chat"), `{N}` = the bare noun ("Horse" · "Pferd") — one token per title, no other.
- **Instructions ≤ 150 chars, one child-level sentence, name ONLY apparatus that is on that face's page** (find: pictures 1 and 2, circle, the tick boxes; the count box; the word list; the fold line; the empty place; the rows; the two word strips — never "draw", "colour in" or "sort"; how-to-draw: the numbered steps, the orange lines, the paper, the grey animal to trace, the grid, the fold, the ruled lines — never a key, never a screen). Fit the chrome (722 body; 677 with a 4-line fi title).
- **The unit forms (how-to-draw) are your literals** — `forms[unit] = {t, stem, n}` per animal: `t` the title form with the article your head wants, `n` the bare noun, `stem` the letter-run every inflection shares (fi "kiss" covers kissa / kissan); the instruction must name the animal in its written-out inflected form. **The word-face noun (`nouns[unit]`) and the three starters (`starters[unit]`) are yours too** — the noun is what a child in your country calls THAT picture (open it), traceable, no article; the starters ≤ 22 chars, no end mark, noun-agreeing.
- **Find-the-differences words come from `image-vocabulary.js`** (the what-changed column, the write strips, the tap labels): you do NOT rewrite them — a word that is wrong for a drawing in your locale goes into `REFUSED_WORDS` (data/fd/review.js) and the SCENE is refused for that face in your locale. Your literals there: the `starter` ("In picture 2," — noun-neutral, no slot), 4-8 `changeWords` (agreement-free: verb forms where your language inflects adjectives), the 6 `tap` strings, `numberWords` (3 / 5 / 7 / 10) and the strand literal ("Visual Perception" in your curriculum's words).
- **No NBSP / soft hyphen / zero-width characters** (fr: a plain space before `:` `!` `?` is accepted by the renderer). Slugs ASCII-kebab, folded (da ø→oe å→aa æ→ae · no ø→o · sv/fi ä→a ö→o · es ñ→n · de ä→ae ö→oe ü→ue ß→ss), unique in your locale.
- **Banks substitute stored literals only — the code never inflects.**
- **Refuse, never pad.** Declare every face your language cannot honestly carry under `refusals` with the reason and omit it from `types`. Expected refusals in this batch: none by design — a how-to-draw unit whose animal has no honest word in your market may be re-pinned through `unitOverrides` (a unit from the sweep that composes in band), never dropped silently.
