## The skill
Odd + odd = even, even + odd = odd: decide a sum's parity from the addends without adding.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE SPARE SOCK: each number is a washing line of socks pegged in pairs; an odd number has one lonely sock left at the end. Two lines are pushed together into one basket: the child predicts 'all paired' or 'one lonely' by tapping, then the lonely socks find each other (two lonely socks pair) and the basket shows the truth. Then bigger numbers appear only as numerals with the ones digit underlined and the socks are hidden — the child decides, and a tap on 'show me' brings the socks back.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Thinking the sum of two odd numbers is odd; needing to add to know.
- **Pictures:** accessories/sock (identical instances per round); washing lines and basket drawn as apparatus
- **Settings:** Numbers: up to 20 (socks visible) / up to 1000 (numerals only) · Addends: two / three
- **Rounds:** Generated pairs/triples, ≥ 9 rounds, odd and even answers balanced 45-55%, each of the four parity combinations ≥ 2 rounds.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G3-386
- **Distinct from:** Even or Odd? classifies a single number; the socks show WHY parities combine. Not Partner Bows (K pairs).
- **Build risk:** Low.
