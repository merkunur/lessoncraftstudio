## The skill
Recognise the mirror image of a picture (a reflection is flipped, not turned), and tell whether a picture is the same on both sides of a line.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE HINGE THAT FOLDS ONTO THE ORIGINAL. A library picture stands left of a vertical hinge (a mirror edge). Three candidates wait: the true reflection, the same picture unflipped, and the picture turned (rotated 180°/90°). The child drags a candidate to the right of the hinge; the right page folds over the hinge onto the left. Right: the reflection lands exactly on the original — outlines merge and a shine runs down the mirror. Miss 1: the copy or the turned picture lands crooked; the non-matching parts stay visible in a second tint (the duck's head on the wrong side). Miss 2: a faint true reflection appears on the right page for the child to pick. SAME-ON-BOTH-SIDES rounds (G2-248): three library pictures each with a dashed line through the middle; the child folds one — a symmetric one closes on itself, others leave an overhang. Free play: drag any picture to the hinge and watch its reflection appear.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Thinking the same picture (or a turned one) is the mirror image.
- **Pictures:** animals with a clear facing direction (duck, dog, horse, fish), vehicles (car, train), shoes, kitchen tools (asymmetric nouns only — a symmetric ball is refused)
- **Settings:** Mirror: side (vertical hinge) / water (horizontal hinge) · Distractors: copy + turn / copy only · Task: find the reflection / which is the same on both sides
- **Rounds:** 9 rounds per task; candidates shuffled with the answer in each slot ≤ 40 %; reflection rounds use only asymmetric pictures (duck, dog, horse, fish, car, train, shoe, watering-can-like kitchen tools), symmetric rounds use shapes theme (heart, star) and front-facing pictures checked for true symmetry.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** K-063
- **Distinct from:** No reflection activity exists; Pip's Museum turns shapes (rotation invariance), this flips pictures (reflection).
- **Build risk:** Low. Pictures that look symmetric but are not exactly (a front-facing cartoon) are refused for the symmetric rounds — measure the alpha mask's left/right difference.
- **Grade span (settings):** K (symmetric-picture rounds: 2)

## Merged into this instrument (cover each as a setting or round set)
- grade 3 "The Fold Mirror" (fold-mirror): A line of symmetry folds a shape onto itself exactly; some shapes have several, some none. — THE FOLD MIRROR: a shape cut from paper on the stage. The child drags a fold line through it (it snaps through the centre at 8 angles) and presses Fold: the half flips over the line. If the halves match, the fold glows and the line is kept as a crease; if not, the overhanging part sticks out in a different shade. The child folds until they think all mirror lines are found and taps the count; a miss shows one fold not yet tried that works.
