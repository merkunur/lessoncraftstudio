## §1 — Concept / world
**The Wobble Museum.** Curator Pip (a round, bespectacled hedgehog in a bow tie) keeps a museum where every room is supposed to be about ONE thing — but exhibits keep wandering in from the wrong rooms. Pip is too polite to be cross; he just says "Oooh — that's true, but it doesn't belong in THIS room." The child is Guest Curator: first **decides what a room will be all about** (commits the topic — BEFORE any cards arrive), then helps build the exhibit, and — the heart — **listens as Pip reads a finished room back as a tiny All-About paragraph and catches the one label that WANDERED off the topic.** The off-topic-but-true card isn't wrong — it goes to the **"For later" shelf** and is re-homed to the room it actually belongs to ("the fact was never wrong, only in the wrong room"). Non-numeric abundance vessel: the **Wonder Lantern** by the door deepens its warm glow + wakes drifting fireflies across a session as more focused rooms are curated (never a count/score). `ctx.correct()` auto-advances; no Check button. Pre-readers: every card/fact = icon + DOM `<text>` + speak-on-tap.

## §2 — The standard + the load-bearing cognition
**W.K.2 (Grade K, SOLE) — compose INFORMATIVE/explanatory texts: name a TOPIC + supply FACTS about it.** Pedagogy ruling: a genuine gradeable composition cognition exists BUT is NARROW + "on a knife's edge" — it is **TOPIC-COHERENCE/FOCUS** (the documented K "knowledge-telling drift" — Bereiter & Scardamalia; Calkins' all-about books; 6+1-Trait "Ideas"). It INSTANTIATES (not touches) W.K.2 ONLY via the THREE crossing-properties: the child PRODUCES the topic (self-set controlling idea), selections ACCUMULATE into ONE artifact, and it READS BACK as connected prose. **The critic's re-center — the genuinely WHOLE-TEXT, non-categorization cognition is CATCH-THE-DRIFT** (find the one sentence in a read-back text that broke the committed controlling idea — a judgment unavailable per-card in isolation), supported by AUTHORIAL TOPIC-COMMITMENT (the topic is set BEFORE the facts, then governs them) + FOR-LATER RE-HOMING (identify the CORRECT OTHER topic an off-topic-true fact belongs to — a contrastive whole-text judgment). All words = real DOM/SVG `<text>` + speak-on-tap. **FENCES:** grade RELEVANCE-to-the-committed-topic, NEVER TRUTH (every distractor is TRUE — truth can't separate ✓/✗); ONE topic, NEVER multi-bin (multi-bin CONTRADICTS the single-topic standard + IS the live E3 sort-bins re-skinned); per-fact on-topic SELECTION = the accumulation MECHANISM/scaffold, NOT the grade (it's the sort-bins/K.MD.B.3 classify act); the OBVIOUS self-insertion lures ("I like ice cream") = SCAFFOLD (innately rejected — the Game-84 native-intuition analog). Topics = concrete/picturable (frogs, the moon, fire trucks, rain, the body...). **Distinctness from the live sort-bins/K.MD.B.3 classify is on the WHOLE-TEXT axis** (catch-the-drift is irreducible to per-card classify; the graded act is NOT category-membership).

## §3 — THE ARCHITECTURE REWORK (the heart; the categorization-re-skin + two cheats + the re-center)
**The critic's catch — as composed, the load-bearing grade is CATEGORIZATION with read-back DECORATION (a §20.8 touches-not-instantiates FAIL):**
- **(A) per-fact "is this on-topic?" = a CLASSIFY judgment** — one card against one category = the LIVE sort-bins / K.MD.B.3 classify-objects ACT (Game-82 ACT-level rule: different answer/costume, SAME graded act → re-skin). "Coherence scored across the whole set" = the CONJUNCTION of N independent per-item classifies = bookkeeping, NOT an emergent whole-text property. The read-back merely SONIFIES a verdict the per-fact classify already reached → decoration, grades nothing new.
- **(B) the OBVIOUS self-insertion lures are native-intuition** — "I like ice cream" in a frog book is a register/genre violation any K child rejects reflexively (the Game-84 native-ear analog) → a floor-raiser, not a discriminator.
- **(C) topic-naming-as-CLUSTER** — "pick the sign the MOST cards fit" is find-the-majority-cluster (the inverse classify), NOT an authorial controlling-idea commitment.

**THE FIX — RE-CENTER onto the whole-text cognition the per-card classifier CAN'T solve (the read-back becomes the graded STIMULUS):**
- **GRADED (load-bearing):**
  1. **CATCH-THE-DRIFT (the spine).** The child hears a FINISHED, mostly-coherent room read back as connected prose ("This is about frogs. Frogs hop. Tadpoles have tails. Frogs eat bugs.") and taps the ONE sentence that DRIFTED. The intruder is knowable ONLY against the committed controlling idea the rest of the text establishes → whole-text, irreducible to any per-card-in-isolation judgment. **Drift-POSITION varied (early/mid/late)** so focus-monitoring must scan the WHOLE text, not just the last addition.
  2. **FOR-LATER RE-HOMING.** The drifted true fact goes to the shelf; the child identifies the CORRECT OTHER topic it belongs to ("Tadpoles have tails" → a book about FROG BABIES / LIFE CYCLES, not Frogs) — a contrastive whole-text judgment (which OTHER controlling idea does this cohere with).
  3. **HARD-LURE FOCUS (the only graded selection).** When selection IS surfaced, the graded discrimination is EXCLUSIVELY the hard lures — **context-neighbor** (frog→"Ponds are cold") + **whole/part** (frog→"Tadpoles have tails") — where K focus genuinely FAILS (the documented misconception).
- **SCAFFOLD (mechanism/texture, NOT graded):** per-fact selection/accumulation (it BUILDS the artifact — the classify mechanism, demoted); the OBVIOUS self-insertion / register-violation lures (texture, innately rejected); **AUTHORIAL TOPIC-COMMITMENT BEFORE the facts** (the child picks WHAT to make a book about before any card appears → a self-set goal that GOVERNS selection — correct-by-commitment, NOT graded as a classify); the For-later shelf existing (motivational, honors true facts).
- **Grade RELEVANCE not TRUTH; ONE topic; the KEYWORD defeat** (off-topic foils SHARE vocab — "frogs live in ponds; ponds are cold" [pond in both] — + on-topic facts WITHOUT the topic word — "they hop" — so topic-word-matching fails).
- **child-output STRICTLY before game-output** (`ANSWER_NOT_PRE_REVEALED`); no correct/incorrect flag on any card (derived); the read-back coherence is the FEEDBACK, generated at tap-time.

## Cohort doctrine this game adds
**The PRODUCTION-COLLAPSES-TO-CLASSIFICATION + WHOLE-TEXT-IS-IRREDUCIBLE + GRADE-THE-RELATIVE-NOT-THE-ITEM lesson (Game 85 — PROGRAM-WIDE; the first WRITING game, and the deepest demonstrate-not-touch test yet).** When a PRODUCTION standard (compose / write / build a coherent whole) is grade-constrained at a young age, the obvious mechanic — "select the elements that belong" — collapses the grade onto a PER-ITEM CLASSIFICATION (does this element belong?), which is the same ACT as a live categorization/sorting activity (Game-82 ACT-level: different answer/costume, same act → re-skin), and any "scored across the whole set" framing is just the CONJUNCTION of N independent classifies — NOT a whole-text cognition. A "read-it-back" / "render-the-whole" feature that merely SONIFIES the per-item verdicts is DECORATION, not a grade (the covering-as-model trap, Game-80). **THE FIX — grade the RELATIVE/EMERGENT judgment a per-item classifier structurally CANNOT make:** here CATCH-THE-DRIFT (find the one element that breaks the committed controlling idea — knowable ONLY against the whole + the self-set goal, irreducible to any per-element function) + the contrastive re-home (which OTHER whole does this element cohere with). The whole-rendering (read-back) flips from decoration to the load-bearing STIMULUS the moment the grade is the relative judgment. Two companions: **(1)** the self-set GOAL must be committed BEFORE the elements appear (else "produce the topic" collapses to find-the-majority-CLUSTER — the inverse classify); **(2)** grade only where the intuition FAILS — the OBVIOUS off-goal element (a register/genre violation) is innately rejected (the Game-84 native-intuition analog), so only the HARD, schema-adjacent off-goal elements (the documented misconception — context-neighbor, whole/part) are the genuine grade. The gate needs a `CATEGORIZATION_BIN` solver that proves the graded verdict is NOT reproducible by any per-item function (the whole-text proof), beside `OBVIOUS_LURE` (only-hard-graded) and `TOPIC_AS_CLUSTER` (committed-before-elements). Generalizes to EVERY composition/whole-construction standard at any age (informative/narrative/opinion writing, argument structure, coherent-explanation, sequence-with-a-throughline): the per-element select/classify is the SCAFFOLD/mechanism; grade the EMERGENT relative property (drift / coherence-break / the-one-that-doesn't-fit-the-whole) that no per-element judgment yields, commit the goal before the elements, and grade only where the native intuition fails. (Lineage: the categorization-re-skin sharpens Game-82's ACT-level-duplicate [the live act is classify; the read-back is the new costume] + Game-80's fill-vs-measure [the read-back is a model, the relative judgment is the measure]; the obvious-lure demotion is Game-84's native-intuition; the whole-text-irreducibility is the composition analog of Game-81's grade-the-inverse [the per-item forward is owned/innate; the emergent relative is the genuine cognition]; and it is the program's hardest §20.8 demonstrate-not-touch save — a production standard rescued from a sorting re-skin by grading the irreducible whole.)
