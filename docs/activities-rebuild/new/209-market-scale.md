## The skill
Compare two objects by weight and tell which is heavier and which is lighter — and that a bigger thing is not always heavier.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE PAN THAT GOES DOWN. A two-pan balance stands level and empty. Two library things wait on the counter, drawn at their REAL relative sizes (the pillow much bigger than the hammer), so size is visible and is the lure. The task line asks 'Which is heavier?' (or lighter) and the left pan carries a down-arrow (or up-arrow) icon. The child drags the one they choose onto the arrow pan; the other slides onto the right pan and the beam tips slowly to the truth, its angle growing with the real weight ratio. Right: the arrow pan sinks (or rises) as predicted and the bubble says 'Yes — the hammer is heavier than the pillow!'. Wrong: the beam tips against the arrow, plain to see; the child then swaps the two (drag either one across) so the round always ends with the truth on the scale — miss 2 makes the lower pan glow before the swap. Free play: any two things from a shelf can be weighed.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Bigger means heavier.
- **Pictures:** big-but-light: around-the-house/pillow, cushion; toys/balloon, ball, kite; easter/feather; clothing/hat. Small-but-heavy: tools/hammer, around-the-house/iron, key, beach/rock, kitchen-tools/pot, at-the-supermarket/can. Ordinary: fruits/apple, watermelon; vegetables/pumpkin, potato. A curated weight table (grams, hand-checked) for every noun used.
- **Settings:** Ask: heavier / lighter / both · Big-but-light rounds: some / many
- **Rounds:** 9 rounds, ≥ 3 big-but-light pairs and ≥ 2 small-but-heavy ones; weight ratio ≥ 3:1 so the answer is unambiguous from real life; the heavier thing is the LEFT counter picture in 40–60 % of rounds and the bigger-on-screen one in ≤ 50 %, so neither side nor size is a shortcut; heavier and lighter questions alternate in 'both'.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** K-038
- **Distinct from:** Bramble (capacity) and Comparing Length cover the other K.MD.A.2 attributes; nothing weighs.
- **Build risk:** Needs a hand-checked weight table for ~60 library nouns (one reviewer pass); feather / balloon may be missing from the library — choose from what exists.
