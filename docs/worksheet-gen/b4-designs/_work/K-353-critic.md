## 4 OPEN items

1. **Answer key (operator / pipeline).** Printable decks ship no answer key; "with answers" (en), "mit Lösungen" (de), "lösning" (sv) are the strongest tails and every closed face plus F2's four shadows HAVE a stored solution. Design assumes NONE and bans the claim (validator rule 3). If a later commission adds a solutions page to printable decks, this family is the first customer.
2. **Animal bank depth (engineer + a human eye).** Only `cat` and `rabbit` are measured animals; `rabbit` (1.75 tall) fits no d2 face and reads weakly in the render; the "Tangram Animals" head (en / de / pt / fr / sv) is real demand. Author >= 5 animals with bbox <= 1.414 x 1.414 (swan, fish, duck, dog, fox candidates), gate each, OPEN each render, then re-title F2 in the affected locales. Not blocking: F2 ships as "Four Shadows".
3. **Concave-capable tiler in the gate.** The scratch tiler is convex-only; the gate must implement point-in-polygon + no-proper-crossing (or triangulation) before any concave mini is admitted; the P4 / rule-7 poison set must include a concave control.
4. **F2 "your tangram" instruction.** A recorded exception to brief rule 9; a native panel may still refuse the wording, in which case the contingency (an inline piece-glyph strip in the title area, expectation unchanged) applies.
5. **fi 4-line-title case.** F2 and F3 pools lose `cat` at 677; the build measures the fi title line count per face and lets `poolFor(face, cfg, bodyH)` resolve it; nothing else in the family is height-sensitive.
6. **Scissor ergonomics of a 57 mm set** are ruled acceptable on straight cuts; the print check (child scissors, mono laser) is the only evidence there will be.

## 5 Quality verdict

Would I print this for a kindergarten and a grade-2 class? Yes, and the base first: one square to cut, a legend a five-year-old reads without a word, two figures drawn with their seams so the child copies line for line instead of guessing, and the same pieces fit the compose outlines and the four shadows on the later pages. The faces are honest moves, not dressings: drawing the seam only where the seam is provably unique, circling a chip that is congruent to the hole (size included), telling a solution that stands like its shadow from turned and flipped copies with the parallelogram as the visible tell, and counting triangles at three sizes and tilts on fragments whose outline cannot be mistaken for a triangle itself. What would embarrass me: the 57 mm square in a class used to 100 mm sets, if the straight cuts turn out fiddly for K scissors (the print check decides, and d1 is the true-size fallback); a "Tangram Animals" title over a bank that holds one animal that fits (so the final refuses that title until the animals exist); a rabbit that a child calls a hand; and a teacher searching "tangram with answers" who lands on a family that, by pipeline contract, ships no key. Everything else that could embarrass me (a second seam, a chip that fits two holes, a mirror that is secretly a rotation, an outline counted as a triangle) is now either measured out or refused at load.
