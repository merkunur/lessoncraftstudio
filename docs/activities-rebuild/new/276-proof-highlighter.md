## The skill
Read a short story (3 short paragraphs) and answer who, what, where, when and why questions with a detail found in the text.

## The apparatus and its invention
The story sits on a page; a question card shows its question word (Who? Where? When? Why?). The answer cards beneath are BLANK until the child drags a yellow highlighter across the sentence that holds the answer; only then the answer cards fill in with words from that sentence and its neighbours, and the child taps one. Highlighting a sentence that does not hold the answer fills the cards with words that do not answer the question word (no person for Who?) — miss 1 rings the paragraph, miss 2 the sentence.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Answering from memory or the picture instead of the text, and answering a Why? with a What? detail: the highlighter makes the child go back to the text, and the question word decides which kind of answer fits.
- **Pictures:** library pictures as one story illustration per story (animals, farm animals, food…); no hand-drawn art
- **Settings:** Questions: who/what/where / plus when/why · Story length: 2 paragraphs / 3 paragraphs
- **Rounds:** ≥ 7 stories × 3 questions per setting, native-written per locale; answer position among 3 cards balanced; the answer sentence is in a different paragraph across questions.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G2-254, G2-269, G2-270, G2-271, G2-272, G2-273
- **Distinct from:** Marlo's Magnifier finds proof for a character TRAIT; Fable's Picture Stories are 3-picture K stories. This anchors every RL.2.1 answer in a highlighted sentence.
- **Build risk:** Medium: 7+ native stories × 11 locales is content cost (one native reviewer per locale batch).
