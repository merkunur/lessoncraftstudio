## §1 — Concept / world
**Crumb & Split.** Crumb, a tiny anxious bakery mouse, runs a one-table pastry stall where customers order a SHARE, not a slice: "the same amount as half this tart, but cut into littler pieces." Crumb's signature tool is the **SPLIT-COMB** — drag a k-tooth comb across the tart and every piece re-cuts into k slivers; **the dough is never added to or taken away, only re-cut** (the multiplicative re-partition structure made tactile). BUT the comb only CUTS — it shades nothing. **Crumb (the child) must then GLAZE the right number of slivers to keep the same share** (1 glazed half → 3 glazed sixths), and prove the share didn't change by reading **where it lands on the number-line shelf** (1/2 and 3/6 sit at the SAME spot — the dough didn't move). Non-numeric abundance vessel: the **GLAZE JAR** rises with one coral brush-stroke per correct share (never a count/score). `ctx.correct()` auto-advances; no Check button.

## §2 — The standard + the load-bearing cognition
**3.NF.A.3 (Grade 3) — fraction EQUIVALENCE: different fractions name the SAME amount (1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6), via the multiplicative re-partition structure (split each of b parts into k → ka/kb, same amount).** Pedagogy ruling: GREEN, distinct from Game-76 (which counts copies of a fixed unit + ranks unit sizes via predict-then-reveal) — the spine is **GENERATE-by-re-partition** (Game-76 never re-partitions) + **co-location-on-a-number-line** (the most leak-resistant frame — no area to eyeball). **THE CRITIC'S RE-CENTER — the GRADED cognition must be the SAME-AMOUNT INVARIANT the child PRODUCES, not the ×k numbers the engine renders:** (a) **child-owned PROPORTIONAL SHADING** (the child glazes the proportional count; the engine never auto-shades) + (b) the **"did the dough MOVE along the number line?" MOTION-JUDGMENT** (unanswerable by the ×k procedure). All numbers = real DOM/SVG `<text>`. **TIGHT (operator-ruled): EQUIVALENCE-ONLY graded; comparison (clause d) + whole-number (clause c) = SCAFFOLD** (they collapse to "bigger bottom = smaller" rule-chants with no foil — grading them dilutes the distinct cognition + re-introduces procedure-leak). **FENCES:** the ×k PROCEDURE ("multiply top and bottom by k" — owned + borderline-Grade-4 → the comb-selection/k-computation is the ungraded VEHICLE, NEVER the grade); two pre-shaded models at check time (the area-overlay leak); same-denominator + unit-fraction comparison (Game-76's turf / perceptual); predict-the-size (Game-76's mechanic — P3 probe kills it). Grade-3 content: base ∈ {1/2, 1/3, 2/3, 3/4}, k ∈ {2,3}, target denominators ∈ {2,3,4,6,8}.

## §3 — THE ARCHITECTURE REWORK (the heart; the procedure-collapse + the re-center)
**The critic's catch — enacting the split is NECESSARY but NOT SUFFICIENT; the GRADED cognition collapsed to the ×k procedure:**
- **(A) raking the right comb IS the ×k procedure MOTORIZED.** A child who memorized "6ths from halves → 6÷2=3 → rake the 3-comb" rakes correctly every round with ZERO grasp of "same amount." The procedure moved from a symbolic numerator-box to a comb-gesture — it was RELOCATED, not removed (the Game-78 owned-op / Game-77-80 motor-flail in fraction clothing).
- **(B) the ENGINE auto-shading = the SOFTWARE demonstrates the invariant, not the child.** If raking auto-flows the glaze to 3-of-6, the child never ASSERTS "1 glazed piece becomes 3 glazed pieces" — the proportionality (the HEART of equivalence) is done by the engine. The invariant the standard is about is demonstrated by the software, not the student.
- **(C) the ADDITIVE foil guards the wrong thing** — it catches a NON-procedure child (who'd add to top/bottom); a procedure-with-no-meaning child rejects 2/3 *because the comb doesn't make it*, not by reasoning amount.
- **(D) the COMB-VOCABULARY BRUTE-FORCE (critic-named deeper cheat)** — a fixed comb palette + a readable target denominator → the child tries combs until the label reads "6," commits; never computes k, never reasons. P3 (shape-swap) doesn't catch it (it reads the LABEL, not the visual).
- **(E) the pre-commit AREA signal** — if raking shows the glaze before commit, the child try-comb / watch-the-glaze / undo until it "looks right" (the line channel was gated behind commit; the area channel was NOT).

**THE FIX — RE-CENTER so SAME-AMOUNT is independently load-bearing (3 changes, each disables a cheat):**
- **GRADED (load-bearing):**
  1. **CHILD-OWNED PROPORTIONAL SHADING (the load-bearing fix — kills B + D).** Raking the comb SPLITS the pieces (geometry) but glazes NOTHING. The child must then GLAZE the proportional count (1→3, 2→6, base-num·k). Error states: glazing the ORIGINAL count (1-of-6 — "more pieces, I glaze the same") or glazing ALL. Comb-elimination CANNOT fake the proportional-glaze assertion → the grade depends on the child producing the invariant.
  2. **THE "DID THE DOUGH MOVE?" MOTION-JUDGMENT (kills A's residue — the procedure can't satisfy it).** Behind commit, NO pre-commit signal, NO draggable dot to align: a BINARY "you changed 1/2 to 3/6 — did the amount MOVE along the number-line shelf? (no — same spot)" + mark its spot. A meaning-child says "no, same spot"; a ×k-procedure-child computed NUMBERS, not a motion-judgment about the AMOUNT → unanswerable by the procedure. This is the independently-graded same-amount existence the design was missing.
  3. **NO PRE-COMMIT AREA SIGNAL (kills E).** Both the glaze (from #1) and the number-line reveal only AFTER commit; the child glazes/judges blind (predicts), confirmation follows.
  4. The **additive foil (1/2→2/3) + incomplete-split foil** are load-bearing IN SERVICE OF #1/#2 (the incomplete-split = a scaffold-integrity check that every piece split equally).
- **SCAFFOLD (the VEHICLE, ungraded):** comb-selection / k-computation (let them compute k = target÷base — the borderline-Grade-4 procedure is the vehicle, NEVER the grade); same-numerator comparison (2/3 vs 2/6 — collapses to a rule-chant, no foil); whole-number equivalence (4/4=1 — memorized fact); the co-location VISUAL (confirmation only, distinct from the graded MOTION-JUDGMENT).
- **child-output STRICTLY before game-output** (`ANSWER_NOT_PRE_REVEALED`); no correct/answer/size field (derived); the confirm/co-location model lives in a HIDDEN container un-hidden ONLY after grading.

## Cohort doctrine this game adds
**The ENACTED-PROCEDURE-IS-STILL-THE-PROCEDURE + WHO-PRODUCES-THE-INVARIANT (engine vs child) lesson — PROGRAM-WIDE.** When a standard's cheap path is a MECHANICAL PROCEDURE (the ×k "multiply top and bottom" rule — owned + often above-grade), **MOTORIZING the procedure into a manipulative gesture (raking a ×k comb, dragging a ×k tool) does NOT remove the procedure-cheat — it RELOCATES it** from a symbolic box to a hand motion (a child who knows "target÷base = k, rake that comb" passes with zero meaning; the Game-78 owned-op + the Game-77/80 motor-flail combined). AND — the deeper structural test — **ask WHO PRODUCES THE INVARIANT the standard is about.** If the ENGINE renders it (auto-shades the proportional pieces, auto-snaps the equivalent), the SOFTWARE demonstrates the target concept and the child only operates the tool → the invariant rides free. **THE FIX — grade the act WHERE THE CHILD MUST PRODUCE THE INVARIANT THEMSELVES** (here: the child owns the PROPORTIONAL SHADING — glaze the same share into the new cuts, with error states the engine can't paper over) **+ add an independently-graded judgment the procedure CANNOT satisfy** (the "did the AMOUNT move?" motion-judgment — a procedure computes numbers, not a sameness-of-quantity judgment; it scores chance on it). Two companions: **(1)** a foil only guards against the cheat it actually stops — the additive foil catches a NON-procedure child, NOT a procedure-without-meaning child (a foil the target cheat passes is not load-bearing against it); **(2)** a FIXED-PALETTE manipulative with a readable target label invites BRUTE-FORCE-BY-ELIMINATION (try tools till the label matches) — defeated by making the grade a child-PRODUCED assertion elimination can't fake (the proportional glaze), not a tool-selection. Generalizes to EVERY "enact the manipulative / use the model" game (fraction bars, base-ten blocks, number-line tools, balance scales): motorizing a procedure ≠ teaching the meaning; CHECK who produces the invariant (engine-renders → re-center onto the child producing it); grade a procedure-ORTHOGONAL judgment; and verify each foil stops the cheat it's aimed at. (Lineage: sharpens Game-78's owned-op [the owned op survives being ENACTED] + Game-80's fill-vs-measure/who-produces [here WHO produces the INVARIANT — engine-auto-shades vs child-shades] + Game-77/80's motor-flail [raking-and-watching] + the brute-force is the Game-79/82 elimination floor on a fixed palette; the who-produces-the-invariant test is the manipulative-game analog of the production-vs-recognition discipline.)
