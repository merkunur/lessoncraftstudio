## The skill
Find the 1st to 10th thing in a line, counting from the front — whichever end the front is.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE DOOR THAT COUNTS FROM ITSELF. A queue of up to ten different library animals waits at a bus stop; the library bus stands at the front, its door at the left in some rounds and at the right in others. An ordinal ticket (3rd — numeral plus the locale's ordinal mark) waits on the kerb; the child drags it onto ONE animal (a ticket can only rest on a single animal). The door opens and the queue boards one at a time from the door end, each animal getting a lit place badge 1st, 2nd, 3rd … as it steps in; boarding stops at the ticket holder, who gets the big badge. If the child counted from the far end, boarding visibly starts at the door, not where they began, and stops on a different animal — the ticket holder is left on the kerb, shaking its ticket. If the child took 'the 3rd' as 'three of them', the badges still show only one '3rd'. Name-the-place mode: one animal holds a star; the child taps its place on a fixed row of ordinal keys 1st–10th. Race face (absorbed Bakery Queue): the same queue stands behind a drawn finish ribbon, which plays the door's role — the runner nearest the ribbon is 1st. Miss 1: the door end pulses and a dotted arrow runs from it along the queue; miss 2: the badges appear on every animal up to the answer. Right: the bus pulls away with the ticket holder in the window.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Counting from the wrong end, and treating "3rd" as "three of them" (taking the first three).
- **Pictures:** vehicles/bus at the front (CSS-mirrored so its door faces the queue); one animal of each kind per queue so every place is nameable: animals, farm-animals, zoo-animals, forest-creatures (e.g. cat, dog, pig, cow, duck, sheep, fox, zebra, panda, rabbit); finish ribbon, ticket and keys drawn as apparatus
- **Settings:** Mode: find the place / name the place · Front: always left / either end · Line: bus stop / finish ribbon (race face)
- **Rounds:** 9 rounds per mode, ordinals 1st–10th each used, the target ordinal never the same as its distance from the far end except when the queue is odd-length and target is the middle (allowed once); door side balanced 50/50 in either-end mode; the picked animal's screen position spread so left/right half ≤ 60 %.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G1-315, G1-316, G1-317, G1-318, K-030, K-031, K-320, K-336
- **Distinct from:** No existing activity teaches ordinals; distinct from Roll Book / Treat Box (cardinal count-out) because the answer is ONE item at a position, and the door end flips.
- **Build risk:** Ordinal marks are locale grammar and live only on the ticket and the keys: en 1st/2nd/3rd, de/da/no/fi 1./2./3., fr 1er/2e/3e, es 1.º, pt/it 1º, nl 1e/2e, sv 1:a/2:a/3:e — the native panel authors the glyph set and the task line (fi: the ordinal's case in the sentence).

## Merged into this instrument (cover each as a setting or round set)
- grade 1 "The Bakery Queue" (ordinal-queue): Ordinal numbers name a place in a line counted from the front: 1st, 2nd, 3rd ... — A QUEUE AT A DOOR: library animals stand in a line facing a shop door (the flag). The child taps the animal in the asked place (or drags the ordinal tag onto it); when the tag is placed, the animals in front step forward one by one, each wearing its place tag, and the door opens for the 1st. A "turn around" setting puts the door on the other side so the count starts from the other end. The race face: runners at different distances from a finish line; the nearest is 1st.
