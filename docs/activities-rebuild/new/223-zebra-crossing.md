## The skill
Read the traffic and pedestrian lights and cross the road safely: stop at the kerb, look both ways, wait for the green figure, cross.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE KERB THAT HOLDS YOU UNTIL IT IS SAFE. A street (apparatus): kerb, zebra stripes, a three-lamp traffic light, and library cars (vehicles car, bus, taxi, van) rolling in two lanes. A library animal walker (farm-animals/pets, one per round) waits at the kerb line. The child presses round step buttons beside the street: Stop · Look left · Look right · Wait for green · Cross. Look turns the walker to face that way (mirror flip) and the lane it faces shows whether a car is coming. Crossing works only after the full sequence with the walkers' green. Miss 1: the walker steps out, a car rolls slowly into the lane it did not check, and the walker steps calmly back onto the kerb (never a collision); the missed step button glows. Miss 2: the buttons light in order. Success: the walker crosses on the stripes while the cars wait. READ-THE-LIGHT mode (K-369, K-373): one lamp is lit; the child drags Stop / Get ready / Go onto the light. ORDER mode (K-374): four step cards into four boxes. SIGNS mode (G1-384, G2-360/361): blocked on pictogram art — shape/colour classes (triangle, red ring, blue disc, square, stop octagon) are drawable once the symbols exist.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Crossing on green without looking; thinking the car light is the walker's light.
- **Pictures:** vehicles (car, bus, bicycle), animals (one walker per round); street, crossing and lights drawn as apparatus
- **Settings:** Mode: read the light / cross the road / steps in order · Lights: car light / walker light (walker light only once the figures exist)
- **Rounds:** 9 rounds; the green arrives after a different number of passing cars each round (no timing tell, no timer); in order mode the step cards are shuffled so each card visits every position across a pass; one right action per state.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G1-384, G2-360, G2-361, K-369, K-373, K-374
- **Distinct from:** No safety activity exists.
- **Build risk:** Sub-part BLOCKED: road-sign pictograms (pedestrian crossing, children, cyclist, no-entry…) and pedestrian-signal figures (red standing / green walking). Look left first in all 11 locales (right-hand traffic). Step wording per locale by the native panel.

## Merged into this instrument (cover each as a setting or round set)
- grade 1 "The Street Corner" (street-corner): Traffic signs and lights tell us what to do: stop, cross here, give way, no bikes. — A LITTLE STREET: a corner with a crossing, cars and a library friend wanting to cross. Sign posts are empty; the child places the sign that fits the situation from a sign tray (signs drawn as standard geometric sign shapes). When the sign is placed, the scene plays it: the car stops at the stop sign, the friend crosses at the zebra. A wrong sign makes the scene pause with the car and the friend facing each other.
- grade 2 "The Sign Sieve — Kinds of Road Signs" (sign-sieve): Know that the shape and colour of a road sign tells its kind (warning triangle, must-not red circle, must-do blue circle, information square) and pick the sign a situation needs. — A SIEVE with three holes shaped like a triangle, a circle and a square. Signs drop from the top; the child drags each to the hole it fits — only the shape decides, so the hole is the rule. Situation mode: a street scene (library vehicles, crossing guard, children) with an empty sign post; the child plants one of three signs and the scene plays what happens (cars stop at the stop sign, slow down at the school sign).
