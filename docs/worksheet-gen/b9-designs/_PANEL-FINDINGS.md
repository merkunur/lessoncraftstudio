## Boundaries (every landing ends with the boundary sentence)
- **pre-writing** (K-236, K-244–248, K-385–387): ABSTRACT strokes (lines, zigzags, loops) and picture-to-picture paths.
  Picture Tracing traces the outline of a whole PICTURE. The four stroke faces (straight / curve / zigzag / wave) name the
  stroke the picture practises, but the page is always a picture.
- **how-to-draw** (K-396, K-402 "trace then draw"): a step ladder that teaches drawing a thing from shapes. Picture Tracing
  has no steps.
- **dot-to-dot / connect the dots**: numbered points. Picture Tracing has no numbers on the guide.

## The honest click model (b8 method)
Picture-tracing demand is on the same scale as the maze family (en 328 vs 410, es 244 vs 204, fr 196 vs 222, pt 173 vs 129,
it 151 vs 102; weaker in de 67 vs 152; Nordic thin; fi near zero). The maze family's model was low 330 · mid 620 · high 940
/day at 9-15 months. Applied here: **low ≈ 320 · mid ≈ 600 · high ≈ 920 clicks/day at 9-15 months**, near zero for the first
2-3 months, conditional on indexation. The 500/day target sits inside the mid band. ~85 % of it is expected from
en/es/fr/pt/it; the Nordic and Finnish pages are built to the same quality for their teachers, not for clicks.
What moves the number: (1) one landing per face that owns a distinct harvested query (trace and colour; preschool; animal;
seasonal; the stroke faces); (2) titles led by the harvested head + the face tail + the grade stamp; (3) the PDF itself
indexable (§17.8.20 reversal: printables are indexable), with the head in the PDF title metadata.
