- **What the child does:** 5 rows. Each row has 2 white cards: [gift] → [potted seedling] ← [gift]. The child circles the one plant that gets BOTH light (`weather/sun`) and water (`weather/raindrop`).
- **Mix:** noWater 2, noLight 2, neither 1. Non-needs are distinct across the page, drawn from the 12 opened toys/clothes/instruments. Every seedling SVG is byte-identical.
- **Config:** `rows 5, rowH 120, rowGap 14, gift 64, potH 104`.
- **Stack:** 5×120 + 4×14 = 656 ≤ 667.
- **Deviations:**
  - The pot is 104 px, not 110. The design's 110 px pot in a 120 px row with 12 padding overflowed its own card (134 > 120).
  - The design's "gift beside pot" became gift → pot ← gift, with SHAFTED arrows. The first render used bare chevrons, and they READ AS MATHS ("sun > pot < drop").
- **Gate:**
  - verify: one winner per row; grows ⇔ gifts ⊇ needs; missing-need multiset === mix; identical seedlings; gifts ≥ 56; pots ≥ 96; no text in the body.
  - Winner-side tells on the SHIPPED page: counts differ ≤ 1, not constant, not alternating.
  - Poisons: PR1, PR2, PS-needs, PA1.
- **PNG:** `out/dev/K-376-null-d2-en.png` (+ `-grey`).

## FILL revision (coordinator review, 2026-09-23): F4 and F5 grow to fill the body

- **G2-364 (jobs).**
  - Plant: minimum 560, column 340 (it grows to ~637 at the default chrome).

  - Text 19 px / 24 line-height; boxes 60×54.
  - Deviation: the line cap is raised 3 → 4. A 90-char job at 19 px in the 213 px column can take 4 lines; the EN jobs take ≤ 2.
- **G3-392 (flower).**
  - The cut-away is the HERO. Its row takes all the height left (minimum 330; it grows to ~428, width-limited to the 519 px cell).
  - The whole-plant thumbnail stands framed BESIDE it, in a 140 px column, vertically centred, so it no longer floats alone.
  - The label card is now a two-column card (1-3 | 4-5; rows 64, glyphH 26, `plantLabelGrid`) pinned to the bottom of the body.
