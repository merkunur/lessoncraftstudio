## Deviations from the design file (each measured)

1. **`pictureStrip` is K-320's `lineUpStrip`, aliased in the spec.** `templates/components-b3.js` refuses a duplicate export name across families and K-320 shipped the design's exact option contract as `lineUpStrip` first (its build record asks the reviewer to fold the two). The spec does `const pictureStrip = C3.lineUpStrip` and requires `lineUpPanel` from the namespace; this family exports only `instructionList`, `truthChips`, `drawBox`. The arrow row renders 16 px (K-320's `startArrow` at flagScale 1: pole 2 px, 10×8 pennant, 2 px rule, 8 px head), not the design's 14, so the strip block is 122 (design 116) and the panel **146** (design 140; d1 **162**, design 156). ⚠ `lineUpPanel({attrs})` must NEVER receive a `style` attribute — a second `style` on the lane drops the inline `padding:10px 12px` (measured: lane 150 / inner 639 until fixed).
2. **The stack budgets 710, not 722.** Measured in the real pipeline (letter, fonts loaded): a 150-char instruction wraps to TWO lines (`LONG_CHROME` → body 733); a legal ≤ 150-char instruction reaches THREE lines only through long-word wrapping — the gate's 146-char German `WORST_CHROME` does (`instrLines` asserted = 3) → body **710**. Row floors are 82 / 85 / 73 (design 84 / 86 / 74): the first quick run put d2 at 85 with the 150 px un-padded lane and overflowed the footer by 2 px, which is the class the lint exists for; with the 146 lane the stacks are 706 / 708 / 705 ≤ 710. Two-line reserve holds at every level: 2 × 1.35 × 19 = 51 + 4 ≤ 82; 48.6 + 4 ≤ 85; 45.9 + 4 ≤ 73. Under the shipped 2-line title (body 766) rows grow to 92 / 95 / 82 — the slack opens in the rows, the strip never moves (measured lane 146 in every render).
3. **d1 is infeasible as tabled; re-cut with a counting argument.** With 6 tiles, 6 rows, cues `unique all` and verbs `circle cross mark` (d1 as written), every marking row needs ≥ 1 disjoint tile: an `all` row needs ≥ 2, so 6 marking rows need ≥ 7 tiles, and 6 unique rows need 6 distinct nouns (the ladder says 4-5). Shipped d1: `verbs` + **`write`** (marks no tile — the design's own §7 says count-and-write is a VERB inside the base), cues + **`first last`** (so the (action, cue-kind) rule has 3 × 3 = 9 single-target combinations for ≥ 4 single rows), **5 nouns** over 6 tiles (`[2,1,1,1,1]` — the only multiplicity shape that seats 6 disjoint rows). Measured: 40/40 seeds compose on every fan theme; the sweep covers all four verbs, all five cues, write answers {1,2}.
4. **d3 `ordMax` 4, not 5.** With 8 tiles and ≥ 5 nouns the largest multiplicity is 4 (5 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 9 > 8), so `ordinal:5` can never be composed; `maxCount` is 4 for the same reason and `build()` throws on `ordMax > maxCount`. The `fixed.ordPic` phrases (2..8) are authored for F3.
5. **`write` rows stamp `data-lcs-cue="count"`** (the design's cue enum has no value for the write row; `unique`/`all` would misstate the count semantics). `line` rows stamp `cue="unique"` (both nouns unique, as §2 verifies).
6. **Same-strip lookalike ban (additive, picture-level).** Every fan-theme picture was opened (contact sheets in the scratchpad): the composer never seats two nouns of one group on a strip (`LOOKALIKES` in the spec, exported as data so the gate asserts it without a second copy): leopard/jaguar/cheetah · chimpanzee/gorilla/orangutan/monkey · antelope/gazelle · duck/duckling/goose/swan/chick · hen/chicken/rooster/turkey/chick · cow/bull/calf/ox · horse/foal/donkey · wolf/dog · cat/tiger · the orange-round fruits · the red-round fruits · lemon/lime · the berries · boat/sailboat/ship/ferry/yacht/canoe · car/jeep/taxi/van · airplane/jet · truck/van/ambulance · crane/excavator/bulldozer/forklift/tractor · scooter/motorcycle · subway/train/bus. "Circle the cheetah" beside a leopard is a discrimination task, not a reading task.
7. **Four toys nouns reviewed OUT of the EN bank:** `baby` (a baby, not a toy), `girl` (a doll lookalike), `lego` (a brand; the picture shows three bricks), `domino` (the picture shows TWO dominoes — a `write` count would be ambiguous). EN bank = 133 nouns; toys keeps 21 usable (≥ 8).
8. **Two per-page variety rules added: `maxPerVerb: 2` and `maxPerCue: 3/2/2`.** The first d3 renders carried three `mark` rows and three `leftof` rows on one page — legal under "(action, cue) distinct" but monotonous. Both are resolved-config keys, stamped and re-checked by `verify()`.
9. **EN `mark` frame is US register: "Put a check mark under {obj}."** The design's EN row says "tick" and itself names the tick/check-mark trap; the platform's EN is US (Color, Kindergarten, Grade 1). `mark:'check'` unchanged.
10. **Slot names are FORM SELECTORS (pinned; the design listed the slots without saying how a cue picks a form).** Under `unique`: `{obj}`/`{all}` → `unique`, `{def}` → `def`, `{dat}` → `dat`, `{gen}` → `gen`, `{allDef}` → `def`; under `all`: `{allDef}` → `allDef`, else → `all`; `ordinal:k` → `ord[k]`; first/last/between/rightof/leftof → `fixed` (its `{A}`/`{B}` take `endpointForm`: unique | dat (de) | gen (fi) | def (sv/da/no)); `line`'s second slot `{obj2}` → unique, `{dat2}` → dat, `{def2}` → def, `{a2}` → a2; `write` `{pl}` | `{part}`. Recorded in the bank header for the panels + `apply-b3-locale.js`.
11. **Text column measured 577 px** (design 585; the row's own 2 px border + 8 px pad each side): a 70-char line at the measured 7.4-8.1 px/char (≤ 567) still fits one line; the longest real EN sentence over the six fan themes is 78 chars ("Put a check mark under the picture between the hippopotamus and the woodpecker.") → wraps to two lines inside the reserve; the 96-char cap ≤ 2 × 577.
12. **F4 truth frames use only `{n} {pl} {obj} {obj2}` and never OPEN with a noun slot** (the code never capitalises; `objForms` are lowercase literals) — the design's `{unique}`-initial example would need a capitalised literal per noun.
