# SUBSTITUTIONS — games replaced during spec writing, with reasons

Format: `NNN | original slug → new slug | reason | date`. A substitution keeps the number and the objective; only the mechanic or content changes, and only when the original could not be built within the brief's constraints (single file, Phaser shapes/emoji/text, ≤ 800 lines, no audio files, tap-first, no losing state). Writer agents flag a substitution with a `SUBSTITUTION:` line at the top of the spec's Identity section; the designer records it here after review.
