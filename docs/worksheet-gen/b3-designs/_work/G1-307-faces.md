## Deviations from §3 (each with the measurement)
1. **Component names are type-scoped** (`oppositeMatch` for `matchColumns`, `oppositeFrameRow` for `frameRow`, `oppositePairLane` for `pairLane`, `oppositeChoiceRow` for `choiceRow`, `oppositePrefixChips` for `prefixChips`, + `oppositeChipRow` / `oppositePrefixRow`). The namespace loader refuses a duplicate export for every family at once and twenty builders are emitting in parallel; the K-319 `feeling…` precedent.
2. **F1 tiles are 250 wide, not 280.** At 280 the two columns (675 − 60 padding = 615 inner) leave a 55 px gap; the coral dots sit 26 px outside each tile, so the two dots are **3 px apart** — no line to draw. At 250 the gap is 115 and the dots 63 px apart (gate asserts the columns ≥ 90 px apart). Word room 250 − 4 − 16 − 80 − 10 = 140 → ≤ 9 glyphs at 26 px (`maxLetters 9`, guard `0.56·26·len`; every pictured en member ≤ 5).
3. **F1 scale pair prints 80 / 56, not 80 / 44.** 56 is the K element floor (`_tokens.js:68`, the brief's band floor) and the design's own verify line says "icons >= 56"; 80/56 = 1.43 keeps the bank's ≥ 1.3 scale-ratio rule (verify + the bank rule both check it). The base's proven d1 cue is the same 64/44 proportion.
4. **F1 `tiers:[1,2]`, not `[1]`.** en has exactly 6 tier-1 pictured pairs (big-small hot-cold happy-sad fast-slow day-night soft-hard); `tiers:[1]` would draw the same six every seed. With heavy-light + sweet-sour (tier 2, both pictured + K-legible from the picture) the pool is 8 → 16 distinct sets over 20 seeds, every pair drawn ≥ 13 times (the design's "every pair in ≥ 12 seeds" holds).
5. **F2 lane geometry re-budgeted for the 700 px body.** Design: lanes h 106, padding 8 16 → inner 90 for a 24 + 4 + 56 = 84 stack. Measured worst legal case = the 4-line fi title (body 700) AND a two-row bank (110): (700 − 110 − 5 × 4) / 6 = 95 → with padding 4 16 the inner is 83 for a **22 + 2 + 56 = 80** stack (line 1 `line-height 22`, gap 2); the grid gets `padding-bottom:4px` so the last ruling never kisses the attribution band (was 915 vs 921 = at the gate's own 6 px margin). 6. **F2 lane widens uniformly up to `laneMax` 300.** The design's fixed 200 × 56 lane holds ≤ 8 glyphs at glyphH 28 (measured 0.75·glyphH per written glyph + 16); fi `hiljainen` (9), nl `verdrietig` (10), pt `barulhento` (10), it `tranquillo` (10) would all be unusable answers.
