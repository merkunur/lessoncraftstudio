## The skill
Look closely to find two pictures that are exactly the same, and the one that is different (in a detail, its size or its shape).

## The apparatus and its invention
THE PEG THAT ONLY CLOSES ON TWINS. A washing line holds 6–8 cards (library pictures, some subtly altered: mirrored, a detail recoloured, scaled, a shape with a corner cut). The child drags one card onto another; a clothes-peg tries to clip them together. Exact twins clip and leave the line together; non-twins: the peg will not close and the two cards hold side by side with the differing spot pulsing. Odd-one-out setting: every card has a twin except one — when all pairs are pegged, the card left alone on the line IS the different one, so "different" is discovered by pairing, not guessed.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Accepting "nearly the same" as the same (same kind ≠ same picture), and missing size or orientation differences.
- **Pictures:** animals, toys, fruits, vehicles, shapes (drawn); alterations are transforms of the same picture (mirror, scale, hue rotate) — no new art
- **Settings:** Task: find the pairs / find the odd one out · Difference: size and shape / small details
- **Rounds:** 9 rounds; card positions shuffled; the odd one sits in each line position ≤ 25 % across a pass; every altered card differs in ONE attribute so one right answer.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** K-039, K-044, K-058, K-062, K-078, K-080
- **Distinct from:** Ziggy's Odd One Out is category reasoning; Partner Bows pairs for COUNTING. Here identity is the content.
- **Build risk:** Detail-alterations (hue on one region) need region masks; keep v1 to mirror / scale / whole-hue / shape variants.
