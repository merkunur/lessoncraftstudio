## The skill
Find the elapsed time between a start and an end time by counting on hours then minutes.

## The apparatus and its invention
THE TIME RIBBON: a clock with the start time; a coloured ribbon is tied to the minute hand. The child drags the minute hand round to the end time (shown on a second, small clock); the ribbon unrolls along the rim, and each full turn coils into a one-hour loop beside the clock while leftover minutes stay as a short strip marked in 5s. The child types hours and minutes from the loops and the strip. Miss: the loops and strip get numbers.

- **Misconception the apparatus makes visible:** Subtracting the clock numbers (3:45 to 5:15 = 2:30) and counting the hours crossed instead of full hours elapsed.
- **Pictures:** none (clock drawn tool-grade; reuse no shared core)
- **Settings:** Steps: whole hours / hours and 5 minutes / to the minute · Cross noon: never / sometimes
- **Rounds:** Generated start/end pairs, ≥ 9 rounds, durations under 1 h, exactly n h, and n h m min all present; typed answers.
- **Worksheet types it covers:** G3-344
- **Distinct from:** clock-elapsed asks for the END time from a duration; the ribbon asks for the DURATION. Not the Learning Clock tool (free play).
- **Build risk:** Own core (0 lines to the clock cores). Start/end times appear as digital readouts in each locale's convention (24-hour in de/fr/es/pt/it/nl/sv/da/no/fi; 12-hour in en), so 'cross noon' rounds read 11:30 → 13:15 outside en; the analog face is unchanged.
