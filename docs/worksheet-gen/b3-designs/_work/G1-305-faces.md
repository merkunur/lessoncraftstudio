## Deviations from §3 (each with the measurement)

1. **Row faces use `cardGrid` rows of `.ws-card`, not `.ws-lane`** (§3 wrote "`cardGrid({cols:1, rows:6}) of .ws-lane`", which is not a thing — cardGrid emits `.ws-card`). Inner 647 with the 20 px badge pad (K-318 / G1-309 precedent) instead of 639; the badge numbers the rows.
2. **Widths re-budgeted to 647 − 20:** Write = 64 + 12 + **196** + 12 + **339** (design 200 / 351 summed to 659 > 647); Scramble = 64 + 12 + 280 + 12 + **255** (design 267 summed to 655). Measured tile rows: three de tiles of `Fle·der·maus` = 226 px; the 280 slot holds every 11-letter word rendered, and verify fails a wrapped row.
3. **Cloze picture 56 → 72.** At the shipping chrome the 2×4 card inner is 159 px and the 56 + 6 + 40 stack left 45 px of cream; 72 fits the worst chrome (139 − 12 padding = 127 ≥ 72 + 6 + 40 + 6 = 124) and the img shrinks (`min-height 54`) if it ever does not.
4. **Sort bank is two lines (~200 px), not "≈ 110 high".** Eight icon chips at wordPx 18 measure ~870 px of chips + gaps on a 675 inner; `chimpanzee` alone is 220. Rather than shrink the chips below the K-2 floor, the columns stretch to the body and the four rulings spread inside them with the gap capped at 36 px (ruling geometry unchanged: h 64 / glyphH 28 = the G1-244 rule).
5. **Sort: "never grouped by count" is enforced structurally**, not just verified — a collation order that is count-monotone is re-drawn (up to 30 samples), else REFUSED. Without this 1 page in 35 would have made the column readable from the bank position while still "in collation order".
6. **Scramble: the first tile is never the true first syllable** (added, G1-249's rule); §3 only said `order !== identity`, under which a three-tile row could ship as `[0,2,1]` and be solved by swapping the last two.
7. **Kings example banner ~76 px, not 64**: word cells at cell 24 (36 tall) + arcs 22 + padding 14 + border 5; the design's 64 could not hold two svg rows. Cards under the worst chrome get inner ~171 and the picture shrinks to 68.7 (≥ 44).
8. **Kings stamps the vowel list on the root** (`data-lcs-vowels` / `data-lcs-vowelextra`) so `verify()` can re-derive the kings in the browser; the node gate asserts the stamp === `letter-knowledge.json vowels[loc]` + the bank's `vowelExtra`.
9. **`data-lcs-cards` on the sort root = 8** (the bank size; the base's `cards:6` would otherwise ride through the d2 spread).
