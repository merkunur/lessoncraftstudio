## 2. Concept & creative hook
**Whistle Valley** — a toy-train valley where the track IS a number line. A friendly **singing engine** runs the valley, but a storm tore out sections of track between two intact **anchor ties** (labeled with their numerals — e.g. 14 … 20). The child is the track-repair crew.
- **THE CONTINUOUS RULER (the critic's + Designer A's resolution — lay track TO SCALE):** the track is a continuous number line with the two anchors labeled. The child takes a **numbered tie** from the supply trolley (over-provided, with distractor ties) and **drags it to the position its magnitude demands** — "16 goes here because it's 2 past 14, two-sixths of the way to 20." There is **NO discrete empty-slot set to sort into** — the tie is graded by whether it lands within a tolerance ε of its true magnitude-position (`|placedPct − valueToPct(n)| < ε`). This **structurally kills the SORT-ASCENDING cheat** (no slots to rank ties into; the child must compute each tie's absolute distance) AND makes magnitude genuinely load-bearing (a tie at the wrong distance reads wrong even if the order is right).
- **K-FRIENDLY continuous (NOT pixel-precision):** a generous tolerance ε (~1/3 unit); the anchors stay visible as the count-on reference (pinned, the critic's F); a **magnitude-preview** rides the dragged tie (a faint indicator of its current read-position relative to the anchors — a self-correction scaffold toward count-on, NOT the answer). The cognition graded = count-on/magnitude (which distance), not motor precision.
- **IN-RANGE DISTRACTORS (the critic's B — the "is this a station?" check):** on skip-count/non-dense rounds the trolley holds ties that sort cleanly but are NOT stations (a by-5 round 10→30 offers 17, 22 alongside 15, 20, 25) → a sorter who places the smallest-N fails; the child must check each numeral against the stride/magnitude.
- **COUNT-ON-FROM-ANY-START (the standard's core):** anchors are mid-sequence (47…53, 83…89), NEVER always 14→20-from-1; the storm-damage windows the line so the child counts on from where the anchors are.
- **NO SEQUENTIAL AUDIO DURING SOLVING (the critic's C — the successor-leak killed):** the gaps are SILENT while solving (singing 48…[gap]…50 would hand over 49). In-solve audio = per-tie numeral-naming only (tap a tie → "seventeen"). The whole count-on SONG is the POST-COMMIT reward.
- **THE PAYOFF (earned by the sequence — Designer B's singing train):** when the child commits and the track is correct, the engine **SINGS the restored sequence whole** ("14, 15, 16, …!") as it chugs across the repaired track — the sequence made audible + whole. `ctx.correct()`. A wrong commit → the engine waits patiently at the station (NO penalty), the trolley reshuffles (anti-brute-force), the child re-places.
- **Anti-generic statement:** NOT a number-line worksheet with a train sticker — (a) continuous place-at-distance makes MAGNITUDE load-bearing + kills the sort cheat (no slots to sort into); (b) in-range distractors force the station-check; (c) count-on-from-any-start (mid-sequence) defeats rote-count-from-1; (d) the singing-train sings the restored sequence (the count-on made audible — the premium delight); (e) the gate proves a SORT_ASCENDING + SUCCESSOR_ONLY + spacing-blind + count-by-1 + brute-force solver all lose while a count-on/magnitude oracle wins.

## 3. Core loop & the honest ~7 distinct cognitions
**Loop (`stage`: read → place → commit):** the storm-torn track shows the anchors + present ties → the child reads each numbered tie from the trolley + drags it to its magnitude-position (continuous, tolerance-graded; magnitude-preview scaffold) → taps "send the train" (commit) → correct → the engine sings the restored sequence + chugs across → `ctx.correct()`. **No fail, no timer, no score, no countdown, no attempts-counter, the train NEVER races.** Wrong commit → the engine waits (no penalty) + the trolley reshuffles + re-randomize (anti-brute-force); NO on-drop feedback (all evaluation at commit — the critic's D). First-attempt-correct is the internal signal (never shown).

**The genuine distinct sequence cognitions (the critic's E — collapsed honest set; a different NUMBER is NOT distinct = the number-variant, blocklisted). Each round carries a `cog`:**
| # | Cognition (`cog`) | The child's act | Why genuinely distinct |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ★ | **count-on-from-any-start** (`count-on` — interior + open-right collapsed) | place ties at +1 positions counting on from a MID-SEQUENCE anchor (47,_,_,50) | the standard's headline skill (count to 120 from ANY start); continuous → magnitude load-bearing |
| 2 ★ | **count-back / descending** (`count-back`) | the train arrives from the RIGHT; place ties descending (25,_,_,22) | predecessor reasoning (distinct from successor) |
| 3 ★ | **skip-count** (`skip` — by-5 + by-10, the stride is the parameter) | place ties at +5/+10 positions (10,_,30,_); the in-range distractors force the station-check | apply a STRIDE (2.NBT.A.2); the stride is load-bearing (rote +1 fails) |
| 4 ★ | **decade-boundary crossing** (`decade`) | place ties across a ×10 tick (38,39,_,_,42) | the 39→40 rollover (a real, separable difficulty) |
| 5 ★ | **bridge-100 / fill-to-120** (`to-120` — the critic's catch) | place ties across 100 (98,99,_,_,102) up toward 120 | the standard's CEILING (literally "count to 120" — must reach it) |
| 6 ★ | **two-gap simultaneous** (`two-gap`) | two non-adjacent gaps in one segment (33,_,35,_) | a simultaneous-constraint reconstruction (defeats one-at-a-time) |
| 7 ★ | **pure-magnitude interval** (`interval`) | ONE tie, anchors far apart (10…30), no neighbor — place 20 exactly halfway | distance is the ONLY cue (no count-on shortcut); genuine magnitude — now real with continuous placement |

The optional write-the-numeral stamp adds an 8th + earns K.CC.A.3. Order-only reshuffle.
- **Free slice = count-on + skip + interval** — the headline count-on-from-any-start + the stride-check + the pure-magnitude; a SORT_ASCENDING solver fails (no slots / in-range distractors), a count-by-1 solver fails (skip + any-start), a brute-force solver fails (commit + reshuffle).

## 4. Win-states & juice (no competition)
No timer/score/streak/countdown/**fail-state**/attempts-counter/"X of Y" — **the train NEVER races/countdowns** (it waits, patient — the whole fiction is a friendly, never-impatient engine). **THE SINGING TRAIN (signature — the count-on made audible, the reward):** correct commit → the rails complete (a left→right sweep) + the engine **SINGS the restored sequence whole** ("14, 15, 16, …!" via `ctx.speak`, digit-words per locale) + chugs across the repaired track + a steam-whistle at the far anchor. **Non-numeric vessel = the CLEARING SKY / the repaired-route map** (`GameCollection` — each repaired segment parts a band of storm-cloud over the valley / fills gold track on the route map; progress is brightness/track, NEVER a count/"N of M"). **Negative signal = the engine WAITS, NON-leaking (the critic's D):** wrong commit → the engine waits at the station (NO red/buzzer/penalty) + the trolley reshuffles + re-randomizes (info-destroying, anti-brute-force); NO on-drop feedback (ties place freely + silently; all evaluation at commit — so a wrong tile at a gap NEVER leaks "wrong gap"). The commit is reversible ("ready?" → the train waits; the child keeps fiddling, zero penalty). `ctx.wrong()` suppressed to a gentle "not yet — the train's still waiting." `ctx.correct()` on the correct commit → auto-advance. Full-pass keepsake = the cleared valley / completed route → cross-game `GameCollection`. CSS/SVG/WebAudio + TTS; reduced-motion (instant train traversal); mute-safe (the line + numerals carry it — the song is reward-polish; in-solve per-tie numeral-naming has a visible-numeral fallback).

- **Grade fit + range:** 1.NBT.A.1 is to 120; windowed segments (lead 0-20, push 40-100, the `to-120` ceiling); count-on-from-any-start the spine; the difficulty ladder count-on → skip/decade → to-120/two-gap → pure-interval.

## 6. Interaction & UX
- **Runtime: read → place-at-distance → commit (continuous).** `track-repair-core.js` REUSES `number-line.js`'s `valueToPct(v) = ((v-min)/(max-min))*100` (position = magnitude) + viewBox/`preserveAspectRatio="none"` geometry as PURE functions (copied, not imported — the mini-tool is a different runtime). The round `{ cog, start, end (anchors, labeled), step (1/5/10), presentTies[], targetTies[] (the ties to place, each with its true value), trayDistractors[] (in-range, non-station), placementMode:'continuous' (tolerance ε), epsilon }` + `placeTie(tieValue, placedPct)` (records the continuous position) + `isRepaired()` (every target tie within ε of its `valueToPct(value)`). **NO discrete slots; the answer = `f(read the numeral → count-on/magnitude → place at the absolute distance)`.**
- **State separation (anti-cheat):** `gateView()` exposes `{ stations (ground truth), targetTies (value + true xPct), trayTies (incl. in-range distractors), anchors, step, startsAtOne, placementMode, epsilon, placements }` for the gate. `facts()` exposes per-round `{ placementContinuous (===true — no discrete slots), inRangeDistractorPresent (===true on non-dense rounds), noSequentialSolvingAudio (===true), evaluateAtCommitOnly (===true), reshuffleOnWrongCommit (===true), anchorsPinnedVisible (===true), reachesCeiling (≥1 round ≥100) }`.
- **The place-at-distance interaction:** drag a numbered tie onto the track; a **magnitude-preview** rides it (its current read-position relative to the anchors — a count-on self-correction scaffold, NOT the answer); release → it settles at the dropped position (within ε of true → reads correct at commit). Generous ε (~1/3 unit) + the pinned anchors for K-motor-friendliness. Tap-to-pick + tap-to-place fallback (280px). Keyboard: Tab to a tie, arrows to nudge its position by unit-steps (count-on by keyboard), Enter to set. For `write`: a digit-stamp-pad builds the numeral.
- **Mobile @280px (the critic's F):** short ≤7-station windowed segments (the storm-damage windows the line); the anchors **PINNED/sticky at the viewport edges** ("←14" / "20→") so the count-on reference NEVER scrolls off; the trolley of ties wraps below; the clearing-sky vessel a thin top strip; the train rides the rail's y-band. Scroll-window only when >7 stations (rare; anchors stay pinned; drag-to-edge autoscroll). `audit-game-mobile.js` 280-768 empty+filled (track + anchors + ties + trolley; ≥44px; legible numerals; no overflow/clip/overlap; to-scale spacing not collapsing ties into overlap).
- **Accessibility:** TTS reads the prompt + (per-tie, on tap) the tie's numeral + (post-commit) the restored sequence; `ctx.announce()` mirrors the place/commit (a count-on nudge "the train was at 47," NEVER the gap answer); full keyboard path (Tab + arrows = count-on by steps); ≥44px; reduced-motion (instant traversal); mute-safe (the line + numerals carry it; the song is reward; in-solve audio has a visible-numeral fallback); real-text numerals (never baked images); not color-only (the train moving + the rail completing + aria-live, never hue alone). **TTS = digit-words (whole numbers), SAFE; NO sequential singing near an unsolved gap.**
