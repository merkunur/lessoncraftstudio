## 2. Concept & creative hook
**Clunk** is a chubby, dented snack-robot whose memory chip cracked. He can still *feel* exactly how hungry he is — *"I need exactly 10 crunch-units, friend!"* — but he **can't add for himself anymore.** So the child isn't begging a vending machine for permission; **the child is Clunk's helper.** The vending wall is full of snack-characters, each wearing a **hunger-number** (how many crunch-units it gives Clunk — a 6-snack, a 4-snack; **NOT a price** — the broken-hungry-robot fiction, not money). The child gathers snacks into a **lunchbox**, **plans a combination that makes exactly 10**, then **feeds Clunk** (pulls his candy-cane lever) — Clunk eats the whole lunchbox at once and discovers if it sat right. The agency hook: **you're the one who can count for Clunk — pick the snacks YOU want, as long as they make his number.**
- **THE PLAN-THEN-FEED SPINE (the converged anti-cheat — no live aggregate):** the child taps snacks off the wall → each travels into the lunchbox as a chip **showing its own hunger-number** (the pretzel reads "3"); same-number reuse allowed (5+5). **The lunchbox shows NO total** — no climbing meter, no "7/10" — so the child must **add it up themselves** to decide it's right. Tap a lunchbox chip → it returns to the wall (free re-planning, silent, no penalty). **There is nothing on screen that equals the sum** → "watch a total climb to 10" is structurally impossible.
- **THE FEED IS THE ONLY EVALUATOR (the commit):** when the child believes the lunchbox is right, they **pull the lever / feed Clunk** — Clunk eats the **whole lunchbox at once** and only NOW is `sum===T` checked. `ctx.correct()` fires from this commit handler ONLY (never per-tap — explicitly breaking the per-tap-win model). NO auto-open the instant sum===T.
- **THE SECOND-DIFFERENT-WAY (the critic's "one thing" — the default loop, the planner-vs-guesser separator):** on a correct feed, Clunk doesn't just say yum — *"so good — make it for me AGAIN, a whole new way!"* The child must produce a **second decomposition of the same T that differs as a MULTISET OF HUNGER-NUMBERS** (6+4 then 7+3 — NOT 4+6, which is the same multiset). A lucky first guess gives ZERO help on the second; the planner who *modeled* 10=6+4 knows to try 7+3. This is K.OA.A.3 in its literal text ("more than one way"), and it raises the brute-force ceiling everywhere.
- **Why this is genuinely load-bearing + non-cheatable:** (a) no live aggregate → no climb surface; (b) the feed is the only evaluator + no auto-open → no tap-watch-stop; (c) a wrong feed EMPTIES the lunchbox (no ratchet) → commit-spam can't accumulate; (d) the wrong state is NON-DIRECTIONAL (no over/under tell) → no hill-climb; (e) the second-different-way + palette-floor (no single snack ≥ T) make planning genuinely cheaper than guessing (the gate proves brute-force needs ≥K commits).
- **THE NO-SHAME EMPTY (the critic's reconcile):** a wrong feed → Clunk does ONE gentle *"hmm — that wasn't quite my size!"* (the SAME whether over or under — non-directional) and **hands the lunchbox back** — the snacks **visibly return to the wall** (not lost, not destroyed; "let's pack a fresh one!"). Anti-ratchet preserved (the lunchbox is empty for the next plan); no-shame preserved (nothing was taken away).
- **THE LUNCHBOX (the keepsake + non-numeric vessel):** every meal Clunk happily eats packs its snacks into a growing **lunchbox gallery** (`GameCollection`) — *the food the child chose*, a warm artifact of their decompositions — NOT "5 of 9," no bar. Clunk's satisfaction is the win, never "correct."
- **Anti-generic statement:** NOT a snack-mashing toy — (a) no live aggregate → the climb is structurally impossible (gate-proven); (b) the second-different-way default loop separates planner from guesser; (c) the multiple-solutions FREEDOM is the K.OA.A.3 soul (kept as the centerpiece); (d) the hunger-not-price fiction keeps it off the money standard + culturally flat; (e) a board ships only if a **climb-and-watch solver cannot exist** (no aggregate node), a **commit-spam solver FAILS** (empties), a **brute-force solver needs ≥K commits** (palette-floor), while a **plan-then-feed solver PASSES** in one commit — and the two-different-ways round rejects a multiset-repeat (§8).

## 3. Core loop & the 7 distinct decomposition cognitions
**Loop (`stage`: assemble → feed):** Clunk states his hunger-number T → the child taps snacks into the lunchbox (costs visible, NO aggregate) → plans a combination → **feeds Clunk** (the lever) → `sum===T` → Clunk eats happily + replays the decomposition ("six and four makes ten!") + (default loop) asks for a *different way* → repeat until the lunchbox gallery is full → `ctx.correct()`. **No fail, no timer, no score, no Check button.** A wrong feed → ONE non-directional *"not quite my size!"* → the snacks return to the wall, re-plan from scratch (NO over/under tell, NO "need 4 more"). First-attempt-correct is the internal signal (never shown).

**The 7 distinct experiences — genuine DECOMPOSITION cognitions (the critic's honest set; "make-10 vs make-12" = a magnitude reskin, BLOCKLISTED; make-ten = a weighted CONFIG not a distinct experience; judge-the-exact-one CUT). distinct = a different decomposition reasoning, NOT a bigger T / a different snack.** Each carries a declared **`schema`**:
| # | Experience (the distinct cognition, `schema`) | Render / act | Why it's distinct |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ★ | **Free-make-T** (`free` — the K.OA.A.3 soul/centerpiece) | empty lunchbox → ANY combination summing to T → feed | open generative decomposition; multiple solutions; the soul |
| 2 ★ | **Two-different-ways** (`two-ways` — the centerpiece cognition; also woven as the default loop) | make T, then a MULTISET-DISTINCT second way (6+4 then 7+3, NOT 4+6) | K.OA.A.3 literal ("more than one way") — the planner-vs-guesser separator; the most climb-proof |
| 3 ★ | **Missing-addend** (`missing-addend` — single completer; make-ten is a weighted CONFIG here) | Clunk pre-holds LOCKED snacks (a 6); find the single completer to T (a+?=T) | 1.OA.D.8 determinative (find THE completer) — distinct from generative free-make; T=10 config = K.OA.A.4 |
| 4 ★ | **Multi-completer-of-a-partial** (`multi-completer` — the completer itself decomposes) | Clunk holds a 6; finish to 10 using EXACTLY TWO snacks (1+3, 2+2, 4+... no) | the completer is itself decomposed (?=a+b) — distinct from #3's single completer; bridges missing-addend + decompose |
| 5 ★ | **Exactly-N-items** (`exact-n` — partition into a fixed count) | make T with EXACTLY 3 snacks (10 = 3+3+4, 2+4+4) | partition-into-N-parts ≠ partition-into-any-parts — a real combinatorial escalation |
| 6 ★ | **Constrained-wall** (`constrained-wall` — limited palette forces a non-obvious partition) | the wall offers only certain hunger-numbers (no 5s) → 10 = 3+3+4, not 5+5 | decomposition under a palette constraint — a genuine planning escalation |
| 7 ★ | **Reduce-to-target** (`reduce` — the subtract direction) | the lunchbox starts OVER-full (snacks summing > T) → put snacks back until exactly T | start-high-go-down (removal) ≠ start-empty-go-up — the subtraction reading; the put-back mechanic |

- **Honest framing (the critic's de-padding + §A.13.60):** **7 genuinely-distinct decomposition cognitions.** **CUT** (the critic): make-ten-as-a-distinct-experience (it's missing-addend pinned to T=10 — kept as a *weighted config* of #3, K.OA.A.4 honestly cited) + judge-the-exact-one (a recognition reskin the pedagogy ruled "a consequence, not a cognition"; + it's the 280px two-lunchbox fail). The variety gate keys on `schema` with a **BLOCKLIST**: "differs only in T (make-10 vs make-12) / a different snack-skin / magnitude" do NOT count; make-ten is a CONFIG of #3; order-or-item-identity does NOT make a "different way" (multiset-of-costs only). Order-only reshuffle.
- **The free-decompose freedom is the CENTERPIECE (the pedagogy guardrail):** #1 + #2 (generative, multiple-solution) are weighted as the spine; #3/#4 (missing-addend) are supporting modalities, NOT a third of the game (else it drifts toward Game 5's single-answer lane).
- **Free slice = free-make (1) + two-different-ways (2) + reduce-to-target (7)** — the generative soul + the multiple-ways separator + the subtract direction.

## 4. Win-states & juice (no competition)
No timer/score/streak/countdown/**fail-state**/attempts-counter/"X of Y". **THE FEED (signature — Clunk eats the whole lunchbox at once):** the child plans → pulls the lever → Clunk whirs (a suspense beat) → eats the lunchbox → IF `sum===T`, his tummy-window glows, a happy full-body wiggle, the snacks cascade down (each *plinks* its hunger-number — replaying the child's decomposition as a gentle self-check: "four… and four… and two… makes ten!"), `ctx.speak()` the warm combination back ("A soda and two gummies — that's a ten!"). **THE SECOND-DIFFERENT-WAY:** "make it AGAIN, a whole new way!" (multiset-distinct). **Non-numeric vessel = the LUNCHBOX gallery** (`GameCollection` — the snacks the child chose, filling with personality) — NO "5/9", no bar; a full happy lunchbox at the end (a soft close). **Negative signal = ONE non-directional state ONLY:** wrong feed → the SAME gentle *"not quite my size!"* whether over OR under (NO hiccup-vs-rumble tell) → the snacks RETURN to the wall (not destroyed) → re-plan from scratch. Never a buzzer/red-X/"wrong" (the no-shame is in the fiction — Clunk's grateful you're helping). **`ctx.wrong()` is a soft "let's pack a fresh one."** `ctx.correct()` fires from the commit handler ONLY, on the full gallery → auto-advance. Full-pass keepsake = the packed lunchbox gallery → cross-game `GameCollection`. CSS/SVG/WebAudio; reduced-motion (instant eat/dispense); mute-safe (the visible cascade + the dispensed snacks carry it).

## 5. Pedagogy
- **K.OA.A.3 + 1.OA.D.8 instantiation:** the child DECOMPOSES T into a self-chosen combination of addends (the multiple-solutions freedom — K.OA.A.3, foregrounded by the second-different-way) AND determines an unknown completer (missing-addend — 1.OA.D.8). The lunchbox the child packs IS the decomposition; the feed verifies it.
- **THE KEYSTONE RULES (non-cheatability — the converged + critic-fixed anti-cheat):** (i) **NO LIVE AGGREGATE + FEED-IS-THE-ONLY-EVALUATOR** — no DOM node ever equals `sum(tray)` during assemble; `ctx.correct()` from the commit handler only; no auto-open → the climb is structurally impossible. (ii) **WRONG EMPTIES + NON-DIRECTIONAL** — a wrong feed empties the lunchbox (no ratchet) with ONE state regardless of over/under (no hill-climb tell) → commit-spam + binary-search both die. (iii) **SECOND-DIFFERENT-WAY (multiset-of-costs)** — the planner-vs-guesser separator; "different" = a distinct sorted multiset of hunger-numbers (6+4 ≠ 7+3; 6+4 === 4+6). (iv) **PALETTE-FLOOR** — no single wall snack ≥ T (no 1-tap trivial solve); ≥2 NON-SUBSET decompositions per target (gate-enforced — no unsolvable/single-answer level); planning provably cheaper than guessing (brute-force needs ≥K commits). (v) **EXACT-ONLY** (exactness = decomposition, not estimation). (vi) **HUNGER-NOT-PRICE** (off the K.OA.A.2 money standard; culturally flat).
- **The trial-and-error ceiling, honestly resolved (the critic's fix):** you can't fully kill blind guessing in a free-decompose game, but the second-different-way default loop + the palette-floor + the wrong-empties cost make **planning genuinely cheaper than guessing** (a lucky first guess gives zero help on a multiset-distinct second solve; the gate proves a random-tray solver needs ≥K commits). This is the engineered floor, not a hand-wave.
- **The free-decompose freedom is real K.OA.A.3, kept as the centerpiece (the guardrail):** "pick any combination that sums to T, more than one way" builds part-whole flexibility (the make-ten facts 1.OA.C.6 fluency rests on). The cheat lived in the FEEDBACK (the live total), never in the freedom — cut the feedback, keep the freedom.
- **also_teaches (re-based to honesty per the pedagogy ruling + the hunger-reskin):** co-primary **K.OA.A.3 + 1.OA.D.8**; secondary **K.OA.A.4** (make-ten — the weighted config of #3) + **1.OA.C.6** (within-20 strategies, when T scales up); **DROP K.OA.A.2** (no posed word problem; the hunger-not-price reskin removes the money framing that would have tempted it).
- **Number ranges (pedagogy + grade-by-mechanic):** **within 10 base (Kindergarten K.OA)** — T ≤ 10, hunger-numbers 1-9, primarily 2-addend (3-addend paths for #5); **within 20 ceiling (Grade 1)** — missing-addend + exactly-3-items, T ≤ 20, 2-3 addends. Per-coordinate grade by mechanic+quantity (§22.1) — a free-make T≤10 coordinate is K, a missing-addend T≤20 is Gr1; do NOT tag the whole game one band. Every T has ≥2 distinct exact decompositions.
- **Distinctness from Games 5 & 22 + the siblings:** Game 5 = repair a SYMBOLIC equation (notation); Game 22 = equalize two live piles; this = generative free-decompose-to-a-target (multiple solutions). The free-decompose FREEDOM is what neither sibling offers (Fixit's has one answer; Sharing Jar has a determined equalization) — Game 22 CEDED this lane. If the game collapsed to single-answer missing-addend, it drifts to Game 5 — REJECTED (the free-decompose centerpiece is the guard).
- **No-shame + §20.8 demonstrate-don't-touch:** the catalog free-climb only TOUCHED adding (a perceptual climb); the no-aggregate plan-then-feed DIRECTLY instantiates decompose-many-ways + the unknown completer — the gate (no climb surface; commit-spam + brute-force fail; multiset-repeat rejected; plan-then-feed passes) is the structural proof. Wrong → ONE warm non-directional return; the freedom is the intrinsic reward (your own meal).

## 8. Runtime mapping + MEASURED build-gate + PER-LOCALE
- **Round descriptor (DATA; the check is a scalar — multiple solutions automatic):** `{ id, schema (the §3 enum), target (T; varies — within-10 K / within-20 Gr1; non-monotonic), wall:[costs] (palette-floored: no single cost ≥ T for free; ≥2 non-subset decompositions exist), given?:[locked] (#3/#4), allow?:[palette] (#6), start?:[over-full] (#7), exactN? (#5) }`. `makeRounds` procedural (within-10 base / within-20 ceiling; the 7 schemas distributed, free+two-ways-weighted as the centerpiece; make-ten as a weighted T=10 config of #3; ≥2 non-subset decompositions/target enforced).
- **`verify-make-total-core.js` (MEASURED — drives the REAL core + a telemetry handle; HALTS build) — the converged + critic-fixed solver set:**
  1. **CLIMB-AND-WATCH solver must FAIL — STRUCTURAL:** assert `hasLiveAggregate===false` + `liveSumNodeQuery` returns 0 matches at EVERY tray state (no DOM node's text equals `sum(tray)` during assemble) → nothing to watch. (The cohort through-line — the gate asserts the ABSENCE of the climb surface.)
  2. **COMMIT-SPAM solver must FAIL:** a wrong commit EMPTIES the tray (no ratchet) → can't accumulate toward T across commits.
  3. **BRUTE-FORCE solver requires ≥K commits (the ceiling teeth):** a random-tray solver must NOT win in ≲3 tries — `noSingleItemGEtarget===true` (palette-floor; no 1-tap solve) + ≥2 non-subset decompositions/target; planning provably cheaper than guessing.
  4. **DIRECTIONAL-FEEDBACK-ABSENCE:** assert `wrongStateDirectional===false` — an over-tray and an under-tray wrong commit are observationally identical (NO hiccup-vs-rumble tell) → no hill-climb.
  5. **MULTISET-REPEAT-REJECTION (#2):** assert the two-ways round rejects a commit whose sorted multiset of costs equals a prior decomposition (6+4 then 4+6 → rejected; 6+4 then 7+3 → accepted).
